The drawing shows a 2-methoxybenzamide whose amide nitrogen is attached to a substituted phenyl ring.

**Benzoyl part:** a benzene ring with an OMe group on the carbon next to the carbonyl-bearing carbon. The carbonyl carbon (C=O) is bonded to NH.

**Aniline ring:** the ring carbon attached to NH is C1. Going around the ring:
- C2 carries a methyl (the upper-right position in the drawing).
- C3 carries the pyrrolidin-1-yl group (N-linked).
- C4 carries a methyl (lower right).
- C5 is an unsubstituted CH.
- C6 carries a methyl (lower left).

This ring is therefore 2,4,6-trimethyl-3-(pyrrolidin-1-yl)phenyl.

**SMILES:** COc1ccccc1C(=O)Nc1c(C)c(N2CCCC2)c(C)cc1C

Check of the aniline ring: c1 (N-bearing), c(C), c(N2CCCC2), c(C), cH, c1C. That gives 6 ring atoms with methyls at positions 2, 4 and 6 and pyrrolidine at 3, which matches the drawing.
