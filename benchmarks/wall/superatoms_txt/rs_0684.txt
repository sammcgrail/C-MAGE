I read the structure in four parts.

- **Amide end:** a morpholine ring is attached through its nitrogen to a C(=O) group, which is followed by a CH2.
- **First stereocentre:** the next carbon carries a wedged CH2–cyclohexyl group and a C(=O)NH group.
- **Second stereocentre:** the amide NH is attached to a carbon bearing a hashed methyl group, which is followed by CH2–NH–aryl.
- **Aryl group:** a para-substituted benzene ring carrying OCF3.

**Stereocentre 1 (wedged CH2-cyclohexyl):**
- Priorities are C(=O)N > CH2C(=O) > CH2-cyclohexyl > H.
- With H pointing away, the sequence runs clockwise, so this centre is R.
- In the SMILES this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.

**Stereocentre 2 (hashed methyl):**
- Priorities are N > CH2N > CH3 > H.
- The drawing shows N → CH2 → CH3 going clockwise, but H points toward the viewer because the methyl is hashed.
- Reversing for that gives S.
- In the SMILES this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.
