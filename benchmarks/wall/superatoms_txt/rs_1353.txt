Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Ring system:** a benzotriazole (benzene fused to a 1,2,3-triazole bearing an N–H). The side chain is on the benzene carbon one position away from a ring-fusion carbon, which is the 5-position.
- **Side chain:** CH2 attached to a CH carrying NH2 and CO2Me (a methyl ester), so this is an α-amino acid methyl ester.
- **Stereochemistry:** the α-carbon has a bold wedge to H. Its other three substituents sit at NH2 (down), CO2Me (upper right) and CH2 (upper left). The CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H.
  - Viewed from the front, N → CO2Me → CH2 runs counterclockwise.
  - H points toward the viewer, so the true sense is reversed, which gives clockwise, i.e. R.
  - The SMILES `N[C@H](CAr)C(=O)OC` encodes R, since L-alanine is `N[C@@H](C)C(=O)O` (S).
