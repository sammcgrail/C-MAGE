The drawing shows a pyridine ring joined through an amide to a spiro ketal.

**Pyridine part.** The ring atoms are N, C2 (bearing the carboxamide), C3 (OH), C4 (OMe), C5 and C6. This is a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide.

**Amide.** The C2 carbonyl carbon is bonded to NH.

**Spiro ketal.** The NH is attached to C3 of a 1,3-dioxane ring. That ring runs CH(N), CH2, O, spiro C, O, CH2, back to CH(N). The spiro carbon is also part of a cyclohexane ring: spiro C, CH2, C(CH3)2, CH2, C(CH3)2, CH2, back to the spiro C. This gives a 3,3,9,9-tetramethyl-1,5-dioxaspiro[5.5]undecane.
