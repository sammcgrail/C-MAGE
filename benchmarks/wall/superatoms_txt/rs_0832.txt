I read the molecule as a 2-oxo-benzoxazolone core carrying an amino-acid side chain, with the following features:

- **Core:** a benzoxazol-2(3H)-one with Br at C4. The ring oxygen and the NH sit on the five-membered ring, with C=O between them.
- **Side chain:** a CH2 at C6 of the core, leading to a CH bearing CO2CH3 and NHCbz. Cbz is the benzyloxycarbonyl group, NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–Ph.
- **Stereochemistry:** the bond from the α-carbon to NHCbz is hashed, so N points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
  - Priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2Ar > H.
  - Viewed from the front, N (bottom) → CO2Me (upper right) → CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise.
  - Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is R (a D-amino acid).
  - Comparing with L-alanine, N[C@@H](C)C(=O)O (S), the neighbour order CO2Me, H, CH2, N is one swap away, so R corresponds to @@.
