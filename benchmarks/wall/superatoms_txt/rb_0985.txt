I read the structure in sections and joined them into one SMILES. It is a VHL-type degrader: a hydroxyproline–tert-leucine–thiazolylbenzylamide E3 ligand joined through a urea to a trans-looking cyclohexyl–pyrimidine–diazabicyclooctane–aminopyridazine–phenol unit.

**Fragments, left to right**
- **Phenol and pyridazine:** a 2-hydroxyphenyl sits on C6 of the pyridazine. C3 carries NH2 and C4 carries the bicyclic amine.
- **Bicyclic amine:** it is a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane. N3 attaches to the pyridazine, and the bridging N8 attaches to C2 of the pyrimidine.
- **Pyrimidine and cyclohexane:** the pyrimidine is 2,5-disubstituted. Its C5 bonds to the cyclohexane, which carries the urea NH at the 1,4-position.
- **Urea and tert-leucine:** the urea links the cyclohexyl NH to the NH of tert-leucine. The tert-butyl group is on the α-carbon.
- **Hydroxyproline and tail:** the proline carries the 4-OH. Its amide goes to a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzyl group.

**Stereochemistry**
- **Cyclohexane:** both ring substituents are drawn with hashed bonds, so they are on the same face. I encoded this as cis (`@` and `@@`). The relative stereo depends on this reading of the hashes.
- **tert-Leucine:** the hashed NH bond gives the L configuration (S), written `N[C@@H](C(C)(C)C)C(=O)`.
