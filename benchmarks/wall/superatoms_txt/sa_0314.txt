- **Backbone:** HO2C–CH(NH2)–CH2–S–Trt, where Trt is triphenylmethyl, C(C6H5)3.
- **Stereocentre:** The only wedge is a hashed bond from the α-carbon to the CO2H group, so that group points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- **CIP assignment:** The priorities are NH2 > CH2S > CO2H > H, since S outranks O. In the drawing NH2 is at the bottom, CH2S at the upper right and CO2H at the upper left, which runs counterclockwise. With H toward the viewer the sense reverses, so the centre is R. That is the L-cysteine configuration.
