I read the molecule as a VHL-type ligand joined through a cyclopropane-dicarboxamide to a substituted piperidinyl-pyridine. I worked through it in these pieces:

- **Left end:** a 4-cyanopyridin-2-yl group on an NH, attached to the 2-position of a central pyridine.
- **Central pyridine:** the 4-position carries a piperidin-4-yl group, and the 6-position carries a 3,3-difluoropyrrolidin-1-yl group.
- **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen is acylated by a cyclopropane carbonyl. The other cyclopropane carbon carries a second amide. Both cyclopropane bonds are hashed, so the two substituents are cis.
- **tert-Leucine unit:** the NH-bearing carbon carries a tert-butyl group and has a wedged N bond.
- **Hydroxyproline:** a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine with the OH hashed and the C2 carbonyl wedged.
- **Right end:** an NH–CH(CH3) benzylic carbon with a wedged methyl, bonded to a phenyl ring. The phenyl carries a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.

I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash and the 2D geometry in the drawing.
