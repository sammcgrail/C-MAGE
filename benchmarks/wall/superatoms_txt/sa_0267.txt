The drawing is N²,N⁶-bis-Fmoc-lysine (Fmoc-Lys(Fmoc)-OH).

- **Backbone:** A CO2H group is attached to the stereocentre, which also carries an NH-Fmoc. A chain of four CH2 groups leads from the stereocentre to a second NH-Fmoc. This is the lysine skeleton.
- **Fmoc groups:** Each Fmoc is expanded as C(=O)OCH2 attached to fluorene, written `C(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12`.
- **Stereochemistry:** The wedge runs from the stereocentre to the CO2H, so the implicit H points away from the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H. Going N (up) → CO2H (lower left) → CH2 (lower right) is counterclockwise, so the centre is S, the L-lysine configuration.
- **SMILES ordering:** L-lysine is `N[C@@H](CCCCN)C(=O)O`. Writing the neighbours in the order CO2H, H, chain, N swaps N and CO2H, so the tag flips to `@`.
