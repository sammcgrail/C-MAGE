The drawing shows Boc–N=N–Boc, which is di-tert-butyl azodicarboxylate (DBAD).

Boc is the tert-butoxycarbonyl group, C(=O)OC(C)(C)C. Attaching one to each end of the N=N unit gives CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N=NC(=O)OC(C)(C)C.

The double bond is drawn with the two Boc groups on opposite sides of the N=N bond, which looks like trans (E) geometry. The two N–Boc bonds do run in a zigzag across the bond, so I include the E stereo marking: `/N=N/`.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)/N=N/C(=O)OC(C)(C)C
