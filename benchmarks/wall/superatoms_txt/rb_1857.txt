- **Left end:** a 3-fluorophenyl-substituted pyrrolidine whose nitrogen is bonded to C6 of an imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine. The pyrrolidine ring is cut off at the bottom edge of the image, so I drew it as an unsubstituted five-membered ring.

- **Right end:** a pomalidomide unit, i.e. a 4-amino-isoindoline-1,3-dione whose imide nitrogen is bonded to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring. That ring carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
- **Stereocentre:** the only stereo mark is the hashed bond from the pyrrolidine C2 to the fluorophenyl ring. The CIP priorities are N > aryl carbon > ring CH2 > H, and with the aryl group pointing back the sequence N → aryl → CH2 runs counterclockwise from the front, so the centre is R. That corresponds to `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order I wrote.
