The drawing shows a benzene ring with a HOOC (carboxylic acid) group on the upper-left vertex and an iodine on the upper-right vertex.

The ring vertices, going around, are:
- top vertex (about 218, 10)
- upper-right vertex (about 270, 40), which carries the I
- lower-right vertex
- bottom vertex
- lower-left vertex
- upper-left vertex (about 130, 40), which carries the COOH

The COOH carbon is attached at the upper-left vertex, and the top vertex sits between the two substituted vertices. That puts the two substituents on carbons separated by one ring carbon, which is a meta (1,3) arrangement.

This is 3-iodobenzoic acid.

SMILES: OC(=O)c1cccc(I)c1
