I read the molecule as a spiro compound: a phthalide (isobenzofuranone) joined at its C3 to a thioxanthene S,S-dioxide that carries acetoxy groups at the 3 and 6 positions.

**Phthalide benzene ring.** The carbon next to the spiro-bearing carbon is a CH. Going around the ring from the spiro-bearing carbon, the substituents are:
- a CH
- C–OMe
- C–N (the crown nitrogen)
- a CH
- the carbon bearing the lactone C=O

So the OMe carbon sits between a CH and the C–N carbon.

**Aza-crown ring.** Tracing the drawn ring from the nitrogen attached to the phthalide gives 15 ring atoms in this order:
- N(attached to the phthalide ring), then CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2
- N(attached to the 2-methoxyphenyl group), then CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, back to the first N

Between the two nitrogens there are two oxygens on one side and one oxygen on the other. The second nitrogen carries a 2-methoxyphenyl group.

**Thioxanthene part.** The spiro carbon is bonded to two aryl rings that are bridged by SO2. Each aryl ring has an OC(=O)CH3 group para to the spiro-bearing carbon.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I included no stereochemistry.
