I read the drawing as a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core with three substituents.

- **Ring N1:** a 1-cyclopropylpropyl group, CH(cyclopropyl)(ethyl), with the H drawn on a hashed bond.
- **Ring C5:** chlorine.
- **Ring C3:** a 7-chloro-5-methoxyindolin-1-yl group, attached through the indoline nitrogen.

**Stereocentre.** The CH carbon has the hashed H pointing away from the viewer.

1. The CIP priorities are N > cyclopropyl (C,C,H) > ethyl (C,H,H) > H.
2. Viewed from the front, N is at the bottom, cyclopropyl at the upper right and ethyl at the upper left. Going N → cyclopropyl → ethyl runs counterclockwise.
3. With H at the back, this gives the S configuration.

**SMILES chirality tag.** For the string `CC[C@H](C1CC1)N...`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is ethyl, H, cyclopropyl, N. Working through the permutation, `@` gives R, so `@@` gives the required S.
