I read the molecule as an antimycin-type nine-membered dilactone carrying three substituents.

**Pyridine fragment.** The ring has N at the bottom. C2 carries the carboxamide C(=O)NH, C3 carries the acyloxy group, and C4 carries OMe.

**Acyl chain on C3.** The O–C(=O) is followed by six CH2 groups and then COOMe, giving a monomethyl suberate ester (8 carbons in the diacid chain).

- CH(CH3) has a wedged methyl.
- CH(O-isobutyryl) has a hashed bond to O.
