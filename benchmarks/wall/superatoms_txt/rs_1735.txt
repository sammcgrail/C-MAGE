I read the drawing as a 4-substituted benzoic acid carrying a 2-carbon linker to the nitrogen of a pyrrolidin-2-one. The ring carbon next to nitrogen bears a trans-alkenyl chain.

- **Aryl acid:** a para-disubstituted benzene with CO2H at one end and a CH2CH2 chain at the other.
- **Lactam:** the chain is attached to N of a five-membered ring containing C=O (the carbonyl is on the carbon next to N, then two CH2 groups, then the substituted CH).
- **Side chain:** the substituted ring carbon carries a wedged bond to CH=CH, drawn as a trans (E) double bond. This leads to CH(OH), which is drawn with plain bonds and so has no stereo label. That carbon is attached to a cyclopentane carbon that also bears a 2-fluorophenyl group.
- **Stereocentre:** at the lactam CH, the priorities are N, then vinyl carbon, then ring CH2, with H pointing back on the hashed side. Going N (up), vinyl (lower right), CH2 (left) is clockwise, so the centre is R. In the SMILES written below, that corresponds to `@@`.
