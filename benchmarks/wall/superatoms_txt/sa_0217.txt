The drawing is colchicine, the tropolone-methyl-ether natural product. I read it as follows:

- **Left ring (seven-membered, tropolone):** it carries a C=O and an adjacent OMe, and the carbonyl is flanked by a ring CH that leads into the carbon bonded to the stereocentre.
- **Right ring (benzene):** it carries three OMe groups on contiguous carbons.
- **Central seven-membered ring:** it links the two rings through a biaryl bond and a CH2–CH2 bridge, ending at the stereocentre that bears NHAc.

**Stereochemistry:** the only wedge is on the NHAc carbon, so that is the only stereocentre assigned. The N points toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away. The CIP priorities are N > ring carbon > CH2. Going N → ring carbon → CH2 on screen is counterclockwise with H at the back, which gives S. This matches natural (S)-colchicine.
