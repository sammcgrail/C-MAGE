I read the structure as a PROTAC-type molecule and wrote it in four pieces.

1. **Left heterocycle:** a 1H-pyrazolo[4,3-d]pyrimidine. Its C5 carries a 2-fluoro-6-methoxyphenyl group, and its C3 carries a para-substituted phenyl ring.
2. **Linker:** that phenyl ring is attached to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated by a heptanedioyl chain, C(=O)(CH2)5C(=O), with five CH2 groups between the two carbonyls. The far carbonyl forms an amide with the NH of the next unit.
3. **Central unit:** a tert-leucine residue, NH–CH(tBu)–C(=O), whose carbonyl is bonded to the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxyproline ring.
4. **Right end:** the proline carboxyl forms an amide with a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamine.

For stereochemistry I took the wedge and hash bonds on the tert-leucine carbon and the two hydroxyproline centres to be the usual VHL-ligand configuration. I wrote those three stereocentres to match the standard VHL-ligand SMILES rather than deriving each one from the drawing, so they are the part I am least sure of.
