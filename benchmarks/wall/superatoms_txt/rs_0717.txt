The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an isatin core.

**Left part.** A benzoic acid ring carries CO2H at one carbon. The ring carbon meta to it is bonded to a second phenyl ring. On that second ring, the carbon bearing the biaryl bond is adjacent to the C-OH carbon. The next carbon bears the NH of the hydrazone.

**Hydrazone linker.** The NH is bonded to N, which is double-bonded to C3 of the indolinone, giving Ar-NH-N=C3.

**Indolinone.**
- C3 is bonded to C2, which is C(=O), and C2 is bonded to N1.
- N1 carries a 3,4-dimethylphenyl group.
- The benzo ring bears Cl. Going around it from C3a: C3a, C4, C5, C6 (Cl), C7, C7a. In the drawing, the ring carbon bonded to C3 is the lower-left vertex of the upper-right ring. Cl sits on the right, on the carbon that is para to C3a... checking the layout, the C-Cl carbon is bonded to the CH adjacent to C7a, so Cl is at position 6.

**N-aryl ring.** The ring is attached at C1. The methyl at the lower right is at the meta position (C3). The methyl at the bottom is at the para position (C4). This gives 3,4-dimethylphenyl.
