I read the structure as a macrocyclic peptide and wrote it residue by residue, starting from the thioether-linked acetyl group.

**Residues and linkages**
- Gly-NH2 is at the C-terminus, attached to the Cys carbonyl.
- The Cys side-chain sulfur forms a thioether with the CH2 of an acetyl group (S–CH2–C(=O)), and that carbonyl is acylated onto the N of the first Phe, which closes the macrocycle.
- Going around the ring from there: Phe, Val, Asn, Thr, Thr, Phe, N-Me-Ala, biphenylalanine (Bip), N-Me-Ala, Lys, Ala, Pro, back to the Cys N.
- The Lys side-chain NH carries a quaternary carbon bearing a methyl and two wavy attachment bonds. I encoded this as NC(C)(*)* with dummy atoms for the open attachment points. I could not tell what it is bonded to beyond the cut lines.

**Stereochemistry**
- I checked the Ala next to Lys against the wedge/hash drawing and it comes out L (S).
- I assumed L configuration for the remaining residues, including both Thr (2S,3R), without re-deriving each wedge from the drawing.
