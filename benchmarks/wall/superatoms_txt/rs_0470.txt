I read the structure in three parts and joined them through the two amide linkages.

- **Left part:** a 1H-indazole attached through its 6-position carbonyl to the nitrogen of a 5,7-dichloro-1,2,3,4-tetrahydroisoquinoline.
- **Middle:** the tetrahydroisoquinoline carries Cl at C5 and C7, and a carboxamide at C6 that links to the amino acid.
- **Right part:** the amide nitrogen is attached to a stereocentre bearing CO2H, with a CH2–NH–C(=O) chain leading to a piperidine. That piperidine nitrogen is part of a urea, and its 3-position carries an OH.

**Stereochemistry:**
- The α-carbon has a wedge to the CH2 group. The priorities are N > CO2H > CH2N. Tracing N → CO2H → CH2 gives counterclockwise with H at the back, so this carbon is S. In the SMILES this is `[C@@H]` when written as `N[C@@H](CN…)C(=O)O`.
- The piperidine C3 has a wedge to OH. The priorities are OH > C2 > C4. Tracing OH → C2 → C4 gives clockwise with H at the back, so this carbon is R. In the SMILES this is `[C@@H]` when written as `N3CCC[C@@H](O)C3`.
