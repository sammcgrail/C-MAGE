- SMILES fragment: O=C1CCC(N2C(=O)c3c(OCC...)cccc3C2=O)C(=O)N1

- SMILES fragment: Oc...OCCOCCOCCC(=O)NC

- SMILES fragment: C12(CNC(=O)...)CC(C1)(C2)COc...
