The drawing shows a 7-azaindole (pyrrolo[2,3-b]pyridine) core with three substituents.

**Core.** The five-membered ring contains N1, C2, C3, C3a and C7a. N1 is the pyrrole nitrogen, C2 carries a methyl group, and C3 carries the CH2CO2H group. The six-membered ring is fused at C3a and C7a. The pyridine nitrogen is bonded to C7a.

**N1 substituent.** N1 is bonded to a CH2 that leads to a benzene ring. Going around that ring from the attachment carbon (C1):
- C1 is attached to the CH2.
- The next carbon, C2, bears CF3.
- C3 is CH.
- C4 bears the SO2Me group.
- C5 and C6 are CH.

The SO2Me group is therefore para to the CH2 attachment point and meta to CF3.

**SMILES assembly.** Starting from the acid: OC(=O)Cc1c(C)n(Cc2ccc(S(C)(=O)=O)cc2C(F)(F)F)c2ncccc12

**Check on the benzene ring.** The attachment carbon is c2, followed by c, c, then c(SO2Me), then c, then c(CF3), which closes the ring. This places the SO2Me group at position 4 and CF3 at position 2 relative to the CH2, matching the drawing.

**Check on the fused ring.** In c2ncccc12, c2 is C7a, then N, then three CH carbons, then C3a, which closes both rings. This is correct.
