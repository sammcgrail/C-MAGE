I read the structure as a 4-substituted benzoic acid joined through an N-CH2CH2 linker to a 2-pyrrolidinone, which carries a trans-hydroxyoctenyl-type side chain at C5.

- **Lactam ring:** a five-membered ring made of N, C(=O), two CH2 groups and the CH stereocentre next to N.
- **N-substituent:** N-CH2-CH2 joined to a para-disubstituted benzene ring that ends in CO2H.
- **Side chain on the ring CH:** a trans CH=CH, then CH(OH), then a straight butyl chain (four carbons).

**Stereochemistry**
- **Ring CH:** the bond to the vinyl group is a wedge, so H points away from the viewer. Seen from the front, N, vinyl and ring CH2 run clockwise, which gives `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order I used.
- **Carbinol CH:** the OH is on a hashed bond, so H points toward the viewer. Working through the neighbour order in the SMILES gives `[C@@H]`.
- **Alkene:** drawn trans, written `/C=C/`.

SMILES: O=C1CC[C@@H](N1CCc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)O)/C=C/[C@@H](O)CCCC
