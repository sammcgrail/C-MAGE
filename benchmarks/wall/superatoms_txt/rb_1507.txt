I read this as an apremilast-type phthalimide on the left and a pomalidomide unit on the right, joined by a PEG-type linker.

- **Left fragment:** a 3-ethoxy-4-methoxyphenyl ring on a stereocentre bearing CH2SO2CH3 and the phthalimide nitrogen. The phthalimide carries an amide NH at the 4-position.
- **Stereocentre:** the CH2SO2Me bond is hashed. The priorities are N > CH2S > aryl > H, with H toward the viewer. N → CH2S → aryl runs clockwise on the page, so the true order is counterclockwise, giving (S). This matches apremilast.
- **Linker:** NH–C(=O)–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH.
- **Right fragment:** a 4-amino-phthalimide whose imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring. The glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
