The drawing shows a benzyl ether (a phenyl ring, a CH2, then O) attached to a para-substituted benzene ring. The para position carries a CH2CH2 chain leading to a nitrogen. That nitrogen bears a Boc group and an n-butyl group.

Checking the butyl chain: from N the bonds go down to the left, then up, then down, then up to the right. That is four carbons, so it is n-butyl.

Boc is C(=O)OC(C)(C)C, so the molecule is the tert-butyl carbamate of N-butyl-2-(4-benzyloxyphenyl)ethylamine.

SMILES: CCCCN(CCc1ccc(OCc2ccccc2)cc1)C(=O)OC(C)(C)C
