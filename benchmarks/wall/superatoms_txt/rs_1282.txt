The structure is a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to a rhodanine-type ring (eltrombopag-like).

**Biphenyl part.** The top-left ring carries CO2H at the meta position relative to the biaryl bond. The second ring is attached to the first at one carbon. Its neighbouring carbon bears OH, and the next carbon bears the NH–N= group.

**Hydrazone and thiazolidine ring.** The NH is bonded to N, which is double-bonded to C5 of the thiazolidine ring. The ring atoms are:
- C5, bonded to S
- S, bonded to C2
- C2 (=S), bonded to N3
- N3, bonded to C4
- C4 (=O), bonded back to C5

N3 carries a 3,4-dimethylphenyl group. The aryl ring is attached at C1. Its methyl groups sit at the position meta to the attachment (lower right) and the position para to it (lower left), which gives 3,4-dimethyl.
