I read the molecule as a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core with three substituents.

- **Pyrazinone N1:** carries a quaternary carbon bearing methyl (wedge bond), ethyl and cyclopropyl.
- **Pyrazinone C2:** is the C=O.
- **Pyrazinone C3:** is bonded to the nitrogen of a 1,2,3,4-tetrahydroquinoline.
- **Pyrazinone C5:** carries Cl, and C6 is CH.
- **Tetrahydroquinoline benzo ring:** Br is at C8, next to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to N, and OMe is at C6.

**Stereocentre:** The only stereocentre is the quaternary carbon, and the methyl is on a wedge, toward the viewer. Looking from the methyl toward the centre, the other three substituents run ethyl (up-left), cyclopropyl (up-right), N (down), which is clockwise. Writing the neighbours in the order methyl, ethyl, cyclopropyl, N therefore gives `@@`.

SMILES: C[C@@](CC)(C1CC1)n1cc(Cl)nc(N2CCCc3cc(OC)cc(Br)c32)c1=O
