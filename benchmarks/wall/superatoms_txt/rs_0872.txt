I read the molecule as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypicolinamide joined through its amide nitrogen to a nine-membered dilactone ring.

**Pyridine part:** The ring has N at the bottom left. The carbon next to N carries the C(=O)NH amide. The next carbon carries OH, and the one after that carries OMe.

**Dilactone ring (9 atoms):** Going around the ring:
1. C(=O)
2. O
3. CH(CH3)
4. CH2
5. CH(CH3)
6. C(=O)
7. O
8. CH(CH3)
9. CH(NH–), which closes back to atom 1

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned each of the four stereocentres from its wedge or hash bond.
- The NH-bearing carbon has a hashed N.
- The adjacent CH(CH3) has a hashed methyl.
- The two other CH(CH3) carbons each have a wedged methyl.
