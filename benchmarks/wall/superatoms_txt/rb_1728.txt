I read the structure in four parts: the left-hand heterocycle, the piperazine amide linker, the PEG chain, and the thalidomide-type end group.

**Left-hand heterocycle.** The core is a 1H-pyrazolo[4,3-d]pyrimidine. The pyrimidine ring contains N, C(aryl), N, C3a, C7a and CH.
- The aryl-bearing carbon (C5) is bonded to the aryl group and to two ring nitrogens.
- One of those nitrogens (N4) connects to C3a, the fusion carbon next to the pyrazole carbon C3.
- The other nitrogen (N6) connects to the CH (C7), which connects to C7a.
- C7a carries the NH of the pyrazole (N1H), then N2, then C3 (bearing the piperazine), then C3a.

Written as SMILES, the core is c1(Ar)nc2c(cn1)[nH]nc2N(piperazine), which expands to c1(-Ar)ncc2[nH]nc(N3CCN(CC3)...)c2n1.

**Aryl group.** This is a 2-fluoro-6-methoxyphenyl: c1c(F)cccc1OC.

**Linker.** The piperazine nitrogen is acylated: C(=O)CCOCCOCCOCCOCCN. The chain is C(=O), CH2, CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, then NH. That gives four oxygens, matching the drawing.

**Right-hand end group.** The NH is attached to C4 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione, whose N is bonded to the 3-position of a piperidine-2,6-dione: c1cccc2c1C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C2=O.

**Full SMILES:**
COc1cccc(F)c1-c1nc2c(cn1)[nH]nc2N1CCN(CC1)C(=O)CCOCCOCCOCCOCCNc1cccc2c1C(=O)N(C1CCC(=O)NC1=O)C2=O
