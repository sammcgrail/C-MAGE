I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule built from four parts:

- **KRAS G12C-type warhead:** a quinazoline core carrying a 4-(4-acryloylpiperazin-1-yl) group, 6-chloro, 8-fluoro, and a 7-(2-fluoro-6-hydroxyphenyl) group.
- **Linker:** a 2-amino substituent, NH–CH2CH2–C(=O)–N(CH3), followed by CH2CH2 and then three O–CH2CH2 units and a final O, which gives four ether oxygens in total.
- **Cereblon ligand:** the final ether oxygen sits on position 5 of a phthalimide, whose nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring (thalidomide-type).
- **Stereochemistry:** the wedge and bridge marks on the hydroxyphenyl ring look like drawing artefacts of an ordinary aromatic ring, so I gave no stereo. The glutarimide CH is drawn with plain bonds, so it also gets none.
