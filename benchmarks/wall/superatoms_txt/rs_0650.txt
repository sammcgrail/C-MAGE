I read the drawing as methyl N-Boc-4-[(6,7-dimethoxy-2-methylquinazolin-4-yl)oxy]pyrrolidine-2-carboxylate. I worked through it region by region.

- **Quinazoline:** a benzene ring fused to a pyrimidine. The benzo ring carries two OMe groups at C6 and C7. The pyrimidine ring has N1 at the top, a methyl on C2, N3, and C4 bearing the ether oxygen.
- **Linker:** C4 of the quinazoline is bonded through an oxygen to C4 of the pyrrolidine.
- **Pyrrolidine:** the ring nitrogen carries a Boc group (C(=O)OC(C)(C)C). C2 carries the methyl ester CO2Me on a wedge bond. C4 carries the aryl ether oxygen on a hashed bond.
- **Stereochemistry:** working out CIP priorities from the wedge and hash bonds, C2 is S and C4 is R, so the ester and ether are trans, as in trans-4-hydroxy-L-proline. I then checked that the SMILES chirality tags reproduce 2S,4R.
