I read the structure as a 14-membered macrolactone fused through a tetrahydropyran. A 2-methyl-α,β-unsaturated ester sits on one side, and a PMB-protected thiazolidin-2-one is attached at the anomeric carbon.

**Macrocycle**
- The ester carbonyl is attached to a CH=C(Me) unit, and the ester oxygen is attached to C4 of the tetrahydropyran.
- Seven CH2 groups link the C(Me)= carbon to C6 of the pyran (the carbon next to the pyran oxygen).
- From the drawing, the ring chain and the C=O are on the same side of the double bond, so the Me is trans to the C=O.

**Tetrahydropyran**
- Ring atoms are O, C6, C5 (CH2), C4 (CH–O ester), C3 (CH2) and C2.
- C2 is the anomeric carbon and carries OMe (bold wedge) and the thiazolidinone substituent.

**Thiazolidinone**
- The ring is C4'–N(PMB)–C(=O)–S–CH2.
- C4' is attached to the anomeric carbon and bears H on a bold wedge.
- PMB is a CH2 group on a 4-methoxyphenyl ring.

**Stereochemistry**
From the wedges and hashes I assigned C6 as R, C4 as R, the anomeric C2 as R and C4' as R. I then converted each to the matching @/@@ tag in the SMILES atom order.
