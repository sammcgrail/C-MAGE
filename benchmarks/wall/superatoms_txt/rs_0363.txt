The structure has three parts: a left benzamide, a central anisole ring, and a 5-chloropyridin-2-yl amide.

**Left ring.** The benzene ring carries an amidine at one position, C(=NH) bonded to a pyrrolidine nitrogen. The carbonyl carbon is attached at the para position. Fluorine sits ortho to the carbonyl, which makes it meta to the amidine. Following the drawing, the ring carbon bearing the C=O is attached to the carbon bearing F, so F is ortho to the carbonyl.

**Central ring.** One carbon bears the NH, and the adjacent carbon bears the C(=O)NH-pyridyl group. The OMe is on the carbon meta to the carbonyl-bearing carbon.

Numbering the central ring: C1 carries the amide C(=O), C2 carries the NH, and C3 and C4 are CH. The drawing places OMe at the top, on the carbon adjacent to the CH that is adjacent to C1. So the ring runs C1 (C=O), then CH, then C(OMe), then CH, then CH, then C2 (NH). The OMe is therefore para to the NH.

**Pyridine.** The ring nitrogen is adjacent to the carbon bearing the NH. Cl is on the carbon para to that carbon, which is position 5 of the pyridine.

Check on the central ring: c2 (NH), then c, c, c(OC), c, then c2 carrying the C(=O). The sequence is C2(NH), C3, C4, C(OMe), CH, C1(C=O), which places OMe para to N and meta to the carbonyl-bearing carbon. This matches the drawing.

The molecular formula is C25H23ClFN5O3, which matches the label.
