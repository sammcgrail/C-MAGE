I read the structure as a macrocyclic benzonitrile–benzene–pyridine core joined through a CH2 linker to a substituted benzimidazole carboxylic acid.

**Macrocycle**
- The benzonitrile ring carries a CH2CH2–O–CH2 bridge to the central benzene ring.
- The same ring also carries a CH2–O bridge to the pyridine 6-position.
- The central benzene ring has three substituents: the CH2–O bridge, the pyridine at the position meta to it, and the CH2 linker to the benzimidazole, which sits ortho to the bridge carbon.
- The pyridine has the ring N next to the aryl-bearing carbon, and the F at the 3-position.

**Benzimidazole**
- C2 bears the CH2 linker to the central benzene ring.
- N1 bears CH2 attached to oxetan-2-yl.
- C4 bears OCH2CH2OCH3.
- C6 bears CO2H.

**Stereochemistry**
- The oxetane C2 is the only stereocentre.
- I took the bond to the oxetane as a stereo bond and assigned it S, which is the configuration in the closely related compound danuglipron. I could not confirm it from the drawing alone.
- The CIP priorities at that carbon are O > CH2N > ring CH2 > H, and `C[C@@H]1CCO1` written with the CH2N carbon first gives S.
