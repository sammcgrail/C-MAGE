The drawing is bambuterol. It shows a benzene ring carrying three substituents in a 1,3,5 pattern.

- One ring carbon bears CH(OH)CH2NH-tBu.
- The other two ring carbons each bear an O-C(=O)N(CH3)2 carbamate group.

The CH(OH) carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I give it no stereo marker.
