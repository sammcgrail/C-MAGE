The drawing shows HO2C attached to a zigzag chain, with an nDec group (n-decyl, 10 carbons) at the far end.

Counting the chain vertices from the carboxyl carbon:
- The carboxyl carbon (HO2C) is the first atom.
- The zigzag vertices follow at about x = 415 (bottom), 585 (top), 757 (bottom), 928 (top) and 1100 (bottom). That is 5 CH2 carbons.
- The last bond runs from the vertex at 1100 up to nDec.

The total chain is therefore COOH, then 5 CH2, then a decyl group (10 carbons).

Total carbons = 1 + 5 + 10 = 16, so the molecule is a hexadecanoic-type acid with a straight chain, which is palmitic acid.
