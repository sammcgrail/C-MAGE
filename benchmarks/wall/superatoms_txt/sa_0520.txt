- **Stereocentre 2:** this carbon carries the OMOM group (hashed bond, pointing back), the phenyl, the first stereocentre and an implicit H. Reading the geometry, with H toward the viewer, gives `[C@@H]` for the neighbour order C, H, O, Ph.
