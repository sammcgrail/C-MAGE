I read the structure in three parts and then joined them.

**Macrocycle**
- The left benzene carries a CN group. Its two ortho-related ring carbons bear a CH2–O– link and a propyl chain, –CH2CH2CH2–O–.
- The propyl chain ends on the O of the middle phenyl ring. That O-bearing carbon is adjacent to the carbon carrying the CH2C(=O) side chain.
- The pyridyl group is meta to that O on the middle ring. The pyridine nitrogen sits between the carbon bonded to the phenyl and the carbon bonded to the O–CH2 link, which closes the macrocycle.

**Central benzene (amide side)**
- The amide N–H sits on the ring carbon between the NH–CH2-oxetane carbon and the O–CH2CH2–N(Boc)(CH2CF3) carbon.
- A methyl ester is on the carbon meta to the amide N.

**Stereochemistry**
- The only stereo marker is the hashed bond from the CH2 to the oxetane C2.
- I took the narrow end of the hash to be at the oxetane carbon, so the CH2N group points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- The CIP priorities are O > CH2N > ring CH2 > H, and the drawing gives S.
- In the SMILES, `NC[C@@H]2CCO2` encodes S, so I used `@@`.
