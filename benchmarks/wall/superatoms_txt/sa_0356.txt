This is fluazinam. I read it as two rings joined by an NH bridge.

**Pyridine ring.** The ring nitrogen is bonded to C2, which carries the NH bridge. C3 carries Cl. C4 is an unsubstituted CH. C5 carries CF3. C6 is a CH bonded to the ring nitrogen.

**Benzene ring.** C1 carries the NH bridge. Going around the ring from C1, the next carbon carries NO2. After that comes the carbon bearing Cl, then the carbon bearing CF3, then a CH, and finally the carbon bearing the second NO2, which closes back onto C1.

**SMILES.** Written from the pyridine side, with the benzene ring's substituents in the order described above:

Clc1cc(C(F)(F)F)cnc1Nc1c([N+](=O)[O-])c(Cl)c(C(F)(F)F)cc1[N+](=O)[O-]
