I read the drawing as a 2-tert-butyl-3-methylimidazolidin-4-one trifluoroacetate salt.

- **Ring:** a five-membered imidazolidinone. Going round it: N(CH3), then C(=O), then CH2, then NH, then CH(t-Bu), which closes back to the N-methyl nitrogen.
- **Stereocentre:** the tert-butyl group on the CH carbon is on a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. From the H side, the substituents N(Me), t-Bu and NH run counterclockwise. Reordering to the SMILES neighbour order (NH, H, N(Me), t-Bu) is an odd permutation, so the centre is written `@@`.
- **Counter-ion:** "•TFA" is trifluoroacetic acid, written as a separate component.

SMILES: CN1C(=O)CN[C@@H]1C(C)(C)C.OC(=O)C(F)(F)F
