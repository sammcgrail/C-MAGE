This reads as a latrunculin-type macrolide: a 16-membered lactone fused through a tetrahydropyran hemiketal to a 2-oxothiazolidine ring. I traced it region by region and assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash by hand; I did not run the SMILES through a parser.

**Macrocycle chain**
- Starting at the ester carbonyl, the ring goes C(=O)–CH=C(Et)–CH2–CH2–CH=CH–CH(Me)–CH2–CH2 and then into the pyran carbon.
- The C(=O)–CH=C(Et) double bond is drawn with the carbonyl carbon and the CH2 on the same side, so it is Z (cis).
- The CH=CH between the two CH2/CH(Me) neighbours is also drawn cis.

**Pyran ring**
- The ring is O–CH(chain, hashed bond)–CH2–CH(O-ester, wedge)–CH2–C(OH, wedge)(thiazolidine).
- The anomeric carbon carries the OH and the thiazolidinone.

**Thiazolidinone**
- The ring is C4(H, wedge)–CH2–S–C(=O)–NH, attached to the anomeric carbon through C4.

**Stereocentres**
- All five stereocentres are drawn with wedges or hashes, so each gets an @/@@ label.
