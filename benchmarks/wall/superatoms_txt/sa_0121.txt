I read the molecule as an ethyl ester, N-linked alanine-type amide of a 2-carboxy-octahydrocyclopenta[b]pyrrole. This is a perindopril-like skeleton, but the bicycle is 5-5 rather than 6-5.

**Skeleton**
- The left bicycle has a pyrrolidine ring (N, C2 bearing CO2H, CH2, C3a, C6a) fused to a cyclopentane ring. The cyclopentane ring has three CH2 groups and the two fusion carbons.
- N is acylated by C(=O)–CH(CH3)–NH–CH(CO2Et)–CH2CH2–Ph.
- Ph and Et are expanded to phenyl and ethyl.

**Stereocentres**
- All five stereocentres are drawn with wedges or hashes, so all five carry stereo marks.
- For each one I took the three drawn neighbours, worked out their rotation direction from the 2D coordinates, and combined that with whether the wedge or hash puts the implicit H in front or behind.
- That gave the SMILES chirality tags below.
