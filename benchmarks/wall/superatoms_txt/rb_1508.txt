I read the structure as a Nutlin-type MDM2 ligand joined through a piperazine and a propargyl linker to a thalidomide-like isoindolinone–glutarimide.

**Fragments, region by region**
- **Aryl on C2:** the left-hand ring is a phenyl bearing an ethoxy group ortho to the imidazoline and a tert-butyl group para to it.
- **Imidazoline core:** a 4,5-dihydroimidazole with a C=N bond. C2 carries the aryl above. C4 and C5 each carry a 4-chlorophenyl group.
- **Linker to piperazine:** N1 of the imidazoline is acylated by a urea-type C(=O) to one piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen carries CH2–C≡C.
- **Right-hand end:** the alkyne attaches at position 4 of an isoindolin-1-one. The lactam nitrogen bears a piperidine-2,6-dione (glutarimide) at its 3-position. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

**Stereochemistry**
Both chlorophenyl groups are drawn on wedge bonds, so the two imidazoline carbons are cis. I worked out the chirality from the 2D coordinates, treating each implicit H as pointing away from the viewer. Both centres come out as `@@` when the SMILES is written in the order N3, C4, C5, N1.
