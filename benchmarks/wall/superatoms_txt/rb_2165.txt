This SMILES is my best reading of the drawing. Two parts are uncertain: the length of the long alkyl chain at the bottom left, and several stereocentres.

**Peptide, N-terminus to C-terminus (read from the drawing):**
- The N-terminal residue is acetyl-Lys, with its side-chain amine acylated by the linker described below.
- Next come the first penicillamine (Pen, the C(CH3)2–S residue), Asn, Thr and 7-methyl-Trp.
- Then Lys(Ac), a second Pen, and Tyr with its phenol oxygen alkylated as O–CH2CH2–NH2.
- Then 2-naphthylalanine, a 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid residue, Lys(Ac), Asn, and 3-pyridylalanine.
- The C-terminus is N-methylglycinamide.
- The two Pen residues are joined by an S–S bond, which closes the macrocycle.

**Linker on the N-terminal Lys side chain:**
- It runs through two repeats of –C(=O)CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–.
- Then comes –C(=O)CH2CH2–CH(–CH2–C(=O)–alkyl)–C(=O)–NH–, a glutamate-type unit.
- That unit's amide nitrogen is attached to CH(CH2COOH)CH2N⁺(CH3)3.

**Uncertainties:**
- I could not count the alkyl chain after the ketone reliably, so I used 12 carbons including the ketone carbon's neighbours, i.e. C(=O) followed by a straight 12-carbon chain.
- I assigned L configuration to most residues as a default. The wedges and hashes at the Pen alpha carbons and at the glutamate-type and carnitine-like centres were not clear enough to settle.
