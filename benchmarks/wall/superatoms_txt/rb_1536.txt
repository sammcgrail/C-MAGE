I read the structure in fragments and joined them. The stereocentres were assigned from the wedge bonds by working out R/S from the drawn geometry. I did not run the SMILES through a parser, so validity is checked by hand only.

**Fragments**

- **Linker:** the alkyne is attached to the isoindolinone aromatic carbon next to the CH2-bearing ring-fusion carbon. After the alkyne come five CH2 groups and then the C=O, which acylates the diazepine nitrogen.
- **Bicyclic core:** a pyrrolo[1,2-a][1,4]diazepinone. It has three wedged stereocentres: the ring-fusion CH (R), the pyrrolidine CH bearing the carboxamide (S), and the CH bearing the NH (S).
- **Side chain on the carboxamide:** the amide nitrogen is attached to a wedged CH (S). That carbon carries a CH2CH2C(=O)NH2 arm and a 4-benzylthiazol-2-yl group.
