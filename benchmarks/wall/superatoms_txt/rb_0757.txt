I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type conjugate: a spiro-oxindole pyrrolidine (MDM2-inhibitor type) joined through a benzamide and a pentyl linker to a thalidomide unit.

**Fragments, from the top of the drawing**
- **Linker:** the amide NH leads into a chain of five CH2 groups, which I counted vertex by vertex.
- **Thalidomide unit:** the pentyl chain is attached at C4 of the isoindoline-1,3-dione. The imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.
- **Benzamide:** a para-disubstituted benzene. One side carries the C(=O)NH to the pentyl linker. The other carries NH–C(=O) to the pyrrolidine.
- **Pyrrolidine core:** the ring runs NH, C2 (carboxamide), C3 (aryl), C4 (spiro to the oxindole), C5 (spiro to the cyclohexane).
- **Aryl group on C3:** a 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl. F is on the ring carbon ortho to the attachment point and Cl is on the next carbon.
- **Oxindole:** a 6-chloro-2-oxindole, spiro-fused at its C3.

**Stereochemistry (from the wedges and hashes)**
- C2 carries a hashed bond to the carboxamide.
- C3 carries a wedged bond to the aryl group.
- The oxindole spiro carbon carries a hashed bond to the aromatic carbon and a wedged bond to the C=O.
- I converted each centre into SMILES chirality from the drawn geometry, with the implicit H treated as pointing opposite the wedge or hash.
- The cyclohexane spiro carbon is symmetric, so it has no stereo label.
