I read the molecule as follows. The only stereo in the drawing is the wedge/hash pair on the cyclobutane and the hash on the 3-position of the left piperidine; the glutarimide carbon is drawn plain, so it carries no stereo.

- **Left part:** a pyrazine-2-carboxamide with an anilino NH at C3. At C5 it carries a piperidin-1-yl ring, and that ring has a 3-(3-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl) substituent on a hashed bond.
- **Middle:** a para-substituted phenyl ring bearing a 4-methylpiperidin-4-yl group. Its nitrogen is bonded to a 1,3-disubstituted cyclobutane.
- **Cyclobutane:** one substituent is wedged (the piperidine N) and the other is hashed (the NH), so they are trans.
- **Right part:** the cyclobutane NH is attached at C4 of a phthalimide-type isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its ring nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) group.
