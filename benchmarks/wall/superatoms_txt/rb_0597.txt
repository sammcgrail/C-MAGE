This is a PROTAC-type molecule: an imidazo[1,2-a]pyrazine kinase-inhibitor warhead, a PEG linker and a thalidomide-type (pomalidomide) ligand.

**Warhead (imidazo[1,2-a]pyrazine).**
- The six-membered ring contains N (top left, bearing the cyclopropyl-substituted carbon), a CH (top right) and the bridgehead N.
- Reading around the pyrazine ring: C(cPr)=CH–N(bridgehead)–C(fused, C8a)=…–C8(NH-aryl)=N.
- The imidazole ring runs N(bridgehead)–C(pyrazolyl)=CH–N=C8a.
- Written as SMILES: c1(C2CC2)cn2c(-c3cn[nH]c3)cnc2c(N...)n1

**Aryl ring.** The NH is on a benzene carbon that has F ortho to it. Para to the NH is the C(=O) attached to piperazine. Written from the NH carbon: c1(F)cc(C(=O)N...)ccc1N.

**Linker.**
- Piperazine: N1CCN(CC1)CC(=O)NCCOCCOCCOCCN-
- The terminal NH is attached to the aromatic ring of the isoindoline-1,3-dione at position 4.
- Glutarimide: N-linked C3 of piperidine-2,6-dione, no stereo drawn.
