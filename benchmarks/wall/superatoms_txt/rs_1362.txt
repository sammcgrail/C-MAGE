The drawing is a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl isatin.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H and is attached at the meta position to the second ring. In the second ring, the carbon bonded to the first ring is next to the carbon bearing OH. The OH carbon is next to the carbon bearing the HN group. So the middle ring is 1-aryl, 2-hydroxy, 3-hydrazinyl.

**Hydrazone and isatin part.** The NH–N= unit connects to C3 of an indolin-2-one. The C=N double bond is drawn with the N on the upper left and the HN to its left, and no cis/trans geometry is clearly marked, so I give no stereo label. The ring nitrogen N1 carries a 2-fluoro-4-methylphenyl group. Going around that ring from the attachment point: C1 is attached to N, C2 bears F, C3 is CH, C4 bears methyl, and C5 and C6 are CH.

- Check on the fluorotolyl ring: N–c4ccc(C)cc4F gives c4 (attached to N), c, c, c(C), c, c4F. The F carbon is adjacent to the attachment carbon, and the methyl carbon is para to the attachment carbon, which matches.
