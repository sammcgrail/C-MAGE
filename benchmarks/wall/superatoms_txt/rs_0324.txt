I read the drawing in four parts and wrote them as one SMILES.

- **Indane acid:** an indane (benzene fused to a five-membered ring) carries a carboxylic acid on the ring carbon C2 of the five-membered ring. The aromatic carbon next to the ring fusion carries the sulfonyl group.
- **Sulfonamide:** the sulfonyl (S with two =O) joins the aromatic ring to N1 of a piperazine.
- **Piperazine:** the two carbons flanking N1 each carry a methyl, and both methyls are drawn as wedges, so the dimethyl pair is cis. N4 carries a CH2CH2 chain.
- **Aryl group:** the chain ends on a benzene ring with F at the 3-position and CF3 at the 4-position.

For the stereocentres I took H as pointing away from the viewer at each methyl-bearing carbon. The upper one (next to N1, with the methyl pointing up) gives `[C@@H]` in the written order. The lower one (with the methyl pointing down-left) gives `[C@H]`. The indane C2 is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
