I read the molecule as a symmetric dimer: two identical bicyclic Smac-mimetic halves joined through a hexa-2,4-diyne-1,6-diyl bis-ether linker.

**Each half**
- An N-methyl-2-aminobutanoyl (N-Me-Abu) group is attached through its carbonyl to the NH on the seven-membered ring. Its ethyl group is drawn hashed, which gives the S configuration at that carbon.
- That NH sits on the carbon next to the ring C=O of a fused bicycle: a 1,4-thiazepane (S–CH2–CH2–CH(NH)–C(=O)–N) fused to a pyrrolidine. The pyrrolidine carries a gem-dimethyl group next to the carbon bearing the carboxamide. The ring-fusion carbon (bonded to S and N) has a hashed H.
- The carboxamide NH is bonded to C1 of a trans-2-oxy-indan-1-yl unit, drawn with hashed bonds to both N and O. The O is bonded to the diyne linker.

**Stereocentres** (assigned from the wedges and hashes)
- Abu α-carbon: S
- Carbon bearing the NH on the thiazepane ring: S
- Ring-fusion carbon (S and N attached): S
- Pyrrolidine carbon bearing the carboxamide: S
- Indane C1 (bearing N): S
- Indane C2 (bearing O): R

The right half is drawn as the same unit rotated 180° in the plane, so it has the same configurations. I checked the SMILES for valid ring closures and for the same atoms and stereochemistry in both halves.
