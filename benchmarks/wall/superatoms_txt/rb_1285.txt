I read the structure in four pieces and joined them.

**Left side (quinazoline).** A 3-chloro-4-fluorophenyl ring is attached through NH to C4 of a quinazoline. The quinazoline carries a methoxy group at C7 and an NH-amide at C6.

**Linker.** The amide is a trans (E) but-2-enamide, C(=O)/C=C/C, with the terminal CH2 bonded to the nitrogen of a piperidine. Piperidine C4 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen bears a CH2 linked to a para-substituted benzene ring. The para position of that ring carries CH2–S.

**Right side (isoindolinone).** The sulfur attaches to C4 of an isoindolinone. The isoindolinone ring has its CH2 at C1 and its C=O at C3, and the lactam N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The sulfur-bearing aromatic carbon is adjacent to the carbon that carries the CH2. In the drawing, the ring-fusion carbon bonded to CH2 sits next to the carbon bonded to S, which fits.
