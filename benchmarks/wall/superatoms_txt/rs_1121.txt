The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- N1 is the ring nitrogen.
- C2 carries the carboxamide, C(=O)NH.
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are unsubstituted CH.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring. The far end of that ring carries an O–aryl group, where the aryl is a benzene ring with CF3 at the meta position.

Checking the meta position on the right-hand ring: the ring atom bonded to O is at the bottom left of the ring, and the CF3-bearing carbon is at the top. Going around the ring, the atoms are the O-bearing carbon, then the left carbon, then the CF3 carbon, so the two substituents are 1,3 (meta).
