Reading the structure region by region:

**Glutarimide and phthalimide.** The glutarimide is a piperidine-2,6-dione attached through C3 to the imide N of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its NH sits between the two C=O groups. The phthalimide benzene carries an NH at position 4, next to the C=O carbon.

**Linker.** From the aryl NH the chain runs: CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)–CH2. That is three oxygens and four ethylene units, ending at the amide, whose CH2 is attached to the quinazolinone N3.

**Quinazolinone.** N3 is bonded to the CH2 of the acetamide. C2 carries the azetidin-3-yl group and is double-bonded to N1. C4 is C=O. The fused benzene ring bears the naphthyl group at C6, para to N1 and meta to C4a. Reading the drawing, the ring-fusion carbon C4a is bonded to C4, and C5 is the CH adjacent to C6.

**Azetidine.** The ring N carries an acryloyl group, C(=O)C=C. C3 of the ring is attached to quinazolinone C2.

**Naphthyl.** The attachment is at naphthalene C1, and the OH is on C3 (the CH between C2 and C4).
