The drawing shows a central CH carbon bearing two Ph groups, bonded to an O. The O connects to a CH2CH2 chain that ends in NMe2. This is diphenhydramine.

Expanding the abbreviations (Ph to phenyl, NMe2 to dimethylamino) gives CN(C)CCOC(c1ccccc1)c1ccccc1. The central carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity only if its two substituents differ, and here both are phenyl, so it is not a stereocentre. The drawing also shows no wedges or hashes, so no stereo is needed.
