- The ring nitrogen is at the bottom left.
- C2 (attached to the C(=O)N group) is bonded to C3, which bears OH.
- C4 bears OMe.
- C5 and C6 are the unsubstituted CH carbons, and C6 closes back to N.
- SMILES for this part: c1(C(=O)N...)nccc(OC)c1O

**Linker and middle ring:**
- The amide is C(=O)NH–c1ccc(O...)cc1, a para-disubstituted benzene.

**Right pyridine ring:**
- The ring nitrogen is at the upper right, with CF3 on the adjacent carbon (C2).
- The oxygen is attached at C4, which is the carbon meta to C2.
- Going around the ring: O–c1ccnc(C(F)(F)F)c1, where c1 is C4, followed by C5, C6, N, C2 (bearing CF3), and C3.
