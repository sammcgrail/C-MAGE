The drawing shows an N‑trityl histidine methyl ester whose α‑amine carries a (4‑dimethylaminopyridin‑2‑yl)methyl group.

- **Imidazole:** the trityl‑bearing nitrogen is bonded to a CH, which is double‑bonded to the second ring nitrogen. That nitrogen connects to the carbon bearing the CH2 chain, which is double‑bonded to the other ring CH. This is the usual Nτ‑trityl histidine pattern.
- **Side chain:** the ring carbon is attached to CH2, then to the stereocentre, which carries CO2Me and an NH.
- **Pyridine:** NH–CH2 connects to the pyridine C2, next to the ring nitrogen, and NMe2 sits at C4.
- **Stereocentre:** the CO2Me group is on a wedge, so the implicit H points away from the viewer. The priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H. Going N (right) → CO2Me (upper left) → CH2 (lower left) is counterclockwise, so the centre is S, as in L‑histidine. In the SMILES below, the neighbour order is ester C, H, CH2, N, and `[C@H]` gives S for that order.
