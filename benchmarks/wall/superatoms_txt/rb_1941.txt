This SMILES is my reading of the drawing, written as a head-to-tail traced cyclic peptide. I traced the chain from the C-terminal glycinamide and closed the macrocycle through the thioether.

**Sequence as read:**
- Gly-NH2 sits at the C-terminus, and its NH is acylated by Cys.
- The Cys sulfur is linked through S–CH2–C(=O) to the N-terminal NH of Tyr, which closes the macrocycle.
- Going around the ring from Tyr: Tyr, N-Me-Ala, Asn, Pro, Dap, Leu, 4-hydroxyproline, Trp, Orn, Trp (N-CH2COOH on the indole nitrogen), N-Me-Nle, N-Me-Nle, Leu, back to Cys.
- The Dap side-chain NH and the Orn side-chain NH each carry a squaramide ring (cyclobutenedione) whose other nitrogen is attached to a 2,6-dichlorophenyl group.

**Stereochemistry:**
- I checked the wedge and hash bonds for Cys, Tyr, N-Me-Ala, Asn, Pro, Dap, Leu and Hyp. All come out as the natural L-configuration.
- Hyp is trans-4-hydroxy-L-proline (2S,4R), with the H at C2 and the OH at C4 both hashed.
- I did not check the remaining stereocentres individually (the other Leu, the two Trp residues, Orn and the two Nle residues). I assigned them L by analogy, so those tags are assumed.
