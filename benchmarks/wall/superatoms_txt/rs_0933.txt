- The carboxylic acid (COOH) is at the bottom of the central ring, at position 1.
- The OMe group is on the adjacent carbon, position 2.
- The phenoxy group (O–phenyl) is at position 4, which is para to the COOH.

Writing the central ring from the acid carbon: C1 bears COOH, C2 bears OMe, C3 is CH, C4 bears OPh, C5 is CH, and C6 is CH.
