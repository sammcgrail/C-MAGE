I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type conjugate: a VHL-ligand end joined through an aryl ether and butyl linker to a pyrimidine–bicyclic amine–pyridazine end.

**VHL end (left)**
- A 1-fluorocyclopropanecarbonyl group sits on the NH of a tert-leucine unit.
- The tert-leucine carbonyl is acylated onto a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine-2-carboxamide. I took the pyrrolidine and tert-leucine stereocentres to be the usual VHL pattern (2S,4R with S tert-leucine), consistent with the drawn wedges and hashes.
- The carboxamide NH is attached through a CH2 to a benzene ring that carries a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.

**Linker**
- The same benzene ring carries an ether O, followed by four CH2 groups to the nitrogen of a piperidine.
- The piperidine C4 is bonded to C5 of a pyrimidine.

**Right-hand end**
- Pyrimidine C2 is bonded to N8 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane.
- N3 of that bicycle is bonded to C4 of a pyridazine. The pyridazine has NH2 at C3 and a 2-hydroxyphenyl group at C6.

I wrote the bicycle as `N2C3CCC2CN(C3)`: N8 bridges the two bridgehead carbons, the C6–C7 two-carbon bridge links them, and N3 sits in the three-atom bridge.
