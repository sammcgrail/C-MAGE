I read the molecule as five connected pieces, left to right:

1. **Left end:** a pyrrolo[1,2-b]pyridazine carrying a nitrile (C#N) on the six-membered ring, and linked through the carbon next to the bridgehead nitrogen on the pyrrole ring.
2. **Pyridine:** it has the pyrrolopyridazine at one carbon, an isopropylamino group (NH–CH(CH3)2) at the next carbon but one, and the thiadiazole on the adjacent carbon.
3. **1,3,4-Thiadiazole:** its other carbon is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen.
4. **Linker:** the piperazine's second nitrogen is acylated by a 6-aminohexanoyl chain, a carbonyl plus five CH2 groups ending in NH. The five CH2 count is my best reading of the zigzag, not a certain one.
5. **Right end:** the NH is attached at the 4-position of a phthalimide, whose imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring (the thalidomide-type group). The stereocentre on that ring is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo marking.

Ring closures check out: the pyrrole ring has five atoms and the pyridazine ring has six.
