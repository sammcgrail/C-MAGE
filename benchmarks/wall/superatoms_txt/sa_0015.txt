I read the drawing as Boc-Lys(Cbz)-OH, i.e. lysine with both amines protected.

- **Chain:** from the Cbz-NH nitrogen there are four CH₂ carbons, then the stereocentre CH.
- **Substituents on the stereocentre:** an NH-Boc group, a CO₂H group, and the chain. The CO₂H is drawn on a hashed bond.
- **Abbreviations expanded:** Cbz is C(=O)OCH₂Ph and Boc is C(=O)OC(CH₃)₃.
- **Stereochemistry:** the priorities are N > CO₂H > CH₂ chain > H. The hashed CO₂H points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Reading N → CO₂H → chain on the page gives clockwise, which reverses to counterclockwise once the H is accounted for. That is S, the L-lysine configuration.
- **SMILES chirality tag:** written with the neighbour order CO₂H, H, chain, N, the S centre takes `@`, giving `[C@H]`.

SMILES: OC(=O)[C@H](CCCCNC(=O)OCc1ccccc1)NC(=O)OC(C)(C)C
