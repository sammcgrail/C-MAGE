I read the molecule as a methanesulfonate ester of a 4-hydroxycinnamyl group, attached through the cinnamyl CH2 to the nitrogen of an N-benzoyl-N-aryl amine. The aryl ring carries a 2-methoxypropanoic acid side chain.

- **Left end:** CH3-SO2-O- on a para-substituted benzene ring (the mesylate).
- **Linker:** an E (trans) CH=CH-CH2 connecting that ring to the nitrogen.
- **Nitrogen:** bears a benzoyl group (C(=O)Ph) and a para-substituted phenyl ring.
- **Right end:** the phenyl ring carries CH2-CH(OMe)-COOH.
- **Stereocentre:** only the carbon bearing OMe has a wedge, with the OMe on a bold wedge. The hydrogen is therefore at the back. The CIP priorities are OMe > COOH > CH2 > H. Viewed from the front, OMe (down) → COOH (upper right) → CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. Written as OC(=O)[C@@H](OC)C..., this neighbour order corresponds to S.
- **Alkene:** the drawing shows trans geometry, written as /C=C/.
