I read the drawing as an L-amino-acid (S) core with the indazole and pyrrolidinediol attached as follows.

- **Indazole:** a 1H-indazole attached through its 6-position (the benzene carbon next to the one bearing the ring NH) to a carbonyl.
- **Tetrahydroisoquinoline:** that carbonyl acylates the ring nitrogen. The ring is 5,7-dichloro, with the carboxamide carbonyl on C6.
- **Amino acid:** the amide NH sits on an α-carbon that carries CO2H and a CH2NH group. The CH2 is drawn on a wedge, which gives the S configuration (the same sense as L-alanine).
- **Cyanoguanidine:** the CH2NH is bonded to C(=N–C≡N), which is also bonded to the pyrrolidine nitrogen.
- **Pyrrolidine:** it carries OH groups at C3 (wedge) and C4 (hash). Working through the CIP priorities gives (3R,4R), a trans diol.
