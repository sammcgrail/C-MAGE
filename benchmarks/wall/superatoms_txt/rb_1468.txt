I read the structure in six pieces and joined them in this order, left to right.

2. **Pyridine:** it is substituted at C2 by the pyrrolopyridazine, at C4 by NHMe, and at C5 by the thiadiazole.
3. **Thiadiazole:** a 1,3,4-thiadiazole, linked through its two carbons to the pyridine and to the bicycle.
4. **Bicycle:** the two bridgeheads are joined by three bridges. The top and bottom bridges clearly have two carbons each. I could not tell whether the middle bridge has one carbon or two, so I took it as two, giving a bicyclo[2.2.2]octane. If it is a single carbon, the ring would be a bicyclo[2.2.1]heptane instead. One bridgehead carries the thiadiazole and the other carries the amide NH.
5. **Amide and piperidine:** the amide is C(=O) on C4 of a piperidine, and the piperidine nitrogen is attached to the aromatic ring of the next group.
6. **Right end:** a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione) whose imide nitrogen bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. No stereochemistry is drawn, so none is given.
