1. **Benzoxaborole (left).** It is a 1-hydroxy-1,3-dihydro-2,1-benzoxaborole. The ether oxygen sits on the benzene carbon next to the CH carbon beside the ring-fusion carbon that carries the CH2.
2. **Central benzene.** It carries CN, the aryl ether oxygen and the heptyl chain. The oxygen is para to the CN, and the chain is meta to the CN and ortho to the oxygen.
