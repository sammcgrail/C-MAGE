The structure is a PROTAC-like molecule. I read it in four parts.

**Indazole part.** The indazole carries a 3,5-difluorobenzyl group at C5 and an NH–C(=O) amide at C3. As SMILES: c1(NC(=O)...)n[nH]c2ccc(Cc3cc(F)cc(F)c3)cc12.

**Benzamide part.** The benzamide ring has the C(=O) at C1, an NH-(tetrahydropyran-4-yl) at C2, and a piperazine N at C4. As SMILES: C(=O)c1ccc(N2CCN(CC2)...)cc1NC1CCOCC1.

**Linker.** The other piperazine N bears a CH2 attached to C2 of a morpholine. The morpholine ring runs O1, C2 (bearing the CH2), C3, N4, C5, C6. N4 is bonded to the isoindoline aryl ring.

**Isoindoline-dione part.** The phthalimide N carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. As SMILES: c1ccc2c(c1)C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C2=O.

Assembled SMILES:

O=C(Nc1n[nH]c2ccc(Cc3cc(F)cc(F)c3)cc12)c1ccc(cc1NC1CCOCC1)N1CCN(CC1)CC1CN(CCO1)c1ccc2C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C(=O)c2c1

The morpholine fragment CC1CN(CCO1) is consistent with the drawing: C2 is attached to O and to C3, C3 to N, N to C5, C5 to C6, and C6 to O.

No stereo is drawn, so none is included.
