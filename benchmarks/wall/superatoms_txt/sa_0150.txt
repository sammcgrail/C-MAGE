The drawing shows Boc-protected methionine: a CH3–S–CH2–CH2 chain attached to the α-carbon, which carries an NH–Boc group and a CO2H group.

**Stereochemistry:** The CO2H is on a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H. On screen, N (upper right) → CO2H (down) → CH2 (upper left) runs clockwise. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This matches L-methionine.

**SMILES:** The Boc group expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C on the nitrogen. L-methionine is written CSCC[C@H](N)C(=O)O, so the Boc-protected form keeps the same chirality tag.
