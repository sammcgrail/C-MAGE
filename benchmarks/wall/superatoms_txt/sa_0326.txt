The drawing shows tBuO2C–CH2–CO2tBu, which is di-tert-butyl malonate.

Reading the labels: each end is a tert-butyl ester, written tBuO2C on the left and CO2tBu on the right. The two ester carbons are joined through a single central carbon, the vertex of the zigzag bond.

Expanding tBu to C(C)(C)C gives CC(C)(C)OC(=O)CC(=O)OC(C)(C)C. There are no stereocentres or charges to represent.
