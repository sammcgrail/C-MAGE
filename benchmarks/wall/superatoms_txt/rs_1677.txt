I read the molecule as an L-cysteine derivative (R configuration) in which the sulfur carries a (biphenyl-4-yl)methyl group and the nitrogen is acylated.

- **Left end:** a benzofuran with a benzyl group on C2 (the CH2–phenyl at the bottom) and the biphenyl attached at C3.
- **Middle:** the biphenyl's far ring carries a CH2 that connects to sulfur.
- **Cysteine unit:** S–CH2–CH(N)–COOH. The alpha carbon is the only stereocentre, drawn with a wedge to NH.
- **Acyl group:** the NH carries C(=O)–CH2–C6H4–NO2, with the nitro group para to the CH2.

For the stereocentre, the hydrogen is implicit and points away from the viewer because the wedge bond runs to N. The CIP priorities are N > CH2S > COOH, since S outranks O. In the drawing, N is at the bottom, CH2S at the upper left and COOH at the right. Going N → CH2S → COOH is clockwise, so the centre is R, matching L-cysteine. The SMILES chirality tag `[C@@H]` was written so that N is the preceding atom and the neighbours then run H, CH2S, COOH.
