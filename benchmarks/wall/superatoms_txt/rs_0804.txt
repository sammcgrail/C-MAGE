I read the molecule in four parts, then worked out the two stereocentres from the wedges.

**Fragments**
- **Indazole:** a 1H-indazole attached through its C6 to a carbonyl. The carbonyl carbon is on the ring carbon adjacent to C7, which sits next to the NH-bearing C7a.
- **Tetrahydroisoquinoline:** the carbonyl links to the ring nitrogen (N2). The benzo ring carries Cl at C5 and C7, and a carboxamide at C6.

**Stereochemistry**
- **Alpha carbon:** the wedge goes to the CH2 group, so H points away from the viewer. The priorities are N > CO2H > CH2N. Going N (lower left) → CO2H (lower right) → CH2 (top) runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. In the SMILES order N, H, CH2, CO2H this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.
- **Pyrrolidine C3:** the wedge goes to OH, so H points away. The priorities are OH > C2 (the CH2 next to N) > C4. Going OH (right) → C2 (lower left) → C4 (upper left) runs clockwise, so the centre is R. In the SMILES order C4, H, O, C2 this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.
