The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxamide built from three aryl pieces joined by two amide links.

**Left biphenyl.** The top-left benzene ring carries SO2NH2 on the carbon next to the ring-to-ring bond (ortho). That ring is bonded to a para-substituted benzene, and the para carbon bears a C(=O)NH group.

**Central ring.** The NH attaches to a benzene ring. The adjacent (ortho) carbon on that ring carries C(=O)NH–, so this is an anthranilamide.

**Pyridine.** The terminal NH is bonded to C2 of a pyridine. The ring N sits next to that carbon, and Br is on C5, which is para to C2 and meta to the ring N.
