I read the structure as a macrocycle linked to a benzimidazole carboxylic acid.

**Macrocycle**
- The left ring is a chlorobenzene. One ortho carbon carries CH2CH2–O–CH2, which runs to the central benzene. The adjacent ortho carbon carries CH2–O, which runs to the 2-position of a pyridine.
- The pyridine's 6-position is bonded directly to the central benzene.
- The central benzene has three substituents. The pyridyl and the ether CH2 sit meta to each other, and the CH2 that leads to the benzimidazole sits ortho to the ether CH2.

**Benzimidazole**
- C2 is attached to the CH2 coming from the central benzene.
- N1 carries a CH2 group attached to an oxetan-2-yl ring.
- C4 carries OCH2CH2N(CH3)Boc, and C6 carries the carboxylic acid.

**Stereochemistry**
- The only drawn stereocentre is the oxetane C2, where the CH2N bond is hashed (pointing away from the viewer).
- The CIP priorities are O > CH2N > ring CH2 > H. With the hashed bond to CH2N, the chirality works out to (S).
- In SMILES, `[C@@H]` is the form that gives S for the neighbour order written below.
