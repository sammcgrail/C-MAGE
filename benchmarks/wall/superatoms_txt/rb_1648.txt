I read the molecule as a KRAS-inhibitor-type PROTAC with three parts joined by a linker.

**Left-hand warhead**
- A naphthalene carries an OH, an ethynyl group and a fluorine.
- The naphthalene is attached to C7 of a pyrido[4,3-d]pyrimidine, and C8 of that core bears a fluorine.
- C4 of the core carries a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane, attached through the ring nitrogen of the three-atom bridge. The bridging NH is free.
- C2 of the core carries an O–CH2–CH(CH3)–N unit.

**Stereocentre**
- The methyl on the CH(CH3)N carbon is drawn on a bold wedge.
- Its neighbours are N, CH2O, CH3 and H (priorities in that order). With H pointing away, N → CH2O → CH3 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S.
- In the SMILES this is written `OC[C@H](C)N`.

**Linker**
- The nitrogen of that CH(CH3)N unit is the N of a piperidine.
- The piperidine's C4 is joined by a CH2 to C4 of a second piperidine.
- The second piperidine's nitrogen is joined by a CH2 to C4 of a third piperidine.
- The third piperidine's nitrogen is attached to the aromatic ring of the right-hand fragment.

**Right-hand fragment**
- An isoindolin-1-one carries a fluorine on the carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the CH2.
- The piperidine nitrogen sits on the aromatic carbon next to that fluorine.
- The isoindolinone nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a piperidine-2,6-dione (glutarimide). That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereochemistry is assigned.
