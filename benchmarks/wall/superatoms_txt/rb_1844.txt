1. **Left end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group sits on the nitrogen of a phthalimide. The phthalimide ring carries an NH at position 4.
2. **Linker:** from that NH the chain runs –(CH2)3–O–(CH2)3–O–(CH2)3–NH–C(=O)–O–, ending in a carbamate oxygen.
3. **Right end:** the carbamate oxygen is on C1 of a decalin diene (lovastatin-type, with C4=C4a and C5=C6 double bonds). It has methyls at C3 and C7. C8 carries a CH2CH2 chain to a 4-hydroxy-6-oxo-tetrahydropyran (the hydroxy-lactone), as in lovastatin.

For stereochemistry, I did not assign each wedge and hash independently. I assumed the drawing matches lovastatin and took the stereo descriptors from its isomeric SMILES, which I recalled from memory rather than checking against the image.
