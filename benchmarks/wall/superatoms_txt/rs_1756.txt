I read the molecule as a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core carrying three groups.

- **Pyrazinone ring:** N1 bears the side chain and is next to the C2 carbonyl. C3 carries the tetrahydroquinoline nitrogen. N4 is the ring nitrogen between C3 and C5. C5 bears Br, and C6 is CH.
- **Side chain on N1:** a stereocentre carrying N, an ethyl group, a CH2–O–CH3 group and an H drawn on a wedge. Ranking the substituents N > CH2O > ethyl > H and using the wedged H, I assign R. In the SMILES this is written `CC[C@H](COC)N…`.
- **Tetrahydroquinoline on C3:** its nitrogen is attached to C3 of the pyrazinone. The saturated ring has three CH2 groups. On the benzene ring, Br sits ortho to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to N, and OMe sits meta to that Br.

SMILES: CC[C@H](COC)n1cc(Br)nc(N2CCCc3cc(OC)cc(Br)c32)c1=O
