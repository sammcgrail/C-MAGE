I read the structure as an antimycin-type nine-membered dilactone, with a 3-acyloxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide on the ring nitrogen side chain.

**Ring and substituents**
- The nine-membered ring runs: C(=O), O, CH(CH3), CH(O-isobutyryl), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), O, CH2, CH(NH).
- The NH carries a pyridine-2-carbonyl group. That pyridine has OMe at C4 and, at C3, an ester O–C(=O) to a 5,6-dichloropyridin-3-yl group.
- The ring carbon next to the lactone oxygen carries a methyl group. The adjacent carbon carries an isobutyrate (OC(=O)CH(CH3)2). The next carbon carries a benzyl group.

**Stereochemistry (from the wedges and hashes)**
- CH–NH (hashed N): `[C@H]`
- CH–CH2Ph (wedged benzyl): `[C@H]`
- CH–O-isobutyryl (hashed O): `[C@@H]`
- CH–CH3 (wedged methyl): `[C@H]`
