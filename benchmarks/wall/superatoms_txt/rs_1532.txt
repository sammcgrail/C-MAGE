The drawing shows these parts:

- **N-benzylpiperidin-4-yl group:** a benzyl group (CH2–Ph) on the piperidine nitrogen, with the ring attached to the central amide nitrogen at C4.
- **Central amide:** the amide nitrogen carries the piperidinyl group, the indoline group and a C(=O) group. The carbonyl continues as a trans CH=CH–Ph (cinnamoyl).
- **Indoline:** the aromatic carbon bearing the amide N is position 6 of the indoline. The indoline nitrogen (N1) carries a trifluoroacetyl group, C(=O)CF3.

Indoline numbering: N1, C2 (CH2), C3 (CH2), C3a, C4, C5, C6, C7, C7a, with C7a bonded to N1. In the drawing, the benzene ring has the CH2CH2 fused at the upper right. The N-bearing carbon (C7a) is at the lower right of the ring, and the substituted carbon sits to its left, one position away from C7a. That is C6, with C7 between them, so the amide N is at position 6.

The trans double bond is written as /C=C/.
