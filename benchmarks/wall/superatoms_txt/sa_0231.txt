I read the drawing as a linear hexapeptide, Gly‑Arg‑Gly‑Cys(SO3H)‑Thr‑Pro, written from the N‑terminus (free NH2) to the C‑terminus (free CO2H).

- **Arg:** the side chain is CH2CH2CH2–N=C(NH2)2 (guanidine), with a hashed bond to the side chain.
- **Gly:** a plain NH–CH2–C(=O) unit.

- **Thr:** the side chain is CH(OH)CH3. The α‑carbon has a wedge to N, and the β‑carbon has a wedge to CH3.
- **Pro:** a five‑membered ring whose α‑carbon carries the CO2H on a hashed bond.
