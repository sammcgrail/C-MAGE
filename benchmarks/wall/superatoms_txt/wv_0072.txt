Reading the drawing from left to right:

- The leftmost vertex carries a short plain bond to a methyl group. Two wavy lines cross at this vertex, so it has two attachment points, written as two `*`. Together with the chain, this makes a quaternary carbon, `*C(*)(C)`.
- From that carbon the zigzag chain has 16 carbon vertices in total.
- The double bond lies between chain carbons 7 and 8.
- The neighbouring chain carbons (6 and 9) both sit below the double bond, on the same side, so the alkene is cis (Z).
- That leaves 6 saturated carbons before the double bond and 8 after it.

The quaternary carbon is not a stereocentre because it carries two identical `*` attachment points.
