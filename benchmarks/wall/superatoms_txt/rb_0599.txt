I read the molecule as an imidazo[1,2-a]pyrazine kinase-binding head joined through a piperazine amide and a long alkyl linker to a VHL-type ligand.

**Head group.** The imidazo[1,2-a]pyrazine core carries three substituents:
- a cyclopropyl at C6;
- a 1H-pyrazol-4-yl at C3;
- an NH at C8 that links to a 2-fluorophenyl ring.

**Linker.** The fluorophenyl ring carries a para carbonyl that forms an amide with a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen bears CH2C(=O)NH, followed by eight CH2 groups and then a C(=O).

**VHL ligand.**
- The linker carbonyl is bonded to the NH of an L-tert-leucine unit, which I wrote as N[C@@H](C(C)(C)C)C(=O). The wedge on the N-bearing bond fixes this configuration.
- The tert-leucine carbonyl is bonded to the nitrogen of a (2S,4R)-4-hydroxyproline. I took the 4R/2S configuration from the wedge and hash bonds, which match the standard VHL ligand.
- The proline carboxamide goes through a CH2 to a phenyl ring bearing a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.

I counted the chain carbons from the zigzag vertices: eight CH2 between the amide NH and the carbonyl.
