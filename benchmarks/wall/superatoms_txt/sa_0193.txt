The drawing is Fmoc-Lys(Alloc)-OH, a lysine with its α-amine carrying Fmoc and its side-chain ε-amine carrying Alloc.

- **Backbone:** the α-carbon bears NH–Fmoc, CO2H, and a chain of four CH2 groups that ends at NH–Alloc. Four CH2 groups is the lysine side chain.
- **Protecting groups:** Fmoc expands to the fluorenylmethyl carbamate, `C(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc21`. Alloc expands to the allyl carbamate, `C(=O)OCC=C`.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CO2H bond is a wedge, so it points toward the viewer and the implicit H points away. With priorities N > CO2H > CH2 chain, the sequence N (up), CO2H (lower left), chain (lower right) runs counterclockwise. That gives S, the natural L configuration.
- **SMILES chirality tag:** writing the stereocentre as chain-carbon, H, N, COOH, the L-lysine pattern `N[C@@H](CCCCN)C(=O)O` becomes `[C@H]` after one swap of neighbours.
