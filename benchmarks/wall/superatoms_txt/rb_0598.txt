I read this as a rifamycin-type ansamycin: a phenoxazine-fused naphthofuranone chromophore carrying a 2-(dimethylamino)ethoxy and a methoxy group, closed by the ansa chain. I assigned the stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes and the alkene geometry from the drawn positions, but did not check it against a database.

**Chromophore**
- The left benzene ring carries OMe and OCH2CH2NMe2.
- The phenoxazine ring has an N=C bond and an ring O.
- The quinone-type ring has a C=O and an NH–acyl carbon.
- The aromatic ring has OH and CH3 substituents and is fused to a furanone.
- The furanone's quaternary carbon carries a methyl and the ether oxygen that starts the ansa chain.

**Ansa chain (amide to ether)**

- Then eight sp3 centres in order: CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OAc), CH(CH3), CH(OMe).
- It ends with CH=CH–O attached to the quaternary carbon.

**Alkene geometry**
- The C(CH3)=CH bond has the carbonyl and the next chain carbon cis.
- The CH=CH bond next to the first stereocentre is trans.
- The enol-ether CH=CH is trans.
