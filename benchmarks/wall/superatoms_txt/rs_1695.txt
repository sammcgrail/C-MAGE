I read the molecule as the amide formed between 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypicolinic acid and O-benzyl-tyrosine methyl ester.

- **Pyridine ring:** the ring nitrogen sits at the bottom. The carbon next to it carries the C(=O)NH group. The next carbon carries OH, and the one after that carries OMe.
- **Amide link:** the NH is attached to the α-carbon by a hashed bond.
- **α-carbon:** its substituents are NH, COOMe and CH2 (leading to the aryl ring), with an implicit H.
- **Aryl side chain:** a para-substituted benzene ring carrying OCH2Ph.

**Stereochemistry:** the hashed bond puts N behind the plane, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > COOMe > CH2 > H. Going from N (left) to COOMe (up) to CH2 (down-right) is clockwise as drawn. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving S, the L-tyrosine configuration. In the SMILES below, `[C@H]` with the neighbour order COOMe, H, CH2, N encodes S.

SMILES: COC(=O)[C@H](Cc1ccc(OCc2ccccc2)cc1)NC(=O)c1nccc(OC)c1O
