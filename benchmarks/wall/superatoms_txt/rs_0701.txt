The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl isatin.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H. The second ring is attached to it at the position meta to the CO2H. On the second ring, the carbon bonded to the first ring is adjacent to the C–OH carbon. The next carbon after C–OH bears the NH. The acid ring is therefore 3-substituted, and the second ring is 2-hydroxy-3-(hydrazinyl).

**Hydrazone linker.** The chain is HN–N=C3, where C3 is the 3-position of the indolin-2-one.

**Indolinone part.** C3 is double-bonded to N, and C2 is a C=O. The ring nitrogen N1 carries a 4-cyclohexylphenyl group, and the other side of the ring is fused to a benzene.

**SMILES assembly.**
- Acid ring: OC(=O)c1cccc(c1)-c1cccc(NN=C2C(=O)N(c3ccc(cc3)C3CCCCC3)c3ccccc23)c1O
- The second ring, starting from the carbon bonded to the acid ring, is c1(...)c(O)c(NN=...)ccc1, which matches the arrangement above.
- The indolinone is written as C2(=NN...)C(=O)N(Ar)c3ccccc23.

The drawing shows no stereochemistry specified for the C=N bond, so I left it out.
