I read the structure as a rifamycin-derived phenoxazine: the ansa macrocycle is fused to the naphthofuranone core, and the phenoxazine carries an OCH2CH2NHAc group.

**Core and substituents**
- **Furanone ring:** the ketal carbon carries a hashed methyl and a bold-wedge O that leads into the O–CH=CH– chain. It also bonds to the ring oxygen and to the C1 carbonyl.
- **Aromatic ring:** the benzene ring carries a methyl and an OH.
- **Phenoxazine:** it is fused to the quinone ring, which has a C=O and an NH–amide link to the macrocycle. The pendant group on the far benzo ring is OCH2CH2NHC(=O)CH3.
