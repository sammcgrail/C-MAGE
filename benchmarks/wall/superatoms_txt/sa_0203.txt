The drawing shows two tBu (tert-butyl) groups, each bonded to one oxygen of an O–O unit. That is di-tert-butyl peroxide.

Expanding each tert-butyl group to C(C)(C)C gives CC(C)(C)OOC(C)(C)C. All bonds are single and there are no stereocentres, so no stereochemistry is needed.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OOC(C)(C)C
