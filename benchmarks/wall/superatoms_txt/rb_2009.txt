I read the molecule as a bifunctional (PROTAC-type) compound. It joins an imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine kinase-inhibitor fragment to a VHL-type hydroxyproline ligand through a piperazine glutaramide linker.

**Fragments, left to right:**
- A 3-fluorophenyl group sits on C2 of a pyrrolidine. Its bond is hashed, and I assigned R from the CIP priorities (N > aryl C > ring CH2 > H).
- The pyrrolidine nitrogen is bonded to C6 of the imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine.
- C3 of the imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine is bonded to C2 of a pyridine, and C6 of the pyridine carries a piperazine nitrogen.
- The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated by a five-carbon linker, C(=O)CCCC(=O), which ends in an amide NH.
- That NH is on the α-carbon of tert-leucine. The hashed bond to NH gives the S (L) configuration.
- The tert-leucine carbonyl is bonded to the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxyproline. The hashed bond at C2 and the wedged OH at C4 match the (2S,4R) pattern of the standard VHL ligand.
- The proline carboxamide NH is bonded to a CH2 on a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl group.

I wrote the stereocentres by working out the parity from the drawing for the pyrrolidine and tert-leucine centres. For the hydroxyproline I used the known VHL-ligand SMILES pattern, which agrees with the wedges as drawn.
