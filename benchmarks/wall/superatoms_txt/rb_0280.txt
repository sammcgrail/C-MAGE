I read the molecule as a spiro compound. A phthalide (isobenzofuranone) shares its spiro carbon with C9 of a 9,10-dihydroanthracene.

- **Anthracene part:** C10 carries two phenyl groups, and each outer benzo ring carries an acetoxy group (OAc) at the position para to the spiro-side ring junction.
- **Phthalide benzene ring:** going around the ring, it has the spiro-attached carbon, a CH, a C–OMe, a C–N (the crown nitrogen), a CH, and the carbon bearing the lactone C=O.
- **Macrocycle:** tracing from the right-hand nitrogen gives N, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, N(2-methoxyphenyl), CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, back to the first N. This is a 15-membered ring with 2 N and 3 O, so a 1,4,7-trioxa-10,13-diazacyclopentadecane. The right-hand N is bonded to the phthalide ring, and the left-hand N is bonded to the 2-methoxyphenyl group.
