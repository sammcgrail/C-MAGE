This is a PROTAC-like molecule. I read it in sections.

**Thienopyridinone core.** The thiophene ring is S1, C2 (bearing the carboxamide), C3 (CH), C3a, C7a. The fused pyridinone is C3a–C4(=O)–N5(CH3)–C6H=C7(aryl)–C7a. As SMILES: c1(C(=O)NR)sc2c(c1)C(=O)N(C)C=C2Ar.

**Amide substituent.** The amide N is attached to a piperidine carbon (C4). Going round the ring, C4 is next to C3, which carries the two F atoms, then C2 (CH2), then N1 (CH3), C6, C5. SMILES: NC1CCN(C)CC1(F)F.

**Aryl group.** The ring is a benzene attached to the thienopyridinone at C1. It has OMe at both ortho positions (C3 and C5 of the ring as numbered from the attachment point) and a CH2–azetidine at C4, between the two OMe groups. Written from the attachment point: c1(cc(OC)c(CN2CC(C2)...)c(OC)c1).

**Azetidine and spiro linker.** The azetidine N is attached to the benzyl CH2. Its C3 carries a CH2 that goes to the N of a piperidine. That piperidine is the 7-aza ring of a 2,7-diazaspiro[3.5]nonane: N7, CH2, CH2, spiro C, CH2, CH2. The spiro carbon is also part of an azetidine whose N (N2) is attached to the isoindolinone aryl ring.

**Isoindolinone.** The benzene ring carries the azetidine N at C5. The lactam is C(=O)–N–CH2 fused to the benzene. The lactam N is attached to the 3-position of a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl).

Check of the ring labels: c1cc2c(s1)c(cn(C)c2=O)… The thiophene is c1 (C2), c (C3), c2 (C3a, bonded to the C4=O carbon closed by "c2=O"), then c(s1) is C7a. The next atom c(…) is C7 with the aryl group, and inside the parentheses cn(C)c2=O gives C6, N5 and C4. This is consistent with the drawing.

The isoindolinone: c2ccc3c(c2)CN(...)C3=O. The attachment carbon is c2, then cc, then c3, which bears the C=O at the end (C3=O). Then c(c2) bears the CH2. In the drawing, the azetidine N is attached to a carbon that is meta to the carbon carrying the CH2 and para to the carbon carrying the C=O. In my SMILES, c2 (attached), c, c, c3 (C=O side): the attachment carbon is separated from c3 by two atoms, so it is para to it. This is right.
