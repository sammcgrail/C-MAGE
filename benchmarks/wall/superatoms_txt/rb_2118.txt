This SMILES was read from the drawing by hand; the stereocentres and two chain lengths are partly inferred, as noted below.

**Linear N-terminal part:** Ac-Thr, then 7-methyl-Trp, then Lys(Ac).

**Macrocycle:** the Lys(Ac) carbonyl acylates the N of the first bridging residue. Going around the ring from there:
- that residue's carbonyl joins the Tyr(OCH2CH2NH2) nitrogen;
- Tyr joins 2-naphthylalanine;
- 2-naphthylalanine joins 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid;
- that residue joins the second bridging residue.

The two bridging α-carbons are linked by –(CH2)3–CH=CH–(CH2)3–. I counted three CH2 on each side of the alkene by eye, so that count is a best reading. The alkene is written without cis/trans geometry.

**C-terminal part:** Asn, then 3-pyridylalanine, then N-methyl-Gly, then Lys-NH2. The Lys side chain carries two OEG linkers, –C(=O)CH2OCH2CH2OCH2CH2NH– repeated twice. These lead to γ-Glu, which is acylated by a hexadecanedioic (C16) diacid. I chose C16 because the zigzag length was ambiguous and C16 is a common choice. The chain could be one or two carbons longer.

**Stereochemistry:** every α-carbon is written as the L-amino acid, and the Thr β-carbon is the L-Thr configuration. This is an assumption; I did not read each wedge or hash from the image.
