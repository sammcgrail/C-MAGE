This is a rifamycin‑type ansamycin in which the naphthoquinone core is fused to a bromo‑substituted phenoxazine. I read it region by region and wrote the SMILES from that reading.

**Core.**
- The furanone ring carries a quaternary carbon with a methyl (hashed bond) and a wedged O‑vinyl ether link to the ansa chain.
- The aromatic ring bears CH3 and OH.
- The quinone ring has a C=O and an NH‑acyl group.
- The phenoxazine has one C=N and one ring O. The Br sits para to the ring‑O‑bearing carbon.

**Stereochemistry.** I assigned each centre from its wedge or hash. The H on each centre points opposite the wedged or hashed substituent. The three double bonds are the two E alkenes and the Z alkene (carbonyl cis to the chain CH) described above. This was done by hand from the 2D coordinates, so the individual @/@@ assignments are the least certain part.
