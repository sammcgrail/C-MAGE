I read the molecule as three parts joined in a line, and wrote the SMILES from left to right.

**Left end**
- A pyridine ring carries a methoxy group at C5 and a carboxamide at C2.
- At C4 there is a trans (E) vinyl group, which is attached to a cyclohexane ring.
- The cyclohexane carries a CF3 group at the 1,4-position. The vinyl bond is hashed and the CF3 bond is wedged, so the two substituents are trans.

**Middle**
- The carboxamide nitrogen is attached to a CH2 on a meta-disubstituted benzene ring.
- The other meta position carries CH2–C(=O)–N(CH3).
- The N-methyl nitrogen is followed by a PEG chain of six ethylene-oxide units: N–(CH2CH2O)×6.

**Right end**
- The last PEG oxygen is attached to a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione) ring.
- The phthalimide nitrogen is bonded to the C3 carbon of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

For the trans-1,4-cyclohexane stereo, I started from the pattern `C[C@H]1CC[C@@H](C)CC1` and swapped the neighbour order at the second centre to match my atom ordering. That gives `[C@H]2CC[C@H](CC2)`.
