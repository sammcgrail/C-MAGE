Reading the structure region by region:

**Oxindole (left).** C2 is a carbonyl (O=C) bonded to N1. C3 is a CH2. The benzene ring carries Cl at C5 (para to N1 when counting from the ring-fusion carbon C7a). N1 is bonded to the piperidine C4.

Checking the Cl position: the benzene ring has C3a at the top left, C4 at the bottom left, C5 at the bottom (bearing Cl), C6 at the bottom right, C7 at the right, and C7a at the top right (bonded to N). That makes it 5-chlorooxindole.

**Piperidine.** The ring N is bonded to a CH2, then CH(OH), then another CH2, which connects to N1 of the pyrazole.

**Pyrazole, fused to a tetrahydropyridine (right).** N1 (bonded to the chain) is bonded to N2, and N2 is double bonded to C3. C3 carries the 4-CF3-phenyl group. C3 is bonded to C3a, and C3a is bonded to C7a, which is bonded to N1. C3a is also bonded to CH2 (C4), which is bonded to N5 (bearing SO2Me). N5 is bonded to CH2 (C6), then CH2 (C7), then back to C7a.
