- **Pyrazine ring:** a six-membered ring with two nitrogens, bearing Cl, two NH2 groups and a carbonyl substituent. This is an amiloride-type core.
- **Linker:** the carbonyl connects through NH to a guanidine, C(=NH)NH. That NH is attached to a four-carbon chain, which ends on the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Piperidine:** a six-membered ring with the nitrogen at one end and an ether oxygen at the 4-position.
- **Side chain:** the ether oxygen is bonded to CH2, then to a stereocentre carrying NH2 and a methyl ester, CH(NH2)C(=O)OCH3.
- **Stereochemistry:** the NH2 is drawn with a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are NH2 > CO2Me > CH2O > H. Viewed from the front the sequence NH2 → CO2Me → CH2O runs clockwise, and because H faces the viewer this reverses to counterclockwise, so the centre is S.
