I read this as Fmoc-L-tryptophan with a Boc group on the indole nitrogen, Fmoc-Trp(Boc)-OH.

- **Backbone:** the stereocentre carries an NH–Fmoc group, a CO2H group on a wedge, and a CH2 that leads to the indole C3. The fourth substituent is an implicit H.
- **Indole:** the nitrogen is substituted with Boc, which expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C. The ring is fused to a benzene ring.
- **Fmoc:** this expands to the carbamate C(=O)OCH2–fluorenyl on the amine.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H. The wedged CO2H points toward the viewer, so the H points away. Going N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise, which gives S, as in L-tryptophan.

For the SMILES, I wrote the centre as OC(=O)[C@H](Cc…)N… and checked the chirality symbol against the standard L-Trp SMILES, N[C@@H](Cc1c[nH]c2ccccc12)C(=O)O. Reordering the neighbours to put CO2H first swaps N and CO2H, which flips @@ to @.
