I read the structure as a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl oxindole.

**Left ring (fluorobenzoic acid).** One carbon carries CO2H, the adjacent carbon carries F, and the carbon meta to the CO2H carbon is bonded to the second ring. Numbering from the CO2H carbon: C1 is the CO2H carbon, C2 is the F carbon, and the biaryl bond sits at the carbon two positions further around the ring from C2 on the far side. In the drawing, CO2H is at the top, the ring carbon bearing F is to its left, and the ring carbon at upper right (C6 on the CO2H side) leads down to the biaryl bond.

**Middle ring.**
- The biaryl carbon, call it Ca, bonds to the left ring.
- The next carbon, Cb, carries OH.
- The next carbon, Cc, carries the NH-N= group.
- Then comes a CH.
- Then a carbon bearing Cl.
- Then a CH, which closes the ring back to Ca.

**Oxindole.**
- The hydrazone N= is attached to C3 of the oxindole.
- C2 is the C=O, and N1 carries the 3,4-dimethylphenyl group.
- The benzo ring carries CF3. Going from C3a, the ring runs C4 (CH), C5 (CH), C6 (CF3), C7 (CH), then C7a, which is bonded to N.

**SMILES assembly.**
- Left ring: OC(=O)c1cc(ccc1F)-c2cc(Cl)cc(NN=C3C(=O)N(c4ccc(C)c(C)c4)c5cc(C(F)(F)F)ccc35)c2O
- Left ring check: c1 (CO2H carbon), then c (CH), then c(...) (the biaryl carbon), then cc, then c1F. The F carbon is adjacent to the CO2H carbon, which matches the drawing.
- Aryl on N: the 3,4-dimethylphenyl is attached at C1, with methyls at C3 and C4. In c4ccc(C)c(C)c4, c4 is C1, then c is C2, c is C3, c(C) is C4, c(C) is C5, and c4 is C6. That is equivalent to 3,4-dimethyl by symmetry.
- Oxindole benzo ring: c5cc(CF3)ccc35 starts at C7a, then C7 (CH), then C6 (CF3), then C5, C4, and C3a, which closes to C3. This matches the drawing.

The hydrazone C=N geometry is not clearly specified, so I give no stereo.
