The drawing shows a pyridazin-3-one ring carrying an imidazole, a chlorine and a 4-nitrophenyl group.

**Pyridazinone ring (six-membered):**
- N1 is the lower nitrogen, bonded to the nitrophenyl group.
- N2 is the "N" at the left, double-bonded to the CH carbon.
- C3 is the CH next to N2.
- C4 is the carbon bearing the imidazole, double-bonded to the carbon carrying Cl.
- C5 is the carbon bearing Cl, bonded to the C=O carbon.
- C6 is the C=O carbon, bonded to N1.

**Imidazol-1-yl substituent:** it is attached through N to C4 of the pyridazinone. The ring is N1(–pyridazinone), C2H=N3, C4H=C5H.

**Aryl group:** a para-nitrophenyl on N1.
