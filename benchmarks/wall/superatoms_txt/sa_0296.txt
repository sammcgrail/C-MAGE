The drawing shows naproxen sodium, the (S)-enantiomer. I read it in three parts:

- **Ring system:** a naphthalene with a methoxy group (MeO) on one ring and the side chain on the other, in the 6,2-relationship found in naproxen.
- **Side chain:** a CH(CH3)COO⁻ group with a Na⁺ counterion. The stereocentre carries a hashed bond to COO⁻, with the aryl carbon and the methyl drawn as plain bonds.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CIP priorities are COO⁻ > aryl > CH3 > H. The COO⁻ is hashed (pointing away), so the implicit H points toward the viewer. On the page, COO⁻ (down), aryl (left) and CH3 (upper right) run clockwise, and reversing for the H facing the viewer gives counterclockwise, which is S.

The SMILES below places the stereocentre in the S configuration, matching the usual SMILES for (S)-naproxen.

SMILES: C[C@@H](c1ccc2cc(OC)ccc2c1)C(=O)[O-].[Na+]
