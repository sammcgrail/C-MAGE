Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left ring:** a methyl ester (COOMe) on a benzene ring, with the sulfonyl group meta to the ester.
- **Sulfonamide:** the sulfonyl S bears two =O and is bonded to N, and the N carries a methyl.
- **Middle ring:** the N is attached to a benzene ring. A methyl sits ortho to N, and the ether O sits para to N.
- **Isoxazole:** the ether O connects through CH2 to C4 of the isoxazole. C5 carries the isopropyl and is bonded to the ring O. C3 carries the 2-(trifluoromethoxy)phenyl group, and the ring O is bonded to N.

Writing the isoxazole ring as c1(COAr)c(C(C)C)onc1-Ar:

COC(=O)c1cccc(c1)S(=O)(=O)N(C)c1ccc(OCc2c(C(C)C)onc2-c2ccccc2OC(F)(F)F)cc1C

Checking the middle ring: N-attached c1, then c, c, c(O), c, c1C. The N-bearing carbon is bonded to the ring-closing carbon that holds the methyl. From the N carbon the ring runs c(H), c(H), c(OR), c(H), then c(CH3). In the picture the methyl carbon is below, adjacent to the N carbon and meta to the O-bearing carbon, which matches.
