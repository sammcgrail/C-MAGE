The drawing shows a piperazine ring with a formyl group on one nitrogen and an amidine-type carbon on the other.

- The left nitrogen carries a methyl group (the unlabeled line to the left) and a CH3 (labeled below), so it is N(C)C.
- That nitrogen is bonded to a central carbon that has a double bond to NH (C(=N)) and a single bond to a ring nitrogen of the piperazine.
- The piperazine ring is a six-membered ring with two nitrogens in the 1,4 positions.
- The other ring nitrogen is bonded to CHO (N-CH=O).
