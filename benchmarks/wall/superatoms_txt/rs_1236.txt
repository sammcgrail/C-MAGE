I read the molecule as a morpholine amide of a 4-oxo-butanamide. It carries a 4-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl group at C2, and the amide nitrogen is attached to a valine-derived chain ending in a 5-fluoro-3,3-dimethylindoline.

**Connectivity**
- Morpholine: a six-membered ring (O, N and four CH2). Its N is acylated by C(=O)–CH2.
- Butanamide core: that CH2 leads to a CH bearing the 4-CF3-phenyl group, then to a C(=O)NH amide.
- Amide N-substituent: NH–CH(CH(CH3)2)–CH2–N(indoline).
- Indoline: N1, CH2, a quaternary C(CH3)2, then the fused benzene ring with F para to the ring nitrogen.

**Stereochemistry**
- The aryl-bearing CH has a hashed aryl bond, so its implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are C(=O)N > aryl > CH2 > H. The C(=O)N → aryl → CH2 sequence runs clockwise in the drawing, and with H toward the viewer the true sense is reversed. This gives S, which corresponds to `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order used below.
- The isopropyl-bearing CH has a bold wedge to the isopropyl group, so its H points away from the viewer. The priorities are N > CH2N > iPr > H. The N → CH2N → iPr sequence runs counterclockwise, which gives S and corresponds to `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order used below.
