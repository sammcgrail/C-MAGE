The drawing shows Fmoc-proline: a pyrrolidine ring with an Fmoc group on the nitrogen and a CO2H group on C2.

- **Ring:** five vertices, one N and four C, closed by plain bonds.
- **N substituent:** Fmoc, which expands to C(=O)OCH2–(9-fluorenyl).
- **Stereocentre:** C2 carries a hashed bond to CO2H, so the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- **CIP priorities:** N > CO2H > ring C3 > H. Viewed from the front, N (down), CO2H (upper left), C3 (upper right) runs clockwise. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving S (L-proline).
- **SMILES:** `OC(=O)[C@@H]1CCCN1` is the standard L-proline SMILES, so I used it with the Fmoc group on N.
