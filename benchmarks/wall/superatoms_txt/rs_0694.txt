I read the structure as a pyrazin-2-one ring joined to a 7-bromo-5-methoxyindoline, with a chiral 1-methoxybutan-2-yl group on the ring nitrogen.

**Pyrazinone core.** Going around the ring: N1 carries the alkyl side chain, C2 is the C=O, C3 bears the indoline nitrogen, N4 is the ring nitrogen with a double bond to C3, C5 bears Cl, and C6 is CH. C5=C6 is a double bond that closes back to N1.

**Indoline.** The indoline nitrogen is bonded to C3 of the pyrazinone. It sits in a five-membered ring with two CH2 groups, fused to a benzene ring. Going around the benzene ring from the nitrogen-bearing carbon (C7a): C7 carries Br, C6 is CH, C5 carries OMe, C4 is CH, and C3a is the ring-fusion carbon.

**Side chain.** The stereocentre bears N, an ethyl group, a CH2OMe group, and a hashed H.

**Stereochemistry.**
- CIP priorities are N > CH2O > CH2CH3 > H.
- In the drawing N is at the bottom, CH2OMe at upper right, and ethyl at upper left. N → CH2O → Et therefore runs counterclockwise.
- The H is on a hash (pointing away from the viewer), so the centre is S.
- In the SMILES `CC[C@@H](COC)n1...`, the neighbour order is ethyl, H, CH2O, N. `@@` gives S for this order, so I used `@@`.
