I read the structure in sections and joined them as follows.

- **Left end:** a 1-methylcyclopropyl ether (`CC1(CC1)O`) sits on C5 of a 2H-indazole. Indazole C3 is bonded to C4 of a pyrimidine.
- **Pyrimidine and piperazine:** pyrimidine C6 carries a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen bears a CH2 group.
- **Cyclobutane:** that CH2 is attached to C1 of a 1,3-disubstituted cyclobutane. C3 of the cyclobutane carries an ether oxygen leading to piperidin-4-yl. One substituent is drawn on a wedge and the other on a hash, so the ring is trans.
- **Piperidine to isoindolinone:** the piperidine nitrogen is bonded to C5 of a 4-fluoro-isoindolin-1-one.
- **Right end:** the isoindolinone nitrogen carries a piperidine-2,6-dione (glutarimide) at its C3. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo label.

For the trans cyclobutane I worked through the chirality tags by hand. `C[C@H]1C[C@@H](C1)O` places the two substituents on opposite faces of the ring, which matches the wedge/hash pair. A trans-1,3-disubstituted cyclobutane has no net chirality, so the assignment does not depend on which carbon is labelled wedge or hash.
