Reading the left ring: the top carbon carries OH, and the upper right carbon carries the CH2 bridge. The lower right carbon is a CH, and the bottom carbon carries OH. The lower left carbon carries OCH3, and the upper left carbon is a CH.

Numbering the left ring from the CH2-bearing carbon as C1:
- C1 bears CH2.
- C2 (top) bears OH.
- C3 (upper left) is CH.
- C4 (lower left) bears OCH3.
- C5 (bottom) bears OH.
- C6 (lower right) is CH.

The left ring is therefore 2,5-dihydroxy-4-methoxyphenyl.

The right ring is a mirror image. The CH2 attaches at its upper left carbon, with OH at the top, then a CH at the upper right, OCH3 at the lower right, OH at the bottom, and a CH at the lower left. This is the same substitution pattern as the left ring.
