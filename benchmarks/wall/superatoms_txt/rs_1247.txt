I read the molecule as a 4-chloro-2-oxo-benzoxazolone (a 2-oxo-2,3-dihydrobenzoxazole) whose 6-position carries a CH2–CH(NHCbz)–CO2CH3 side chain. This is the methyl ester of an N-Cbz-protected amino acid.

- **Ring:** the benzoxazolone has O1, C2(=O) and N3–H, with Cl on C4 and the side chain on C6.
- **Side chain:** a CH2 links the ring to the alpha carbon, which bears CO2CH3 and NHCbz (NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–Ph).
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the alpha carbon, with a hashed bond to N. The implicit H therefore points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H. Viewed from the front, N → CO2Me → CH2 runs counterclockwise. Because H faces the viewer, this reverses to clockwise, which gives R.
- **SMILES chirality:** written as COC(=O)[C@@H](Cc1...)N..., the neighbour order is CO2Me, H, CH2, N. Moving H to the front is one swap, and the resulting sense of rotation corresponds to R, so the tag is @@.
