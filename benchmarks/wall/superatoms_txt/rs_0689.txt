The drawing shows a pyridine ring with a carboxamide at C2, OH at C3 and OMe at C4. The amide nitrogen is attached to a meta-substituted phenyl ring. That ring carries an ether oxygen linked to C4 of a 2,2,6,6-tetramethyltetrahydropyran.

**Pyridine ring:** N1, C2 (bearing C(=O)N), C3 (OH), C4 (OMe), C5, C6. The SMILES is COc1ccnc(C(=O)N...)c1O.

**Amide and phenyl:** the amide N attaches to the phenyl ring, with the O substituent meta to it: Nc1cccc(OC2...)c1.

**Tetrahydropyran:** C4 carries the ether O. Its neighbours are CH2, then C(CH3)2, then the ring O, then C(CH3)2, then CH2, back to C4. The SMILES is C1CC(C)(C)OC(C)(C)C1.

The C4 carbon of the pyran is not a stereocentre because the ring is symmetric, and no wedges are drawn.

SMILES: COc1ccnc(C(=O)Nc2cccc(OC3CC(C)(C)OC(C)(C)C3)c2)c1O
