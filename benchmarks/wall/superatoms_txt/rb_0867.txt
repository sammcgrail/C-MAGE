I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type structure with three parts joined in a line.

1. **VHL-type ligand (left).** A 4-methylthiazol-5-yl phenyl group is linked through a CH2–NH amide to a 4-hydroxyproline. The proline nitrogen is acylated by a tert-leucine whose NH is acylated in turn.
2. **Linker (middle).** The tert-leucine NH forms an amide with a spiro[3.3]heptane carboxylic acid. The opposite cyclobutane carries a piperidine nitrogen. The piperidine C4 is bonded to C5 of a pyrimidine, and pyrimidine C2 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen.
3. **Target ligand (right).** The other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to C4 of a 3-aminopyridazine. C6 of the pyridazine carries a 2-hydroxyphenyl group.

Stereochemistry is taken from the wedges and hashes on the proline C2, proline C4 and the tert-leucine α-carbon. I checked my SMILES against these by working out the CIP descriptors. The result is 2S, 4R for the hydroxyproline and S for the tert-leucine, which is the usual VHL-ligand configuration. The spiro ring and its substituents are drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is assigned there.
