I read the molecule as a macrocyclic KRAS G12C-type inhibitor with an acrylamide-bearing spiro-morpholine/pyrrolidine urea tail.

**Macrocycle**
- A piperazic acid (hexahydropyridazine-3-carboxylate) forms an ester with a neopentyl CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2 linker.
- That linker is attached to C3 of an indole.
- The indole N1 carries CH2CF3.
- Indole C2 carries a pyridin-3-yl group, and the pyridine C2 bears a CH(CH3)OCH3 group.
- Indole C5 is bonded to C4 of a thiazole.
- Thiazole C2 is connected through CH2 to the alpha carbon, which carries NH and a C(=O) bonded to the piperazic N1, closing the ring.

**Side chain**
- The NH is acylated by an N-methyl valine unit, C(=O)–CH(iPr)–N(CH3).
- Its N-methyl nitrogen is part of a urea, C(=O)–N, linking to the pyrrolidine nitrogen.
- The pyrrolidine is spiro-fused at its 3-position to a morpholine. The morpholine nitrogen carries an acryloyl group (C(=O)CH=CH2).
