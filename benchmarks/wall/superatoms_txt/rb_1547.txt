I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type degrader with three parts:

- **Left end:** a 6-(trifluoromethyl)pyridine-2-carboxamide. Its NH is bonded to C5 of a 6-methoxy-2H-indazole.
- **Middle:** the indazole N2 carries a cyclohexane ring. The drawing shows a bold wedge at the N-bearing carbon and a hashed bond at the CH2-bearing carbon, which is a 1,4-trans arrangement. The cyclohexane CH2 connects to the nitrogen of a 2-azaspiro[3.3]heptane. A two-carbon chain (CH2CH2) runs from the other ring of the spiro unit to an NH.
- **Right end:** that NH is attached at C4 of a phthalimide-type isoindoline-1,3-dione. The imide nitrogen is bonded to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring (a thalidomide/pomalidomide-type group). This carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.

For the trans cyclohexane, I worked out the chirality tags for the SMILES ordering I used (`n...[C@H]1CC[C@@H](CC1)CN...`). That ordering gives the 1,4-trans arrangement.
