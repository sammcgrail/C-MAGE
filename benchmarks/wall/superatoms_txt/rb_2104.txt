This SMILES is my best reading of the drawing. Three points are uncertain: the amide N next to the pyridylalanine, the length of the fatty-acid chain, and the stereochemistry.

**Reading of the structure**
- The macrocycle is closed by a Pen–Pen disulfide (two β,β-dimethylcysteines joined S–S). Going round the ring from the N-terminus: Ac-Pen, Asn, Thr, 7-methyl-Trp, Lys(ε-acetyl), Pen.
- The linear tail then runs from the second Pen: Tyr(O-CH2CH2NH2), 2-naphthylalanine, 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid, Glu, Asn, 3-pyridylalanine, an N-substituted glycine, and a C-terminal Lys amide.
- The Lys side-chain amine carries two 8-amino-3,6-dioxaoctanoyl (–C(=O)CH2OCH2CH2OCH2CH2NH–) linkers in series, capped by a long acyl chain.

**Uncertain points**
- The N between the pyridylalanine C=O and the glycine CH2 has a third bond that runs into the pyridine ring drawing. I read this as an N-methyl (sarcosine) overlapping a ring vertex.
- I counted the fatty acid as palmitoyl (C16, with the carbonyl carbon). The zigzag was hard to count exactly, so it could be one carbon longer.
- I checked the Tyr and Nal α-carbons against the wedges and both come out L (S). I assumed L (natural) configuration for the remaining residues, including the Thr β-carbon, without checking each wedge.
