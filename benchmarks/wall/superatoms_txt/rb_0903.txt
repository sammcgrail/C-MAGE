I read the molecule as a VHL-ligand-based PROTAC (a heterobifunctional degrader) with three parts joined by a linker.

**VHL ligand end**
- A 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group sits on a phenyl ring, which carries a CH2–NH–C(=O) amide.
- The amide is attached to a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine-2-carboxamide. I took the ring as the usual (2S,4R) VHL hydroxyproline, with the hydroxyl hashed and the carboxamide wedged.
- The pyrrolidine nitrogen is acylated by a CH(isopropyl) carbon that bears an isoxazol-5-yl group. The isopropyl is on a wedge, and I worked out the stereodescriptor from the drawn geometry, with the implicit H pointing away.

**Linker**
- The isoxazole 3-position carries an O–(CH2)4 chain.
- The chain ends on the nitrogen of a piperidine, which is substituted at its 4-position by a pyrimidin-5-yl group.

**Target-binding end**
- The pyrimidine 2-position is bonded to N8 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane. No wedges are drawn on this bicycle, so I gave it no stereo.
- N3 of the bicycle is attached to the 4-position of a 3-aminopyridazine. Its 6-position carries a 2-hydroxyphenyl group.
