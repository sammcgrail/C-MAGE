I read the structure as a macrocycle built from two identical benzimidazole units joined by a but-2-enyl linker on one side and a bridged-amine glycerol-type linker on the other.

**Each benzimidazole unit**
- C2 carries an NH–C(=O) amide to a 1-ethyl-3-methylpyrazol-5-yl group.
- C5 carries an ethyl ester, CO2Et.
- C7 carries an ether oxygen.
- N1 carries the butenyl chain.

**But-2-enyl linker**
- It runs between the two N1 atoms as N–CH2–CH=CH–CH2–N.
- The two CH2 groups lie on opposite sides of the double bond, so I assigned it as E (trans).

**Oxygen-bridged linker**
- The two C7 oxygens are joined as O–CH2–CH(N)–CH2–O.
- The central CH carries the nitrogen of a bicyclic amine.
- I read that amine as 6-oxa-3-azabicyclo[3.1.1]heptane. Its bridges are CH2–N–CH2 (three atoms), one CH2, and one O. The drawing of the one-carbon bridge was not fully clear, so this is my best reading.
