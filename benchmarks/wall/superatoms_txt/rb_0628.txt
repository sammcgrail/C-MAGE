I read the structure in four parts and joined them in SMILES.

1. **Left fragment:** a phenol ring (OH) fused to a seven-membered ring. That ring runs aromatic C, CH2, CH2, CH2, C(Ph)=C(Ar), aromatic C. The alkene carbon next to the benzo ring carries the 4-substituted phenyl, and the other alkene carbon carries a phenyl.
2. **Middle linker:** the para-phenylene is attached through its nitrogen to a piperidine. Piperidine C4 carries a CH2 that leads to a piperazine nitrogen.
3. **Right fragment:** the other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to C5 of an isoindolin-1-one. The lactam nitrogen carries a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl).
4. **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre drawn is the glutarimide C3, which has a bold wedge. I took the wedge as placing the lactam nitrogen toward the viewer, which puts hydrogen behind. Reading N → C(=O) → CH2 then runs clockwise, giving R. The `[C@@H]` in the SMILES below encodes this.

The exact end of the wedge was hard to make out, so the R assignment carries some uncertainty.
