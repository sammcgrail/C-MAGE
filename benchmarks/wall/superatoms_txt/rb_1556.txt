This molecule is an apremilast-type phthalimide joined through an octanamide linker to a 4-(piperidin-4-yl)isoindolinone glutarimide, which is a lenalidomide-like unit.

**Left fragment (apremilast-like):**
- The aryl ring carries a 4-methoxy and a 3-ethoxy group.
- It is attached to a stereocentre that bears a CH2–SO2–CH3 arm and the phthalimide nitrogen.
- The phthalimide has an amide NH at position 4.

**Stereocentre:**
- The wedge runs to the CH2SO2Me carbon, so the hydrogen points away from the viewer.
- The priorities are N, then CH2S, then aryl.
- On screen, N (lower right) → CH2 (upper right) → aryl (left) runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S, as in apremilast.
- This corresponds to `[C@@H]` when written as aryl–`[C@@H]`(CS(C)(=O)=O)N.

**Linker:**
- The amide C=O is followed by 7 CH2 groups, counted vertex by vertex in the zigzag, so the acyl chain is an octanoyl.
- The last CH2 connects to the piperidine nitrogen.

**Right fragment:**
- The piperidine C4 is bonded to C4 of an isoindolin-1-one.
- The isoindolinone nitrogen carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) group.
