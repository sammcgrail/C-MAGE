The drawing shows O-benzyl serine carrying a Boc-protected amine: BnO–CH2–CH(NHBoc)–CO2H.

**Connectivity:**
- The left end is a benzyl ether (BnO), attached to a CH2 group.
- That CH2 is bonded to the central stereocentre.
- The stereocentre carries the NH–Boc group (to the right) and the CO2H group (lower left).

**Stereochemistry:** The CO2H bond is hashed, so it points away from the viewer, and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2OBn > H.
- Viewed from the front, N (right) → CO2H (lower left) → CH2 (upper left) runs clockwise.
- The H faces the viewer, so the true sense is reversed, giving S.
- This is the Boc-O-benzyl-L-serine configuration.
