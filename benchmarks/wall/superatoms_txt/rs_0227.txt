I read the molecule as the methyl ester of Cbz-protected phenylalanine, with a 3,4-dinitro-5-methyl-substituted phenyl ring.

- **Aromatic ring:** The ring carbon bearing the CH2 group sits between two ring CH positions. Going round the ring from there, the substituents are a methyl, then a nitro, then a second nitro. The nitro carbons are adjacent, and the methyl carbon is next to the first nitro carbon.
- **Side chain and ester:** The CH2 leads to the α-carbon, which carries a methyl ester (C(=O)OCH3) and an NH.
- **Protecting group:** The NH is acylated as a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–phenyl, which is a Cbz group.
- **Stereochemistry:** Only the α-carbon is a stereocentre, and its bond to the CH2 is hashed (pointing away from the viewer). The implicit H therefore points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > C(=O)O > CH2 > H. Viewed from the front, N → C(=O) → CH2 runs counterclockwise, and reversing for the H pointing toward the viewer gives R. In the SMILES below, writing the α-carbon as `[C@@H]` after the ester carbon gives R, which matches.

SMILES: COC(=O)[C@@H](Cc1cc(C)c([N+](=O)[O-])c([N+](=O)[O-])c1)NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1
