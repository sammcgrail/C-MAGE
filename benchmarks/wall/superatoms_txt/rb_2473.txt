**Stereochemistry**
- The CIP priorities at the stereocentre are triazole > aryl > chain CH2 > methyl.
- The methyl, the lowest priority, is drawn on a wedge toward the viewer. The triazole bond is also drawn as a wedge, but I treated it as roughly in plane.
- Viewed from the front, triazole → aryl → chain runs clockwise. Because the lowest-priority group faces the viewer, this reverses to S.
- In my SMILES the neighbour order is aryl, ring closure to triazole, methyl, chain. That is an even permutation of the priority order, so `@` corresponds to S.
