I read the molecule as a morpholine urea on a benzyl-protected cysteine, with the cysteine carbonyl forming an amide to a 1-(arylamino)propan-2-yl group.

**Connectivity**
- Morpholine nitrogen bonded to a carbonyl (urea): `O=C(N1CCOCC1)N`.
- That urea NH is attached to the cysteine α-carbon, which carries a CH2–S–CH2–phenyl (S-benzyl) side chain.
- The α-carbon's carbonyl forms an amide to NH–CH(CH3)–CH2–NH.
- The terminal NH is attached to a para-substituted benzene bearing OCF3.

**Stereochemistry**
- **Cysteine α-carbon:** the CH2S bond is hashed. With priorities N > CH2S > C(=O) > H, this centre is R, the L-cysteine configuration. In SMILES written as `N[C@@H](CS...)C(=O)...`, this is `@@`.
- **Alanine-derived carbon:** the methyl is drawn on a wedge. With priorities N > CH2N > CH3 > H, this centre is S. Written as `N[C@@H](C)CN...`, this is `@@`.

SMILES: O=C(N1CCOCC1)N[C@@H](CSCc1ccccc1)C(=O)N[C@@H](C)CNc1ccc(OC(F)(F)F)cc1
