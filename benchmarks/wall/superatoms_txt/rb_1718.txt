I read the molecule as a 4-cyano-3,5-dimethylphenyl ether on a tetramethylcyclobutane, with the cyclobutane carrying an amide NH. The amide is a para-substituted benzamide whose benzylic CH2 sits on a morpholine nitrogen. The morpholine's 3-position carries a CH2–piperazine, and the piperazine is attached to a thalidomide-type isoindoline-1,3-dione carrying a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.

**Fragments**
- **Aryl ether:** 4-cyano-3,5-dimethylphenyl–O–.
- **Cyclobutane:** the ring runs C(O-aryl), C(CH3)2, CH(NH), C(CH3)2. It is a 2,2,4,4-tetramethylcyclobutane with the O and N on opposite ring carbons.
- **Amide and morpholine:** NH–C(=O)–C6H4 (para)–CH2–N(morpholine). The morpholine's 3-position carbon carries a CH2 leading to the piperazine.
- **Isoindoline:** the piperazine's second nitrogen is on the 5-position of the isoindoline-1,3-dione. The ring nitrogen bears the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, whose stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds and so gets no stereo label.

**Stereo (from the wedges and hashes)**
- The O-bearing cyclobutane carbon has a wedged O, so its H is at the back. This gives `O[C@H]2`.
- The N-bearing cyclobutane carbon has a hashed N, so its H is toward the viewer. This gives `[C@H](N…)`.
- The morpholine C3 has a wedged CH2, so its H is at the back. This gives `[C@@H]4`.
