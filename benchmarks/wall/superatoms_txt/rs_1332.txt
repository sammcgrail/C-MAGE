I read the molecule as a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core carrying three substituents.

**Pyrazinone ring.** Going round from the ring nitrogen on the left of the carbonyl: N1 bears the side chain, C2 is the C=O, C3 bears the indoline nitrogen, N4 is the ring imine nitrogen, C5 bears Cl, and C6 is CH.

**N1 side chain.** This is a CH(Et)(CH2OCH3) group. The stereocentre is drawn with a bold wedge to H, so H points toward the viewer.
- The CIP priorities are N > CH2OMe > ethyl > H.
- Viewed from the front, N (bottom), then CH2OMe (upper right), then ethyl (upper left), runs counterclockwise.
- Because H points toward the viewer, the sense reverses, so the centre is R.
- In the SMILES written as `CC[C@H](COC)N...`, the `@` tag corresponds to R.

**C3 substituent.** This is a 5-methoxy-7-methylindolin-1-yl group. The indoline nitrogen is attached to C7a, which sits next to C7 bearing the methyl, and OMe is on C5.

SMILES: CC[C@H](COC)N1C=C(Cl)N=C(C1=O)N1CCc2cc(OC)cc(C)c21
