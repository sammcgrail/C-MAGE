The drawing shows a para-nitrophenyl ring joined through a CH2 to a CH carbon. That CH carries an ethyl ester (CO2Et) and an isopropoxy group (O–CH(CH3)2). All bonds are plain, so the stereocentre gets no stereo marking.

Reading it region by region:
- Ring: a benzene ring with NO2 on one end and the CH2 chain on the para position.
- Chain: ring–CH2–CH(OiPr)–CO2Et.
- Ester: CO2Et is C(=O)OCC.
- Ether: the oxygen is bonded to an isopropyl group, OC(C)C.
