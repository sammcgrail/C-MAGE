2. That ester oxygen is acylated by the acrylate, O–C(=O)–CH=CH–CH2CH2–N(CH3). The wavy bond on the double bond means no E/Z is specified.

4. This is followed by proline, then 4-(NHBoc)proline, then phenylglycine.
5. Phenylglycine is linked to the succinamide –NH–C(=O)CH2CH2C(=O)–NH–, which is attached to the meta position of the aryl ring.
