This is a PROTAC-type molecule: a VHL ligand joined through a PEG linker to a simvastatin-derived hydroxy-lactone. I read it in three parts.

- **VHL ligand:** a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl phenyl group on a benzylamide, attached to a 4-hydroxyproline. The proline nitrogen is acylated by a tert-leucine residue. I took the stereochemistry as the standard (2S,4R)-hydroxyproline and (S)-tert-leucine.
- **Linker:** the tert-leucine NH carries a C(=O)CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)CH2CH2C(CH3)2–C(=O) chain, which ends in an ester.
- **Statin end:** the ester oxygen sits on the hexahydronaphthalene ring of simvastatin. The ring has two methyl groups and a two-carbon chain to the 4-hydroxy-6-oxo-tetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone. I assigned the natural simvastatin stereochemistry, which is consistent with the wedges and hashes I could see.

I did not independently re-derive each stereocentre from the wedges. The statin stereo tags come from the known simvastatin SMILES, and the proline and tert-leucine tags from the standard VHL ligand SMILES.
