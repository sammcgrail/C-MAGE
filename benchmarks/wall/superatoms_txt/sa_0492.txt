I read the molecule as a TSAO-type spiro-sultone nucleoside, built from a furanose ring, a spiro 1,2-oxathiole 2,2-dioxide, and a triazole carboxamide.

**Regions of the drawing**
- **Sultone ring:** S(=O)(=O) is bonded to a CH that is double-bonded to a C bearing NH2. That carbon is bonded to the spiro carbon, which carries the ring O (wedge) that closes back to S.
- **Furanose ring:** the spiro carbon is bonded to the C bearing OTBS (wedge). That carbon is bonded to the anomeric C, which is bonded to the ring O. The ring O is bonded to the C bearing CH2OTBS (hashed), which closes back to the spiro carbon.
- **Base:** the anomeric C carries a hashed bond to N1 of a 1,2,3-triazole. The triazole C4 bears C(=O)NH2.
- **Silyl groups:** both TBS groups are expanded to Si(C)(C)C(C)(C)C.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned each centre from its wedge or hash, treating the implicit H as pointing the opposite way. This gives `[C@]` at the spiro carbon, `[C@@H]` at the OTBS carbon, `[C@H]` at the anomeric carbon, and `[C@@H]` at the CH2OTBS carbon.
