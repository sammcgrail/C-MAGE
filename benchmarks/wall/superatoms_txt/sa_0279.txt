This is adefovir dipivoxil: the adenine N9 carries a CH2CH2–O–CH2–P(=O) chain, and the phosphorus bears two O–CH2–O–Piv groups.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- Adenine: the six-membered ring has N at the top and N at the right, plus the C–NH2 carbon. The five-membered ring has N9 bearing the chain and N7 at the bottom.
- Chain: N9 connects to CH2, then CH2, then O, then CH2, then P.
- Phosphorus: it has one =O and two O–CH2–O–Piv arms.

"Piv" is pivaloyl, C(=O)C(C)(C)C, so each arm is OCOC(=O)C(C)(C)C. The "OPiv" label is an O attached to Piv, which gives the CH2–O–C(=O)tBu ester.

The adenine SMILES with N9 substituted is n1cnc(N)c2ncn(R)c12.
