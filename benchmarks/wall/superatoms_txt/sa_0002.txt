The drawing shows a benzene ring with four substituents:

- A dipropylamino group, N(nPr)2, on the ring carbon at the right.
- A nitro group on each of the two ring carbons next to it (top and bottom).
- A CF3 group on the ring carbon opposite the amine (left).

This is trifluralin, which is N,N-dipropyl-2,6-dinitro-4-(trifluoromethyl)aniline. Each nPr is written as CCC.
