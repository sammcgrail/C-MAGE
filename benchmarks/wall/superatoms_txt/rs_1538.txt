Adamantane has bridgeheads A, B, C, D and CH2 groups between them. C2 lies between bridgeheads A and B. Write C2 as C1(C)(...) and then continue:

- C2 is bridgehead A, opening ring 2.
- Then CH2, then C3 (bridgehead C, opening ring 3), then CH2, then C(C2), which is bridgehead D (a CH) bonded to a CH2 that closes onto A. This works.
- Then CH2, then C1, which is bridgehead B closing onto the quaternary carbon, and finally C3, a CH2 joining B to C.
