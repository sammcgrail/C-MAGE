I read the drawing as a pyranose ring with an acetylated CH2OH side chain, three acetate esters on the ring and a bromine on the anomeric carbon (an acetobromo sugar).

**Ring and substituents**
- The ring has five carbons and one oxygen, with the ring oxygen at the bottom.
- The ring carbon at the lower right carries Br on a wedge.
- Going around the ring from that carbon, the next three carbons carry OAc groups: wedge, hash, wedge.
- The last ring carbon, next to the ring oxygen, carries a hashed CH2–OAc group.
- Each Ac is expanded to an acetyl group, C(C)=O, attached through the oxygen.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned @/@@ at each of the five stereocentres from the wedge/hash bonds and the in-plane neighbour positions, with the implicit hydrogen taken as pointing opposite to the wedged or hashed substituent.
