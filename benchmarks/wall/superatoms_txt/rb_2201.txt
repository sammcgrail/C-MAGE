I read the molecule as a Gly‑Gly‑Gly‑Val‑Cit peptide linked through a p‑aminobenzyl carbamate (PAB) and an N,N'‑dimethylethylenediamine carbamate to a seco‑CBI (chloromethyl benzo[e]indoline) unit. The seco‑CBI nitrogen carries an indole‑2‑carbonyl group bearing a 5‑(2‑dimethylaminoethoxy) substituent.

**Peptide side (left to right):**
- H2N‑CH2‑CO‑NH‑CH2‑CO‑NH‑CH2‑CO‑ is the triglycine segment.
- Next is valine, with an isopropyl side chain.
- Next is citrulline, with a –CH2CH2CH2‑NH‑CO‑NH2 side chain.
- The citrulline carbonyl forms an anilide to the PAB ring, a para‑substituted benzene.

**Linker:**
- The PAB benzylic CH2 is bound to O‑C(=O)‑N(CH3)‑CH2CH2‑N(CH3)‑C(=O)‑O.
- That last oxygen is attached to C5 of the benzo[e]indoline.

**Seco‑CBI and indole side:**
- C1 of the indoline carries the CH2Cl group, which is drawn hashed.
- The ring nitrogen is acylated by the indole‑2‑carbonyl.

**Stereochemistry:**
- Val and Cit are drawn with wedge/hash bonds that I took to be the natural L configuration.
- For the CBI C1, the hashed CH2Cl gives priorities CH2Cl > CH2N > C9b > H. With H pointing toward the viewer, the sequence appears clockwise, so the centre is S. This corresponds to `[C@@H]` in the SMILES as written below.
