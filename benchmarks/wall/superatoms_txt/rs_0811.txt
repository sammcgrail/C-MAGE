The drawing shows a cyclobutane ring with two substituents on one carbon. One is a methyl ester (MeO–C(=O)–) and the other is NH3Cl, which is the hydrochloride salt of the amine written as a single label.

Written with the salt as separate ions, the compound is methyl 1-aminocyclobutanecarboxylate hydrochloride: COC(=O)C1(N)CCC1.Cl. Following the drawing's NH3Cl label literally gives a covalent N–Cl bond, which would be a neutral NH3Cl and is not a valid structure. The ionic form is the more sensible reading: COC(=O)C1([NH3+])CCC1.[Cl-].
