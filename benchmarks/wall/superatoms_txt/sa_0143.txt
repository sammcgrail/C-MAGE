The drawing shows Fmoc‑His(Boc)‑OH, the L‑histidine derivative with both protecting groups.

- **Imidazole ring:** the ring nitrogen at lower left carries the Boc group (N–C(=O)OC(C)(C)C). The other ring nitrogen is the unsubstituted =N–. The CH2 side chain sits on the ring carbon next to that =N–, which gives the pattern c1cn(Boc)cn1.
- **Side chain and α‑carbon:** the ring carbon connects through CH2 to the α‑carbon, which carries CO2H on a wedge bond and NH–Fmoc on a plain bond.
- **Fmoc:** NC(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc21 (the fluorenylmethyl carbamate).
- **Stereochemistry:** the implicit hydrogen points back, and the sequence N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S, which is the L configuration. Writing the α‑carbon as [C@H] with the neighbour order C(=O)O, H, CH2, N reproduces this.
