This is an EZH2-inhibitor-based PROTAC (an EPZ-6438 analog) linked to pomalidomide through a triazole.

**Left fragment.** The pyridone is 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl, attached through CH2-NH-C(=O) to the central benzene ring.

**Central benzene ring.** Numbering from the amide carbon:
- C1 bears the C(=O)NH group.
- C2 bears a methyl.
- C3 bears N(ethyl)(tetrahydropyran-4-yl).
- C4 is CH.
- C5 bears the aryl group (the biphenyl link).
- C6 is CH.

**Biphenyl and linker.** The second phenyl ring is para-substituted, with CH2 leading to a piperazine. The other piperazine nitrogen carries CH2 attached to C4 of a 1,2,3-triazole. The triazole N1 carries a propyl chain, (CH2)3-NH-, which connects to C4 of the phthalimide.

**Pomalidomide part.** The isoindoline-1,3-dione N is attached to C3 of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. The NH is on the aromatic ring at position 4.

**SMILES assembly.**
- Pyridone: Cc1cc(C)c(CNC(=O)...)c(=O)[nH]1
- Central ring: c1(C(=O)N...)c(C)c(N(CC)C2CCOCC2)cc(c1)-c1ccc(CN2CCN(CC2)Cc2cn(CCCNc3cccc4c3C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C4=O)nn2)cc1

The triazole is written as c2cn(...)nn2. The ring atoms run C4 (bearing CH2), C5 (H), N1 (bearing the propyl chain), N2, N3. This matches the drawing, where N1 is bonded to C5, which is bonded to C4.
