I read the structure as a 9-membered dilactone ring (an antimycin-type scaffold) carrying four substituents. The ring atoms, in order, are C(=O), O, CH(CH3), CH(O-isobutyryl), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), O, CH2 and CH(NH-acyl).

- **Amide side:** the NH on the ring carbon is joined to a pyridine-2-carbonyl group. The pyridine bears a 4-OMe and a 3-O-C(=O)-(3-methylphenyl) ester.
- **Ring substituents:** the ring carries a methyl, an isobutyrate ester (OC(=O)CH(CH3)2) and a benzyl group.

Stereochemistry was assigned from the wedges and hashes at the four ring stereocentres, which I worked out as:

| Ring carbon | Configuration |
|---|---|
| CH–N (hashed NH) | S |
| CH–CH2Ph (wedged benzyl) | R |
| CH–O-isobutyryl (hashed O) | R |
| CH–CH3 (wedged methyl) | S |
