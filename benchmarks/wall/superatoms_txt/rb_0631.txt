I read the molecule region by region, left to right, and then checked the cyclobutane stereochemistry.

- **Pyrimidine:** a 5-bromopyrimidine with an NH at C2 and an NH at C4.
- **Indoline on C4:** the C4 NH goes to C7 of an indoline whose ring nitrogen carries a methylsulfonyl group.
- **Aryl on C2:** the C2 NH goes to a benzene ring bearing an ortho OMe. Its other substituents are a piperidin-1-yl group and a methyl group ortho to it. The piperidine C4 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Piperazine and cyclobutane:** the other piperazine nitrogen is acylated by a cyclobutane-1-carbonyl group. The cyclobutane C3 carries an N-methyl amino group.
- **Cyclobutane stereochemistry:** the carbonyl is on a wedge and the N-methyl amino group is on a hash, so the 1,3-substituents are trans. I worked out the SMILES chirality tags from the 2D geometry and got `[C@H]` for the carbonyl-bearing carbon and `[C@@H]` for the nitrogen-bearing carbon.
- **Phthalimide end:** the N-methyl nitrogen is attached to an isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, and that carbon is drawn without stereo, so it has no tag.
