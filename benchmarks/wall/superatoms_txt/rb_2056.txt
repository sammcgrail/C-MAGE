This is an EZH2-degrader PROTAC: an EZH2 inhibitor (a biphenyl benzamide carrying a dimethylpyridone) joined through a linker to a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand. I read it region by region.

**Cereblon ligand (top).** The glutarimide is O=C1CCC(N2...)C(=O)N1. It is attached to the phthalimide nitrogen. The phthalimide is N2C(=O)c3cccc(OCC...)c3C2=O, with the ether oxygen on the aromatic carbon next to the carbonyl-bearing carbon.

**Linker.** From the ether oxygen: OCCNC(=O)CCC(=O)N, where that last N is the piperidine nitrogen.

**Piperidine.** N1CCC(CC1) with the 4-position bearing N(CC) (an N-ethyl amine), which connects to the central aryl ring.

**Central benzene ring.** Going around the ring:
- C1 carries the N(Et)-piperidinyl group.
- C2 carries the methyl group.
- C3 carries the C(=O)NHCH2-pyridone group.
- C4 is CH.
- C5 carries the aryl group.
- C6 is CH, adjacent to C1.

**Pyridone.** The CH2 is attached to C3 of the pyridone. C4 carries a methyl, C5 is CH, C6 carries a methyl, N1 is NH, and C2 is C=O. As SMILES: Cc1cc(C)[nH]c(=O)c1C.

**Aryl substituent.** A para-substituted phenyl bearing CH2-morpholine (N-linked).
