- The piperazic acid (hexahydropyridazine) C3 carries the H wedge. Its carbonyl is bonded to an N–OH, which is followed by CH2–C(=O).
- Next come N(CH3)–CH2–C(=O), then NH–CH2–C(=O), then N(CH3)–CH2–C(=O).
- The ring closes through an ester O onto CH(isopropyl). That carbon is bonded to CH(NH–), whose C(=O) acylates the piperazic N1.

**Side chain.**

- That carbon is bonded to a cyclic hemiketal carbon, drawn with plain bonds and so given no stereo. The ring is C(OH)–O–CH–CH(OMe)–CH2–CH2.
- The ring CH next to the ring O carries the alkenyl chain on a hash. The OMe-bearing carbon has its OMe on a wedge.

**Alkene and chain.**
- The alkenyl chain is C(CH3)=CH–CH(CH3)–CH2–CH(CH3)–CH2–CH(CH3)–CH2CH3.
- The ring CH and the chain CH are trans across the double bond.
- The first two methyl-bearing carbons have hashed methyls, and the third has a wedged methyl.
