The drawing shows an NH group bonded to two groups labelled "Tf". Tf is the triflyl group, CF3SO2, so the molecule is bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide, HN(Tf)2.

Expanding each Tf gives S(=O)(=O)C(F)(F)F, and joining the two through the nitrogen gives the SMILES below.

SMILES: O=S(=O)(NS(=O)(=O)C(F)(F)F)C(F)(F)F
