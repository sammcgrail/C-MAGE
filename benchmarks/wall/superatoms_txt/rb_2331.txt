I could not check the SMILES with software.

- Its amine is acylated by a dodecanedioyl chain (10 CH2 between the two C=O groups).
- The chain's other end acylates the α-amine of Glu. The Glu γ-carboxyl is joined to the linker, and its α-COOH is free.
