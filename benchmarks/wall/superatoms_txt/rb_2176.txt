I read the structure in four parts and wrote the SMILES in that order. Several stereocentres were hard to read and are best guesses, listed at the end.

**Sugar end.** A tetrahydropyran ring has three OH groups, a 2-phosphonoethyl group on one side and a 3-carbon chain on the other. That chain ends in an oxime ether, O–N=C(CH3), carrying a CH2CH2C(=O)NH linker (levulinic-type).

**Lysine linker.**
- Lys1 is acylated at its α-nitrogen by levulinic acid, CH3C(=O)CH2CH2C(=O)–.
- Its ε-NH carries the oxime-linked acyl chain.
- Its α-carboxyl is amidated to the ε-NH of Lys2.
- Lys2 ends as a primary amide, C(N)=O, and its α-NH is acylated by Gly.

**Macrocycle.** Starting at Cys and following the ring, the residues are:
- Cys, whose side chain forms a thioether through CH2–S–CH2–C(=O)– to the Phe nitrogen
- Phe, Val, Pro, Thr, Thr
- Bip (biphenylalanine), N-Me-Ala, Bip, N-Me-Ala
- Glu, Ala, Pro, which closes back onto the Cys nitrogen

**Stereochemistry.** I worked out the sugar-ring centres from the wedges and hashes, and the lysines, Cys, Val and the N-Me-Alas from their wedges. The rest I assumed.
- The Phe, Bip and Glu centres are assumed L, with the benzyl and side-chain bonds read as hashed.
- Both Thr residues are assumed L.
- The Ala centre next to Glu is assumed L.
- The lower Pro (next to Thr) is assumed L.
- The upper Pro (next to Ala) is read as D from its bold wedge.

I could not read the Pro ring bonds or the Ala methyl cleanly in the image, so those assignments are the least certain.
