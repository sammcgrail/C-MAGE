I read the drawing as a 4-iodo-benzoxazol-2-one core with a side chain at C6 (the ring carbon para to the ring N–H carbon's neighbour, i.e. meta to the iodine).

- **Ring system:** the five-membered ring contains O, C(=O) and N–H, fused to the benzene ring. The iodine sits on the benzene carbon next to the carbon bearing N–H.
- **Side chain:** the ring carries CH2–CH(NHCbz)–CO2CH3. Cbz is expanded to C(=O)OCH2Ph.
- **Stereochemistry:** only the alpha carbon is a stereocentre, and the NHCbz bond is hashed. With the hashed N pointing away from the viewer, the priorities N > CO2Me > CH2 > H give the R configuration. That corresponds to `[C@@H]` when the SMILES is written as CH2, H, N, CO2Me.

SMILES: O=C1Nc2c(I)cc(C[C@@H](NC(=O)OCc3ccccc3)C(=O)OC)cc2O1
