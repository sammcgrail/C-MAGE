I read the structure in pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

- **Left end:** a benzothiophene-2-carboxamide with a CF2–PO3H2 group at position 5.
- **Bicyclic core:** an eight-membered 1,4-diazocine ring fused to a pyrrolidine. The ring nitrogen carries a CH2CH2NMe2 group. The ring carbon bearing the amide NH is next to a lactam C=O that joins the pyrrolidine nitrogen.
- **Peptide chain:** the pyrrolidine carbonyl is linked to an arginine residue (guanidine side chain), then to a 4-tert-butylphenylalanine residue.
- **Linker:** the phenylalanine carbonyl forms an amide with a piperidine nitrogen. The piperidine C4 carries a three-carbon chain ending in an NH.
- **Right end:** that NH is attached to the 4-position of a lenalidomide-type isoindolinone, which carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. This stereocentre is drawn plain, so I gave it no stereo label.

For stereochemistry I used only the drawn wedges. I worked out each centre's @/@@ from the geometry around it, with the hydrogen pointing away from the viewer. The arginine and phenylalanine alpha carbons both come out as the L-configuration, as expected.
