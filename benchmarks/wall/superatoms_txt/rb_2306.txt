I read the drawing as a head-to-side-chain cyclic peptide. The thioether macrocycle runs from Cys to a chloroacetyl-capped Tyr, and each of the two squaramide arms carries a 4-(ethoxycarbonyl)phenylamino group.

**Sequence, C-terminus to N-terminus as written in the SMILES:**
- Gly-NH2, then Cys, whose sulfur is bridged through S-CH2-C(=O) to the N-terminal Tyr.
- Leu, N-Me-norleucine, N-Me-norleucine.
- Trp carrying N1-CH2COOH on the indole.
- Dab, whose side-chain NH is attached to a squaramide.
- Trp, then trans-4-hydroxy-Pro, then Leu.
- Dap, whose β-NH is attached to a squaramide.
- Pro, Asn, N-Me-Ala, and finally Tyr, which carries the thioacetyl cap.

**Two readings I settled by counting:**
- The long line from the top squaramide NH runs to a side chain with two CH2 groups after the α-carbon, so that residue is Dab (not Orn).
- The two norleucine side chains each have four carbons, so they are butyl.

**Stereochemistry:** I worked out each centre from the wedges and hashes, and they all correspond to L-amino acids. Cys is R by CIP priority, and the 4-hydroxyproline is (2S,4R), i.e. trans.
