I read this as a thalidomide-based PROTAC: a 4-substituted thalidomide linked through a propargyl–PEG3 chain and a carbamate to a statin-type decalin lactone.

- **Thalidomide end:** a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl) attached to the nitrogen of a phthalimide. The phthalimide carries an alkyne at position 4.
- **Linker:** the alkyne continues as C≡C–CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, which ends in a carbamate N–C(=O)–O.
- **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on the decalin ring carbon that would carry the ester in lovastatin. The ring has two methyl-bearing CH centres and two C=C bonds, one at C4=C4a and one at C5=C6. At C8 there is an ethylene chain to a 4-hydroxy-6-oxotetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone.
- **Stereochemistry:** I did not derive each centre independently from the wedges and hashes. I assumed the natural lovastatin configuration and used the lovastatin SMILES with the same neighbour ordering at each centre, replacing the 2-methylbutanoyl ester with the carbamate. The glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
