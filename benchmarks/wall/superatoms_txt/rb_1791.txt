I read the molecule as three parts joined in a row, and wrote the SMILES from that reading.

- **Left end:** a pomalidomide-type unit. A 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group sits on the N of a phthalimide, and the benzene ring of the phthalimide carries an NH at the 4-position.
- **Linker:** NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, then a carbamate C(=O)–O.
- **Right end:** a statin-like hexahydronaphthalene core. The carbamate oxygen is on C1, and methyls are on C3 and C7. The diene is C4=C4a and C5=C6. At C8 a CH2CH2 chain leads to a six-membered lactone, which carries F at C4 where simvastatin has OH.

For stereochemistry, I took the decalin centres to follow the natural simvastatin/lovastatin configuration rather than re-deriving each one from the wedges. I did check the two lactone centres against the drawing:

- **C4 (the F carbon):** F is on a wedge and H is hashed. With H pointing away, F, C3 and C5 run clockwise, and the priorities are F > C3 > C5 > H, so this centre is R.
- **C6 (the carbon bonded to the ring O):** H is on a wedge. The priorities are O > C5 > chain > H, and the centre comes out R.

Both match the natural (4R,6R) lactone arrangement, so I copied the simvastatin stereo tags and replaced the lactone OH with F in the same spatial position.
