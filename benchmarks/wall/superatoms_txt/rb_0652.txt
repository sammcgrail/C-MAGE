I read the structure in four parts and joined them.

1. **Left end:** a glutarimide (piperidine-2,6-dione) ring joined through its 3-position carbon to the nitrogen of an isoindolin-1-one.
2. **Isoindolinone:** the CH2 side of the ring leads to an aromatic CH, then a carbon bearing the piperidin-4-yl group, then a carbon bearing F, then an aromatic CH, then the ring carbon next to the C=O.
3. **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen carries a chain of 7 CH2 groups ending in an ether oxygen. I counted this from the horizontal span of the zigzag, about 8 bonds between N and O, which gives 7 carbons.
4. **Right end:** the ether oxygen sits on a phenyl ring that has F on the carbon next to the biaryl bond. That bond leads to the quinazoline, which carries:
   - Cl at C6 and F at C8,
   - a 4-acryloylpiperazine on the pyrimidine carbon at C4.

There are no wedges or hashes in the drawing, so I included no stereochemistry.
