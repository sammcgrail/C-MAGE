The drawing shows a pyridine ring whose nitrogen is at the bottom left. Reading around the ring:

- C2 carries the carbonyl (C(=O)N).
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are CH, with C6 next to N.
