The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an isatin core.

**Left part.** The top ring carries CO2H and is linked to a second ring, so this is a biphenyl. On the second ring, the carbon attached to the first ring is adjacent to the carbon bearing OH. The next carbon along bears the NH–N= group. Numbering the biphenyl with the COOH ring as 1–6 and the second ring as 1'–6', the substituents are 3-COOH, 2'-OH and 3'-NHN=.

**Hydrazone and isatin.** The N= nitrogen is double-bonded to C3 of the oxindole. C2 is C=O, and N1 carries the 3,5-dimethylphenyl group. The oxindole benzo ring has Cl on the carbon para to C3a, which is position 6. Position 7 is next to C7a, which bonds to N1. In the drawing, Cl sits on the right-hand carbon of the lower edge, adjacent to the carbon bonded to C7 (the CH next to C7a), so this is position 6.
