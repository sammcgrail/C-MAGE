This structure is a thalidomide-linked PROTAC-style molecule with a lovastatin-type statin attached through a carbamate. I read it in four parts and joined them.

1. **Glutarimide–phthalimide end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the imide nitrogen of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. The aromatic ring carries an ether oxygen at position 4.
2. **Linker:** the ether continues as O–CH2–C(=O)–NH–CH2, which attaches to a pyridine at C3. The pyridine's C5 carries CH2–NH–C(=O)–O, a carbamate.
3. **Pyridine:** a 3,5-disubstituted pyridine, with the ring nitrogen between the two substituted carbons' neighbours (as drawn).
4. **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on C1 of a hexahydronaphthalene. That ring has a methyl at C3 and a C4=C4a double bond. It also has a C5=C6 double bond, a methyl at C7, and an ethylene chain at C8. The chain ends in a 4-hydroxy-6-oxo-tetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone.

For stereochemistry I took the wedges and hashes in the drawing to match natural lovastatin. I wrote the stereodescriptors from the lovastatin SMILES rather than deriving each one from the wedges, so they are the less certain part of the answer. The thalidomide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds and is left unspecified.
