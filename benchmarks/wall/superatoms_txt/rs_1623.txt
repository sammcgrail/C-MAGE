Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Amidine end:** an NMe2 group is bonded to a carbon that carries =NH, and that carbon is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring. This gives CN(C)C(=N)c1ccc(cc1)...
- **First amide:** the benzene ring carries a C(=O)NH group, and that NH is attached to the pyrazole at C5.
- **Pyrazole:** N1 carries the methyl group and is bonded to C5, which bears the NH. N2 is bonded to N1 and to C3, which is the CH. C4 bears the carboxamide C(=O)NH.
- **Pyridine:** the amide NH is attached to pyridine C2. The ring N is adjacent to C2, and C3 carries F. The next positions are C4 (H), C5 (Cl) and C6 (H), with C6 bonded to the ring N.

Pyrazole SMILES: Cn1ncc(C(=O)N...)c1NC(=O)Ar

Pyridine SMILES: c1nc(NC(=O)...)c(F)cc1Cl, i.e. n1cc(Cl)cc(F)c1N...

Full SMILES: CN(C)C(=N)c1ccc(cc1)C(=O)Nc1c(C(=O)Nc2ncc(Cl)cc2F)cnn1C
