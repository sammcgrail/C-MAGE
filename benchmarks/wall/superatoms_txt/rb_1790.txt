I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule: a thalidomide-type ligand joined through an amino-alkyl ether linker to a lovastatin-like core, with the ester side chain replaced by a carbamate.

- **Glutarimide and isoindoline-1,3-dione:** the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring is attached through N to the isoindoline-1,3-dione. The aryl NH sits at position 4, next to a carbonyl-bearing ring carbon. The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
- **Linker:** NH, then four CH2, then O, then three CH2, then NH. This was counted vertex by vertex along the zigzag.
- **Carbamate:** the linker NH–C(=O)–O connects to C1 of the decalin.
- **Decalin core:** this is the hexahydronaphthalene of lovastatin, with a methyl at C3, a CH=C(ring fusion) double bond, a CH=CH double bond, and a methyl at C7. C8 carries the CH2CH2 chain.
- **Lactone:** a 4-hydroxy-6-oxotetrahydropyran-2-yl ring.

I took the stereochemistry from the known lovastatin configuration rather than re-deriving every centre from the wedges and hashes. The drawn wedges and hashes look consistent with it, but I did not check each one.
