Reading the drawing from left to right:

1. A methyl sulfone, CH3–SO2–, is attached through a CH2 to a benzene ring at a meta position.
2. The same ring carries a three-carbon chain, CH2CH2CH2, at its other meta position, ending in an NH.
3. The NH is bonded to a para-disubstituted benzene ring.
4. The para position of that ring carries CH2–CH(OMe)–CO2Et.

For the stereocentre, the OMe is on a bold wedge and the H is on a hashed bond. The CIP priorities are OMe > CO2Et > CH2Ar > H. With H pointing away from the viewer, OMe → CO2Et → CH2Ar runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S.

In the SMILES `CCOC(=O)[C@@H](OC)Cc1ccc(...)cc1`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is C(=O), H, OMe, CH2. Moving H to the front shows that `@@` for this order corresponds to S, which matches the drawing.
