The drawing shows a three-carbon chain (CH2–CH–CH2) with an acetoxy group (AcO) on each carbon. This is glycerol triacetate (triacetin).

The central carbon is not a stereocentre, since the two end groups are identical, and no wedges or hashes are drawn, so the SMILES carries no stereo.

Expanding each Ac to acetyl, C(=O)C, gives the SMILES below.

SMILES: CC(=O)OCC(OC(C)=O)COC(C)=O
