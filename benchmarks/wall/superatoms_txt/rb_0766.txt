I read the molecule as a protein-degrader-type compound made of four linked parts:

- **Kinase-binding end:** a 7H-pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidine. Its 4-NH is attached to a 1-(methylsulfonyl)indolin-7-yl group. Its 2-NH is attached to a 3-methoxyphenyl ring.
- **Linker:** the para position of that phenyl ring (relative to the NH) carries a piperidine N. The piperidine 4-position is bonded to a piperazine N, and the other piperazine N is acylated.
- **Stereocentre:** the acyl group is the C3 carbonyl of a pyrrolidine, and the wedged bond to the carbonyl fixes that centre. Working through the neighbour order gives `[C@@H]` when the carbonyl precedes the centre in the SMILES. The pyrrolidine N is bonded to the 5-position of an isoindoline-1,3-dione.
- **Cereblon-binding end:** the isoindoline-1,3-dione N carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
