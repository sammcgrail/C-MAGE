The drawing shows a silicon atom bonded to three iPr groups (isopropyl) and to a triple-bond line on the right.

The triple bond is drawn as three lines with a single bond from Si leading into it. The three lines end at the right with no atom label, so they are most plausibly a terminal alkyne, C≡CH, with the first alkyne carbon at the junction of the Si bond and the triple bond.

Reading it that way gives triisopropylsilylacetylene, (iPr)3Si–C≡CH.

SMILES: C#C[Si](C(C)C)(C(C)C)C(C)C
