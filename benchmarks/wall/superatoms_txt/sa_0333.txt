This is rhodamine B hydrochloride, a xanthene dye. The drawing shows:

- A central xanthene core with an oxygen bridge.
- A diethyliminium group (=N⁺Et₂) on the left ring.
- A diethylamino group (NEt₂) on the right ring.
- A 2-carboxyphenyl group on the central carbon, drawn as HO₂C on the phenyl ring.
- A chloride counterion.

I wrote the SMILES by placing the iminium nitrogen on the left ring, following the drawn bond pattern.

Left ring: C1 (=N⁺) is bonded to CH=CH, which leads to C(a). C(a) is the ring-fusion carbon attached to the central carbon C9 (=C). The ring continues through the other fusion carbon, which bears O, and then CH back to C1.

Right ring: the central carbon C9 attaches to a fusion carbon, which carries a C=C bond to the other fusion carbon (the one bonded to O). The ring then continues through CH, C(NEt₂), and CH=CH back to the fusion carbon.

SMILES: CCN(CC)c1ccc2c(c1)oc1cc(=[N+](CC)CC)ccc1=c2-c1ccccc1C(=O)O.[Cl-]
