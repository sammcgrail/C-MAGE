I read the molecule region by region and wrote the SMILES from that reading.

- **Pyrazine core:** the carboxamide sits on one ring carbon. The adjacent carbon carries the NH link to the aryl group. The carbon on the opposite side of the ring carries the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Piperidine:** the 3-position carries a 3-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl group. It is drawn with a hashed bond, so the H is toward the viewer. The priorities are N (imidazolidinone) > C2 (next to the ring N) > C4 > H. Seen from the front, N → C2 → C4 runs clockwise. Because H points toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is S. In the SMILES this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.
- **Tetrahydroisoquinoline:** the NH is attached at the 6-position of the ring. The ring nitrogen carries a CH2 link.
- **Azetidine:** the CH2 link goes to azetidine C3, and the azetidine nitrogen is bonded to the aromatic ring of the isoindoline-1,3-dione.
- **Glutarimide:** the isoindoline-dione nitrogen is attached to the 3-position of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
