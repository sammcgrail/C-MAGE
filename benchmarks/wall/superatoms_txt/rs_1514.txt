I read the molecule as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide whose nitrogen is bonded to a cyclohexanone ring. That ring is fused to a bicyclo[2.2.2]octane.

**Pyridine part:** The ring nitrogen is next to the carbon bearing the amide C(=O)NH group (C2). C3 carries OH and C4 carries OMe.

**Bicyclic part:** The cyclohexanone ring has C=O, then the CH–NH carbon, then two CH2 groups, then two ring-fusion CH carbons. These two fusion carbons are adjacent members of the bicyclo[2.2.2]octane. The bridgehead carbons are joined by three two-atom paths: the fusion carbons, the right-hand CH2CH2, and the inner ethano bridge.

**Stereochemistry:** I worked out each centre from the wedges, hashes and ring geometry.
- The two ring-fusion hydrogens are both wedged, so the fusion is cis. Both centres come out as @@ in the SMILES order I wrote.
- The NH on the carbon next to the ketone is on a hashed bond. That centre also comes out as @@.
