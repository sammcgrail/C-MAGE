I read the molecule as a cyclic peptide. A piperidine amide of an isoleucine-type residue is acylated by an N‑methyl‑Phe, which is in turn acylated by the α‑carbonyl of Asp. The Asp side‑chain carboxyl closes the macrocycle.

Going round the ring from the Asp side chain, the residues are:
1. Asp β‑C(=O)–NH
2. Ala
3. N‑Me‑Leu
4. Thr, with a CH(OH)CH3 side chain
5. Sarcosine (N‑Me‑Gly)
6. N‑Me‑Phe
7. Leu
8. Back to the Asp α‑NH

I assigned each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes in the drawing:
- Ile‑type α carbon: R, with the β carbon S.
- N‑Me‑Phe (exocyclic): S.
- Asp α: S.
- Ala: S.
- N‑Me‑Leu: S.
- Thr: 2S, 3R.
- Ring N‑Me‑Phe: S.
- Ring Leu: S.

I did not run the SMILES through a parser; I checked ring closures and the neighbour order at each stereocentre by hand.
