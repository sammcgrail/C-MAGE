**Pyridine ring.** The nitrogen is at the bottom. Going around the ring, the carbon next to N bears the CH2 group. The next carbon carries a methyl, the one after that carries OCH3, and the next carries a methyl. The last carbon is a CH that closes back to N. This gives 4-methoxy-3,5-dimethylpyridin-2-yl.

**Linker.** The CH2 connects to a sulfoxide S(=O). No wedges or hashes are drawn, so no stereochemistry is included.

**Benzimidazole.** The sulfoxide sulfur is attached to C2 of the benzimidazole. One ring nitrogen is drawn as =N and the other as NH. The methoxy group is on the benzene ring. It is drawn on the carbon at the top right, which is para to the lower ring-fusion carbon bonded to NH. The methoxy therefore sits at position 5 relative to the =N side, as the drawing shows.
