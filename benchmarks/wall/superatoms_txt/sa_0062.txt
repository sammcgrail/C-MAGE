The drawing shows a linear chain with F3C at the left end and SO3H at the right end.

Counting the chain carbons from the left: the F3C carbon is the first, followed by six carbons that each carry two F atoms (the six vertical F pairs, at x = 288, 433, 579, 724, 870, 1015, and 1160). That is seven vertical F pairs, so there are seven CF2 carbons plus the CF3 carbon, giving 8 carbons in total.

The fluorine count is 3 + 7×2 = 17, so the chain is C8F17.

The right-hand end is SO3H, which I read as a sulfonic acid, written S(=O)(=O)O. This molecule is perfluorooctanesulfonic acid (PFOS).

SMILES: OS(=O)(=O)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)F
