**Reading of the structure**
- **Chelator:** a DOTA-type 12-membered tetra-aza ring with three N-CH2COOH arms and a fourth N-CH2C(=O)- arm. The "Ga" label has no bonds drawn to it, so I left it out of the SMILES.
- **Linker and first residues:** the fourth arm is acylated onto cysteic acid (CH2SO3H side chain). Its carbonyl is amidated by the ε-NH of a lysine.
- **Lysine nitrogen:** that lysine's α-nitrogen is drawn as the N of an isoindole ring rather than as an amide NH. The isoindole carbon next to the N carries a sulfur.
- **Peptide chain from that lysine:** Tyr, Lys(N-isopropyl), Arg, 2-naphthylalanine, Ala.
- **Closing the macrocycle:** after Ala comes a C(=N–)–CH2–S unit, with the sulfur bonded to the isoindole. I wrote the N on that carbon as a plain imine N with no H, since a double bond and two substituents leave no room for one.
- **C-terminus:** the same carbon also bears C(=O)NH–Lys(N-isopropyl)–NH2.

**Stereochemistry:** for the lysine α-carbon, whose neighbours are written in a different order, I used `[C@H]` so that it is L.

I checked that every ring-closure digit is opened and closed.
