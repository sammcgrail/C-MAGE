I read the drawing as Boc-protected histidine: a Boc-NH group, a stereocentre bearing a CH2-imidazole side chain, and a CO2H group.

- **Imidazole ring:** the five-membered ring has an NH and a ring N, separated by a CH. The CH2-bearing ring carbon is bonded to the NH, so the ring is written `c1[nH]cnc1` starting from that carbon.
- **Stereocentre:** the CO2H bond is a wedge, so it points toward the viewer and the implicit H points away. Looking from the front, the priority order N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise, which gives S. That is the L-histidine configuration.
- **SMILES:** writing the neighbours in the order N, H, CH2, CO2H, the S centre is `[C@@H]`.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N[C@@H](Cc1[nH]cnc1)C(=O)O
