I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule. A VHL ligand (hydroxyproline, tert-leucine and a 4-methylthiazolyl-benzylamide) is joined through a PEG linker to a piperazinyl-phenyl-triazolopyrimidine bearing a furfurylamino group.

1. **VHL end:** the 4-methylthiazol-5-yl-phenyl-CH2-NH amide attaches to the proline carbonyl. The hydroxyproline stereocentres are taken from the wedge and hash bonds as the standard VHL configuration. The proline nitrogen is acylated by the tert-leucine, whose NH is acylated in turn by the linker.
2. **Linker:** from that amide carbonyl the chain is CH2CH2, then five ether oxygens each followed by CH2CH2, ending on the piperazine nitrogen. That is C(=O)CC-O-CC-O-CC-O-CC-O-CC-O-CC-N, with five O atoms in total, matching the five oxygens I counted in the drawing.
3. **Other end:** the piperazine's second nitrogen is bonded to a para-phenylene. The phenylene carries the [1,2,4]triazolo[4,3-c]pyrimidine at C8, and C5 of that ring system bears an NH-CH2-(2-furyl) group.

I did not independently re-derive the @/@@ labels; they follow the standard VHL ligand pattern.
