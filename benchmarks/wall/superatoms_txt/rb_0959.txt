I read the structure in sections and joined them into one SMILES.

- **Left piperidine:** its 3-position bears a 3-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl group on a hashed bond. Ranking the substituents at that carbon as N > C2 > C4 > H, with H toward the viewer, gives R. In the SMILES `N3CCC[C@H](C3)N4CCN(C)C4=O`, the `@` encodes R.
