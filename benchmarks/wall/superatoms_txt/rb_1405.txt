I read the structure in three parts: a coumarin core, a polyether linker, and a lenalidomide-type end group.

**Coumarin core.** The 2H-chromen-2-one ring has these substituents:
- C4 carries an NH linked to a 3,5-dichloropyridin-4-yl group.
- C7 carries OMe.
- C8 carries the linker oxygen.

**Linker.** Starting from the C8 oxygen, the chain is O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2CH2-NH.
- Counting from the drawing, the oxygens are: the ring-attached O, then O at x≈645, O at x≈818, and O at x≈990.
- Between the ring O (≈470) and the first ether O (≈645) there are two CH2 groups, and the same holds between each following pair of oxygens.
- After the last O at 990 the chain runs through three CH2 groups to the NH.
- The linker is therefore O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2CH2-NH.

**End group.** The NH attaches to C4 of an isoindolin-1-one ring. The ring nitrogen bears a piperidine-2,6-dione at its 3-position, and the CH2 sits at C3 of the isoindolinone.

**SMILES assembly.**
- Coumarin: O=C1C=C(Nc2c(Cl)cncc2Cl)c2ccc(OC)c(OCCOCCOCCOCCCNc3cccc4C(=O)N(C5CCC(=O)NC5=O)Cc34)c2O1
- In the isoindolinone, the aromatic carbon bearing NH is adjacent to the carbon bearing CH2, which is consistent with the drawing, where the NH-bearing carbon is next to the CH2-bearing ring-fusion carbon.
- Isoindolinone fragment: c3cccc4C(=O)N(...)Cc34. Atom c3 bears the NH, then three CH carbons, then c4 bears the C=O, and the final c34 bears the CH2 and closes both rings, so c3 and c34 are adjacent. This matches.
