I read the structure region by region and wrote it as one SMILES. I have not run it through a parser, so the validity check was done by hand.

**Fragments identified**
- Two identical arms, each built from (S)-2-cyano-4,4-difluoropyrrolidine, an N-acylglycine and a quinoline-4-carboxamide. Each quinoline carries an 8-NH-succinyl linker.
- One arm ends on the α-amine of an Asp whose side chain forms an amide with the Lys-derived residue's α-NH. The other arm ends, through a succinamide, on a CH2-NH that sits on a 1,4-disubstituted triazole.
- The triazole N1 is joined by a (CH2)4 chain to the α-carbon of the Lys-derived residue.
- The peptide runs: Asp(side-chain isopeptide) – Lys(triazole) – Asp – Lys – Asp – Cys.
- The Cys sulfur is attached to a succinimide whose N carries a fluorescein, a spiro-lactone with two phenolic OH groups. The succinimide carbon bonded to S is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.

**Stereochemistry**
- Both cyanopyrrolidine centres work out as S (one wedged, one hashed).
- All six amino-acid α-carbons work out as the natural L configuration: S for Asp and Lys, R for Cys.

**Judgement call**
- I placed the maleimide nitrogen on the 5-position of the fluorescein benzo ring (meta to the spiro-bearing carbon). The drawing was not clear enough to rule out the 6-isomer.
