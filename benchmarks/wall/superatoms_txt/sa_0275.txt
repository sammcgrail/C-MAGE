I read the molecule as a 1,2,3,4-tetrahydroquinoline core, a type of structure seen in CETP inhibitors such as torcetrapib.

**Tetrahydroquinoline core**
- The ring nitrogen N1 carries an ethyl carbamate, CO2Et.
- C2 carries an ethyl group on a hashed bond.
- C3 is a plain CH2.
- C4 carries an exocyclic nitrogen on a hashed bond.
- The benzo ring has a CF3 on the carbon para to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to N1 (position 6).

**Substituent on the C4 nitrogen**
- A 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)benzyl group.
- A pyrimidin-2-yl ring bearing an O–CH2CH2CH2–CO2H chain at its 5-position. This is three CH2 groups between the ether oxygen and the carboxylic acid.

**Stereochemistry**
- Only the two hashed centres, C2 and C4, are stereocentres.
- Working from the drawn geometry, with the hashed group at the back and the implicit H toward the viewer, both centres come out as `@` in the SMILES order I wrote.
