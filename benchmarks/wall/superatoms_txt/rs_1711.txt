The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl isatin.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H. The ring below it is attached to the top ring at one carbon. Its next carbon bears OH, and the carbon after that bears the NH–N= group. So the lower ring has the aryl substituent, then OH, then NH on adjacent carbons.

**Hydrazone and indolinone.** The NH is bonded to N, which is double-bonded to C3 of the indolin-2-one. C2 is C=O and is bonded to N1. N1 carries a 3,5-dichlorophenyl group. The C3 double bond to N has no drawn cis/trans geometry that I can assign, so I give no stereo.
