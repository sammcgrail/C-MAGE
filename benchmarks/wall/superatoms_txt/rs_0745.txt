I read the molecule as a 9-membered dilactone core (an antimycin-type ring) carrying an amide-linked pyridine that bears a benzoate ester.

**Macrocycle.** Going around the ring from the top-left carbonyl:
- C(=O)
- ring O
- CH(CH3), with a wedged methyl
- CH(O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2), with a hashed bond to the isobutyrate oxygen
- CH(CH2Ph), with a wedged benzyl
- C(=O)
- ring O
- CH2
- CH(NH–), with a hashed bond to N, which closes back to the first carbonyl

**Amide side chain.** The NH is acylated by a pyridine-2-carbonyl group. On the pyridine, C3 carries the ester oxygen and C4 carries OMe. The ring nitrogen is adjacent to C2.

**Ester.** The C3 oxygen is acylated by a 4-fluoro-2-(trifluoromethyl)benzoyl group.

**Stereochemistry.** I assigned @/@@ at the four ring stereocentres from the wedge and hash bonds. Each centre has an implicit H, and I set the neighbour order to match the order written in the SMILES.
