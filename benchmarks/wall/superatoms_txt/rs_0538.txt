The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl isatin.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H and is attached at its meta position to the second ring. On the second ring, the carbon bearing the first ring is adjacent to the carbon bearing OH. The next carbon carries the NH of the hydrazone.

**Hydrazone and isatin.** The NH–N= unit links to C3 of an indolin-2-one. C2 is a C=O, and N1 bears a 4-butylphenyl group.

**Butyl chain.** The chain from the phenyl ring has four carbons: the zigzag shows the ring carbon followed by CH2, CH2, CH2 and CH3, which gives butyl.
