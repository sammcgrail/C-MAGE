I read this as a DBCO–PEG4–Val–Cit–PAB linker attached to an N‑alkoxy tubulysin‑type payload. I assembled the SMILES region by region.

**Regions, left to right**
- **DBCO end:** a dibenzoazacyclooctyne, N‑acylated with a succinyl chain (N–C(=O)CH2CH2C(=O)NH–).
- **PEG4 spacer:** NH–CH2CH2 followed by four ether oxygens, each separated by CH2CH2, ending in CH2CH2C(=O)–.
- **Val–Cit–PAB:** L‑valine, then L‑citrulline (side chain CH2CH2CH2NHC(=O)NH2), then a p‑aminobenzyl carbamate (the PAB unit).
- **Tup unit:** a 4‑amino‑2,2‑dimethyl‑5‑arylpentanoic acid, with the aryl ring bearing the carbamate NH.
- **Thiazole:** a thiazole‑4‑carboxamide whose C2 carries a CH(OAc) group.
- **Tuv unit:** the CH2 and CH(iPr) that follow, with the nitrogen carrying an O–CH2CH2CH2C≡CH (pentynyloxy) group.
- **Ile–Mep end:** an isoleucine acylated by an N‑methyl pipecolic acid.

**Stereochemistry**
- Tup C4, the acetoxy carbon and the Tuv carbon bearing the iPr group were assigned from the drawn wedges. The Tuv wedge direction was ambiguous and I took it as pointing to the CH2.
- Val, Cit and Ile were taken as the natural L configuration.
- The N‑methyl pipecolic acid was taken as R, as in natural tubulysin.
- For these last four centres the wedge/hash marks were not clear enough in the image, so they rest on the natural configuration rather than the drawing.
