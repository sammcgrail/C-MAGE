I read the molecule as a central glutamic acid residue carrying two identical arms and one acylated dehydro-glutamate residue.

**Central residue.** The central glutamic acid has no stereo drawn. Its γ-carboxyl and α-carboxyl are each an amide to NH–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)CH2CH2CH2–O–aryl.

**Right-hand residue.** The α-NH of the central glutamic acid is acylated by a residue with a wedged stereocentre.
- This stereocentre carries a CH2CH2C(=O)O-tBu side chain.
- Its NH is acylated by a dehydroglutamate unit: C(=O)–C(NH–Cbz)=CH–CH2–C(=O)O-tBu.
- The C=C geometry is not clearly drawn, so I left it unspecified.
- I took the wedged centre as L (S).

**Each arm.** Reading from the ether oxygen outwards:

- Indazole N1 carries CH2CH2CH2–NH–(1H-imidazol-2-yl).
- The top arm's wedge gives the L (S) configuration.
- The bottom arm's wedges overlap and are hard to read. I assigned it the same S configuration.
