The trans relationship is encoded as `[C@H]...[C@@H]` in the order the cyclobutane atoms are written. I derived this from the known pattern for 1,3-disubstituted rings and adjusted it for the neighbour order in my string. I did not check it with a toolkit. The trans cyclobutane has a mirror plane, so it adds no absolute stereochemistry. The glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
