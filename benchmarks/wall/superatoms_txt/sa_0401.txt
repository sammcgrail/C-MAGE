The drawing shows Boc-protected, S-trityl-protected cysteine (Boc-Cys(Trt)-OH).

- **Backbone:** a central CH carbon carries NH–Boc, a CH2–S–Trt side chain, and a CO2H group.
- **Protecting groups:** Boc is C(=O)OC(CH3)3 on the nitrogen, and Trt is triphenylmethyl, C(C6H5)3, on the sulfur.
- **Stereochemistry:** only the CO2H bond is hashed, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
  - Priorities are N > CH2S > CO2H > H. CH2S outranks CO2H because sulfur beats oxygen at the first point of difference.
  - On screen, N (upper right) → CH2S (upper left) → CO2H (down) runs counterclockwise.
  - Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed to clockwise, so the centre is R. This is L-cysteine.
- **SMILES chirality tag:** N[C@@H](CS)C(=O)O is the L-cysteine pattern, so I used @@ in the full SMILES.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N[C@@H](CSC(c1ccccc1)(c1ccccc1)c1ccccc1)C(=O)O
