I read the molecule as a VHL-based degrader (PROTAC): a VHL ligand joined through a spiro linker to a bicyclic aminopyridazine–phenol group. The SMILES below was assembled region by region from the drawing.

- **VHL ligand:** a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamide of (2S,4R)-4-hydroxyproline. The proline nitrogen is acylated by an L-tert-leucine, whose NH is acylated in turn. I matched the stereocentres to the standard VH032 ligand.
- **Spiro linker:** the tert-leucine NH is acylated by a carboxamide on C7 of a spiro[3.5]nonane. C2 of the cyclobutane carries the piperidine nitrogen. The C7 carbonyl is wedged and the C2–N bond is hashed.
- **Piperidine to pyrimidine:** the piperidine's 4-position is bonded to C5 of a pyrimidine.
- **Bicyclic amine:** pyrimidine C2 is bonded to N8 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane. Its N3 is bonded to the pyridazine. No stereo is drawn for this bicycle.
- **Pyridazine and phenol:** the pyridazine carries an NH2 next to the ring nitrogen and a 2-hydroxyphenyl group.
