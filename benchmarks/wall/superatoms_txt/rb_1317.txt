I read the molecule as a VHL-type ligand (the hydroxyproline unit) joined through a tert-leucine, an acetyl-piperazine linker and an aryl-pyridazine to a 7-indolyl group. I did not derive the stereocentres from the wedges myself. I assigned them from the standard VHL ligand configuration, which the drawing is consistent with, and re-checked the neighbour order after rewriting each centre.

**Fragments, left to right:**
- **Indole:** attached through C7 (the carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon beside NH) to C3 of the pyridazine.
- **Pyridazine:** C3 carries the indole, C5 carries the phenyl, and the two nitrogens are adjacent.
- **Phenyl and piperazine:** a para-substituted phenyl joins one piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen carries CH2–C(=O)–NH.
- **tert-Leucine:** NH–CH(tBu)–C(=O), drawn with a wedge, taken as (S).
- **Hydroxyproline:** the N-acylated pyrrolidine with 4-OH and a 2-carboxamide, taken as (2S,4R).
- **Amine end:** the amide N is attached to CH(CH3) (wedge methyl, taken as (S)) on a para-substituted phenyl. That phenyl carries a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.

**Stereo notation:** I wrote the stereocentres in the order of the PubChem SMILES for VH032, a known VHL ligand. Where I swapped neighbour order (the hydroxyl carbon and the tert-leucine α-carbon), I inverted the @/@@ symbol to compensate.
