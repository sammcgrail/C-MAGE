This is a PROTAC-type molecule: an EGFR-targeting quinazoline joined through a linker to a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**Left fragment.** The quinazoline carries a 3-chloro-4-fluoroanilino group at C4 and an acrylamide (NHC(=O)C=C) at C6. At C7 there is an O–CH2CH2CH2 chain leading to a piperidine nitrogen.

**Linker.** The piperidine is substituted at its 4-position by a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated: C(=O)CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH.

**Right fragment.** The NH attaches to C4 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its ring nitrogen bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The stereocentre on that ring is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
