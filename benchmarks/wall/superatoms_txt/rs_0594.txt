The structure breaks into three parts joined by an amide and an ether.

**Biphenyl part.** One benzene ring carries SO2NH2 and is bonded to a second, para-substituted benzene ring. That second ring bears the C(=O)NH group.

**Central aniline ring.** The amide nitrogen is on one carbon of this ring. The adjacent carbon carries the O-aryl ether. The SO2Me group sits para to the ether oxygen and meta to the NH.

Ring atoms, numbered from the NH-bearing carbon (C1):
- C1: NH
- C2: O (the ether)
- C3 and C4: CH
- C5: CH, bonded to C4 and to C6
- C6: CH, bonded to C1

In the drawing, the NH-bearing carbon is at the top left of the ring. Going around:
- top left: NH
- top: CH
- top right: SO2Me
- bottom right: CH
- bottom: CH
- bottom left: O

So the SO2Me carbon is separated from the NH carbon by one CH, which makes it meta to NH. It is three bonds from the O carbon, which makes it para to O.

**Isoquinoline part.** The ether oxygen is on C7 of an isoquinoline. C1 of the isoquinoline carries NH2, and N2 is adjacent to C1. In the drawing, C8 sits between C7 and C8a, with C8a bonded to C1.

SMILES, written from the aniline ring:
- Aniline ring: c1(NC(=O)...)cc(S(C)(=O)=O)ccc1Oc...
- Isoquinoline: c1ccc2ccnc(N)c2c1, with the O attached at C7. Written as Oc1ccc2ccnc(N)c2c1, the atom c1 is C7, then c is C6, c is C5, c2 is C4a, c is C4, c is C3, n is N2, c(N) is C1, c2 is C8a, and the final c1 is C8. This matches the drawing.
