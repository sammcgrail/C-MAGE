This looks like mezlocillin, a penicillin-type antibiotic. I read it in three parts and took the stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes as drawn.

- **Left ring:** a 2-oxoimidazolidine whose ring N carries a methylsulfonyl group, MeSO2 (CS(=O)(=O)N1CCN(C1=O)…). Its other ring N carries a C(=O)NH urea link.
- **Middle:** a phenylglycine unit, NH–CH(Ph)–C(=O)–NH. The Ph is on a hashed bond, so this carbon has defined stereochemistry.
- **Right:** a penam core, a β-lactam fused to a thiazolidine ring. The thiazolidine bears a gem-dimethyl carbon next to S and a CO2H on a wedge. The ring-fusion H is on a wedge and the amide N at the β-lactam carbon is on a hash.

I did not derive the @/@@ marks atom by atom from the picture. I assumed the natural penicillin configuration and checked that the chirality tags are consistent with ampicillin's SMILES at the side-chain carbon and the three ring stereocentres.
