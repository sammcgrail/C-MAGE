This SMILES was written from the drawing by reading it in sections; I did not run it through a parser. Four things in it rest on assumption or recollection rather than being read from the image.

- **Overall structure:** a central tertiary amide nitrogen carries two identical arms and an acyl group.
  - The acyl group is the maleimide-terminated linker, C(=O)CH2OCH2CH2OCH2CH2–N-maleimide.
  - Each arm runs N–CH2CH2–O–CH2–(1,4-disubstituted 1,2,3-triazole)–CH2CH2(OCH2CH2)2O–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)CH2CH2– into the 2-position of a benzoselenophene.
- **Benzoselenophene:** it carries a β-D-glucuronic acid O-linked at C4 and a CH2–O–C(=O)–N(Me) carbamate at C7.
- **Payload:** the carbamate nitrogen is the N-methyl-valine end of an MMAE-type chain. The chain runs N-Me-Val, Val, N-Me-dolaisoleucine (with OMe), dolaproine (with OMe) and the norephedrine unit.
- **Stereochemistry assumptions:**
  - I took the payload stereochemistry from my recollection of the MMAE SMILES and re-derived it for the reversed writing order, rather than checking every centre against the wedges.
  - I assumed the bottom arm is the top arm flipped over in 3D, which would explain wedges becoming hashes, so both arms get identical stereo.
  - I took the glucuronide as β-D, with the centres derived from the β-D-glucose SMILES by permutation.
