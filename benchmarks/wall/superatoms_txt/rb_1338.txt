I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule with four parts: a quinazoline core, an acetylpiperazine, a PEG-type linker and a thalidomide end group.

- **Quinazoline core:** the 4-position carries a 4-acetylpiperazin-1-yl group. The 6-position is Cl and the 8-position is F. The 2-position is an NH that continues into the linker.
- **Linker:** NH–CH2CH2–C(=O)–N(CH3)–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O, ending on the 5-position of the phthalimide.
- **Thalidomide end:** an isoindoline-1,3-dione whose ring nitrogen bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
- **7-substituent:** this ring is drawn unusually, as a six-membered ring with two double bonds rather than a normal phenol. Going round the ring: C1 (attached to the quinazoline) is double-bonded to C2 (F), then C3=C4, then a CH2, then a CH(OH). The hashed bond sits on the CH(OH) carbon, so I assigned that carbon S from the drawing. I read the ring as drawn rather than correcting it to a fluorophenol.
