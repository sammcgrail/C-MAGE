**Quaternary carbon.** It carries a methyl (drawn with a wavy bond, so no stereo is specified), the aryl ring, a triazole carbon, and a CH2CH2 chain leading to O.

**Macrocycle chain.** From the quaternary carbon: CH2-CH2-O-C(CH3)2-CH2-S(=O)(=O)-CH2-CH2-, which ends at the indole C4.

**Triazole.** Its carbon C3 is attached to the quaternary carbon. The ring runs C3=N-C5(-aryl)-N(CH3)-N=, closing back to C3. In the drawing, the top carbon is attached to the quaternary carbon and to N (lower right, "N") and N (left, "N"). **Fluorophenyl ring.** The triazole C5 is attached to the aromatic carbon a. Going around the ring: a has an adjacent CH (b) that bears the O at its neighbour, and the other neighbour of a is the C-F carbon.
