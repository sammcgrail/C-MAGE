I read the molecule as a 2,3,4,6-tetra-O-benzyl pyranose with a free anomeric hydroxyl, and worked out the chirality at each ring carbon from the wedge and hash bonds.

**Connectivity**
- The six-membered ring has one ring oxygen and five carbons.
- C1 is the anomeric carbon. It carries an OH and has plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
- C2, C3 and C4 each carry an OBn group.
- C5 carries a CH2OBn group.
- Bn is expanded to CH2–C6H5.

**Stereochemistry, read from the drawing**
- C2: OBn on a wedge, giving `[C@H]` in the SMILES order used below.
- C3: OBn on a hash, giving `[C@H]`.
- C4: OBn on a wedge, giving `[C@@H]`.
- C5: CH2OBn on a hash, giving `[C@H]`.

I wrote the SMILES starting from the anomeric carbon and going round the ring through O, C5, C4, C3 and C2. The chirality symbols were assigned with the neighbour order that this writing produces, including the ring-closure digit at C2.
