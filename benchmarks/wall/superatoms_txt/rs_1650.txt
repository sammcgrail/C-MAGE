I read the drawing as 3-phenylcyclopentanone. The ring has five carbons, one of which carries the C=O at the top. The phenyl group sits on the ring carbon at the lower right, attached by a hashed bond. Only that carbon is a stereocentre, so it is the only one given a stereo mark.

To assign the configuration, I looked at the three drawn neighbours of that carbon. Seen from the front, they run C2 (up), then C4 (left), then Ph (down-right, pointing away), which is counterclockwise. The implicit H therefore points toward the viewer. Writing the SMILES in the order C2, H, Ph, C4 and working out the permutation from "H first, then C2, C4, Ph counterclockwise" gives `@`.

SMILES: O=C1C[C@H](c2ccccc2)CC1
