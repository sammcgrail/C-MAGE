The drawing shows a pyranose ring (ring O at the top) with four acetate groups and one amine: OAc on C1, NH2 on C2, OAc on C3, OAc on C4, and CH2OAc on C5. This matches 1,3,4,6-tetra-O-acetyl-2-amino-2-deoxy-glucopyranose.

Stereochemistry: I worked out C5 from the wedged CH2OAc bond and got the D-gluco configuration. For the other centres I assumed the drawing is the α-D-gluco isomer, based on the hashed NH2 at C2, the hashed OAc at C4 and the wedged OAc at C3. I read the C1 OAc as hashed (α), but I did not independently work out each centre from the wedges, so these assignments rest partly on the gluco assumption.
