This is a PROTAC-type molecule: a spirooxindole MDM2-binding unit joined through a benzamide and a four-carbon diamine linker to a thalidomide-type (pomalidomide-like) cereblon ligand.

**Regions read from the drawing**
- **Spirooxindole core:** a 6-chlorooxindole whose C3 is spiro-fused to a pyrrolidine. The pyrrolidine NH-adjacent carbon is itself spiro to a cyclohexane.
- **Pyrrolidine substituents:** one carbon carries a 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl group and the neighbouring carbon carries the carboxamide, whose N-aryl group is the central 1,4-phenylene.
- **Linker:** the phenylene's para position carries a C(=O)NH–(CH2)4–NH–C(=O)CH2–O chain, counted as four CH2 units.
- **Cereblon ligand:** the oxygen sits at position 4 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione, whose nitrogen is attached to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring. That stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it unspecified.

**Stereochemistry:** there are three stereocentres with wedge or hash bonds.
- The carboxamide-bearing carbon has a hashed bond to the carbonyl.
- The aryl-bearing carbon has a wedge to the aryl group.
- The oxindole spiro carbon has a wedge to the oxindole C=O.

I assigned @/@@ from the 2D geometry by neighbour order. The spiro carbon on the cyclohexane is not a stereocentre, so it has no label.
