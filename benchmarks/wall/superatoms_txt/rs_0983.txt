The drawing is a biphenyl-2-carboxamide.

**Biphenyl part.** The left ring carries OMe at the position para to the biaryl bond. Counting from the carbon bearing the C=O as C1, the biaryl carbon is C2, so the OMe sits at C5 of that ring. The right ring is a 3-methylphenyl group attached at C2.

**Amide.** The C(=O) is on ring carbon C1, and its N-H connects to the aryl ring below.

**N-aryl ring.** The carbon bonded to N is C1'. Its neighbours are:
- C2' (upper right), which bears a methyl.
- C6' (lower left), which bears a methyl.

Continuing around the ring:
- C3' bears the pyrrolidin-1-yl group.
- C4' bears a methyl (lower right).
- C5' is CH.

So the ring is 2,4,6-trimethyl-3-pyrrolidinyl-phenyl.
