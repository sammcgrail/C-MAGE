I read the structure as a PROTAC-type molecule: an aminopyridazine–phenol ligand joined by a PEG-like linker to a VHL-type hydroxyproline ligand. The SMILES follows from these regions.

- **Left end:** a 3-amino-pyridazine bearing a 2-hydroxyphenyl group at C6 and a pyrazol-4-yl group at C4.
- **Linker:** from the pyrazole N1 it runs CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2CH2 (four ether oxygens, then three CH2) to C3 of an isoxazole.
- **Isoxazole:** C5 carries the branched CH(isopropyl)–C(=O) unit.
- **Valine-like centre:** the wedge points to the isopropyl carbon. Assigning CIP priorities (C=O > isoxazole > isopropyl > H) gives R, which corresponds to `[C@@H]` in the atom order used below.
- **Hydroxyproline:** a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine-2-carboxamide with the usual VHL (2S,4R) configuration. I assumed this standard stereochemistry rather than re-deriving it from the wedge and hash bonds in the drawing.
- **Right end:** the amide N is benzylated with a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl group.
