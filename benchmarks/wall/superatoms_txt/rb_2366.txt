I read the molecule as a macrocycle built from these parts:

- **Quaternary carbon:** it carries a CHO group, a 3-(2-carboxyethyl)phenyl ring, and the triazole C3, and it starts the chain.
- **Chain from the quaternary carbon:** three CH2 groups, then C(CH3)2, then CH2, then SO2, then two CH2 groups that end on indole C4.
- **Indole:** C4 bears the chain, C5 bears the ether oxygen, C6 bears F, and C7 is CH. N1 is NH.
- **Fluoroaryl ring:** the ether oxygen attaches to this ring. Its carbon next to the C–F carbon carries the triazole.
- **Triazole:** a 1-methyl-1,2,4-triazole. Its C5 is bonded to the fluoroaryl ring and sits next to N1(CH3). Its C3 is bonded to the quaternary carbon.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I gave no stereochemistry.
