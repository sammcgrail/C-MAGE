This is a PROTAC-type molecule: a BRD4 or SMARCA2 binder joined through a linker to a cereblon ligand. I read it in three parts.

**Left fragment.** A thieno[2,3-c]pyridin-7-one carries an N-methyl group. The ring runs S1–C2=C3–C3a=C4–C5=... with C7 as the carbonyl, and C4 bears the aryl group.
- Written out: thieno ring `c1csc2c1c(cn(C)c2=O)` with aryl at C4.
- The aryl is a benzene with two OMe groups flanking a CH2–N(piperidine) substituent, giving `c1c(OC)c(CN...)c(OC)cc1`.

**Middle linker.**
- The CH2 is attached to N of a piperidine at its 1-position, and the piperidine C4 carries a CH2.
- That CH2 goes to N7 of a 7-azaspiro[3.5]nonane. The piperidine ring is N, CH2, CH2, C(spiro), CH2, CH2.
- The spiro carbon belongs to a cyclobutane in which the carbon opposite the spiro atom bears the O-aryl group.

**Right fragment.** An isoindolinone, with the aryl–O on the benzene ring. The lactam N is attached to the 3-position of a piperidine-2,6-dione (glutarimide).

**Assembly.**
- Left end: `Cn1cc(c2ccsc2c1=O)` with the aryl at C4.
- Aryl–CH2–N: `c1cc(OC)c(CN2CCC(CC2)CN3CCC4(CC3)CC(C4)Oc5ccc6c(c5)CN(C6=O)C7CCC(=O)NC7=O)c(OC)c1`.
- Isoindolinone: the O-bearing carbon is on the benzene ring adjacent to a ring-fusion carbon that carries the CH2. In the drawing, the O-bearing carbon sits next to the CH carbon attached to the CH2N. The carbonyl-bearing fusion carbon is on the other side, para to the O-carbon's neighbour. `Oc5ccc6c(c5)CN(C6=O)...` places the carbonyl carbon on c6 and the CH2 on the c(c5) neighbour, which matches the drawing.

No stereochemistry is drawn, so none is included.
