The drawing shows an oxetane ring: a four-membered ring with one O and three carbons. The O is at the left, and the right-hand ring carbon is quaternary.

That quaternary carbon carries two substituents:
- a methyl group, drawn as a line going up and to the right;
- a horizontal bond going right, which is crossed by the wavy line. This is the attachment point.

The wavy line crosses the horizontal bond, so the bond continues past it. The line to the right of the wavy line is probably just the continuation of the bond, and the attachment is a single * on that bond.

The fragment is therefore 3-methyloxetan-3-yl with one attachment point.

SMILES: *C1(C)COC1
