I read the drawing as a naphthalene core carrying two acetoxy (OAc) groups. One OAc is on the lower-left carbon of the left ring, and the other is on the upper-right carbon of the right ring.

These two positions are related by inversion through the centre of the ring system, so the substitution is 2,6 rather than 2,7. That makes the molecule naphthalene-2,6-diyl diacetate.

I wrote the SMILES with the first OAc-bearing carbon as C2. Walking round the ring: C3, C4, fusion carbon C4a, C5, then C6 bearing the second OAc, then C7, C8, fusion carbon C8a, and C1 closing back to C2. This gives OAc at C2 and C6, matching the drawing.

SMILES: CC(=O)Oc1ccc2cc(OC(C)=O)ccc2c1
