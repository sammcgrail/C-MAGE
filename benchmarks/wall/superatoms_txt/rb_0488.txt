I read this as a cyclic peptide: Ac‑Arg‑Glu‑His‑(2‑naphthyl)Ala‑Arg‑Trp. The Glu side‑chain carbonyl is joined through a –NH(CH2)4NH– linker (a putrescine unit) to the Trp C‑terminal carbonyl, which closes the macrocycle.

I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash and the neighbour order around it:
- Arg1, Glu, His and Arg5 come out as L (S).
- 2‑Naphthylalanine comes out as D (R).
- Trp comes out as D (R).
