The drawing shows a dibenz[b,e]oxepine core (the olopatadine scaffold) with a piperidylidene group and a propanoic acid chain.

**Dibenzoxepine core.** The upper benzene ring carries a CH2OH group. It is bonded to an oxygen, and the oxygen connects through a CH2 to the lower benzene ring. Both benzene rings are bonded to the exocyclic alkene carbon, which closes the seven-membered ring.

**Piperidine and side chain.** The exocyclic double bond joins the core to C4 of a piperidine ring. The piperidine nitrogen bears a CH2CH2COOH chain, which gives the 3-(piperidin-1-yl)propanoic acid fragment.

**Substituent position on the upper ring.** Label the upper ring atoms as follows:

CH2OH is on a4, which is para to a1 (the carbon bonded to O). This is the same position as the carboxymethyl group in olopatadine, where the CH2COOH is para to the O-bearing carbon.
