I read this as a phosphatidylcholine with two identical acyl chains. It is distearoyl phosphatidylcholine (DSPC), drawn without stereochemistry.

- **Head group:** a trimethylammonium (N⁺ with three methyls) is attached to a CH₂CH₂ linker. The linker goes to an O–P(=O)(O⁻)–O phosphate.
- **Glycerol backbone:** the phosphate connects through O–CH₂ to a central CH. The CH carries an acyloxy group (O–C(=O)–) and a CH₂–O–C(=O)– arm.
- **Acyl chains:** in each chain I counted 8 carbons from the carbonyl carbon to the vertex bonded to the "nDec" label. That label is an n-decyl group (10 carbons), so each chain has 18 carbons in total (stearoyl).
- **Stereochemistry:** the central glycerol carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo marks.

Each acyl group is therefore C(=O) followed by 17 further carbons.

SMILES: C[N+](C)(C)CCOP(=O)([O-])OCC(OC(=O)CCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCC)COC(=O)CCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCC
