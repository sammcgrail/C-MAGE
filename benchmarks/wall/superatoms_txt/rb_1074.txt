I read the structure in four pieces and joined them.

- **Pyrazine core:** a carboxamide sits on one ring carbon. The adjacent carbon carries the NH–aryl link, and the carbon across the ring from it carries the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Piperidine side:** the 3-position bears an N-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl group on a hashed bond, marked "abs".
- **Central linker:** the NH-bearing phenyl ring is para-substituted by one nitrogen of a decahydro-2,7-naphthyridine. The ring-fusion carbons are drawn with plain bonds, so I left them without stereo.
- **Other end:** the second naphthyridine nitrogen is attached to the 4-position of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. That dione's nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, also drawn without stereo.

For the one stereocentre, the piperidine C3, the priorities are the imidazolidinone N, then the CH2 next to the ring N, then the other ring CH2, then H. The hashed bond puts the N behind the plane, so H points toward the viewer. On screen the sequence N → CH2(N) → CH2 runs counterclockwise, which reverses to clockwise with H toward the viewer, so the centre is R. In the SMILES below, `[C@H]` with the neighbour order C4, H, C2, N gives R, which matches.
