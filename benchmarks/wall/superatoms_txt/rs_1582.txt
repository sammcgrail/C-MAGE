The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- Br on the carbon next to the ring nitrogen (C6).
- CO2H on the other carbon next to the nitrogen (C2).
- OH on the carbon adjacent to C2 (C3).

This is 6-bromo-3-hydroxypicolinic acid (6-bromo-3-hydroxypyridine-2-carboxylic acid).

Writing the SMILES from the carboxylic acid carbon: OC(=O)c1nc(Br)ccc1O. The ring atoms in order are c1 (C2), n, c(Br) (C6), c (C5), c (C4), c1(O) (C3). This closes the ring correctly, with OH on C3 next to C2 and Br on C6 next to N.
