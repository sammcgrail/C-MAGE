This is a PROTAC-type molecule. It has an androgen receptor ligand (a RD162/enzalutamide-like thiohydantoin), a pyridyl-phenyl ether linker, and a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand joined through an ether.

**Left aryl ring:** The ring carries CN, OMe and F, and is attached to the thiohydantoin N. Reading around the ring, C1 bears the N. C2 bears F, C3 bears OMe, C4 bears CN, and C5 and C6 are CH. SMILES fragment: c1(N...)ccc(C#N)c(OC)c1F.

**Thiohydantoin:** N1 is bonded to the aryl ring and to C(=S). That C(=S) is bonded to the second N, which carries the pyridine. That N is bonded to C(C)(C), which is bonded to C(=O), which closes back to N1. SMILES: N1(Ar)C(=S)N(Py)C(C)(C)C1=O.

**Pyridine:** It is attached to the thiohydantoin N at the 5-position and to the phenyl at the 2-position. Fragment: c1cnc(cc1)-c1ccc(OCC...)cc1. In the drawing, the N-bearing carbon sits at the lower left of the ring, and the ring N is adjacent to the carbon bearing the phenyl.

**Linker:** The phenyl is para-substituted, and its O carries CH2CH2NHC(=O)CH2O-.

**Thalidomide part:** The isoindoline-1,3-dione is substituted at the 4-position with the O. The N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. SMILES: O=C1c2cccc(OCC(=O)N...)c2C(=O)N1C1CCC(=O)NC1=O.
