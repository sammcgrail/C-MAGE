The drawing shows a central carbon with three Ph labels (top, left, bottom) and one Cl (right). Each Ph is a phenyl group, so the molecule is trityl chloride (triphenylmethyl chloride).

All bonds are plain lines, so there is no stereochemistry. The central carbon carries three identical phenyl groups, so it is not a stereocentre anyway.
