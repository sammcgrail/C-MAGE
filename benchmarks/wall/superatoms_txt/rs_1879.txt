The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl oxindole.

**Left biaryl part.** The ring carries CO2H at one carbon and F on the adjacent carbon. The ring carbon para to the CO2H bonds to the second phenyl ring. Numbering the first ring from the biaryl bond: C1 is the biaryl carbon, C2 is CH, C3 bears F, C4 bears CO2H, C5 is CH and C6 is CH. This makes the acid 3-fluoro-4-carboxy.

**Middle ring.** C1' bonds to the first ring, C2' bears OH, C3' bears the NH–N= group, and C4', C5' and C6' are CH.

**Oxindole.** The hydrazone carbon is C3 of the oxindole, double-bonded to N and single-bonded to C2 (=O). C2 is bonded to N1. N1 carries the 3,4-dimethylphenyl group and bonds to C7a.
