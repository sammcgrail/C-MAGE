I read the macrocycle as a linear peptide (Gln, Lys, N‑methyl‑Trp) closed through a pyridine–benzene thioether linker, with the three amino‑acid stereocentres taken from the wedges.

**Residues, from the left of the drawing:**
- **Gln:** the side chain is CH2CH2C(=O)NH2. Its α‑NH is alkylated by the CH2 on the pyridine 3‑position. Its C=O forms an amide with the Lys NH.
- **Lys:** the side chain is (CH2)4NH2. Its C=O is bonded to the N‑methyl nitrogen of Trp.
- **N‑Me‑Trp:** the side chain is CH2 to indol‑3‑yl. Its C=O forms an amide with an NH that carries the benzylic CH2.
- **Linker:** the pyridine carries the S at C2 and the CH2NH at C3. The S is bonded to a benzene ring that also carries Cl, the CH2NH group and a 4‑carboxyphenyl group.

**Stereochemistry:** the bold wedges at the Gln, Lys and Trp α‑carbons all give the L (S) configuration. For Gln the wedged side chain leaves the implicit H pointing back. The priorities N > C(=O) > CH2 then run counterclockwise, which is S. In the SMILES this appears as `[C@H]` for Gln, because its ring‑closure neighbour is listed in a different order. Lys and Trp are `[C@@H]`.
