The structure is a coumarin (2H-chromen-2-one) core. I read it region by region.

- **Core ring system:** The pyranone ring contains O1, C2(=O), C3 and C4, fused to the benzene ring through C4a and C8a.
- **C3 substituent:** a CH2C(=O)NH-aryl chain.
- **C4 substituent:** a 3-bromophenyl group.
- **Benzo ring:** the carbon attached to C4a is C5 (an unsubstituted CH), C6 carries the CH2-piperazine group, C7 carries Cl, and C8 is CH, which bonds to C8a and then to O1.
- **Piperazine:** one nitrogen is bonded to the CH2 on C6 and the other to a phenyl group.
- **Amide aryl group:** the NH is attached to a ring carbon that has CF3 on one adjacent carbon. Going around the ring from the NH carbon (C1), C2 bears CF3, C3 is CH, C4 bears F, and C5 and C6 are CH. This is a 4-fluoro-2-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl group.
