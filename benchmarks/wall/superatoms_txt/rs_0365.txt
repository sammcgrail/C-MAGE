I read the molecule as an antimycin-type nine-membered dilactone carrying a substituted pyridine amide and a hexyl carbonate.

**Substituents**
- **Pyridine:** C2 bears the amide C(=O)N, C3 bears the O–C(=O)–O–hexyl carbonate, and C4 bears OMe. The ring nitrogen sits next to C2.
- **Hexyl chain:** I counted six carbons from the carbonate oxygen.

**Nine-membered ring**
- Going around the ring: CH(N) – C(=O) – O – CH(CH3) – CH(OC(=O)iPr) – CH(CH2Ph) – C(=O) – O – CH2, then back to CH(N).
- The isobutyrate is OC(=O)CH(CH3)2 and the benzyl group is CH2Ph.

**Stereochemistry (from the wedges and hashes)**
- CH(N): the N–H bond is hashed.
- CH(CH2Ph): the benzyl bond is a wedge.
- CH(O-isobutyrate): the ester oxygen is hashed.
- CH(CH3): the methyl is a wedge.
