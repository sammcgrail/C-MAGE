I read the structure in four pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

**Left end.** A 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group (glutarimide) is attached through its nitrogen to a phthalimide. The phthalimide carries an ether oxygen at the 5-position of the benzene ring.

**Spiro linker.** That oxygen is bonded to a cyclobutane carbon. The cyclobutane is spiro-fused to a piperidine, giving a 7-azaspiro[3.5]nonane. The piperidine nitrogen is acylated.

**Chain.** From the acyl carbonyl the chain runs C(=O)–CH2–CH2–CH2–O–CH2–CH2–CH. I counted three CH2 groups before the ether oxygen and two after it. The final CH is the stereocentre.

**Right end.** The stereocentre bears an NH amide to a 1-(methylsulfonyl)pyrrole-3-carboxamide. It also bears a C(=O)NH group on a 4-phenylthiazol-2-yl ring.

**Stereochemistry.** The only stereocentre is the CH with the hashed bond to NH, so the nitrogen points away from the viewer and the implicit hydrogen points toward the viewer. Viewed from the hydrogen side, the nitrogen, carbonyl carbon and CH2 run clockwise. For the SMILES neighbour order (CH2, H, N, C=O) this corresponds to `[C@H]`.
