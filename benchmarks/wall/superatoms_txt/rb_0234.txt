I read the molecule as three connected pieces.

1. **Macrocycle.** It is a 15-membered diaza-trioxa crown ring. Going round the ring: N(aryl), CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, N, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, back to the first N. One nitrogen carries a 2-methoxyphenyl group, and the other is bonded to the benzo ring of the lactone.

2. **Benzo ring of the lactone.** The ring carbon bearing OMe sits next to the carbon bearing the macrocycle N and next to a CH. That CH leads to the ring carbon bonded to the spiro centre. The lactone C(=O) is on the ring carbon adjacent to the CH that neighbours the C–N carbon.

3. **Xanthene.** The spiro carbon joins the lactone O and two fluorinated aryl rings bridged by an oxygen. Each aryl ring has F ortho to an OAc group, and both OAc groups are para to the spiro carbon's attachment through the bridging oxygen side as drawn.

The spiro carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave no stereo.
