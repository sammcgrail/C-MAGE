The drawing shows a [1,2,4]triazolo[4,3-b]pyridazin-3-one core, built from a five-membered triazolone fused to a six-membered pyridazine.

**Triazolone ring (five-membered):**
- N= (upper left) is double-bonded to the fusion carbon C8a.
- N2 (upper right) carries the CH2 substituent.
- C3 is the C=O carbon.
- N4 is the bridgehead nitrogen, which is also part of the six-membered ring.
- C8a is the other fusion carbon.

**Pyridazine ring (six-membered):**
- C8a is bonded to CH (C8).
- C8 is bonded to C7, which carries the 4-chlorophenyl group.
- C7 is bonded to C6, which carries the 2-chlorophenyl group.
- C6 is double-bonded to N, and that N is bonded to the bridgehead N4.
- N4 is bonded back to C8a.

The ring bonds are C8a=N (in the triazole), C8=C7, and C6=N.

**SMILES for the core:** O=C1N(Cc2ccc(nc2)C(F)(F)F)N=C3C=C(c4ccc(Cl)cc4)C(c5ccccc5Cl)=NN13

**Substituent on N2:** the CH2 is attached to a pyridine at its 3-position. The pyridine nitrogen is at the top, and the CF3 sits on the carbon adjacent to that nitrogen, which is position 6. The substituent is therefore (6-trifluoromethylpyridin-3-yl)methyl. In the SMILES, Cc2ccc(nc2)C(F)(F)F places CH2 on c2, followed by c, c, then c(CF3) next to n, then c2 closing the ring. The ring atom bearing CF3 is adjacent to n, and n is adjacent to the CH that closes the ring back to the CH2-bearing carbon, so the CH2 carbon is position 3 and CF3 is position 6, as drawn.

**Check of the core string:** C1(=O) is bonded to N(CH2Ar), then N=C3 (C8a), then C=C(Ar) (C8=C7), then C(Ar')=N–N13, where the final N closes ring 1 to the carbonyl carbon and ring 3 to C8a. This matches the drawing.
