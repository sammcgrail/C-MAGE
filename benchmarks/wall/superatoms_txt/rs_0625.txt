This is an eltrombopag-like biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl isatin.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H and is attached at the meta position to the second ring. In the second ring, the carbon bearing the first ring sits next to the carbon bearing OH, and the next carbon carries the NH–N= group. That gives a 2-hydroxy-3-hydrazinyl-biphenyl-3'-carboxylic acid.

**Indolinone part.**
- C3 of the indolinone is double-bonded to the hydrazone N.
- C2 is the C=O, and N1 carries the 3,4-dimethylphenyl group.
- The methyl on the benzo ring is at position 6. The ring fusion carbon C7a is bonded to N1, so C7 comes next and C6 follows, which is the methyl-bearing carbon in the drawing.
