I read the structure as a latrunculin-type macrolide: a 2-thiazolidinone joined to a hemiketal tetrahydropyran, which sits inside a 16-membered macrolactone.

**Thiazolidinone.** The ring is NH–C(=O)–S–CH2–CH. The ring CH carries a wedged H and is bonded to the pyran hemiketal carbon. I assigned it R.

**Tetrahydropyran.**
- The ring runs O, C(OH)(thiazolidinyl), CH2, CH(O-ester), CH2, CH.
- The OH on the hemiketal carbon is wedged.
- The ester oxygen on the next ring carbon is wedged.
- The chain bond on the last ring carbon is hashed.

**Macrocycle chain.** Starting from the pyran CH, the chain runs CH2, CH2, CH(Et), CH=CH, CH2, CH2, C(Me)=CH, C(=O)–O, and the ester oxygen closes onto the pyran carbon.
- The CH(Et) has a wedged ethyl.
- The CH=CH is drawn cis (Z).
- In the trisubstituted alkene, the chain CH2 and the carbonyl sit on the same side, so it is Z.

I derived each @/@@ by working out the geometry from the wedges and hashes, so they are worked by hand and not checked with software.
