**Ring sequence.** Starting at the 4-(thiomethyl)benzoyl unit and going around:
- Benzamide C(=O)–NH on the left-hand Phe-derived carbon, which carries a benzyl group.
- Reduced-amide CH2–N(acyl) with a cyclopropylacetyl group, then a glycyl CH2–C(=O)–NH.
- A Leu-derived carbon (isobutyl), then CH2–N(cyclopropylacetyl), then CH2–C(=O)–NH.
- A Lys-derived carbon (4-aminobutyl chain), then CH2–N(phenylacetyl), then CH2–C(=O)–NH.
- A Phe-derived carbon (benzyl), then CH2–N. This last nitrogen carries a CH2C(=O)NH2 arm and a C(=O)CH2CH2–S acyl group.
- The sulfur closes the ring through CH2 to the para position of the benzene ring.

**Stereochemistry.** Each of the four substituted carbons has a wedged side chain, an NH to one side, a CH2N, and an implicit H pointing away. In every case, going NH → CH2N → side chain appears counterclockwise with H behind. In the SMILES order N, H, side chain, CH2N, this corresponds to `@@` for all four centres.
