I read the structure as a macrocyclic peptide built from an ornithine, a lysine and an N-methyltryptophan, closed through two pyridine rings and a thioether.

**Residues and substituents**
- **Ornithine:** H2N–CH2CH2CH2–CH, with the alpha carbon bearing an NH (to a CH2 on the left pyridine) and a C(=O) that forms an amide to the lysine NH.
- **Lysine:** the side chain is four CH2 groups ending in NH2. Its C(=O) joins the N-methyl nitrogen of the tryptophan.
- **N-methyltryptophan:** the side chain is CH2 to indol-3-yl. Its C(=O) forms an amide NH–CH2 to the right pyridine.
- **Right pyridine:** the CH2 is on C4, a 4-carboxyphenyl is on C3, and the S is on C5.
- **Left pyridine:** the CH2 is on C3 and the S is on C2, so the thioether links the two pyridines.

**Stereochemistry**
I treated each wedge as starting at the alpha carbon, with the side chain toward the viewer. Working out priorities at each centre (N > C=O > side-chain CH2 > H) gives S at all three alpha carbons, which corresponds to L-ornithine, L-lysine and L-tryptophan. I wrote the chirality tags to match, using the L-amino-acid pattern N[C@@H](R)C(=O). For the ornithine the neighbour order in my string differs, so its tag is [C@H].
