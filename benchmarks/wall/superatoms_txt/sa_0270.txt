I read this as the 6-chloro-17-acetoxy-pregna-4,6-diene-3,20-dione skeleton (the chlormadinone acetate constitution), but with every stereocentre drawn in the mirror-image sense.

**Connectivity**
- The steroid core has an enone in ring A: a C=O on the right, conjugated with the C4=C5 double bond.
- Ring B carries a second double bond (C6=C7), with Cl on C6.
- C17 on the five-membered ring D carries both an acetoxy group (the "OAc" label, O–C(=O)CH3) and an acetyl group (the "Ac" label, C(=O)CH3).
- There are two angular methyl groups, at C10 and C13.

**Stereochemistry**
- Both methyls (C10 and C13) are drawn with hashed bonds.
- The ring-fusion hydrogens are drawn as wedge at C9 (top, near the ring B/C junction), hashed at C8, and wedge at C14.
- The acetyl group at C17 is hashed.
- This is the opposite of the natural steroid arrangement (methyls wedged, C9–H hashed, C8–H wedged, C14–H hashed, C17 acetyl wedged). So the drawing shows the enantiomer of the natural series.
- I wrote the natural-series SMILES from memory of the progesterone pattern, not from re-deriving each centre from the picture. I then inverted every stereocentre to match the drawing.
