I read the image as a lipidated, disulfide-cyclised peptide and wrote the SMILES residue by residue, from the lauroyl tail to the C-terminal amide.

**Lipid tail and linkers**
- Lauroyl (C12 acyl) is attached to the α-nitrogen of a γ-linked L-Glu, which keeps its free α-COOH.
- The Glu side chain is linked through two 2-(2-aminoethoxy)ethoxyacetyl (AEEA) spacers to the N-terminus of the first penicillamine (Pen).

**Cyclic part (disulfide between the two Pen residues)**
- Pen1, then Asn, Thr, 7-methyl-Trp and Nε-acetyl-Lys, then Pen2.
- The two Pen CMe₂ sulfurs are joined by the S–S bond, closing the macrocycle.

**Tail beyond Pen2**
- Tyr with its phenol O-alkylated by CH₂CH₂–NH–C(=NH)NH₂ (a guanidinoethyl ether).
- 2-Naphthylalanine.
- 4-Aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid, which has no stereocentre.
- Glu with a free side-chain COOH.
- Asn.
- 3-Pyridylalanine.
- C-terminal N-methylglycinamide (sarcosinamide), N(CH₃)CH₂C(=O)NH₂.

**Stereochemistry**
- Wedge and hash bonds are hard to resolve at several centres. I assumed natural L configuration for Glu, Asn, Thr, Trp, Lys, Tyr, 2-Nal and 3-Pal.
- By my reading of the drawing, Pen1 is L (R) and Pen2 is D (S). These two assignments are the least certain.
