Reading the structure region by region:

**Left side.** A morpholine ring is attached through its nitrogen to a two-carbon chain (N-CH2CH2-). That chain ends at N3 of a benzimidazol-2-one. The ring carbon C2 carries the C=O. The other benzimidazolone nitrogen, N1, is bonded to the C4 carbon of a piperidine.

**Middle.** The piperidine nitrogen is bonded to CH2-CH(OH)-CH2, and that chain ends at N1 of a pyrazole.

**Right side.** The pyrazole is fused to a tetrahydropyridine ring.
- The pyrazole atoms are N1, N2, C3 (bearing the 4-CF3-phenyl group), C3a and C7a.
- The six-membered ring runs C3a-CH2-N(Ac)-CH2-CH2-C7a.
- The nitrogen carries an acetyl group, C(=O)CH3.

- In the pyrazole, n2 is N1, then n is N2, then c(Ar) is C3, then c3 is C3a. After C3a comes CH2, then N(Ac), then CH2CH2, and the ring closes at c32, which is C7a. C7a closes ring 3 to C3a and ring 2 to N1.
- The hydroxyl carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
