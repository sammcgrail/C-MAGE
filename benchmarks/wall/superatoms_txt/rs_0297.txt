**Stereochemistry**

The ring CH2 outranks the chain CH2 because its path reaches the C=O carbon (O,O,N) sooner. With H pointing back, N → ring CH2 → chain runs counterclockwise, so this centre is S. In the SMILES this is `[C@H]`.

With OH hashed and H toward the viewer, the sense of the OH → ring-side → CH3-side sequence is reversed, so this centre is also S. In the SMILES this is `[C@@H]`.
