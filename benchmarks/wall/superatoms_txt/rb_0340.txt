I read the molecule in four parts and joined them in this SMILES.

- **Left end:** a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl) is attached through its nitrogen to a phthalimide. The phthalimide's benzene ring carries a piperidin-1-yl group.
- **Linker:** that piperidine is substituted at C4 by CH2, which leads to the nitrogen of a second piperidine. The second piperidine is substituted at C4 by CH2–NH, and that NH is attached to C6 of an isoquinoline.
- **Isoquinoline end:** C1 of the isoquinoline carries a 2-(aminomethyl)phenyl group. The CH2–NH of that group is bonded to C4 of a pyrazolo[1,5-a][1,3,5]triazine.
- **Triazine end:** the triazine has an isopropyl group at C8 and a piperidin-3-yloxy group at C2.

The only drawn stereochemistry is the wedge on the piperidin-3-yl carbon bearing the oxygen. The CIP priorities there are O > C2 (the CH2 next to N) > C4 > H. With H pointing away, O → C2 → C4 runs clockwise, so the centre is R. In the SMILES this corresponds to `O[C@@H]3CCCNC3`. The glutarimide C3 is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
