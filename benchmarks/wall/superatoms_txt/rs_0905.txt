Reading the chain from the aldehyde end: O=CH–CH2–CH(Et)–CH2–CH(CH3)–CH3. This is 3-ethyl-5-methylhexanal.

**Stereochemistry:** C3 is the only stereocentre drawn with a wedge, and the wedge goes to the ethyl group. The implicit H therefore points away from the viewer.

CIP priorities at C3:
1. C2 is highest. Its branch leads to the CHO carbon, which carries (O,O,H).
2. C4 is next. Its branch leads to C5, which carries (C,C,H).
3. The ethyl group is below those two.
4. H is lowest.

Viewed from the front with H at the back, C2 (upper right) → C4 (upper left) → Et (down) runs counterclockwise, so C3 is S.

In the SMILES `O=CC[C@H](CC(C)C)CC`, the neighbour order at C3 is C2, H, C4, Et. The `@` symbol corresponds to S for that order.
