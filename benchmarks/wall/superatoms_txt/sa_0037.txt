The drawing shows F3C on the left, then five CF2 carbons in a row, then "nOct" on the right.

- The left end is a CF3 group.
- Each of the five carbons in the chain carries two F atoms.
- "nOct" is n-octyl, which is CCCCCCCC (8 carbons).

Atom by atom: FC(F)(F) followed by five C(F)(F) units, then the octyl chain. That gives 1 + 5 = 6 fluorinated carbons plus 8 octyl carbons, which is 1-perfluorohexyl-octane (C6F13-C8H17).
