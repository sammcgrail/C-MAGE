Reading the drawing region by region:

- The left end is BnO2C, a benzyl ester, which is C(=O)OCc1ccccc1.
- This is bonded to a CH2, which is bonded to the stereocentre.
- The stereocentre carries NH–Boc on the right, where Boc is C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.
- It also carries a CO2H group on a hashed bond, pointing away from the viewer.
- The implicit hydrogen therefore points toward the viewer.

This is Boc-aspartic acid β-benzyl ester, Boc-Asp(OBn)-OH.

For the stereochemistry, the CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H. Going from N (right) to CO2H (lower left) to CH2 (upper left) runs clockwise in the drawing. The hydrogen faces the viewer, so the true sense is reversed, which gives counterclockwise and S. That matches the L-amino acid.
