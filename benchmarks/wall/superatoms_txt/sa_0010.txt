This is Danishefsky's diene: 1-methoxy-3-trimethylsilyloxy-1,3-butadiene.

Reading the drawing, atoms run left to right:
- MeO at the left, bonded to C1 at about (512, 715).
- C1=C2, where C2 is at about (718, 880).
- C2–C3, where C3 is at about (965, 780).
- C3=CH2, with the CH2 at the top (about (980, 520)).
- C3–OTMS, with the OTMS at the lower right.

**Stereochemistry:** The C1=C2 double bond has OMe on C1 and the C3 chain on C2. The OMe goes from C1 down-left to O at (310, 795), and the C3 chain goes from C2 up-right. The two substituents point to opposite sides of the double bond, so the geometry is trans (E).

In SMILES, written as CO/C=C/C(=C)O[Si](C)(C)C, the "/" marks place the OMe and the C3 chain on opposite sides, which is E.
