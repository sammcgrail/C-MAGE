This is a macrocyclic STING-agonist-type dimer: two benzimidazole units joined by a but-2-ene linker on the N1 atoms and by a 2-(imidazol-1-yl)propane-1,3-diyl bis-ether on the 7-oxygens.

**Pieces read from the drawing**
- Each pyrazole is 1-ethyl-3-methylpyrazole-5-carbonyl, attached through an amide to the 2-amino group of its benzimidazole.
- Each benzimidazole carries a primary carboxamide (C(N)=O) at C5 and an ether oxygen at C7.
- The N1 atoms are linked by CH2–CH=CH–CH2. The drawing gives no clear cis/trans geometry, so I left the double bond unspecified.
- The two oxygens are joined through OCH2–CH(n-imidazolyl)–CH2O.

**Top half**
- Pyrazole: CCn1nc(C)cc1C(=O)N...
- Benzimidazole 2-position: c2nc3cc(C(N)=O)cc(O...)c3n2(CC=CC...)
