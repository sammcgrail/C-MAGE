The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl isatin core.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H. A second ring is attached to it at the position meta to the acid. On that second ring, the carbon bonded to the first ring is adjacent to the carbon bearing OH, which is adjacent to the carbon bearing the NH. So the ring is 2-hydroxy-3-(hydrazinyl) relative to the biaryl bond.

**Hydrazone linker.** The ring NH is bonded to N, and that N is double-bonded to C3 of the indol-2-one.

**Indolinone part.**
- C3 is bonded to C2, which is C(=O), and C2 is bonded to N1.
- N1 carries a 3,4-dimethylphenyl group.
- The benzo ring has Br at the position para to C3a. Going around the ring from C3a: C4, C5, C6 (Br), C7, C7a. In the drawing, C3a is at the top and its neighbour at the upper right is C4. The next vertex is C5, then C6 at the right, which bears Br, then C7 at the bottom, then C7a. So Br is at C6, matching the name 6-bromo.

**Stereochemistry.** The C=N double bond has no clear E/Z geometry that I can assign with confidence, so I leave it unspecified.
