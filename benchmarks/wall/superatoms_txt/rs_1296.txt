- a methyl ester (CO2Me) at C1
- a benzyloxy group at C2
- a second benzyloxy group at C3
- a CF3 group at C5

Reading around the ring: C1 carries the ester and sits at the top left. C2 is at the top right and carries OBn. C3 is at the bottom right and carries OBn. C4 is at the bottom and is a CH. C5 is at the bottom left and carries CF3. C6 is at the left and is a CH.

This gives methyl 2,3-bis(benzyloxy)-5-(trifluoromethyl)benzoate.
