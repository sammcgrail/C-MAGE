This is a PROTAC-type molecule: an EGFR inhibitor (afatinib-like quinazoline) joined through a linker to a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**Quinazoline end.** The 3-chloro-4-fluorophenyl group is attached through NH to C4 of the quinazoline. N1 and N3 are the ring nitrogens, C6 carries the amide NH, and C7 carries OMe.

**Linker.** The amide NH–C(=O)–CH=CH–CH2 chain is drawn with an E double bond. The CH2 is attached to the piperidine nitrogen. Piperidine C4 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen carries C(=O)CH2CH2C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–NH–, which leads to the aryl ring.

**Thalidomide end.** The aryl ring is the 4-amino-substituted isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its imide nitrogen bears the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The stereocentre there is drawn with plain bonds, so I give it no stereo.
