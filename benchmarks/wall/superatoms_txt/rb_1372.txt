I read the molecule in six pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

1. **Left end:** a pyrrolo[1,2-b]pyridazine with a nitrile on the six-membered ring. Its pyrrole carbon next to the bridgehead nitrogen is bonded to a pyridine.
2. **Pyridine:** the carbon bonded to the pyrrolopyridazine is C2. C4 carries an isopropylamino group (NH–CH(CH3)2). C5 is bonded to a 1,3,4-thiadiazole.
3. **Thiadiazole and linker:** the thiadiazole's other carbon carries a piperazine nitrogen. The far piperazine nitrogen is acylated by a C=O that leads to C4 of a piperidine.
4. **Piperidine to pyrrolidine:** the piperidine nitrogen is linked through a CH2 to C3 of a pyrrolidine. That CH2 is drawn on a hashed bond.
5. **Right end:** the pyrrolidine nitrogen is bonded to C5 of a phthalimide-type isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
6. **Stereochemistry:** the only drawn stereocentre is the pyrrolidine C3. Ranking its substituents gives ring C2 (next to N) first, the exocyclic CH2 second, ring C4 third and H last. With the CH2 hashed, so that H points toward the viewer, the arrangement is R. I wrote this as `[C@H]` in the SMILES order used here. I worked out the chirality symbol by hand and did not run it through a toolkit.
