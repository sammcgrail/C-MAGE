I read the drawing as a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core with three substituents.

**Pyrazinone ring.** N1 carries the chiral side chain and is bonded to C2 (C=O). C3 is bonded to the indoline nitrogen and is double-bonded to N4. C5 carries Cl and is double-bonded to C6, which closes back to N1.

**Side chain on N1.** The stereocentre has an ethyl group (upper left), a cyclopropyl group (upper right), the ring nitrogen (below) and an H on a wedge bond toward the viewer. Looking from the H side, ethyl → cyclopropyl → N runs clockwise. Converting to SMILES neighbour order (ethyl, H, cyclopropyl, N) takes one swap, which gives `[C@H]`.

**Indoline on C3.** The indoline nitrogen is bonded to the pyrazinone ring. Its ring is N–CH2–CH2–C3a, with the fused benzene bearing OMe at C5 and CH3 at C7 (adjacent to C7a, which is attached to N).

The SMILES is `CC[C@H](C1CC1)n1cc(Cl)nc(N2CCc3cc(OC)cc(C)c32)c1=O`.
