I read this as a cyclic peptide and wrote it out residue by residue.

- **C-terminus:** Thr-NH2.
- **Apc residue:** a 4-aminopiperidine-4-carboxamide, whose ring nitrogen is acylated by the side-chain carbonyl of a Glu-type residue. This acylation is what closes the macrocycle.
- **Macrocycle sequence:** going from the Glu-type residue, the chain runs Ala, Trp, His, Leu, Gly, Glu, Nle (a butyl side chain), Val, Trp, and then the Apc residue.
- **N-terminal Asp:** it is attached to the Glu-type residue through its carbonyl, and its amine carries a substituent cut off by a wavy bond. I could not tell whether that substituent is a methyl or a tert-butyl group, so I wrote N-methyl as a guess.

I assigned stereochemistry using L-amino-acid configuration at each α-carbon, and I read L-Thr from the drawing. I did not carefully check the hashed and wedged bonds, so some centres, such as the Glu-type residue, which may be drawn as D, are guesses.
