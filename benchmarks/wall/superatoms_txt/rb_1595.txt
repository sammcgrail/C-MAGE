I read the molecule as a VHL-ligand–linked PROTAC-type compound. I wrote the SMILES in pieces and then joined them.

**Pieces, right to left in the drawing**
- **Cap:** a 1-fluorocyclopropane-carboxamide, `NC(=O)C1(F)CC1`, sits on the amino group of a tert-leucine (tert-butyl on the α-carbon).
- **Hydroxyproline:** the tert-leucine carbonyl acylates the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine. The hydroxyl is on the hashed bond and the 2-carboxamide is on the wedged bond. I took this to be the standard (2S,4R) VHL ligand and used its usual SMILES.
- **Benzylic centre:** the proline amide NH is attached to a stereocentre carrying CH2C(=O)NH–, a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl group on a hashed bond, and H. Working through the hashed bond gives `@@` when written from the proline side.
- **Cyclobutane:** it is 1,3-disubstituted, with the amide NH on a wedge and the thiadiazole on a hash. That makes it trans, written `N[C@@H]2C[C@H](C2)…`.
- **Thiadiazole:** a 1,3,4-thiadiazole joins the cyclobutane to a pyridine.
- **Pyridine:** it carries an NHMe group, with the thiadiazole next to it and the pyrrolopyridazine at the other end.
- **Pyrrolo[1,2-b]pyridazine:** it has a nitrile at the 5-position and is attached to the pyridine through the carbon next to the bridgehead nitrogen.

**Checks**
- All ring-closure digits are opened and closed, and digits are reused only after being closed.
