The sulfonyl group sits on the benzene ring at the position adjacent to the ring-fusion carbon, which is C4 of the indane. The sulfonyl is attached to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen carries a benzyl group, and that benzene ring has OCF3 at the meta position.

Atom check on the indane:
- C1 is the CH bearing the COOH.
- The CH2 next to it leads into the aromatic carbon c2, then cccc runs around the ring, and the c(...) bearing the sulfonyl is followed by c2, the other fusion carbon, which closes onto the second CH2 (C1).
- Reading around the ring: c2 (fusion), c, c, c, c(S) (C4), c2 (fusion, C3a). This gives 4 CH/C-substituent atoms plus 2 fusion atoms, which is 6 aromatic atoms, so the ring is correct.

The sulfonyl carbon is adjacent to the fusion carbon, which matches the drawing. No wedges or hashes are shown, so there is no stereochemistry.
