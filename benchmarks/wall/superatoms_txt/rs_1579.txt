I read the molecule as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide whose amide nitrogen is attached to a para-substituted phenyl ring. That ring carries a C=N–OH (oxime) carbon, which is also bonded to a 3-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl ring.

- **Pyridine ring:** the ring nitrogen sits next to C2, which carries the C(=O)NH group. C3 has OH and C4 has OMe. C5 and C6 are plain CH.
- **Oxime geometry:** the oxime nitrogen is drawn below the central carbon, with its OH pointing to the right. The OH is therefore on the same side as the CF3-phenyl ring and opposite the para-substituted phenyl ring. I encoded this with `/C(=N/O)`, which puts the oxygen trans to the aminophenyl ring.
