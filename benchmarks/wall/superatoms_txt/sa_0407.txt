This structure is cefpirome. I read it region by region and worked out the stereochemistry from the drawn wedge and hash bonds.

- **Aminothiazole:** a 2-aminothiazol-4-yl ring (S, C–NH2, N, and two ring carbons).
- **Oxime:** a C=N–OMe group between the thiazole and the amide carbonyl. The OMe and the amide C=O are on the same side of the C=N, so it is the Z (syn) oxime. I wrote the thiazole as trans to OMe in the SMILES.
- **β-Lactam and dihydrothiazine core:** a cephem bicyclic core with a lactam C=O, an S in the six-membered ring, and a ring C=C bearing a carboxylate (COO⁻).
- **Stereocentres:** the NH-bearing carbon has a hashed bond to N, and the ring-fusion carbon has a wedged H. From these I assigned the NH-bearing carbon as `@` and the ring-fusion carbon as `@@` in the SMILES order below.
- **Side chain:** a CH2 on the ring C=C carries a pyridinium nitrogen. That pyridinium is fused to a cyclopentane (a 2,3-cyclopentenopyridinium) through the two carbons next to the ring junction.
