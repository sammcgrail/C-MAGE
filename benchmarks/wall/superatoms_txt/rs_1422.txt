I read the drawing as a methyl acrylate (dehydroamino acid ester) carrying a Cbz-protected amine on the alpha carbon and a 3-cyanoindol-5-yl group on the beta carbon.

- **Ester:** a methyl ester (OMe on a C=O) sits on the alpha carbon.
- **Carbamate:** the alpha carbon also carries an NH linked to a benzyloxycarbonyl group, NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–phenyl.
- **Double bond:** the alpha carbon is double-bonded to a CH carbon, which is bonded to C5 of the indole.
- **Indole:** the ring has a free N–H and a nitrile (CN) at C3.
- **Geometry:** the double bond is drawn vertically. On the alpha carbon the ester points left and the NH points right. On the beta carbon the indole points right. The NH and the indole are therefore on the same side (Z). In the SMILES this puts the ester trans to the aryl group.
