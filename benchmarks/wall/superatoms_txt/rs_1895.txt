I read the molecule as methyl 2-(benzyloxycarbonylamino)-3-(3-cyano-7-methyl-1H-indol-5-yl)acrylate (a Cbz-protected dehydroamino acid ester).

- **Ester and carbamate:** the left side is a methyl ester, COC(=O)–. The α-carbon carries an NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–phenyl (Cbz) group.
- **Alkene:** the α-carbon is double-bonded to a CH that is attached to the indole at C5.
- **Indole:** the numbering is N1(H), C2(H), C3(C≡N), C3a, C4(H), C5 (vinyl attachment), C6(H), C7(CH3), C7a.
- **Geometry:** in the drawing, the NH on the α-carbon and the indole on the β-carbon sit on the same side of the double bond. NH outranks the ester carbon on the α-carbon, and the indole outranks H on the β-carbon, so the alkene is Z. In SMILES this means the ester and the indole are trans, written `C(=O)/C(...)=C/Ar`.

SMILES: COC(=O)/C(NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1)=C/c1cc(C)c2[nH]cc(C#N)c2c1
