The drawing is a PROTAC-type molecule, so I read it in four parts and joined them.

**Quinazoline warhead.** The aniline is 3-chloro-4-fluorophenyl, attached through NH to C4 of the quinazoline. C6 carries an acrylamide, NHC(=O)C=C. C7 carries an O-CH2CH2CH2 chain.

**Linker.** The propyl chain ends on the nitrogen of a piperidine. Piperidine C4 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated: C(=O)CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-NH.

**E3 ligand.** The terminal NH is bonded to C4 of a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione). The imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. This carbon has plain bonds in the drawing, so I gave it no stereo.

**Assembly.** Quinazoline ring: c1(Nc2ccc(F)c(Cl)c2)ncnc2cc(OCCCN...)c(NC(=O)C=C)cc12.
