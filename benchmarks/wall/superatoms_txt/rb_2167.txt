**Main peptide chain** (from the Lys side, reading toward the ring):

- Sarcosine (N-methylglycine)
- 3-pyridylalanine
- Asn
- Glu (free side-chain acid)
- 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid (a quaternary THP residue)
- 2-naphthylalanine
- Tyr with its phenol O-alkylated as OCH2CH2NH2

**Macrocycle.** Penicillamine (Pen) is C(CH3)2 attached to S, so the two Pen residues are joined by a disulfide. The ring runs Pen – Lys(ε-NHAc) – 7-methyl-Trp – Thr – Asn – Pen′. Pen′ carries an N-terminal Ac-Lys(ε-NMe3⁺).

**Judgement calls:**
- The diacid is taken as 16 CH2 plus the two carbonyl carbons (the chain was not counted vertex by vertex).
- The trimethylated Lys side chain is taken as 4 CH2 between the α-carbon and N⁺.
- All stereocentres are written as the L-amino-acid configuration. This includes the Nal, Pen and Pen′ residues, where the hashed bonds were hard to read.
