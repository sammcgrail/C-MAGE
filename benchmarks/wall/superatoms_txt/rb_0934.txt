I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule with a VHL-binding end, a hexyl linker, and an aminopyridazine (hydroxyphenyl) end. I wrote it in sections, left to right.

- **VHL end:** a 1-fluorocyclopropane carboxamide on the tert-leucine nitrogen. Its carbonyl links to a 4-hydroxyproline. The proline carboxamide goes through NH–CH2 to a benzene ring bearing a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group and an ether oxygen.
- **Linker:** the O–(CH2)6–N chain runs from the benzene to a piperidine nitrogen. I counted six carbons by tracing the vertices of the drawn chain.
- **Middle:** the piperidine C4 is attached to C5 of a pyrimidine. Pyrimidine C2 is attached to N8 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane.
- **Far end:** N3 of the bicycle is attached to C4 of a pyridazine. That pyridazine carries NH2 at C3 and a 2-hydroxyphenyl at C6.

For stereochemistry, I took the tert-leucine and hydroxyproline centres to have the usual VHL-ligand configuration (S at tert-leucine; the 4-hydroxyproline as in the common VHL ligand). I assigned @/@@ from that convention and the wedges and hashes shown, adjusting the tert-leucine tag for my atom ordering. The bicyclic bridgeheads are drawn with plain bonds, so they get no stereo marks.
