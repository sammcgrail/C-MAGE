I read the molecule as a capped tetrapeptide linked through a self-immolative benzyl ether to a spiro-adamantyl dioxetane (a Schaap-type caged chemiluminescent probe).

**Fragments, left to right**
- The N-terminus is an acetyl group on the amine of a 2-indanylglycine residue.
- Next is 4-chlorophenylalanine.
- Next is lysine, with a free terminal NH2 on its four-carbon side chain.
- Next is leucine, whose C-terminus forms an anilide with a para-substituted aniline.
- The aniline's para CH2 is bonded to an aryl ether oxygen.
- On that aryl ring, the carbon bearing the ether oxygen is flanked by a chlorine-bearing carbon and by the carbon carrying the (E)-CH=CH–CO2Me group.
- Next to the chlorine-bearing carbon is the dioxetane carbon, which also carries OMe. The dioxetane is spiro-fused to adamantane at its 2-position.

**Stereochemistry**
- I assigned the four peptide α-carbons from the wedges and hashes.
- Indanylglycine and lysine have the side chain on a wedge, with N on the left and C=O on the right. Both are S.
- 4-Chlorophenylalanine and leucine have the side chain on a hash, with N on the upper left or left and C=O on the right. Both are S, the natural L configuration.
- The acrylate double bond is drawn trans, so I wrote it as E.
- The dioxetane carbon bearing OMe and the aryl ring is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.

I checked the adamantane SMILES by counting atoms: 10 carbons, 4 bridgehead CH and 6 CH2 including the spiro carbon.
