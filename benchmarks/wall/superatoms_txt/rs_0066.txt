I read the molecule as a 4-sulfonyl indane-2-carboxylic acid whose sulfonyl group is attached to the nitrogen of a 2,6-dimethylpiperazine.

- **Left fragment:** an indane (benzene fused to a five-membered ring) with a carboxylic acid on C2 of the five-membered ring. The CH carrying the acid is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
- **Linker:** an SO2 group joins the aromatic carbon next to the ring fusion (position 4) to piperazine N1.
- **Piperazine:** the two carbons flanking N1 each carry a methyl, and both methyls are drawn as wedges. This gives the cis (meso) arrangement.
- **Right fragment:** N4 of the piperazine is attached through a CH2 to a phenyl ring. That ring carries F at the 3-position and CF3 at the 4-position.

I assigned the two wedge centres by working out the chirality from the drawn geometry: the top carbon came out as `[C@@H]` and the bottom carbon as `[C@H]`, in the neighbour order used in the SMILES below.

SMILES: OC(=O)C1Cc2cccc(c2C1)S(=O)(=O)N1[C@@H](C)CN(Cc2ccc(C(F)(F)F)c(F)c2)C[C@H]1C
