This reads as two copies of the same tropane ester (the tropic-acid ester of N-methyl-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octan-3-ol, as in atropine/hyoscyamine) plus one sulfuric acid, written as a salt.

**Each ester unit**
- The acyl part is Ph–CH(CH2OH)–C(=O)–O–, with Ph expanded to c1ccccc1. The CH(Ph) carbon has no wedge, so it carries no stereo mark.
- The alcohol part is a tropane bicycle. Two bridgehead CH carbons are joined by an N-methyl bridge. One side is a two-carbon bridge (CH2–CH2). The other side is a three-carbon bridge (CH2–CH(O-ester)–CH2).
- The two bridgehead hydrogens are drawn as wedges, so both bridgeheads get stereo marks. I assigned them from the wedge geometry as `[C@H]2` for the top bridgehead and `[C@@H]1` for the bottom one. The ester-bearing carbon is drawn plain, so it has no stereo mark.

**The acid**
- The "HO–SO3H" label is sulfuric acid, which I wrote as OS(=O)(=O)O.

The three components are separated by dots.

SMILES: CN1[C@H]2CC[C@@H]1CC(OC(=O)C(CO)c1ccccc1)C2.CN1[C@H]2CC[C@@H]1CC(OC(=O)C(CO)c1ccccc1)C2.OS(=O)(=O)O
