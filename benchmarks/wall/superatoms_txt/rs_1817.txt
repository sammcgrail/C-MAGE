The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- The ring nitrogen sits at the bottom left.
- The carbon next to the nitrogen carries the carboxamide, C(=O)NH.
- The next carbon carries OH (position 3).
- The next carbon carries SMe (position 4).
- The remaining two carbons are plain CH.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a cyclohexyl ring at C1. Going around that ring, C1 is followed by CH2, then a quaternary carbon with two methyls (C3), then CH2 (C4), then a second quaternary carbon with two methyls (C5), then CH2 (C6), which closes back to C1. This is a 3,3,5,5-tetramethylcyclohexyl group.
