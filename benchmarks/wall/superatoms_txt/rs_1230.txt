I read the molecule as a pyrrolidin-2-one whose ring nitrogen carries a 4-carboxyphenethyl group, and whose C5 carries a trans-alkenyl side chain.

**Ring and N-substituent**
- The lactam ring is five-membered: N, C=O, two CH2 groups, and the substituted CH.
- The N-substituent is N–CH2CH2–C6H4–CO2H, with the carboxyl para to the ethyl link.

**Side chain on the substituted ring carbon**
- It runs CH=CH (drawn trans), then CH(OH), then CH2CH2CH2, then CH(CH3)2. I counted three CH2 groups between the carbinol carbon and the isopropyl CH.

**Stereochemistry**
- The ring carbon has a bold wedge to the vinyl group. Its priorities are N > vinyl > ring CH2 > H. With H pointing back, N → vinyl → CH2 runs clockwise, so this centre is R.
- The carbinol carbon has a hashed bond to OH. Its priorities are OH > vinyl > CH2 > H. With H pointing toward the viewer, OH → vinyl → CH2 runs clockwise, which reverses to S.
- The double bond is drawn trans, so it is written as `/C=C/`.
- Translating to SMILES gives `[C@H]` for the R ring carbon and `[C@@H]` for the S carbinol carbon, in the atom orders written below.
