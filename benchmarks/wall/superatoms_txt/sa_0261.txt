This is the gadoversetamide-type ligand (a DTPA bis(methoxyethylamide)) drawn with a separate Gd3+ ion.

Reading the structure from left to right:
- MeO–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)–CH2–N(CH2COO−)–CH2CH2–N(CH2COO−)–CH2CH2–N(CH2COO−)–CH2–C(=O)–NH–CH2CH2–OMe.
- The three carboxylates are on the first, middle and last nitrogens, so there are three COO− groups in total.
- The gadolinium is drawn as a separate species, written [Gd+3]. The image labels it "Gd3+·" with a radical dot, but I treat it as a plain ion.

Charge check: three carboxylates give −3 and Gd gives +3, so the complex is neutral overall.
