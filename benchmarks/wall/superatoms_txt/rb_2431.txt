This is my reading of the drawing as a macrocyclic HCV NS3 protease inhibitor-type structure. I'm unsure of two things: the chain length in the macrocycle and some of the stereodescriptors.

**Fragments read from the image**
- **Aryl ether:** a 7-methoxy-3-(trifluoromethyl)quinoxalin-2-yl group attached through oxygen to C4 of the pyrrolidine.
- **Proline core:** the pyrrolidine nitrogen is acylated by the C=O of the amino-acid unit, and its C2 carries a C(=O)NH amide.
- **Carbamate:** the amino acid's NH is part of an O-(1-cyclopropylethyl) carbamate.
- **Macrocycle:** the amino acid α-carbon continues through a chain of CH2 groups to a cis CH=CH. That alkene attaches to a cyclopropane carbon, and the adjacent quaternary cyclopropane carbon bears the NH that closes the ring onto the proline amide.
- **Sulfonamide:** the quaternary cyclopropane carbon also carries C(=O)NH–SO2–(1-methylcyclopropyl).

**Uncertain points**
- I counted six CH2 groups in the macrocycle chain, but the drawing is hard to count there.
- I assigned the proline centres from the usual L-proline and trans-4-substituted pattern (2S,4R) and checked the SMILES descriptors against that.
- The α-carbon is assigned as S, an L-amino acid, on the same basis.
- The cyclopropane centres and the cyclopropylethyl carbon are my best guess rather than a clear reading of the wedges.
