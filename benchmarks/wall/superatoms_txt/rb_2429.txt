I read the structure as a macrocycle built from these pieces:

- **Urea tail:** an isobutyl(methyl)urea, CC(C)CN(C)C(=O)N–, sits on the (S) alpha carbon.
- **Thiazole:** the alpha carbon's CH2 links to C2 of a thiazole. Thiazole C4 is bonded to C5 of the indole.
- **Indole:** the indole N carries an ethyl group. C2 bears a pyridin-3-yl ring, and the pyridine C2 carries the (S)-CH(CH3)OMe group.
- **Linker:** indole C3 carries CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2–O, which forms the ester with the piperazic acid carbonyl.
- **Piperazic acid:** the (S) ring carbon bears the ester carbonyl. The ring N1 is acylated by the C(=O) attached to the alpha carbon, which closes the macrocycle.

I assigned each stereocentre from its CIP priorities and then chose the SMILES chirality tag that gives S in each case:

- Alpha carbon, priorities N > C(=O) > CH2 > H: [C@H].
- Piperazic C3, priorities N > C(=O)O > CH2 > H: [C@@H].
- Methoxyethyl carbon, priorities O > pyridyl C > CH3 > H: [C@H].
