The drawing shows a piperazine ring. One ring nitrogen carries a CHO (formyl) group. The other ring nitrogen is bonded to a central carbon, C(=N–CH3)(NH–CH3), which is an amidine/guanidine-type carbon.

Reading the substituents on that central carbon:
- A double bond to N, which carries a CH3 group (N–CH3).
- A single bond to NH, which carries a methyl group (the plain line at the far left).
- A single bond to the piperazine nitrogen.

The ring is N1–CH2–CH2–N4–CH2–CH2, closing back to N1.

SMILES: CNC(=NC)N1CCN(C=O)CC1
