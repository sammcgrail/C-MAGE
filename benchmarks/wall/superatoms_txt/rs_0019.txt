The structure is a xanthone joined to a chromone (flavone-type) unit, so I read it as two ring systems and then connected them.

**Xanthone part**
- The left ring of the xanthone carries, from the top: an acetyl group (C(=O)Me) next to the ring oxygen, a methyl group, and the bond to the chromone.
- Going around this ring: C4 bears the acetyl, C3 bears the methyl, C2 bears the chromone, C1 is the CH, then C9a, C9 (the C=O), and C4a, which is bonded to the ring O.
- The right ring carries a COOH, two OH groups and a CH. Starting at the carbon next to C9 (C8a): C8 has COOH, C7 has OH, C6 has OH, C5 is CH, and C4b is bonded to the O.

**Chromone part**
- C2 is attached to the xanthone and to the ring O1.
- C3 is a CH and C4 is the C=O.
- C4a is the fusion carbon next to C4, and C5 bears COOMe.
- C6 is a CH, C7 bears OH, C8 is a CH, and C8a is bonded to O1.

**SMILES assembly**
- Left xanthone ring: c1(C(C)=O) c(C) c(–chromone) c c2 c1 O c3 c c(O) c(O) c(C(=O)O) c3 C2=O.
- Written out: CC(=O)c1c(C)c(cc2c1Oc1cc(O)c(O)c(C(=O)O)c1C2=O)-c1cc(=O)c2c(C(=O)OC)cc(O)cc2o1.

Ring closures check out: in the xanthone, ring 1 opens at the acetyl-bearing carbon and closes at c1 (C4a) after "c2c1". In "c2c1Oc1cc(O)c(O)c(C(=O)O)c1C2=O", the label 2 opens at C9a and closes at C9 (C2=O). The new ring label 1 is reused for the right ring, which is allowed because the first ring 1 has already closed. In the chromone, c1cc(=O)c2c(C(=O)OC)cc(O)cc2o1 closes properly.
