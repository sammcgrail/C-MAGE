The drawing shows a central silicon atom with four substituents: tBu (tert-butyl), two Ph (phenyl) groups, and Cl. This is tert-butyldiphenylchlorosilane.

Expanding the abbreviations gives tert-butyl as C(C)(C)C and each phenyl as c1ccccc1. The bonds are all plain, so there is no stereochemistry to include.
