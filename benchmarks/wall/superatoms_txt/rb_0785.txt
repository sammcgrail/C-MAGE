I read the molecule as a 6‑chloro‑oxindole spiro‑fused to a 5,5‑dimethyl‑4‑phenylpyrrolidine‑2‑carboxamide. That amide is attached to a trans‑1,4‑cyclohexane, whose other end carries a carboxamide to a PEG3 chain. The chain ends in an NH on the 4‑position of a lenalidomide‑type isoindolinone–glutarimide.

**Stereochemistry**
- Spiro carbon: the bond to the aryl carbon is hashed and the bond to the oxindole C=O is wedged. This gives `@` in the atom order aryl C, C=O, CH(Ph), CMe2.
- Pyrrolidine C2: the bond to the amide carbon is hashed, which gives `@@`.
- Pyrrolidine C4: the phenyl bond is wedged, which gives `@@`.
- Cyclohexane: one amide bond is wedged and the other hashed, so the ring is trans‑1,4. This is written `[C@H]…[C@H]`, and it is not a true stereocentre pair.
- The glutarimide CH is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.

I worked out the `@`/`@@` assignments by hand from the wedge geometry, so they are the part most worth double-checking.
