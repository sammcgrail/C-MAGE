The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl isatin.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H and is attached at the meta position to the second ring. On the second ring, the carbon bonded to the first ring sits next to a carbon bearing OH. The next carbon along bears the HN group. The ring therefore has the biaryl bond at C1, OH at C2 and NH at C3.

**Hydrazone linker.** The chain is Ar–NH–N=C3, where C3 is the 3-position of the indolinone.

**Indolinone part.** C3 is double-bonded to N and bonded to C2 (C=O) and to the fused benzene ring. C2 is bonded to N1, which carries a 3,4-dichlorophenyl group.

**Dichlorophenyl ring.** The ring is attached to N at C1. Going round the ring, Cl is at C3 (the bottom-right Cl) and at C4 (the bottom Cl).

**Stereochemistry.** The drawing does not give clear geometry for the C=N bond, so I include none.
