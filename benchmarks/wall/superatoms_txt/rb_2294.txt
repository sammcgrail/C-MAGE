**Residues, N-terminus to C-terminus:**
- **Fmoc:** the fluorenylmethoxycarbonyl group on the Arg α-nitrogen.
- **Arg(Pbf):** the guanidine nitrogen carries the Pbf sulfonyl group. Its ring has 2,2-dimethyl-dihydrobenzofuran fused to a trimethyl-substituted benzene.
- **Ser(tBu):** the serine side chain is an O-tert-butyl ether.
- **Leu:** an isobutyl side chain.
- **Pro:** a plain pyrrolidine ring.
- **Oic:** octahydroindole-2-carboxylic acid. I derived its three stereocentres from the wedges and hashes. The ring-fusion hydrogens are both hashed, so the ring fusion is cis, and the C2 carbonyl is a wedge.
- **Ile:** isoleucine, with natural stereochemistry assumed.
- **Cys(Trt):** the cysteine sulfur carries a trityl group (S–C(Ph)₃).
- **Ile (C-terminal):** isoleucine, esterified to the resin bead. I wrote the bead as a `*` atom.

For the Arg, Ser, Leu, Pro, Ile and Cys centres I assumed natural L-configuration rather than reading each wedge individually.
