I read the molecule as a macrocyclic bis-benzimidazole with the following parts:

- **Two identical benzimidazole units.** Each carries a 5-carboxamide (C(N)=O), a 7-alkoxy group, and a 2-NH–C(=O) amide to a 1-ethyl-3-methylpyrazol-5-yl ring.
- **Butene linker.** The two benzimidazole N1 atoms are joined by CH2–CH=CH–CH2. The two CH2 groups sit on opposite sides of the double bond in the drawing, so I assigned E (trans).
- **Central bridge.** Each 7-O is attached to a CH2, and both CH2 groups meet at one CH. That CH is bonded to the pyrrolidine nitrogen, which closes the macrocycle.
- **Pyrrolidine.** Its C3 carries O–P(=O)(OtBu)2, a di-tert-butyl phosphate. The wedge on the C–O bond makes this carbon S. The other stereocentre, the central CH, is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.

For C3, the priorities are O > C2 (attached to N) > C4 > H. With H pointing back, O → C2 → C4 runs counterclockwise, which is S. In the SMILES this corresponds to `[C@@H]` for the order written.
