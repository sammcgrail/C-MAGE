The drawing shows three parts joined by two amide bonds.

**Biphenyl part.** The top-left ring carries SO2NH2 at the ortho position and is bonded to a para-substituted phenyl ring. That second ring ends in a C(=O)NH amide.

**Central benzene ring.** The ring atoms, numbered from the amide-bearing carbon, are:
- C1: bears the NH of the biphenyl amide.
- C2: bears the C(=O)NH-pyridyl group; it is adjacent to C1 and sits at the bottom left of the ring.
- C3: the CH at the bottom right.
- C4: the CH at the right.
- C5: bears NHSO2Me, at the upper right.
- C6: the CH at the top.

So the NHSO2Me group is para to the C(=O)N carbon and meta to the NH amide carbon.

**Pyridine part.** The amide NH is attached to C2 of the pyridine. The ring nitrogen is next to C2, and the Br sits at position 5, so the pyridine is 5-bromopyridin-2-yl.
