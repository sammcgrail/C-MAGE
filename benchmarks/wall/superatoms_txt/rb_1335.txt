I read the molecule as three parts joined by a polyether linker.

- **Left end:** a 5-methyl-5H-pyrido[4,3-b]indole (γ-carboline). Its 7-position is bonded to the 5-position of a pyridine ring that carries an oxygen at C2.
- **Linker:** from the pyridine oxygen, the chain is O–(CH2)5–O–(CH2)5–O. I counted five carbons in each zigzag segment from the bond spacing. The chain ends on a cyclobutane ring.
- **Cyclobutane:** it is 1,3-disubstituted. One oxygen is on a hashed bond and the other on a bold wedge, so the two substituents are on opposite faces (trans). I assigned the trans centres by matching the pattern of the trans-1,4-disubstituted cyclohexane SMILES I remembered, rather than deriving it atom by atom.
- **Right end:** the second cyclobutane oxygen is attached to C5 of a phthalimide, whose nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
