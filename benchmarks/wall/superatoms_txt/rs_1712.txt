**Central ring.** This benzene ring has the NH (the amide from the left fragment) at one carbon. The adjacent carbon bears a C(=O)NH-pyridyl group. The OMe sits para to the NH, which I checked by counting positions:
- Take C1 as the carbon bonded to NH, at the lower left.
- C2 is the carbon bearing the carboxamide, at the lower right.
- C3 is the upper right carbon, a CH.
- C4 is the top carbon, bearing OMe.
- C5 and C6 are the upper left and left CH carbons.

C4 is para to C1, so OMe is para to the NH and meta to the carboxamide carbon.
