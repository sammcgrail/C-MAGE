I read the molecule as a BTK-targeting PROTAC: an ibrutinib-like warhead joined through a linker to a lenalidomide-type cereblon ligand. I worked through it region by region.

- **Warhead (left):** a 4-amino-pyrazolo[3,4-d]pyrimidine bearing a 4-phenoxyphenyl group at C3. N1 is bonded to the 4-position of a piperidine ring.
- **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen carries a straight pentyl chain (five CH2, counted from the zigzag vertices). This is followed by O–CH2CH2–O, which attaches to the isoindolinone.
- **Cereblon ligand (right):** an isoindolin-1-one with the ether oxygen at C5. Its lactam nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a piperidine-2,6-dione (glutarimide).
- **Stereochemistry:** the glutarimide C3 is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it unspecified.
