I read the molecule as a VHL-type hydroxyproline ligand joined through a seven-carbon amido linker to a piperazinyl-pyridinyl-imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine unit that carries a 2-(3-fluorophenyl)pyrrolidine.

**Fragments, left to right:**
- A 3-fluorophenyl ring is attached to C2 of a pyrrolidine, and the pyrrolidine nitrogen is bonded to C6 of an imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine.
- C3 of the imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine is bonded to C2 of a pyridine, whose C6 carries a piperazine nitrogen.
- The other piperazine nitrogen bears CH2–C(=O)–NH, followed by six CH2 groups (counted vertex by vertex along the zigzag), then C(=O)–NH.
- That NH joins a tert-leucine residue, which is acylated onto a 4-hydroxyproline nitrogen. The proline carboxamide goes to a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamine.

**Stereochemistry:**
- The aryl-bearing pyrrolidine carbon has a hashed aryl bond. Applying the CIP priorities (N > aryl C > ring CH2 > H) to the drawn geometry gives R.
- The tert-leucine carbon is S.
- The proline carbons are 2S and 4R, the usual VHL-ligand configuration. I assigned the proline and tert-leucine centres by matching the standard VH032 stereo-SMILES and re-deriving the @/@@ marks for my atom ordering, rather than from an independent CIP analysis of the wedges.
