I read the drawing as a spiro bicyclic core carrying a methacryloyl urea side chain and two TBS ethers.

**Rings**
- Five-membered cyclic sulfonate ring: S(=O)(=O)–O–C(spiro)–C(NH2)=CH–back to S.
- Five-membered tetrahydrofuran ring sharing the spiro carbon: C(spiro)–CH(OTBS)–CH(N)–O–CH(CH2OTBS)–back to C(spiro).

**Substituents**
- The CH(N) carbon of the furan ring carries an NH–C(=O)–NH–C(=O)–C(=CH2)CH3 chain (a methacryloyl urea).
- The CH(OTBS) carbon carries OTBS.
- The CH(CH2OTBS) carbon carries CH2–OTBS.
- Each TBS is expanded to [Si](C)(C)C(C)(C)C.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned the four stereocentres from the wedge and hash bonds:
- The CH(N) carbon has a hashed bond to N.
- The CH(OTBS) carbon has a wedged bond to O.
- The CH(CH2OTBS) carbon has a hashed bond to CH2.
- The spiro carbon has a wedged bond to the ring O.
