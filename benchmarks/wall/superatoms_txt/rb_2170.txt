I read the structure as a symmetric molecule built around a central ethylenediamine bis-amide, with a dodecyl chain at each of the four outer amide nitrogens.

- **Dodecyl chains:** Each zigzag chain has 12 vertices, counted at roughly 52 px spacing from the free end to the carbon bonded to the amide NH. That gives four dodecyl groups.
- **Central unit:** The core is NH–CH2CH2–NH, with each NH acylated by a C(=O)CH2CH2 group.
- **Each half of the molecule:** The propanoyl CH2CH2 is bonded to an N-methyl tertiary amine. That nitrogen is joined through a CH2CH2 link to a second tertiary nitrogen. This second nitrogen carries two CH2C(=O)NH-dodecyl arms.
- **Stereochemistry:** None is drawn, so none is specified.
