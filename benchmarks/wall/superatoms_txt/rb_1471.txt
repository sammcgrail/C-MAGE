I read the structure in three parts: the roflumilast-like head group, the PEG-type linker, and the lenalidomide end.

**Head group.** A 3,5-dichloropyridin-4-yl ring is attached through NH to a C(=O). That carbonyl sits on a benzene ring carrying OCHF2 at the para position and the linker oxygen at the meta position.

**Linker.** Starting from the aryl oxygen, the chain is O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2CH2-NH. Reading the drawing:
- Aryl O, then CH2CH2, then O.
- CH2CH2, then O.
- CH2CH2, then O.
- Then three CH2 units (the zigzag from the last O to the NH), then NH.

That gives three ethylene units between four oxygens, followed by a propyl group.

**Lenalidomide end.** The NH is bonded to the isoindolinone at position 4. The isoindolinone N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, and the ring carbonyl is C1. The stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so I give no stereochemistry.
