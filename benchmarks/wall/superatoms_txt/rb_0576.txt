Reading the structure region by region:

**Aryl group.** The benzene ring carries a CH2N(CH3)2 group, with a methoxy group on each side of it (2,6-dimethoxy relative to the CH2NMe2). The ring is attached to the thienopyridinone at its para position.

**Thieno[3,2-c]pyridin-4-one core.**
- The thiophene has S bonded to C2 (which bears the carboxamide) and to C7a.
- C2=C3, and C3 is bonded to C3a.
- C3a is bonded to C4, the C=O carbon, which is bonded to N5 (N-methyl).
- N5 is bonded to C6H, and C6=C7.
- C7 carries the aryl group and is bonded to C7a, which is bonded to S.

SMILES for the core: c1(C(=O)N...)cc2c(s1)c(Ar)cn(C)c2=O

**Amide side chain.** The amide NH is bonded to CH2, which is bonded to a cyclobutane carbon. The cyclobutane is spiro-fused at its opposite carbon to a piperidine, giving 7-azaspiro[3.5]nonane. The cyclobutane carbon bearing the CH2 is C2, and C1 and C3 are the CH2 groups beside the spiro carbon.
- Fragment: NCC1CC2(C1)CCN(CC2)...
- The piperidine N is bonded to a propyl chain (CCC), which ends at a piperazine N.
- The other piperazine N is bonded to the aromatic carbon of the isoindoline-1,3-dione (position 5).
- The isoindoline N is bonded to the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. No stereochemistry is drawn.
