I read the molecule as a 5-amino-thiazolo[4,5-d]pyrimidin-2-one core with these substituents:

- **Position 7:** a tosyloxy group (p-methylphenyl-SO2-O–) on the pyrimidine carbon next to the ring sulfur side.
- **Position 5:** a free NH2 on the pyrimidine carbon between the two ring nitrogens.
- **Position 3 (thiazolone N):** a ribofuranosyl group carrying acetates on the 2′, 3′ and 5′ oxygens.

For stereochemistry, the base is attached by a wedge bond, and the 2′ and 3′ OAc groups are drawn with hashed bonds. The 5′ CH2OAc is drawn with a wedge. Together these match a β-D-ribofuranose, so I used the same chirality pattern as adenosine (`[C@@H]1O[C@H](CO)[C@@H](O)[C@H]1O`), with the N written before C1′ as in that pattern.
