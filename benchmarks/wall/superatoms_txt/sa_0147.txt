The drawing shows a central sulfur bonded to a CF3 group, two doubly bonded oxygens, and an "OTf" group.

"OTf" is the triflate group, which is O–S(=O)(=O)–CF3. So the molecule is triflic anhydride, CF3SO2–O–SO2CF3.

The drawing is therefore an abbreviated form of the anhydride, with one triflyl group written out and the other collapsed to Tf.

Expanding the abbreviation gives:

FC(F)(F)S(=O)(=O)OS(=O)(=O)C(F)(F)F

This has a C–S bond to CF3, two S=O bonds, and an S–O–S linkage to the second triflyl group.

SMILES: FC(F)(F)S(=O)(=O)OS(=O)(=O)C(F)(F)F
