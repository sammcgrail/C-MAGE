**Cyclohexane ring:** Starting from the CH bearing NH (C1), going up gives CH2 (C2), then C(CH3)2 (C3), then CH–O (C4), then C(CH3)2 (C5), then CH2 (C6), and back to C1. The two gem-dimethyl carbons are therefore both adjacent to the CH–O carbon.

**Ester:** O–C(=O)–CH2–CH(CH3)2, which is an isovalerate group.
