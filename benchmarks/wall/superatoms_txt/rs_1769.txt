The drawing shows a quinoline core with three substituents.

- **C4:** an NH group linked to a pyridin-4-yl ring. The pyridine nitrogen sits at the top right, para to the carbon bearing the NH.
- **C3:** a fluorine.
- **C2:** a 2-carbamoylphenyl ring, i.e. a benzene ring carrying CONH2 on the carbon adjacent to the point of attachment.
