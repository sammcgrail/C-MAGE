I read the molecule as a thalidomide-type isoindolinone linked through a long alkynyl amide chain and a sulfonamide to a pyrimidine-substituted piperidine.

- **Left end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) ring sits on the nitrogen of a 1-oxoisoindoline. The alkyne is on the aromatic carbon next to the CH2-bearing ring carbon (position 4).
- **Chain:** from the alkyne I counted two sp carbons, then eight CH2 groups, then the amide C=O. That makes an undec-10-ynamide. The chain count is the least certain part of my reading, since it rests on the zigzag spacing in the drawing.
- **Linker:** the amide N–H is followed by CH2CH2–SO2, and the sulfonyl group is bonded to the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Right end:** the piperidine carries an NH at C4, bonded to C2 of a pyrimidine. The pyrimidine has Br at C5 and, at C4, an NH bonded to a 1,3-benzodioxol-4-yl group.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I included no stereochemistry.
