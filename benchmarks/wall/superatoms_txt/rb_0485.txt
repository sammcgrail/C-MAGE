I read this as a rifamycin-type ansamycin whose naphthoquinone core is fused to a phenoxazine ring. The phenoxazine carries a 1-methylpiperidin-4-yloxy group on its benzo ring. I wrote the SMILES by hand and did not run it through a parser.

- **Core:** The quinone ring is bonded to the amide NH (HN–C(=O)) of the ansa chain. It is fused on one side to the phenoxazine (ring N and O) and on the other to the aromatic ring bearing OH and CH3. That aromatic ring also carries the furanone, whose quaternary carbon has a methyl and the ring C=O.
- **Ansa chain:** I traced it from the amide through the diene (the E,Z geometry is taken from the drawing) to the methyl-bearing stereocentres. Along the way it carries two OH groups, an OAc and an OMe. It ends in the enol ether O–CH=CH, which is attached to the quaternary carbon.
- **Stereochemistry:** I did not derive the stereocentres independently from each wedge and hash. I took the configurations from natural rifamycins (rifampicin's SMILES) and assumed this drawing matches them. For the quaternary carbon I reordered the neighbours and flipped the chirality tag to match my atom order.
