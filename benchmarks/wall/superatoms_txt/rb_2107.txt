I read the drawing as a linear peptide with one disulfide bridge, written N-terminus to C-terminus.

**Sequence as drawn:** Ac-Ile-Cys-Val-Trp(1-Me)-Gln-Asp-Trp-Gly-Ala-His-Arg-Cys-Thr, followed by a linker and a lysine amide.

- **N-terminus:** acetylated isoleucine.
- **Disulfide:** the two Cys side chains are joined S–S. In the SMILES this is a ring closure between the two sulfurs.
- **Methylated Trp:** the first Trp has a methyl on the indole nitrogen (N1). The second Trp has a free indole N–H.
- **Other side chains:** Gln (amide side chain), Asp (free carboxylic acid), His (imidazole), and Arg (guanidine).
- **C-terminal side:** the Thr carbonyl is joined to –NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)– (a short ether-chain linker), which is joined to the α-amine of a lysine. The lysine ends as a primary amide, –C(=O)NH2.
- **Lysine side chain:** the ε-nitrogen carries a wavy bond to a carbon with two short stubs. I read this as an isopropyl-like group, –NH–CH(CH3)2. It may really be an attachment point to something not shown, so this part is uncertain.
- **Stereochemistry:** I assumed all residues are the natural L-amino acids (L-Ile, L-Thr and so on) and wrote the stereocentres to match, without checking each wedge or hash in the drawing against that assumption.
