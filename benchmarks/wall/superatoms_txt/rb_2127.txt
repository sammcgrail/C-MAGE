I read this as a DOTA-capped, cyclised peptide. The sequence, from the DOTA end, is DOTA-Cys-Thr-Glu-Cys-Trp-Val-Asp-Gly-Trp-Val-Pro-Cys-NH2. The three cysteine sulfurs are joined through CH2 groups to a 1,3,5-trisubstituted benzene core. The DOTA macrocycle is a 12-membered ring with four N atoms, three CH2CO2H arms and one CH2C(=O)– arm linking to the first cysteine.

I wrote all stereocentres as the L-amino-acid configuration, with Thr as (2S,3R). Some wedge and hash assignments in the drawing, especially the cysteines, were hard to read with certainty, so those are assumed to be L.

I checked the SMILES by hand for ring closure and for the Thr and Pro chirality. I did not run it through a parser.
