The SMILES below is my reading of the drawing, built region by region. Two points are less certain than the rest: the chain length, which I counted from the zigzag spacing, and the hydroxyproline stereocentres, which I took to be the standard VHL-ligand configuration rather than deriving from the wedges.

**Left side**
- A phenyl ring carries an ortho CH2–NH–CH3 group and is bonded to C4 of a thiophene.
- The thiophene is 2,4-disubstituted. Its C2 carries a CH(CH3) group that links through NH to the quinazoline.
- The methyl on that CH is drawn on a bold wedge. Assigning CIP priorities (N > thienyl > CH3 > H) gives R.

**Quinazoline core**
- C4 carries the NH, C2 carries a methyl, C6 and C7 each carry OMe, and C5 is CH.
- C8 carries the chain.

**Linker**
- The chain runs from C8 as eight CH2 groups followed by a C(=O), giving a nonanamide.
- The amide nitrogen is attached to the alpha carbon of tert-leucine.
- That alpha carbon has a wedged tert-butyl group, which gives S (the L-amino acid).

**Right side**
- The tert-leucine C=O acylates the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxyproline, which I took as the (2S,4R) standard VHL configuration.
- The proline C2 carboxamide NH is bonded to CH2 of a para-substituted benzene.
- The benzene's other end carries a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.
