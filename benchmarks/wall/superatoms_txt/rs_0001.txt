The drawing shows a benzene ring with two adjacent CN groups at the right-hand positions. Each of the two ring carbons next to them carries an OTos group, one at the top and one at the bottom. The two remaining ring carbons on the left are CH.

Tos is a tosyl group, 4-methylphenylsulfonyl: S(=O)(=O)c1ccc(C)cc1. So each OTos is an O–S(=O)(=O)–C6H4–CH3 unit.

The ring is c1(OTos)c(C#N)c(C#N)c(OTos)cc1, which gives 3,6-bis(tosyloxy)phthalonitrile.

SMILES: Cc1ccc(cc1)S(=O)(=O)Oc1ccc(OS(=O)(=O)c2ccc(C)cc2)c(C#N)c1C#N
