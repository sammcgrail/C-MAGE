The drawing shows Boc-protected leucine.

- The central carbon carries CO2H (up), NH–Boc (lower left) and an isobutyl group (lower right, hashed bond), plus an implicit H.
- Boc expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C, and iBu is CH2CH(CH3)2.
- For the stereochemistry, the hashed isobutyl puts the implicit H toward the viewer. The priorities are N > CO2H > iBu > H. Reading N → CO2H → iBu in the drawing is clockwise, and reversing for the H pointing toward the viewer gives counterclockwise, so the centre is S. This is the L-leucine configuration.

SMILES: CC(C)C[C@H](NC(=O)OC(C)(C)C)C(=O)O
