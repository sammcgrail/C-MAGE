I read the molecule as a 2,5-disubstituted pyrazine joined through an ethylene linker to a 3-mesyloxy pyrrolidine.

**Pyrazine ring.** The ring has two nitrogens para to each other. One ring carbon carries OMe (bottom left). The carbon on the opposite side carries the CH2CH2 chain (upper right). This gives `COc1cnc(CC...)cn1`.

**Linker.** Two CH2 groups join the pyrazine to the pyrrolidine nitrogen.

**Pyrrolidine.** The five-membered ring is N plus four carbons. The carbon at the upper right (C3) carries OMs, which is OS(=O)(=O)C, on a wedge bond. The wedge puts the implicit H at the back.

**Stereochemistry at C3.**
- CIP priorities are OMs > C2 (the CH2 bonded to N) > C4 > H.
- Viewed from the front with H at the back, the path O → C2 → C4 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S.
- In the SMILES `N2CC[C@H](OS(C)(=O)=O)C2`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is C4, H, O, C2. The `@` symbol corresponds to S for that ordering.

SMILES: COc1cnc(CCN2CC[C@H](OS(C)(=O)=O)C2)cn1
