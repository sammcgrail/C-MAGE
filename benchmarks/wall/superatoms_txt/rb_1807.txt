- **Linker:** Ar–CH2CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)–O–. This is three carbons, then O, two carbons, O, two carbons, then NH.
- **Carbamate:** its oxygen is attached to C8 of the decalin-type ring system.
- **Bicyclic core:** two fused six-membered rings.
