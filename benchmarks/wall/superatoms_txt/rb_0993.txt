The SMILES below was read region by region from the drawing.

- **Pyrazine core:** the carboxamide sits on one carbon. The adjacent carbon carries the NH link to the pyrazole. The carbon on the opposite side of the ring carries the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Stereo-substituted piperidine:** it is attached to the pyrazine through its nitrogen. Its 3-position carries a 3-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl group on a hashed bond, so this is the only stereocentre I assigned. Treating the hashed nitrogen as pointing away, which puts the implicit H toward the viewer, gives `@@` in the SMILES atom order used.
- **Pyrazole:** it is 4-amino substituted, and its N1 is bonded to the 4-position of a second piperidine.
- **Linker:** that piperidine's nitrogen is attached through a CH2 to the 3-position of an azetidine. The azetidine nitrogen is bonded to the 5-position of the isoindoline-1,3-dione.
- **Glutarimide:** the isoindoline nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. Its stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
