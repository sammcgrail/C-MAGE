The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an isatin-derived oxindole.

**Left ring (benzoic acid).** The ring carries CO2H on one carbon, F on the adjacent carbon, and the biaryl bond to the second ring on the carbon meta to CO2H. Numbering from the CO2H carbon: C1 has CO2H, C2 has F, C3 and C4 are CH, C5 is CH, and C6 is the CH next to C1 that is attached to the biaryl carbon. In the drawing, CO2H sits on the top carbon, F on the upper-left carbon, and the biaryl bond on the upper-right carbon. This gives 2-fluoro-5-aryl benzoic acid.

**Middle ring.** It is attached to the left ring at one carbon. The adjacent carbon bears OH, and the next carbon bears the NH–N= group. The remaining three carbons are CH.

**Oxindole.**
- C3 is the hydrazone carbon (C=N).
- C2 is the carbonyl (C=O).
- N1 carries the 3,4-dimethylphenyl group.
- The fused benzene ring has F at C4 and CF3 at C6.
