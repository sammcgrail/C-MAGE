Reading the structure region by region:

**Pyridine ring.** N is at the bottom. Going around the ring from N: C2 (bearing the C(=O)NH group), C3 (bearing the ester O), C4 (bearing OMe), C5 (CH), C6 (CH), back to N.

**C3 substituent.** C3 carries O–C(=O)–CH2–CH2–OMe, a 3-methoxypropanoate ester.

**C2 substituent.** C2 carries C(=O)NH–CH(COOMe)–CH2–O–C(=O)–CH2CH2–Ph. The O-acyl group is 3-phenylpropanoyl.

**SMILES assembly.** The pyridine ring is written as c1(C(=O)N...)nccc(OC)c1OC(=O)CCOC. The amide side chain is NC(COC(=O)CCc2ccccc2)C(=O)OC.

The stereocentre at the CH(COOMe) carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is assigned.
