Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Acid end:** a para-disubstituted benzene ring carries CO2H.
- **Linker:** an ethylene chain (CH2CH2) joins the ring to the nitrogen of a pyrrolidin-2-one (a five-membered lactam with a C=O next to N).
- **Ring substituent:** the lactam carbon next to N (C5) bears a CH=CH group. The double bond is drawn trans, so it is E. This ring carbon has plain bonds, so I assigned it no stereo.
- **Side chain:** the CH=CH leads to a CH2, then to a CH(OH) carbon, then to a quaternary cyclobutane carbon that also carries an ethyl group.

**Stereochemistry at CH(OH):** the OH is on a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priorities are OH > cyclobutyl quaternary C > CH2 > H. In the drawing the OH is up, the quaternary C is lower right and the CH2 is left, which runs clockwise as seen from the front. Because the H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is S. In the SMILES below, `[C@H]` with the neighbour order CH2, H, O, Cq gives S.

SMILES: OC(=O)c1ccc(CCN2C(=O)CCC2/C=C/C[C@H](O)C2(CC)CCC2)cc1
