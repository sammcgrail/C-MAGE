I read this as a cyclosporin A derivative, and I took the stereochemistry from natural cyclosporin A rather than deriving each centre from the wedges and hashes. The drawn Ala wedges and hashes do agree with L-Ala followed by D-Ala, but I did not check the other centres bond by bond.

**Macrocycle.** The ring is the cyclosporin A undecapeptide: Abu, Sar, MeLeu, Val, MeLeu, Ala, D-Ala, MeLeu, MeLeu, MeVal, and the modified MeBmt residue closing back onto Abu.

**MeBmt side chain.** The usual crotyl-type end (the CH2–CH=CH–CH3 unit) is replaced. The chain is now CH(CH3)–CH2CH2CH2–C(=O)–NH, with the hydroxyl on the neighbouring carbon as in cyclosporin A.

**Linker and tail.**
- The amide continues through an ethylenediamine unit, –NH–CH2CH2–NH–, to a benzamide.
- The benzene ring carries a 5-(trifluoromethyl)pyrazol-1-yl group at the para position.
- The pyrazole C4 carboxamide is attached to a 3-chloro-5-acrylamidophenyl ring.

The macrocycle stereo tags were re-derived for the linear writing order used. L-residues are written N[C@@H](R)C(=O) and D-Ala as N[C@H](C)C(=O).
