I read the molecule as a macrocyclic peptide built from three residues, closed through a pyridine–S–benzene (diaryl sulfide) linker.

**Peptide part**
- The first residue is Dab (2,4-diaminobutyric acid). Its alpha carbon carries a CH2CH2NH2 side chain and is drawn with a wedge. Its alpha-NH is joined to a CH2 on the pyridine ring.
- The second residue is Lys. Its alpha carbon is wedged to the (CH2)4NH2 chain. Its alpha-NH is joined to the Dab carbonyl.
- The third residue is N-methyl-Trp. Its nitrogen carries a methyl group and is joined to the Lys carbonyl. The alpha carbon is wedged to CH2 and then to the indol-3-yl group. Its carbonyl is joined to an NH, which leads to a benzylic CH2.

**Aromatic linker**
- The pyridine has N1 and C2 bonded to S. C3 bears the CH2NH that connects to the Dab nitrogen.
- The benzene ring has six substituted positions in order. C1 is bonded to S. C2 bears the CH2NH that connects to the Trp carbonyl. C3 bears a 4-carboxyphenyl group. C4 and C5 are CH. C6 bears Cl and sits next to C1.

**Stereochemistry**
Working out CIP priorities at each wedged centre gives S for Dab, Lys and Trp, matching the natural L-amino acids. I set @@ for the Dab carbon and @ for the Lys and Trp carbons to encode this.
