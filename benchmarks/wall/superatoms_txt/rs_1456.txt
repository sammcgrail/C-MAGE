The drawing shows a thiophene ring with S at the left. Going around the ring, the top carbon bears OH, the next carbon carries the C(=O)NH group, and the remaining carbon is CH next to S.

Reading the thiophene atoms: S1, C2 (H, the upper-left carbon), C3 (OH, upper right), C4 (carboxamide, lower right), C5 (H, lower left). So it is a 4-hydroxythiophene-3-carboxamide.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring. The other para position carries an O linking to a second benzene ring, which has CF3 at the meta position.

The SMILES is: O=C(Nc1ccc(Oc2cccc(C(F)(F)F)c2)cc1)c1cscc1O

Check of the thiophene: c1cscc1O starts at C4 (attached to the carbonyl), then c (C5, H), s, c (C2, H), then c1 (C3) closing the ring with the OH. The ring is therefore C4–C5–S–C2–C3, which matches the drawing.
