The drawing matches telaprevir. I read it as a pyrazine-2-carboxamide on cyclohexylglycine, then tert-butylglycine, then a cis-fused bicyclic proline (octahydrocyclopenta[b]pyrrole). That is followed by a norvaline-derived α-ketoamide ending in an N-cyclopropylamide.

I checked one stereocentre against the drawing directly. The cyclohexylglycine carbon has a wedged NH and plain bonds to the cyclohexyl and carbonyl carbons, which gives S, and the SMILES below encodes that. I did not re-derive the remaining stereocentres from the wedges. They are taken from the known telaprevir stereochemistry, which I am recalling from memory.
