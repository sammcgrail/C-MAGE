I read the structure as a methyl ester chain on one side of a cyclopentanone ring and a hydroxy-dimethyl-alkenyl chain on the other.

- **Ester chain:** MeO2C–CH=CH–(CH2)4– is attached to a ring carbon by a wedge bond. The C=C is trans, because the ester carbon and the CH2 lie on opposite sides of the double bond.
- **Ring:** a cyclopentanone with five carbons in order: the carbon bearing the ester chain (wedge), the C=O carbon, a CH2, a CH–OH (wedge OH), and a carbon bearing the side chain (hashed bond).
- **Side chain:** –CH=CH–CH(OH)–C(CH3)2–nBu. The C=C is trans, and the OH on the carbinol carbon is drawn with a wedge. The nBu expands to CCCC.

I assigned @/@@ at each of the four stereocentres from the wedge/hash geometry and the implicit hydrogen position, but I did not check the result with a toolkit.
