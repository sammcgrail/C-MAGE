I read the molecule as a macrocyclic peptide and wrote the SMILES by following the ring from the lysine residue around to the diaminobutyric acid residue.

- **Lysine residue:** the α-carbon carries a wedged 4-aminobutyl chain, and the C-terminal carbonyl is bonded to an N-methyl nitrogen.
- **N-methyl tryptophan residue:** the α-carbon carries a wedged CH2 to an indol-3-yl group, and its carbonyl links through NH–CH2 to the fluorinated benzene ring.
- **Fluorinated benzene ring:** going round the ring, the CH2-bearing carbon sits between the S-bearing carbon and the carbon bearing the pyridone. F is on the carbon next to the S-bearing carbon, and two CH carbons complete the ring.
- **Pyridone and pyridine:** the pyridone is attached at its 4-position, with N1 carrying an (E)-CH=CH–COOH group. A thioether S links the benzene ring to C2 of the pyridine, and the pyridine C3 carries CH2–NH.

**Stereochemistry:** from the wedge directions, all three α-carbons come out as S (L-configuration). The SMILES patterns match standard L-amino-acid SMILES. The enamide-type double bond is drawn trans, so I wrote it as E.
