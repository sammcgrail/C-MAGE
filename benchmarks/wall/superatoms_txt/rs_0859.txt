2. **Middle ring:** a para-substituted phenyl ring, with the biphenyl bond on one side and C(=O)NH on the other.
3. **Central ring:** the amide NH is attached to a benzene ring. Going around that ring:
   - C1 bears the NH of the biphenylcarboxamide.
   - C2 is the CH next to C1.
   - C3 bears NH2 (upper right).
   - C4 and C5 are CH.
   - C6 bears the C(=O)NH-pyridyl group (lower left).
