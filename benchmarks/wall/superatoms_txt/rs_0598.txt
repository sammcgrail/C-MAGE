Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Core:** a cyclopenta[b]indole, i.e. an indole whose C2–C3 bond is fused to a five-membered carbocycle.
- **Indole nitrogen:** carries a 4-chlorobenzyl group (CH2 linked to a para-chlorophenyl ring).
- **Benzo ring:** bromine on the carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the nitrogen, and fluorine on the carbon two positions further around the ring.
- **Cyclopentane ring:** the carbon adjacent to the N-bearing indole carbon carries a CH2CO2H group on a hashed bond.

Stereochemistry: the hashed CH2CO2H points away from the viewer, so the implicit hydrogen points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are aromatic ring carbon > CH2CO2H > ring CH2 > H. In the drawing these three run counterclockwise, and with H toward the viewer this reverses to clockwise, giving R. In the SMILES below, `[C@H]` with the neighbour order written corresponds to R.

SMILES: OC(=O)C[C@H]1CCc2c1n(Cc1ccc(Cl)cc1)c1c(Br)cc(F)cc21
