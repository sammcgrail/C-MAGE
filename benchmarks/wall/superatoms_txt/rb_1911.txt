**Main chain, C-terminus to N-terminus**
- Glycinamide carrying an N-methyl group, then 3-pyridylalanine, Asn and Glu.
- The Glu nitrogen is joined through a urea (NH–C(=O)–NH) to the carbonyl of a 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid unit.
