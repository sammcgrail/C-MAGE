- The top carbon bears NH2.
- Next is the ring N, on the right.
- Next is the fused carbon C7a, attached to the NH of the imidazole.
- Next is the fused carbon C3a, attached to the imidazole N.

**Imidazole ring.** The ring contains N= (left), C(CF3), NH (right), and the two fused carbons. The N=C(CF3) bond is double.
