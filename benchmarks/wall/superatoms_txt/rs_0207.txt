I read the molecule as a 1-benzylcyclobutyl carbamate of an amino ketone that carries a triphenylphosphorane ylide and a nitrile.

- **Left end:** a benzyl group (CH2–phenyl) sits on a cyclobutane carbon. That same carbon carries the carbamate oxygen, so the group is 1-benzylcyclobutyl–O–C(=O)–NH.
- **Stereocentre:** the NH-bearing carbon has a hashed bond to a butyl chain, so the butyl points away from the viewer. The implicit H points toward the viewer, with N at upper left and the ketone carbon at upper right.
- **Chain:** the stereocentre is attached to C(=O), then to a carbon that is double-bonded to PPh3 and carries C≡N.
- **Chirality assignment:** the CIP priorities are N > C(=O) > butyl > H. N → C(=O) → butyl runs clockwise on the page with H toward the viewer, so viewed from the side opposite H it runs counterclockwise, giving S. In the SMILES below, `N[C@@H](CCCC)C(=O)...` encodes S, so `@@` is correct.

SMILES: O=C(OC1(Cc2ccccc2)CCC1)N[C@@H](CCCC)C(=O)C(C#N)=P(c1ccccc1)(c1ccccc1)c1ccccc1
