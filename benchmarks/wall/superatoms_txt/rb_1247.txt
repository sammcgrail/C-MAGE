I read the structure as a PROTAC-like molecule built from three parts: an EGFR quinazoline warhead, a piperidine–piperazine linker, and a lenalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**Quinazoline (left).** The ring carries a 4-(3-chloro-4-fluoroanilino) group and a 7-methoxy group. The 6-position bears an NH that forms an amide with a trans (E) but-2-enoyl group.

**Linker.**
- The butenoyl chain is NC(=O)/C=C/CN, with the terminal N being the piperidine nitrogen.
- The piperidine is substituted at C4 by a piperazine nitrogen.
- The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated by a 6-aminohexanoyl chain.
- Counting the chain from the picture: C(=O), then five CH2 groups, then NH. After the carbonyl there are bends at 1180, 1230, 1290, 1350 and 1410, giving 5 CH2 before the NH at about 1465. That is C(=O)CCCCCN, i.e. a hexanoyl with 6 carbons including the carbonyl.

**Cereblon ligand (right).** The NH attaches at position 4 of an isoindolinone. The isoindolinone nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, and the stereocentre there is drawn with plain bonds, so I give no stereo.

**Assembly.**
- Quinazoline with substituents: COc1cc2ncnc(Nc3ccc(F)c(Cl)c3)c2cc1NC(=O)/C=C/CN4CCC(CC4)N5CCN(CC5)C(=O)CCCCCNc6cccc7C(=O)N(C8CCC(=O)NC8=O)Cc67
- In this SMILES, the quinazoline ring c2…c2 has C4 (the anilino carbon) next to the ring fusion carbon, which in turn is attached to the CH (C5) that neighbours C6 carrying the amide.
- For the isoindolinone, c6cccc7C(=O)N(...)Cc67 places the NH-bearing aromatic carbon adjacent to the carbon that bears the CH2, which matches the drawing.
