This SMILES was written by adapting the standard metal-free phthalocyanine SMILES, with each benzo ring replaced by a naphtho ring. I did not run it through a parser, so it is checked only by hand.

The drawing is a metal-free tetrabenzo-fused naphthalocyanine. It has four isoindole-type units joined by four bridging aza nitrogens, and each isoindole carries a linearly fused extra benzene ring, which makes four naphtho units. The two NH pyrrole nitrogens are the top-left and bottom-right units. The other two ring nitrogens, top-right and bottom-left, are unprotonated imine-type N atoms. No stereochemistry is drawn.

I checked the ring closures by hand. Each naphtho unit is written so that its two fusion carbons attach to the alpha carbons of the pyrrole ring. The Kekulé bond placement follows the standard H2Pc pattern and may differ in detail from the drawn double bonds, but the connectivity and hydrogen placement match.
