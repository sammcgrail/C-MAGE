The drawing is a chalcone. The left ring carries a para OMOM group, and the right ring is a 2-hydroxy-4,6-bis(OMOM)phenyl ketone.

MOM is methoxymethyl, CH2OCH3, so OMOM is OCOC (O–CH2–O–CH3).

The alkene is drawn with a trans (E) geometry: the left ring attaches at the upper carbon and the carbonyl sits at the lower-right carbon, on opposite sides of the double bond.

Right ring: the carbonyl-bearing carbon is flanked by C–OH (below) and C–OMOM (above). The other OMOM is para to the carbonyl attachment, at the lower right.
