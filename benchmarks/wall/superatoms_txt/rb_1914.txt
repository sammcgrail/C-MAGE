This is a cyclosporin A-type cyclic undecapeptide whose MeBmt side chain has been modified and linked to a lapatinib-like fragment. I read it in three parts.

**Macrocycle.** Tracing the ring from the modified MeBmt residue, the sequence is MeBmt–Abu–Sar–MeLeu–Val–MeLeu–Ala–D-Ala–MeLeu–MeLeu–MeVal. This matches cyclosporin A, so I took its stereochemistry from the known cyclosporin A SMILES rather than reading each wedge individually. That stereo assignment is therefore not checked against the drawing.

**Modified side chain.** In cyclosporin A the MeBmt side chain ends in CH2–CH=CH–CH3. Here it is CH(CH3)–CH2CH2CH2–C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–NH–CH2–, so the alkene is replaced by a saturated chain ending in an amide. That chain runs through an ethylenediamine linker to a furan at its 2-position.

**Lapatinib-type fragment.** The furan's 5-position is bonded to C6 of a quinazoline. Quinazoline C4 carries an NH–aryl group, where the aryl is 3-chloro-4-[(3-fluorobenzyl)oxy]phenyl.

I did not recount every atom of the macrocycle against the image, so the final SMILES has not been verified atom for atom.
