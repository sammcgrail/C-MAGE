The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an oxindole (isatin-type) core.

**Biphenyl part.** The top-left ring carries CO2H (a 3-substituted benzoic acid). It is bonded to a second ring. On that second ring, the carbon next to the biaryl bond bears OH, and the carbon after that bears the HN group. So the second ring is 2-hydroxy-3-(hydrazinyl) with the aryl at position 1.

**Hydrazone and oxindole.** HN–N= links to C3 of the oxindole. C2 is C=O and N1 carries a 3,4-dimethylphenyl group. The fused benzo ring carries a phenyl at C6. Here C7a is bonded to N, C7 follows, then C6 (bearing the phenyl), C5, C4, and C3a.

**Ring positions.** In the drawing, C3a is the upper-left fusion carbon and C4 is the top carbon. C5 is upper right, C6 is right (bearing the phenyl), and C7 is bottom. This matches the 6-phenyl assignment.
