The drawing is a PROTAC. A spiro-oxindole MDM2-type ligand is joined through a benzamide, a piperidine and a PEG-type amine linker to a thalidomide unit.

**Fragments, from left to right**
- **Oxindole:** a 6-chloro-2-oxindole, spiro-fused at C3 to a pyrrolidine.
- **Pyrrolidine:** its ring carbons carry the following.
  - C2 has the carboxamide.
  - C3 has a 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl group.
  - C4 is the spiro carbon shared with the oxindole.
  - C5 is a second spiro carbon that closes a cyclohexane ring.
  - The ring nitrogen is NH.
- **Amide link:** the carboxamide nitrogen is attached to a para-phenylene, whose other end carries a C(=O) bonded to a piperidine nitrogen.
- **Linker:** the piperidine 4-position bears an NH that continues as (CH2)3–O–(CH2)2–O–(CH2)2–O–(CH2)3–NH.
- **Thalidomide end:** that last NH is attached to C4 of the phthalimide ring. The phthalimide nitrogen bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, which is drawn without stereochemistry.

**Stereochemistry**
- Pyrrolidine C2 (hashed bond to the carboxamide): `[C@@H]`.
- Pyrrolidine C3 (wedged bond to the aryl group): `[C@H]`.
- Spiro C4 (hashed bond to the oxindole aromatic carbon, wedged bond to the oxindole C=O): `[C@]`.
- The cyclohexane spiro carbon is not a stereocentre, since its two ring arms are identical.

I assigned the @/@@ marks by working out the geometry from the wedge and hash bonds by hand, so they have not been checked with software.
