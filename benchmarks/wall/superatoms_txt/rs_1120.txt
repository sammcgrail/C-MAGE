The drawing shows a naphthalene ring system with a CHO group on the top-right ring. The CHO is attached at the carbon next to the ring fusion, which is position 1 of naphthalene.

The right ring has its top-left vertex at the fusion carbon (C8a), and the vertex to the right of it carries the CHO. That makes the substituted carbon C1, adjacent to the fusion carbon, so the molecule is 1-naphthaldehyde.
