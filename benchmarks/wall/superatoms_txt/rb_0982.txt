This SMILES was read from the drawing region by region; the cis cyclohexane assignment is the least certain part.

**Regions, left to right**
- **Phenol:** a 2-hydroxyphenyl ring is attached to the pyridazine.
- **Pyridazine:** the ring reads N=N–C(NH2)=C(pyrazolyl)–CH=C(aryl). The amino group sits next to a ring nitrogen, and the pyrazole is on the adjacent carbon.
- **Pyrazole:** it is linked through C4 to the pyridazine and through N1 to the piperidine C4. This gives `c1cnn(c1)` in the SMILES.
- **Piperidine:** it is substituted at C4 by the pyrazole N and at N1 by the cyclohexane.
- **Cyclohexane:** it carries the piperidine N on one carbon and a CH2–NH group on the carbon opposite (1,4). Both of these bonds are drawn as hashes.
- **Right-hand end:** the NH is bonded to C5 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. The imide N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, which has no stereo marking and so gets none.

**Stereochemistry:** with both hashed bonds pointing the same way on the ring, the 1,4-substituents are cis. For the SMILES pattern `N[C@H]1CC[C@@H](C...)CC1`, working through the geometry gives `@,@@` as cis, so I used that.
