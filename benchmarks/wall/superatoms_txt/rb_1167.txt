This is a PROTAC-type molecule: an EGFR inhibitor (a quinazoline with a crotonamide) joined through a linker to a thalidomide-type (pomalidomide) ligand.

**Left fragment.** The 3-chloro-4-fluoroanilino group sits on C4 of the quinazoline. The quinazoline carries a 7-methoxy group and, at C6, an NH-C(=O)-CH=CH-CH2 amide (drawn as E, trans).

**Linker.** The CH2 is attached to the nitrogen of a piperidine. The piperidine C4 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen, and the other piperazine nitrogen is acylated: C(=O)CH2CH2C(=O)NH-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-NH-.

**Right fragment.** The NH is attached to C4 of the isoindoline-1,3-dione, which carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on its nitrogen. The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo marking.

- Ring closures: the quinazoline ring labels 1 and 2 are reused, but the order in the string is valid. The piperidine and piperazine use label 3, which is closed before reuse. The aryl ring uses 3 and 4, and the glutarimide inside it uses 3 again while 3 is still open on the aryl ring.
