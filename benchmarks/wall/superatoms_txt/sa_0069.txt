The drawing shows N-Boc-proline. It has a five-membered pyrrolidine ring made of one N and four C atoms. The N carries a Boc group, and the carbon next to N carries a HO2C group on a hashed wedge.

Stereochemistry at C2:
- The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > ring C3 > H, and the implicit H points toward the viewer, since the CO2H bond is hashed.
- Viewed from the front, N (down) → CO2H (upper left) → C3 (upper right) runs clockwise.
- The H points toward the viewer, so the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This is the L-proline configuration.

The Boc group expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C. I wrote the SMILES from the standard Boc-L-Pro-OH form and recalled the `[C@H]` tag rather than deriving it independently.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N1CCC[C@H]1C(=O)O
