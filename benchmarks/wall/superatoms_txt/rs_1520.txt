Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Pyrazinone ring:** N1 carries the side chain, C2 is C=O, C3 bears the indoline nitrogen, N4 follows, C5 bears Cl, and C6 is CH.
- **Side chain on N1:** a stereocentre with ethyl, CH2–O–CH3, the ring nitrogen, and H on a hashed bond.
- **Indoline:** N1 is bonded to the pyrazinone C3 and runs N–CH2–CH2 into the benzene ring. On the benzene ring, C7 (next to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to N) carries Cl, and C5 carries OMe.

**Stereochemistry:** With H on the hashed bond pointing back, the ethyl, CH2OMe and N substituents run clockwise (ethyl → CH2O → N) when viewed from the front. The CIP priorities are N > CH2O > ethyl, which runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. Writing the SMILES from the ethyl carbon as `CC[C@@H](COC)n...` encodes this S configuration.
