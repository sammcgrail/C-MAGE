This is strontium ranelate: a thiophene ring carrying the substituents below, plus two Sr²⁺ counterions.

**Ring.** S is bonded to C2 (bearing COO⁻) and to C5 (bearing N). C2=C3, where C3 carries CH2COO⁻. C3–C4 is a single bond, and C4=C5. C4 carries the C#N group.

**Substituents.**
- C2: COO⁻
- C3: CH2COO⁻
- C4: C#N
- C5: N(CH2COO⁻)2

**SMILES.** Writing the ring starting from S: s1c(C(=O)[O-])c(CC(=O)[O-])c(C#N)c1N(CC(=O)[O-])CC(=O)[O-]

The charges balance: 4 carboxylates give −4, and 2 Sr²⁺ give +4.

SMILES: [Sr+2].[Sr+2].[O-]C(=O)CN(CC([O-])=O)c1sc(C([O-])=O)c(CC([O-])=O)c1C#N
