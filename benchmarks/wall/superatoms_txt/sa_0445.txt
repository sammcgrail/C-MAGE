The drawing is orphenadrine without its N-methyl group (tofenacin). It shows an ortho-methylphenyl ring attached to a CH carbon that carries a Ph label and an O. The O is linked through CH2CH2 to NHMe.

Reading the structure region by region:
- The ring is a benzene with a methyl on the carbon next to the one bearing the CH.
- The CH carbon has four substituents: the ring, Ph, O, and an implicit H.
- All bonds at that carbon are plain, so no stereochemistry is written.
- The chain runs from O to CH2, then CH2, then NH, then CH3.
