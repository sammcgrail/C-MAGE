I read the structure as a cyclic heptapeptide, H-Cpa-cyclo[D-Cys-Tyr-D-Phe(4-phenylureido)-Lys(Nε-Me)-Thr-Cys]-D-Tyr-NH2, closed by a disulfide between the two Cys residues. I wrote the SMILES in sequence order.

- **N-terminus:** 4-chlorophenylalanine (Cpa) with a free NH2, drawn as the S (L) configuration.
- **Ring:** D-Cys, Tyr, D-Phe carrying a 4-(phenylureido) group on the aryl ring, Lys with an N-methylated side-chain amine, Thr, then Cys. The two Cys sulfurs are joined as S–S.
- **C-terminus:** D-Tyr-NH2 attached to the ring Cys carbonyl.

Some wedges and hashes were hard to judge. For the D-Phe and the Thr I set the stereocentres from the residue names rather than from my reading of the wedges. For Thr I used the natural L-configuration (2S,3R). For D-Phe the drawing read as S, but I used the named D (R) form. The Cys, Tyr, Lys and D-Tyr centres agree with the names.
