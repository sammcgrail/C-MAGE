I read the drawing as N-Cbz-phenylalanine: a Cbz group on the nitrogen, an NH joined to the α-carbon, a CO2H group on that carbon, and a CH2 leading to a phenyl ring.

**Stereochemistry.** The only stereocentre is the α-carbon, and the wedge to CO2H shows it points toward the viewer. That leaves the implicit hydrogen pointing away from the viewer.

The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2Ph. On the page, N is at the upper left, CO2H at the lower left and CH2 on the right. Going N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise. With the hydrogen at the back, this is the S configuration, which is the L-amino-acid (Cbz-L-phenylalanine) arrangement.

I wrote the SMILES with the Cbz carbamate first, then the stereocentre. The `@@` tag matches the standard L-Phe pattern `N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)O`.
