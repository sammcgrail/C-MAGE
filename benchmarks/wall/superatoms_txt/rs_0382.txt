The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl oxindole (isatin 3-hydrazone).

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries HO2C. Its para position bonds to a second benzene ring. On that ring, the carbon next to the biaryl bond bears OH, and the next carbon bears the NH of the hydrazone. This gives 3'-hydrazinyl-2'-hydroxybiphenyl-4-carboxylic acid.

**Hydrazone and oxindole.** The chain is NH–N=C3, where C3 is the oxindole carbon. The oxindole has C2=O and N1 bearing a 4-ethylphenyl group. The fused benzo ring completes the indole core.

**SMILES.**
- Acid ring: OC(=O)c1ccc(cc1)
- Central ring: c2cccc(NN=C3...)c2O
- Oxindole: C3C(=O)N(c4ccc(CC)cc4)c5ccccc35

The C=N stereochemistry is not clearly specified, so I left it out.
