I read the molecule as a linear acyl-peptide tail attached to a thioether-bridged macrocyclic peptide. I could not confirm the stereochemistry from the drawing alone, so I assigned L-configuration to every α-carbon, including the Cys. I did not check each wedge and hash against that assumption.

**Tail (top left):**
- A 4-iodophenyl-butanoyl group is acylated onto the α-amine of Glu1.
- Glu1's side-chain carboxyl forms an amide with the α-amine of Glu2.
- Glu2's side-chain carboxyl forms an amide with the α-amine of a Lys that has a C-terminal primary amide.
- That Lys's ε-amine is acylated by the Cys carbonyl, which leads into the macrocycle.

**Macrocycle sequence**, starting from Cys and following the backbone:
1. Cys, whose sulfur is joined through CH2C(=O) to the amine of Phe(4-COOH). This is the thioether bridge.
2. Phe(4-COOH).
3. N-methyl-Lys.
4. Val.
5. Val.
6. Ser.
7. Asn.
8. Trp, alkylated on its indole nitrogen.
9. Phe(4-COOH).
10. Lys, with a free ε-amine.
11. 5-hydroxy-Trp.
12. Aib (the C(CH3)2 residue).
13. Val, whose carbonyl closes back onto the Cys α-nitrogen.

The N-methyl-Lys ε-amine is acylated by CH2C(=O), and that CH2 is attached to the indole nitrogen of the Trp. This forms the second ring, closed in the SMILES with ring label %20.
