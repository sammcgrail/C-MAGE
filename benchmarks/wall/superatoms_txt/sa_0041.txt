This is nimodipine, a 1,4-dihydropyridine, drawn with plain bonds, so no stereochemistry is included.

Reading the structure region by region:

- **Dihydropyridine ring:** an NH bonded to two ring carbons, each carrying a methyl group. These carbons are double-bonded to the two ring carbons that carry the ester groups. The sp3 C4 sits between those two carbons and carries the aryl group.
- **Left ester:** C(=O)OCCOC, which is a 2-methoxyethyl ester (MeO–CH2CH2–O–C(=O)–).
- **Right ester:** C(=O)OC(C)C, an isopropyl ester.
- **Aryl group:** a benzene ring attached at C4 with NO2 at the meta position. Going around the ring from the attachment carbon, the next carbon is unsubstituted (the upper-left vertex at 868,537 is CH), and the NO2 carbon is separated from the attachment carbon by one CH (the lower-right vertex at 1116,606). That makes it 3-nitrophenyl.
