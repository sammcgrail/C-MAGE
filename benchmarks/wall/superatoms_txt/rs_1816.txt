I read the molecule as a 5-substituted benzotriazole carrying a CH2–CH(NHCbz)–CO2Me side chain.

- **Ring:** the left-hand bicycle is a benzotriazole (benzene fused to a triazole with N, N, NH). The side chain is attached at the benzene carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon, giving the 5-substituted isomer.
- **Side chain:** the ring carbon is bonded to a CH2, then to the stereocentre. The stereocentre carries H, CO2Me and NHCbz. NHCbz is NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–Ph.
- **Stereochemistry:** the wedge is on the bond to H, so H points toward the viewer. The other three substituents run N (down), CO2Me (upper right) and CH2 (upper left). The priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H. Going N → CO2Me → CH2 appears counterclockwise from the front. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is R.
- **SMILES chirality tag:** for the SMILES written as `COC(=O)[C@@H](Cc1ccc2[nH]nnc2c1)NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is C(=O), H, CH2, N. Working through that order, `@` would give S, so `@@` is needed for R.
