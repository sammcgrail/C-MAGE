I read this as a hexanoyl-capped cyclic peptide with one disulfide bridge between two penicillamine residues. The sequence, from the N-terminus, is:

hexanoyl–Arg–Pen–Gln–Thr–Trp–Gln–Pen–Tyr(O-CH2CH2-NH2)–2-Nal–Thp–Glu–Asn–Thp–NH2

Here Pen is penicillamine (the two beta carbons each carry two methyls and one sulfur), 2-Nal is 2-naphthylalanine, and Thp is 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid.

- **Ring:** The disulfide joins the two Pen beta carbons, so the macrocycle runs from the first Pen through Gln, Thr, Trp and Gln to the second Pen.
- **Tail:** From the second Pen, the chain continues through Tyr(OCH2CH2NH2), 2-Nal, Thp, Glu (free carboxylic acid), Asn and a final Thp ending in a primary amide.
- **Stereochemistry:** I assigned the @/@@ marks from my reading of the wedges and hashes but did not check them against CIP rules, so some of them may be wrong. The two Thp residues are achiral and carry none.
