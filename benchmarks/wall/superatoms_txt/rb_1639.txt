I read the molecule as a sunitinib-type kinase-inhibitor head joined through a piperazine linker to a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand. I went through it region by region:

- **Left end:** a 5-fluoro-2-oxindole joined by an exocyclic C=C to a CH on a pyrrole. I wrote the double bond with the Z geometry that sunitinib has (the oxindole C=O and the pyrrole NH on the same side), which is how the drawing appears to show it.
- **Pyrrole:** the NH ring carries the CH= at C2, a methyl at C3, the carboxamide at C4 and a methyl at C5.
- **Linker:** the carboxamide N–H connects through a three-carbon chain (NCCC) to one nitrogen of a piperazine. The other piperazine nitrogen carries CH2–C(=O)–NH, followed by a seven-carbon chain (counted vertex by vertex along the zigzag).
- **Right end:** the seven-carbon chain attaches to the 4-position of a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione). The imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring. That ring's stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo label.
