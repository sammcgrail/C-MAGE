I read the molecule as an N-acetylated tetrapeptide linked through a 4-aminobenzyl ether to a chloro-aryl acrylic acid carrying an adamantylidene methoxy-1,2-dioxetane.

**Peptide chain, left to right:**
- Acetyl-NH on an indan-2-ylglycine residue. The indane carbon is not a stereocentre.
- 4-Chlorophenylalanine.
- Lysine, with the free NH2 at the end of its four-carbon chain.
- Leucine, whose C=O is bonded to the aniline NH of the 4-aminobenzyl group.

**Stereochemistry of the four α-carbons:** From the wedges and hashes and the CIP priorities, all four α-carbons come out S, the L-configuration.

**Aryl portion:**
- The benzyl CH2 is bonded through O to the central aromatic ring.
- Adjacent to that O-bearing carbon, going around the ring, are first the Cl-bearing carbon and then the dioxetane-bearing carbon.
- Two CH carbons follow, and the ring closes on the carbon carrying the acrylic acid, which is therefore next to the O-bearing carbon.
- The acrylic acid double bond is drawn trans (E).

**Dioxetane:** One dioxetane carbon carries OMe and the aryl ring. The other is the C2 spiro carbon of adamantane. I checked the adamantane ring closures in the SMILES by hand: four bridgehead carbons and six CH2 groups including the spiro carbon.
