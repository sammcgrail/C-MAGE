I read the drawing as 2-methyl-1,3-benzothiazole-6-carboxylic acid.

- **Thiazole ring:** a five-membered ring with S at the top and N at the bottom. The carbon between them (C2) carries a methyl group on the left. The S–C2 bond is single and the C2=N bond is double.
- **Benzene ring:** fused on the right of the thiazole. The carbon bonded to S is C7a and the carbon bonded to N is C3a.
- **Carboxylic acid:** COOH sits on the upper-right carbon of the benzene ring. Going around the ring from C7a, that is C7a, C7, then C6. This carbon is para to C3a, the N-bearing carbon, so it is position 6.

Checking the SMILES atom by atom: the ring atoms after the thiazole N run C3a, C4, C5, C6 (bearing COOH), C7, C7a, then S, which matches the drawing.
