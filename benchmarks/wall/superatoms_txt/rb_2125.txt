I read the structure as a symmetric dimer built around a hexa-aza cage, with a cyclic RGD-type peptide at each end. I did not run the SMILES through a parser, so validity is checked by hand only.

**Cyclic peptide (each end).** The ring is cyclo(Asp–Phe–Lys–Arg–Gly), closed through backbone amides. The Lys side chain (four CH2) is acylated by a 3-maleimidopropanoyl group. That maleimide is attached to the thiol of a Cys-NH2 as a thiosuccinimide.

**Linker to the cage (each end).**
- The Cys α-NH is acylated by a 6-aminohexanoyl unit, and its amine nitrogen is acylated by the Tyr carbonyl.
- The Tyr α-NH is acylated by a glutaryl group.
- The other end of the glutaryl group forms an amide with the NH on a cage bridgehead carbon.

**Cage.** Two bridgehead carbons, each bearing an NH amide. They are joined by three CH2–NH–CH2CH2–NH–CH2 bridges.

**Stereochemistry.** I assumed L-Arg, L-Asp, L-Tyr and L-Cys, and D-Phe, as in the usual cyclo(RGDfK) peptide. I did not verify these against the individual wedges in the drawing. The Lys α-carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it unspecified, as I did for the thiosuccinimide carbons.
