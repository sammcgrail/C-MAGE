I read the structure in three parts and joined them through the linker.

- **Left end:** a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl) on the nitrogen of an isoindolin-1-one. The isoindolinone carries a fluorine on the benzene ring and, on the carbon next to the CH2-bearing ring-fusion carbon, a piperidin-4-yl group.
- **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen is attached to a straight chain of 9 CH2 groups ending in an ether oxygen. I counted the zigzag vertices from the piperidine nitrogen to the oxygen and got 9 carbons.
- **Right end:** the ether oxygen is on a phenyl ring that also has an ortho fluorine and an ortho link to the quinazoline. The quinazoline has Cl at C8, the aryl group at C7, F at C6, and a 4-propanoylpiperazin-1-yl group at C4.
- **Stereochemistry:** the bold wedge in the drawing sits on a ring bond of the quinazoline (C8–C7). It looks like an atropisomer axis marker rather than a stereocentre, so I left stereochemistry out.
