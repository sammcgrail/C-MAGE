I read the molecule as a spiro[cyclohexane-pyrrolidine-oxindole] MDM2-type ligand joined through a 4-aminobenzamide and a propylenediamine linker to a pomalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

- **Linker:** the isoindoline 4-NH is connected through three CH2 groups to an amide NH. That amide C(=O) is attached to a para-substituted benzene.
- **MDM2 end:** the other para position of that benzene carries an NH–C(=O) group on the pyrrolidine C5. The pyrrolidine ring runs NH, C2, C3, C4, C5.

  - C3 is spiro to a 6-chloro-oxindole (Cl on the carbon meta to the ring-fusion carbon bearing N).

- **Stereochemistry:** I assigned C5 and C4 from the drawn wedge and hash bonds. At the spiro C3 the oxindole bonds are ambiguous, so I took only the wedge to the lactam C=O as the stereo-defining bond.
