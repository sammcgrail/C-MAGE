The drawing shows N-tosyl phenylalanine: a Ph–CH2 group, a central stereocentre bearing NH–Ts, and a hashed bond to CO2H.

**Stereocentre.** The CO2H bond is hashed (pointing away), so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2Ph > H. Reading N (right), then CO2H (lower left), then CH2 (upper left) runs clockwise on the page. With H toward the viewer, that reverses to counterclockwise, so the centre is S. This matches the L-phenylalanine configuration.
