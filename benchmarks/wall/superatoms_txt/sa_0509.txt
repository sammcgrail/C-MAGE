I read the structure as a ruxolitinib-type intermediate carrying an N-SEM protecting group.

- **Stereocentre:** it bears a cyclopentyl ring, a CH2CHO arm drawn with a wedge, and the pyrazole N1. Its CIP priorities are N > cyclopentyl > CH2CHO > H. With H pointing back, the sequence N → cyclopentyl → CH2CHO runs clockwise, so the centre is R. Written from the CH2 side, that corresponds to `[C@H]`.
- **Pyrazole:** it is attached through N1 to the stereocentre and through C4 to the bicyclic core.
- **Bicyclic core:** a pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidine (7-deazapurine). Its C4 is bonded to the pyrazole, and its pyrrole nitrogen carries the SEM group.
- **SEM:** expanded to CH2–O–CH2CH2–Si(CH3)3.

SMILES: O=CC[C@H](C1CCCC1)n1cc(-c2ncnc3c2ccn3COCC[Si](C)(C)C)cn1
