The molecule is a spiro compound with a xanthene core and a phthalide (isobenzofuranone) ring joined at one spiro carbon.

- **Xanthene part:** the two outer benzene rings each carry an acetoxy group (OAc) and a phenyl group. The acetoxy sits next to the ring carbon that is bonded to the xanthene oxygen, and the phenyl sits on the adjacent carbon.

The spiro carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but the drawing uses only plain bonds, so I have included no stereo marks.
