The SMILES describes a benzoxazol-2(3H)-one core carrying a CH2–CH(NH2)–CO2Me side chain on the benzene ring.

- **Ring:** the benzene is fused to an oxazolone. The ring O sits on the upper-left fusion carbon, and the NH sits on the lower-left fusion carbon. The C=O is on the carbon between O and NH.
- **Attachment point:** the CH2 is on the benzene carbon para to the N-bearing fusion carbon, which is position 6 of the benzoxazolone.
- **Side chain:** CH2 leads to a stereocentre bearing NH2, CO2Me and an H drawn on a bold wedge.
- **Stereochemistry:** the priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H. The drawing places N at the bottom, CO2Me at the right and CH2 at the left. Seen from the front, with the wedged H toward the viewer, N → CO2Me → CH2 runs counterclockwise. Because H points toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed (clockwise), so the centre is R. In the SMILES `COC(=O)[C@H](N)Cc1ccc2[nH]c(=O)oc2c1`, the `@` tag gives R.
