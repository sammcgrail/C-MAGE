I read the drawing as a head-to-side-chain cyclic hexapeptide. The ring is closed through the Glu side chain and a 1,4-diaminobutane linker, and the N-terminus carries a 1-naphthylacetyl cap.

**Connectivity, traced around the ring**
- The cap is 1-naphthyl–CH2–C(=O)–NH on the Glu α-nitrogen.
- The Glu side chain runs CH2CH2C(=O)–NH–(CH2)4–NH–C(=O) and ends on the Trp α-carbon.
- The Glu C=O links to the His NH, the His C=O to the 4-fluorophenylalanine NH, the Phe C=O to the Arg NH (guanidine side chain), and the Arg C=O to the Trp NH.
- The Trp C=O closes the ring through the putrescine NH.
- The indole is attached at C3, and the imidazole is attached through its 4-position.

**Stereochemistry**
For Glu, the wedge on the side chain with N up and C=O lower-left gives S, which is the L configuration. For Arg, the hashed side chain also gives S (L). I took the His, Phe and Trp centres as L as well, without checking each wedge. After adjusting for the atom order in the SMILES, every ring α-carbon is written `[C@H]`.
