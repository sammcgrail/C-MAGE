I read the drawing as a quaternary carbon with four substituents: two methyl groups (the vertical lines up and down), a CH2 leading to the BzO group on the left, and an NH carrying nPr on the right.

BzO is benzoyloxy, PhC(=O)O–. I took "Bz" to mean benzoyl rather than benzyl because that is the usual meaning of the abbreviation. The "O" in the label is then the ester oxygen attached to the CH2.

nPr is n-propyl, CH2CH2CH3.

Assembled, the molecule is the benzoate ester of 2-methyl-2-(propylamino)propan-1-ol: O=C(c1ccccc1)OCC(C)(C)NCCC

SMILES: CCCNC(C)(C)COC(=O)c1ccccc1
