The pyridine ring has N at the bottom left. The carbon next to N (C2) carries the carboxamide, C3 carries OH, and C4 carries OMe. C5 and C6 are CH.

The amide is C(=O)NH attached to a cycloheptene ring. I numbered that ring from the N-bearing carbon:
- C1 is the CH bonded to N.
- Going up, C2 is a CH2, then C3 is a quaternary carbon bearing two methyls (the gem-dimethyl at the top).
- C3 connects to C4=C5, a double bond on the right side of the ring.
- C6 is a CH with a methyl, at the lower right.
- C7 is a CH with a methyl, at the bottom, and it bonds back to C1.

That gives 7 ring atoms: C1, CH2, C(Me)2, CH=, =CH, CH(Me), CH(Me).
