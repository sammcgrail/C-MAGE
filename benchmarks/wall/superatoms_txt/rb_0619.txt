This is a VHL-ligand-based PROTAC carrying a chloroacetamide warhead. I read it in four parts and joined them.

- **VHL ligand:** a hydroxyproline carrying a 2-methoxy-4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzyl amide, with a tert-leucine on the proline nitrogen. I took the stereochemistry as the natural (2S,4R)-hydroxyproline and L-tert-leucine, which matches the wedges and hashes shown. I wrote the SMILES chirality tags from my memory of the standard VH032 SMILES and did not re-derive them from the drawing.
- **Linker:** the tert-leucine NH is acylated by a succinyl group (C(=O)CCC(=O)), which ends in an NH and a four-carbon chain (the count is approximate from the drawing). The chain ends in an ether oxygen.
- **Diaryl ether:** the chain oxygen is attached to a para-phenylene, which is joined through an ether oxygen to a second para-phenylene.
- **Warhead:** the second phenylene carries N-benzyl-N-(chloroacetyl)amine.
