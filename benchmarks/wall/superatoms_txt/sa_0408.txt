I read the structure as the (S)-configured aryl propanamide known as andarine.

**Fragments read from the drawing:**
- **Left ring:** a benzene ring bearing NH (the amide nitrogen) at one vertex. It has CF3 on the adjacent meta position and NO2 on the para position, so it is 4-nitro-3-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl.
- **Amide:** the ring NH is bonded to a C=O carbon, which is bonded to the quaternary carbon.
- **Quaternary carbon:** it carries OH, a hashed methyl, and a CH2 that leads to an aryl ether O.
- **Right ring:** a para-substituted benzene with the ether O on one end and NHAc (acetamido) on the other.

**Stereochemistry:**
- The CIP priorities at the stereocentre are OH > C(=O)N > CH2O > CH3.
- The methyl is hashed, so it points away from the viewer.
- From the front, OH (upper right) → C(=O) (lower left) → CH2 (lower right) runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S.
- Written as C[C@](O)(COAr)C(=O)N, the SMILES neighbour order from the methyl gives the same S assignment.

SMILES: C[C@](O)(COc1ccc(NC(C)=O)cc1)C(=O)Nc1ccc([N+](=O)[O-])c(C(F)(F)F)c1
