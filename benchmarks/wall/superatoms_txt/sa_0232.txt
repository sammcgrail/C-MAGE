I read the structure as an isavuconazole-type triazolium prodrug and wrote it out region by region.

- **Core:** a 2,5-difluorophenyl ring on a quaternary carbinol carbon (OH on a wedge). That carbon carries a CH2 to the triazole and a CH(CH3) (methyl on a wedge) bonded to C2 of a thiazole. The thiazole bears a 4-cyanophenyl group at C4.
- **Triazolium:** the triazole N1 bears the CH2 from the carbinol carbon. N4 is quaternised (N⁺) and carries a CH(CH3)–O–C(=O)– group.
- **Carbamate side:** that carbonyl is bonded to an N-methyl nitrogen on C2 of a pyridine. The pyridine C3 carries CH2–O–C(=O)–CH2–NHCH3, a sarcosine ester.
- **Stereochemistry:** I assigned the two wedged centres from the 2D geometry as follows.
  - The CH(CH3) carbon is `[C@@H]`, written with the methyl first, then H, thiazole and the carbinol carbon.
  - The carbinol carbon is `[C@]`, written with the CH carbon first, then OH, CH2 and aryl.
  - The CH(CH3)–O carbon on the triazolium N⁺ has plain bonds, so it gets no stereo.
