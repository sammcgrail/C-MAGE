I read this as a resin-bound decapeptide, written from the N-terminus:

Fmoc-Cys(Trt)-Ser(tBu)-Arg(Pmc)-Ser(tBu)-Leu-Pro-Oic-Ile-Cys(Trt)-Ile-O-resin

Here Oic is octahydroindole-2-carboxylic acid, Trt is trityl, Pmc is the 2,2,5,7,8-pentamethylchroman-6-sulfonyl group on the Arg side chain, and the resin bead is written as a bare `*` on the C-terminal ester oxygen.

I did not work out the stereocentres from the wedges and hashes one by one. I took the amino acids as the natural L-forms and Oic as the usual (2S,3aS,7aS) isomer, then checked each chirality tag against a reference SMILES for that residue.
