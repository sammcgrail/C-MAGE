I read the molecule as a 2-amino-benzothiazole joined through a tropane ether to a substituted isoxazole.

- **Benzothiazole core:** the benzene ring carries F and CO2H. It is fused to the thiazole ring, which has S and N and a C2 carbon.
- **Bicyclic amine:** the benzothiazole C2 is bonded to the nitrogen of an 8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octane (tropane). Its two bridgehead carbons carry hashed H atoms, both pointing away from the viewer.
- **Ether link:** the C3 carbon of the tropane bears an O–CH2 group. The drawing overlaps the bonds here, so the line from that carbon passes through the ring crossing to O.
- **Isoxazole:** the CH2 is attached to C4 of an isoxazole. C5 carries a cyclopropyl group, and C3 carries a 2-(trifluoromethoxy)phenyl group.

I assigned the stereo marks only to the two hashed bridgeheads. The C3 carbon of the tropane is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo mark.
