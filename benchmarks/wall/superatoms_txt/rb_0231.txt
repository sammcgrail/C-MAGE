This is a maleimidocaproyl‑Gly‑Gly‑L‑Phe‑Gly linker joined through an NH‑CH2‑O‑CH2‑C(=O)‑N(benzyl)‑CH2 spacer to a fluoro‑methyl camptothecin (exatecan‑type) core.

- **Maleimide end:** a maleimide ring on N, then five CH2 groups and a C(=O) (caproyl).
- **Peptide:** NH‑CH2‑C(=O) (Gly), NH‑CH2‑C(=O) (Gly), NH‑CH(CH2Ph)‑C(=O) (Phe), NH‑CH2‑C(=O) (Gly).
- **Phe stereocentre:** the wedged CH2Ph gives the L (S) configuration, written `N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)`.
- **Spacer:** the Gly carbonyl is followed by NH‑CH2‑O‑CH2‑C(=O)‑N(CH2Ph)‑CH2, which is attached at the camptothecin carbon next to the benzo ring.
- **Camptothecin core:** the benzo ring runs fusion carbon, CH, C‑CH3, C‑F, CH, fusion carbon bonded to the ring N. The pyridone and the lactone carry the quaternary C(Et)(OH).
- **Quaternary stereocentre:** the ethyl is hashed and the OH is wedged. By CIP priorities (OH > C(=O)O > ring C > Et, with Et at the back) this is S, the natural 20S configuration. My SMILES uses the same neighbour ordering as the known camptothecin SMILES, so `[C@@]` is correct.
