This is my reading of the drawing. It is a peptide-like chain of ten repeating units. Each unit is an amino-acid-derived CH(R) carbon, then CH2, then a sulfonylated nitrogen N(SO2R′), then CH2–C(=O) leading into the next unit.

The chain runs from an N-terminal acetyl group to a C-terminal primary amide. Reading left to right:

| Unit | Side chain on CH(R) | Sulfonyl group on N |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CH2CH2COOH (Glu-like), N-acetylated | SO2CH2CH2NH2 |
| 2 | (CH2)3NHC(=NH)NH2 (Arg-like) | SO2CH3 |
| 3 | Arg-like | SO2CH3 |
| 4 | isobutyl (Leu-like) | SO2CH2CH2NH2 |
| 5 | CH(OH)CH3 (Thr-like) | SO2CH2CH(CH3)2 |
| 6 | isobutyl | SO2CH2CH2NH2 |
| 7 | Arg-like | SO2CH2CH(CH3)2 |
| 8 | sec-butyl (Ile-like) | SO2CH3 |
| 9 | isobutyl | SO2CH2CH(CH3)2 |
| 10 | isobutyl | SO2CH2Ph, then CH2C(=O)NH2 |

For stereochemistry I assumed the L-amino-acid configuration at every CH(R) carbon, written N[C@@H](R)C…. The Thr and Ile side-chain centres follow L-Thr (2S,3R) and L-Ile. I did not check the individual wedges and hashes against this assumption.
