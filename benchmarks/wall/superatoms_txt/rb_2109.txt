For stereochemistry, I assumed every α-carbon has the L-amino-acid-like arrangement, as the bold wedges suggest. I did not check each wedge individually. The Thr and Ile β-carbons are written as in L-Thr and L-Ile.

I wrote each α-carbon as N[C@@H](R)CN, the same neighbour order as an L-amino acid N[C@@H](R)C(=O). Written from the α-carbon outward, the Thr β-carbon is [C@@H](C)O and the Ile β-carbon is [C@@H](C)CC.
