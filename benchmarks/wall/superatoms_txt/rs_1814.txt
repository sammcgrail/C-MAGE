The drawing shows a diaryl ether: a phenyl ring joined through O to the para position of a second benzene ring. That second ring carries a C(=O)NH amide. The amide nitrogen is attached to a CH2CH2 chain ending in NH–Boc.

Boc is expanded to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.

Reading the SMILES from left to right: the phenyl ring is c1ccccc1, then O, then the para-substituted ring c1ccc(cc1), then C(=O)NCCNC(=O)OC(C)(C)C.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)NCCNC(=O)c1ccc(Oc2ccccc2)cc1
