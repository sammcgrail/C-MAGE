I read the drawing in three parts and then fixed the stereocentre.

**Left tricycle.** A pyridazine ring is fused to a dihydropyrazine ring (bearing an NH and a CH2). That ring is fused in turn to a piperazine ring.
- The pyridazine carbon farthest from the fusion carries a 2-hydroxyphenyl group.
- The piperazine nitrogen farthest from the fusion is acylated by the central C=O.

**Linker and right side.**
- The central C=O links to a piperazine through its nitrogen.
- The other piperazine nitrogen carries CH2CH2NH, which is attached to the aromatic ring of the isoindoline-1,3-dione (phthalimide).
- The phthalimide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. Its stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.

**Stereocentre.** The only wedge is on the ring-fusion CH. Its neighbours are the CH2 next to NH (wedged, toward the viewer), the CH2 next to the acylated nitrogen, the ring nitrogen, and an implicit H pointing away. Viewed from the front, the order CH2(NH side) → CH2(N-acyl side) → ring N runs clockwise. In the SMILES neighbour order (CH2 on the NH side, H, ring N, CH2 on the N-acyl side), this corresponds to `@`.
