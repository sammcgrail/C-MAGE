**Ring with the nitrile.** The nitrile is drawn as "NC–" attached to a ring carbon, so it is a C#N group. That carbon sits para to the S-bearing carbon: the ring runs from the S-bearing carbon (779,453) to the top carbon (652,299), then (455,332), then the nitrile-bearing carbon (386,519), then (513,673), then the N-bearing carbon (709,640). Counting from the S-bearing carbon, it is three atoms away (779 → 652 → 455 → 386), so it is para to S. This is the usual 2-cyano position of a phenothiazine, as in cyamemazine.
