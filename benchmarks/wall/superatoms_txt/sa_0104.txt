The drawing shows Fmoc-protected alanine: an Fmoc group on the nitrogen, an NH, a central carbon bearing a methyl group, and a CO2H group on a wedge bond.

**Stereocentre.** The central carbon has N at lower left, CH3 straight up and CO2H on a wedge toward the viewer. The implicit hydrogen therefore points away from the viewer.
- CIP priorities: N > CO2H > CH3 > H.
- Viewed from the front, N (lower left) → CO2H (lower right) → CH3 (top) runs counterclockwise.
- With H at the back, counterclockwise means S, so this is the L-alanine configuration.

**SMILES.** The Fmoc group is the carbamate C(=O)OCH2 attached to the 9-fluorenyl system. L-Alanine written as C[C@H](N)C(=O)O is S, which matches the drawing.
