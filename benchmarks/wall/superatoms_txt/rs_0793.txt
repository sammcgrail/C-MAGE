The drawing is read in three parts.

**Left ring (fluorobenzoic acid).** The ring carries CO2H, then F on the adjacent carbon, and the biaryl bond to the middle ring. Numbering from the biaryl carbon, the ring is C1 (biaryl), C2 (CH), C3 (C–CO2H), C4 (C–F), C5 (CH), C6 (CH). So the acid sits meta to the biaryl bond and F sits para to it.

**Middle ring.** One carbon bears the biaryl bond, the adjacent carbon bears OH, and the next carbon bears the NH–N= hydrazone. The remaining three carbons are CH.

**Right part (indolinone).** The hydrazone N= is double-bonded to C3 of the indolin-2-one. C2 is C=O, and N1 carries a 3,4-dimethylphenyl group. The fused benzene ring carries CF3. From the drawing, C3 is attached to C3a, and C3a sits next to the CH at the top right. Going round the ring: C3a, C4 (CH), C5 (CH), C6 (C–CF3), C7 (CH), C7a (attached to N). So CF3 is at position 6.
