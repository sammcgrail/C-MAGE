The structure is a PROTAC-like molecule with a thieno[3,2-c]pyridinone core, a dimethoxyphenyl linker, a spiro piperidine-azetidine, and a lenalidomide-type isoindolinone with a glutarimide. I read it in four parts.

**Thienopyridinone core.** The 5-methyl-4-oxo-thieno[3,2-c]pyridine carries an N-methyl carboxamide at C2 and the aryl group at C7.
- Written as SMILES: CNC(=O)c1cc2c(s1)c(cn(C)c2=O)-Ar.
- Ring check: the thiophene is S–C2(carboxamide)=C3–C3a–C7a. C3a bears the C4 carbonyl, and C7a bears C7. The pyridone runs C4(=O)–N5(Me)–C6=C7–C7a.
- Full core: O=c1n(C)cc(-Ar)c2sc(C(=O)NC)cc12.

**Aryl group.** The benzene is attached to the core at C1. C3 and C5 carry OMe, and C4 carries the CH2 that links to the piperidine nitrogen.
- Written as SMILES: c1(OC)cc(core)cc(OC)c1CN.

**Spiro amine.** The piperidine nitrogen is bonded to the CH2. The piperidine's 4-position is a spiro carbon shared with an azetidine, giving 2,7-diazaspiro[3.5]nonane.
- Piperidine: N1CCC2(CC1)CN(C2)-Ar'.
- The azetidine nitrogen carries the isoindolinone aryl group.

**Isoindolinone.** The aryl ring is attached at position 5 or 6 of the isoindolinone.
- The lactam carbonyl carbon is bonded to the aromatic carbon at the top of the ring, and the azetidine N-bearing carbon sits meta to that carbonyl-bearing carbon.
- The aromatic ring atoms are: C7a (bears C=O), C7 (CH, top), C6 (bears N), C5 (CH), C4 (CH), C3a (bears CH2).
- So the azetidine nitrogen is on the carbon adjacent to C7, which is adjacent to the carbonyl-bearing carbon.
- Written as SMILES: O=C1N(C2CCC(=O)NC2=O)Cc2ccc(N3...)cc12.
- Ring check: c2 (C3a), c (C4), c (C5), c(N) (C6), c (C7), c12 (C7a). This matches the drawing.
