The structure is a PROTAC-type molecule with three parts, read from left to right.

1. **Pomalidomide end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group sits on the nitrogen of a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione). The aromatic ring carries an NH at the 4-position.
2. **Linker:** the NH is followed by CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, and that NH forms a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O–.
3. **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen is on the hexahydronaphthalene ring of a lovastatin-type core. This core has two ring methyls, the C4a=C double bond, and a second CH=CH in the other ring. A CH2CH2 chain leads to a six-membered lactone that carries a 4-methoxy group in place of lovastatin's 4-hydroxy.

For stereochemistry I assumed the natural lovastatin configuration at every centre, including the methoxy-bearing lactone carbon. I did not check each wedge and hash against this assignment individually.
