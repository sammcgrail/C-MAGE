The structure breaks down into these parts:

- **Benzofuran:** a 2-butyl, 3-substituted benzofuran. C2 carries the butyl chain, C3 carries a CH2 linker, and the ring oxygen sits between C2 and the fused benzene ring.
- **Linker:** the CH2 at C3 connects to a phenyl ring (the first ring of the biphenyl), which is attached para to a second phenyl ring.
- **Second phenyl ring:** it carries an NH–C(=O)–C(=O)–OEt group (ethyl oxamate) and an O–CH2CH2CH2–Ph group (3-phenylpropoxy) on adjacent carbons.

Tracing the second ring: C1 is attached to the first phenyl ring. Going around the ring, the top-right carbon is CH, then the carbon bearing O (C4), then the carbon bearing NH (C3), then CH (C2). So the NH and the ether O are ortho to each other, and the biaryl bond is para to the O and meta to the NH.

Chain from the benzofuran: c1(CCCC)oc2ccccc2c1C, i.e. 2-butylbenzofuran with the CH2 at C3.
