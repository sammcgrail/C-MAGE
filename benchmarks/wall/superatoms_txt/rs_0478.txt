Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Pyridine ring:** the ring nitrogen is at the lower left. Going round from the carbon next to N, C2 carries the amide carbon, C3 carries OH, and C4 carries OMe. The remaining two ring carbons are CH.
- **Amide:** C2 bears C(=O)NH, and the NH is attached to a CH2.
- **Chain:** NH–CH2–CH=C(CH3)–CH2–CH2–CH=C(CH3)2, which is a geranyl-type chain.

**Double-bond geometry (CH=C(CH3) near the amide):** in the drawing, the N–CH2 group and the methyl group lie on the same side of the double bond. The N–CH2 and the chain CH2 are therefore on opposite sides. The higher-priority groups are CH2N on one carbon and the CH2CH2 chain on the other, and they are trans, so this is the E isomer, as in geraniol. In SMILES, `C/C=C(\C)CC` places the methyl cis to the N–CH2, which matches. The terminal CH=C(CH3)2 has two identical methyls, so it needs no stereo label.

SMILES: COc1ccnc(C(=O)NC/C=C(\C)CCC=C(C)C)c1O
