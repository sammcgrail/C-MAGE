This is an EZH2 inhibitor (EPZ-6438-like) linked to thalidomide as a PROTAC.

**Pyridone part:** the ring is 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl, attached through CH2-NH-C(=O). SMILES: Cc1cc(C)c(CNC(=O)...)c(=O)[nH]1.

**Central benzene:** the amide carbon sits on C1. C2 carries a methyl, C3 carries N(Et)(tetrahydropyran-4-yl), C4 is H, C5 carries the aryl group, and C6 is H.

**Central ring SMILES:** c1(C(=O)NCc2c(C)cc(C)[nH]c2=O)cc(-c3ccc(CN4CCN(CCCCCOc5cccc6c5C(=O)N(C5CCC(=O)NC5=O)C6=O)CC4)cc3)cc(N(CC)C3CCOCC3)c1C

**Linker:** from the piperazine N, the chain runs through five CH2 groups to O. Counting the vertices in the drawing: N, then 5 chain carbons, then O. This gives CCCCC, which is five carbons.

**Phthalimide:** the O is attached at position 4 of the isoindoline-1,3-dione, and the ring N bears the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.

SMILES: CCN(C1CCOCC1)c1cc(-c2ccc(CN3CCN(CCCCCOc4cccc5c4C(=O)N(C4CCC(=O)NC4=O)C5=O)CC3)cc2)cc(C(=O)NCc2c(C)cc(C)[nH]c2=O)c1C
