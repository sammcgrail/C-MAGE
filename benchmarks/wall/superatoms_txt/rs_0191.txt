I read the drawing as an eight-membered dilactone ring (a 1,5-dioxocane-2,6-dione) with a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide on the NH side. This is the antimycin-type scaffold.

**Ring, going round from the top-left carbonyl:**
- C(=O), the lactone carbonyl at the top left.
- Ring O.
- CH(CH3), with the methyl on a wedge.
- CH bearing O–C(=O)–NH–iPr (isopropyl carbamate), drawn with a hashed bond.
- CH bearing CH2Ph (benzyl), drawn with a wedge.
- C(=O), the second lactone carbonyl at the bottom.
- Ring O.
- CH2.
- CH bearing the amide NH, drawn with a hashed bond, which closes back to the first carbonyl.

**Side chain on the amide nitrogen:** the pyridine has N1, C2 carrying the C(=O)NH, C3–OH and C4–OMe, with C5 and C6 as plain CH.

**Stereocentres:** all four ring stereocentres are assigned from the wedges and hashes by working out the neighbour order around each carbon.
