I read the molecule as a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand joined by an ether-linked alkyl amide chain to a sulfonyl piperidine, which carries a bromopyrimidine bearing an oxindole-type group.

1. **Left end:** a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl) is attached to the nitrogen of a phthalimide. The phthalimide carries an ether oxygen at the ring position next to the carbonyl-bearing carbon (position 4).
2. **Chain:** from that oxygen, the zigzag has 8 CH2 groups followed by the amide C=O, which is a nonanamide (9 carbons including the carbonyl). The amide N–H then leads to CH2CH2–SO2.
3. **Middle:** the sulfonyl sulfur is bonded to the nitrogen of a piperidine. The piperidine C4 bears an NH that connects to the pyrimidine.
4. **Pyrimidine:** it is substituted at C2 by the piperidinyl-NH, at C4 by an NH–aryl group, and at C5 by Br.
5. **Right end:** the aryl group is a 3-oxoisoindoline (isoindolin-1-one). The NH-bearing aromatic carbon sits next to the ring-fusion carbon that carries the C=O, so it is the 7-position of the isoindolinone.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I included no stereochemistry. The glutarimide C3 is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds.
