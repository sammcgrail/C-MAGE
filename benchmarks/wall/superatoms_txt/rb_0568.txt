I read this as a cyclic peptide: Ac‑Nle, then Asp, Met sulfone, 1‑naphthylalanine (Nal), Arg and Trp. The Asp side chain forms an amide with a 1,4‑diaminobutane linker, and the other end of that linker is amidated by the Trp carboxyl, which closes the ring.

I assigned the stereocentres from the wedges and hashes as follows:
- **Nle:** L (S), assumed.
- **Asp:** S (L), from the wedged CH2.
- **Met sulfone:** S (L), from the hashed side chain.
- **1‑Nal:** R (D), from the wedged CH2.
- **Arg:** S (L), from the hashed side chain.
- **Trp:** S (L). The bond to the indolylmethyl CH2 was hard to read; I took it as hashed. If it is actually wedged, this centre would be D.
