**Fragments, left to right**
- **Phenol-pyridazine:** a 2-hydroxyphenyl sits on C3 of a pyridazine. The aryl group is on C5, and the ring is N=N with two CH carbons (C4 and C6).
- **Fluorophenyl:** the phenyl is attached to the pyridazine at one carbon. F sits on the carbon adjacent to the one carrying the piperidine.
- **Linker:** the piperidine is 4-substituted, and its N is joined through CH2–C(=O)–NH to the tert-leucine α-carbon.
- **tert-Leucine:** the α-carbon carries a tert-butyl group and the amide N-H (bold wedge), and its C(=O) is attached to the proline N.
- **Hydroxyproline:** the OH is on a hashed bond, and the C2 carboxamide is on a bold wedge. I read this as the (2S,4R) form.
- **Cap:** the carboxamide N-H is attached to a CH(CH3) with the methyl on a bold wedge. This CH carries a phenyl that bears the 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group at the para position.
