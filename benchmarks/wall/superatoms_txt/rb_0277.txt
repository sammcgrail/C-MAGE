I read the structure region by region and assembled the SMILES from these parts.

- **Glutarimide and isoindoline-dione:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group sits on the imide nitrogen of a 1,3-dioxoisoindoline. The aromatic carbon next to a carbonyl carbon (position 4) carries a tertiary amine nitrogen.
- **Substituents on that nitrogen:**
  - A CH2CH2 chain leads to C4 of a piperidine. That piperidine nitrogen bears a CH2 group joined to a cyclohexane.
  - A four-carbon chain leads to a second piperidine nitrogen. That piperidine's C4 is attached to a para-substituted phenyl ring, which carries an NH on a pyrazine. The pyrazine has a carboxamide, and a piperidin-1-yl group sits on the carbon next to the ring nitrogen.
- **Cyclohexane:** it is 1,4-disubstituted. One bond is a wedge to the benzothiazole C2 and the other is a hash to the CH2, so the two substituents are trans.
- **Benzothiazole:** the ring carbon next to the C(CH3)2OH-bearing carbon is a CH, and the carbon next to that bears the amide NH. The NH is acylated by 6-(trifluoromethyl)picolinic acid.

For the trans cyclohexane I used the template X[C@H]1CC[C@H](Y)CC1. Because the two ring substituents differ, the ring is achiral and only the cis/trans relationship matters, so this encodes the drawn geometry.
