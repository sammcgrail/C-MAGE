I read the molecule as a cyclic octapeptide amide closed by a Cys–Cys disulfide, with a free N-terminal amine. The sequence, from N-terminus to C-terminus, is:

1. 4-Cl-Phe (L)
2. Cys (D, by the wedge at its α-carbon)
3. N-Me-Tyr (L)
4. 4-carbamoyl-Phe (D, from the hashed CH2 bond)
5. Lys (L)
6. N-Me-Thr (L-configured at Cα, 3R at the hydroxyl-bearing carbon)
7. Cys (L)
8. Tyr-NH2 (D, C-terminal primary amide)

The disulfide joins the side chains of Cys2 and Cys7, and I wrote it as ring closure %10. I assigned each stereocentre from the drawn wedges and hashes using CIP priorities. The alpha–beta bond of the N-Me-Thr and the CH2 bond of Cys7 look hashed in the drawing, which I assumed when assigning those centres.
