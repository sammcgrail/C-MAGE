I read the drawing as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide whose amide nitrogen carries a CH2 link to a 15-crown-5 ring.

**Pyridine ring:** The ring nitrogen sits at the bottom left. The carbon next to it (C2) bears the C(=O)NH group. C3 carries OH and C4 carries OMe. C5 and C6 are plain CH.

**Linker:** The amide NH is bonded to a CH2, which is bonded to a ring CH of the crown ether.

**Crown ether:** I traced the ring from the substituted CH:
- CH, then CH2, then O.
- Then four repeats of CH2CH2 followed by O, which closes back onto the CH.

That gives 15 ring atoms with 5 oxygens, so it is a 15-crown-5.

**Stereochemistry:** The substituted crown CH is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I added no stereo marks.
