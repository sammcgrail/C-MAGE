I read the drawing as a tetrahydropyran core with a pent-4-enyl chain, a 4-(methylsulfonyl)benzoate ester, an anomeric methoxy group and an N-PMB thiazolidin-2-one. I assigned the stereocentres from the wedges and hashes using CIP priorities. These assignments rest on my reading of the wedges, in particular the OMe bond at the anomeric carbon, which I took to be a wedge.

**Ring and substituents**
- The pyran ring is O1, C2 (anomeric), C3 (CH2), C4, C5 (CH2) and C6.
- C6 carries the chain, drawn hashed. I counted five carbons, CH2CH2CH2CH=CH2, so it is pent-4-enyl.
- C4 carries the ester O–C(=O)–C6H4–SO2Me (para), drawn with a bold wedge.
- C2 carries OMe, taken as a wedge, and the thiazolidinone through its C4' carbon.
- The thiazolidinone ring is C4'(H), N(PMB), C(=O), S, CH2. C4' has a wedged H, and the PMB group is 4-methoxybenzyl.

**Stereocentres**
- C6 is R, written `[C@@H]`.
- C4 is R, written `[C@@H]`.
- C2 (anomeric) is R, written `[C@@]`.
- C4' of the thiazolidinone is R, written `[C@@H]`. Here CH2–S outranks the anomeric carbon, because S beats O at the first point of difference.
