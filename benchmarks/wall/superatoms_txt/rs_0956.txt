The drawing shows a pyrimidine ring with these substituents:

- C2 carries CF3, between the two ring nitrogens.
- N3 is at the lower right of the ring, adjacent to C4.
- C4 carries the 4-(methylsulfonyl)phenyl group.
- C5 carries the 4-chlorophenyl group.
- C6 carries Cl and is bonded to N1 at the upper right.

Ring connectivity: C6(Cl)=N1–C2(CF3)=N3–C4(Ar)=C5(Ar')–C6.
