1. **Xanthone (top right).** The left aromatic ring carries an acetyl group, a methyl group and the bond to the chromone. The right aromatic ring carries two OH groups and a COOH next to the ketone, and the central pyranone ring contains the ring oxygen and the C=O.
2. **Chromone (left).** The ring carries 7-OH, 6-OH, 5-COOH and a 4-C=O. C2 is bonded to the xanthone, and C3 is bonded to a CH2 linker.
3. **Pyrano[3,2-c]chromenone (bottom).** The pyranone ring has a C=O, a methyl group and the CH2 linker. It is fused to a second ring containing an OCH2, which is fused to a benzene ring bearing two OH groups.
