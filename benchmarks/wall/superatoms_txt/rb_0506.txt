Reading the structure region by region:

**Right side, thieno[3,2-c]pyridinone core.**
- The thiophene ring is S, C2 (bearing the carboxamide), C3 (CH), C3a and C7a.
- The fused pyridinone ring continues from C3a to C4 (=O), then N5 (methyl), C6 (CH), and C7, which carries the aryl group and bonds to C7a.
- SMILES for the core: c1(C(=O)N...)cc2c(s1)c(Ar)cn(C)c2=O

**Aryl group.**
- It is a phenyl ring bearing two OMe groups and a CH2N(CH3)2 group between them, so it is 3,5-dimethoxy-4-(dimethylaminomethyl)phenyl.
- SMILES: c1(OC)cc(...)cc(OC)c1CN(C)C

**Amide and piperidine.**
- The amide NH attaches to C4 of a piperidine.
- C3 of that piperidine carries two fluorines (CF2), next to C2, which bonds to the ring N.
- The piperidine N attaches to the isoindolinone.
- SMILES: NC1CCN(Ar)CC1(F)F

**Isoindolinone.**
- The ring N bears the 1-methyl-2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
- The C=O is on the carbon attached to the aromatic ring carbon adjacent to the one bearing the piperidine N, so the piperidine sits at position 5 of the isoindolinone.
- Aromatic ring: c1 (C=O side, C7a), then CH (C7), then C6 bearing the piperidine N, then CH, CH, and finally C3a bearing the CH2.
- In the drawing, the C=O-bearing carbon connects to a CH, which connects to the C–N(piperidine) carbon. That carbon then goes to a CH, a CH, and the CH2-bearing carbon.
- SMILES: O=C1N(C2CCC(=O)N(C)C2=O)Cc2ccc(N3...)cc12
