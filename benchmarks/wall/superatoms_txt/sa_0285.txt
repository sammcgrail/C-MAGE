The drawing shows a central CH carbon bonded to three groups: a carboxylic acid (HO2C), an n-butyl chain (nBu), and a carbonyl carbon (C=O).

The carbonyl carbon is bonded to a nitrogen that carries a phenyl group. That nitrogen is also bonded to an NH, which carries a second phenyl group. This is a 1,2-diphenylhydrazide: C(=O)N(Ph)NHPh.

The central carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but all its bonds are drawn plain, so I give no stereo.

Expanding the abbreviations gives HOOC–CH(CH2CH2CH2CH3)–C(=O)–N(c1ccccc1)–NH–c1ccccc1.
