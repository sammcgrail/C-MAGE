I read the structure as a macrocycle and wrote the SMILES by following the ring around from the dimethoxy-hydroxyphenyl group.

**Fragments, in order around the ring**
- **Aryl group:** a 2-hydroxy-4,5-dimethoxyphenyl ring joined to a CH2CH2 chain.
- **Benzylic carbon:** a CH carrying the ester oxygen and a meta-substituted phenylene ring. The hashed bond to the CH2 makes it R.
- **Oxyacetamide link:** the phenylene's oxygen goes through OCH2C(=O) to an N-methyl norleucine (butyl side chain, drawn as S).
- **Naphthylalanine:** a 2-naphthylalanine residue (S), joined to the next residue through an N-methyl amide.
- **Serine residue:** an N-methyl O-tert-butyl serine (S), followed by a glycine.
- **Unsaturated linker:** an N-methyl aminocrotonate unit, N(C)CC=CC(=O)O. The wavy bond means the double-bond geometry is unspecified, so I left it without a label.
- **Dimethyl diketo unit:** OCH2C(CH3)2C(=O)C(=O)N, which acylates the nitrogen of a pipecolic acid.
- **Pipecolic acid:** drawn as S, with its carboxyl closing the macrocycle as an ester onto the benzylic oxygen.

I assigned each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes, using CIP priorities, and then chose the @/@@ symbols to match.

The ring closure %10 joins the benzylic carbon to the ester oxygen. The digit 2 is reused for the phenylene, naphthalene and pipecolic rings, but never while another ring with that label is open.
