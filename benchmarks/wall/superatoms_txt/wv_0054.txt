The drawing shows a maleimide ring: N-substituted pyrrole-2,5-dione with a C=C in the ring.

The nitrogen carries a chain. Going from N, the first carbon (CH2) leads to a second CH2, then a third CH2 that rises to a quaternary carbon. Reading the zigzag from N: the first bond goes up-right to a vertex at about (172,172), then up to (230,118), then on to (300,118), then up to the quaternary carbon at (300,65). That gives the vertices at 172,172, 230,118 and 300,118 as three CH2 groups, followed by the quaternary carbon at 300,65.

The quaternary carbon has a methyl going up-right to (335,5). It also has two wavy lines, one to the upper left at (245,35) and one to the lower right at (360,95). It therefore carries a methyl and two attachment points, so there are two * atoms.

The chain is N-CH2-CH2-CH2-C(CH3)(*)(*).
