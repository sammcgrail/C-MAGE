I read this as a nine-membered dilactone ring carrying a substituted pyridine amide, similar to an antimycin-type scaffold.

**Fragments read from the drawing**
- **Lactate side chain:** an acetyl ester (CH3C(=O)–O) on the CH(CH3) carbon, which is joined through its C(=O)–O to the pyridine C3 oxygen.
- **Pyridine:** C2 carries C(=O)NH, C3 carries the lactate ester oxygen, C4 carries OMe, and N is the ring nitrogen.
- **Nine-membered ring**, going around: C(=O), O, CH(CH3), CH(O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), O, CH2, CH(NH–). The NH is the amide nitrogen attached to the pyridine C2 carbonyl.

- CH(CH2Ph) ring carbon (wedged CH2Ph): R.
- CH(O-isobutyrate) ring carbon (hashed O): R.
