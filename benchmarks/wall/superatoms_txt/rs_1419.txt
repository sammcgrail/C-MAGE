The drawing shows a benzene ring with an iodine on the left and a CH2–CN group on the right. The two substituents sit at opposite ends of the ring, so they are para to each other. This is 4-iodophenylacetonitrile.

The ring bonds are drawn in a Kekulé pattern, which does not change the SMILES. The CH2 carbon is joined to the nitrile carbon, which is triple-bonded to N. There are no stereocentres.

SMILES: N#CCc1ccc(I)cc1
