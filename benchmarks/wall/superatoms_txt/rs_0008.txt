I read the molecule as a 4-substituted benzoic acid joined through a CH2CH2 linker to the nitrogen of a 2-pyrrolidinone (five-membered lactam).

- **Lactam ring:** N, C=O, two CH2 groups, and the C5 carbon, which carries the side chain.
- **Side chain on C5:** a trans CH=CH, then CH(OH), then three CH2 groups (counted from the zigzag), then CH(OH), then CH3.
- **Aryl part:** N–CH2CH2–C6H4–CO2H, para-substituted.

**Stereochemistry:**
- The ring carbon C5 has a wedge to the vinyl group, which gives `[C@H]` in the SMILES as written.
- The terminal carbinol carbon has a hashed OH, which gives `[C@H]` in the SMILES as written.
- The alkene is drawn trans (E).
- The allylic CH(OH) carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

SMILES: O=C1CC[C@H](/C=C/C(O)CCC[C@H](C)O)N1CCc1ccc(C(=O)O)cc1
