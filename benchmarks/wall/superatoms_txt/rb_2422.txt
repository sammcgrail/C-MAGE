I read the molecule as a C2-symmetric 2,2'-biindole dimer, written here with its two methanesulfonic acid counter-ions.

**Each half of the molecule**
- Each indole carries a fluorine on C6, the benzo carbon next to C7, which is adjacent to the C7a–NH carbon.
- The indole C3 is bonded to a CH2 group, which leads to C2 of a pyrrolidine ring.
- The pyrrolidine C4 carries an acetoxy group (OC(C)=O).
- The pyrrolidine nitrogen is acylated by a 2-aminobutanoyl unit (ethyl side chain), which is itself amide-linked to N-methylalanine (CH3NH–CH(CH3)–C(=O)).
- The two indole C2 atoms are bonded to each other.

**Stereochemistry (from the wedges and hashes)**
- The alanine carbon is S, the same as L-alanine.
- The aminobutanoyl carbon is S.
- The pyrrolidine C2 (bearing the CH2-indole) is R.
- The pyrrolidine C4 (bearing the acetoxy group) is S.
- The lower half is the drawn 180° rotation of the upper half, so I took it to have the same descriptors. Its SMILES chirality tags were re-derived for the reversed atom order.
