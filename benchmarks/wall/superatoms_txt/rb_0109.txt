I read this as a rifamycin-type ansa macrolactam with the naphthoquinone fused to a phenoxazine ring. The phenoxazine carries a 2-(2-azabicyclo[2.2.1]heptan-2-yl)ethoxy group.

**Core.**
- The furanone has a quaternary carbon bearing a methyl group, the ring oxygen, the C=O and a wedged O–CH=CH ansa bond.
- The aromatic ring carries a methyl and an OH.
- The quinone ring is fused to the phenoxazine, with the NH-amide on the ring carbon next to the quinone C=O.

**Phenoxazine substituent.** The benzo ring bears O–CH2CH2–N. The nitrogen is N2 of an azabicyclo[2.2.1]heptane: it is bonded to one bridgehead carbon and to a CH2 that leads to the other bridgehead.

**Ansa chain.** Starting from the vinyl ether, the chain runs:
- O–CH=CH (trans)
- CH(OMe)
- CH(CH3)
- CH(OAc)
- CH(CH3)
- CH(OH)
- CH(CH3)
- CH(OH)
- CH(CH3)
- CH=CH (trans)
- CH=C(CH3) (cis)
- C(=O)NH

**Stereochemistry.** I worked out each centre from the wedges and hashes. The one-line summary:
- The OMe is hashed.
- The OAc and the two methyls at the OAc end are wedged or hashed as drawn.
- The OH groups are hashed.
- The methyl next to the diene is wedged.
- The quaternary carbon has the hashed methyl and the wedged O-vinyl bond.

I wrote the ring system in Kekulé form to avoid aromaticity problems with the quinone carbonyls.
