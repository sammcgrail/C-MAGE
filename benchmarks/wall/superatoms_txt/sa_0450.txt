I read the structure in four parts and wrote the SMILES from them.

- **Left side:** a 2-aminothiazol-4-yl ring, with its C4 attached to a C=N–O–CH2–CO2H oxime ether.
- **Linker:** that oxime carbon is bonded to a C(=O)–NH amide.
- **Right side:** a β-lactam (azetidin-2-one) with N-SO3H. Its C3 carries the amide NH on a hashed bond, and its C4 carries a CH2–O–C(=O)NH2 group on a hashed bond.
- **Oxime geometry:** the oxime O and the amide C(=O) lie on the same side of the C=N bond. The thiazole is therefore trans to the O, which gives `O/N=C(/c1...)`.

I assigned the two ring stereocentres from the hashed bonds, with the implicit H toward the viewer, and both come out as `@@` in the SMILES atom order I used.

SMILES: OC(=O)CO/N=C(/c1csc(N)n1)C(=O)N[C@@H]1C(=O)N(S(=O)(=O)O)[C@@H]1COC(N)=O
