The SMILES below is my reading of the drawing, built region by region; I did not run it through a parser.

**Macrocycle.**
- The chlorobenzene ring carries Cl, a CH2CH2–O–CH2 chain and a CH2–O link.
- The CH2CH2–O–CH2 chain runs to the central benzene ring.
- The CH2–O link goes to a 2,6-disubstituted pyridine.
- The pyridine's other carbon is bonded directly to the central benzene ring.

**Central benzene ring.** It carries three substituents:
- the pyridine, attached directly;
- the CH2–O–CH2CH2 chain to the chlorophenyl ring;
- a CH2 group leading to the benzimidazole.

**Benzimidazole.**
- C2 carries the CH2 that links to the central benzene ring.
- N1 carries a CH2 attached to the oxetan-2-yl carbon.
- C4 carries OCH2CH2O–Si(CH3)2–C(CH3)3, i.e. an O-TBS ether.
- C6 carries the methyl ester, CO2CH3.

**Stereochemistry.** The only stereocentre drawn with a wedge or hash is the oxetane C2, where the CH2N bond is hashed. Its CIP priorities are O > CH2N > ring CH2 > H. Working through the geometry gives S. In the SMILES the neighbour order at that atom is CH2N, H, ring-closure O, ring CH2, which corresponds to `@@` for S.
