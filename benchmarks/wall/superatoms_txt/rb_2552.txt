I read the molecule as a macrocycle built from four parts, joined in this order: a diaminobutyric acid (Dab) unit, a lysine, an N-methyl tryptophan, and a fluoro-aryl/pyridyl thioether linker.

**Fragments read from the drawing**
- Dab unit: H2N-CH2CH2-CH(NH-CH2-pyridine)-C(=O)-NH, with a wedge on the CH2 side chain.
- Lysine: CH(CH2CH2CH2CH2NH2)-C(=O)-N(CH3), with a wedge on the side chain.
- N-methyl tryptophan: CH(CH2-indol-3-yl)-C(=O)-NH, with a wedge on the CH2.
- Pyridine: 3-CH2 attached, 2-S attached, ring N present.
- Benzene ring, in order around the ring: C-CH2NH, C-S, C-F, CH, CH, C-pyridone.
- Pyridone: 4-linked to the benzene, with N-CH2COOH and a 2-oxo group.

**Stereocentres**
For each of the three wedged centres I took the implicit H as pointing back and worked out the clockwise or counterclockwise order of the other three substituents on the page. I then converted that to @/@@ in the order the atoms appear in the SMILES. The result is @@ for the Dab carbon and @ for the lysine and tryptophan carbons.
