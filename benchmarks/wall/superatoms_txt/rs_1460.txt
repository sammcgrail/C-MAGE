The drawing shows a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide (a picolinamide) with these parts:

- **Pyridine ring:** N at the bottom, C2 bearing the C(=O)N amide, C3 bearing OH, C4 bearing OMe, then C5 and C6 as CH.
- **Amino acid unit:** the amide NH is attached to a CH. That CH carries an isopropyl ester, C(=O)OCH(CH3)2, and a CH2–O side chain (a serine residue).
- **Side-chain ester:** the serine oxygen is acylated by C(=O)CH2CH(CH3)O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2, i.e. a 3-(isobutyryloxy)butanoyl group.

The alpha carbon has a plain bond to the NH, with no wedge or hash visible on the stereocentres, so I give no stereochemistry.
