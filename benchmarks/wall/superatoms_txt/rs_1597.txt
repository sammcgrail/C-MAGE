- **Chain:** the central ring's other para position carries a CH2, then the stereocentre, then a carboxylic acid (C(=O)OH).

- **Chirality assignment:** the CIP priorities are OEt > COOH > CH2Ar > H. With H pointing away, O (bottom) → COOH (upper right) → CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S.
