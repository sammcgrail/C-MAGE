I read the structure in four pieces and then joined them.

**Left aryl group.** A benzene ring carries a CH2N(CH3)2 group at the top, flanked by two OMe groups (the ring positions ortho to the CH2NMe2). The ring is attached to the core at the position para to the CH2NMe2 carbon. SMILES: c1(OC)c(CN(C)C)c(OC)cc(c1)-

**Thieno[3,2-c]pyridinone core.**
- The thiophene S is bonded to C2, which carries the carboxamide, and to C7a.
- C7a is bonded to C7, which carries the aryl group.
- C7 is double-bonded to C6 (the CH), which is bonded to N(CH3). N is bonded to C(=O), then C3a, then C3 (CH), then C2.
- SMILES: c1(Ar)cn(C)c(=O)c2cc(C(=O)N...)sc12

**Amide side chain.** The amide N is bonded to CH2, which is bonded to a cyclobutane carbon. The cyclobutane is spiro-fused to an azetidine, giving 2-azaspiro[3.3]heptane. The azetidine N bears a propyl chain.
- Spiro system: C1CC2(C1)CN(C2)...
- CH2 attached to the cyclobutane CH: NCC1CC2(C1)CN(C2)CCCN3CCN(CC3)...

**Right side.** The propyl chain ends at a piperazine N. The other piperazine N is bonded to the isoindoline-1,3-dione at position 5, and the imide N carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
- Aryl: c1ccc2c(c1)C(=O)N(C2=O)C1CCC(=O)NC1=O
