I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type conjugate and wrote its SMILES from that reading. The stereocentres are copied from the natural lovastatin core (PubChem isomeric SMILES) on the assumption that the drawing shows that configuration. I did not re-derive each wedge or hash from the image.

- **Left end:** a thalidomide-type unit, i.e. a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the nitrogen of a phthalimide, with an NH at the 4-position of the aromatic ring.
- **Linker:** that NH is acylated, giving NH–C(=O)–CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH. The terminal NH forms a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O.
- **Right end:** the carbamate oxygen is attached to C1 of a lovastatin-type hexahydronaphthalene.
  - The ring has methyl groups at C3 and C7, a C4=C4a double bond and a C5=C6 double bond.
  - The C8 side chain is CH2CH2 leading to a δ-lactone (6-oxo-tetrahydropyran-2-yl).
  - The lactone carries an OTBDMS group, written as O–Si(CH3)2–C(CH3)3.
