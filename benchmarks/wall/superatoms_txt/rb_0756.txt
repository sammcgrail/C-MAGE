I read the molecule in four parts and then joined them.

1. **Left end:** a glutarimide (piperidine-2,6-dione) is attached through its C3 to the nitrogen of an isoindolin-1-one. The isoindolinone has a fluorine on the benzene ring and a piperidin-4-yl group at the position next to the CH2 carbon. Both stereocentres are drawn with plain bonds, so I gave no stereochemistry.
2. **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen carries a straight chain of nine CH2 groups, counted vertex by vertex, ending in an ether oxygen.
3. **Aryl ether:** the oxygen is on a benzene ring. The carbon next to it bears the quinazoline, and the carbon after that bears a fluorine.
4. **Quinazoline:** C4 carries a piperazine whose far nitrogen is acylated with propionyl. C6 carries Cl, C7 is bonded to the aryl ring, and C8 carries F.
