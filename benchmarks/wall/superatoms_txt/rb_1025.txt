I read the structure region by region and wrote the SMILES from that reading.

- **Left end:** a 2-hydroxyphenyl sits on a pyridazine. The pyridazine carries an NH2 and, at the adjacent carbon, a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Linker:** the piperazine's other nitrogen goes through a CH2 to a para-substituted benzene. That benzene continues through CH2CH2–O to the central benzene ring.
- **Central benzene:**
  - The O-bearing carbon is ortho to the CH2NH carbon.
  - The thiazole is attached para to the CH2NH carbon.
- **Thiazole:** C5 is bonded to the central benzene, and C4 carries an indazol-6-yl group (N1 is the NH).
- **Right end:** the CH2NH group is an amide of hydroxyproline. The proline nitrogen is acylated by tert-leucine, whose NH is acylated by 1-fluorocyclopropanecarbonyl.

I did not derive the stereocentres atom by atom. I assigned them by matching the standard VHL ligand pattern (2S,4R-hydroxyproline with the tert-leucine stereocentre), which is how the wedges and hashes appear to be drawn.
