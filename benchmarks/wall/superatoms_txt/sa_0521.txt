The drawing shows a benzene ring with three adjacent substituents: THPO, OMe and OMe.

THP is tetrahydropyran-2-yl, attached through its C2 carbon, which is an acetal carbon (O-CH-O). The THP carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn without wedges, so I give it no stereo.

SMILES for the THP ether: C1(OC2CCCCO2)=C(OC)C(OC)=CC=C1

Ring atoms:
- C1 (bottom left) bears OTHP.
- C2 (bottom) bears OMe.
- C3 (bottom right) bears OMe.
- The remaining three ring atoms are CH.

This gives a 1,2,3-trisubstituted ring with the THPO group at one end, which is what the drawing shows.

SMILES: COc1cccc(OC2CCCCO2)c1OC
