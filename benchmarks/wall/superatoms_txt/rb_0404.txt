I read this as a cyclic peptide: Ac‑Nle‑cyclo[Glu‑His‑2Nal‑Arg‑Trp‑NH‑(CH2)4‑NH‑], where the Glu side‑chain carboxyl is joined as an amide to a 1,4‑diaminobutane linker. The other end of that linker is amidated to the Trp C‑terminus, which closes the ring.

Residue by residue:
- **N‑terminus:** an acetyl group on a norleucine (n‑butyl side chain).
- **Glu:** its side chain CH2CH2C(=O) forms the amide to the linker NH.
- **His:** a 4‑substituted imidazole with an NH.
- **2‑Naphthylalanine:** the side chain is a CH2 on the 2‑position of naphthalene.
- **Arg:** a three‑carbon chain ending in a guanidine.
- **Trp:** a 3‑indolylmethyl side chain, with its C‑terminal C(=O) bonded to the NH(CH2)4NH linker.

Stereochemistry: I assigned each centre from the wedge bond to its side chain, with the implicit H pointing back. This gave D‑Nle, L‑Glu, D‑His, D‑2Nal, D‑Arg and D‑Trp. The mix is unusual for this kind of peptide (a ligand of this type would normally have L residues apart from D‑2Nal), but I followed the drawing as I read it. I could not confirm from the image whether any wedge is actually hashed.
