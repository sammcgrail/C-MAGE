I read this as Boc-protected glutamine. The chain is HN–CH(CO2H)–CH2–CH2–C(=O)NH2, with a Boc group on the nitrogen.

The α-carbon is the only stereocentre, and the drawing marks it with a hashed bond to CO2H. The CO2H group therefore points away from the viewer, and the implicit hydrogen points toward the viewer.

Priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H. On the page, N (lower left) → CO2H (up) → CH2 (lower right) runs clockwise. The hydrogen faces the viewer, so the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This matches the L-glutamine configuration.

The Boc group expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C on the nitrogen.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N[C@@H](CCC(N)=O)C(=O)O
