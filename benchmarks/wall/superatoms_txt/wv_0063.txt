The drawing shows a 4-chlorophenyl ring joined through an ether oxygen to a second benzene ring, which carries the attachment point.

Substituents on the right-hand ring, numbered from the attachment point:
- C1 is the carbon bearing the wavy line (the attachment point).
- C2 is the CH next to it, at the bottom right.
- C3 bears Cl, at the bottom.
- C4 bears the O, at the lower left.
- C5 and C6 are CH carbons.

So the right ring is a 3-chloro-4-(4-chlorophenoxy)phenyl group.
