I read the molecule in three parts and joined them with an E-alkene–CH2–amide linker.

- **Left part:** a 2,2-diphenyl-1,3-benzodioxole. It carries a trifluoroacetyl group (C(=O)CF3) at position 5 and the amide carbonyl at position 7. The amide carbonyl sits on the ring carbon next to the carbon bearing the lower dioxole oxygen.
- **Linker:** the amide N–H connects to CH2 (7′), then an E-configured CH=CH (6′, 5′), which attaches to C4′ of the sugar.
- **Right part:** a 2′,3′-O-isopropylidene ribofuranose bearing adenine through N9 at C1′. The thick bonds at the bottom of the furanose show the usual D-ribo arrangement. I took the stereo tags from the known 2′,3′-O-isopropylideneadenosine SMILES (recalled, not derived from the drawing) and replaced the 5′-CH2OH with the vinyl linker, which keeps the neighbour order and so the same tags.

I checked ring-closure numbers for reuse and balanced parentheses by hand.
