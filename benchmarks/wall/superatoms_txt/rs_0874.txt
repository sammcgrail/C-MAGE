I read the molecule as a 2-pyrrolidinone (γ-lactam) with two substituents. The ring nitrogen carries a CH2CH2 linker to a para-substituted benzoic acid. The adjacent ring carbon (C5) carries an (E)-CH=CH–CH(OH)–CH2CH2– chain ending in a morpholine nitrogen.

- **Lactam ring:** a five-membered ring with C=O, two CH2 groups, the stereocentre C5 and N.
- **N-substituent:** N–CH2–CH2–C6H4–CO2H, with the acid para to the linker.
- **C5 side chain:** drawn on a wedge bond, so C5 is the only stereocentre I assign. The double bond is drawn trans (E). The carbinol carbon CH(OH) has plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
- **Morpholine:** N, two CH2, O, two CH2 in a six-membered ring, attached through N.

To set the chirality at C5, I viewed it from the front with H pointing away. The ring CH2 (west), N (north) and the wedged vinyl carbon (south-east) run clockwise. Writing the neighbours in SMILES order (ring CH2, H, vinyl, N) then gives `[C@H]`.

SMILES: O=C1CC[C@H](/C=C/C(O)CCN2CCOCC2)N1CCc1ccc(C(=O)O)cc1
