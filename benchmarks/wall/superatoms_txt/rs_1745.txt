The drawing shows a benzene ring fused to a methylenedioxy (1,3-dioxole) ring on its right side.

Ring atoms, going clockwise from the top:
- Top vertex (C-a): OCH3
- Upper right (C-b): O, part of the dioxole
- Lower right (C-c): O, part of the dioxole
- Bottom (C-d): H
- Lower left (C-e): OH
- Upper left (C-f): OCH3

The ring is therefore: C-a (OMe), C-f (OMe), C-e (OH), C-d (H), C-c (O), C-b (O).

SMILES, starting from the top OMe carbon: COc1c(OC)c(O)cc2OCOc12

Atom check:
- c1 is C-a, carrying OMe.
- c(OC) is C-f, carrying OMe.
- c(O) is C-e, carrying OH.
- c is C-d, carrying H.
- c2 is C-c, with its O bonded to CH2.
- OCO is the O–CH2–O bridge.
- c12 is C-b, closing both rings. It bonds to C-a (ring closure 1) and to the O that closes the dioxole ring (closure 2).

This describes 4,5-dimethoxy-1,3-benzodioxol-6-ol, which matches the drawing.
