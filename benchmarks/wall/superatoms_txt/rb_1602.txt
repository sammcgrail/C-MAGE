I read this as a thalidomide-based PROTAC. A 4-oxyacetamide thalidomide unit is linked through a benzylamine and an ethylenediamine carbamate to a lovastatin-type hexahydronaphthalene bearing the hydroxy-lactone side chain.

- **Left end:** a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl) is attached through its ring nitrogen to a phthalimide. The phthalimide carries an O–CH2–C(=O)–NH group at the 4-position.
- **Linker:** the amide NH is bonded to a CH2 on a para-disubstituted benzene. The other ring position carries NH–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)–O, a carbamate.
- **Right end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on C1 of a hexahydronaphthalene. This ring has a CH(CH3) at the bottom, a C=C into the ring fusion carbon, and a CH=CH leading to a second CH(CH3). The side chain is a CH2CH2 group leading to a 4-hydroxy-6-oxo-tetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone.
- **Stereochemistry:** I did not read each wedge individually. The right-hand fragment matches the lovastatin skeleton, so I used lovastatin's stereodescriptors for those centres. The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with a plain bond, so it has no stereo label.
