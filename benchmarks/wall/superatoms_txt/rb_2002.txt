This is an EZH2-inhibitor-based PROTAC. It has an EZH2 binding part (a pyridone amide), a linker, and a thalidomide-type cereblon binder.

**Pyridone part:** 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl, attached through CH2 to the amide NH. SMILES: Cc1cc(C)c(CNC(=O)...)c(=O)[nH]1.

**Central benzene ring:**
- C1 carries the C(=O)N amide.
- C2 carries a methyl.
- C3 carries N(Et)(tetrahydropyran-4-yl).
- C4 is H.
- C5 carries the aryl group.
- C6 is H.

**Linker from the aryl group:** the phenyl ring is para-substituted with CH2–N of a piperazine. The other piperazine N bears CH2C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2CH2–O–Ar.

**Cereblon binder:** the phthalimide carries the O substituent at the 4-position. Its N is attached to the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
