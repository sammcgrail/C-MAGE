Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Pyridazine ring:** six atoms with two adjacent nitrogens. One ring carbon carries the chain, and the carbon two positions away from it, on the side away from the nitrogens, carries OMe. This gives a 3-substituted 5-methoxypyridazine.
- **Linker:** two CH2 groups join the pyridazine carbon to the pyrrolidine nitrogen.
- **Pyrrolidine ring:** a five-membered ring with N, then C2, C3 and C4, then C5. C3 carries an OMs group (OS(C)(=O)=O) on a wedge bond.
- **Stereochemistry:** with the hydrogen pointing back, the wedged O sits up-right, C4 lower-right and C2 lower-left. Going O → C4 → C2 is clockwise. The CIP priorities are O > C2 (attached to N) > C4 > H, so O → C2 → C4 is counterclockwise, which makes C3 **S**. In the SMILES below, written as C4, H, O, C2 around that atom, `[C@H]` encodes this S configuration.
