This is a PROTAC-type molecule built from a BET-binding imidazo[1,2-a]pyridine, a PEG linker and a pomalidomide-type cereblon ligand. I read it in four parts.

**Left fragment.** A 3,5-dimethylisoxazol-4-yl group is attached at position 7 of an imidazo[1,2-a]pyridine. Position 3 of that core carries an NH-benzyl group. Position 2 carries a para-substituted phenyl ring.

- Isoxazole: Cc1noc(C)c1-, with O bonded to the C(Me) and N bonded to the other C(Me).
- Imidazo[1,2-a]pyridine: the bridgehead N is bonded to C3, to C5 (the CH=CH side) and to C8a. C8a bonds to N1 and to C8 (CH). C8 bonds to C7, which bears the isoxazole. C7 bonds to C6, C6 to C5, and C5 to the bridgehead N.
- SMILES for this part: c1(-c2c(C)noc2C)cc2nc(-c3ccc(cc3)N...)c(NCc3ccccc3)n2cc1

**Middle.** The phenyl ring is bonded to a piperazine N. The other piperazine N is acylated: C(=O)CCOCCOCCOCCN, which is a propanoyl group followed by three ether oxygens. Counting along the chain: C(=O), CH2, CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, NH.

**Right fragment.** The NH is attached to position 4 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its imide N is bonded to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.

**Assembled SMILES:**
Cc1noc(C)c1-c1ccn2c(NCc3ccccc3)c(-c3ccc(cc3)N3CCN(CC3)C(=O)CCOCCOCCOCCNc3cccc4c3C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C4=O)nc2c1

Checking the right-hand ring: c3cccc4c3C(=O)N(...)C4=O has the aromatic carbon bearing NH (c3), then three CH, then c4 fused, then back to c3. The ring closure is c3cccc4c3, which is wrong because c3 closes to the first atom. Rewritten as Nc3cccc4c3C(=O)N(...)C4=O, the atoms are c3 (NH-bearing), c, c, c, c4 (fused), c3 (fused, closing to the first). That gives six atoms: 1 (NH-bearing), 3 CH, and 2 fused. The fused atom c4 bears C4=O at the end, and the last fused atom bears C(=O). This is consistent.
