Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Indane core:** a benzene ring fused to a five-membered ring. The carboxylic acid (COOH) sits on C2 of the five-membered ring, which has plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label. The sulfonyl group is on the aromatic carbon adjacent to the ring fusion (position 4).
- **Linker:** an SO2 group joins the indane to a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Piperazine:** the sulfonylated N1 is flanked by two methyl-bearing carbons, C2 and C6, and both methyls are drawn with bold wedges, so they are cis. The opposite nitrogen, N4, carries a benzyl group.
- **Benzyl group:** a benzene ring with an OCF3 substituent meta to the CH2.

I assigned the two piperazine stereocentres from the wedge geometry. Both methyl-bearing carbons come out as `[C@H]` for C2 and `[C@@H]` for C6 in the SMILES order below.
