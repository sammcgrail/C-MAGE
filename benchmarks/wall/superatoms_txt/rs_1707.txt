Reading the structure region by region:

- **Left ring:** a benzoic acid (HO–C(=O) on the ring), with a second substituent meta to the acid. That substituent is a C(=O)N(CH3) amide.
- **Middle ring:** the amide nitrogen carries a methyl and is attached to a benzene ring. Going around that ring, the carbon ortho to the N-bearing carbon has a methyl, and the carbon para to the N-bearing carbon has the ether oxygen (O–CH2). The methyl-bearing carbon is also meta to the oxygen-bearing carbon, which is consistent with the drawing.
- **Isoxazole:** the CH2 is attached to C4 of the isoxazole. C5 carries the isopropyl group and is bonded to the ring O. The ring O is bonded to N, and N is double-bonded to C3. C3 carries the 2-(trifluoromethoxy)phenyl group. The ring is therefore c1(CH2)c(iPr)onc1Ar.
