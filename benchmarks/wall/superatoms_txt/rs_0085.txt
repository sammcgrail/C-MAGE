The left ring is drawn as N joined to CH2–CH2 on the top and CH2–CH2 on the bottom, with a vertical bond between the two left-hand CH2 groups. That makes N, CH2, CH2, CH2, CH2, which is a pyrrolidine (5-membered ring).

The N of the pyrrolidine is attached to a para-substituted benzene. This ring connects through CH2CH2 to a second para-substituted benzene, which carries N(Ph)(4-methoxyphenyl).
