The drawing shows an MMAE (monomethyl auristatin E) payload attached through a self-immolative p-aminobenzyl carbamate linker (PAB-Val-Ala) to a 6-aminohexanoyl spacer. The spacer ends in a methyl (Z)-maleamate (methyl ester of a cis-butenedioic acid monoamide).

Reading left to right:
- **Maleamate cap:** a methyl ester on a cis C=C, followed by an amide NH.
- **Spacer:** five CH2 groups and a carbonyl (the 6-aminohexanoyl unit), then an amide to valine.
- **Dipeptide and PAB:** Val, then Ala, then an anilide on a para-substituted benzene whose CH2–O–C(=O) forms the carbamate.
- **Auristatin core:** N-methyl-valine, valine, N-methyl-dolaisoleuine (with its OMe), dolaproine (pyrrolidine with its OMe and methyl), and a norephedrine-type amino alcohol bearing a phenyl ring.

I assumed L-Val and L-Ala. For the auristatin stereocentres I took the configuration from the known MMAE SMILES, re-expressing each centre for my atom ordering, and checked each against the drawn wedges. The norephedrine centres follow MMAE as well.
