The structure is a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an oxindole.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H. The second ring is attached to it at the ring carbon next to the carbon bearing OH. The carbon bearing the NH–N= group is adjacent to the OH carbon on its other side. So the second ring reads: C1 (attached to the first ring), C2 (OH), C3 (NH), then C4, C5 and C6 as CH.

**Hydrazone linker.** The ring NH is bonded to N, which is double-bonded to C3 of the oxindole.

**Oxindole part.** C3 is bonded to C2 (C=O), which is bonded to N1. N1 carries a 4-isopropylphenyl group. The fused benzene ring has F at C5. In the drawing, C3a is the ring carbon at the upper left of the benzo ring, and F sits on the upper-right carbon, which is C5. The chain runs C3a, C4 (CH), C5 (F), C6, C7, C7a.
