The drawing shows a thiazolo[4,5-d]pyrimidin-2-one core with these substituents:

- **C5 of the pyrimidine ring:** an NH2 group.
- **C7:** an O–CH2 link to a 5-methyl-2-oxo-1,3-dioxol-4-yl ring. That ring is a cyclic carbonate with a C=C bearing the methyl group.
- **N3 of the thiazolone:** a furanose sugar attached through the ring nitrogen, carrying 2'-OAc, 3'-OAc and a 5'-CH2OAc group. The acetates are expanded to OC(C)=O.

The sugar stereochemistry comes from the wedges and hashes. The base at C1' and the CH2OAc at C4' are both bold (up), and the 2'- and 3'-OAc groups are hashed (down). This matches a β-D-ribofuranosyl pattern. I used the same stereo-descriptor pattern and neighbour ordering as adenosine, `n[C@@H]1O[C@H](CO)[C@@H](O)[C@H]1O`, with the acetates substituted on the oxygens.
