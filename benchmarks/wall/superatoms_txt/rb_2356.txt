I read this as an amatoxin-type bicyclic octapeptide. A tryptathionine thioether links Cys to C2 of the Trp indole. The residues are 4,5-dihydroxy-isoleucine, 4-hydroxyproline, Asp, Cys, Gly, Ile, Gly and Trp.

I traced the ring through the amide bonds:
- Hyp N to the Asp C=O.
- Asp NH to the Cys C=O.
- Cys NH to a Gly C=O.
- That Gly NH to the Ile C=O.
- Ile NH to the second Gly C=O.
- That Gly NH to the Trp C=O.
- Trp NH to the dihydroxy-Ile C=O.
- Dihydroxy-Ile NH to the Hyp C=O.

I did not read the stereocentres off the wedges and hashes one by one. I assigned them to match natural amatoxin (L-amino acids, Dhi 2S,3R,4R, Hyp 2S,4R) and checked each @/@@ against CIP priorities. I only glanced at the drawing to confirm it looked consistent with that.
