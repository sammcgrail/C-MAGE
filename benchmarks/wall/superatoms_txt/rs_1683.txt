The structure is a tetrahydrocarbazole: an indole fused to a cyclohexane ring, with substituents as follows.

- The indole nitrogen carries a 4-chlorobenzyl group.
- C1 of the saturated ring (next to the indole C2) carries a CH2CO2H group.
- On the benzene ring, fluorine sits at C6 and a methylsulfonyl group at C8, next to the carbon bonded to N.

The bond from C1 to the ring carbon looks bold (wedge-like) near the junction, but it is drawn only as a thickened line. I treat it as plain, so there is no stereo.
