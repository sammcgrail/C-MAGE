- C1 (bearing N) is bonded to C2 and to C10a.
- C2 and C3 are CH, and C3 connects to C3a.
- C3a connects to C4 (CH), which connects to C5 (CH), which connects to C5a.
- C5a connects to C6, C7 and C8, then to C8a.
