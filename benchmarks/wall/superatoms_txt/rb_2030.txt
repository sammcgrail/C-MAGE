This SMILES is read from the drawing. The macrocycle is a peptide closed by a thioether, and I traced the ring residue by residue from the glycinamide end.

**Sequence, in the order the amide bonds run around the ring:**
- Gly-NH2 is the C-terminal amide.
- Cys is attached to Gly through its C=O, and its side-chain sulfur forms the thioether S–CH2–C(=O) to the N-terminus of the next residue.
- Phe, Arg, N-Me-Phe, Ser, Val, Asn, Tyr, Lys, Arg, Asp follow in that order.
- Tic (tetrahydroisoquinoline-3-carboxylic acid, a cyclic amino acid whose ring nitrogen is acylated by Asp) comes next.
- Asn and N-Me-Phe follow, and the N-Me-Phe carbonyl closes the ring onto the Cys nitrogen.
- Each Arg side chain ends in a guanidine, and the Asp side chain is a free carboxylic acid.

**Stereochemistry:** I worked out each alpha-carbon from its wedge or hash and the CIP priorities. Every residue comes out L (S, or R for Cys because of the sulfur) except Val, whose hashed isopropyl gives D-Val. The upper Asn is read as L on the assumption that its side-chain bond is a wedge, since the bond is hard to make out in the image.
