The drawing shows Ph–CH2–CH(NHBoc)–CO2H, which is Boc-protected phenylalanine. The stereocentre carries a hashed bond to the CO2H group.

**Connectivity:**
- Ph is a phenyl ring, `c1ccccc1`, attached to a CH2 group.
- The CH2 is bonded to the central CH, which carries NH–Boc.
- Boc expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.
- The remaining substituent on the central carbon is the carboxylic acid, C(=O)O.

**Stereochemistry:**
- The CO2H is hashed, so it points away from the viewer, and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H.
- Viewed from the front, N (right) → CO2H (lower left) → CH2 (upper left) runs clockwise.
- Because the lowest-priority H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is S. This is the L-phenylalanine configuration.
