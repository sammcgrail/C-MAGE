Reading the structure region by region:

- **Biphenyl part:** the top-left ring carries SO2NH2 on the carbon adjacent to the ring-junction carbon, so it is an ortho-substituted sulfonamide. That ring is bonded to a second ring.
- **Second ring:** it has the amide NH at one position and F ortho to the NH. The biaryl bond sits para to the NH (the carbon at the top left of the ring). Numbering from the NH carbon as C1, F is at C2, C3 is the CH between F and the biaryl carbon, C4 carries the biaryl bond, and C5 and C6 are CH. This gives the fragment N-c1ccc(-c2ccccc2S(N)(=O)=O)cc1F.
- **Benzamide part:** the amide C(=O) is attached to a ring carbon. The adjacent ring carbon carries the O-aryl group. NO2 is on the carbon meta to the carbonyl carbon and para to the O. Going around the ring: C1 (C=O), C2 (OAr), C3 (CH), C4 (CH), C5 (NO2), C6 (CH). The NO2 carbon is the upper-right vertex, next to the C6 CH, which is next to C1. So NO2 is at C5, para to the O-bearing C2.
- **Amidine ring:** the phenoxy ring carries C(=N)N at the meta position.
