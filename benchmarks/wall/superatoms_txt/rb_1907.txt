Reading the structure region by region:

**Left side:** a glutarimide ring (NH between two C=O, plus CH2CH2 and a CH) attached through the CH to the N of a phthalimide. The phthalimide has two C=O groups, and its benzene ring carries an O substituent at position 4.

**Linker:** the aryl-O is followed by a chain O-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH2-C(=O)-NH. Counting the vertices between O and C=O from the drawing gives five CH2 groups: O, then vertices at about 425, 480, 535, 595 and 650, then the carbonyl carbon at about 710. Five CH2 groups is a reasonable count.

The amide N is followed by CH2CH2, then S(=O)(=O), then the piperidine N.

**Piperidine:** the ring N is attached to the sulfonyl, and C4 carries an NH that links to pyrimidine C2.

**Pyrimidine:** C2 bears the NH-piperidine. C4 bears the NH-aryl. C5 bears Br, and C6 is CH.

**Aryl ring:** the carbon attached to NH is adjacent to the carbon bearing C(=O)NH2, which is adjacent to the carbon bearing F.

- Glutarimide and phthalimide: O=C1CCC(N2C(=O)c3c(OCCCCCC(=O)N...)cccc3C2=O)C(=O)N1
- Linker and piperidine: NCCS(=O)(=O)N1CCC(CC1)Nc1ncc(Br)c(n1)Nc1cccc(F)c1C(N)=O

The chain written as OCCCCCC(=O) has five CH2 groups followed by the carbonyl C, which matches the count above.
