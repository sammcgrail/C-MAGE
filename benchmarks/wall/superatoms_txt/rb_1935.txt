This SMILES was read from the drawing, not checked with software. It describes a bicyclic peptide on a triazinane (TATA-type) scaffold, with a BCN-triazole at the C-terminal lysine and a phosphate linker ending in an open attachment point.

**Peptide sequence**
- The sequence runs from the N-terminus: Ac-Cys1, Pro, Hyp, Asp, Ala, Tyr, Leu, Gly, Cys2, tert-butyl-Gly, Ser, Tyr, Cys3, Glu, Pro, Trp, Lys-NH2.
- Hyp is 4-hydroxyproline.
- I wrote all residues as the natural L-forms. For Cys1 and Cys2 I checked the wedge/hash geometry and it comes out as R, which is L-cysteine. I did not check the other residues individually.

**Scaffold**
- The three cysteine sulfurs are each linked through S–CH2–C(=O) to the three nitrogens of a hexahydro-1,3,5-triazine ring.

**C-terminal lysine**
- The lysine side chain ends in a 1,2,3-triazole fused to a cyclooctene, which is also fused to a cyclopropane. This is a BCN-type unit.
- The cyclopropane CH2–O is hashed and both ring-fusion H atoms are wedged, so I assigned stereo from that geometry. It is the endo form, with ring-fusion stereo C@, C@@, C@ in the order written.

**Linker**
- The BCN CH2–O is a carbamate to NH–(CH2)3–CH(CH2OH)–CH2–O–P(=O)(OH)–, ending at the wavy bond. I counted three CH2 groups between NH and the branch carbon by estimating bond lengths in the drawing. This is the least certain part of the reading.
