I read the structure as a pomalidomide-type PROTAC: a 4-amino-substituted thalidomide-like unit, a piperidine, a five-carbon chain, a carbamate, and a TBDMS-protected lovastatin-type lactone.

- **Left end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the nitrogen of a phthalimide, with an NH at the 4-position of the benzene ring.
- **Linker:** that NH is attached to C4 of a piperidine. The piperidine nitrogen carries five CH2 groups, ending in an NH that forms a carbamate, NC(=O)O.
- **Right end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on C1 of a hexahydronaphthalene. It has a C3 methyl, a C4=C4a double bond, a C5=C6 double bond, and a C7 methyl. C8 carries a CH2CH2 chain to the lactone.
- **Lactone:** a six-membered lactone with an OTBS group, written as O[Si](C)(C)C(C)(C)C.

I counted the chain as five carbons from the zigzag in the drawing. For stereochemistry I assumed the natural lovastatin configuration and did not independently verify each wedge and hash against it. The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
