I read the molecule as a VHL-type ligand joined by a keto-diamide linker to a spiro-indane oxazolidinedione, and wrote the SMILES region by region.

**VHL end (left).**
- A 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group sits on a para-substituted benzene.
- The benzene carries CH2–NH–C(=O) attached to a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine.
- The pyrrolidine nitrogen is acylated by a tert-leucine whose NH continues into the linker.
- The stereocentres are the usual (2S,4R) hydroxyproline and (S) tert-leucine; the wedges and hashes match this.

**Linker.** Counting zigzag vertices from the left amide gives C(=O), then 7 CH2, then a ketone C(=O), then 2 CH2, then C(=O)–NH. This is written as `C(=O)CCCCCCCC(=O)CCC(=O)N`, where the first seven C's after the carbonyl are CH2 and the eighth is the ketone carbon.

**Right end.**
- The NH is on the indane benzene ring, para to the carbon bonded to the spiro centre.
- The spiro carbon bears O (hashed), C(=O) (wedged), the aryl carbon, and the ring CH2. I assigned `[C@@]` from the 2D geometry using the neighbour order in the SMILES (CH2, aryl, C=O, O).
- The oxazolidine-2,4-dione nitrogen carries CH2–C(=O)–N(4-fluorobenzyl)–CH(CH3)CF3.
- The methyl on that CH is hashed. The priorities N > CF3 > CH3 > H give S, which is written `[C@@H]` in `N[C@@H](C)C(F)(F)F`.
