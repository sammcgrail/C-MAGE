This SMILES was read from the drawing and not checked with software. The stereo marks follow the usual L-configuration for the standard residues, not each individual wedge, so they are my best guess.

The molecule is a disulfide-cyclised peptide, read from the N-terminus:

1. Ac-Arg (N-acetylarginine).
2. Penicillamine (Pen), whose two gem-dimethyl β-carbons carry the S–S bridge.
3. Asn.
4. Thr.
5. 7-methyl-Trp.
6. Lys, with its side-chain amine acylated by a chain of two 8-amino-3,6-dioxaoctanoyl (OEG) linkers. These are followed by γ-Glu, whose α-amine carries a lauroyl group (dodecanoyl, 11 carbons after the C=O).
7. A second Pen, closing the disulfide ring with the first.
8. Tyr with the phenol O-alkylated as OCH2CH2NH2.
9. 2-Nal (2-naphthylalanine).
10. 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid.
11. Glu.
12. Gln.
13. Phe.
14. Sarcosinamide (N-methylglycinamide) at the C-terminus.

The macrocycle runs Pen1 Cα, its C=O, Asn, Thr, Trp, Lys, then Pen2 and the S–S bond back to Pen1.
