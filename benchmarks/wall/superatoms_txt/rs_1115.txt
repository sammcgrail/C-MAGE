I read the drawing as three parts: a pyridine head group, a nine-membered diester ring, and a Cbz-protected amine.

**Pyridine part.** The ring has OMe at C4, OH at C3 and the carboxamide at C2, next to the ring nitrogen. This gives COc1ccnc(C(=O)N…)c1O.

**Central ring.** The amide NH is attached to a ring CH. Going round the ring from that CH, the atoms are CH2, O, C(=O), CH(NHCbz), CH2, C(=O), O, CH2, which closes back on the first CH. That is nine ring atoms in total.

**Side chain.** The CH(NHCbz) carbon carries NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–Ph, which is the benzyl carbamate (Cbz).

**Stereochemistry.** Only the CH bearing the Cbz-NH group is drawn with a wedge. The CH bearing the pyridine amide is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label. From the wedge geometry the wedged centre is S. In the SMILES below, the written neighbour order at that carbon is C(=O), H, N, CH2. With `@@` this gives S, which matches the drawing.

SMILES: COc1ccnc(C(=O)NC2COC(=O)[C@@H](NC(=O)OCc3ccccc3)CC(=O)OC2)c1O
