**Structure as I read it**
- **Sugar arms:** three identical GalNAc units, each a β-O-glycoside with an N-acetyl group, linked through O-(CH2)4-C(=O)-NH-(CH2)3-NH-C(=O)-CH2CH2-O-CH2-. All three arms attach to one central quaternary carbon.
- **Spacer:** the central carbon carries an NH-C(=O)-CH2CH2- group, then nine O units separated by CH2CH2, then CH2CH2-C(=O)-NH. This amide is attached to the Lys side-chain ε-nitrogen of the peptide.
- **Cyclic peptide:** the ring runs Lys, Asn, Tyr, Sar (N-methylglycine), Arg, Asp, Tic (tetrahydroisoquinoline-3-carboxylic acid), Asn, N-Me-Phe, then Cys, which carries a C-terminal Gly-NH2. The ring closes through a thioether: a chloroacetyl-derived CH2-S bridge links the Cys sulfur to the N-terminal Phe. From that Phe the chain continues Arg, N-Me-Phe, Ser, and back to the Lys nitrogen.

**Stereochemistry**
- The sugar stereocentres are written as β-D-GalNAc, applied identically to all three arms.
- The amino acids are written as L, including Tic, because I could not reliably resolve each wedge in the image.

I wrote the peptide ring in the C→N direction so that the ring-closure digits and chirality tags stay consistent.
