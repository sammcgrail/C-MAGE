This looks like a STING agonist macrocycle: two benzimidazole units joined by a but-2-ene linker on N1 of each, with the 7-oxygens linked through a CH2-CH(N-piperidinyl)-CH2 bridge. The piperidine carries a 4-phosphate.

**Upper benzimidazole:** N1 carries the butenyl chain, C2 carries NHC(=O)-pyrazole, and N3 is the unsubstituted ring nitrogen. The benzene ring has the carboxamide at C5 and the O-CH2 at C7. The pyrazole is 1-ethyl-3-methyl-pyrazol-5-yl: CCn1nc(C)cc1C(=O)N.

**Butene linker:** N-CH2-CH=CH-CH2-N. The double bond is drawn as a plain zigzag, but it sits in a ring and the geometry looks like E. The upper CH2 goes down to the CH=, which then goes down-left to the next CH=, and then to CH2 and the lower N. I will assign E (trans), though the drawing is ambiguous.
