I read this as a decavalent α-D-mannoside cluster built on four L-lysines and ending in an NHS ester. The SMILES below was assembled from that reading and was not run through a parser.

**Reading of the structure**
- **Activated end:** an N-hydroxysuccinimide ester of a 6-aminohexanoic acid chain, with 5 CH2 groups between the ester carbonyl and the NH.
- **Lysine backbone:** four lysines in a row. Each lysine's carboxyl is amidated by the previous lysine's ε-NH, starting from the 6-aminohexanoyl NH at the top.
- **Branches on lysines:** each lysine α-NH carries a –C(=O)CH2–N(CH2C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–O–Man)2 branch, giving 8 mannoses.
- **Tail:** the last lysine's ε-NH is acylated by a 7-aminoheptanoyl chain, which I counted as 6 CH2. This count was hard to read from the drawing. Its terminal NH carries one more iminodiacetic bis-mannoside branch, so there are 10 mannoses in total.
- **Sugar:** each is an α-D-mannopyranoside linked through O1 to the aminoethyl group. I derived the stereo string by rewriting the α-D-mannose SMILES so it starts from the glycosidic oxygen.
- **Lysine stereochemistry:** the drawn wedges and hashes give S, i.e. L-lysine, at each α-carbon.
