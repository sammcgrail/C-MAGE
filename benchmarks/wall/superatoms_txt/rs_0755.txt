The drawing shows a biphenyl-2-carboxamide with a methoxy group on one ring and an N-aryl amide substituent.

**Biphenyl part.** The left ring carries the amide carbon at one position. The adjacent ring carbon bears the phenyl group. The OMe group sits on the carbon para to the phenyl-bearing carbon, which is meta to the carbonyl-bearing carbon.

Tracing the left ring: C1 (carbonyl-bearing, bottom right), C2 (phenyl-bearing, top right), C3 (top, left of C2), C4 (upper left), C5 (OMe-bearing, left), C6 (bottom). The OMe carbon is therefore adjacent to C6, which is adjacent to C1. That puts OMe para to C2 (the phenyl carbon) and meta to C1.

**Anilide part.** The N-bound ring carbon is C1'. Going around the ring:

- C3' has the pyrrolidin-1-yl group.
- C4' has a methyl.
- C5' is CH.
- C6' has a methyl.
