The molecule is a benzamide core carrying three substituents, read as follows.

**Central benzene ring.** The ring carbon at the top bears OMe. Going around the ring, the next carbon (upper right) is CH, followed by the carbon bearing the C(=O)NH–pyridyl group. After that comes the carbon bearing NH–C(=O)–aryl, then two CH carbons back to the OMe carbon.

Numbering from the carboxamide carbon, C1 carries the amide, C2 carries the NHC(=O)Ar group, C3 is CH, C4 is CH, C5 carries OMe and C6 is CH. This places OMe para to the NH carbon and meta to the carboxamide carbon, which matches the drawing.

**Amide to pyridine.** The carboxamide nitrogen is attached to C2 of a pyridine ring. The ring nitrogen is adjacent to that carbon, and Br sits on C5 of the pyridine.

**Other amide.** The NH on the central ring is acylated by a 4-cyanobenzoyl group.
