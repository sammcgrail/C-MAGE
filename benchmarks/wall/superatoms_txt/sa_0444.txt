The other NH carries a methyl carbamate (NH–C(=O)OCH3, drawn as MeO2C). Viewed from the front with the H pointing away, methyl → CH2 → N runs clockwise. That corresponds to `C[C@@H](CN...)N...` in SMILES order.

**Central rings.** The pyrimidine is attached at C2 to the NH and at C4 to the pyrazole C4. The pyrazole has an N-isopropyl group (iPr) on N1, and its C3 carries the aryl ring.

**Aryl ring.** Going around from the pyrazole attachment, the ring has F, then NH–SO2CH3 (drawn as MeO2S, i.e. a methanesulfonamide), then CH, then Cl, then CH.
