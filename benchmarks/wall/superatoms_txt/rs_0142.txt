The drawing shows a methyl ester of an amino acid: CO2Me on the alpha carbon, which carries H2N, and a CH2 linking the alpha carbon to a pyridine ring.

Pyridine ring: N sits at the lower left, and the C–OMe carbon is at the bottom, adjacent to N. The CH2 attachment is at the upper left carbon, which is bonded to the CH= carbon next to N. Numbering from N as 1, the OMe carbon is C2 and the carbon next to N on the other side is C6. The CH2-bearing carbon is bonded to C6, so it is C5. This gives a 2-methoxy-5-substituted pyridine, i.e. 6-methoxypyridin-3-yl.

The alpha carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
