I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule with three parts joined in a chain:

1. **Pomalidomide end (left):** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the nitrogen of a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione). The phthalimide carries an NH at position 4.
2. **Linker (middle):**
   - NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, a short PEG chain ending in an amide nitrogen.
   - That amide is C(=O)–CH2CH2–C(CH3)2–C(=O)–O, a 2,2-dimethylpentanedioic acid unit whose ester end attaches to the statin.
3. **Simvastatin end (right):** the statin's hexahydronaphthalene core, esterified through the linker, with its 6-membered lactone bearing the 4-hydroxy group on the side chain.

For the statin stereocentres I used the standard simvastatin stereodescriptors, which match the wedges and hashes in the drawing. The piperidinedione carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

I wrote the SMILES by joining these parts and checked that the ring-closure digits and atom ordering at each stereocentre are unchanged from the simvastatin pattern.
