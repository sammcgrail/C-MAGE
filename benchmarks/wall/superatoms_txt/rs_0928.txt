I read the molecule as a pyrido-benzoxazepine core joined through a pyrrolidine to a methoxypyridazine by an ethylene linker.

- **Pyridazine:** the ring has two adjacent N atoms. The carbon next to one N carries the CH2CH2 chain, and the carbon two positions further round, adjacent to the CH that sits next to the other N, carries OMe.
- **Linker and pyrrolidine:** the CH2CH2 chain goes to the pyrrolidine N. The ring carbon at position 3 bears the tricyclic N.
- **Tricyclic core:**
  - The pyridine N sits next to the carbon that bonds to the bridging N.
  - The adjacent pyridine carbon carries a CH2, which links to O and then to the benzene ring.
  - The benzene carbon next to the one bearing O is CH, followed by C–F.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is pyrrolidine C3, drawn with a hashed bond to the tricyclic N. The priorities are N(tricyclic) > CH2 next to the pyrrolidine N > ring CH2 > H, and the H points toward the viewer. The three remaining substituents appear counterclockwise from the front, so the centre is R. In the SMILES below, `[C@H]` gives R.

SMILES: COc1cc(CCN2CC[C@H](C2)N2c3ncccc3COc3cc(F)ccc32)nnc1
