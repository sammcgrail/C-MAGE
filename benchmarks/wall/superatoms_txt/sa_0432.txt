The drawing shows a 3-cyanoquinoline core (the pelitinib/EKB-569 type of scaffold), here carrying a pyridylmethoxy-substituted anilino group.

**Quinoline core**
- Ring N is at the bottom, bonded to C2 (CH), then C3 (bearing CN), then C4 (bearing the NH-aryl group), then C4a.
- The benzo ring runs C4a, C5 (CH), C6 (NH-amide), C7 (OEt), C8 (CH), C8a.

**Substituents**
- The amide is NH–C(=O)–CH=CH–CH2–NMe2. The double bond has trans geometry as drawn, so it is written as E.
- The ethoxy group is on C7.
- The 4-NH links to a phenyl ring that carries Cl and the O–CH2–(2-pyridyl) group. Numbering from the NH-bearing carbon, Cl is at the meta position and the OCH2-pyridyl group is para.

**SMILES assembly**
- Amide side chain: CN(C)C/C=C/C(=O)N
- Quinoline: c1c(NC(=O)/C=C/CN(C)C)c(OCC)cc2ncc(C#N)c(Nc3ccc(OCc4ccccn4)c(Cl)c3)c12

Checking the ring closures: c1 is C5, then C6 (with the amide), C7 (OEt), C8 (cc2 gives C8 and then C8a), n, c (C2), c(C#N) (C3), c(NAr) (C4), and c12 (C4a) closes to C5 and C8a.
