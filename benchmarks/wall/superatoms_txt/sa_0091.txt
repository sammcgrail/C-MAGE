The drawing shows N-Boc-proline methyl ester: a five-membered pyrrolidine ring (one N and four carbons) with a Boc group on the nitrogen and a CO2Me group on C2.

**Ring and substituents:** The ring is N, C2, C3, C4, C5, all single bonds. Boc on N expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C. MeO2C on C2 expands to C(=O)OC.

**Stereochemistry at C2:** The CO2Me bond is a hashed wedge, so that group points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > C3 > H.
- In the drawing, N is at the bottom, CO2Me at the upper left and C3 at the upper right. Going N → CO2Me → C3 is clockwise.
- Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This is the L-proline configuration.

The SMILES `OC(=O)[C@@H]1CCCN1` encodes L-proline, so I used the same chirality tag with the ester and Boc groups added.
