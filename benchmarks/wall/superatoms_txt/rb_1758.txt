This is an EZH2-targeting PROTAC: an EZH2 inhibitor (the dimethylpyridone benzamide) joined through a triazole linker to a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**Left fragment (pyridone):** The ring is 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl, attached through a CH2 to the amide NH. SMILES: O=c1[nH]c(C)cc(C)c1CNC(=O)...

**Central benzamide ring:**
- C1 carries the C(=O)N group.
- C2 carries a methyl.
- C3 carries N(Et)(tetrahydropyran-4-yl).
- C4 is H.
- C5 carries the aryl group.
- C6 is H.

SMILES: C(=O)c1c(C)c(N(CC)C2CCOCC2)cc(c1)-c1ccc(CN...)cc1

**Linker:** The para-substituted phenyl bears a CH2 attached to piperazine N1. Piperazine N4 carries a CH2 that leads to the triazole C4. The triazole N1 carries CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-NH-, which is attached to the phthalimide at the 4-position.

Triazole: c1cn(CCOCCNAr)nn1, with the CH2 on c4.

**Right fragment:** The phthalimide is N-substituted with 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl, and the NH sits at position 4 of the isoindoline-1,3-dione: O=C1CCC(N2C(=O)c3cccc(NCC...)c3C2=O)C(=O)N1
