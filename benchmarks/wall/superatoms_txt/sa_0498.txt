I read the structure as a trans-decalin core carrying five features:

- a spiro-epoxide on the upper ring;
- an (E)-vinyl linker from that ring to a butenolide (2(5H)-furanone) attached at its α-carbon;
- an angular methyl on one ring-fusion carbon, drawn hashed;
- a ring-fusion H, drawn as a wedge;
- a quaternary carbon bearing a wedged methyl and a CH2OTIPS group, next to a CH carrying a hashed OAc.

Stereo marks were assigned only at the six centres with wedges or hashes, plus the trans C=C:

- the vinyl-bearing CH (hashed bond to the vinyl carbon);
- the angular-methyl carbon;
- the OAc carbon;
- the quaternary C(CH3)(CH2OTIPS);
- the ring-fusion CH;
- the spiro epoxide carbon.

I worked the @/@@ marks out by hand from the drawn geometry and did not run the SMILES through a parser.
