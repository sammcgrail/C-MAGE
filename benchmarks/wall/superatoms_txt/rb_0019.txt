I read this as a symmetric carotenoid (astaxanthin-type) skeleton with a hydrogen phosphate monoanion on each end. I wrote it in three parts.

**End rings (each one).** A cyclohexene ring carries a gem-dimethyl carbon, a CH₂, and a CH–O–P(=O)(OH)O⁻ carbon. Next to that is a C=O, then a C(CH₃)= carbon that is double-bonded to the ring carbon bearing the chain. That ring carbon sits next to the gem-dimethyl carbon.

**Polyene chain.** Starting from the left ring, the chain is CH=CH–C(CH₃)=CH–CH=CH–C(CH₃)=CH–CH=CH–CH=C(CH₃)–CH=CH–CH=C(CH₃)–CH=CH, ending at the right ring. The four chain methyls match the drawing, and the pattern is symmetric.

**Stereochemistry.** Every chain double bond is drawn trans, so I used `/…/` throughout. The ring stereocentres (the CH–O–P carbons) are drawn with plain bonds, so I gave them no stereo.
