This is a PROTAC-type molecule that I read in three parts, left to right.

- **Left end:** a 3-amino-6-(2-hydroxyphenyl)pyridazine carrying an ether oxygen at C4. That oxygen is attached to a CH2 on a bicyclo[1.1.1]pentane.
- **Linker:** the other bridgehead of the bicyclopentane carries CH2–NH–C(=O)–CH2. This is followed by four ether oxygens separated by CH2CH2 units (O–CC–O–CC–O–CC–O) and a final CH2–C(=O)–NH.

For stereochemistry, I took the standard VH032 configuration (S tert-leucine, hydroxyproline as in VH032). I then reordered the neighbours for the direction I wrote the SMILES in.
