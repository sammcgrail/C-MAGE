The drawing shows a biphenyl unit in which the first benzene ring carries a sulfonamide (SO2NH2) at the ortho position. The second benzene ring is para-substituted and bears a C(=O)NH group.

That NH is attached to the 3-position of a pyridine ring. At the 2-position of this pyridine (adjacent to the ring nitrogen) there is a C(=O)NH group. This second amide NH is bonded to the 2-position of a 5-chloropyridine ring.

Checking the pyridine ring positions: the pyridine nitrogen sits at the lower right of the ring. The carbon bearing the carboxamide is next to that nitrogen, and the carbon bearing the NH amide is next to it. The ring is therefore a pyridine-2-carboxamide with a 3-amido substituent.

For the chloropyridine ring, the NH is attached at C2, next to the ring nitrogen. Chlorine is at the position para to C2, which is C5.
