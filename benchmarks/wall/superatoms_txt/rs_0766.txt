I read the structure as two ring systems joined by a single bond: a xanthone on the right and a chromone (flavone-type) on the left.

**Xanthone (right)**
- The left benzene ring carries, going around from the ring oxygen:
  - C4a (attached to O)
  - C4, bearing the acetyl group C(=O)C
  - C3, bearing Me
  - C2, bearing the chromone substituent
  - C1, a CH
  - C9a, attached to the C=O carbon
- The right benzene ring carries, from the ring oxygen:
  - C(O) attached to O
  - CH
  - C-OH
  - C-OH
  - C-COOH
  - C attached to the C=O carbon
- The central ring contains O, the C=O carbon, and the fused carbons.

**Chromone (left)**
- C2 is attached to the xanthone.
- C3 is a CH.
- C4 is the C=O.
- C4a is a fusion carbon.
- C5 bears COOH.
- C6 is a CH.
- C7 bears OH.
- C8 is a CH.
- C8a is attached to O.
