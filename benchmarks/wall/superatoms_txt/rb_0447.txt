I read this as a symmetric dimer of a bicyclic thiazepine–pyrrolidine lactam, a Smac-mimetic type scaffold. Two copies are joined through a 1,2-disubstituted indane ether linker with a phenyl–alkyne spacer.

**Each half of the molecule**
- The bicyclic core is a 7-membered ring containing S, fused to a pyrrolidine that carries a gem-dimethyl group. The ring-fusion carbon bears a hashed H, and the lactam C=O sits next to the ring N.
- The core's 7-membered-ring carbon carries an NH–C(=O)–CH(CH3)–NHCH3 side chain (an N-methyl-alanine amide). The wedge/hash drawing gives the L-alanine configuration.
- The pyrrolidine CH carries a wedged C(=O)NH group that leads to the indane.

**Linker**
- The left indane is attached to the core amide N at C1 and carries an O–CH2 on C2. That CH2 is bonded to a para-substituted benzene.
- The benzene's other para position holds a C≡C–CH2–O group, which leads to C2 of the right indane.
- The right indane's C1 carries the NH of the second core amide.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash and the 2D geometry of its neighbours, then converted that to @/@@ by counting neighbour permutations. I did not re-derive the CIP labels independently.
