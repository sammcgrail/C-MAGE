I read this as the MacMillan imidazolidinone organocatalyst, as its TFA salt.

**Ring and substituents**
- The five-membered ring is an imidazolidin-4-one. Going round it: N3 carries a methyl, C4 is the C=O, C5 carries the CH2–indole group, N1 is the NH, and C2 carries the tert-butyl group.
- The side chain is CH2 attached to the 3-position of a 1H-indole.
- "•TFA" is trifluoroacetic acid, written as a separate component.

**Stereochemistry**
- C2: the tert-butyl bond is hashed, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N3 > N1 > tBu > H. N3 → N1 → tBu runs clockwise on the page, which reverses to counterclockwise with H toward the viewer, so C2 is S.
- C5: the CH2 bond is hashed, so the implicit H again points toward the viewer. The priorities are N1 > C4 (C=O) > CH2 > H. N1 → C4 → CH2 runs clockwise on the page, which reverses to counterclockwise, so C5 is S.
- The molecule is therefore (2S,5S). In the SMILES below, `@@` at both centres gives S at each.

SMILES: CN1[C@@H](C(C)(C)C)N[C@@H](Cc2c[nH]c3ccccc23)C1=O.OC(=O)C(F)(F)F
