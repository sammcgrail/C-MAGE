The structure is a PROTAC-like molecule: a ribociclib-type pyrrolopyrimidine joined through an alkyne linker to a lenalidomide-type isoindolinone.

**Pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidine part.** The pyrrole carbon bears C(=O)N(C)C, and N7 carries a cyclopentyl group. The pyrimidine C2 carries the NH link to the benzene ring.
SMILES for this fragment: CN(C)C(=O)c1cc2cnc(Nc3ccc(cc3)...)nc2n1C1CCCC1

**Linker.** The benzene ring carries C(=O)NH–CH2CH2CH2–C≡C–. Counting from the amide NH, the chain goes CH2, CH2, CH2, then the alkyne, so the amide N is followed by three CH2 groups and then C≡C. The alkyne end attaches to the isoindolinone aromatic ring.

**Isoindolinone part.** The aromatic carbon bearing the alkyne is adjacent to the ring-fusion carbon that connects to the CH2. That CH2 bonds to N, and N bonds to the C=O, which in turn bonds to the other fusion carbon. N also carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The stereocentre of the glutarimide is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is assigned.
