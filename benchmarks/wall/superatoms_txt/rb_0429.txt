I read the structure in four pieces and then joined them.

**Thienopyridinone.** The thieno[3,2-c]pyridin-4(5H)-one core has the S-bearing thiophene fused to a pyridone. The N-methyl nitrogen is attached to the C=O carbon (C4) and to C7, which carries the aryl group. In the drawing, C7 is bonded to C7a, which is bonded to S. The thiophene runs S–CH=CH–C3a, and C3a is bonded to C4(=O). The SMILES for this fragment is Cn1cc(-Ar)c2sccc2c1=O.

**Aryl ring.** This is a benzene ring bearing two OMe groups and a CH2–N(piperidine) group between them. The ring is c1(thienopyridone)cc(OC)c(CN...)c(OC)c1.

**Piperidine and spiro unit.** The piperidine N is attached to the benzylic CH2, and its C4 carries a CH2 that links to N7 of the 2,7-diazaspiro[3.5]nonane. That spiro system has a piperidine ring, with N and the spiro carbon, plus an azetidine whose N is attached to the isoindolinone aryl ring.

**Isoindolinone.** The aryl ring is fused to a CH2–N(lactam)–C(=O) ring. The lactam N is attached to the 3-position of the piperidine-2,6-dione (glutarimide). That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I assign no stereochemistry.
