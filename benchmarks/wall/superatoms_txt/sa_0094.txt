I read the drawing as a 1,5-benzothiazepine 1,1-dioxide core carrying a glycine-terminated side chain. I assigned the stereocentre by hand from the hashed bond.

**Core ring:** a seven-membered ring containing a sulfonyl sulfur (S with two =O) and an N-phenyl nitrogen. Between them lie a CH2 and a quaternary carbon bearing two n-butyl groups, and the ring is fused to a benzene ring.

**Benzene substituents:** going round the ring from the carbon bonded to S, there is a CH, then a carbon bearing the O–CH2 ether, then a carbon bearing SMe, then a CH, then the carbon bonded to N.

**Side chain:**
- The ether chain is O–CH2–C(=O)–NH.
- The NH is attached to a stereocentre carrying a phenyl group (hashed bond).
- That carbon continues to C(=O)–NH–CH2–CO2H.

**Stereochemistry:** the CIP priorities at the stereocentre are N, then C(=O)N, then phenyl, then H. The phenyl is on a hashed bond, so H points toward the viewer. From the front, N → C(=O) → Ph runs counterclockwise, which reverses to clockwise once H is accounted for, giving R. In the SMILES below, `[C@H]` written as N[C@H](Ph)C(=O) corresponds to R, so it matches the drawing.
