Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Top left:** a benzene ring carrying SO2NH2, attached at the adjacent (ortho) position to a second benzene ring. This is a 2-sulfamoylbiphenyl unit.
- **Middle:** the second benzene ring is para-substituted. Its far end carries C(=O)NH, an amide.
- **Central ring:** the amide NH is bonded to a benzene ring carbon. The ortho carbon of that ring carries C(=O)NH-(5-bromopyridin-2-yl). The NH2 sits on the ring carbon para to the NH-bearing carbon.

To check the NH2 position, I numbered the central ring. C1 bears the NH (top left of the ring), C2 bears the carboxamide (bottom left), and C3 is the CH at the bottom. C4 carries NH2 at the lower right, and C5 and C6 are the top CHs. NH2 on C4 is therefore meta to C2 and para to C1, which gives the 2-amido-5-aminobenzamide pattern.

- **Pyridine:** the ring nitrogen is adjacent to the carbon bearing NH (C2). Br is on C5, which is para to C2.
