I read the structure in four parts and joined them.

1. **Left end:** a glutarimide (piperidine-2,6-dione) is attached through its C3 carbon to the nitrogen of an isoindolin-1-one. The isoindolinone has fluorine at C6 and a piperidin-4-yl group at C4.
2. **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen carries a straight chain of 7 CH2 groups. Counting the zigzag vertices from N to the ether oxygen gives 7, so it is a heptyl linker.
3. **Middle:** the oxygen joins a benzene ring that has fluorine on one ortho position and the quinazoline on the other.
4. **Right end:** the quinazoline is attached to the benzene at its C7. It has Cl at C6 and F at C8. Its C4 carries a piperazine whose far nitrogen is acylated with a cyclopentanecarbonyl group.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I included no stereochemistry. The glutarimide C3 is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds.
