**Stereochemistry**
- The only drawn stereocentre is the oxetane C2, which has a hashed bond to the CH2N group.
- The priorities are O > CH2N > ring CH2 > H. With the hashed CH2N pointing back, the H points toward the viewer, so the apparent sense is reversed.
- O → CH2N → ring CH2 appears clockwise, so the true configuration is counterclockwise, which is S.
- In the SMILES, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is CH2N, H, O, C3. Here `@@` gives S, so I used `[C@@H]`.
