Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Pyrimidine ring:** the ring has two nitrogens. NH2 sits on the carbon between them (C2). SMe (S–CH3) sits on one adjacent carbon, with a ring CH between it and the vinyl-bearing carbon.
- **Linker:** a C=C double bond joins the pyrimidine to the benzene ring. The substituents lie on opposite sides of the double bond, so it is drawn as trans (E).
- **Benzene ring:** it is para-substituted, with the vinyl group on one side and OTHP on the other.
- **OTHP:** this is a tetrahydropyran-2-yloxy group, written O–C1CCCCO1. The acetal carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
