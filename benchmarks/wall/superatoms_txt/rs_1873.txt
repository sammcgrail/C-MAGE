The drawing shows a 3-cyanobenzenesulfonamide whose nitrogen is bonded to a substituted phenyl ring.

**Left ring:** a benzene ring with a CN group at one carbon. The sulfonyl group sits on the carbon two positions away, so the cyano and sulfonyl groups are meta to each other.

**Linker:** the sulfonyl S carries two =O groups and is bonded to NH.

**Right ring:** the carbon attached to NH is C1. Going around the ring:
- C2 carries a methyl (the top one).
- C3 carries the pyrrolidin-1-yl group.
- C4 carries a methyl (lower right).
- C5 is CH.
- C6 carries a methyl (lower left).

**SMILES:** writing the left ring as c1(C#N)cccc(c1)S(=O)(=O) and the right ring from the NH carbon gives:

N#Cc1cccc(c1)S(=O)(=O)Nc1c(C)c(N2CCCC2)c(C)cc1C

This has the right ring as c1 (N-bearing), c(C), c(N-pyrrolidine), c(C), cH, c1(C), which matches the drawing.
