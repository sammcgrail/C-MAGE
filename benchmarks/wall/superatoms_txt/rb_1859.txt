I read the molecule as a CRBN-recruiting degrader, built from three regions joined in a chain.

- **Right side:** a thalidomide-type unit. A 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group sits on the nitrogen of a phthalimide. The phthalimide benzene carries an NH–C(=O)–CH2 amide.
- **Middle:** the amide CH2 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to C2 of a pyridine, and the pyridine C6 is bonded to C3 of an imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine.
- **Left side:** C6 of the imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine carries a pyrrolidin-1-yl group. Its C2 bears a 3-fluorophenyl group on a hashed bond.

**Stereochemistry:** only the pyrrolidine C2 is drawn with a wedge/hash, so only it gets a descriptor. The aryl group is on the hashed bond, so the implicit hydrogen points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N, then aryl carbon, then ring CH2, then H. Going N → aryl → CH2 runs counterclockwise on the page, and reversing for the hydrogen facing the viewer gives R. In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]` written after the ring CH2 encodes R, which I checked by reordering the neighbours.

The glutarimide C3 has plain bonds in the drawing, so it gets no stereo label.
