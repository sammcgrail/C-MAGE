Reading the structure region by region, from left to right:

1. **Morpholine–pyridine amide.** A morpholine nitrogen is attached to C6 of a pyridine. C2 of the pyridine carries a C(=O)NH group. This gives O=C(Nc...)c1cccc(N2CCOCC2)n1.

2. **Indazole.** The amide NH is on C5 of a 2H-indazole, and C6 carries OMe. The ring N2 bears the spiro substituent. For the 2H-indazole, the SMILES is c1(NC(=O)...)c(OC)cc2nn(R)cc2c1. Here the carbon bonded to the NH is C5. Its neighbours are C4 (the CH next to C3a) and C6 (the C–OMe). C6 is bonded to C7 (CH), which is bonded to C7a, and C7a is bonded to N1. N1 is then bonded to N2 (bearing R), then C3 (CH), then C3a. This is consistent with the drawing.

3. **Spiro linker.** N2 is attached to a cyclobutane carbon (the CH). The cyclobutane is spiro-fused to a piperidine, giving 2,7-diazaspiro[3.5]nonane-like geometry but with the cyclobutane carbon bonded directly to the indazole. The spiro carbon is shared by the cyclobutane (CH, CH2, spiro C, CH2) and a piperidine ring (spiro C, CH2, CH2, N, CH2, CH2). So it is 7-azaspiro[3.5]nonane with the 2-position on the indazole N and the 7-N acylated. SMILES: C1(n...)CC2(C1)CCN(CC2)C(=O)...

4. **Difluoropiperidine.** The carbonyl is attached to C4 of a piperidine. C3 is CF2, adjacent to C2 (CH2), which is bonded to N1. N1 is attached to the aryl group. SMILES: C(=O)C1CCN(Ar)CC1(F)F.

5. **Aryl and glutarimide.** The aryl group is a phthalimide bearing a substituent at C5 (para to one carbonyl-bearing carbon), with N-(2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl). SMILES: c1ccc2C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C(=O)c2c1.
