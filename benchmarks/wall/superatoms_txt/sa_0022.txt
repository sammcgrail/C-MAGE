The central carbon carries an OH group and three substituents: two CH2CO2Et arms and one CO2Et group. This is triethyl citrate without the acetyl group.

The drawing gives these connections:
- Central carbon to CH2, then to C(=O)OEt (upper left).
- Central carbon to CH2, then to CO2Et (right).
- Central carbon directly to CO2Et (lower left).
- Central carbon to OH.

The central carbon has two identical CH2CO2Et arms, so it is not a stereocentre and needs no stereo marks.

SMILES: CCOC(=O)CC(O)(CC(=O)OCC)C(=O)OCC
