This is an EZH2-targeting PROTAC: an EZH2 inhibitor (an EPZ-6438/tazemetostat-like core) joined through a PEG linker to a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**EZH2 ligand.** The central benzene ring carries these substituents:
- a 4-(morpholinomethyl)phenyl group;
- a methyl group;
- an N-ethyl-N-(piperidin-4-yl)amino group;
- a C(=O)NHCH2 amide linked to 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl.

**Linker.** The piperidine nitrogen is acylated: N–C(=O)CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–O–Ar. Reading the image, the chain is C(=O), CH2, CH2, O, CH2, CH2, O, CH2, CH2, O, CH2, CH2, C(=O), NH, which fits this.

**Cereblon ligand.** The aryl group is a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione) attached through its 4-oxygen, with N-(2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl) on the imide nitrogen. The stereocentre on the glutarimide is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

**Central ring substitution pattern.** Going around the ring:
- C1 carries the aryl group (the morpholinomethylphenyl).
- C2 is the CH between C1 and the amino-bearing carbon.
- C3 carries the N(Et)(piperidinyl) group.
- C4 carries the methyl.
- C5 carries the C(=O)NH amide.
- C6 is the CH between C5 and C1.
