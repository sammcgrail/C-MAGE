I read the structure as a macrocyclic lactone fused through a tetrahydropyran ring, with a PMB-protected thiazolidin-2-one attached at the anomeric carbon. The number of chain CH2 groups in the macrocycle is the least certain part of my reading.

**Tetrahydropyran core**
- The ring has an oxygen, an anomeric carbon (C2) bearing OMe, a CH2, a CH carrying the ester oxygen (wedge), another CH2, and a CH carrying the macrocycle chain (hashed bond).

**Macrocycle**
- The ester oxygen connects to a C(=O), then to a chain of CH2 groups that returns to the ring carbon with the hashed bond.
- I counted 11 CH2 groups in that chain from the zigzag. This count is my best estimate and may be off by one or two.

**Thiazolidinone**
- The anomeric carbon is bonded to C4 of the thiazolidin-2-one, which has a wedged H.
- The ring runs C4–CH2–S–C(=O)–N, and N carries the PMB group (4-methoxybenzyl).

**Stereochemistry**
- I assigned each of the four stereocentres from its wedge or hash and checked the SMILES chirality tags against that geometry.
- The ring carbon bearing the chain is R, and the ester-bearing CH is R.
- The anomeric carbon and the thiazolidinone C4 were assigned from the drawing in the same way.
