The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- N1 is the ring nitrogen at the lower left.
- C2 carries the carboxamide, C(=O)NH.
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are unsubstituted CH.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a phenyl ring at its 1-position. At the 3-position (meta) of that ring is an S–C6H4–CF3 group, with the CF3 para to the sulfur.

Writing the SMILES from the pyridine ring first: COc1ccnc(C(=O)Nc2cccc(Sc3ccc(C(F)(F)F)cc3)c2)c1O

Atom check on the pyridine ring: c1 (C4, bearing OMe), c (C5), c (C6), n, c (C2, bearing the amide), then c1O closes the ring at C3, which bears the OH. This matches the drawing.
