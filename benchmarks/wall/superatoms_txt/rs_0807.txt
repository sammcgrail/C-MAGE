I read the molecule as a nine-membered dilactone core carrying an amide-linked pyridine unit, an isobutyrate ester and a benzyl group.

**Core ring (nine atoms, in order):** C(=O), ring O, CH(CH3), CH(O-isobutyryl), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), ring O, CH2, CH(NH-). The last CH closes back onto the first C(=O).

**Substituents:**
- The NH is acylated by a pyridine-2-carbonyl group. That pyridine has 4-OMe and, at C3, an ester O-C(=O) linked to a 6-chloropyridin-3-yl ring.

- The CH(O-isobutyryl) carbon carries a hashed ester oxygen, with the isobutyryl group OC(=O)CH(CH3)2.
- The CH(CH2Ph) carbon carries a wedged benzyl group.
- The CH(N) carbon carries a hashed NH.
