The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl oxindole.

**Biphenyl part:** The top ring carries CO2H. A second ring is attached to it at the meta position. On that second ring, the carbon bearing the first ring is adjacent to the carbon bearing OH, and the OH carbon is adjacent to the carbon bearing the NH–N= group. The acid is therefore 2'-hydroxy-3'-hydrazinyl-biphenyl-3-carboxylic acid.

**Hydrazone and oxindole:** The NH is attached to N=, which is double-bonded to C3 of the oxindole. C2 is C=O, and N1 carries the aryl group. The fused benzene ring completes the indole.

**N-aryl group:** A para-substituted phenyl bears CH(OH)CF3. The wavy bonds mean the stereocentre is undefined, so I give no stereochemistry. The hydrazone C=N geometry is not clearly specified either, so I omit it.
