This is a bicyclic peptide on a tris(thiomethyl)benzene core, with a DOTA chelator on the N-terminus and a C-terminal primary amide. I read it region by region.

**Sequence, from the DOTA end:** DOTA–Cys–Ala–Asn–Glu–1Nal–Ala–Cys–Glu–Asp–Phe–Tyr–Asp–tert-leucine–Cys–NH2.

- **DOTA:** a 12-membered ring with four nitrogens and three free CH2CO2H arms. The fourth arm is a CH2C(=O) amide to the N-terminal Cys.
- **Scaffold:** a benzene ring with CH2 groups at positions 1, 3 and 5. Each CH2 is bonded to the sulfur of one of the three Cys residues, so the three thioether links form two peptide loops.
- **Side chains:** Glu is CH2CH2CO2H, Asp is CH2CO2H, Asn is CH2C(=O)NH2, and Phe is benzyl. Tyr is 4-hydroxybenzyl, and 1Nal is naphthalen-1-ylmethyl. The Ala residues carry a methyl, and the tert-leucine carries a tert-butyl.
- **Stereochemistry:** I assigned every α-carbon as the natural L configuration, which is how the wedges and hashes read, rather than checking each one individually.
