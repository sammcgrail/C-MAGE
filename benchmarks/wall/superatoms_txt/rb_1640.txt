I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type structure with three parts joined by an amide linker.

**Left end.** A 2-fluoro-6-methoxyphenyl group sits on C5 of a 1H-pyrazolo[4,3-d]pyrimidine. C3 of that core carries a para-phenylene ring, which is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen.

**Linker.** The other piperazine nitrogen bears CH2–C(=O)–NH. That amide nitrogen leads into a chain of 10 CH2 groups ending in a C(=O). I counted the chain by zigzag vertices (about 36 px per bond), giving 11 carbons including the carbonyl. That carbonyl is an amide to the next nitrogen.

**Right end (VHL ligand).**
- The amide nitrogen attaches to an L-tert-leucine unit, which I assigned as (S).
- Its carbonyl is bonded to the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxyproline ring, assigned (2S,4R).
- The proline carboxamide is linked through NH–CH2 to a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl group.
