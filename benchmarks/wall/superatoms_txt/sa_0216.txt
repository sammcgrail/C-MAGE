The drawing shows a pyridine ring joined at its 2-position to a quaternary carbon. That carbon carries a methyl group, a phenyl group (drawn as "Ph") and an O–CH2–CH2–NMe2 chain. The molecule is doxylamine.

Ring check: N is bonded to C2 (the carbon attached to the quaternary carbon) and to C6, with the double bond N=C6. The remaining ring bonds are C6–C5, C5=C4, C4–C3 and C3=C2, which gives a normal aromatic pyridine.

The quaternary carbon has four different substituents (methyl, phenyl, pyridyl, alkoxy), so it is a stereocentre. It is drawn with plain bonds, so I give no stereo.
