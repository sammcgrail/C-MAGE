The drawing shows OHC–CH2–OTBS, which is (tert-butyldimethylsilyloxy)acetaldehyde.

- OHC is the aldehyde group, written O=C.
- The vertex at the bottom of the V is a CH2 carbon.
- OTBS is an oxygen bonded to a tert-butyldimethylsilyl group, Si(C)(C)C(C)(C)C.

Joining these gives O=CCO[Si](C)(C)C(C)(C)C. The valences are correct, and there are no stereocentres or wedge bonds to encode.

SMILES: O=CCO[Si](C)(C)C(C)(C)C
