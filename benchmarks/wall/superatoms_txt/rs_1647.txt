The drawing shows these parts:

- **Biphenyl unit:** a benzene ring carrying SO2NH2 is bonded at its ortho position to a para-substituted benzene ring. That second ring carries a C(=O) group.
- **Central pyridine:** the carbonyl on the biphenyl forms an amide, C(=O)NH, to the C2 position of a pyridine ring. The ring nitrogen is adjacent to C2, and C3 carries a second carbonyl.
- **Second amide:** the C3 carbonyl is C(=O)NH and is bonded to C2 of a pyridine ring. This pyridine has Br at C5, so it is 5-bromopyridin-2-yl.
