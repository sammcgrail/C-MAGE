I read the molecule as an EZH2-ligand/thalidomide-type PROTAC, built from five fragments joined in this order.

1. **Left-hand benzamide.** The central benzene ring carries:
   - a C(=O)NH–CH2 amide to a 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl group;
   - a methyl group;
   - an N(ethyl)(piperidin-4-yl) amine;
   - a 4-(morpholin-4-ylmethyl)phenyl group.
2. **Piperidine.** Its ring nitrogen is acylated by the linker carbonyl.
3. **Linker.** After the carbonyl comes CH2CH2, then seven ether oxygens. The first six are each followed by an ethylene unit (CH2CH2), and the seventh is followed by CH2CH2–C(=O). I counted the oxygens at x ≈ 710, 880, 1050, 1230, 1405, 1575 and 1750 in the drawing.
4. **Amide and ether tether.** The linker ends in an amide, C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–O–, which is attached to the aryl ring of the phthalimide.
5. **Right-hand imide.** The phthalimide's imide nitrogen is attached to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so the piperidinedione stereocentre is left unspecified.
