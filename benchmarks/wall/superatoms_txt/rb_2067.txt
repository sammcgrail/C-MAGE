I read the structure in four pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

- **Left end:** a 4-methoxybenzyl ether on a 3-methoxy-4-oxy-benzyl group. That benzyl is attached to N3 of a 2-amino-3H-imidazo[4,5-b]pyridine.
- **Core:** the pyridine carbon at position 6 of the imidazopyridine carries a pyrazol-4-yl group. The pyrazole N1 bears a CH2C(=O)NH linker.
- **Linker:** NH–CH2CH2 followed by four repeats of O–CH2CH2, where the last repeat leads into C(=O). In SMILES this is NCCOCCOCCOCCOCCC(=O), with four ether oxygens, as drawn.
- **Right end:** a tert-leucine residue, then a 4-hydroxyproline, then an amide to 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamine. This is the VHL ligand VH032 pattern.

For stereochemistry, I took the wedges and hashes on the tert-leucine α-carbon and on the proline C2 and C4 carbons as the natural VH032 configuration. I wrote each centre in the neighbour order of my own string, and the tags below come from converting the standard VH032 SMILES to that order.
