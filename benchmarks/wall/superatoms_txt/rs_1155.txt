Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Side chain:** the stereocentre carries a methyl, a CH2–O–CH3 group, the ring nitrogen, and an H on a wedge.
- **Pyrazinone ring:** N1 (bearing the side chain) is bonded to a C=O carbon and to a CH. That CH is double-bonded to the C–Cl carbon, which connects to ring N. The ring N is double-bonded to the carbon that carries the tetrahydroquinoline nitrogen, and that carbon is bonded to the C=O.
- **Tetrahydroquinoline:** N1 is joined to a three-carbon saturated chain (CH2CH2CH2) that closes onto the benzene ring. The benzene ring carries Br at C8, ortho to the ring nitrogen, and OMe at C6.

**Stereochemistry:** the H is on a wedge, so it points toward the viewer. On the page, methyl (upper left), CH2 (upper right) and N (bottom) run clockwise. Viewed from the H side, these three therefore run clockwise, which gives @@ for the neighbour order (H, Me, CH2, N). Rewriting the order as (Me, H, CH2, N) swaps two neighbours and flips the label to @, giving C[C@H](COC)n1…
