I read this as a rifamycin-type ansa macrolide fused to a benzoxazine (phenoxazine) ring, carrying an OCH2CH2N(Me)Ac side chain on the benzo ring.

**Core and side chain**
- The side chain on the benzo ring is OCH2CH2N(CH3)C(=O)CH3.
- The phenoxazine has C=N on one side and ring O on the other.
- The adjacent quinone ring has a C=O and an NH amide carbon.
- The naphthoquinone-type core carries a phenolic OH and an aromatic CH3.
- The core is fused to a five-membered ketal ring made of the C=O, the quaternary carbon (CH3, O, O-vinyl) and the ring O.

**Ansa chain**, running from the amide N:
- C(=O)–C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH, with the first double bond cis and the second trans.
- Then CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OAc), CH(CH3), CH(OMe).
- Then trans CH=CH–O, which closes onto the ketal carbon.

**Stereochemistry**
