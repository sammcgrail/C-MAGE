**Amide nitrogen substituents**
- One substituent is a 1-benzylpiperidin-4-yl group: a piperidine whose N carries a CH2–phenyl, attached to the amide N through the ring carbon opposite the N (C4).
- The other is a 1-acetylindolin-6-yl group. The indoline N carries the acetyl, and the attachment point is on the benzene ring.

**Position of the attachment on the indoline**
The indoline benzene ring has its fused carbons at the right side of the drawing. The upper fused carbon (C3a) joins the CH2CH2 part, and the lower fused carbon (C7a) is bonded to N1. **Acyl part**
