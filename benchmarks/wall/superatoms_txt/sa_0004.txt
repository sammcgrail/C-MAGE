The drawing shows Boc-protected tyrosine with a benzyl ether on the phenol oxygen (Boc-Tyr(Bn)-OH).

- **Backbone:** the alpha carbon carries an NH–Boc group, a CO2H group and a CH2 group leading to a para-substituted benzene ring. The ring carries OBn (O–CH2–C6H5) opposite the CH2.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CO2H bond is drawn as a wedge, so it points toward the viewer and the implicit H points away. Viewed from the front, N (up) → CO2H (lower left) → CH2 (lower right) runs counterclockwise. With H at the back, that gives S, the L-amino-acid configuration.
- **SMILES form:** the fragment N[C@@H](CR)C(=O)O encodes L-tyrosine, so I used the same pattern here.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N[C@@H](Cc1ccc(OCc2ccccc2)cc1)C(=O)O
