The drawing is a diacylhydrazine (a tebufenozide-type structure). I read it in three parts:

- **Left ring:** a benzene ring bearing an ethyl group (Et) para to a C(=O)NH group.
- **Centre:** the NH is bonded to a second nitrogen, which carries a tert-butyl group (tBu) and a second carbonyl.
- **Right ring:** a benzene ring with methyl groups at the 3 and 5 positions, attached through the second carbonyl.

Expanding Et to CH2CH3 and tBu to C(C)(C)C gives the SMILES below.

SMILES: CCc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NN(C(C)(C)C)C(=O)c1cc(C)cc(C)c1
