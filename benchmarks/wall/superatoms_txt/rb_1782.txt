I read the molecule as a PROTAC-style structure made of four parts, joined by CH2 linkers:

- **Left tricycle:** a pyridazine fused to a dihydropyrazine (bearing the NH) and to a piperazine. The pyridazine carries a 2-hydroxyphenyl group.
- **Linker:** the piperazine N–CH2 goes to the 4-position of a piperidine. The piperidine N–CH2 goes to the 3-position of a pyrrolidine.
- **Aryl link:** the pyrrolidine N is attached to the aromatic ring of an isoindoline-1,3-dione.
- **Right end:** the isoindoline-1,3-dione N carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) group, drawn without stereo.

The only drawn stereocentre is the ring-fusion CH next to the wedged CH2. Its priorities are ring N, then the CH2 on the piperazine N, then the CH2 on the NH, then H. The wedge geometry gives S, which corresponds to `@@` in the SMILES order I wrote.
