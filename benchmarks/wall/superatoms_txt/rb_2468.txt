**Regions of the molecule**
- **Piperazic acid ring:** A six-membered hexahydropyridazine. Its C3 carries the ester carbonyl on a hashed bond and is labelled (S). The adjacent NH is free, and the other ring nitrogen is acylated by the amino acid carbonyl.
- **Macrocyclic linker:** The ester oxygen connects through OCH2–C(CH3)2–CH2 to C3 of the indole.
- **Indole:**
  - C2 carries a pyridin-3-yl group, which has a (S)-1-methoxyethyl group on the carbon next to the ring nitrogen.
  - N1 carries an ethyl group.
  - C5 is bonded to C4 of the thiazole.
- **Thiazole:** C2 carries a CH2 that leads to the (S) α-carbon of the amino acid. That α-carbon bears the NH of the urea and the acyl carbonyl attached to the piperazic nitrogen.
- **Urea side chain:** NH–C(=O)–N(CH3)–CH2–CH(CH3)–OCH3, with the methoxy-bearing carbon labelled (S).

**Stereodescriptors in the SMILES:** `[C@@H]1` for the piperazic carbon, `[C@H]` for the amino acid α-carbon, `[C@H]` for the urea side-chain carbon, and `[C@H]` for the methoxyethyl carbon on the pyridine. All four correspond to the (S) labels in the drawing.
