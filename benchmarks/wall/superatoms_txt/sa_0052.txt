I read the drawing as Boc-L-glutamic acid 5-benzyl ester, Boc-Glu(OBn)-OH.

- **Chain:** BnO2C–CH2–CH2–CH, so the benzyl ester sits on the far end of a two-carbon chain.
- **Stereocentre:** the α-carbon carries NH-Boc, a CO2H drawn on a hashed bond (pointing away from the viewer), the chain, and an implicit H (pointing toward the viewer).
- **Configuration:** the priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 chain > H. Going N → CO2H → chain appears clockwise on the page. The H points toward the viewer, so the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This matches the L-glutamic acid configuration.
- **SMILES chirality:** I checked that `[C@H]` in `CC[C@H](N...)C(=O)O` corresponds to S by comparing it with L-Glu written as `N[C@@H](CCC(=O)O)C(=O)O`. Swapping the chain and N positions flips `@@` to `@`, so the two agree.
