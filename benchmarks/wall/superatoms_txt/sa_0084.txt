I read the drawing as TPCK (tosyl phenylalanyl chloromethyl ketone), with the stereocentre assigned from the single wedge.

**Connectivity**
- A chloromethyl ketone, Cl–CH2–C(=O)–, is attached to the stereocentre.
- The stereocentre also carries an NH–Ts group (Ts = p-toluenesulfonyl) and a CH2–Ph (benzyl) group, drawn on a wedge.
- The fourth substituent is an implicit H.

**Stereochemistry**
- CIP priorities at the stereocentre are N > C(=O)CH2Cl > CH2Ph > H.
- The benzyl CH2 is on the wedge, toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away.
- On the page, N is to the right, C(=O) to the upper left, and CH2Ph below. Going N → C(=O) → CH2Ph is counterclockwise, so the centre is S. This matches the L-phenylalanine configuration.
- For the written order N[C@@H](CPh)C(=O)…, the @@ pattern is the same as in L-phenylalanine, which gives S.

SMILES: Cc1ccc(cc1)S(=O)(=O)N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)CCl
