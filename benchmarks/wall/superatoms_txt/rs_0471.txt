**Acylguanidine.** The carbonyl is bonded to NH, which bonds to C(=NH), which bonds to NH. That NH leads into the butyl chain: C(=O)NC(=N)N.

**Butyl linker.** The chain between the guanidine NH and the piperazine N is drawn as four carbons: the zigzag runs from NH through CH2, CH2, CH2, CH2 to N. So the fragment is NCCCCN1CCN(CC1)...

**Piperazine and propyl chain.** The second piperazine N carries a propyl chain (CH2CH2CH2) leading to NH. That NH bonds to C(=NBoc)NHBoc.

**Boc groups.** Each Boc is C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.
