The drawing is a 2,6-dimethylmorpholine ring cut at the nitrogen, with one wavy attachment line on N, so the SMILES has one `*` on N.

**Ring:** The six-membered ring contains N at the top right and O at the bottom left, with two CH2 groups and two CH(CH3) carbons. The CH(CH3) carbons flank the oxygen, so they are C2 and C6.

**Stereochemistry:** The methyl on C2 (upper left) is drawn with a wedge, and the methyl on C6 (bottom) is drawn with a hash.
- At C2, the priorities are O > CH2(N) > CH3. With the H pointing away, O → CH2 → CH3 runs counterclockwise on the page, so C2 is S.
- At C6, the priorities are the same. The hashed methyl puts the H toward the viewer, and O → CH2 → CH3 runs clockwise on the page. Reversing for the H gives counterclockwise, so C6 is S.

The result is (2S,6S), the trans isomer. I assigned `@` and `@@` in the SMILES below by working through the neighbour order at each stereocentre, and both give S.
