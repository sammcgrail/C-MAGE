The drawing shows a 4-methoxybenzenesulfonamide whose nitrogen is attached to a substituted phenyl ring.

**Sulfonyl side.** A para-substituted benzene carries OMe on one carbon and the sulfonyl group on the opposite carbon. Sulfur has two double-bonded oxygens and is bonded to the NH.

**Aniline ring.** Numbering from the carbon bonded to NH:
- C1 bears the NH.
- C2 (upper right, adjacent to C1) bears a methyl, drawn as the line going up.
- C3 bears the pyrrolidin-1-yl group, attached through its nitrogen.
- C4 bears a methyl, drawn at the lower right.
- C5 is CH.
- C6 bears a methyl, drawn at the lower left.

The ring is therefore 2,4,6-trimethyl with pyrrolidine at position 3.

**SMILES.** Writing the sulfonamide first and then the aniline ring:

COc1ccc(cc1)S(=O)(=O)Nc1c(C)c(N2CCCC2)c(C)cc1C

Check of the aniline ring: c1 (N-bearing), c(C) at C2, c(N2CCCC2) at C3, c(C) at C4, c at C5 (H), c1C at C6 closing the ring. This matches the drawing.
