The structure is a PROTAC-type molecule, a thalidomide-based cereblon ligand joined through a linker to a lovastatin-like core. I read it in four parts.

1. **Thalidomide end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the phthalimide nitrogen, with the stereocentre drawn plain, so it gets no stereo label. The phthalimide carries an ether oxygen at the 4-position (OCH2C(=O)–).
2. **Linker:** the OCH2C(=O) group acylates the nitrogen of a piperidine. The piperidine 4-position carries a C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–NH group, which ends in a carbamate, NC(=O)O–.
3. **Statin core:** the carbamate oxygen is attached to the hexahydronaphthalene ring at the position where lovastatin has its 2-methylbutanoate ester. The ring has the same diene (C=C2C=C), the two methyl groups and the ethyl-linked hydroxy-lactone as lovastatin.
4. **Stereochemistry:** the wedges and hashes shown match lovastatin, so I used lovastatin's stereo descriptors. I replaced only the acyl group with the carbamate, which keeps the same neighbour order at the ester-bearing carbon.
