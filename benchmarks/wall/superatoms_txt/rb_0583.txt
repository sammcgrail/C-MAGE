I read the drawing as a DOTA chelator joined to a quinoline-based fibroblast activation protein (FAP) inhibitor. The pieces are:

- **Chelator:** a cyclen (12-membered ring with four nitrogens and four ethylene bridges). Three nitrogens carry CH2COOH arms. The fourth carries CH2C(=O) linked to a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Linker:** the other piperazine nitrogen bears a three-carbon chain (CH2CH2CH2) ending in an ether oxygen on C6 of a quinoline.
- **Quinoline:** C4 carries a C(=O)NH group leading to CH2C(=O).
- **Warhead:** that carbonyl is bonded to the nitrogen of a 4,4-difluoropyrrolidine whose C2 carries a nitrile.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only drawn stereocentre is the pyrrolidine C2, where the CN is on a wedge. Ranking its substituents as N > CN > C3 > H, and viewing with H pointing away, N → CN → C3 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. In the SMILES, `[C@H]` written as `C[C@H]5C#N` gives S, which matches.

SMILES: OC(=O)CN1CCN(CC(=O)N2CCN(CCCOc3ccc4nccc(C(=O)NCC(=O)N5CC(F)(F)C[C@H]5C#N)c4c3)CC2)CCN(CC(=O)O)CCN(CC(=O)O)CC1
