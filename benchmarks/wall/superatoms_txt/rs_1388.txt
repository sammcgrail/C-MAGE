The drawing shows three parts joined by two amide links.

**Left ring:** a benzene ring carrying an amidine group, C(=N)N, at one end. At the other end it carries a C(=O)NH group.

**Central ring:** a benzene ring.
- The NH from the left amide is attached at the bottom-left carbon.
- The adjacent carbon bears C(=O)NH–pyridyl.
- The OMe sits at the top, on the carbon para to the NH-bearing carbon.
- Taking the NH-bearing carbon as C1, the amide-bearing carbon is C2, then C3 is CH, C4 carries OMe, C5 is CH, and C6 is CH.
- In the drawing, the ring's top-right carbon is attached to the C(=O) carbon via the carbon below it. The OMe carbon is at the top, so OMe is meta to the carboxamide carbon and para to the NH carbon. This matches the 5-methoxy-anthranilamide pattern.

The OMe carbon is then meta to the amide carbon, which matches the drawing.
