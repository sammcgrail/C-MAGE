This reads as an antimycin-type nine-membered dilactone carrying a benzyl group, an isobutyrate ester and a methyl group. It is acylated on nitrogen by a 3-(butenyloxycarbonyloxy)-4-methoxypicolinoyl group.

**Pyridine side chain**
- The ring is a pyridine with the carboxamide (C=O–NH) at C2.
- C3 carries an O–C(=O)–O–CH2CH2CH=CH2 carbonate (but-3-enyl carbonate).
- C4 carries OMe.

**Nine-membered dilactone ring**, in order around the ring:
1. Cα, bearing NH on a hashed bond
2. Lactone C(=O)
3. Ring O
4. CH(CH3), with the methyl on a wedge
5. CH–O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2 (isobutyrate), with the O on a hashed bond
6. CH–CH2Ph, with the benzyl on a wedge
7. Lactone C(=O)
8. Ring O
9. CH2, which closes back to Cα

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned the four stereocentres from the wedge and hash bonds in the drawing, giving Cα `@@`, CH(Bn) `@`, CH(O-isobutyrate) `@@` and CH(Me) `@`. I worked these out by hand, so the `@`/`@@` labels are the part most worth rechecking.
