The molecule is a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide whose amide nitrogen is attached to a substituted pyridine.

**Left ring (pyridine):** N1 is at the bottom left. C2 carries the C(=O)N amide. C3 carries OH. C4 carries OMe. C5 and C6 are CH.

**Amide and right pyridine:** The amide NH is bonded to C3 of the right pyridine. Its ring N is at the top right, adjacent to the carbon bearing the O-aryl group. Numbering the right ring from N1 gives C2 bearing O (bottom right, attached to the O), C3 CH, C4 CH, C5 bearing NH, and C6 CH between C5 and N. So the amide N is on C5 and the aryloxy group on C2, which is a 6-aryloxypyridin-3-yl substituent.

**Aryl group:** The oxygen links to a phenyl ring bearing CF3 at the meta position.
