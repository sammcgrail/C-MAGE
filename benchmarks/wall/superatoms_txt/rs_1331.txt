The drawing shows a pyrazin-2(1H)-one ring joined to an indoline.

**Pyrazinone ring.** N1 carries a pentan-3-yl group (CH(CH2CH3)2). C2 is the carbonyl (C=O). C3 is bonded to the indoline nitrogen and double-bonded to N4. C5 carries Br. C6 is the CH, and it is double-bonded to C5.

**Indoline.** The nitrogen is attached to pyrazinone C3. It is followed by CH2CH2 and then the aromatic ring. Going around the benzene ring from C7a, C7 carries the methyl, C6 is CH, C5 carries OMe, C4 is CH, and C3a joins back to the CH2CH2.
