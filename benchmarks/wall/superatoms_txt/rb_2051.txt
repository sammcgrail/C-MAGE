I read the molecule as a cyclic peptide, built residue by residue from the N-terminus.

**Sequence and ring**
- N-terminus: hexanoyl–Arg, with a free guanidine side chain.
- Ring: Pen–Asn–Thr–(7-methyl)Trp–Lys(Ac)–Pen, closed by an S–S bridge between two penicillamine residues (each carrying a C(CH3)2–S group).
- Tail after the ring: Tyr with an O-CH2CH2NH2 ether, then 2-naphthylalanine, then 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid, then Glu (free acid), then Asn, then α-methyl-Phe as the C-terminal primary amide.

**Stereochemistry**
- I took the drawn wedges and hashes at each α-carbon and assigned them from the geometry.
- Tyr, 2-Nal, Thr (2S,3R) and α-methyl-Phe come out as L (S).
- Pen1 (next to the Arg) comes out as D, and Pen2 as L.
- Arg, Asn, Trp, Lys, Glu and the second Asn are assumed L by default.
- The Pen1 assignment depends on reading its wedge bond to NH correctly, so that is the least certain stereocentre.
- The tetrahydropyran carbon is not a stereocentre.

I wrote the SMILES by hand and checked the ring closures and stereo tags for each residue. I did not run it through a parser.
