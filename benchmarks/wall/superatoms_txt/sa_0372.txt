I read this as N-Cbz-alanine: a central carbon bearing a methyl group, an NH–Cbz group, and a CO2H group.

- **Cbz:** the benzyloxycarbonyl group expands to C(=O)OCc1ccccc1 on the nitrogen.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CO2H bond is drawn as a hashed wedge, so CO2H points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- **CIP assignment:** the priorities are N > CO2H > CH3 > H. In the drawing, N (lower left) → CO2H (lower right) → CH3 (top) runs counterclockwise. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving clockwise, which is R. This is the D-alanine configuration.
- **SMILES chirality:** C[C@H](N)C(=O)O is the S form, so the R form is written with @@.

SMILES: C[C@@H](NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1)C(=O)O
