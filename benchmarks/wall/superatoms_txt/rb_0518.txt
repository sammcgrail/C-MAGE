The image shows a rifamycin-type ansa macrolide fused to a phenoxazine, i.e. a benzoxazino-rifamycin, with a 3-(dimethylamino)prop-1-ynyl group on the benzo ring.

**Core**
- The naphthoquinone-furanone core carries the amide NH, a C=O, a phenolic OH and an aryl methyl.
- The quinone ring is fused to the phenoxazine: one carbon bears N= and the neighbouring carbon bears the ring O.
- The alkyne carbon sits meta to the carbon bearing the phenoxazine O.

**Ansa chain**
- Starting from the amide C=O, the chain runs C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–CH(OAc)–CH(CH3)–CH(OMe)–CH=CH–O.
- The chain ends at the quaternary carbon of the furanone, which carries a methyl group.

**Stereochemistry**
- The wedge and hash tags and the E/Z geometry of the chain were not read independently from the drawing. I copied them from the rifampicin-type ansa chain as I remember its SMILES, so they are less certain than the connectivity.
- Only the quaternary stereocentre was re-derived: its neighbour order differs from the reference, so I recomputed the tag.

The ring-closure numbers and branch parentheses were checked by hand for validity, not by a parser.
