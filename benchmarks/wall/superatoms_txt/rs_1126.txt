I read the molecule as four parts joined in a chain and wrote the SMILES from that.

1. **Indazole amide:** The indazole is attached through its 6-position, the benzene carbon next to the one bonded to the NH-bearing ring nitrogen. That carbon carries a C=O, which forms an amide with the ring nitrogen of the tetrahydroisoquinoline.
2. **Tetrahydroisoquinoline core:** The aromatic ring has Cl at C5 and C7, and the C6 carbon carries a C(=O)NH group.
3. **Amino acid unit:** The NH is bonded to a stereocentre bearing CO2H and a CH2 that leads on to an NH. The CH2 is drawn on a wedge, so the hydrogen points away from the viewer. By CIP priorities (N > CO2H > CH2) this centre is S, which corresponds to `[C@@H]` when written as `N[C@@H](CH2…)C(=O)O`.
4. **Cyanoguanidine end:** The NH is bonded to a carbon that carries =N–C≡N and a 3-hydroxyazetidin-1-yl group.
