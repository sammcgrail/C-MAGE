The image shows Boc-protected tyrosine (Boc-Tyr-OH).

- **Left side:** a Boc group on an NH, attached to the α-carbon, which also carries the CO2H group.
- **Side chain:** a CH2 links the α-carbon to a benzene ring, with an OH on the carbon para to the CH2.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the α-carbon, and the CO2H bond is drawn as a wedge. The H is implicit and points away from the viewer. Taking the substituents in priority order, N, then CO2H, then CH2, they run counterclockwise, so the centre is S. This matches L-tyrosine.
