I read the drawing as the cephalosporin core, a 7-amino-3-formyl-ceph-3-em-4-carboxylic acid.

- **Beta-lactam ring:** a four-membered ring made of C7 (bearing NH2 on a wedge), C6 (bonded to S by a wedge), the ring nitrogen, and the lactam carbon C8 (C=O).
- **Six-membered ring:** S1, C2 (CH2), C3 (bearing CHO), C4 (bearing COOH), and the ring nitrogen, with a C3=C4 double bond.
- **Stereochemistry:** both wedges point to the same face, so the NH2 and the C6–S bond are cis. I took this to be the natural 6R,7R cephalosporin configuration, as in 7-ACA. I did not derive the @/@@ marks independently. I copied them from the known 7-ACA SMILES `CC(=O)OCC1=C(N2[C@H](SC1)[C@H](N)C2=O)C(=O)O` and replaced the acetoxymethyl group with CHO. The atom order around both stereocentres is unchanged, so the marks carry over.
