This is a maleimidocaproyl‑Val‑Cit‑PABC‑MMAF‑type drug‑linker. I read it left to right in the drawing.

1. **Maleimide and linker:** a maleimide ring (2,5‑dioxopyrrole) is attached through N to a five‑CH2 chain ending in an amide C=O (the caproyl group).
2. **Val‑Cit:** the caproyl C=O acylates the N of valine (isopropyl side chain, drawn with a wedge). Valine is followed by citrulline, whose side chain is (CH2)3‑NH‑C(=O)NH2. The citrulline centre is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.
3. **PAB spacer:** citrulline's C=O forms an anilide with a para‑substituted benzene. The benzene carries CH2‑O‑C(=O)‑ at the other end, a carbamate.
4. **N‑methyl valine:** the carbamate N carries a methyl and a valine residue.
5. **Valine:** the next residue is valine, joined by an amide.
6. **Dolaisoleuine:** its N is methylated. It bears a sec‑butyl group and a CH(OMe)CH2C(=O) unit, which acylates the proline N.
7. **Dolaproine:** the pyrrolidine ring carries CH(OMe)‑CH(CH3)‑C(=O)‑NH.
8. **Phenylalanine:** the chain ends in a phenylalanine with a free CO2H.

For stereochemistry I took the standard MMAF/MMAE configurations for the peptide, dolaisoleuine and dolaproine centres. I re‑derived each @/@@ for the atom order I wrote. The Val, N‑Me‑Val and Phe centres are assigned as L from the usual convention rather than read off individual wedges.
