I read the drawing as N‑tosyl L‑arginine methyl ester.

- **Backbone:** the chain runs from the tosyl‑bearing NH, through the alpha carbon, then three CH2 groups, to the guanidine nitrogen.
- **Guanidine:** the last nitrogen is drawn with a C=N double bond to a carbon carrying two NH2 groups, giving N=C(N)N.
- **Ester:** the alpha carbon bears CO2Me, which expands to C(=O)OC.
- **Tosyl:** Ts on the nitrogen expands to S(=O)(=O)c1ccc(C)cc1.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CO2Me bond is hashed, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
  - CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H.
  - In the drawing, N (lower left) → CO2Me (up) → CH2 (lower right) runs clockwise with H toward the viewer.
  - Viewing with H toward the viewer reverses the sense, so the centre is S, the natural L configuration.
  - In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]` written after the carbonyl carbon encodes S.
