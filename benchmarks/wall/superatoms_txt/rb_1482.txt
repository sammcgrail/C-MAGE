I read the molecule as a thalidomide-type (isoindolinone–glutarimide) group joined through an alkyne chain to a bicyclic diazocine–pyrrolidine core, which carries two further amide-linked pieces.

**Fragments, left to right**
- **Glutarimide:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring attached to the isoindolinone nitrogen.
- **Isoindolinone:** the lactam carbonyl and the CH2 sit on the benzene ring, and the alkyne is on the ring carbon next to the CH2-bearing carbon.
- **Linker:** an alkyne (C≡C), then five CH2 groups, then a C(=O) that acylates the diazocine nitrogen.
- **Bicyclic core:** an eight-membered ring containing N(acyl), CH2, CH(NH-acyl), C(=O), the pyrrolidine N, the ring-fusion CH, and two CH2 groups, fused to a pyrrolidine.
- **Carboxamide side chain:** the pyrrolidine carbon carries C(=O)NH–CH(thiazol-2-yl)CH2CH2C(=O)NH2.
- **Benzothiophene amide:** the NH on the diazocine is acylated by a benzothiophene-2-carbonyl group. Its C5 carries CF2–P(=O)(OH)2.

**Stereochemistry:** the glutarimide CH has no wedge in the drawing, so it is left unspecified. The four wedged centres are the NH-bearing diazocine carbon, the ring-fusion carbon, the pyrrolidine CH bearing the carboxamide, and the CH bearing the thiazole.
