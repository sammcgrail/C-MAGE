I read the molecule as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide whose nitrogen is attached to a nine-membered dilactone ring (a 1,5-dioxonane-2,6-dione).

**Pyridine part:** Going round the ring from N: N, C2 (bearing the amide C(=O)), C3 (OH), C4 (OMe), then two CH carbons back to N.

**Ring:** Starting at the upper-left carbonyl, the nine ring atoms are:
1. C(=O)
2. O
3. CH(CH3)
4. CH2
5. CH(CH2Ph)
6. C(=O)
7. O
8. CH2
9. CH(NH–), which closes back to atom 1

**Stereocentres:** Each assignment uses the drawn wedges and hashes plus the CIP priorities.
- **CH–N:** the N bond is hashed and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priority order N > C(=O) > CH2 gives S.
- **CH–CH3:** the methyl is wedged and the H is at the back. The priority order O > CH2 > CH3 gives S.
- **CH–Bn:** the benzyl is wedged and the H is at the back. The priority order C(=O) > ring CH2 > CH2Ph gives R.
