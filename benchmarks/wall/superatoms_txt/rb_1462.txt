I read the molecule as an apremilast-type phthalimide (the 3-ethoxy-4-methoxyphenyl, methylsulfonyl-ethyl and NHAc groups) joined through a propyl linker and a piperidine to a fluoro-isoindolinone carrying a glutarimide.

**Fragments**
- **Left side:** an N-acetyl-amino isoindoline-1,3-dione whose ring nitrogen bears a CH(aryl)CH2SO2CH3 group. The aryl is 3-ethoxy-4-methoxyphenyl.
- **Linker:** a three-carbon chain from the isoindoline ring to the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Right side:** a piperidine with its 4-position attached to a 6-fluoro-isoindolin-1-one. That isoindolinone nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) group.

**Stereochemistry**
Only one stereocentre has a wedge or hash: the benzylic carbon bearing the hashed CH2SO2Me bond. The CIP priorities are N > CH2SO2Me > aryl > H. With the CH2 bond hashed, H points toward the viewer. Going N (east) → CH2 (southwest) → aryl (northwest) is clockwise on the page, so the true sense is counterclockwise, giving S. This matches apremilast. In the SMILES, written as `C[C@H](aryl)N`, the `@` tag corresponds to S.

The glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it without stereo.
