I read the structure as a macrocycle and wrote it out piece by piece.

- **Ornithine unit:** H2N–CH2CH2CH2–CH, three CH2 groups between the amine and the α-carbon. Its α-NH is linked through a CH2 to a pyrazine.
- **Lysine unit:** four CH2 groups between the α-carbon and the terminal NH2. It is joined to the ornithine C=O through an NH amide.
- **N-methyl tryptophan:** the amide nitrogen carries a methyl, and the side chain is CH2–indol-3-yl. Its C=O links through NH–CH2 to the central benzene.
- **Pyrazine:** a CH2 on one carbon and the sulfur on the adjacent carbon, with both ring nitrogens drawn on the far side.
- **Central benzene:** the carbon bearing S sits next to the carbon bearing CH2NH, and the Cl carbon is on the other side of the S carbon. The CH2NH carbon is also next to the carbon bearing the aryl group.
- **Benzoic acid ring:** the carbon bearing OMe is next to the carbon bearing COOH.

The wedge bonds on the three α-carbons are ambiguous about which end is narrow. I assumed all three are L-configured (S) amino acids and set the @/@@ marks accordingly.
