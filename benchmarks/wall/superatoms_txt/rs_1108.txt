- **Linker:** the stereocentre also carries an NH, which connects through a CH2 to C5 of the indole.
- **Indole:** its C2 is bonded to C7 of the heterocycle on the right.
- **Heterocycle:** a 5-amino-2-methyl-imidazo[4,5-b]pyridine. The pyridine ring runs C7, CH, C(NH2), N, C3a. The fused imidazole has an NH and an N= and carries a methyl on its C2.

Written as `COC(=O)[C@H](CN...)N...`, the `@` tag corresponds to S, so I used `[C@H]`.
