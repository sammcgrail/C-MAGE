I read the molecule as an MDM2-targeting spiro-oxindole degrader: a pomalidomide-type cereblon ligand joined through an ethylenediamine linker and a 4-aminobenzamide to the spiro-oxindole pyrrolidine.

**Regions of the drawing**
- **Cereblon ligand:** a 4-amino-substituted isoindoline-1,3-dione whose imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The 3-position of the piperidine ring is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo.
- **Linker:** the aryl NH–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O) chain leads to a para-substituted benzene ring, whose other end carries an NH–C(=O) amide to the pyrrolidine.
- **Pyrrolidine:** the five-membered ring has an NH, a C2 carrying the carboxamide, a C3 carrying the 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl group, a spiro C4 shared with the oxindole, and a spiro C5 shared with a cyclohexane.
- **Oxindole:** a 6-chloro-2-oxindole whose C3 is the spiro carbon shared with the pyrrolidine C4.

**Stereochemistry**
- Four centres are marked by wedges or hashes: C2 (carboxamide hashed), C3 (aryl wedged), and the spiro oxindole carbon (aromatic bond hashed, C=O bond wedged).
- I worked out each @/@@ assignment from the wedge and hash geometry in the drawing, so it was not checked with software.
- The cyclohexane spiro carbon is symmetric and gets no stereo label.
