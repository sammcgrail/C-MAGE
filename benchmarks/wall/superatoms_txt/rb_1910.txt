I read this as a lipidated cyclic peptide, a Pen–Pen disulfide macrocycle with two γGlu–(AEEA)₂–acyl side chains. Two features of the SMILES are guesses rather than clear readings of the drawing: the acyl chain lengths and the naphthalene attachment position.

**Backbone, N-terminus to C-terminus**
- Pen1 is acylated on its amine with the first lipid arm: palmitoyl–γGlu–AEEA–AEEA. The AEEA unit is –NH–CH₂CH₂–O–CH₂CH₂–O–CH₂–C(=O)–. Pen1 sits at the top of the ring.
- The ring continues through Asn, Thr, 7-methyl-Trp and Lys to Pen2, which closes the ring through the S–S bond to Pen1.
- Lys carries the second lipid arm on its side-chain amine: AEEA–AEEA–γGlu–palmitoyl.
- After Pen2 the chain runs Tyr(O-CH₂CH₂NH₂), 2-naphthylalanine, 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid, Glu, Asn and 3-pyridylalanine.
- The C-terminus is N-methylglycinamide, –N(CH₃)CH₂C(=O)NH₂.

**Stereochemistry**
I worked each wedge or hash out by CIP priorities. Every amino acid centre comes out as the natural L form: Pen R, Asn S, Thr S, Trp S, Lys S, Tyr S, 2-Nal S, Glu S, Pya S, and both lipid-arm Glu S. The Thr β-carbon has no clear wedge, so I assumed the natural L-Thr (3R) configuration.

**Uncertain points**
- The acyl chains are cut off or hard to count in the image. I took both as palmitoyl (C16), but the pixel lengths fit 14–15 bonds about as well.
- I took the naphthalene as 2-naphthyl.
