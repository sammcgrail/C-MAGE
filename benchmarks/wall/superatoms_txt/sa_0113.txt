This structure is apalutamide.

**Pyridine ring.** The ring atoms are N, C(CN), C(CF3), CH, C(attached to the thiohydantoin N), and CH. The nitrile carbon is bonded to the ring carbon adjacent to the ring N. The CF3 sits on the next ring carbon. The ring carbon bearing the thiohydantoin N lies between a CH on the CF3 side and a CH on the ring-N side.

**Spiro thiohydantoin.** A 5-membered ring containing N, C(=O), a spiro carbon, N, and C(=S). The spiro carbon is part of a cyclobutane. One N is bonded to the pyridine and sits between C=O and C=S. The other N is bonded to the fluorobenzamide aryl group and the spiro carbon.

**Benzamide ring.** The carbon attached to N is para to the carbon bearing C(=O)NHMe. The fluorine is on the carbon ortho to the amide carbon and meta to the N-bearing carbon.
