This is a PROTAC-type molecule: an EZH2 inhibitor warhead joined through a piperazine and a spiro-diamine linker to a thalidomide-type (pomalidomide) degron.

**Left part (EZH2 ligand).** The pyridone is 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl, attached through CH2-NH-C(=O) to the central benzene ring. In SMILES: O=C1NC(C)=CC(C)=C1CNC(=O)...

**Central benzene.** Going around the ring:
- C1 carries the amide.
- C2 carries the methyl.
- C3 carries N(Et)(tetrahydropyran-4-yl).
- C4 is CH.
- C5 carries the aryl group.
- C6 is CH.

This gives c1(C(=O)NCc2c(C)cc(C)[nH]c2=O)cc(-c3ccc(CN...)cc3)cc(N(CC)C4CCOCC4)c1C.

**Linker.** The benzyl CH2 is attached to piperazine N1. Piperazine N4 carries CH2-C(=O)-N of the spiro system, a 3,9-diazaspiro[5.5]undecane. One spiro nitrogen is acylated and the other is attached to a butyl chain (four CH2 groups) ending in NH on the phthalimide ring.

Spiro system: N1CCC2(CC1)CCN(CC2)...

**Right part (pomalidomide-like).** The aryl NH sits at position 4 of the isoindoline-1,3-dione, and the N-substituent is 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl. The stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is specified.
