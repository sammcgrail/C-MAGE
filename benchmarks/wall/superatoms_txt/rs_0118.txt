The drawing shows a pyridine ring with the ring N at the upper left. Reading around the ring:

- The carbon next to N at the top bears no substituent.
- The next carbon carries the OH (position 3).
- The next carbon carries the C(=O)NH group (position 4).
- The remaining two carbons are CH.

So the acid part is 3-hydroxyisonicotinamide.

The amide N is bonded to a CH that carries a methyl (the plain bond going down) and a 4-methoxyphenyl ring. The CH is a stereocentre by connectivity, but all its bonds are plain, so no stereo is assigned.

Writing the SMILES from the ring N: n1cc(O)c(C(=O)NC(C)c2ccc(OC)cc2)cc1. The ring atoms are N1, C2 (CH), C3 (C–OH), C4 (C–C(=O)), C5 (CH), C6 (CH), which matches the drawing.
