I read the molecule as a thalidomide-type (glutarimide–phthalimide) ligand joined through a long amide chain to a bicyclopentane-linked aminopyridazine phenol.

- **Left end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) ring on the nitrogen of a phthalimide. The phthalimide carries an NH substituent at its 5-position.
- **Chain:** from that NH I counted 12 CH2 groups, then the amide C=O. This count rests on the spacing of the zigzag vertices (about 48 px each), so it is the least certain part of the reading.
- **Amide nitrogen:** NH–CH2 attached to one bridgehead of a bicyclo[1.1.1]pentane.
- **Other bridgehead:** CH2–O linked to C4 of the pyridazine.
- **Pyridazine ring:** the 3-position carries NH2, the 4-position carries the ether oxygen, and the 6-position carries a 2-hydroxyphenyl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** none is drawn, so none is included.
