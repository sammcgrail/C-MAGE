I read the molecule as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide whose amide nitrogen is bonded to the bridge carbon of an N-benzyl-2-thia-5-azabicyclo[2.2.1]heptane.

- **Pyridine ring:** the ring nitrogen is adjacent to the carbon carrying the C(=O)N group. The next ring carbon bears OH, and the one after that bears OMe.
- **Bicycle:** the bridge carbon (C7) carries the NH of the amide. It joins two bridgehead carbons.
  - One bridgehead is bonded to S and to a CH2 that leads to the benzylated nitrogen.
  - The other bridgehead is bonded to a CH2 that leads to S and to a second CH2 that leads to the benzylated nitrogen.
- **Stereochemistry:** the bond from the bridge carbon to the amide NH is a wavy line, so that centre is undefined. The other bicycle centres are drawn with plain bonds. I therefore gave no stereo marks.
