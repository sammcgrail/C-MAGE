This is aztreonam. I read it from the drawing in four parts:

- **β-lactam ring:** a four-membered azetidinone with a C=O. Its nitrogen carries an SO3H group. The ring carbon next to the nitrogen has a wedged methyl, and the next ring carbon has a hashed bond to the amide NH.
- **Amide link:** the NH is acylated by a C(=O) that connects to a C=N–O oxime carbon.
- **Oxime ether:** the oxime oxygen is attached to a C(CH3)2–CO2H group.
- **Thiazole:** a 2-aminothiazol-4-yl ring on the oxime carbon.

**Stereochemistry:**
- Both ring stereocentres were assigned from the wedge and hash. The wedged methyl carbon is written `[C@H]` and the hashed amide-bearing carbon is written `[C@@H]`.
- In the drawing, the oxime O and the amide C(=O) lie on the same side of the C=N bond. This is the Z oxime, written `/C(=N\O...)/` in the SMILES.
