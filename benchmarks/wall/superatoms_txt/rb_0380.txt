I read this as an N-acetylated cyclic peptide: Ac-Nle followed by a ring of Glu, His, 2-naphthylalanine (2Nal), Arg and Trp. The Glu side-chain carbonyl is joined through a 1,4-diaminobutane linker to the Trp C-terminal carbonyl, which closes the macrocycle.

Working around the ring from the Glu side chain: Glu γ-C(=O)–NH–(CH2)4–NH–C(=O)–Trp–Arg–2Nal–His–back to the Glu α-NH–C(=O) (the Glu backbone carbonyl).

**Stereochemistry**, assigned from the wedges and hashes by CIP priorities:
- Nle: S (L)
- Glu: S (L)
- Trp: S (L)
- Arg: S (L)
- 2Nal: R (D)
- His: not worked out from the drawing; I assumed L.
