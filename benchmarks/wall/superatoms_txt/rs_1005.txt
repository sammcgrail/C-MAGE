I read the structure in four parts, then fixed the stereochemistry from the wedges.

- **Indane acid:** a 2,3-dihydro-1H-indene bearing a COOH on the five-membered ring carbon (C2). The sulfonyl group sits on the benzene carbon next to the ring fusion (C4).
- **Sulfonamide:** the S(=O)(=O) links the indane to a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Piperazine:** the sulfonylated N is flanked by two CH(CH3) carbons, and both methyls are drawn with wedge bonds, so the two methyls are cis. The other ring nitrogen carries the acyl group.
- **Acyl group:** C(=O)CH2 attached to a para-CF3 phenyl ring.

For the stereocentres, the top CH(CH3) is S by CIP priorities (N > CH2 > CH3, with H pointing back). The bottom one is its mirror image, R, which gives the cis (meso) 2,6-dimethylpiperazine. The indane C2 has plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

Written as SMILES with the ring closure on the sulfonylated N, the two centres come out as `N3[C@@H](C)...[C@H]3C`.

SMILES: OC(=O)C1Cc2cccc(S(=O)(=O)N3[C@@H](C)CN(C(=O)Cc4ccc(C(F)(F)F)cc4)C[C@H]3C)c2C1
