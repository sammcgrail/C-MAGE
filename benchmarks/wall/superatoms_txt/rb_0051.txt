The drawing shows a triarylamine: a central nitrogen bonded to three para-substituted benzene rings.

Each of the three arms is the same: the central-N-bearing phenyl ring carries, at the para position, a CH2CH2 linker. That linker joins a second para-phenylene ring, which ends in N(CH2CH3)2.

Writing one arm as c1ccc(CCc2ccc(N(CC)CC)cc2)cc1, the full molecule is N(Ar)(Ar)Ar with three identical arms.

SMILES: CCN(CC)c1ccc(CCc2ccc(N(c3ccc(CCc4ccc(N(CC)CC)cc4)cc3)c3ccc(CCc4ccc(N(CC)CC)cc4)cc3)cc2)cc1
