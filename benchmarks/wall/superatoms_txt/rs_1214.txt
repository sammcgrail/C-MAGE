I read this as a nine-membered dilactone (antimycin-type) carrying a pyridine carboxamide on the ring nitrogen side.

**Pyridine part:** a pyridine ring with the carboxamide at C2, a phenyl carbonate (OC(=O)OPh) at C3 and OMe at C4. The amide NH is attached to the ring.

**Nine-membered ring**, going around from the amide-bearing carbon:
- CH(NH) bearing the amide, with a hashed bond to N
- C(=O) lactone carbonyl
- ring O
- CH(CH3), methyl on a wedge
- CH(O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2), isobutyrate on a hash
- CH(CH2Ph), benzyl on a wedge
- C(=O) lactone carbonyl
- ring O
- CH2, which closes back to the CH(NH)

**Stereo:** I assigned CIP labels from the wedges and hashes and then converted them to @/@@. The CH(N) centre is S, the CH(CH3) centre is S, the CH(O-isobutyrate) centre is R, and the CH(CH2Ph) centre is R.
