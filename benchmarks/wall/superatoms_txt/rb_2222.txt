I read the structure as a maleimide–PEG–amide linker on a central tertiary amide nitrogen. Two identical arms branch from that nitrogen, and each arm carries a glucuronide-protected benzofuran linked to an MMAE-type payload. I wrote the SMILES by region and then checked the stereocentres against CIP descriptors.

**Linker core**
- The maleimide N is attached to CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–N.
- That amide N carries two CH2CH2–O–CH2– arms, each ending on C4 of a 1,2,3-triazole.

**Each arm**
- Triazole N1 → CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)–CH2CH2–, attached to C2 of a benzofuran.
- Benzofuran C4 carries O‑β‑D‑glucuronic acid. Benzofuran C7 carries CH2–O–C(=O)–, a carbamate onto the payload.

**Payload (MMAE-type)**
- N‑Me‑Val, then Val, then N‑Me dolaisoleuine (sec-butyl, OMe), then pyrrolidine (dolaproine OMe, methyl), then amide to the (1S,2R)‑1‑hydroxy‑1‑phenylpropan‑2‑yl (norephedrine) group.

**Stereochemistry**
- Both valines are L.
- Dolaisoleuine is 3R,4S,5S.
- The pyrrolidine Cα is S.
- The dolaproine OMe carbon and its methyl carbon are R,R.
- Norephedrine is 1S,2R.
- The glucuronide is the β‑D form.
