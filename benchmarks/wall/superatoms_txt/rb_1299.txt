I read the drawing as a PROTAC-type molecule: a KRAS G12C-binding quinazoline joined through a PEG linker to a lenalidomide-like isoindolinone–glutarimide.

**Warhead (left side)**
- The quinazoline core carries a 6-chloro and an 8-fluoro substituent.
- C4 bears a piperazine whose far nitrogen is acylated with acryloyl (C=CC(=O)–).
- C7 bears a 2-fluoro-6-hydroxyphenyl ring.
- C2 carries an NH–CH2CH2–C(=O) chain.

**Linker**
- The chain continues as an N-methyl amide: C(=O)N(C)CH2CH2.
- Then come four ether oxygens separated by CH2CH2 units, written OCCOCCOCCO.

**E3-ligase ligand (right side)**
- The last ether oxygen is attached at position 5 of an isoindolin-1-one. That oxygen-bearing carbon is meta to the CH2-bearing ring carbon and para to the carbonyl-bearing ring carbon.
- The isoindolinone nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) group.

**Stereochemistry**
- The hydroxyphenyl ring has a wedge bond, which suggests an atropisomer. I could not assign a reliable configuration for it, so I left it out.
- The glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it also has no stereo marking.
