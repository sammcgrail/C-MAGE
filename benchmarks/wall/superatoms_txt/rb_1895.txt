I read the drawing as the cyclic peptide H-Cpa-cyclo[D-Cys-Tyr-D-Phe(4-ureidomethyl)-Lys-Thr-Cys]-D-Tyr-NH2, with a disulfide between the two Cys side chains.

The residues, from the N-terminus:
1. **Cpa** is 4-chlorophenylalanine with a free NH2. From the hashed NH2 bond I worked out an S centre, so it is the L form.
2. **D-Cys** has a wedged N bond and works out to S, which is D for cysteine. Its CH2–S forms the disulfide.
3. **Tyr** is L, with the wedged CH2 on the alpha carbon.
4. **D-Phe(4-ureidomethyl)** carries CH2NHC(=O)NH2 at the para position of the ring. I took the D configuration from the name rather than re-deriving it from the wedge.
5. **Lys** is L, with a free (CH2)4NH2 side chain.
6. **Thr** is L, (2S,3R), with the beta OH.
7. **Cys** is L, and its sulfur closes the disulfide to residue 2.
8. **D-Tyr-NH2** is the C-terminal primary amide.

In the SMILES, the disulfide is written with ring-closure label %10 on the two sulfurs.
