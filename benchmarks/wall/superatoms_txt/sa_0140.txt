The drawing shows N-Cbz-protected tyrosine, with a free carboxylic acid and a para-hydroxyphenyl group on the side chain.

- **Side chain:** a CH2 attached to a benzene ring that carries an OH para to the CH2 (from the ring OH label).
- **Backbone:** a stereocentre bonded to NH-Cbz, CO2H, the CH2 and an implicit H.
- **Cbz:** expanded to benzyloxycarbonyl, C(=O)OCH2Ph.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CO2H bond is a wedge, so it points toward the viewer and the implicit H points away. The priorities are N > CO2H > CH2. On the page, N is at the top, CO2H at lower left and CH2 at lower right, so N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise. That gives S, which is the L-tyrosine configuration.

For the SMILES, I write the neighbours in the order N, H, CH2, COOH. For S this order takes `@@`.
