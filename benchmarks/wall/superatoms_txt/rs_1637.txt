Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Lactam ring:** a five-membered pyrrolidin-2-one with C=O at the top left, the ring N at the lower right of the carbonyl, and two CH₂ groups on the left.
- **N-substituent:** an N–CH₂–CH₂ chain leading to a para-substituted benzene ring that carries CO₂H.
- **Ring C5 (next to N):** carries a wedged bond to a trans CH=CH. The trans geometry is read from the drawn double bond.
- **Side chain:** after the alkene comes a CH(OH) drawn with a hashed bond, then CH₂ and a C(CH₃)₃ group.

**Stereocentres:**

- At ring C5 the priorities are N > vinyl > ring CH₂ > H. The wedged vinyl points toward the viewer, so H is in back. N (up), vinyl (down-right) and CH₂ (left) run clockwise, which gives R.
- At the carbinol carbon the priorities are OH > vinyl CH= > CH₂ > H. The OH is hashed, so H points toward the viewer. OH (down-left), vinyl (up-left) and CH₂ (right) run clockwise as drawn. Because H faces the viewer, this reverses to S.
