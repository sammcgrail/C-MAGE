I read the molecule as a macrocycle and wrote the SMILES in the order below.

With the methyl hashed, the priorities ester > CH2Ar > CH3 > H give R. In the SMILES order `C(=O)[C@H](C)C`, the `@` mark encodes this R configuration.

**Macrocycle chain.** The benzene ring carries a quaternary carbon bearing a methyl and the pyrazole N1. From this carbon the chain runs:
- three CH2 groups,
- a C(CH3)2 group,
- a CH2,
- the sulfone S(=O)(=O),
- two CH2 groups,
- indole C4.

This quaternary carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.

**Indole.** It is a 1H-indole with the chain on C4, the aryl ether oxygen on C5, and fluorine on C6. C7 carries an H.

**Diaryl ether and pyrazole.** The oxygen links to a benzene ring that has fluorine ortho to the pyrazole attachment. The pyrazole is attached through its C3 to this ring and through N1 to the quaternary carbon.
