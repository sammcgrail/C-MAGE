Reading the structure region by region:

**Left fragment (3,4-dihydroquinolin-2-one):**
- The lactam carbonyl C(=O) is bonded to N, and N carries the piperidin-4-yl group.
- Going around the ring: C(=O), then CH2, CH2, then the aromatic carbon, then the aromatic carbon bonded to N.
- Chlorine sits on the benzene ring at the position para to N. The ring runs N–C8a, C8, C7, C6(Cl), C5, C4a. In the drawing, the bottom-left carbon bears Cl and is adjacent to the carbon at the ring fusion (C4a) through C5, so Cl is at position 6.

**Piperidine:**
- C4 is attached to the lactam N, and the ring nitrogen is attached to CH2–CH(OH)–CH2.

**Right fragment (4,5,6,7-tetrahydropyrazolo[4,3-c]pyridine):**
- N1 carries the chain, N2 is double-bonded to C3, and C3 bears the 4-CF3-phenyl group.
- C3 connects to C3a, which connects to C4 (CH2), then N5 (bearing SO2Me), then C6 (CH2), C7 (CH2), and C7a. C7a is bonded to N1 and double-bonded to C3a.

**SMILES assembly:**
- Lactam part: O=C1CCc2cc(Cl)ccc2N1C1CCN(CC1)...
- Chain: CC(O)Cn1nc(c2c1CCN(C2)S(C)(=O)=O)-c1ccc(cc1)C(F)(F)F
- Pyrazole ring: n1 (N1), n (N2), c(Ar) (C3), c2 (C3a), c1 (C7a) closing the ring to N1. C3a continues to C4 (CH2) and N5. C7a continues to C7 and C6, then N5.
- Written as n1nc(Ar)c2c1CCN(C2)S(C)(=O)=O: c2 is C3a and c1 is C7a. C7a then goes CC (C7, C6) to N, and N connects via C2 (C4) back to C3a. This is consistent.

The hydroxyl-bearing carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is included.
