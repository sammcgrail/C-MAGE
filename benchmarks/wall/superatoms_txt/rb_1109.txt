Reading the structure region by region:

**Quinazoline part.** The 4-position carries an NH linked to a 3-chloro-4-fluorophenyl ring. The 6-position carries NHC(=O)C=C (acrylamide). The 7-position carries an O–CH2CH2CH2 chain attached to the piperidine nitrogen.

**Piperidine and piperazine.** The piperidine is substituted at its 4-position by a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated with C(=O), followed by CH2CH2CH2CH2 and then S. The carbonyl is connected to four CH2 groups: C(=O)–CH2–CH2–CH2–CH2–S. In the drawing, the carbonyl carbon is followed by vertices at about 1015, 1070, 1125 and 1180, and then S. That gives four CH2 groups.

**Isoindolinone part.** The sulfur is attached to the aromatic ring at position 4 of the isoindolinone. The ring CH2 sits next to the N, and the C=O is on the other side. The N bears the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
