This is a 1,4-dihydropyridine of the nifedipine type: a ring NH with a methyl on each flanking carbon, a 2-nitrophenyl group at C4, and an ester on each of C3 and C5.

The labels need careful reading. On the left, the chain runs C(=O)–O–CH2–"Ac", and "Ac" is the acetyl abbreviation. Expanded, that gives an O–CH2–C(=O)CH3 group, so the ester is an acetonyl ester: C(=O)OCC(C)=O.

The other ester is CO2Me, which is C(=O)OC.

Ring atoms:
- N1 (NH)
- C2, bearing a methyl, the top carbon attached to NH
- C3, bearing the acetonyl ester
- C4, the sp3 CH bearing the 2-nitrophenyl group
- C5, bearing CO2Me
- C6, bearing a methyl

SMILES: CC1=C(C(=O)OCC(C)=O)C(c2ccccc2[N+](=O)[O-])C(C(=O)OC)=C(C)N1
