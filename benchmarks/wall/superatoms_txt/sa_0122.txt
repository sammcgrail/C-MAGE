The structure is flutamide: an isobutyramide on an aniline nitrogen, with a benzene ring carrying CF3 and NO2.

Reading the drawing:
- The left group is iPr (isopropyl) attached to C(=O), which is bonded to NH. The acyl group is therefore isobutyryl, written CC(C)C(=O)N.
- The NH attaches to a ring carbon. Going around the ring, the next carbon (up and right) is an unsubstituted CH, then the carbon bearing CF3. After that comes the carbon bearing NO2, then two more CH carbons back to the start.
- This puts CF3 meta to the NH and NO2 para to the NH, with CF3 and NO2 adjacent to each other.
