I read the molecule as a maleimide–PEG3–Gly-Gly-Phe-Gly linker joined through an amide to a fluoro-amino camptothecin core, an exatecan-type payload.

**Linker, left to right**
1. A maleimide ring (`O=C1C=CC(=O)N1`) is attached through its nitrogen to the chain.
2. The chain is CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–C(=O), so three ether oxygens.
3. The peptide follows as NH–Gly–Gly–Phe–Gly, with a benzyl side chain on the Phe.
4. The Phe α-carbon is drawn with a hashed bond to its CH2. I wrote this centre as `[C@@H]`.
5. The last glycine carbonyl is bonded through NH to the aromatic ring of the core.

**Camptothecin-type core**
1. The benzo ring carries the NH on one carbon and F on the adjacent carbon.
2. The benzo ring is fused to a pyridine (the ring N is labelled in the drawing).
3. The pyridine is fused to a pyrroline CH2–N ring, then to a pyridone (C=O on the ring N).
4. The pyridone is fused to a lactone ring, OCH2 and C(=O)O.
5. The lactone ring closes at a quaternary carbon bearing OH (bold wedge) and ethyl.

**Stereochemistry at the quaternary carbon**
I worked out the chirality from the wedge geometry. The result is `[C@]4(O)CC` when written after the lactone carbonyl, which matches the natural (S) camptothecin configuration.
