I read the molecule as an N-acetylated, peptoid-like chain of 12 repeating units, ending in a primary amide. Each unit has the form NH–CH(R)–CH2–N(SO2R′)–CH2–C(=O)–.

The chain ends with CH2C(=O)NH2.

Stereochemistry: every α-carbon is drawn with a bold wedge to its side chain, with the NH on the left and the CH2N on the right. Working through the geometry with the implicit H at the back gives N[C@@H](R)CN for each one. The Thr-like β-carbon and the methyl-bearing β-carbon in unit 8 are drawn with plain bonds, so they carry no stereo marks.
