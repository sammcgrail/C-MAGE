I read the molecule as a DBCO-linked peptide–drug conjugate and wrote the SMILES region by region.

- **DBCO end:** the dibenzocyclooctyne nitrogen carries a succinyl group, C(=O)CCC(=O), which is linked through an amide to a 6-aminohexanoyl chain.
- **Peptide:** the chain runs Trp–Trp–Glu–Trp–Trp and ends as a C-terminal primary amide (NH2). The Glu side chain is acylated through its γ-carboxyl by Val–Cit.
- **Linker and drug:** the Cit carbonyl forms an anilide with a p-aminobenzyl alcohol. Its benzylic oxygen forms a carbamate with a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to a 2-CF3-phenyl ring, which carries a benzo[c][2,7]naphthyridin-4-one core bearing a quinolin-3-yl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** the Trp, Glu, Val and Cit α-carbons are all assigned the L configuration. I assumed this for the Trp and Glu centres; the Val and Cit centres, where I could see wedge and hash bonds, were also taken as L.
