The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an isatin core (an eltrombopag-like structure).

**Hydrazone and indolinone.** The NH–N= unit is attached at C3 of an indol-2-one. C2 is C=O and N1 carries a 3,4-dimethylphenyl group. The fused benzene ring bears an isopropyl group at C6. N1 is attached to C7a, and C7 lies next to C7a, so the isopropyl sits on the carbon one position further round from C7, at C6.

**Fused ring numbering.** From the drawing, C3a joins the upper right of the benzene ring. The ring then runs C4 (top), C5 (right), and C6 (bottom right, bearing the isopropyl group), then C7 (bottom) and C7a (bonded to N). This matches the drawing.
