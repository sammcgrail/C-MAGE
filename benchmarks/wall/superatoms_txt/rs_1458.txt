I read the structure as a morpholine amide, a cyclohexylmethyl-bearing succinamide, and a 4-(trifluoromethoxy)anilino-substituted phenylbutyl amine.

**Connectivity, left to right**
- Morpholine nitrogen is acylated by a C(=O), which leads to CH2 and then to a stereocentre.
- That stereocentre carries a CH2-cyclohexyl group (hashed bond) and a C(=O)NH amide.
- The amide nitrogen is attached to a second stereocentre. This carbon carries a CH2CH2Ph chain (wedged bond) and a CH2-NH-aryl group.
- The aryl group is a para-substituted benzene bearing OCF3.

**Stereochemistry (CIP assignment from the wedges and hashes)**
- First stereocentre: the priorities are C(=O)N, then CH2C(=O)N(morpholine), then CH2-cyclohexyl, then H. The cyclohexylmethyl is hashed, so H points toward the viewer. The 1→2→3 path looks counterclockwise from the front, so reversing for H gives **R**. In the SMILES this is `[C@@H]`.
- Second stereocentre: the priorities are N, then CH2NHAr, then CH2CH2Ph, then H. The phenethyl group is wedged, so H points away. The 1→2→3 path is counterclockwise, which gives **S**. In the SMILES this is `[C@@H]`.

SMILES: O=C(N1CCOCC1)C[C@@H](CC2CCCCC2)C(=O)N[C@@H](CCc1ccccc1)CNc1ccc(OC(F)(F)F)cc1
