I read this as a tacrolimus (FK506)-type macrolide and wrote the SMILES from that scaffold. The stereocentres are copied from my recollection of tacrolimus, with only the cis/trans geometry and wedge pattern of the drawing considered. I did not re-derive each centre from the wedges.

- **Core:** the 23-membered macrolide has the pyran hemiketal, the α-keto amide to the pipecolate N, the ester, the two OH groups, the ketone and the two trisubstituted alkenes, as in FK506.
- **Cyclohexane end:** the drawing shows H₃C and OH on this ring, where tacrolimus has OMe and OH. I therefore replaced the methoxy with a methyl.
- **Side chain:** the allyl group of FK506 is replaced by –CH₂CH₂CH₂–S–CH₂CH₂–NH–C(=O)–CH₂–(2-pyridyl). This is the thiol-ene adduct of the allyl group.

I checked the parentheses and ring-closure digits (1 to 5) against the drawing.
