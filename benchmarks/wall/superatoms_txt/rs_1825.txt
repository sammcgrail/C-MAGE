The drawing shows HOOC–CH(NH2)–(CH2)4–, followed by a dangling bond with a period after it. The dangling bond is an open attachment point, as in a lysine side chain linked to something else.

Reading the structure atom by atom:
- HOOC is a carboxylic acid group, C(=O)O.
- The alpha carbon carries an H and an NH2, with plain bonds only, so there is no stereo.
- (CH2)4 is four CH2 groups, ending in the open bond.

The open bond is not capped with any atom. Because the answer must be a single valid SMILES, I used a terminal wildcard atom [*] to mark the attachment point.
