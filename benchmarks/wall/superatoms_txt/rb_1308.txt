I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type structure with three parts joined by a linker.

- **Left part:** a quinazoline with a 4-propionylpiperazin-1-yl group at C4 and an NH at C2. The benzo ring carries 6-Cl, 7-(3-hydroxynaphthalen-1-yl) and 8-F.
- **Linker:** from the C2 NH, –CH2CH2C(=O)N(CH3)– followed by –CH2CH2OCH2CH2OCH2CH2O–, which attaches to the aryl ring of the right part.
- **Right part:** a benzylamide ring bearing a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group, attached through NH–C(=O) to a 4-hydroxyproline. The proline nitrogen is acylated by CH(3-methylisoxazol-5-yl)–CH(CH3)2.

Stereochemistry is the uncertain part. I gave the hydroxyproline the usual VHL-ligand (2S,4R) configuration, written as `[C@@H]…[C@@H](O)`. For the side-chain stereocentre I reused the VH032 chirality order. I did not confirm that against the wedge shown at that carbon in the drawing.
