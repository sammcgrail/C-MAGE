The image shows a benzene ring with three substituents.

- The top-left carbon carries NO2.
- The left carbon carries Me, and it sits directly below the NO2 carbon, so these two groups are ortho.
- The right carbon carries I.

Numbering the ring carbons to place the iodine relative to the others:
- C1 is the Me carbon (left, lower).
- C2 is the NO2 carbon (top-left, adjacent to C1).
- C3 is the top-right CH.
- C4 is the right carbon bearing I.
- C5 and C6 are the two bottom CH carbons.

The iodine on C4 is para to the methyl on C1, so the compound is 4-iodo-1-methyl-2-nitrobenzene.
