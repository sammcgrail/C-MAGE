I read the molecule as a 1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl group joined through a CH2CH2 chain to the nitrogen of a pyrrolidine. The pyrrolidine carries a mesylate (OMs, i.e. OS(=O)(=O)CH3) at C3, drawn on a wedge bond.

**Stereochemistry at C3.** The wedge puts the O substituent toward the viewer, so the hydrogen points away.
- The CIP priorities are O > C2 (attached to N) > C4 (attached to C) > H.
- Viewed from the front, with H at the back, O is at the top, C2 at the lower left and C4 at the lower right.
- Going O → C2 → C4 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S.

**SMILES check.** In `CS(=O)(=O)O[C@H]1CCN(CCc2ccc3OCOc3c2)C1`, the neighbours of the stereocentre are listed in the order O, H, C2 (ring-closure digit 1), C4. With `@`, H → C2 → C4 runs counterclockwise when viewed from O. Swapping O and H once gives `@@` for the order H, O, C2, C4. Viewed from H, O → C2 → C4 is then clockwise, so from the opposite side (H at the back) it is counterclockwise, which is S. This matches the drawing.
