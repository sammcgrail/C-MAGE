The structure is trimethoprim: a 2,4-diaminopyrimidine joined through a CH2 at C5 to a 3,4,5-trimethoxyphenyl ring.

Pyrimidine ring, read from the drawing:
- The top-left carbon carries NH2 and is bonded to the top N and to the lower-left N.
- The lower-left N is bonded to the bottom carbon, which carries the second NH2.
- That bottom carbon is bonded to C5, which carries the CH2 link.
- C5 is bonded to the CH carbon, which closes the ring to the top N.

Benzene ring: the CH2 attaches to the ring carbon at the lower left. The two meta positions and the para position each carry an OMe, giving a 3,4,5-trimethoxyphenyl group.

SMILES: COc1cc(Cc2cnc(N)nc2N)cc(OC)c1OC
