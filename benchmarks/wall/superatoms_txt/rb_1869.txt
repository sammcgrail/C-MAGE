I read this as a cyclosporin A-type cyclic undecapeptide whose MeBmt side chain has been turned into a linker to a KRAS G12C-type quinazoline. I wrote the SMILES by modifying the known cyclosporin A SMILES from memory, not by tracing every wedge in the drawing. The stereocentres are therefore assumed to follow natural cyclosporin A, including the hashed methyl on the side-chain CH(CH3), which I did not check against the picture.

**Macrocycle.** Going round the ring from the modified MeBmt residue:
- MeBmt carries N-methyl, an alpha-CH, a CH(OH), and a CH(CH3) that continues into the linker.
- Abu (CH with an ethyl group)
- Sar (N-methylglycine)
- MeLeu
- Val
- MeLeu
- Ala
- D-Ala
- MeLeu
- MeLeu
- MeVal, which closes back onto the MeBmt nitrogen

**Linker.** The CH(CH3) continues through three CH2 groups to a C(=O) that acylates a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen carries C(=O)CH2CH2NH–, which is attached to C2 of the quinazoline.

**Quinazoline.**
- C4 bears a piperazine whose far nitrogen is acylated with acryloyl (C(=O)C=C).
- C6 bears Cl.
- C7 bears a 2-fluoro-6-hydroxyphenyl group.
- C8 bears F.
