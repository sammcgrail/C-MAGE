I read this as a PROTAC: an EZH2-inhibitor-type warhead joined through a piperazine-acetamide and hexyl linker to a VHL ligand. Two parts of the drawing needed a judgement call.

**Left-hand fragment.** The "pyridone" is drawn as an open chain, so I transcribed it as drawn: NH2–C(=O)–C(CH2NH–)=C(CH3)–CH=CH–CH3.
- The central C=C is cis, with the primary amide and the CH= on the same side.
- The terminal CH=CH is trans.

**Benzamide carbon.** The carbon between the NH and the aromatic ring is labelled with an "O" but no visible double bond. I took it as a C=O.

**Remaining pieces**
- The central ring carries the methyl, the N-ethyl-N-(tetrahydropyran-4-yl)amino group, the amide and the aryl substituent.
- The aryl group is a para-substituted benzene bearing CH2 to the piperazine.
- The linker is piperazine–CH2C(=O)NH–(CH2)6–NH.
- The VHL end is tert-leucine, then (4R)-hydroxyproline, then the 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamide.

**Stereochemistry.** The wedges and hashes give the standard VHL configuration, (S)-tert-leucine and (2S,4R)-hydroxyproline. I checked the chirality tags against the known VH032 SMILES.
