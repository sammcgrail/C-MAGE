Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Glutarimide:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring is attached to the isoindoline-1,3-dione nitrogen.
- **Linker:** the isoindoline benzene carries an O–CH2–C(=O)–NH– group at the 4-position. That NH is followed by a chain of five CH2 groups (counted vertex by vertex), ending at the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Piperidine:** the ring is N-substituted by the pentyl chain. Its C3 carries an NH with a wedge bond.
- **Aryl groups:** that NH is attached to C2 of a pyrimidine. The pyrimidine has Cl at C5 and an indol-3-yl group at C4.

**Stereochemistry at piperidine C3:**
- The wedged NH points toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away.
- The CIP priorities are N > C2 (the CH2 next to the ring nitrogen) > C4 > H.
- In the drawing, N sits at the lower right, C2 at the lower left and C4 at the top, so N → C2 → C4 runs clockwise.
- With H pointing away, clockwise gives the R configuration.
- In the SMILES, writing the centre as `[C@H](C4)N` after the preceding ring carbon gives R, which I checked by permuting the neighbour order.

The isoindoline-dione glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
