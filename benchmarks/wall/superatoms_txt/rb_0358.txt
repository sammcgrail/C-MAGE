I read the drawing as a cyclic peptide: Ac‑Arg‑Asp‑Ser(OBn)‑D‑Phe‑Arg‑Trp, with the Asp side‑chain carboxyl and the Trp C‑terminus joined through a 1,5‑diaminopentane linker.

**Connectivity**
- The N‑terminus is acetylated (CH3C(=O)NH–) on the first Arg, which carries a guanidinopropyl side chain.
- The Asp α‑carboxyl continues the backbone to Ser. The Asp side‑chain CH2C(=O)NH– runs into the linker.
- Ser carries a benzyl ether (CH2OCH2Ph). Phe has a benzyl side chain, the second Arg a guanidinopropyl chain, and Trp an indol‑3‑ylmethyl side chain.
- The Trp C=O is amidated by the other end of the linker, which closes the macrocycle.
- I counted five CH2 groups in the linker. That count is the least certain part of my reading.

- Ser: S (L)
- Phe: R (D)
- Arg2: S (L)
