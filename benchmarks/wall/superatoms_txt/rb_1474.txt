Checking the pyrazolopyridazine: n2 is N1, bonded to c(ccc2c1). Here c is C7 (the pyridine-bearing carbon), followed by cc, then c2 (the fusion carbon, which closes the ring to n2), then c1. The sequence is N#Cc1 c n n2 c(...) c c c2 c1. The ring 1 atoms are c1(CN), c, n, n2, … c2, c1, which gives the six-membered ring c1, c, n, n2, c2(fusion), c1(closing CH), i.e. six atoms. That matches the drawing, where the CN carbon sits between two CH carbons, one of which is next to the fusion carbon and the other next to N.

For the isoindolinone, c1cccc2C(=O)N(...)Cc12 has the aromatic atoms c1 (bearing CH2), c, c, c, c2 (bearing C=O), and the final c12 (bearing the CH2–N). The CH2 substituent is on c1, which is adjacent to the CH2-bearing fusion carbon. This is correct.
