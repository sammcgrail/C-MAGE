I read the molecule as a maleimidocaproyl–Gly–Gly–Phe–Gly linker. The linker continues through –NH–CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH–CH2– to a camptothecin-type core that carries a fluoro and a methyl on the benzo ring.

**Linker, left to right:**
- The maleimide ring is O=C1C=CC(=O)N1, attached through its nitrogen to a five-carbon chain ending in C(=O) (a 6-aminocaproyl-type acyl group).
- That acyl group is followed by Gly, Gly, Phe and Gly, joined by amide bonds.
- The Phe benzyl group is drawn on a wedge. I took this as L-Phe, written N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O).
- The last Gly carbonyl continues into NCOCC(=O)N, then a CH2 that attaches to the aromatic core.

**Core:**
- It is a camptothecin-type pentacycle: a quinoline fused to a pyrrolo-pyridone and a lactone ring.
- The benzo ring carries F and CH3 on adjacent carbons.
- The CH2NH linker is attached to the quinoline carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the CH2–N.

**Quaternary carbon:**
- Its neighbours are the OH (wedge), the ethyl (hash), the lactone C(=O)O and the ring carbon.
- With the hashed ethyl pointing away, OH → C(=O)O → aromatic C runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S, as in natural camptothecin.
- In my SMILES, the [C@@] in …C(=O)[C@@](O)(CC)c4… encodes S, which I checked by a permutation argument.
