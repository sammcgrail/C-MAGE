The drawing shows N-τ-trityl-L-histidine, read as follows.

- **Ring:** The five-membered ring is an imidazole. Its bottom-left nitrogen carries the Trt (trityl) group, which expands to C(Ph)₃. The top nitrogen is the unsubstituted ring N, drawn with a C=N double bond to the CH on its left. The ring carbon bonded to the CH₂ side chain is adjacent to that unsubstituted N, so the ring is 1-trityl-4-substituted.
- **Side chain:** The ring carbon bears a CH₂ that leads to the α-carbon. The α-carbon carries NH₂ (drawn down) and CO₂H (drawn on a wedge), plus an implicit H.
- **Stereochemistry:** The CO₂H wedge points toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away. Priorities are N > CO₂H > CH₂ > H. Going from N (down) to CO₂H (right) to CH₂ (upper left) runs counterclockwise on the page, which gives S, the L configuration. For L-amino acids this is written N[C@@H](R)C(=O)O.
