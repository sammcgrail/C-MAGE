I read this as a cyclic peptide: Ac‑Nle‑cyclo[Glu‑His‑D‑Phe(2‑CN)‑Arg‑Trp‑NH‑(CH2)4‑NH‑], where the Glu side‑chain amide closes the ring through a putrescine linker to the Trp C‑terminus.

**Fragments read from the drawing**
- N‑terminus: an acetyl group on norleucine (n‑butyl side chain).
- Ring, in order from the Glu α‑carbon: Glu (side‑chain C=O), then NH–(CH2)4–NH (four CH2 groups), then the Trp carbonyl, Trp (indol‑3‑ylmethyl), Arg (guanidinopropyl), 2‑cyanophenylalanine, His (imidazol‑4‑ylmethyl), and back to the Glu backbone carbonyl.
- Ring closure: the His NH forms an amide with the Glu backbone C=O, closed with the label %11 in the SMILES.

**Stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes**
- Nle, Glu, His, Arg and Trp each work out as S, which is the L configuration.
- The 2‑cyanophenylalanine has a bold wedge to its CH2 and works out as R, so it is the D configuration.
