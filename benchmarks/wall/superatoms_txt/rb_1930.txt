I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule: a larotrectinib-like imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine (a Trk-inhibitor-type ligand) joined through a piperazine amide and a PEG linker to a VHL ligand (hydroxyproline–tert-leucine).

**Left to right**
- **Aryl pyrrolidine:** a 3-fluorophenyl group sits on C2 of a pyrrolidine, through a hashed bond. Assigning CIP priorities (N > aryl > ring CH2 > H) gives R, which is written `[C@H]` in the SMILES below.
- **Core:** the pyrrolidine nitrogen attaches to C6 of the imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine. C3 of that bicycle carries a pyridin-2-yl group.
- **Piperazine:** the pyridine's other α-carbon bears a piperazine, which is acylated by the linker.
- **Linker:** C(=O)CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH, a three-ether PEG chain with amide ends.
- **tert-Leucine:** the NH is bonded to a tert-butyl-bearing α-carbon (hashed bond), which I took to be the natural S configuration.
- **Hydroxyproline:** the tert-leucine carbonyl acylates the hydroxyproline nitrogen. The ring carries a 4-OH and a 2-carboxamide, drawn with the usual (2S,4R) VHL-ligand stereochemistry.
- **Right end:** the carboxamide nitrogen is attached to a benzyl group whose para position carries a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl ring.

I matched the tert-leucine and hydroxyproline stereo tags against the standard VH032 SMILES rather than re-deriving them from the drawing.
