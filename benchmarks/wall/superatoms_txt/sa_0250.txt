The drawing shows a central silicon atom bonded to three isopropyl (iPr) groups and one SH group. This is triisopropylsilanethiol.

Each iPr group is CH(CH3)2, written as C(C)C in SMILES. The SH group is a sulfur with one hydrogen, so it is written as S.

Putting these together on silicon gives CC(C)[Si](S)(C(C)C)C(C)C. This has Si with four substituents (three isopropyl groups and one S) and no stereochemistry is drawn.

SMILES: CC(C)[Si](S)(C(C)C)C(C)C
