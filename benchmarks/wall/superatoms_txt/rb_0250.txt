I read the molecule as a spiro xanthene–isobenzofuranone (a fluorescein-type core) carrying a diaza-crown ether substituent.

**Spiro core.** The spiro carbon joins a lactone ring (O–C(=O)–aryl) and a xanthene.

**Xanthene.** Each of the two outer benzene rings carries, in order from the xanthene oxygen bridge: Cl, OAc, OMe, then an aromatic CH next to the spiro-bearing carbon. Both rings are substituted identically.

**Isobenzofuranone benzene ring.** Going around the ring from the spiro-attached carbon:
- an aromatic CH,
- a carbon bearing OMe,
- a carbon bearing the crown nitrogen,
- an aromatic CH,
- the carbon attached to the lactone C=O.

**Aza-crown.** Starting at the nitrogen on the isobenzofuranone ring, the 15-membered ring runs N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2 and closes back on the first N. It has two N and three O atoms (a 1,7-diaza-15-crown-5 type ring). The second nitrogen carries a 2-methoxyphenyl group.

The spiro carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I have given no stereochemistry.
