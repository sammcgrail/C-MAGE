This is a spiro fluoran dye: a xanthene core spiro-fused to a phthalide (isobenzofuranone).

**Left ring.** It carries the N(Et)(isopentyl) group. The isopentyl chain is N–CH2–CH2–CH(CH3)2, with the branch at the third carbon, which matches the drawing.

**Right ring.** It carries a methyl group and an NHPh group. Going around the ring from the oxygen-bearing carbon, the next carbon is CH, then C(Me), then C(NHPh), then CH, then the carbon attached to the spiro carbon. So Me sits next to the CH adjacent to C–O, and NHPh sits next to the CH adjacent to the spiro-bound carbon.

**Spiro carbon.** It bonds to the left ring, the right ring, the lactone oxygen, and the benzo ring. The lactone is O–C(=O)–aryl.

**SMILES assembly.**
- Spiro carbon with the lactone: C1(OC(=O)c2ccccc12)
- Left ring: c3ccc(N(CC)CCC(C)C)cc3O…, where the ring closure connects the carbon bearing O to the right ring.
- Right ring: c4cc(Nc5ccccc5)c(C)cc4 closes back to O. Written from the spiro-bound carbon, the right ring is c4cc(NPh)c(C)cc4O.

Full SMILES: CCN(CCC(C)C)c1ccc2c(c1)Oc1cc(C)c(Nc3ccccc3)cc1C21OC(=O)c2ccccc21

**Check of the ring closures.**
- Left ring: c1ccc2c(c1)O… The N-bearing carbon is c1. Then c, c, then c2, which is the spiro-attached carbon, then c(c1), which is the carbon bearing O. This gives N-carbon, CH, CH, C(spiro-side), C–O, CH back to N-carbon, so N is meta to O. That is correct.
- Right ring: Oc1cc(C)c(Nc3ccccc3)cc1C2… The O-bearing carbon is c1, then CH, then C(Me), then C(NHPh), then CH, then c1 (spiro-attached). Me is adjacent to the CH next to C–O, and NHPh is adjacent to the CH next to the spiro-attached carbon. This matches the drawing.
- Spiro carbon: C21 closes ring 2 to the left ring's c2 and opens a new ring 1 for the lactone, ending in c2ccccc21. The label 2 is reused after it closes. In "C21OC(=O)c2ccccc21", the spiro atom closes ring 2 and opens ring 1. Then c2 opens a new ring 2 and the final c21 closes both ring 2 (benzo) and ring 1 (spiro). This is valid.

SMILES: CCN(CCC(C)C)c1ccc2c(c1)Oc1cc(C)c(Nc3ccccc3)cc1C21OC(=O)c2ccccc21
