I read this as finerenone, a 1,4-dihydro-1,6-naphthyridine with an aryl group on the sp3 carbon.

**Ring system**
- The left ring is a dihydropyridine: N–H, a methyl-bearing carbon, a carbon carrying the primary amide (C(N)=O), the sp3 CH, and two fused carbons.
- The fused ring is a pyridine. Going round it: the carbon bearing a methyl, a CH, the ring N, a carbon bearing OEt (C=N), and the two fusion carbons.

**Substituents**
- The sp3 CH carries the aryl group on a wedge bond. The aryl is a benzene bearing OMe ortho to the attachment point and C≡N para to it.

**Stereochemistry**
- Only the wedged CH is a stereocentre. I assigned it by taking the neighbours in SMILES order: the pyridine-fusion carbon, H, the aryl carbon, and the amide-bearing carbon.
- The aryl is wedged toward the viewer, so H points away from the viewer. Seen from the front, the three drawn neighbours run counterclockwise: fusion carbon at lower left, then aryl at right, then amide-bearing carbon at top.
- Viewed from the H side, that sequence is clockwise. Moving H to the front of the neighbour list takes one swap, so the original order is `@`.
