The drawing shows a benzene ring with two substituents in a meta relationship: a CH(CH3)CO2H group and a "Bz" group.

"Bz" normally means benzoyl (C(=O)Ph), so I read it that way. This gives ketoprofen, 2-(3-benzoylphenyl)propanoic acid.

The ring carbon bearing the propanoic acid chain is at the right, and the Bz carbon is at the lower left. Going from the chain carbon (838,708) to (637,910) and then to the Bz carbon (363,837), the Bz carbon is two bonds away, so the groups are meta.

The stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so I include no stereochemistry.

SMILES: CC(C(=O)O)c1cccc(c1)C(=O)c1ccccc1
