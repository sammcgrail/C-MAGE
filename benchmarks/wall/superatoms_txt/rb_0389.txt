I read the structure as a head-to-side-chain cyclic peptide: Ac-Nle linked to a ring containing Asp, Met sulfone, 1-naphthylalanine, Arg, Trp and a pentane-1,5-diamine linker.

**Regions of the drawing**
- **Top:** an acetyl-capped norleucine. The butyl chain (H3C–CH2CH2CH2–) is attached to the α-carbon, and the acetylated NH sits on the other side.
- **Asp:** its side-chain carbonyl forms an amide with an NH that leads into a chain of 5 CH2 groups. That count is the least certain part of the reading.
- **Linker end:** the CH2 chain ends in an HN that forms an amide with the Trp carbonyl.
- **Ring, going round:** Asp → Met sulfone (side chain CH2CH2–SO2–CH3) → 1-naphthylalanine → Arg (side chain (CH2)3–NH–C(=NH)NH2) → Trp (indol-3-ylmethyl side chain) → the diamine linker → back to the Asp side chain.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned each α-carbon from its wedge or hash bond using CIP priorities (N > C=O > side-chain CH2 > H). The result is Nle R, Asp S, Met(O2) S, 1-Nal R, Arg R and Trp S, so the drawing shows D-Nle, D-1-Nal and D-Arg alongside L-Asp, L-Met(O2) and L-Trp. I then converted these to @/@@ tags for the atom order in the SMILES below.
