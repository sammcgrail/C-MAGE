The drawing shows Fmoc-protected O-tert-butyl-tyrosine (Fmoc-Tyr(tBu)-OH).

- **Side chain:** a para-substituted benzene ring carries an O–tBu ether on one side and a CH2 on the other.
- **Alpha carbon:** the CH2 links to the stereocentre, which bears NH–Fmoc, a CO2H group drawn on a wedge, and an implicit H.
- **Fmoc group:** I expanded it as NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–fluorenyl.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CO2H wedge points toward the viewer, so H points away. Viewed with H at the back, N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise. The priorities are N > CO2H > CH2, so the centre is S, which is the L-configuration.
- **SMILES chirality tag:** with the atoms written in the order CH2, H, N, CO2H, the S centre takes the tag `@`.
