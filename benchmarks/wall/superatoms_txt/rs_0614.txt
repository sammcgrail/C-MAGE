The drawing shows a pyridine ring with the nitrogen at the upper left. Reading around the ring:

- N is at the top left, bonded to a CH at the top.
- That CH is bonded to the C bearing OH (the 3-position).
- Next is the C bearing the amide carbonyl (the 4-position).
- The remaining ring atoms are two CH carbons, closing back to N.

The ring is therefore a pyridine with a 3-hydroxy group and a 4-carboxamide, and the amide nitrogen carries a benzyl group (NH–CH2–Ph).
