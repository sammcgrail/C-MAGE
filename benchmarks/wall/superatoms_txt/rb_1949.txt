I read the drawing as a cyclic peptide of nine residues with a piperidine amide on the Asp α-carboxyl. Going round the ring from the proline nitrogen, the residues are:

1. Pro: the ring nitrogen is acylated by the Leu carbonyl, and its Cα carries the carbonyl that continues the ring.
2. Asp: the α-carboxyl is a piperidine amide (C(=O)N1CCCCC1). The β-carboxyl is the one in the macrocycle, bonded to the next N-methyl nitrogen.
3. N-Me-Ala.
4. N-Me-Ala.
5. N-Me-Phe.
6. Thr: the Cβ bears OH and CH3.
7. N-Me-Phe.
8. Leu: the side chain is an isobutyl group, CH2CH(CH3)2.
9. Back to Pro.

I assigned each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes. All α-carbons come out as the natural L (S) configuration, and Thr Cβ is R, so the Thr is L-Thr (2S,3R).

I wrote the SMILES with the Pro nitrogen opening ring labels 1 and 9. Label 9 is closed by the final Leu carbonyl.
