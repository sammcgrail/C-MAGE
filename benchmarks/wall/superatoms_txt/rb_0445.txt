This is a PROTAC-like molecule with a thieno[3,2-c]pyridin-4-one core (an EZH2 or similar ligand) linked to a lenalidomide-type isoindolinone through a pyrrolidine.

**Core.** The thieno[3,2-c]pyridinone is built from these atoms:
- Thiophene: S, C2 (carrying the carboxamide), C3 (H), C3a, C7a.
- Pyridone ring: C3a–C4(=O)–N5(Me)–C6H=C7(aryl)–C7a.

As SMILES: O=C1N(C)C=C(Ar)C2=C1C=C(C(=O)N...)S2.

**Aryl group.** The phenyl ring bears 3,5-dimethoxy groups and a 4-CH2N(CH3)2 group: c1(OC)cc(...)cc(OC)c1CN(C)C.

**Amide side.** The amide N is attached to C3 of a pyrrolidine. The pyrrolidine N is attached to C5 of the isoindolinone.

**Isoindolinone.**
- The C=O carbon (C1) is bonded to the aromatic carbon C7a.
- C7a is adjacent to C7 (CH), which is adjacent to C6, the carbon bearing the pyrrolidine N.
- Aromatic ring: C7a, C7, C6 (N-substituted), C5 (H), C4 (H), C3a.
- C3a is bonded to CH2 (C3), which is bonded to the lactam N.
- The lactam N carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.

As SMILES: O=C1N(CC2=CC=C(N3CCC(C3)NC(=O)...)C=C12)C4CCC(=O)NC4=O.

**Stereochemistry.** No wedges or hashes are drawn, so no stereo is included.

**Full SMILES.**
CN(C)Cc1c(OC)cc(cc1OC)C1=CN(C)C(=O)c2cc(sc12)C(=O)NC1CCN(C1)c1ccc2CN(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C(=O)c2c1

The ring-closure label 1 is reused after the first ring closes. That is valid because the earlier label 1 was already closed at "C1=CN..." and then reopened later. The first "cc(cc1OC)" closes the ring-1 opened at "c1c(OC)", and then "C1=C..." opens a new ring 1 that closes at "sc12", so the labels are consistent. The later "NC1CCN(C1)" and the next "c1ccc2..." are also consistent.
