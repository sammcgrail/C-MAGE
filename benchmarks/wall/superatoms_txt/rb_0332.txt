I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type degrader in three parts: a VHL ligand, a PEG linker, and a pyrrolotriazine–isoquinoline ligand.

**VHL end**
- The hydroxyproline carries a hashed OH. I took it as the standard (2S,4R) form.
- The tert-leucine is drawn with a wedge, and I took it as the standard (S) form.
- The 1-[4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl]ethylamide has a wedged methyl. I assigned it by matching the standard VH032-type SMILES.

**Linker**
- From the tert-leucine NH the chain runs C(=O)–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH.
- I counted three ether oxygens in the drawing.

**Other end**
- The linker NH is attached at C6 of an isoquinoline.
- C1 of the isoquinoline carries a 1,2-disubstituted benzene.
- The benzene's second substituent is CH2–NH, which is bonded to a pyrrolo[2,1-f][1,2,4]triazine.
- The triazine has an isopropyl group on the pyrrole ring.
- It also has an O-linked piperidin-3-yl group. The wedge on the piperidine carbon, with priorities O > C2 (next to N) > C4, gives (R). I checked that the SMILES chirality tag encodes R.
