I read the drawing as a head-to-side-chain cyclic peptide: Ac-Nle followed by a ring of Glu, His, 4-fluoro-Phe, Arg and Trp.

- **N-terminus:** an acetyl group caps the norleucine (n-butyl side chain).
- **Ring closure:** the Glu side-chain carboxyl forms an amide with a 1,4-diaminobutane linker. The other end of that linker is amidated by the Trp C-terminus.
- **Side chains:** His is an imidazol-4-ylmethyl group, Phe carries a para-fluorine, Arg has a guanidine on a three-carbon chain, and Trp has an indol-3-ylmethyl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** I worked out the wedge/hash geometry for Nle (hashed butyl, S) and 4-F-Phe (wedged CH2, S) and both come out as the L configuration. I assumed the Glu, His, Arg and Trp centres are also L, which fits their wedges and hashes without my having checked each one.
