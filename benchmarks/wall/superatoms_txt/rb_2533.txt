This SMILES was written from the drawing as read, and I did not run it through a parser, so the valence and ring-closure checks are by eye. The stereocentres on the two prolines, the threonines, glutamate and biphenylalanines are partly guesses from hard-to-resolve wedges.

**Left side (glycocluster):**
- Three peracetylated β-D-GalNAc units, each linked through an O-(CH2)4-C(=O)-NH chain.
- The bottom sugar carries an ethoxy group at C3 instead of an acetate, as drawn.
- The top and bottom arms continue as NH-(CH2)3-NH-C(=O)CH2CH2-O-CH2- to a central quaternary carbon.
- The middle arm is written as drawn: NH-CH2CH2-O-CH2-C(=O)-CH2CH2-O-CH2-.
- The central carbon also carries NH-C(=O)-(CH2)6-C(=O)-NH, a suberoyl linker to the lysine side chain.

**Peptide, read N to C:**
1. The N-terminus is acylated by the CH2C(=O) of a thioether link to Cys.
2. The residues run D-Phe, Val, D-Pro, Thr, Thr, Bip, N-Me-Ala, Bip, N-Me-Ala, Glu, Ala, Pro, Cys (Bip = biphenylalanine).
3. The Cys side-chain sulfur closes the macrocycle through that CH2C(=O) group.
4. After Cys the chain continues Gly, then Lys-NH2, where the lysine side-chain amine carries the suberoyl linker.

**Stereochemistry:**
- Assigned from the wedges: D-Phe, L-Val, L-Cys, L-Lys, L-Ala, L-N-Me-Ala, the top L-Pro, L-Bip.
- Assumed or only tentatively read: L-Thr, L-Glu, and the bottom Pro as D.
- Sugars are taken as β-D-galacto.
