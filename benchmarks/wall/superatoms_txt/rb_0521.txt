I read this as a symmetric dimer: two identical N‑methylalanyl‑amino bicyclic thiazepine/pyrrolidine lactam units, each carrying a gem‑dimethyl group and a carboxamide to a 2‑oxy‑indan‑1‑yl group. The two indanyl oxygens are joined through CH2 groups to the para positions of a biphenyl.

I assigned stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes in the left half and then wrote the right half with the same configurations, since it is the left half rotated 180° in the plane.

- **N‑methylalanine:** drawn as L‑Ala (S).
- **Thiazepine C–NH carbon:** S.
- **C8a (the S–CH–N carbon bearing the hashed H):** S.
- **Pyrrolidine C7 (bearing the carboxamide):** S.
- **Indane:** both the C1–N and C2–O bonds look hashed on the left, so I took the indane as cis, giving (1S,2R). The drawing is hard to read here, so this is my best reading rather than a certain one.
