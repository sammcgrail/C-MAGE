I read the structure in four pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

- **Pyrazine core:** the ring carries the carboxamide on one carbon, the anilino NH on the neighbouring carbon, and the piperidin-1-yl group on the carbon across the ring from the carboxamide.
- **Piperidine substituent:** the piperidine C3 bears a 3-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl group on a wedge bond. With the wedged N as top priority, then C2, then C4, with H pointing back, the sequence runs clockwise, so the centre is R. In the SMILES this is written `[C@H]` when preceded by C4.
- **Linker:** the anilino phenyl is para-linked to a piperazine, and the other piperazine nitrogen carries a four-carbon chain. I counted four carbons from the zigzag of the drawn chain, though the exact peak positions were hard to read.
- **Isoindolinone end:** the chain attaches at the 4-position of a 1-oxoisoindolin-2-yl group. Its nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
