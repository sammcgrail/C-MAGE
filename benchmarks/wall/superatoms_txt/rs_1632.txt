The drawing shows a 2,6-dimethoxybenzamide whose amide nitrogen is attached to a substituted phenyl ring.

**Benzoyl part:** The left ring carries an OMe at the top and another at the bottom, both ortho to the carbonyl-bearing carbon. The carbonyl carbon has a C=O and is bonded to an NH.

**Anilide ring:** Number the ring from C1, the carbon bonded to N, and go around it.
- C1 bears the NH.
- C2 (upper) bears a methyl.
- C3 bears the pyrrolidin-1-yl group.
- C4 (lower right) bears a methyl.
- C5 is CH.
- C6 (lower left) bears a methyl.

So the ring is 2,4,6-trimethyl-3-(pyrrolidin-1-yl)phenyl.

**SMILES:** COc1cccc(OC)c1C(=O)Nc1c(C)cc(C)c(N2CCCC2)c1C

Check on the anilide ring in this SMILES: c1 (N-bearing), c(C) (C6), c (C5, CH), c(C) (C4), c(N2CCCC2) (C3), c1C (C2 with methyl). This matches the drawing.
