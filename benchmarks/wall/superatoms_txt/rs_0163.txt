I read the molecule as a morpholine amide linked through a CH2 to a stereocentre bearing a cyclohexylmethyl group and a second amide. That amide nitrogen is attached to an isopropyl-bearing stereocentre, whose CH2 carries an NH-aryl group with a para-OCF3.

**Fragments**
- Morpholine ring: N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2, with its nitrogen acylated (N–C(=O)).
- Chain: C(=O)–CH2–CH(CH2-cyclohexyl)–C(=O)–NH.
- Second stereocentre: CH(isopropyl)–CH2–NH–C6H4–OCF3 (para-substituted benzene).

**Stereocentre 1** (the CH bearing the hashed cyclohexylmethyl bond)
- Priorities: C(=O)N > CH2C(=O) > CH2-cyclohexyl > H.
- Viewed from the front, with the hashed group pointing away and H toward the viewer, the sequence C(=O)N → CH2C(=O) → CH2Cy runs counterclockwise. Reversing for H toward the viewer gives R.
- In the SMILES below this centre is written `[C@@H]`.

**Stereocentre 2** (the CH bearing the wedged isopropyl group)
- Priorities: N > CH2N > isopropyl > H.
- With the isopropyl wedged toward the viewer and H pointing back, N → CH2 → iPr runs counterclockwise, which gives S.
- In the SMILES below this centre is written `[C@@H]`.

SMILES: O=C(N1CCOCC1)C[C@@H](CC2CCCCC2)C(=O)N[C@@H](C(C)C)CNc3ccc(OC(F)(F)F)cc3
