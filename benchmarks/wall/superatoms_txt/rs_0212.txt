**Right-hand part.** The nitrogen carries a benzyl group whose ring has CF3 at the meta position. Counting in the other direction gives attachment, upper right, lower right, bottom, which is also three atoms away. Positions 1, 2, 3 would then be attachment, lower-left or upper-right, and bottom, so the substituent is meta.
