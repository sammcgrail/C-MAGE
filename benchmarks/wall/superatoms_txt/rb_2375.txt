I read the molecule as a macrocycle built from these pieces:

- **Piperazic acid:** a six-membered ring (CH–CH2–CH2–CH2–N–NH) whose ring carbon carries the ester carbonyl. The ring nitrogen is acylated.
- **Linker:** the acylated nitrogen is bonded to C(=O)–CH(NH–acyl)–CH(OEt), and the CH(OEt) carbon is bonded to C4 of an oxazole.
- **Oxazole to indole:** the oxazole C2 is bonded to C5 of an N-ethylindole.
- **Indole to ester:** indole C3 carries CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2–O, which forms the ester to the piperazic carbonyl and closes the macrocycle.
- **Pyridine:** it is attached at indole C2. It bears a CH(CH3)OMe group next to the ring nitrogen and an octahydropyrido[2,1-c][1,4]oxazine at its 5-position.
- **Cyclopropane:** the amide acyl group is a 2,3-dimethylcyclopropane-1-carbonyl with both methyls wedged (cis) and the carbonyl hashed.

I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash, working out the CIP descriptor and converting it to the SMILES tag. The results are:

- Piperazic C3: S
- The N-bearing α-carbon: S
- CH(OEt): S
- CH(CH3)OMe: S
- The bicyclic carbon attached to the pyridine: R
- The three cyclopropane carbons: tags worked out from the ring geometry

The ring-fusion carbon of the bicyclic amine is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it unspecified.
