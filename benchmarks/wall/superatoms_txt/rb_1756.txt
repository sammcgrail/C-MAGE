I read this as a thalidomide-based PROTAC-type molecule. It has three parts:

- **Cereblon-binding end:** a glutarimide attached to the nitrogen of a phthalimide, with the phthalimide carrying an NH at its 4-position.
- **Linker:** that NH is joined through a CH2 to C4 of an imidazole. The imidazole N1 carries an n-propyl chain ending in NH–C(=O)–O, a carbamate.
- **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on the hexahydronaphthalene ring of a lovastatin-type core. This core has the 2- and 6-methyl groups and two ring C=C bonds, plus the ethylene link to the hydroxy-δ-lactone.

I did not derive each stereocentre independently from the wedges and hashes. Instead I assumed the statin core has the natural lovastatin configuration, which the drawing appears to match, and wrote its stereo tags from the lovastatin SMILES. The glutarimide CH is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.

I checked that the ring-closure digits are paired and that the stereo tags keep the same neighbour order as in lovastatin.
