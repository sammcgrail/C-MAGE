The drawing is an oxindole (isatin) hydrazone joined to a biaryl carrying a carboxylic acid, a fluorine and a phenol.

**Left biaryl.** The top ring has F at the upper left and CO2H at the left. The ring atoms run C(F), C(CO2H), CH, C(attached to the second ring), CH, CH. The carbon bearing CO2H is adjacent to the carbon bearing F, and the biaryl bond is para to F. In the ring, the F carbon is at the top left and the CO2H carbon is below it. Going around, the next atom is a CH at the bottom, then the carbon bearing the lower ring (at the right, lower), then the top right CH, then the top CH adjacent to the F carbon. So the attachment carbon is para to the F carbon, which is consistent.

**Middle ring.** The ring carbon attached to the top ring is adjacent to the carbon bearing OH. The next carbon carries the NH of the hydrazone. So the ring is 1-aryl, 2-OH, 3-NH–N.

**Hydrazone and oxindole.** The NH–N= unit leads to C3 of the oxindole. C2 is C=O and N1 carries the 3,4-dimethylphenyl group. The benzo ring carries CO2Me at C6. C6 is meta to C3a-linked... the ester sits on the carbon para to C4, which is the carbon attached to C7, and C7 is adjacent to C7a, which bears N.
