I read the molecule as a VHL-type PROTAC: a hydroxyproline VHL ligand joined through an isoxazole and a butoxy linker to a SMARCA2-type ligand.

**Left, VHL ligand**
- A 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group sits on a phenyl ring, which carries a CH2NH–C(=O) group.
- The amide is on a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine-2-carboxamide. I took the wedge at C2 and the hash at C4 as the usual (2S,4R) VHL arrangement and wrote it as `[C@@H]2C[C@@H](O)CN2`, copied from the standard VH032 pattern rather than re-derived from the drawing.
- The pyrrolidine nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)–CH(iPr)–isoxazol-5-yl. That CH is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

**Linker**
- The isoxazole carries an O–CH2CH2CH2CH2 chain on its 3-position; I counted four chain carbons.
- The chain ends on a piperidine nitrogen, and the piperidine C4 is bonded to pyrimidine C5.

**Right, SMARCA2-type ligand**
- Pyrimidine C2 is bonded to N8 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane.
- N3 of that bicycle is bonded to C4 of a pyridazine.
- The pyridazine has NH2 on C3 and a 2-hydroxyphenyl on C6.

The bicycle is written as `N5CC6CCC(C5)N6`, with N3 closing ring 5 and N8 closing ring 6.
