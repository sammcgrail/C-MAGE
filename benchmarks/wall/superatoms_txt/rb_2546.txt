- The quaternary C(CH3) carries an O-propyl group.

- C1: the glycosidic O
- C2: OH
- C3: N(CH3)2
- C4: CH2
- C5: CH(CH3)
- the ring O closes back to C1

**Stereochemistry.** The CH of the macrocycle attached to N has only plain bonds, so it has no stereo label. All other drawn wedge and hash centres are included.
