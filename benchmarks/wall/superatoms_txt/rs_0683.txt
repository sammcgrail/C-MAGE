- **Linker:** the CH2CH2 chain ends in an NH that is bonded to the right-hand benzene ring.
- **Right ring:** a para-substituted benzene whose other substituent is CH2–CH(OEt)–COOH.
- **Stereocentre:** the only wedge in the drawing is the bold bond from the CH carbon to OEt, so the stereocentre is the CH bearing OEt. Viewed with H pointing away, OEt (bottom) → COOH (upper right) → CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S.
- **SMILES chirality:** for the written order O, H, CH2, COOH, `[C@@H]` corresponds to S.
