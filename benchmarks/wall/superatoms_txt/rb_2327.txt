This SMILES was read directly from the drawing. It describes a head-to-side-chain macrocyclic peptide closed by a thioether, and I traced it residue by residue from the C-terminal glycinamide.

**Sequence, C-terminus to N-terminus:**

- The Cys side-chain sulfur forms a thioether with an S–CH2–C(=O) group. This carbonyl acylates the N-terminal Tyr nitrogen, which closes the macrocycle.
- Leu, then N-Me-norleucine (butyl side chain), then another N-Me-norleucine.
- Trp whose indole nitrogen carries CH2COOH, then ornithine. The ornithine side-chain NH is attached to an isopropoxy-squarate (a cyclobutenedione ring).
- Trp (free indole NH), then trans-4-hydroxyproline.
- Leu, then 2,3-diaminopropionic acid (Dap). The Dap side-chain NH is attached to a second isopropoxy-squarate.

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash bond and the neighbouring-group priorities. All residues come out as the natural L configuration:
- Cys is R.
- The other α-carbons are S.
- The hydroxyproline C4 is R, which makes it trans-4-hydroxy-L-proline.
