- **Left chain:** Ph–CH2–CH2–CH(CO2Et)–NH–, with the CO2Et group on a wedge bond.
- **Alanine unit:** the NH is attached to a CH(CH3) carbon whose methyl is on a hashed bond. That carbon is bonded to C(=O), which forms an amide with the ring nitrogen.
- **Ring:** a 6,7-dimethoxy-tetrahydroisoquinoline. The ring nitrogen sits next to a CH2 that joins the aromatic ring, and the ring carbon bearing CO2H is on a wedge bond.

I checked the first stereocentre (the one bearing CO2Et) against the drawing. With the implicit H pointing away, the ester, N and CH2 neighbours run clockwise as ester → N → CH2.
