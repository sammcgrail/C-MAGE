The drawing shows a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core joined to an indoline.

**Pyrazinone ring.** N1 carries a sec-butyl group, CH(CH3)CH2CH3. C2 is the carbonyl (C=O). C3 is bonded to the indoline nitrogen and double-bonded to N4. C5 carries Cl and C6 is a CH. The ring double bonds are C5=C6 and C3=N4.

**Indoline.** The nitrogen bonds to CH2, then CH2, then the aromatic ring. On the benzene ring, C7a is bonded to N and C7 carries the methyl group. C6 is a CH, C5 carries OMe, C4 is a CH, and C3a joins the CH2.

**SMILES.** Writing the pyrazinone first gives CCC(C)N1C=C(Cl)N=C(N2CCc3cc(OC)cc(C)c32)C1=O.

Checking the indoline part, N2CCc3cc(OC)cc(C)c32: after c3 (C3a) comes c (C4), then c(OC) (C5), c (C6), c(C) (C7), and c32 (C7a), which closes the benzene ring and bonds back to N. This matches the drawing.

The sec-butyl carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is specified.
