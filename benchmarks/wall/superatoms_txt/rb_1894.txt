I read the molecule in sections and then joined them into one SMILES.

1. **Left end:** a 4,5-dihydrotetrazolo[1,5-a]pyrimidine. The ring carries an N-methyl, a C-methyl on the C=C, and a CH2 on the bridgehead tetrazole nitrogen. The carboxamide sits on the alkene carbon next to the CH2.
2. **Amide link:** the C(=O)NH joins this ring to C5 of a 1H-indazole.
3. **Indazole:** C3 carries a pyridin-4-yl group. The pyridine C2 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen.
4. **Piperazine:** a 2,6-dimethylpiperazine with both methyls on wedge bonds, so it is the cis (meso) isomer. Working out the CIP labels for my first draft gave R,R, which is trans. I therefore flipped one centre to get R,S (cis), matching the two wedges.
5. **Linker:** the other piperazine nitrogen carries a CH2 to C4 of a piperidine. That piperidine's nitrogen carries a CH2 to C4 of a second piperidine.
6. **Right end:** the second piperidine's nitrogen is bonded to C5 of a phthalimide. The imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, whose stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds and so has no stereo label.
