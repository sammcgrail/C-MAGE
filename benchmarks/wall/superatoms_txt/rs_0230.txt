I read the molecule as a methanesulfonate ester on a para-substituted benzene. That ring carries a three-carbon chain (CH2CH2CH2) to an NH, which is attached to a second para-substituted benzene. That ring bears CH2–CH(OEt)–CO2H.

**Stereocentre:** The carbon bearing OEt has a hashed H and a wedged OEt, so it is the only stereocentre drawn with stereo bonds.
- CIP priorities are OEt > CO2H > CH2Ar > H.
- H points away from the viewer. Going OEt (lower right) → CO2H (upper right) → CH2Ar (upper left) is counterclockwise, so the centre is S.
- In the SMILES `C[C@H](OCC)C(=O)O`, the neighbour order is CH2, H, OEt, CO2H. This order with `@` corresponds to S.

SMILES: CS(=O)(=O)Oc1ccc(CCCNc2ccc(C[C@H](OCC)C(=O)O)cc2)cc1
