**Coumarin core**
- The ring oxygen O1 is bonded to C2, which carries the C=O.
- C3 carries the CH2C(=O)NH-aryl chain.
- C4 carries the aryl group.
- On the benzo ring, C4a and C8a are the fusion carbons. Going from C4a, C5 is CH, C6 bears the methyl, C7 bears Cl, C8 is CH, and C8a is bonded to O1.

**C3 side chain**

- Numbering from the N-attached carbon (C1), the CF3 is at C2 (below it in the drawing) and the F is at C4 (para to N).

**C4 aryl group**
- A phenyl ring attached at C4 of the coumarin, with CH2CO2Me at the meta position.
