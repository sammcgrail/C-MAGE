I read the molecule in four parts and joined them into one SMILES.

- **Pyrazine core:** The ring carries a primary carboxamide, an NH link to a 3-methylisothiazol-5-yl group, and a piperidin-1-yl substituent. The remaining ring position is CH.
- **Piperidine and urea:** The piperidine is substituted at its 3-position by an NH that forms a urea. The wedge on that NH bond fixes the stereocentre. From the drawing I assigned it R. The `[C@H]` in the SMILES below encodes that configuration with the neighbour order I used.
- **Spiro diamine:** The urea carbonyl connects to a 3,9-diazaspiro[5.5]undecane. Its other nitrogen is bonded to the aromatic ring of the isoindoline-1,3-dione.
- **Imide end:** The isoindoline-1,3-dione nitrogen is attached to a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl), whose stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds and so carries no stereo label.
