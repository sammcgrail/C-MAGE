I read the structure in four pieces and assigned each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes using CIP priorities.

**Left fragment.** This is a 7-hydroxy-5-methyl-6,7-dihydro-5H-cyclopenta[d]pyrimidine whose C4 carries a piperazine nitrogen. The drawing gives 5R (hashed methyl) and 7R (wedged OH), the same as the (5R,7R) pattern of ipatasertib.

**Acyl piperazine and chlorophenyl centre.** The piperazine's other nitrogen is acylated by a C(=O)–CH(4-chlorophenyl)–CH2–NH unit. The aryl group is on a wedge, and the CIP analysis gives S. The priorities are C(=O)N > CH2N > aryl.

**Linker.** From the NH the chain is CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–C(=O)–NH. That is a PEG-type linker ending in an amide to the next fragment.

**VHL ligand.**
- The tert-leucine unit has S configuration (L), with the tert-butyl on a hash.
- The 4-hydroxyproline is (2S,4R).
- The proline carboxyl forms an amide with (S)-1-[4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl]ethylamine.

I wrote the SMILES with the chirality tags worked out centre by centre. For the hydroxyproline and the benzylic amine I also checked the tags against the known SMILES of L-hydroxyproline and (S)-1-phenylethylamine.
