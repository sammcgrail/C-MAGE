I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule with three parts.

- **Left, thalidomide-type binder:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on an isoindoline-1,3-dione. An NH at the 4-position of the benzo ring carries the linker.
- **Linker:** NH–CH2CH2–O–(CH2)5–O–CH2CH2–NH. I counted five carbons between the two ether oxygens in the middle of the chain. The terminal NH forms a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O.
- **Right, statin-type unit:** the carbamate oxygen is on the hexahydronaphthalene ring of lovastatin. That ring system has two ring methyls and a C=C–C=C diene pattern. A two-carbon chain leads to a 4-hydroxy-6-oxotetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone.

I did not derive the stereocentres independently from the wedges. I assumed the statin portion has the natural lovastatin configuration and reused its stereo-SMILES, with the 2-methylbutanoate ester replaced by the carbamate. The thalidomide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
