**Ring and substituents**
- The lactam ring has five atoms: N, C=O, two CH2 groups and the C5 stereocentre.
- N–CH2CH2 leads to a para-disubstituted benzene ring bearing CO2H.
- The side-chain double bond next to the ring is drawn trans (E), and the chain ends in a terminal vinyl group.

**Stereocentres**
