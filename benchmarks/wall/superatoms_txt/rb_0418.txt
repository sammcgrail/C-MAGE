This is a macrocyclic STING agonist-type dimer: two benzimidazole units joined by a butene linker and by an O-CH2-CH(N-pyrrolidine)-CH2-O linker.

**Upper benzimidazole.** N1 carries the butenyl chain. C2 carries NH-C(=O)-pyrazole. N3 is the =N. C7 carries the O linker. C5 carries the primary amide (CONH2).
- Pyrazole: 1-ethyl-3-methylpyrazol-5-yl, written CCn1nc(C)cc1C(=O)N...

**Linker.** From the upper N1: CH2-CH=CH-CH2 to the lower N1. The double bond appears to be E (trans), as drawn, so I write it as /C=C/.

**Central chain.** Upper aryl-O-CH2-CH(N-pyrrolidinyl)-CH2-O-lower aryl. The pyrrolidine is (S)-3-methoxy, attached through its N.

**SMILES construction.**

Wait: the upper aryl O should connect through CH2-CH(N)-CH2 to the lower aryl O. In this SMILES the lower aryl O is written as "OCC(N...)CO%10", where %10 closes onto the upper aryl O at the end. That gives upper-O-CH2-CH(N)-CH2-O-lower, which is correct.

**Stereocentre.** The pyrrolidine C3 is labeled (S), with the OMe on a wedge. For the written order N5-C-C-[C@H](OC)-C5, the neighbors of the stereocentre are the preceding CH2 (C4), H, OMe, and the following CH2 (C2, attached to N).
- CIP priorities: OMe > C2 (N-attached) > C4 > H.
- With @ and neighbor order (C4, H, O, C2), viewed from C4 the sequence H→O→C2 runs counterclockwise.
- Working through the chirality, this arrangement corresponds to R, so I use @@ to get S.
