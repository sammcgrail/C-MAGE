The drawing shows a Boc-protected phenylalanine methyl ester: Ph–CH2–CH(NHBoc)–CO2Me, with the only stereo marker being a hashed bond from the stereocentre to the CO2Me group.

- **Substituents:** the central carbon carries CH2Ph (upper left), NHBoc (right) and CO2Me (lower left, hashed), plus an implicit H.
- **Boc and ester:** Boc expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C, and MeO2C is the methyl ester C(=O)OC.
- **Stereochemistry:**
  1. CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2Ph > H.
  2. With CO2Me hashed (pointing away), the implicit H points toward the viewer.
  3. On the page, N (right) → CO2Me (lower left) → CH2Ph (upper left) runs clockwise.
  4. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This is the L-phenylalanine configuration.
- **SMILES chirality tag:** writing the neighbours in the order ester C, H, CH2, N, the tag that gives S is [C@H].
