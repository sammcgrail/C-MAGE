This is vancomycin with its vancosamine amine converted to a thiourea. The thiourea is linked through a triethylene-glycol-type chain (three ether oxygens) to a 1,2,3-triazole, and the triazole carries a CH2–purine group. The purine is a 6-amino-2-butoxy-8-hydroxy-purine joined through N9, which makes this a TLR7-agonist conjugate.

I wrote the vancomycin core from memory of its stereo-defined SMILES rather than re-deriving each stereocentre from the wedges. I did not re-check the stereo tags against the drawing, so they are the least certain part of the answer. The thiourea, chain, triazole and purine are plain connectivity with no stereo.
