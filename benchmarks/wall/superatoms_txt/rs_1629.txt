- The CH(CH3) carbon has a bold-wedge methyl.
- The next carbon carries an isovalerate ester (OC(=O)CC(C)C) on a hashed bond.
- The carbon after that carries a benzyl group on a bold wedge.
- The NH is on a hashed bond.
