The drawing shows sodium valproate: a central CH carbon bonded to two n-propyl groups and to a carboxylate, with a separate Na+ counterion.

- Central carbon: three plain bonds, to nPr (up), nPr (lower left) and COO⁻ (lower right).
- nPr expands to CCC, so each branch is a straight three-carbon chain.
- Charges: the carboxylate carries a negative charge and sodium carries a positive charge.
- Stereochemistry: the central carbon has two identical propyl groups, so it is not a stereocentre and none is needed.
