I read the structure as a lipidated peptide with one disulfide-bridged macrocycle. The stereocentres are an assumption (see the end).

**Macrocycle:** N-acetyl-penicillamine, Asn, Thr, 7-methyl-Trp, N-ε-acetyl-Lys, then a second penicillamine. The two penicillamine sulfurs are joined by the S–S bond.

**Chain leaving the second penicillamine:**
1. Tyr with a 4-(2-aminoethoxy) group on the ring.
2. 2-Naphthylalanine.
3. 4-Aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid, whose ring carbon carries both the amide N and the carbonyl.
4. Glu.
5. Asn.
6. 3-Pyridylalanine.
7. N-methylglycine.
8. A lysine-like residue in which the alpha carboxyl is replaced by a methyl ketone.
9. The lysine side-chain nitrogen carries γ-Glu, which is acylated by a long fatty acyl chain.

**Fatty chain length:** I could not count the chain precisely from the drawing. I took it as palmitoyl (C16), a common choice for this kind of γGlu lipid, so that length is an estimate.

**Stereochemistry:** I did not resolve every wedge and hash individually. I assigned the usual L-amino-acid configurations, so the @/@@ marks are my best guess rather than a full reading of the drawing.
