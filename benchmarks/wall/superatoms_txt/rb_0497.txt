This looks like a STING agonist: a macrocycle built from two benzimidazole units joined by a but-2-enyl linker on one side and a 2-(4,4-difluoropiperidin-1-yl)propane-1,3-diyl bis-ether on the other.

**Building blocks**
- Each half has a 1-ethyl-3-methylpyrazole-5-carbonyl group. Its SMILES is CCn1nc(C)cc1C(=O)N.
- The amide NH is attached to C2 of a benzimidazole.
- N1 of the benzimidazole carries the butenyl chain.
- C7 carries the O-CH2 ether.
- C5 carries the primary amide, C(N)=O.
- The alkene is drawn as a trans (E) double bond. N-CH2-CH=CH-CH2-N is drawn with the two CH2 groups on opposite sides, so I wrote it as E.

**Top benzimidazole**
- N1 is bonded to the CH2 of the butenyl chain.
- C2 is bonded to the NH-acyl group.
- N3 is the "N" at the top.
- C3a leads to C4(H), then C5 (bearing CONH2), then C6(H), then C7 (bearing O), then C7a, which bonds back to N1.

SMILES for this half: n1(C/C=C/C...)c(NC(=O)c2cc(C)nn2CC)nc3cc(C(N)=O)cc(OC...)c13

**Bottom benzimidazole**
- It has the same pattern: N1 is attached to the CH2 of the butenyl chain, C7 carries O, and C5 carries CONH2.
