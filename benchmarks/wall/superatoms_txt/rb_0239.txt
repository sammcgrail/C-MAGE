I read the drawing as a macrocyclic peptide made of three residues, closed through a thioether-linked aryl/pyridine bridge.

**Residues, from the left of the drawing**
- **Dab residue:** a 2,4-diaminobutyric acid with a free terminal NH2. Its α-amine is an NH that continues as CH2 to the pyridine, and its carbonyl forms an amide to the next residue.
- **Lys residue:** a lysine with a free terminal NH2 on a four-carbon chain. It is joined to the N-methyl residue through its carbonyl.
- **N-Me-Trp residue:** an N-methyl tryptophan whose indole carries a 2-methyl group. Its carbonyl forms an amide NH that continues as a CH2 to the aryl ring.

**Bridge**
- The pyridine is substituted at C3 by the CH2NH that connects to the Dab nitrogen, and at C2 by the sulfur.
- The sulfur is attached to the central benzene ring.
- On that benzene, the neighbouring position carries the CH2NH (linked to the Trp carbonyl). The position on the other side carries a 4-carboxyphenyl group.

**Stereochemistry:** all three α-carbons are drawn with wedges, and I took them to be the L configuration. I set the @/@@ marks from that assumption by checking neighbour order against the standard L-amino-acid SMILES, not by tracing each wedge individually.
