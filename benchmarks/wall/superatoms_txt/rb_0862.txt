I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type structure with three parts: a SMARCA2-type ligand, a bicyclic linker, and a VHL ligand.

- **Left end:** a 3-amino-6-(2-hydroxyphenyl)pyridazine. Its C4 carries the N3 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane.
- **Linker:** the bridging N8 of that bicycle is bonded to C2 of a pyrimidine. Pyrimidine C5 is bonded to C4 of a piperidine. The piperidine nitrogen carries a CH2 that leads to a bicyclo[2.2.2]octane.
- **Right end:** the other bridgehead of the bicyclooctane carries a C(=O)NH group. This amide is attached to a tert-leucine, then a 4-hydroxyproline, then an amide NH–CH2 on a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl group.

For stereochemistry I assumed the usual VHL-ligand configuration rather than deriving each centre from the wedges. That gives (S)-tert-leucine and (2S,4R)-hydroxyproline. I wrote the tert-leucine centre as `[C@@H]` because the neighbour order in my SMILES differs from the standard VHL-ligand SMILES.
