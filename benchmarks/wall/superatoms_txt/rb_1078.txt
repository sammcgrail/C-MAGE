Reading the drawing region by region:

**Pyrazine core.** The ring atoms are N, CH, C(piperidinyl), N, C(NH-pyrazolyl), C(CONH2). The carboxamide carbon is attached to a ring carbon that is adjacent to a ring N. The NH-pyrazole carbon is adjacent to that carbon and to the second ring N. The piperidine-bearing carbon is next to the CH.

Pyrazine SMILES: c1(C(N)=O)c(Nc2cnn(C)c2)nc(N3CCCC(C3)...)cn1

**Piperidine.** The N is attached to the pyrazine. The ring runs N–CH2–CH(substituent)–CH2–CH2–CH2 back to N, so it is a 3-substituted piperidine.

**Spiro oxazolidinone.** The piperidine C3 bears the oxazolidinone N. The five-membered ring is N–C(=O)–O–C(spiro)–CH2–N. The spiro carbon is shared with an azetidine: C(spiro), CH2, N, CH2. The spiro-azetidine N carries a CH2 group.

Oxazolidinone/azetidine SMILES: N1C(=O)OC2(C1)CN(C2)C...

**Linker and isoindoline.** The CH2 is attached to C3 of a second azetidine, whose N is bonded to the isoindoline-1,3-dione aromatic ring at position 5. The isoindoline N is attached to the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
