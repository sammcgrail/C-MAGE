The drawing is Boc-protected threonine: a three-carbon chain with the Boc-NH group on C2, a hydroxyl on C3, a methyl on C3 and a carboxylic acid on C2.

**Connectivity:**
- The alpha carbon carries NH–Boc, the CO2H group and the beta carbon.
- The beta carbon carries OH, the CH3 and the alpha carbon.
- Boc expands to C(=O)OC(C)(C)C, attached through the carbamate carbon to N.

**Stereochemistry:** the wedges are on the CO2H bond (alpha carbon) and the CH3 bond (beta carbon). The implicit hydrogen on each stereocentre points away from the viewer.
- Alpha carbon: the priorities are N > CO2H > CB. Viewed from the front, N → CB → CO2H runs clockwise, so N → CO2H → CB runs counterclockwise, giving S.
- Beta carbon: the priorities are OH > Cα > CH3. Going OH → Cα → CH3 runs clockwise, giving R.

This is the (2S,3R) configuration, as in L-threonine. The SMILES below is L-threonine written with the Boc group on nitrogen, with the neighbour order unchanged so the chirality tags carry over.

SMILES: C[C@@H](O)[C@H](NC(=O)OC(C)(C)C)C(=O)O
