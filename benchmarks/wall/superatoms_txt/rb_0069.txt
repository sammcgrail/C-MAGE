I read the molecule as seven fragments and joined them in one SMILES.

- **Carbamate end:** an isopropyl ester, `CC(C)OC(=O)`, on an acylguanidine nitrogen, `N=C(N…)N…`.
- **Guanidine substituents:** one NH leads to the side chain. The other nitrogen is the N of a pyrrolidine carrying a 2-pyridyl group at C3, written `N1CCC(c2ccccn2)C1`.
- **Side chain:** NH–CH2–CH(CH3)–, attached to C4 of the fused ring system.
- **Fused core:** a 6H-thieno[2,3-b]pyrrole. The pyrrole NH is next to the carbon bonded to S. C5 carries a 3,5-dimethylphenyl group and C4 carries the side chain.
- **Thiophene substituent:** the thiophene carbon next to S carries C(CH3)2–C(=O)–N.
- **Amide nitrogen:** it is the bridging N of a 7-azabicyclo[2.2.1]heptane, written `N1C2CCC1CC2`.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only drawn stereocentre is the side-chain CH, which has a hashed methyl. Working through the neighbour order gives `[C@@H]` when the SMILES is written as `NC[C@@H](C)c…`.

The pyrrolidine C3 is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
