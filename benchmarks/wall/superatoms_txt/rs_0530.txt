- **Morpholine amidine:** a morpholine ring joined through its nitrogen to a carbon bearing =NH. That carbon attaches to a para-substituted benzene ring, giving N=C(N1CCOCC1)c1ccc(cc1)...
- **First amide:** the benzene ring carries C(=O)NH, and this NH is attached to the central benzene ring.
- **Central ring:** the carbon bearing the NH has an ortho carbon carrying C(=O)NH-pyridyl. The OMe sits on the carbon para to the NH carbon and meta to the carboxamide carbon. The OMe carbon is therefore adjacent to the upper right CH, which is adjacent to the C(C=O) carbon, so the OMe is meta to the carboxamide and para to the NH.
