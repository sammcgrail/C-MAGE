I read the macrocycle as a linear chain of residues that closes back on itself, and wrote the SMILES by tracing that ring from the N-methyl cyclopentylglycine nitrogen.

3. Azepane-2-carboxylic acid (the azepane ring is the cyclic amine of the previous residue).
4. Isoleucine, drawn with the L configuration at both the α and β carbons.
5. Sarcosine.
6. Sarcosine.

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash bond:
- Cyclopentylglycine α-carbon: wedge to the cyclopentyl group.
- Aspartic acid α-carbon: wedge to the morpholine carbonyl.
- Azepane C2: wedge to the ring CH2.
- Isoleucine β-carbon: wedge to the methyl group.
- N-propyl phenylalanine α-carbon: hash to the benzyl CH2.
- Homophenylalanine α-carbon: hash to the chain CH2.
- Proline α-carbon: wedge to the ring CH2.

The isoleucine α-carbon is the least certain, because I could not tell which end of its wedge is narrow. I took the wedge as pointing toward the NH, which gives the natural L-isoleucine configuration.
