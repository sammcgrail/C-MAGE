I read the structure in fragments from left to right.

1. The far left is a methylsulfonyl group (CH3SO2) on the nitrogen of a pyrrole. The pyrrole carries a 3-carboxamide, so this fragment is CS(=O)(=O)n1ccc(c1)C(=O)N.
2. That amide nitrogen connects through a glycine unit, NCC(=O)N, to the 2-amino position of a thiazole.
3. The thiazole is substituted at C4 by a meta-disubstituted benzene ring.
4. The other meta position of the benzene carries C3 of a pyrazole. The pyrazole N1 bears a CH2C(=O)NH group.
5. An unbranched chain of 8 CH2 groups links that amide NH to a second NH. I counted the zigzag vertices as 8, but this was an estimate from the drawing and the least certain part of the reading.
6. That NH is acylated by C(=O)CH2O, and the oxygen is attached to the 4-position of a phthalimide ring.
7. The phthalimide nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine (glutarimide). That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereochemistry.
