I read the molecule as a VHL-ligand-based PROTAC: a hydroxyproline/tert-leucine unit joined by a PEG-type linker to a pyrazolo[1,5-a][1,3,5]triazine bearing an isopropyl group and a piperidin-3-yloxy group.

**VHL end**
- The (4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl ring carries a CH(CH3)–NH– group with the stereocentre drawn on the methyl.
- That NH forms an amide with a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine-2-carboxylic acid. The OH is drawn hashed and the C2 carbonyl bond is drawn as a wedge.
- The pyrrolidine nitrogen is acylated by tert-leucine, whose NH is drawn on a wedge.

**Linker**
- The tert-leucine NH is acylated by C(=O)CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–.
- The terminal NH is attached to C6 of an isoquinoline.

**Warhead end**
- C1 of the isoquinoline is bonded to a benzene ring, which carries an ortho CH2–NH– group.
- That NH is attached to the pyrazolo[1,5-a][1,3,5]triazine. The ring also carries an isopropyl group on the pyrazole carbon and an O-linked piperidin-3-yl group on the triazine.

**Piperidine stereochemistry**
- The C–O bond to the piperidine C3 is drawn as a wedge. Ranking the substituents gives O > C2 (next to N) > C4 > H, with H pointing back and the sequence O→C2→C4 running clockwise, so the centre is (R).
- In the SMILES, `O[C@@H]3CCCNC3` encodes that (R) configuration.
