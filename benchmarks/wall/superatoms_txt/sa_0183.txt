The drawing shows a central silicon atom bonded to three isopropyl (iPr) groups and one chlorine atom. This is chlorotriisopropylsilane.

Expanding each iPr into explicit atoms gives CH(CH3)2, so the SMILES is Cl[Si](C(C)C)(C(C)C)C(C)C. All bonds are plain and there are no stereocentres.

SMILES: CC(C)[Si](Cl)(C(C)C)C(C)C
