I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule with three parts joined in a row.

1. **Thalidomide-type end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group sits on the nitrogen of a phthalimide (1,3-dioxoisoindoline). The 4-position of the aromatic ring carries an NH that links to the 4-position of a piperidine.
2. **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen is joined to a four-carbon chain (N–CH2CH2CH2CH2–NH), which ends in a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O.
3. **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on C1 of a hexahydronaphthalene core, in the position of the ester oxygen in simvastatin. The core has two ring C=C bonds, a methyl on each ring, and an ethyl chain leading to a six-membered lactone. The lactone carries an OTBS group, which I expanded to O–Si(CH3)2–C(CH3)3.

The OTBS substituent occupies the same neighbour position as the OH in simvastatin, so its chirality tag is unchanged.
