I read the structure as a maleamic acid–aminocaproyl–Val–Cit–PABC linker attached through a carbamate to the N-methyl nitrogen of MMAE (monomethyl auristatin E).

- **Maleamic acid end:** HOOC–CH=CH–C(=O)–NH with the double bond drawn cis (Z).
- **Spacer:** five CH2 groups followed by a C=O (a 6-aminohexanoyl unit).
- **Dipeptide:** Val–Cit, with the citrulline side chain ending in a urea, –(CH2)3–NH–C(=O)NH2.
- **Self-immolative spacer:** a para-aminobenzyl carbamate (PABC), written as O–CH2–C6H4–NH–.
- **MMAE part:** N-Me-Val, Val, dolaisoleucine, dolaproine and the norephedrine-type amino alcohol.

I did not derive the stereocentres atom by atom from the wedges. I assigned L-configuration to the Val and Cit residues and took the MMAE stereochemistry from the literature SMILES. I then rewrote the chain from the MMAE end and re-checked the stereo tags of the Cit and the two Val residues after the reordering.
