I read the drawing as a 4-substituted benzoic acid joined through an ethylene linker to the nitrogen of a pyrrolidin-2-one.

- **Benzoic acid end:** a para-substituted benzene ring carries CO2H, and a CH2CH2 chain runs from the ring to the lactam nitrogen.
- **Lactam ring:** the five-membered ring has N, a C=O carbon, two CH2 carbons, and a stereocentre carbon next to N.
- **Side chain on the stereocentre:** a trans (E) CH=CH leads to a CH(OH) carbon, which is bonded to a quaternary cyclobutane carbon. That cyclobutane carbon also carries a 4-chlorophenyl ring.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only wedge is the bond from the lactam C5 to the vinyl group. With H pointing away, the priorities are N, then the vinyl carbon, then the ring CH2. Going N (up), vinyl (lower right), CH2 (left) is clockwise, so the centre is R. In the SMILES below this corresponds to `[C@@H]`. The CH=CH is drawn trans, so it is written `/C=C/`. The carbinol carbon has plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

SMILES: OC(=O)c1ccc(CCN2C(=O)CC[C@@H]2/C=C/C(O)C2(CCC2)c2ccc(Cl)cc2)cc1
