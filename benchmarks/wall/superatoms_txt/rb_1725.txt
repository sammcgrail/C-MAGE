The structure is a thalidomide-based PROTAC. A lovastatin-like ligand is joined to the thalidomide unit through a carbamate and a piperidine linker. I read it in these pieces:

1. **E3 ligand:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) group on the nitrogen of a phthalimide, with an ether oxygen at the 4-position of the benzene ring.
2. **Linker:** that ether oxygen is followed by CH2–C(=O)–N of a piperidine. The piperidine 4-position carries an O–CH2CH2CH2–NH group, which forms a carbamate N–C(=O)–O.
3. **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on the C1 carbon of a hexahydronaphthalene. This ring has a C=C at the ring fusion, a second C=C at the top right, and two methyl groups. The 1-position also carries the CH2CH2 chain to a six-membered lactone with a hydroxyl group.
4. **Stereochemistry:** this part matches lovastatin, with the 2-methylbutanoate ester replaced by the carbamate. I took the stereocentres from the lovastatin isomeric SMILES, keeping the same neighbour order at the carbamate-bearing carbon. The glutarimide carbon is drawn with a plain bond, so it has no stereo label.
