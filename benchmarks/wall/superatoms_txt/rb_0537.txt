I read the molecule as a macrocycle fused to a benzimidazole ester, and built the SMILES in four parts.

**Macrocycle**
- The left benzene ring carries Cl and F. It is linked through a CH2CH2–O–CH2 bridge to the central benzene ring.
- The same left ring is also linked through a CH2–O bridge to the 2-position of a pyridine.
- The 6-position of the pyridine is bonded directly to the central benzene ring.
- The central benzene ring carries one F, one CH2 that connects to the benzimidazole, and the pyridyl and ether-bridge attachments. Its six ring positions, in order, are: ether CH2, CH, pyridyl, C–F, CH, CH2-benzimidazole.

**Benzimidazole**
- C2 bears the CH2 that connects to the central ring.
- N1 carries a CH2 group attached to an oxetan-2-yl ring.
- Ring carbon 4 (next to the N3 side) carries OCH2CH2OMe.
- Ring carbon 6 carries CO2Me.

**Stereochemistry**
- The only drawn stereocentre is the oxetane C2, which has a hashed bond to the CH2N group.
- The CIP priorities are O > CH2N > ring CH2 > H.
- With the hashed CH2N pointing back and H implicitly toward the viewer, the O → CH2N → CH2 sequence looks clockwise from the front. Because H points toward the viewer, the true configuration is S.
- In the SMILES written as `n1C[C@@H]1CCO1`, `@@` encodes S for this neighbour order.
