I read the structure in four parts and joined them.

- **Left core:** a 5-methyl-4-oxo-pyrrolo[3,2-c]pyridine with an N-methyl pyridone. The pyrrole NH is drawn, and C2 carries the carboxamide.
- **Aryl group at C7:** a 2,2,4-trimethyl-3-oxo-1,4-benzoxazine attached through the ring carbon next to the ring oxygen. It also carries a methylsulfonyl group on the benzene ring.
- **Linker:** an amide NH followed by an unbranched alkyl chain. I counted six CH2 groups, though the zigzag was hard to count exactly, so this is my best reading.
- **Right end:** a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione) substituted at the 4-position by the chain. Its nitrogen is bonded to the 3-position of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. That carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.
