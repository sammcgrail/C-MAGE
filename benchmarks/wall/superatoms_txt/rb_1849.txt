This is a PROTAC-type molecule: a BET bromodomain-binding pyrrolopyridone, a butyl linker, and a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**Pyrrolopyridone core.** The six-membered ring is N(Me)–C(=O)–C3a=C... fused to a pyrrole. Atom by atom:
- N5 carries the methyl, C4 is the C=O, and C3a is fused.
- C3 is the pyrrole CH, C2 bears the carboxamide, and N1 is the pyrrole NH.
- C7a is the other fusion carbon, C7 bears the aryl group, and C6 is the CH next to N5.

SMILES for the core: CN1C=C(Ar)c2[nH]c(C(=O)N...)cc2C1=O

**Aryl group.** The phenyl ring is attached to C7. Its ortho position carries O–Ar', where Ar' is 4-fluoro-2,6-dimethylphenyl. Its para position relative to the oxygen carries C(C)(C)O.

Ring atoms: c1 (attached to C7), c2 (bearing the O-aryl), then c3 and c4 as CH, c5 bearing C(C)(C)O, and c6 as CH. In the drawing, the ring carbon attached to C7 is adjacent to the carbon bearing O, and the C(CH3)2OH carbon sits meta to C7 and para to the O-bearing carbon.

Aryl SMILES: c1(C7)c(Oc2c(C)cc(F)cc2C)ccc(C(C)(C)O)c1

**Linker.** From the amide NH: NCCCCN, four CH2 groups, then NH–C(=O)–CH2–O–Ar.

**Thalidomide part.** The aryl ether sits at position 4 of the phthalimide. The imide N is attached to the glutarimide ring, C1CCC(=O)NC1=O.
