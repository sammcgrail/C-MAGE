This structure is papaverine: an isoquinoline with a 3,4-dimethoxybenzyl group at C1 and methoxy groups at C6 and C7.

**Isoquinoline core.** C1 is bonded to N and to the CH2 linker. N is double-bonded to C1 and single-bonded to C3. C3=C4 is a double bond, and C4 joins the fused benzene ring at C4a. The other fusion atom, C8a, is bonded to C1.

**Benzo ring.** Going round from C8a, the ring is C8 (CH), C7 (OMe), C6 (OMe), C5 (CH), C4a. In the drawing, the OMe at the top is on the carbon next to C8, and the OMe at the right is on the next carbon. This gives 6,7-dimethoxy.

**Benzyl ring.** The ring carbon attached to CH2 sits between a CH and another CH. Its neighbours are one CH that leads to C(OMe), then a second C(OMe), and the ring is completed by two more CH atoms. This gives 3,4-dimethoxy.
