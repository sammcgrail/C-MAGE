I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type conjugate with three parts joined by a succinyl linker. The stereochemistry is assigned from the standard VHL ligand configuration, not from my own wedge-by-wedge reading of the drawing.

**VHL ligand end**
- A 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group sits on a para-substituted phenyl ring.
- The phenyl carries a CH(CH3)–NH–C(=O) group, written as the (S)-1-arylethylamide.
- The amide is attached to a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine, taken as (2S,4R)-hydroxyproline, whose ring nitrogen is acylated.
- That acyl group is a tert-leucine, taken as (S), with a C(CH3)3 side chain and an NH–C(=O) on its α-nitrogen.

**Linker**
- The tert-leucine NH carries a –C(=O)CH2CH2C(=O)– succinyl group.
- The far end of the succinyl group is an amide to one nitrogen of a piperazine.

**Kinase-binding end**
- The other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to C2 of a 1,3,4-thiadiazole.
- Thiadiazole C5 is bonded to C5 of a pyridine.
- That pyridine has an isopropylamino group at C4.
- Its C2 carries a pyrrolo[1,2-b]pyridazine, attached at C7 of the bicycle.
- The bicycle has a nitrile at C3.
