The drawing is N‑Cbz‑proline: a pyrrolidine ring with the nitrogen carrying a Cbz group and the C2 carbon carrying a CO2H group.

- **Ring:** five vertices, namely N, the stereocentre C2, and three CH2 carbons.
- **N substituent:** Cbz is benzyloxycarbonyl, so N–C(=O)OCH2–C6H5.
- **C2 substituent:** CO2H on a hashed wedge, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- **Configuration:** the priorities are N > CO2H > ring C3 > H. Viewed from the front, N (down) → CO2H (upper left) → C3 (upper right) runs clockwise. Because the H points toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This is the L‑proline configuration.
