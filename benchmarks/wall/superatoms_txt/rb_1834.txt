2. **Stereocentre:** the only wedge is at the ring-fusion CH, drawn with the wedge toward the CH2–NH carbon. Working through the neighbour order gives `[C@H]` for the SMILES as written.
3. **Linker:** the amide carbonyl is attached to C4 of a 2-methylpiperidine. Its nitrogen is joined by a CH2 to C4 of a second piperidine, whose nitrogen is bonded to the isoindolinone aromatic ring.
4. **Right end:** the isoindolinone ring (CH2 on one side, C=O on the other) has its nitrogen attached to the 3-position of a piperidine-2,6-dione. That position is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label, and neither does the methyl-bearing carbon.
