The drawing shows a quaternary carbon with a methyl group on the left and two wavy bonds, one going up and one going down. Each wavy bond is an attachment point, so the quaternary carbon carries two * atoms. A CH2 leads from this carbon to a carbonyl carbon (C=O), which is bonded to N(CH3)2.

The quaternary carbon therefore has four substituents: the methyl, the two attachment points, and the CH2.
