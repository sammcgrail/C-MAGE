I read the structure as a head-to-side-chain cyclic peptide: Ac-Nle-Glu-His-D-Phe(3-Cl)-Arg-Trp, closed by a 1,4-diaminobutane bridge.

**Connectivity**
- The N-terminus is acetylated (CH3C(=O)NH) on a norleucine, which carries an n-butyl side chain.
- The Glu side-chain carboxyl forms an amide with one NH of a –(CH2)4– linker (1,4-diaminobutane).
- The other NH of that linker forms an amide with the Trp C-terminal carbonyl, which closes the macrocycle.
- The Glu backbone carbonyl is bonded to the His NH, so the ring runs Glu → His → 3-Cl-Phe → Arg → Trp → linker → Glu side chain.
- The His imidazole attaches through the carbon adjacent to the ring N that has no H, so I wrote it as `c2c[nH]cn2`.
- The Phe ring has Cl at the meta position.
- Arg ends in a free guanidine (`NC(N)=N`).
- Trp has an indol-3-yl side chain.

**Stereochemistry** (assigned from the wedges and hashes at each α-carbon)
- Nle: S (L).
- Glu: S (L).
- His: S (L).
- 3-Cl-Phe: R (D), drawn with a wedge to the benzylic CH2.
- Arg: S (L).
- Trp: S (L).
