I read the structure as a cyclic peptide-like macrocycle and traced the ring residue by residue, starting from the cyclobutane carbonyl:

1. Cyclobutane carbonyl, then N-methyl cyclopentylglycine.
2. N-methyl aspartic acid dimethylamide, linked through its side-chain CH2–C(=O).
3. N-methyl homoserine O-methyl ether.
4. Isoleucine NH.
5. Sarcosine.
6. An N-methyl amino acid whose α-carbon sits in an 8-membered lactam (azocane, 5 CH2 groups).
7. An N-substituted α-carbon carrying a 4-fluorobenzyl group, followed by another sarcosine.
8. A homophenylalanine bearing a 3,5-difluoro-4-CF3-phenyl group.
9. A 4-ethoxyproline, closing the ring back through the 1-aminocyclobutane carboxamide.

Stereocentres were assigned from the wedges and hashes in the drawing. Each centre was assigned by hand from the wedge/hash geometry and the SMILES neighbour order, not by a toolkit, so the @/@@ marks are the least certain part.
