I read the molecule in four regions and wrote the SMILES from them.

1. **Left end:** a 2-hydroxyphenyl group sits on a pyridazine ring (N=N in the ring). The pyridazine is fused to a six-membered ring containing an NH, a CH2 and the stereocentre CH. That ring is in turn fused to a piperazine.
2. **Stereocentre:** the wedge points to the CH2 on the NH side. With the implicit H pointing away, the order N, CH2 (NH side), CH2 (acylated-N side) runs clockwise. That corresponds to `[C@H]` in the SMILES order I wrote.
3. **Centre:** the piperazine N is acylated by a bicyclo[2.2.2]octane bridgehead carbon. The opposite bridgehead carries an NH–CH2 link to the 4-position of a piperidine.
4. **Right end:** the piperidine N is bonded to the 5-position of an isoindolin-1-one. The isoindolinone N carries a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl), whose stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds and so has no stereo marker.

I counted the bicyclooctane as two bridgeheads joined by three two-carbon bridges. In the isoindolinone, the aromatic CH lies between the piperidine-bearing carbon and the CH2-bearing ring-fusion carbon.
