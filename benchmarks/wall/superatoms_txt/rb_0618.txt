I read the molecule as an MDM2-type spiro-oxindole degrader: a spiro-oxindole/pyrrolidine/cyclohexane core joined through a PEG-type linker to a lenalidomide-like glutarimide–isoindolinone.

**Fragments**
- **Oxindole:** 6-chloro-2-oxindole, with its C3 as the spiro centre.
- **Pyrrolidine:** the ring runs spiro-C3 of the oxindole, then CH(3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl), then CH(C(=O)NH-aryl), then NH, then the cyclohexane spiro carbon, which closes back to the oxindole C3. The cyclohexane spiro carbon is symmetric, so it carries no stereo label.
- **Linker:** the amide NH-aryl is a 4-aminobenzamide, whose C(=O)NH leads into CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–C(=O)NH–.
- **E3 ligand:** the terminal NH is on C4 of an isoindolin-1-one whose N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
