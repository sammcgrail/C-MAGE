I read the molecule as an MDM2-targeting PROTAC. It has a spiro-oxindole/pyrrolidine/cyclohexane core, a PEG-type linker and a thalidomide-type phthalimide end.

**Core**
- The oxindole is 6-chloro, with its C3 spiro-fused to the pyrrolidine.
- The pyrrolidine carries a 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl group on the carbon next to the oxindole spiro carbon.
- The pyrrolidine carbon next to the NH bears a carboxamide.
- The carbon on the other side of the NH is spiro-fused to a cyclohexane.

**Linker and ligand**
- The carboxamide nitrogen is bonded to a para-substituted benzene.
- The other para position carries C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2CH2–.
- The linker ends on the 4-position of an N-(2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl)phthalimide. The piperidinedione stereocentre is drawn plainly, so it has no stereo label.

**Stereochemistry**
Three stereocentres are shown with wedges or hashes. I assigned them from the drawn geometry (CIP configurations in parentheses):
- the CH bearing the carboxamide, with a hashed bond (R);
- the CH bearing the aryl group, with a wedge (S);
- the oxindole spiro carbon, with a hash to the aromatic ring and a wedge to the C=O (R).

The cyclohexane spiro carbon is not a stereocentre because the ring is symmetric.
