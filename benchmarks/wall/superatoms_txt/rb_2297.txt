I read the molecule as a cyclooctyne–Gly–Gly–Phe–Gly linker joined through an –NH–CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH– spacer to an exatecan-type camptothecin core.

**Linker, left to right**
- A cyclooctyne ring: 8 carbons with one C≡C, and the sp3 carbon next to the alkyne carries the O.
- The O is followed by –CH2–C(=O)–, then Gly, Gly, Phe and Gly (each –NH–CH2–C(=O)– except Phe, which is –NH–CH(CH2Ph)–C(=O)–).
- The chain ends with –NH–CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH–, and that last NH is bonded to the stereocentre of the core.

**Core**
- It is the camptothecin-type hexacyclic system: a lactone ring with a tertiary alcohol and an ethyl group, a pyridone, a pyrroline, a quinoline-type ring, a saturated six-membered ring bearing the NH, and an aromatic ring bearing F and CH3.

**Stereochemistry**
- The quaternary carbon bearing OH and ethyl is drawn with a bold wedge to OH and a hash to ethyl. I assigned it S, the natural camptothecin configuration, and wrote the SMILES to give S.
- The carbon bearing the NH is drawn with a hashed bond to N. I assigned it S and wrote `[C@H]` accordingly.
- The Phe α-carbon is drawn with a hashed bond to NH. I assigned it S (L-Phe) and wrote `[C@H]` for the way the atoms are ordered in this SMILES.
- The cyclooctyne carbon bearing O is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
