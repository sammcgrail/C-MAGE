I read the molecule as four parts joined in a chain:

- **Left:** an indazole attached through its C6 position to a carbonyl. The ring NH sits at N1 and the N=CH at N2/C3.
- **Middle:** that carbonyl acylates N2 of a 1,2,3,4-tetrahydroisoquinoline. The benzo ring carries Cl at C5 and C7, and a carboxamide at C6.
- **Amide link:** the carboxamide NH is bonded to a stereocentre carrying CO2H and a CH2 group.
- **Right:** the CH2 leads to an NH, then to a C(=N–C≡N) carbon bearing a pyrrolidine nitrogen (an N-cyanoguanidine).

For stereochemistry, the wedge on the stereocentre points to the CH2 group. The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2N > H. With H pointing away, the sequence N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise in the drawing, so the centre is S. The SMILES `N[C@@H](CN...)C(=O)O` was checked to give S as well.
