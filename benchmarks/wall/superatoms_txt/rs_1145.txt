I read the molecule as a pyridine carrying three substituents, joined through an amide to a cyclooctene ring.

**Pyridine ring.** Going around from the ring nitrogen:
- C2 carries the carboxamide, C(=O)NH.
- C3 carries the acetate, OC(=O)CH3.
- C4 carries the methoxy group, OCH3.
- C5 and C6 are plain CH.

**Cyclooctene ring.** Starting at the CH bonded to the amide nitrogen and counting the eight ring atoms:
1. CH(N)
2. CH2
3. C(CH3)2
4. CH2
5. CH=
6. =CH
7. C(CH3)2
8. CH2, which closes back to atom 1

**Stereochemistry.** The ring CH–N carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo label. The ring double bond sits in an eight-membered ring and is drawn cis, so I wrote it as `/C=C\`.
