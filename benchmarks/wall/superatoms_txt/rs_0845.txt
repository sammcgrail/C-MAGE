I read the molecule as an antimycin-type nine-membered dilactone carrying a substituted picolinamide.

**Picolinamide part.** The pyridine carries a carboxamide at C2, a cyclobutanecarbonyloxy group at C3 and a methoxy group at C4. The amide nitrogen is attached to the macrocycle.

**Nine-membered ring.** Going around the ring from the nitrogen-bearing carbon:
- CH(NH–), drawn with a hashed N bond
- C(=O)
- ring O
- CH(CH3), methyl on a wedge
- CH(O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2), isobutyrate on a hash
- CH(CH2Ph), benzyl on a wedge
- C(=O)
- ring O
- CH2, which closes back to the nitrogen-bearing carbon
