- C2 carries the amide, C(=O)NH-.
- C3 carries OH.

The amide nitrogen is bonded to a cyclohexyl ring. Numbering from the carbon attached to N as C1:
- C1 is the CH bearing the NH.
- C2 is the CH2 that leads to the quaternary carbon.
- C3 is the quaternary carbon bearing a methyl and an ethyl.
- C4 is a CH2.
- C5 is the quaternary carbon bearing two methyls.
- C6 is a CH2 that returns to C1.

This gives a 3-ethyl-3,5,5-trimethylcyclohexyl group. No wedges or hashes are drawn, so no stereochemistry is included.
