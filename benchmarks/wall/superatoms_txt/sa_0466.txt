The drawing shows Fmoc-glycine: HO2C–CH2–NH–Fmoc.

Fmoc is the 9-fluorenylmethoxycarbonyl group, C(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12. It attaches to nitrogen through the carbonyl carbon, so the nitrogen carries a carbamate.

Written out, the molecule is OC(=O)CNC(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12.

Atom check: the glycine part is the carboxylic acid carbon plus the CH2, the nitrogen carries one H, and the carbamate carbonyl and oxygen link to the CH2 of the fluorenyl group. The fluorene core is a five-membered ring joined to two benzene rings. No stereochemistry is drawn.

SMILES: OC(=O)CNC(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12
