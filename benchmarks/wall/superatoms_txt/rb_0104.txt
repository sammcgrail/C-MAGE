I read the structure as a BTK-degrader PROTAC, with three regions: a tricyclic pyrazolo-benzodiazepine, a piperidine–piperazine linker, and a fluoro-thalidomide group.

**Tricyclic core (left).** A pyrazole carries a carboxamide (C(N)=O) on one carbon and a 4-phenoxyphenyl group on the adjacent carbon. The pyrazole ring is C3(C(N)=O)=C(Ar)–N=N... more precisely, the ring atoms are C(CONH2), C(Ar), N, N and the fused carbon. The ring N bonds to a CH2CH2 unit that leads to the benzene ring. The fused carbon bonds to an NH, and the NH bonds to the benzene ring, closing the seven-membered ring.

Pyrazole SMILES: c1(C(N)=O)c(Ar)nn2c1Nc1cc(C(=O)N...)ccc1CC2

Checking the seven-membered ring: pyrazole C(fused) – NH – benzene C – benzene C (ortho) – CH2 – CH2 – N(pyrazole). That is 7 atoms: C, N, C, C, CH2, CH2, N, which is correct.

**Benzene ring.** The NH-bearing carbon sits next to a CH carbon, which is next to the carbon bearing the carbonyl. The carbonyl-bearing carbon is meta to the NH carbon. The ring then continues through two CH carbons to the carbon attached to the CH2.

**Linker.** The carbonyl is an amide to a piperidine nitrogen. The piperidine C4 carries a CH2 that connects to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is attached to the aryl ring of the isoindoline-1,3-dione.

**Thalidomide part.** The isoindoline aromatic ring carries F and the piperazine. The imide nitrogen bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
