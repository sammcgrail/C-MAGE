This is enzalutamide. I read it as follows.

- **Left aryl ring:** it carries a CF3 group and a nitrile (NC) on adjacent carbons, and it attaches to the imidazolidine N at the position meta to CF3 and para to the CN-bearing carbon's neighbour. The ring atoms in order are C1 (attached to N), C2 (CH, upper), C3 (C-CF3), C4 (C-CN), C5 (CH), C6 (CH).
- **Central ring:** a thiohydantoin. The left N is bonded to the C=O carbon and to the C=S carbon. The right N is bonded to the C(CH3)2 carbon and to the C=S carbon. The C(CH3)2 carbon is also bonded to the C=O carbon.
- **Right aryl ring:** it is attached to the right N. Going around the ring, the carbon bearing N is followed by a CH, then the carbon bearing F, then the carbon bearing C(=O)NHMe, then two CH carbons. The F carbon and the amide carbon are adjacent.

SMILES for the left ring, written from the N attachment: c1(N...)cc(C(F)(F)F)c(C#N)cc1.

Full SMILES: CNC(=O)c1ccc(cc1F)N1C(=S)N(C(=O)C1(C)C)c1ccc(C#N)c(c1)C(F)(F)F

Checking the right ring: c1 (amide-bearing), c, c, c(N) (para to the amide carbon), c, c1F. This gives the amide carbon adjacent to the F carbon, and the F carbon adjacent to a CH that is adjacent to the N-bearing carbon. That matches the drawing.
