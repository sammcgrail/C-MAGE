I read the molecule as a macrocycle and wrote it out by tracing the ring from the Leu isobutyl group around to the N-methyl on the Leu nitrogen.

**Ring fragments, in order**
- **Leu:** N-methyl-leucine, with the isobutyl group on a wedged bond.
- **Linker 1:** its C=O joins N(CH3)–CH2–CH=CH–C(=O)–O. The wavy bond means the alkene geometry is unspecified, so I wrote it without / or \.
- **Linker 2:** the ester O–CH2–C(CH3)2–C(=O)–C(=O) leads into the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Pipecolic acid:** the ring carbon next to that nitrogen carries the ester C(=O)–O.
- **Benzylic carbon:** that ester O attaches to a CH that bears a 3,4-dimethoxyphenethyl chain and a meta-substituted phenylene.
- **Succinamide:** the phenylene NH continues through C(=O)CH2CH2C(=O)NH.
- **Phenylglycine:** this NH joins CH(Ph)–C(=O)NH, with no stereo drawn.
- **Tyr:** the next unit is Tyr, CH(CH2-C6H4-OH)–C(=O).
- **Pro:** the Tyr carbonyl joins the proline nitrogen, and the proline C(=O) closes the ring onto the N-methyl Leu nitrogen.
