This structure is a cyclosporin A–dasatinib conjugate. I did not derive the stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes. I took the cyclosporin A stereocentres from its known SMILES, and I did not check each one against the drawing.

**Macrocycle (cyclosporin A core).** The undecapeptide ring is cyclo[MeBmt-Abu-Sar-MeLeu-Val-MeLeu-Ala-D-Ala-MeLeu-MeLeu-MeVal], with the N-methylated residues drawn as in the image.

**Modified MeBmt side chain.** The usual butenyl group is replaced. From the hydroxylated carbon the chain runs CH(CH3)–CH2–CH2–CH2–C(=O), and that carbonyl is an amide to a piperazine nitrogen.

**Dasatinib-type part.**
- The piperazine's other nitrogen is bonded to C6 of a 2-methylpyrimidine.
- The pyrimidine C4 carries an NH that links to C2 of a thiazole.
- The thiazole C5 carries a C(=O)NH group attached to a 2-chloro-6-methylphenyl ring. I ignored the stray dot at the right edge of the image.
