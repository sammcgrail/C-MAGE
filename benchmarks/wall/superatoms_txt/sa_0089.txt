This drawing matches heroin (diacetylmorphine), and I took the stereochemistry from the known natural configuration rather than deriving each centre from the wedges. I did not check each wedge and hash against the SMILES atom by atom.

- **Aromatic ring:** a benzene ring carries an OAc group at the top left.
- **Furan ring:** the ring oxygen bridges the aromatic ring to the CH carbon bearing a hashed H.
- **Cyclohexene ring:** it has one C=C double bond on the right, and the carbon bearing the wedged OAc is adjacent to the CH–O carbon.
- **Ethylene bridge:** the hashed bond from the quaternary carbon leads to a CH2 that sits behind the "H" label. That CH2 connects to a second CH2, then to N, which carries an N-methyl group.
- **Piperidine ring:** it is closed through the N-bearing CH, which has a wedged H and is linked to the benzylic CH2.

SMILES: CC(=O)O[C@H]1C=C[C@H]2[C@H]3CC4=C5[C@]2([C@H]1OC5=C(C=C4)OC(=O)C)CCN3C
