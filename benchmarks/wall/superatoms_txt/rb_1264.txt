This is a PROTAC-type molecule. I read it in four parts and joined them into one SMILES.

- **Warhead:** a quinazoline with a 4-(4-acryloylpiperazin-1-yl) group, 6-chloro, 8-fluoro and a 7-(2-fluoro-6-hydroxyphenyl) group.
- **Linker:** an NH at C2 of the quinazoline carries CH2CH2C(=O)N(CH3). The nitrogen is followed by five repeats of OCH2CH2 (five ether oxygens), and the last oxygen is bonded to the aromatic ring of the isoindolinone.
- **Ligase ligand:** an isoindolin-1-one with the linker oxygen at the 5-position and a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the ring nitrogen.
- **Stereochemistry:** the glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label. The bold bonds on the hydroxyfluorophenyl ring look like an axial (atropisomer) depiction, which I could not express reliably, so I left stereo out entirely.

I counted the linker as N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–aryl, which gives the five oxygens labelled in the drawing.
