The drawing shows a 1,2,3,4-tetrahydroquinoline whose ring nitrogen carries an acyl group, COCH2-aryl.

**Tetrahydroquinoline part.** N1 is bonded to C8a of the benzene ring and to the saturated CH2CH2CH2 chain. The chain ends at C4, which is bonded to C4a.
- Going around the benzene ring from C8a: C8 is the CH at the top, next to C8a.
- C7 carries OCH3 (upper left) and C6 carries OH (lower left).
- C5 is the CH, and it joins C4a.

**Acyl part.** The N–C(=O)–CH2 group is attached to a phenyl ring. Numbering that ring from the CH2 attachment as C1:
- C1 is attached to the CH2.
- Going up from C1 gives the CH at C2, then C3 bearing OH (top).
- C4 bears OCH3 (right).
- C5 and C6 are CH.

So the aryl group is 3-hydroxy-4-methoxyphenyl.

**SMILES.** Writing the tetrahydroquinoline as N1CCCc2cc(O)c(OC)cc21:
- N1 is followed by CCC for the chain.
- c2 is C4a, then c is C5, c(O) is C6, c(OC) is C7, c is C8, and c21 is C8a, closing both rings.

The full SMILES is O=C(Cc1ccc(OC)c(O)c1)N1CCCc2cc(O)c(OC)cc21.
