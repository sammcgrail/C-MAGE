I read the molecule as a spirooxindole–pyrrolidine carboxamide (MDM2-inhibitor type) joined through a benzamide to a trans-1,4-cyclohexane. That cyclohexane carries a cis-alkene leading to a lenalidomide-type isoindolinone–glutarimide.

**Fragments, region by region**
- **Glutarimide–isoindolinone:** the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group sits on the isoindolinone nitrogen. The isoindolinone ring has a C=O and a CH2, and the vinyl group is on the aromatic carbon at the 4-position. The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
- **Alkene:** the cyclohexyl and the aryl lie on the same side of the C=C in the drawing, so it is cis (Z).
- **Cyclohexane:** one substituent is a wedge (NH) and the other a hash (vinyl) on a 1,4-pattern, which means trans.
- **Linker:** NH–C(=O)–C6H4 (para) –NH–C(=O) connects to the pyrrolidine.
- **Pyrrolidine ring:** C2 carries the carboxamide (hashed), N1 is NH, C5 is spiro to a cyclohexane, C4 is spiro to the oxindole, and C3 carries the 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl (wedge).
- **Oxindole:** the spiro carbon is bonded to the C=O and to the aromatic carbon, with a 6-chloro substituent. Its two spiro bonds are drawn as one hash and one wedge.
