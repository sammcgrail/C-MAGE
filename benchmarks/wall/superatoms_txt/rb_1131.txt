I read the molecule as a quinazoline-based PROTAC: a quinazoline warhead joined through a PEG-type linker to a VHL-type ligand.

**Warhead (left side)**
- The quinazoline carries a 4-(4-propionylpiperazin-1-yl) group.
- C6 bears Cl, C8 bears F, and C7 bears a 3-hydroxynaphth-1-yl group.
- C2 bears an NH.

**Linker**
- From the quinazoline NH the chain runs NH–CH2CH2–C(=O)–N(CH3)–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH.

**VHL ligand (right side)**
- The linker ends on an amide NH attached to a tert-leucine unit.
- That unit is acylated onto the nitrogen of 4-hydroxyproline.
- The proline carboxamide is bonded to NH–CH(CH3)–C6H4–(4-methylthiazol-5-yl).

**Stereochemistry**
- I took the wedges and hashes as the standard VHL ligand configuration and checked the SMILES atom by atom.
- The hydroxyproline comes out (2S,4R), the tert-leucine carbon S, and the methylbenzylic carbon S.
