I read the drawing in four parts and joined them into one SMILES.

- **Pyrazine end:** the pyrazine carries a primary carboxamide, a 3-methylisothiazol-5-ylamino group, and a piperidine nitrogen attached at the ring position opposite the carboxamide.
- **Piperidine:** the piperidine C3 bears an NH–C(=O) urea linkage. This is the only wedge in the drawing, so it is the only stereocentre I assigned. With the wedge toward the viewer and the hydrogen behind, the three substituents run clockwise as CH2 (towards N1), then ring C4, then NH. That gives `[C@H]` when the SMILES is written as `N1CCC[C@H](C1)N...`.
- **Linker:** the urea carbonyl is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen carries a CH2 that leads to C3 of an azetidine.
- **Thalidomide end:** the azetidine nitrogen is bonded to C5 of the isoindoline-1,3-dione. The isoindoline-dione nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, and that carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
