The drawing shows a pyridine ring with a methoxy group at C4, a hydroxyl at C3, and a carboxamide at C2, next to the ring nitrogen.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a tetrahydrothiopyran-4-one ring. Going around that ring:
- C3 carries the NH.
- C4 is the ketone carbon.
- C5 carries the phenyl group and is drawn with a wavy bond, so its stereochemistry is undefined.
- C6 is a CH2 bonded to S.
- S is S1.
- C2 is a CH2 bonded to C3.

C3, the carbon bearing the NH, is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo either.
