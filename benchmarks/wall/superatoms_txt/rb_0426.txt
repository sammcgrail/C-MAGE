I read the molecule as a macrocycle and wrote it out in four parts.

**Macrocycle backbone.** It runs in this order:
- a secondary amine NH on the left;
- the first ornithine α-carbon;
- C(=O)–NH;
- the second ornithine α-carbon;
- C(=O)–N(CH3);
- the tryptophan α-carbon;
- C(=O)–NH–CH2;
- a benzene ring;
- S;
- a pyridine ring;
- CH2, which closes back to the amine NH.

**Side chains.**
- Each ornithine carries a CH2CH2CH2–NH–Boc side chain.
- The tryptophan CH2 is attached to indol-3-yl, whose ring nitrogen carries a Boc group.

**Aromatic rings.**
- The pyridine is attached to the S at C2 and to the CH2–N at C3.
- On the benzene, the S-bearing carbon is next to the carbon bearing the CH2–NH group, and that carbon is next to the carbon bearing Br.

**Stereochemistry.** All three α-carbons have wedge bonds. Assigning CIP priorities (N > C=O > side-chain CH2 > H) with H pointing away gives S at each centre, consistent with L-amino acids. In the SMILES this corresponds to `@` for the first ornithine (written with its ring-closure N) and `@@` for the second ornithine and the tryptophan.
