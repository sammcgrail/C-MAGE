I read the drawing as a 2-methoxy-tetrahydropyran carrying a hept-6-enoate ester, a pent-4-enyl chain and an N-PMB thiazolidin-2-one. I assigned the stereocentres by hand from the wedge and hash bonds, so the @/@@ labels are the least certain part.

**Ring and substituents**
- **Pyran ring:** six-membered, with the ring O at the bottom left. Going around from the O:
  - C2 is the anomeric carbon. It carries OMe on a bold wedge and the thiazolidinone on a plain bond.
  - C3 is CH2.
  - C4 is CH and carries the ester O on a bold wedge.
  - C5 is CH2.
  - C6 is CH and carries the pentenyl chain on a hashed bond.
- **Ester:** C(=O) followed by four CH2 groups and then CH=CH2, which is hept-6-enoate. The chain length is my best count of the vertices in the drawing.
- **Alkyl chain on C6:** CH2CH2CH2CH=CH2, a pent-4-enyl group.
- **Thiazolidinone:** the ring runs N(PMB)–C4'(H)–CH2–S–C(=O), closing back to N.
  - C4' joins the pyran C2 and has its H on a bold wedge.
  - PMB is the 4-methoxybenzyl group, CH2-C6H4-OMe.

**Stereo assignments:** working through each centre from the 2D positions of its neighbours and the wedge or hash bond gave @@ at pyran C6, C4 and C2, and at thiazolidinone C4'.
