The drawing shows an N-Cbz-protected glutamic acid, which I read as follows.

- **Chain:** HO2C–CH2–CH2–CH(NH–Cbz)–CO2H. This is a five-carbon chain with a carboxylic acid at each end, i.e. a 2-aminopentanedioic acid (glutamic acid) skeleton.
- **Cbz group:** The label on the nitrogen expands to the benzyloxycarbonyl group, C(=O)OCH2Ph.
- **Stereocentre:** The alpha carbon carries a hashed bond to its CO2H, so CO2H points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
  - The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H.
  - Going N (upper right) → CO2H (down) → CH2 (upper left) is clockwise on the page.
  - With H toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is counterclockwise, which is S (the L-amino acid).
- **SMILES chirality:** I checked that `[C@H]` in the SMILES below, written with the neighbours in the order CH2, H, N, CO2H, matches L-glutamic acid, `N[C@@H](CCC(=O)O)C(=O)O`.

SMILES: OC(=O)CC[C@H](NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1)C(=O)O
