I read the molecule as an MDM2-type spiro-oxindole/pyrrolidine ligand joined through a PEG-like linker to a lenalidomide-type isoindolinone, and wrote it in SMILES from that reading.

- The chain therefore has 3 carbons after the second oxygen.
- It attaches at the isoindolinone carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the CH2.
- The isoindolinone nitrogen carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
