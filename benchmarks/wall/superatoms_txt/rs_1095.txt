- The ring runs N(PMB)–C(=O)–S–CH2–CH, with the carbonyl carbon at the bottom, between N and S.
- The ring nitrogen carries a PMB group, which is 4-methoxybenzyl.

For the stereochemistry, the hashed bond puts the amide carbon behind the plane, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CH2S > C(=O)N > H. Reading N → CH2S → C(=O) in the drawing runs counterclockwise. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving clockwise, so the centre is R.
