**Second benzene ring**

**Benzimidazole**

- N1 carries a CH2 attached to an oxetan-2-yl group.

**Stereocentre**

- With the CH2N group pointing away from the viewer, I get the S configuration. In the SMILES, `[C@@H]` written after the CH2 gives S for this neighbour order.
