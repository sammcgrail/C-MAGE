This is clotrimazole, drawn with the two phenyl groups abbreviated as "Ph".

- **Imidazole:** the ring is attached through its N1 nitrogen to the central carbon. Going round the ring, N1 connects to C2, then N3 (the double-bonded N), then C4 and C5. The drawing shows C=N between the top carbon and the left N, and C=C between the two lower carbons, which fits an aromatic imidazole.
- **Central carbon:** it carries the imidazole N, two Ph groups, and the 2-chlorophenyl ring.
- **Chlorophenyl ring:** Cl sits on the ring carbon next to the carbon attached to the central carbon, so it is ortho-substituted.
