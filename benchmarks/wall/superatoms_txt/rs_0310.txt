I read the molecule as a 4-substituted benzoic acid joined through a CH2CH2 linker to the nitrogen of a 2-pyrrolidinone (lactam) ring.

- **Lactam ring:** a five-membered ring with C=O next to N. The carbon on the other side of N carries the side chain.
- **Aryl group:** N–CH2CH2–C6H4–CO2H, with the two substituents para on the benzene ring.
- **Side chain on the ring carbon:** a trans CH=CH (drawn as E), then CH2, then a quaternary carbon bearing OH, ethyl and n-butyl. I counted four chain carbons for the butyl group, but the drawing was slightly ambiguous there.
- **Stereochemistry:** the quaternary carbinol carbon has a hashed OH, so I assigned it as `[C@]` with the allyl CH2 written first. The ring carbon bearing the side chain is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
