I read the molecule as a 3-acylindole ketone with a single stereocentre, and assigned that centre from the hashed bond.

**Fragments read from the drawing**
- Left ring: a benzene bearing SO2Me, OMe and NH in a 1,3,5 pattern.
- Stereocentre: a CH with a hashed bond to the NH, a bond to a C=O, and a bond to a second aryl ring.
- Lower aryl ring: 4-chloro-2-methoxyphenyl, attached to the stereocentre at C1.
- Ketone: C=O linking the stereocentre to C3 of an indole.
- Indole: a free N–H, with OCF3 at C5.

**Stereochemistry**
- The NH is on the hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- Viewed from the H side, the order N → C(=O) → aryl runs clockwise.
- For the SMILES atom order N, H, aryl, C(=O), the permutation from (H; N, C=O, aryl) is two swaps, which is even. Clockwise therefore maps to `@@`.
