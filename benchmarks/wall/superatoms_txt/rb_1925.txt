I read the structure in four pieces and joined them in the SMILES below.

- **Indazole end:** a 1H-indazol-3-yl amide NH, with a 3,5-difluorobenzyl group on C5 of the indazole.
- **Central benzamide:** the carbonyl sits on the ring carbon next to the one bearing NH-(tetrahydropyran-4-yl). A piperazine nitrogen is attached para to the carbonyl.
- **Linker:** the other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to C3 of an azetidine. The azetidine nitrogen is bonded to C5 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione (phthalimide).
- **Glutarimide end:** the phthalimide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. This is the only drawn stereocentre, with a wedge from the ring carbon to the phthalimide nitrogen.

For the stereocentre, the CIP priorities are N > C(=O) > CH2 > H. With the wedge bringing N toward the viewer, H points away. Going N → C(=O) → CH2 then runs counterclockwise, which gives S. In the SMILES written as `N([C@H]4CCC(=O)NC4=O)`, the `@` symbol encodes S.

I could not tell from the image which end of the wedge is narrow. I assumed the narrow end is at the ring carbon, which is the usual convention and matches the (S)-glutarimide seen in lenalidomide-type compounds.
