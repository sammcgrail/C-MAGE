I read the structure in four parts and joined them into one macrocycle.

1. **Acid side chain.** HO–C(=O)–CH(CH3)–CH2 is attached to a benzene ring at the meta position. The methyl is drawn hashed, and working through the CIP priorities (COOH > CH2Ar > CH3) gives R. That corresponds to `[C@H]` in the SMILES order used below.

2. **Quaternary carbon.** It carries a methyl (on a wavy bond), the meta-substituted phenyl, the triazole carbon, and the chain. The wavy bond means no stereo is specified here.

3. **Chain to indole.** From the quaternary carbon the chain runs CH2CH2CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2–SO2–CH2CH2 to C4 of the indole.

4. **Triazole, fluorophenyl and indole.**
   - The triazole is a 1,2,4-triazole with N1-methyl. Its C3 is bonded to the quaternary carbon and its C5 to the fluorophenyl ring.
   - The fluorophenyl ring has F ortho to the triazole attachment. A CH2 bridge sits para to that F and meta to the triazole.
   - The CH2 bridge links the fluorophenyl ring to indole C5. The indole also carries F at C6 and has a free N–H.
