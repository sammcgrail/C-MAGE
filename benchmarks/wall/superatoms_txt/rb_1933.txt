Reading the structure region by region:

- **Indazole:** The 1H-indazole is attached at C3 to the amide NH. Its C5 carries a CH2 linked to a 3,5-difluorophenyl ring.
- **Central benzamide:** The carbonyl carbon is on ring carbon 1. Ring carbon 2 carries NH-(tetrahydropyran-4-yl). Ring carbon 4 carries the piperazine nitrogen, which is para to the carbonyl.
- **Piperazine and linker:** The other piperazine nitrogen bears CH2CH2, which is attached to the isoindoline-1,3-dione.
- **Isoindoline-dione:** The ethylene attaches at C5 of the isoindoline ring. The imide nitrogen is bonded to the 3-position of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. This stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo.
