I read the structure as a lipidated, disulfide-cyclised peptide and wrote it out residue by residue from the N-terminus:

- **N-terminus:** an acetyl group on a penicillamine (Pen) residue.
- **Macrocycle:** Pen, Asn, Thr, 7-methyl-Trp, Lys(N-acetyl), Pen. The two Pen sulfurs are joined by a disulfide bond (S–S).
- **Exocyclic chain:**
  - Tyr, with its phenol O alkylated as O–CH2CH2–NH2
  - 2-naphthylalanine
  - 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid
  - Glu
  - Asn
  - Phe
  - Sar (N-methylglycine)
- **C-terminus:** a Lys whose carboxyl is replaced by a methyl ketone. Its side-chain NH carries a myristoyl group. I counted 14 carbons in the zigzag chain, so C(=O) followed by 13 CH2/CH3 carbons.

I did not read the stereo bonds individually. I assigned L-configuration to every stereocentre except the two penicillamine centres, which I set by assumption (see below). The Thr side-chain centre is set to the L-Thr pattern. Neither Pen centre was read from the drawing; the first is set as L, and I wrote the second as L by the same assumption. The quaternary tetrahydropyran carbon is not a stereocentre.
