I read the structure as a VHL-ligand PROTAC: a hydroxyproline VHL binder joined through an isoxazole, a cyclohexyl ether, a piperidine and a pyrimidine to an aminopyridazine-phenol group.

- **VHL end:** The 4-methylthiazol-5-yl phenyl benzylamide of 4-hydroxyproline carries the drawn stereocentres at C2 and C4. The proline nitrogen is acylated by a CH(isopropyl) group that bears the isoxazole. That CH is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.
- **Isoxazole:** C5 carries the CH(isopropyl) group, and C3 carries the ether oxygen.
- **Cyclohexane:** It is 1,4-disubstituted with the O and the piperidine N both on wedge bonds. Two wedges on the same face make it cis, so I wrote it as cis.
- **Piperidine to pyrimidine:** The piperidine N is attached to the cyclohexane, and its 4-position is attached to pyrimidine C5.
- **Bicyclic amine:** Pyrimidine C2 is bonded to the bridging N8 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane. The N3 of that ring is bonded to the pyridazine. No stereo is drawn for the bridgeheads.
- **Pyridazine end:** The pyridazine carries an NH2 and a 2-hydroxyphenyl group.
