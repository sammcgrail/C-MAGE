This reads as a PROTAC-type molecule with three parts: a pomalidomide-type unit, a PEG-like linker, and a lovastatin-derived core.

1. **Pomalidomide end:** The isoindoline-1,3-dione is attached through its N to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring. The 3-position of that ring is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it without stereo. The aromatic ring carries an NH at the 4-position.
2. **Linker:** From that NH the chain runs –(CH2)3–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–(CH2)3–NH–. It ends in a carbamate, –NH–C(=O)–O–.
3. **Statin end:** The carbamate oxygen sits on C1 of the bicyclic core, where lovastatin normally carries its 2-methylbutanoate ester. The core has two methyl groups and two ring C=C bonds. At C8 the 2-(tetrahydropyranone) ethyl chain carries a 4-hydroxy-6-oxo lactone.
4. **Stereochemistry:** I took the stereocentres of the statin part from the known lovastatin configuration, since the drawing matches that skeleton. I did not check each wedge and hash individually against it. I then replaced the ester acyl group with the carbamate linker.
