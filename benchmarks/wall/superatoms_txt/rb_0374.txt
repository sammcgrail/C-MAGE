This is my reading of the structure, region by region.

- **Right-hand head:** a 4-oxo-5-methyl-thieno[3,2-c]pyridine. Position 7 carries a phenyl ring bearing two OMe groups and a CH2NMe2 group between them. Position 2 carries the carboxamide.
- **Amide nitrogen:** attached to C4 of a 3,3-difluoropiperidine. The bond to this carbon is hashed, so I assigned it stereochemistry. The substituents are NH (behind the plane), CF2 (upper left), CH2 (down) and an implicit H (towards the viewer). The CIP priorities are N > CF2 > CH2 > H. Viewed with H pointing away, N→CF2→CH2 runs clockwise, so the centre is R. This corresponds to `[C@@H]` in the SMILES as written.
- **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen is bonded to C4 of a second piperidine, whose nitrogen is bonded to the aromatic ring of an isoindolinone.
- **Left-hand end:** the isoindolinone nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. Its C3 is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereochemistry.
