**Right ring:** a benzene ring attached to the azo group at one carbon. Going around from there:
- The next carbon (lower, toward the left) carries OCH3, drawn as H3CO.
- The following carbon is CH.
- The next carbon carries N(Et)2, para to the azo attachment.
- The next carbon is CH.
- The top carbon carries OCH3.
- The last carbon is CH, which closes the ring.

The two OCH3 groups sit at positions 2 and 5 relative to the azo carbon, and the diethylamino group is at position 4. The ring is therefore 4-(diethylamino)-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl.
