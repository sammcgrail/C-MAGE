I read the structure as a macrocycle and assembled the SMILES in pieces.

- **Macrocycle core:** a piperazic acid ring (S at the alpha carbon) whose ring NH side carries an ester, and whose other nitrogen carries an amide C(=O).
- **Ester linker:** the ester oxygen connects through OCH2–C(CH3)2–CH2 to C3 of the indole.
- **Indole:** N-ethyl, with C2 bonded to the pyridine and C5 bonded to a chain of four CH2 groups. I counted four from the drawing, but the vertices are slightly ambiguous.
- **Chain end:** the four CH2 groups lead to the (S) CH, which carries the NH–C(=O)–(2-methylcyclopropyl) group and the C(=O) that acylates the piperazic nitrogen.

- **Cyclopropane:** both stereocentres are S. The carbonyl is drawn hashed on one carbon and the methyl wedged on the other, which gives a trans arrangement.
