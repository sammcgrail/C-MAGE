**Left to right**

- **Benzothiazole:** the phenyl ring is attached at C2. The ring carbon at position 6 carries an O.
- **Linker:** O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O), which then forms an amide to the NH of tert-leucine.

- **Amide substituent:** an (S)-CH(CH3) attached to a para-phenylene ring. That ring bears a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.
