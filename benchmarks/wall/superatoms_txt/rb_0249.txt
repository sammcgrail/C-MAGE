- **Left end:** a thalidomide-type unit, 2-(2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl)isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its 4-position carries a tertiary amine nitrogen.
- **Branches on that nitrogen:**
  - a CH2C(=O)NH– arm leading to a six-carbon chain and then a piperazine nitrogen;
  - a four-carbon chain ending in a secondary NH that is bonded to CH2 on a cyclohexane.
- **Cyclohexane:** it is 1,4-disubstituted, with the CH2NH carbon on a hashed bond and the indazole nitrogen on a wedge. These are opposite faces, so the ring is trans, and I wrote the stereo accordingly.

- **Right-hand side:** the piperazine's other nitrogen is acylated by a para-phenylene carbonyl. That ring carries an NH on a pyrazinone, which has an N-methyl and a C=O. The pyrazinone's aryl group is 2-methyl-3-(4-tert-butylbenzamido)phenyl.
