This structure looks like a FAPI-type conjugate: a (4,4-difluoro-2-cyanopyrrolidinyl)glycyl amide on a quinoline-4-carboxamide, linked through an 8-amino succinamide and an ethylenediamine to a DOTA-glutamic acid unit (DOTA-GA).

**Left fragment.** The pyrrolidine nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)CH2NH. C2 carries the nitrile, C3 is CH2, C4 is CF2, and C5 is CH2 bonded to N. The C2 stereocentre is drawn with a bold wedge to the CN group. I wrote it as N#C[C@@H]1CC(F)(F)CN1C(=O)CNC(=O)... and checked the chirality below.

**Chirality at C2.** Its neighbours are CN, N (ring), CH2 (ring C3) and H. In the drawing C2 sits at about (95, 200), with the CN above at (75, 125) on the wedge, N to the right at (140, 225), and C3 to the lower left at (40, 235). H is implied and points away from the viewer.

In the SMILES `N#C[C@@H]1CC(F)(F)CN1...`, the neighbour order at C2 is CN (preceding atom), H, ring-closure 1 (which is N), then C3. For `@@`, looking from the first neighbour (CN), the remaining neighbours H, N, C3 run clockwise.

Check against the drawing, viewed from the front: the wedged CN is toward the viewer, and the other three are N (right), C3 (lower left) and H (back). Viewed from the front with H behind, the priorities are N > CN > C3 (CN has N,N,N; C3 has C,H,H). Going N (right, lower) → CN (up) → C3 (lower left) runs counterclockwise on screen, which gives S with H at the back. This is the usual (S)-2-cyanopyrrolidine, as expected for FAPI compounds.

For the SMILES, the chirality at `[C@@H]` with neighbours in the order CN, H, N, C3 must be converted to CIP. With H as the second neighbour, `@@` from CN means H, N, C3 are clockwise. Swapping to put H first gives the order H, CN, N, C3, which is one swap and so `@`. Looking from H, CN → N → C3 is then counterclockwise. Viewed from the side opposite H, it is clockwise: CN → N → C3. The priority order N → CN → C3 is an odd permutation of that, so it is counterclockwise, giving S. This matches the drawing.
