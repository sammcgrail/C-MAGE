Reading the structure region by region:

- **Left ring:** a benzoate with a methyl ester (COOMe). The ester carbon is attached to the ring at position 1, and the ring oxygen is at position 3 (meta).
- **Linker:** that oxygen connects to a CH2, which connects to the central benzene ring.
- **Central ring:** the CH2 is on one carbon. The carbon next to it bears Cl (ortho), and the carbon para to the CH2 bears an O–CH2 group.
- **Isoxazole:** the O–CH2 attaches at C4 of the isoxazole. C5 carries isopropyl and is bonded to the ring O. C3 carries the 2-(trifluoromethoxy)phenyl group and is bonded to N, which is bonded to the ring O.

Central ring numbering: C1 bears the CH2O, C2 bears Cl, C3 is CH, C4 bears the O-CH2-isoxazole. In the drawing, the CH2 carbon is at the lower left of the ring and Cl is on the lower carbon, adjacent to it. The ether O is at the upper right, so it is para to the CH2 carbon, and Cl is meta to that ether O. This is consistent.
