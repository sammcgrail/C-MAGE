The drawing shows two benzene rings joined by an NH, which makes it a diarylamine.

- **Left ring:** one carbon carries the NH and the adjacent carbon carries CO2H, so the two groups are ortho to each other.
- **Right ring:** the carbon bonded to the NH is at the lower left of the ring, and the carbon bearing CF3 is two positions away, going up through the top vertex to the upper right. That puts CF3 meta to the NH.

This is flufenamic acid, 2-[3-(trifluoromethyl)anilino]benzoic acid.
