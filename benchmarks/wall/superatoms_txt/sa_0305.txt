The drawing shows a piperidine ring. The ring nitrogen carries a Boc group, and the carbon opposite the nitrogen (position 4) carries a CO2H group.

Expanding Boc gives the tert-butoxycarbonyl group, C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.

The ring has six vertices: N plus five carbons. These are the C4 carbon bearing the carboxylic acid, two CH2 carbons on each side of it, and the two CH2 carbons attached to N. Because the substituents sit at opposite ring positions and are drawn with plain bonds, there is no stereochemistry.

The SMILES is OC(=O)C1CCN(CC1)C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.
