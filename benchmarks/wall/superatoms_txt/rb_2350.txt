I read the drawing as a cyclic peptide with a free N-terminus and a C-terminal primary amide. I assumed L-amino-acid stereochemistry throughout and checked only the 4-chloro-Phe centre against the wedges. I did not check the other centres against their wedges or hashes, and the Ser centre could be D.

**Residues, from the N-terminus:**
1. 4-Chloro-Phe, with a free NH2.
2. Dap (2,3-diaminopropionic acid), whose side-chain NH is acylated by the Asp side chain, forming the macrocycle.
3. Tyr.
4. Phe carrying a para phenylurea, written as 4-(NH–C(=O)–NH–Ph).
5. Lys, with a free (CH2)4NH2 side chain.
6. Ser, with a CH2OH side chain.
7. Asp, linked through its side-chain carbonyl to the Dap nitrogen.
8. Tyr-NH2 at the C-terminus.

**Stereochemistry check:** For the 4-chloro-Phe α-carbon, the NH2 is hashed (pointing away) and the H is toward the viewer. Reading N → C(=O) → CH2 gives a clockwise path on screen, so the true order is reversed, which gives S, the L configuration. I then wrote the SMILES with L configuration at every residue, adjusting the @/@@ symbols for each atom's neighbour order.
