I read this as the N‑Cbz, methyl ester of 3,4‑diaminophenylalanine.

- **Ring:** a benzene ring carrying two NH2 groups on adjacent carbons. One is para to the CH2 side chain and the other is meta to it.
- **Side chain:** the ring is joined through a CH2 to the alpha carbon. That carbon carries CO2Me, NHCbz (an N‑benzyloxycarbonyl group, NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–Ph) and an H drawn on a wedge.
- **Stereochemistry:** the H is on a wedge, towards the viewer. The other three substituents run CH2 (left), CO2Me (right) and N (down). CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H. N → CO2Me → CH2 appears counterclockwise with H toward the viewer, so the true sense is clockwise, giving R (a D‑amino acid).
- **SMILES chirality tag:** for `COC(=O)[C@@H](Cc1ccc(N)c(N)c1)NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is ester C, H, CH2, N. I worked out that `@@` for this order corresponds to R, so the SMILES uses `@@`.
