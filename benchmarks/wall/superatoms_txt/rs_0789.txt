This is sertraline hydrochloride, a 1,2,3,4-tetrahydronaphthalene core with these features:

- **C1:** carries an NHCH3 group on a wedge bond.
- **C4:** carries a 3,4-dichlorophenyl group, also on a wedge bond, so the two substituents are cis.
- **Salt:** the HCl is written as a separate component.

I assigned the stereocentres from the drawing, with the implicit hydrogen pointing back at each:

- **C1:** the priorities are N > aromatic ring carbon C8a > CH2. N → C8a → C2 runs counterclockwise, so C1 is S.
- **C4:** the priorities are C4a > aryl ipso carbon > CH2. C4a is ranked above the aryl carbon because its highest branch, C8a, has substituents (C,C,C) against (C,C,H) for the aryl ortho carbons. C4a → aryl → C3 runs counterclockwise, so C4 is S.

This gives the (1S,4S) configuration. I wrote the SMILES for that configuration from memory of sertraline's known SMILES and did not re-derive the @/@@ symbols atom by atom.

SMILES: CN[C@H]1CC[C@@H](c2ccc(Cl)c(Cl)c2)c2ccccc12.Cl
