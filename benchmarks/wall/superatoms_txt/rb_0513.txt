I read the structure in four parts and joined them with the linker.

- **Left-hand heterocycle:** a 6-cyclopropyl imidazo[1,2-a]pyrazine carrying a 1H-pyrazol-4-yl group at C3 and an NH-aryl group at C8.
- **Aryl and linker:** the NH-aryl ring is a 2-fluorophenyl bearing a para carbonyl, which forms an amide with a piperazine. The other piperazine nitrogen carries CH2CH2NH–C(=O)CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH.
- **tert-Leucine unit:** the NH leads to the tert-leucine carbon, which is drawn with a wedge bond. This gives the (S) configuration of the standard VHL ligand.
- **Hydroxyproline and tail:** the tert-leucine carbonyl is attached to the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine (hydroxyproline), whose wedge and hash bonds give the usual (2S,4R) pattern. Its 2-carboxamide is attached to a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamine.

For the stereo SMILES I started from the standard VH032 pattern and checked the tert-leucine centre. Because I write the substituents in the order N, H, tBu, C(=O), which differs from the standard order by one swap, the tert-leucine centre is written as [C@@H]. The hydroxyproline centres match the standard form, [C@H](O) and [C@H]2.
