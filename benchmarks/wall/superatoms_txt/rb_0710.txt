I read the structure in five parts and joined them in this order.

1. **Left end:** a glutarimide (piperidine-2,6-dione) attached through its C3 carbon to the nitrogen of an isoindolin-1-one. The C3 carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.
2. **Isoindolinone ring:** the benzene ring carries a fluorine, and next to it a piperidin-4-yl group.
3. **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen carries an unbranched chain that ends at an ether oxygen. I counted 7 chain carbons from the zigzag: the nitrogen and the oxygen both sit on the low side, which fits an odd number of carbons, and the 456 px span divides into 8 bonds of about 57 px.
4. **Central aryl ring:** the ether oxygen is on a benzene ring that has a fluorine on one ortho position. The other ortho position carries the quinazoline.
5. **Quinazoline:** it is attached at C7 and carries 6-Cl and 8-F. At C4 it has a piperazine whose far nitrogen is acylated with propionyl, giving N-C(=O)CH2CH3.
