I read the molecule as a bivalent compound with three parts joined by a long chain.

- **Left end:** a 3,5-dimethylisoxazol-4-yl group sits on position 7 of an imidazo[1,2-a]pyridine. Position 3 of that ring system carries an NH-cyclohexyl group. Position 2 carries a CH2CH2 link to a para-substituted benzene.
- **Middle:** the benzene carries an NH–C(=O) amide. The carbonyl carbon plus 11 CH2 groups form a 12-carbon chain that ends in an NH.
- **Right end:** that NH is attached to position 4 of a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione). The phthalimide nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. This is the thalidomide/pomalidomide-type unit.

I counted the chain vertices one at a time in the drawing: the carbonyl carbon, then 11 CH2, then NH. The SMILES below has the same chain length. No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I included no stereochemistry, including at the piperidine C3.
