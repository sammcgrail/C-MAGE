I read this as a 7‑substituted camptothecin: the pentacyclic camptothecin core with a 2‑(trimethylsilyl)ethyl group on the carbon next to the benzo ring. That is karenitecin.

- **Core:** the lactone ring has a ring C=O and a CH2–O. The pyridone ring has its C=O on the nitrogen. The CH2 on that nitrogen leads into the five‑membered ring. The quinoline N sits in the aromatic benzo‑fused ring.
- **Substituent on the benzo side:** CH2CH2 followed by the TMS label, expanded to Si(CH3)3.
- **Stereocentre:** the quaternary carbon carries OH, ethyl, the lactone C=O and a ring carbon. The ethyl is drawn on a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer. With ethyl at the back, OH → lactone C(=O) → ring carbon runs counterclockwise on the page, which gives S. The SMILES below, with `[C@@]` and ethyl written first, also encodes S.
