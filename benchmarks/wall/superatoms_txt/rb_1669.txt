I read this as a pomalidomide-type PROTAC: a glutarimide–phthalimide with a 4-amino PEG linker, joined through a carbamate to a lovastatin/simvastatin-like decalin that carries an acetylated lactone. I did not derive every stereocentre independently from the wedges. I took the natural statin configuration and checked only the C6 methyl centre against a recalled PubChem SMILES, so the stereo marks are the least certain part.

- **Left fragment:** the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group is attached through its nitrogen to the isoindoline-1,3-dione. The aromatic ring carries an NH at the 4-position.
- **Linker:** NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, ending in a carbamate N–C(=O)–O.
- **Decalin:** the carbamate oxygen sits on C1. There is a methyl at C3 (hashed) and a methyl on the other ring. Two C=C bonds are present, one at C4=C4a and one at C5=C6. The side chain at C8 is a two-carbon ethylene bridge.
- **Lactone:** a six-membered δ-lactone with an acetate (OAc) on the 4-position.
