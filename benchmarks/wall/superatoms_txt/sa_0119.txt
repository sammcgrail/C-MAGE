The drawing shows Cbz-protected alanine (Cbz-Ala-OH).

- **Backbone:** the central carbon carries a methyl group (the plain bond going up), an NH bonded to Cbz (lower left), and a CO2H group (lower right, drawn as a wedge).
- **Cbz expanded:** Cbz is benzyloxycarbonyl, so the nitrogen substituent is C(=O)OCc1ccccc1.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CO2H wedge points toward the viewer, so the implicit hydrogen points away. The priorities are N > CO2H > CH3 > H. Going N (lower left) → CO2H (lower right) → CH3 (top) is counterclockwise with H at the back, so the centre is S, which is the L-alanine configuration.
- **SMILES:** C[C@H](N)C(=O)O is the L-alanine chirality, and adding the Cbz group on nitrogen gives the answer below.

SMILES: C[C@H](NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1)C(=O)O
