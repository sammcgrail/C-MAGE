The drawing shows a central C=C double bond. Each alkene carbon carries two substituents drawn as "NC" (left side) or "CN" (right side), so each is a nitrile group attached through carbon. The labels read NC and CN rather than the C≡N bond being drawn explicitly, but they are written with carbon next to the alkene, so each substituent is a cyano group, C#N.

This gives tetracyanoethylene. The four cyano groups are identical, so there is no stereochemistry to specify.

SMILES: N#CC(C#N)=C(C#N)C#N
