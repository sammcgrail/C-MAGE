**Stereochemistry**
- The hashed bond puts CO2Me behind the plane, so the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- CIP priorities are CO2Me (O,O,O) > CH2O (O,H,H) > CH3 > H.
- On screen, CO2Me (lower left) to CH2O (lower right) to CH3 (top) runs counterclockwise. With H toward the viewer this reverses to clockwise, so the centre is R.
- In the SMILES `COC(=O)[C@H](C)CO[Si]...`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is C(=O), H, CH3, CH2. Working through the permutation, `@` corresponds to R here, so `[C@H]` is correct.
