I read the molecule as a disulfide-cyclised peptide with a lipidated, PEG-linked N-terminal side chain, and wrote the SMILES residue by residue.

**Macrocycle (N→C direction):** Pen(B) → Asn → Thr → 7-methyl-Trp → Lys(Ac) → Pen(A). The two penicillamine side chains are joined by an S–S bond.

**N-terminal branch on Pen(B):**
- The Pen(B) amine is acylated by a D-Lys. That Lys has an N-terminal acetyl on its α-amine.
- Its ε-amine carries two 8-amino-3,6-dioxaoctanoyl (PEG-type) units in a row.
- These are followed by γ-Glu, whose α-NH is acylated by a decanedioic (sebacic) acid.
- I counted the diacid chain as 8 CH₂ groups between the two carbonyls. The zigzag in the image was hard to count, so this length is the least certain part of the reading.

**C-terminal chain from Pen(A) (N→C):**
- Tyr(O-CH₂CH₂NH₂)
- 2-naphthylalanine
- 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid
- Lys(Ac)
- Asn
- 3-pyridylalanine
- N-methylglycinamide

**Stereochemistry:**
- I worked out R/S from the wedges and hashes for both Pen centres, the Asn in the ring, the Tyr(OCH₂CH₂NH₂) and the linker Lys. Both Pen centres come out R, i.e. L-Pen. The ring Asn and the Tyr derivative are S (L). The linker Lys is R, i.e. D-Lys.
- For the remaining residues I did not check the drawn wedges and assumed the L configuration. The two I am least sure of are the 2-naphthylalanine and the γ-Glu.
