**Fragments, left to right**
- **Aryl ether:** a 4-cyano-3,5-dimethylphenyl group, linked through O to a cyclobutane.
- **First cyclobutane:** it carries four methyls (2,2,4,4-tetramethyl). The O-bearing carbon has a wedge bond and the NH-bearing carbon has a hashed bond.
- **Linker:** the NH is part of an amide to a para-substituted benzene (a benzamide). The benzene is bonded to the nitrogen of a piperidine.
- **Piperidine:** its 4-position carries an N-methylamino group, which is bonded to a second cyclobutane.
- **Second cyclobutane:** 1,3-disubstituted, with N on a wedge and O on a hash, so the two substituents are trans.
- **Isoindoline-1,3-dione:** the second cyclobutane's O is attached at the 6-position, with a 4-methoxy group. Its ring nitrogen carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) group, whose stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds and so has no stereo label.
