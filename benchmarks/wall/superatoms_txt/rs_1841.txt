I read the drawing as an α-D-glucosamine core with all oxygens acetylated and the 2-amino group acylated by 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypicolinic acid. I did not derive the @/@@ tags from first principles. I took them from the standard α-D-glucopyranose SMILES and kept the neighbour order unchanged when swapping substituents.

**Pyridine part.** The ring is a pyridine with N at the bottom left. The carbon next to N carries the C(=O)NH amide link. The next ring carbon carries OH, and the one after that carries OMe.

**Sugar ring.** The ring is a six-membered pyranose (five carbons plus the ring oxygen), numbered C1 to C5 from the anomeric carbon:
- C1 (bottom left) has an OAc on a hashed bond, so it points down (α).
- C2 has the amide NH on a hashed bond (down).
- C3 has an OAc on a wedge (up).
- C4 has an OAc on a hashed bond (down).
- C5 has the CH2OAc group on a wedge (up).

This pattern of up/down substituents matches α-D-glucosamine with 1,3,4,6-tetra-O-acetyl groups.
