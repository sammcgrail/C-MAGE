**Target-binding end (quinazoline):**
- The quinazoline carries a 4-(4-carbamoylpiperazin-1-yl) group.
- It has 6-chloro and 8-fluoro substituents.
- The 7-position bears a 2-fluoro-6-hydroxyphenyl ring.
- The 2-position carries an NH.

**Linker:**
- From the quinazoline NH: NH–CH2CH2–C(=O)–N(CH3)–.
- Then five ethylene-oxy units, counted from the five ring-free oxygen labels in the drawing: (CH2CH2O)×5.
- Then –CH2–C(=O)–NH–.

**E3-ligase end (VHL-type ligand):**
- tert-Leucine, with the wedge on the NH bond. I assigned it S, which is the L-amino-acid form.
- 4-Hydroxyproline, with the usual (2S,4R) configuration.
- The prolyl carbonyl is joined by an amide to a (1-aryl)ethylamine, whose aryl group is 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl. The wedge-drawn methyl gives the stereocentre written as `[C@@H]`.

**Stereochemistry choices:**
- The hashed bond on the hydroxyphenyl ring looks like an atropisomer marker. It does not define a clear stereocentre, so I left it out.
- For the hydroxyproline and the benzylic carbon, I used the standard VHL-ligand stereochemistry rather than re-deriving each centre from the wedges.
