I read the structure region by region and wrote the SMILES below. I did not run it through a parser, so validity rests on my manual atom-by-atom check.

**Left tricyclic core.** A pyridazine ring (N=N, with a 2-hydroxyphenyl on the carbon next to N) is fused to a dihydropyrazine bearing the NH. That in turn is fused to a piperazine whose far nitrogen carries the carbonyl.

**Stereocentre.** The only wedge is on the ring-fusion CH. Its neighbours are the CH2 leading to NH (wedged, toward the viewer), the CH2 leading to the amide nitrogen, and the ring nitrogen, with H pointing away. Seen from the front, CH2NH → CH2 → N runs clockwise. Written in SMILES as `...NC[C@H]2C1`, with neighbour order CH2NH, H, N, CH2, this corresponds to `@`.

**Linker.** The urea carbonyl joins the piperazine nitrogen to a second piperazine. Its other nitrogen carries a CH2 that attaches to C2 of a morpholine. The morpholine nitrogen is bonded to the aromatic ring of the isoindoline-1,3-dione. That ring's imide nitrogen carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The morpholine C2 and the glutarimide C3 are drawn with plain bonds, so they get no stereo marks.
