This is Fmoc-Glu(OtBu)-OH, the N-Fmoc-protected L-glutamic acid with a tert-butyl ester on the side chain.

**Reading the drawing**
- The central carbon carries an HO2C group (drawn on a hashed bond), an NH–Fmoc group, and a CH2CH2 chain.
- The chain ends in CO2tBu, so it is CH2–CH2–C(=O)O–C(CH3)3.
- Fmoc is expanded as the fluorenylmethoxycarbonyl group, NC(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12.

**Stereochemistry**
- The CIP priorities at the stereocentre are N > CO2H > CH2 > H.
- The CO2H is hashed (pointing away), so the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- Going N → CO2H → CH2 appears clockwise in the drawing. With H toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is S, which is the L configuration.
- Writing the SMILES in the order CO2H, H, N, chain gives `@@`.

SMILES: O=C(O)[C@@H](NC(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12)CCC(=O)OC(C)(C)C
