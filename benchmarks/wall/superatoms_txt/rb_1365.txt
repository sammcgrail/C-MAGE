I read the structure in three parts and joined them into one SMILES.

**Left part.** A 6-(trifluoromethyl)pyridine-2-carboxamide is attached through its amide NH to C5 of a 2H-indazole. The indazole carries a 6-methoxy group and a 3-methyl group, and its N2 is bonded to a cyclohexane ring.

**Linker.**
- The cyclohexane is 1,4-disubstituted, with the indazole N on one carbon and a CH2 on the other.
- The CH2 leads to the nitrogen of a 7-azaspiro[3.5]nonane, i.e. a piperidine spiro-fused to a cyclobutane.
- The cyclobutane carbon opposite the spiro centre carries a CH2CH2NH chain.

**Right part.** The NH is attached to the 4-position of a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione). The imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group (the thalidomide-type unit).

**Stereochemistry.** The cyclohexane has a wedge on one 1,4-carbon and a hash on the other, which makes it trans. Because the two carbons are related by symmetry, only this relative configuration is defined, so I encoded it as `[C@H]...[C@@H]`. The glutarimide CH and the cyclobutane CH are drawn with plain bonds, so they get no stereo marks.
