I read the molecule as three parts joined by an amide-linked PEG-type chain.

**Left fragment:** an isoquinoline numbered C1 to C8a.
- C8 carries NH2 and C7 carries F.
- C6 carries a pyridin-3-yl group. This pyridine has a methyl at C4 and an NH2 at C5.
- C3 carries an NH–C(=O) amide.

**Linker:** the isoquinoline-3-amide continues as C(=O)CCCC(=O)NCCOCC(=O)N. That is a glutaryl diamide, then an ethylene–O–CH2 unit, then an amide to the next fragment.

**Right fragment:** a VHL-type ligand.
- The tert-leucine residue has a tert-butyl group on the alpha carbon.
- Its carbonyl is bonded to the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxyproline ring.
- The proline carboxamide is bonded through NH–CH2 to a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl group.

**Stereochemistry:** I could not resolve the wedges and hashes with certainty, so I assigned them as the standard VHL ligand configuration. That is (S)-tert-leucine and (2S,4R)-hydroxyproline.
