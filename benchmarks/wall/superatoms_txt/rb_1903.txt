I read the structure as a maleamic acid–caproyl–Val–Ala–PABC linker carbamate-bonded to an MMAE-type payload.

**Linker**
- A Z (cis) maleamic acid, HOOC–CH=CH–C(=O)NH–, is linked to a chain of five CH2 groups and a C=O (a caproyl unit).
- This is followed by L-Val (wedged isopropyl) and L-Ala (hashed methyl), then an anilide NH on a para-substituted benzene ring.
- The ring carries CH2–O–C(=O)– (the PABC carbamate) on the other side.

**Payload (MMAE-like)**
- The carbamate nitrogen belongs to N-methyl-L-Val.
- This is followed by L-Val and an N-methyl dolaisoleuine unit (sec-butyl, OMe, CH2C(=O)).
- That unit acylates a pyrrolidine, which carries a CH(OMe)–CH(CH3)–C(=O)NH– group.
- The chain ends in a norephedrine-type residue, NH–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–Ph.

**Stereochemistry**
- I assigned the Val and Ala centres from the wedges and hashes: both come out as L.
- I took the payload stereocentres from the known MMAE configuration and re-ordered the SMILES neighbour lists to match my atom order.
