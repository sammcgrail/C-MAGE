**Amide link:** the pyridine C2 carries C(=O)–NH, and the NH is bonded to the cyclohexane.

**Cyclohexane:** the 1,4-disubstituted ring has the NH at C1 and the aryl ether oxygen at C4. Both bonds are drawn as bold wedges, so the two substituents are on the same face, which is the cis isomer. Working through the SMILES chirality tags, `N[C@H]2CC[C@@H](CC2)O` puts both substituents on the same face.

**Aryl ether:** the oxygen links to a para-substituted benzene ring bearing CF3.
