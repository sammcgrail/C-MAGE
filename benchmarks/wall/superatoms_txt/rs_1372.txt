I read the molecule as a pyrimidin-5-yl group joined through a two-carbon chain (CH2CH2) to the nitrogen of a pyrrolidine ring. The ring carbon at position 3 carries a mesylate (OMs, i.e. OS(=O)(=O)CH3) on a wedge bond.

**Stereocentre.** The wedge puts O toward the viewer, so H points away.
- Seen from the front, the substituents run O (upper right), then C4 (lower right), then C2 (lower left), which is clockwise.
- CIP priorities are O > C2 (attached to N) > C4 > H. The order O → C2 → C4 is therefore counterclockwise with H at the back, so the centre is S.

**SMILES check.** In `CS(=O)(=O)O[C@H]1CCN(C1)CCc1cncnc1` the neighbours of the stereocentre are listed as O, H, C2 (via the ring closure), C4. With `@` this ordering corresponds to S, which matches the drawing.
