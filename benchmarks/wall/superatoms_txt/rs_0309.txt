**Pyridine fragment.** The ring has N at the bottom. C2 carries the carboxamide (C(=O)NH). C3 carries the propionate ester (OC(=O)CH2CH3). C4 carries OMe. C5 and C6 are plain CH.

**Nine-membered ring**, going around from the NH-bearing carbon:
1. CH bearing the NH, with the N on a hashed bond.
2. Ring C(=O), then ring O.
3. CH(CH3), with the methyl on a wedge.
4. CH bearing the isobutyrate ester OC(=O)CH(CH3)2, on a hashed bond.
5. CH bearing CH2Ph, on a wedge.
6. Ring C(=O), then ring O, then CH2, which closes back to the NH-bearing CH.

**Stereochemistry.** I assigned each of the four stereocentres from its wedge or hash, treating the implicit H as pointing opposite the drawn wedge or hash. The SMILES tags come from that analysis, but I did not run the string through a toolkit to confirm them.
