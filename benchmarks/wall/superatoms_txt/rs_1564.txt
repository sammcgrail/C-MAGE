Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Acid end:** a COOH group on a carbon that carries an ethoxy group (O–CH2CH3). That carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
- **Linker:** a CH2 joins this carbon to a para-disubstituted benzene ring.
- **Ether side:** the para position of that ring bears an O–CH2–CH=C unit.
- **Alkene end:** the double-bond carbon at the far end carries both a phenyl ring and a 2-chlorophenyl ring.
- **Double-bond geometry:** in the drawing the phenyl points up and the CH2 on the other alkene carbon also points up, so phenyl and CH2 are cis. The 2-chlorophenyl is therefore trans to the CH2.

In SMILES, `C/C=C(\c2ccccc2)...` places the CH2 and the phenyl on the same side of the double bond, which matches this geometry.

SMILES: CCOC(Cc1ccc(OC/C=C(\c2ccccc2)c2ccccc2Cl)cc1)C(=O)O
