This SMILES was read from the drawing by hand. The lauroyl chain length and most of the stereocentres are partly guesses.

**Reading, N-terminus to C-terminus:**
- Ac-Arg, with the guanidine drawn as an acetylated arginine side chain.
- A penicillamine, Pen1, whose gem-dimethyl carbon carries one sulfur of the disulfide.
- Asn, Thr, then 7-methyl-Trp, then Lys(NHAc).
- A second penicillamine, Pen2, which closes the macrocycle through the S–S bond.
- Tyr with a 2-aminoethoxy group on the phenol oxygen.
- 2-Naphthylalanine.
- A 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid residue, drawn as a quaternary carbon in a six-membered ring containing O.
- Lys, with the side-chain amine acylated by two AEEA (–CO–CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–) units. These lead to γ-Glu, whose α-amine is acylated by a lauroyl group (12 carbons, as best I could count).
- Asn, then 3-pyridylalanine, then an N-methylglycinamide (sarcosinamide) C-terminus.

**Uncertain points:**
- **Lauroyl chain:** my count of the zigzag was 11 or 12 carbons, and I chose 12 (dodecanoyl).
- **Stereochemistry:** I could not resolve every wedge and hash. Most centres are written as L, and the Lys carrying the linker and the naphthylalanine are written as D.
