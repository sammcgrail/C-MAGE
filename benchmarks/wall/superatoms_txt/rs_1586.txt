I read the molecule as a 4-substituted benzoic acid whose para position carries a CH2CH2 link to the nitrogen of a 2-pyrrolidinone (γ-lactam).

- **Lactam ring:** a five-membered ring with C=O, N, and three further carbons. The carbon next to N (C5) bears the side chain.
- **N-substituent:** N–CH2–CH2–C6H4–CO2H, with the ring carbons para to each other.
- **C5 side chain:** CH=CH (drawn trans, so E), then CH(OH), then CH2CH2CH2, then O–CH3.

**Stereocentres**

- **Ring C5** has a wedged bond to the vinyl carbon. The priorities are N > vinyl carbon > ring CH2 > H. With H pointing away, N → vinyl → CH2 runs clockwise, so this centre is R, written `[C@H]` in the SMILES below.
- **Carbinol carbon** has a hashed OH. The priorities are OH > vinyl carbon > CH2 > H. With H toward the viewer, OH → vinyl → CH2 appears clockwise, which reverses to counterclockwise for the true view, so this centre is S, written `[C@@H]`.

SMILES: O=C1CC[C@H](/C=C/[C@@H](O)CCCOC)N1CCc1ccc(C(=O)O)cc1
