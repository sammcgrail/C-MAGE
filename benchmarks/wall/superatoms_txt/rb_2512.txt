I read this as a tacrolimus (FK506)-type macrolide whose right-hand side is truncated at the wavy bond. I wrote that open bond as a `*` attached to a CH2.

The pieces I read from the drawing, with the SMILES built to match:
- **Cyclohexane ring:** it carries a trans-arranged OH and OMe on adjacent carbons, and is attached through a wedged bond to a trisubstituted alkene bearing a methyl group.
- **Pipecolate:** a six-membered ring with a ring N and an ester carbonyl, linked to the macrocycle through the lactone O.
- **Tricarbonyl region:** the ring N is acylated by an α-keto amide that leads into a hemiketal tetrahydropyran. That pyran carries a methyl, a ring OMe and a hemiketal OH.
- **Macrocycle chain:** it has two more OMe groups and a methyl branch, then a trisubstituted alkene with a methyl, then a ketone. The carbon next to the ketone is hashed and carries the CH2 that leads to the wavy bond. The chain then continues through a secondary OH and a methyl-bearing carbon back to the lactone O.

I filled in the stereo descriptors largely from my memory of tacrolimus rather than deriving each centre from the wedges and hashes, so some of them may be wrong.
