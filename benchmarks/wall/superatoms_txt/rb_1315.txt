This is a PROTAC-type molecule: an EGFR-binding quinazoline joined through a piperazine linker to a thalidomide (pomalidomide-type) group.

**Quinazoline part.** The 4-position carries an NH-aryl group, 3-chloro-4-fluorophenyl. The 6-position carries an acrylamide, NHC(=O)C=C. The 7-position carries an O–(CH2)3– chain that leads to the piperazine.

**Linker.** From the 7-oxygen: O-CH2CH2CH2-N(piperazine), then the second piperazine N, then CH2CH2CH2-NH, which attaches to the aromatic ring of the phthalimide.

**Thalidomide part.** The phthalimide is attached to the linker NH at position 4. Its imide nitrogen bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring. The stereocentre on that ring is drawn with plain bonds, so I give it no stereo label.

**SMILES assembly.**
- Quinazoline core: c1(Nc2ccc(F)c(Cl)c2)ncnc2cc(OCCCN3CCN(CCCNc4cccc5C(=O)N(C6CCC(=O)NC6=O)C(=O)c45)CC3)c(NC(=O)C=C)cc12
- Ring closure check: atom c1 is C4, followed by n (N3), c (C2), n (N1), c2 (C8a), c (C8), c(O...) (C7), c(NHC(=O)C=C) (C6), c (C5), and c12 (C4a), which closes both rings.
- Phthalimide: c4cccc5C(=O)N(...)C(=O)c45. The c4 bearing the NH is adjacent to c45, the carbon bonded to the C=O, so the NH sits at the 4-position, as drawn.
