I read the molecule as a 2-pyrrolidinone whose ring nitrogen carries a –CH2CH2– link to the 5-position of a thiophene-2-carboxylic acid.

- **Lactam ring:** the five-membered ring has a C=O next to N, then two CH2 groups, then a CH at the ring carbon beside N.
- **N-substituent:** N–CH2–CH2– is attached to thiophene C5. The thiophene S is flanked by C2 (bearing CO2H) and C5.
- **Side chain on the ring CH:** a trans (E) CH=CH leads to a CH(OH) carbon, which carries an n-pentyl chain (five carbons).
- **Stereochemistry:** the carbinol carbon is drawn with a hashed bond to OH, so OH points away from the viewer and H points toward the viewer. The priorities are OH > vinyl > pentyl > H. OH, vinyl and pentyl run clockwise as drawn, and because H faces the viewer the true configuration is reversed, giving S. In the SMILES this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.
- **Unspecified centre:** the ring CH is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

SMILES: OC(=O)c1ccc(CCN2C(=O)CCC2/C=C/[C@@H](O)CCCCC)s1
