I read the drawing as an antimycin-type nine-membered dilactone carrying a substituted pyridine amide. I worked through it in three parts.

**Pyridine part:** The ring runs N, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6.
- C2 carries the amide C(=O)NH.
- C3 carries the OC(=O)CH2OCH2Ph (benzyloxyacetate) ester.
- C4 carries OMe.

**Nine-membered dilactone ring:** Going around the ring:
- CH(NH–) at the hashed N bond
- C(=O)
- ring O
- CH(CH3), with the methyl on a wedge
- CH(OC(=O)CH(CH3)2), with the ester O on a hash
- CH(CH2Ph), with the benzyl on a wedge
- C(=O)
- ring O
- CH2, which closes back to the CH(N)

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned the four stereocentres from the wedge and hash bonds. The implicit hydrogen sits behind the plane at the methyl and benzyl carbons and in front at the N-bearing and isobutyrate carbons. This gives @ for the CH(N) carbon, @ for the CH(Bn) carbon, @@ for the CH(O-isobutyrate) carbon, and @ for the CH(CH3) carbon in the SMILES order below.
