I read the molecule as a VHL-ligand conjugate: a 2-hydroxyphenyl aminopyridazine on one side and a (2S,4R)-hydroxyproline/4-methylthiazolyl-benzylamide on the other, joined through a pyrazole, a piperidine and an acetamide–tert-leucine linker.

**Left side.** A 2-hydroxyphenyl is attached to the pyridazine at the carbon next to a ring N. The pyridazine carries a pyrazol-4-yl group at the next ring carbon and NH2 on the carbon beyond that, adjacent to the other ring N. The pyrazole N1 bears a CH2 that leads to the piperidine.

**Piperidine.** The CH2 is attached at C3 of the piperidine, drawn with a hashed bond. Using CIP priorities (CH2–pyrazole, then C2, then C4, then H), I assigned this centre as S. The piperidine nitrogen carries CH2C(=O)NH–.

**tert-Leucine.** The amide NH is bonded to a CH that carries a hashed tert-butyl group and a C(=O) that links to the proline nitrogen. I assigned this centre as S, which is the natural L-configuration.

**Hydroxyproline.** The ring has a hashed carboxamide at C2 and a bold-wedge OH at C4. I wrote these as the standard VHL (2S,4R) pattern rather than deriving each centre independently from the picture.

**Right side.** The proline C2 carboxamide is N-benzylated. The benzyl is a para-substituted phenyl bearing a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.

I checked the stereo tags only for the piperidine and tert-leucine centres; the hydroxyproline tags are taken from the standard VHL ligand pattern.
