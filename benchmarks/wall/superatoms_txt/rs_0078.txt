I read the molecule in four parts and then worked out the three stereocentres from the wedges and hashes.

**Fragments**
- **Indazole:** the carbonyl is on the benzo ring carbon next to C7, which makes it indazole-6-carbonyl. The ring has a free N–H.
- **Tetrahydroisoquinoline:** the indazole acyl group sits on N2. The aromatic ring carries Cl at C5 and C7, and the C6 carboxamide lies between them.
- **Side chain:** the amide NH leads to a CH bearing CO2H, then CH2–NH. That NH is attached to C(=N–C≡N), which is bonded to the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Piperidine:** it carries OH groups at C3 (bold wedge) and C4 (hashed bond).

**Stereochemistry (CIP)**
- **α-Carbon:** the CH2 is on a wedge, and the priorities are N > CO2H > CH2N > H. Viewed with H at the back, N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. This corresponds to `N[C@@H](CN…)C(=O)O` in the SMILES.
- **Piperidine C3:** the OH is on the wedge, with priorities OH > C4 > C2 > H. This gives S, written `[C@H]` in the SMILES.
- **Piperidine C4:** the OH is hashed, so H faces the viewer. The priorities are OH > C3 > C5 > H, and the reversed reading gives S, written `[C@@H]` in the SMILES.
