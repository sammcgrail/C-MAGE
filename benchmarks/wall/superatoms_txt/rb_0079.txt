I read the structure in sections and wrote the SMILES for each, then joined them.

1. **Left end:** a 2-amino-3,4,5,6-tetrahydropyridine (cyclic amidine) is linked through NH–CH2CH2 to N1 of an indazole.
2. **Indazole:** C5 carries a carboxamide, C(=O)NH–CH2–.
3. **Amino-acid core:** the CH2 leads to a CH bearing the sulfonamide NH and a methyl ester. The wedge to the ester gives this carbon @ in the SMILES below.
4. **Aryl sulfonyl:** the sulfonamide S(=O)(=O) is on a benzene ring with 2,6-dimethyl groups and a 4-O–CH2CH2CH2–C(=O)NH– chain.
5. **Linker:** the chain continues NH–CH2CH2–N(H). The drawing shows the N without an H, but I took it as NH since it has two bonds.
6. **Glutamate units:** the linker N is acylated by the first glutamate, which has a CH2CH2CO2tBu side chain, and its α-NH is acylated by the second glutamate (same side chain). Both wedged centres work out to @ in the SMILES order I used.
7. **Macrocycle:** the second glutamate's NH is acylated by C(=O)CH2 on a 12-membered tetraaza ring (cyclen, DOTA-type). Each of the other three ring nitrogens carries a CH2CO2tBu arm.
