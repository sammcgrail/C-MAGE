This is a maleimidocaproyl–Val–Lys(sarcosine decamer)–PABC–exatecan drug-linker. I read it in sections from left to right.

- **Maleimide and linker:** A maleimide ring is joined through N to five CH2 groups and then C(=O), which is the 6-maleimidocaproyl unit. It is amide-linked to valine. The valine isopropyl group is on a wedge, which gives L-Val (S).
- **Lysine:** The Val C(=O) is amide-linked to the lysine α-NH. The hashed chain bond at the lysine α-carbon gives L-Lys (S), and the four-CH2 side chain ends in an amide NH.
- **Sarcosine chain:** The lysine ε-NH is acylated by a chain of 10 N-methylglycine units, N(CH3)CH2C(=O), capped at the N-terminus with acetyl. I counted ten N-methyl nitrogens along the chain.
- **PABC spacer:** The lysine C(=O) is linked through NH to a para-substituted benzene bearing CH2–O–C(=O)–NH.
- **Exatecan:** The carbamate NH sits on the wedged benzylic carbon of the tetrahydro ring, which works out to S. That ring is fused to the fluoro-methyl-quinoline, the pyrrolo-pyridone and the lactone. The quaternary carbon carries OH and ethyl on wedges, which gives 20S.

I checked each stereocentre by CIP priority against the drawn wedges and hashes.
