Reading the structure region by region:

**Left end.** A 4,4-difluorocyclohexyl ring is attached through a hashed bond to a trans CH=CH, which joins the pyridine at C4. The pyridine carries a 5-methoxy group and a 2-carboxamide (N is position 1, C2 bears the amide, C4 the vinyl, C5 the OMe).

**Middle.** The amide NH connects through CH2 to a benzene ring, which is meta-disubstituted. The other substituent is CH2–C(=O)–N(CH3)–(CH2)4–O–.

**Right end.** The O links to C5 of a phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione). Its N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.

**Stereochemistry.** The hashed bond on the cyclohexyl carbon is not a real stereocentre, because the 4,4-difluoro ring is symmetric. I therefore leave it out. The double bond is drawn trans, so it is written as /C=C/.

**SMILES assembly.**
- Cyclohexyl and vinyl: FC1(F)CCC(CC1)/C=C/
- Pyridine: c1cc(C(=O)N...)ncc1OC, with the vinyl at C4
- Pyridine written out: C4 attached to the vinyl, then c(cc(C(=O)NCc...)n c5 OC)
- Full pyridine: c1(/C=C/C2CCC(F)(F)CC2)cc(C(=O)NCc2cccc(c2)CC(=O)N(C)CCCCOc2ccc3c(c2)C(=O)N(C2CCC(=O)NC2=O)C3=O)ncc1OC

The vinyl is attached at the ring carbon adjacent to the C5 carbon bearing OMe, and the ring order is C4(vinyl), C3(H), C2(amide), N1, C6(H), C5(OMe), which matches the SMILES above.

**Phthalimide check.** The ring is c2ccc3c(c2)C(=O)N(...)C3=O. The O-bearing carbon is c2, next is cH, then cH, then c3 (a fusion carbon), then c(c2), which is the other fusion carbon bonded to the c2-adjacent CH. This is the 5-substituted pattern, as drawn.
