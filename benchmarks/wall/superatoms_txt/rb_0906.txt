**Sulfonamide.** The group is NH–S(=O)(=O)–thiophen-2-yl, written `NS(=O)(=O)c1cccs1`.

**Triazole.** The ring atoms are C3 (bearing S), N4, C5(H), N1 (bearing the chain) and N2. The drawing shows N at the top, then CH, then N1 bearing the chain, then N2 at the bottom, with C3 attached to S. As SMILES, `Sc1ncn(CC...)n1` matches: C3 is bonded to N4, N4 to C5, C5 to N1, N1 to N2, and N2 back to C3.

**Linker and imide end.** From N1 the chain is CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–aryl. The aryl is the phthalimide benzene, with the O at the 5-position. The imide nitrogen carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring.

- Phthalimide: `c3ccc4c(c3)C(=O)N(...)C4=O` places the O-bearing carbon c3, then cc, then the fusion carbon c4, then c(c3), which is the other fusion carbon bonded to the CH.
