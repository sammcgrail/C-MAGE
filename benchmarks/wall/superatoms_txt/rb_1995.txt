I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type structure: a morpholinyl-oxazole carboxamide on the left, a central benzene ring carrying a piperidine and a piperazine, a PEG linker, and a VHL-type hydroxyproline ligand on the right.

**Left side**
- A morpholine nitrogen is attached to C2 of an oxazole, which has a C4 carboxamide.
- That amide NH is attached to a benzene ring.
- The ring carries a 4-carbamoylpiperidin-1-yl group on the carbon next to the NH.
- It also carries a CH2–piperazine group para to the NH.

**Linker**
- The piperazine's other nitrogen is acylated: C(=O)CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–C(=O).
- The terminal carbonyl forms an amide with the NH of the tert-leucine unit.

**Right side**

- Pyrrolidine C2 carries a carboxamide NH–CH2–C6H4, with the para position bearing a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.
