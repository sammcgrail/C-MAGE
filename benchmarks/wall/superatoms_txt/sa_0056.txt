The drawing shows a central sulfur with two double-bonded oxygens (left and right), a CF3 group below, and an OTMS group above.

TMS is trimethylsilyl, Si(CH3)3, so OTMS expands to O[Si](C)(C). This gives trimethylsilyl trifluoromethanesulfonate (TMSOTf).

The SMILES is C[Si](C)(C)OS(=O)(=O)C(F)(F)F. It has one sulfur, three oxygens, one silicon and four carbons, which matches the drawing atom for atom. There are no stereocentres.
