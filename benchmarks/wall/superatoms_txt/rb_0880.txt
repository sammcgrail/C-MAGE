**Linker.** The piperidine nitrogen is attached to CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2-NH-, which gives N(CCOCCOCCN...).

**Right fragment.** The linker NH is bonded to the isoindolinone benzene ring at position 5. The ring is a 1-oxoisoindolin-2-yl bearing a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the nitrogen. The stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereochemistry is given.
- Isoindolinone with glutarimide: c1ccc2C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)Cc2c1

**Attachment position on the isoindolinone.** The aromatic carbon bearing NH is para to the carbonyl-bearing carbon C3a. From the drawing, the ring carbon attached to C=O is at the top right, and the CH2-bearing carbon is at the bottom right. The NH-bearing carbon is at the left-bottom, which is adjacent to the carbon next to the CH2-bearing carbon. In SMILES, the ring is c1(N)ccc2C(=O)N(...)Cc2c1. Here the atom order is: c1 (bearing N), c, c, c2 (bearing C=O), then c2 (bearing CH2), then c1 (CH between the CH2-bearing carbon and the NH-bearing carbon). The NH-bearing carbon is therefore meta to the CH2-bearing carbon and para to the carbonyl-bearing carbon, which matches the drawing.
