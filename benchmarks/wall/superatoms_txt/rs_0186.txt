I read the drawing as three parts joined together, and assigned the stereocentres from the wedges and hashes.

**Pyridine part.** The ring has N, then C2 bearing the amide carbonyl, C3 bearing the 2-chloroethyl carbonate O–C(=O)–O–CH2CH2Cl, C4 bearing OMe, and C5 and C6 as plain CH.

**Ring.** It is a 9-membered dilactone. Going round from the top left, the atoms are:

- CH–O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2, the isobutyrate, with the O on a hash

- C(=O)

**Stereochemistry.** For each of the four stereocentres I took the drawn wedge or hash, worked out the clockwise or counterclockwise order of the three other neighbours with the implicit H opposite, and converted that to the SMILES order.
