**Middle fragment.**

- A 1,3-related N-methylamino group sits on the same ring. The oxygen is drawn with a wedge and the nitrogen with a hash, so the ring is trans-1,3-disubstituted. I worked out the @/@@ assignment from the drawn wedge and hash geometry.
- The amine nitrogen is bonded to a carbon of a 2,6-diazaspiro[3.3]heptane. The other nitrogen of that spiro system carries a straight chain of five CH2 groups ending in an O.

**Right fragment.** The chain oxygen is attached at the 5-position of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its imide nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. That C3 is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo label.
