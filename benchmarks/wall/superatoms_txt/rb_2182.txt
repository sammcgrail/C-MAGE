This SMILES is my reading of the drawing, written as a peptide chain from the N-terminus. I assigned L-type stereochemistry to the amino-acid centres by convention rather than reading each wedge, so those stereo marks are an assumption. I checked the Thr centres against L-threonine.

**Chain as read, left to right:**
1. The N-terminus is acetyl-Pen(SH), where Pen is penicillamine, drawn as C(CH3)2–SH.
2. Next come Asn, Thr and 7-methyl-Trp.
3. Then Lys with an acetylated side-chain amine (NHAc), a second Pen(SH), and Tyr with its phenol O-alkylated as OCH2CH2NH2.
4. Then 2-naphthylalanine, whose NH is bonded to the 4-position of a tetrahydropyran ring.
5. That ring carbon carries a ketone, C(=O)CH2, leading to a stereo CH. I transcribed this fragment exactly as drawn.
6. The stereo CH has a (CH2)3–NH side chain and a C(=O)NH link to the next residue.
7. The chain continues with Asn, then 3-pyridylalanine, then N-methylglycinamide, N(CH3)CH2C(=O)NH2.

**Side-chain linker:** The (CH2)3–NH chain carries two repeats of C(=O)CH2OCH2CH2OCH2CH2NH, then C(=O)CH2CH2–CH(COOH)–NH (a γ-linked glutamic acid). The glutamic acid NH is acylated by a C18 diacid, C(=O)(CH2)16COOH. I counted 16 CH2 groups from the zigzag spacing in the drawing.
