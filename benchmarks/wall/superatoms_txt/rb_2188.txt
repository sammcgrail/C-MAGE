I read the structure region by region and wrote the SMILES from that. Several stereocentres were hard to read from the drawing, so those are uncertain.

**Glycan arms (two identical)**
- Each is a tetrahydropyran carrying a 2-phosphonoethyl group, three OH groups and a 3-carbon chain.
- The wedges and hashes give an α-mannose-type (C-glycoside) configuration.
- Each chain ends in an oxime: –CH2CH2CH2–O–N=C(CH3)–CH2CH2–C(=O)–. I left the oxime C=N geometry unspecified because the drawing shows none.

**Linker**
- The first oxime is acylated onto the ε-NH of Lys A.
- The second oxime is acylated onto the α-NH of Lys A.
- Lys A's carbonyl is bonded to the ε-NH of Lys B, which carries a C-terminal primary amide.
- Lys B's α-NH is bonded to Gly, then to the carbonyl of Cys.

**Macrocycle**
- The Cys side-chain CH2 is bonded through sulfur to the CH2 of a chloroacetyl-type cap (–S–CH2–C(=O)–) on the N-terminal Phe.
- Reading N→C, the ring sequence is: cap-Phe, Val, Pro, Thr, Thr, Bip, N-Me-Ala, Bip, N-Me-Ala, Glu (free CO2H), Ala, Pro, then back to Cys.
- Bip is 4-biphenylalanine.

**Stereochemistry**
- I took every wedge or hash literally, which gives D-Phe, D-Thr (both), D-Bip (both) and D-Glu.
- Val, Cys, N-Me-Ala (both), the Lys residues and the second glycan ring follow directly from the drawn wedges.
- The prolines and the Ala next to the Glu were not clear from the drawing, so I assumed L.
- The Thr β-carbons are assigned by analogy rather than read directly.
