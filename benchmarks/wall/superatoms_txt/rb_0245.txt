This SMILES describes the drawing as a rifamycin-type ansamycin whose naphthoquinone core is fused to a phenoxazine ring that carries a 2-(imidazol-1-yl)ethoxy group. I wrote it by reading the structure region by region. I took the macrocycle stereochemistry and double-bond geometry from the known natural rifamycin pattern (as in rifampicin), not by re-deriving each wedge and hash from the image, so those assignments are less certain than the connectivity.

- **Ansa chain:** The NH amide carries a dienoyl unit, C(=O)–C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH. This leads into the polyketide chain with its secondary methyls, two OH groups, the OAc and the OMe, and ends in the enol ether O–CH=CH.
- **Furanone:** The quaternary carbon bears a methyl and is bonded to the ring oxygen, the C=O and the enol-ether oxygen.
- **Core:** The aromatic ring has a methyl and an OH, and it is fused to a quinone ring with a C=O next to the NH-bearing carbon. That quinone ring is fused in turn to the phenoxazine (an N= and an O bridge to the benzo ring).
- **Side chain:** The benzo ring carries O–CH2CH2–N(imidazole).

I checked the ring closures and valences by hand, and the macrocycle closes through ring label 1.
