I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type degrader: a spiro-oxindole MDM2-binding unit joined through a benzamide and a PEG linker to a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**Fragments, region by region**
- **Spiro core:** the pyrrolidine ring has an NH, a CH–C(=O)NH carboxamide carbon, a CH bearing the aryl group, and two spiro carbons. One spiro carbon is shared with a 4,4-dimethylcyclohexane and the other with a 6-chloro-oxindole.
- **Aryl group:** a 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl on the pyrrolidine CH.
- **Linker:** the carboxamide NH is attached to a para-substituted benzene. Its other substituent is a C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH chain.
- **Cereblon ligand:** the chain ends at the 4-position of a phthalimide ring, which is N-linked to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it unspecified.

**Stereochemistry**
- I assigned the wedge and hash centres from the drawing: the carboxamide CH, the aryl CH, and the oxindole spiro carbon.
- The cyclohexane spiro carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but has plain bonds, so it carries no stereo label.
