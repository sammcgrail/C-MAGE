The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- Br on the carbon next to the ring nitrogen (C6).
- OH on C3.
- A carboxamide on C2, the other carbon next to the nitrogen.

The amide nitrogen carries a CH(CH3) group attached to a 4-methoxyphenyl ring. The stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so I include no stereochemistry.

Writing the SMILES from the pyridine ring first: the C2 carbon bears the amide, then the ring continues through C3 (OH), C4, C5 and C6 (Br), and closes back through the nitrogen.
