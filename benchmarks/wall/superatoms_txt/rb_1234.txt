I read the structure as a PROTAC with a covalent KRAS G12C-type warhead joined through a PEG linker to a VHL ligand, and wrote the SMILES in those three parts.

**Warhead (left side).**
- A quinazoline core carries an acryloyl-piperazine at C4.
- It has a 2-NH substituent, an 8-fluoro group, a 6-chloro group, and a 3-hydroxynaphthalen-1-yl group at C7.

**Linker.**
- The 2-NH is attached to –CH2CH2C(=O)N(CH3)–.
- Next comes a chain of five ethylene-oxy units: N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH. I counted five ether oxygens along the drawn chain.

**VHL ligand (right side).**
- The linker amide NH connects to tert-leucine, then to 4-hydroxyproline, then to an (S)-1-[4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)phenyl]ethylamine.

**Stereochemistry.** I took the stereocentres to match the standard VHL ligand configuration: tert-leucine S, hydroxyproline (2S,4R) and the methylbenzylamine carbon S. I wrote the chirality tags from that convention, adjusted for atom order in my SMILES, rather than re-deriving each wedge from the drawing.
