I read this as a lovastatin-type core, a hexahydronaphthalene with two ring methyls and a 4-hydroxy-lactone side chain. The core carries a carbamate at C1 in place of the usual ester. The carbamate nitrogen is N-methylated and linked through a diethylene-glycol-type chain (NCCOCCOCC) to the 4-amino position of a thalidomide-type phthalimide, which bears the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The result is a statin–thalidomide conjugate.

I did not assign each stereocentre independently from the wedges. Instead I took the stereo descriptors from the known lovastatin/simvastatin SMILES, since the drawn core, methyl positions and lactone match that scaffold. I replaced the ester oxygen's acyl group with the carbamate. The chirality tags depend only on neighbour order, so they carry over unchanged. The piperidinedione C3 is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
