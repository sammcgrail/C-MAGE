The structure is a meta-disubstituted benzene (1,3-phenylenediamine) carrying two different nitrogen substituents.

**Substituents on the central benzene ring**
- One nitrogen carries an ethyl group (CH2CH3) and a CH2 linked to a naphthalen-1-yl group.
- The other nitrogen carries a p-tolyl group (4-methylphenyl) and a naphthalen-2-yl group.

**Naphthyl attachment points**
- The right-hand naphthalene is attached through CH2 at the carbon adjacent to the ring fusion, so it is a 1-naphthyl (1-naphthylmethyl) group.
- The lower naphthalene is attached through the nitrogen at a carbon one position away from the ring fusion, so it is a 2-naphthyl group.
