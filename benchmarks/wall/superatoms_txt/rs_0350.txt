The drawing shows a quinoline core with three substituents:

- C4 carries an NH group linked to a pyridin-4-yl ring.
- C3 carries a fluorine.
- C2 carries a 4-cyanophenyl group.

Writing the SMILES from C4 around the quinoline ring: the 4-aminopyridine fragment is c1cnccc1N, attached through N to C4, giving N(c1ccncc1)c1c(F)c(-c2ccc(C#N)cc2)nc2ccccc12.

Ring check for the quinoline: C4 is c1, then C3 is c(F), then C2 is c(-aryl), then n, then the fused carbon c2, then cccc, then the closing fused carbon c12. That is 10 ring atoms, as expected.
