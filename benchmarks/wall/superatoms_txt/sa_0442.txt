The drawing shows diisopropyl azodicarboxylate (DIAD): iPr–O–C(=O)–N=N–C(=O)–O–iPr, where iPr is isopropyl, CH(CH3)2.

The N=N double bond is drawn without any cis/trans geometry, so I left stereochemistry out. The drawing places the two nitrogens in a zigzag, which would suggest the trans (E) isomer. Since the instruction is to include stereo only where it is actually shown, I wrote the SMILES with no stereo marks.

SMILES: CC(C)OC(=O)N=NC(=O)OC(C)C
