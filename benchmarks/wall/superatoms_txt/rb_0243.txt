This is a rifamycin-type ansa macrolactam fused to a phenoxazine. The phenoxazine carries a methylsulfonyl group and a 2-(dimethylamino)ethoxy group.

I read the structure in three parts:

- **Chromophore:** a naphtho-furanone core (a quaternary C bearing a methyl, a lactone C=O, the ring O and the chain O) fused to the phenoxazine. The phenoxazine ring has an N= and a ring O, with the SO2Me next to the ring N-bearing carbon.
- **Ansa chain:** starting from the amide NH, the chain runs C(=O)–C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–CH(OAc)–CH(CH3)–CH(OMe)–CH=CH–O, ending on the quaternary carbon.
- **Stereochemistry:** I did not derive each centre from the wedges and hashes. I took the chain and quaternary-centre stereo from the known rifamycin/rifampicin SMILES, which this drawing matches in connectivity. The quaternary-carbon tag was re-derived for my atom order.

The double-bond geometry follows the drawing.
