Reading the structure region by region:

**Thienopyridinone (top right).** This is a thieno[3,2-c]pyridin-4-one. The N-methyl nitrogen is bonded to the C=O carbon (C4) and to CH=, which is C6. C7 carries the aryl group and is bonded to C7a, which bears the sulfur. C3a connects to the carbonyl carbon and to C3 (CH). C2 carries OMe and is bonded to S.

SMILES for this part: O=C1N(C)C=C(Ar)C2=C1C=C(OC)S2

**Central aryl ring.** The ring carries the thienopyridinone at C1, OMe at C3 and C5, and the CH2 at C4. The CH2 is attached to the piperidine nitrogen.

**Spiro system.** The piperidine and azetidine share a spiro carbon. The piperidine is N, CH2, CH2, C(spiro), CH2, CH2. The azetidine is N, CH2, C(spiro), CH2. This is a 2,7-diazaspiro[3.5]nonane. The azetidine nitrogen is attached to the isoindolinone aryl ring.

**Isoindolinone.** The C=O is at C1, N2 carries the glutarimide, and C3 is CH2. The azetidine nitrogen sits on the benzo ring at the position para to the CH2-bearing carbon (C3a). Following the drawing, the ring carbon attached to the C=O (C7a) is bonded to a CH, then to the carbon bearing the azetidine N. So the substituent is at C5 or C6. The carbon bearing N is two bonds from C7a, via the CH, which makes it C6 in isoindolinone numbering. In the picture, the C=O-bearing carbon is at the top of the ring, the CH is at its upper left, and the N-bearing carbon is at the left. The CH2-bearing carbon is at the bottom, and the remaining CH carbons are at the bottom left and right. This is consistent.

**Glutarimide.** N-C3H attached to C(=O)NHC(=O)CH2CH2. No stereochemistry is drawn.
