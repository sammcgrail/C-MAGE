I read the molecule in four parts and joined them at the tertiary amine.

1. **Left end:** a methyl ester (CH3–O–C(=O)–CH2) is attached to a benzene ring at the meta position. The other meta substituent is an ether oxygen leading into a three-carbon chain, O–CH2CH2CH2–N.
2. **Amine nitrogen:** it carries three groups: the propyl chain, a benzyl group, and a CH2–CH(OAc)Ph arm.
3. **Benzyl group:** the benzene ring has Cl at the ortho position and CF3 at the adjacent meta position, counted from the CH2 attachment point.
4. **Stereocentre:** the CH bearing the acetoxy group and the phenyl ring is the only centre with a wedge or hash (the C–O bond is hashed). The priorities are O > CH2N > phenyl > H. With the O behind the plane and the implicit H toward the viewer, the centre comes out as R. In SMILES, `CC(=O)O[C@H](c1ccccc1)CN...` encodes this R configuration.

SMILES: CC(=O)O[C@H](c1ccccc1)CN(CCCOc1cccc(CC(=O)OC)c1)Cc1cccc(C(F)(F)F)c1Cl
