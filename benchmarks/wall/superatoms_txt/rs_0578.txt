The drawing shows a thioxanthen-9-one: a central ring with a C=O at the top and S at the bottom, fused to a plain benzene ring on the left and a substituted benzene ring on the right.

On the right ring, the carbon fused next to C=O is C9a. Going around from there: C1 (the CH at the top left of the ring), C2 (bearing the upper Et), C3 (CH), C4 (bearing the lower Et, adjacent to the S-bearing carbon C4a). So the substituents are 2,4-diethyl, as in 2,4-diethylthioxanthen-9-one.

SMILES, written from the carbonyl carbon: O=C1c2ccccc2Sc2c(CC)cc(CC)cc12
