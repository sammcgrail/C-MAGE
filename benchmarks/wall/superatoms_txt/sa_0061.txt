The drawing shows Cbz-NH on a central carbon that also carries a CH2Ph group and a CO2Me group, which is the methyl ester of N-Cbz-phenylalanine.

- **Substituents:** Cbz is a benzyloxycarbonyl group, so the nitrogen carries C(=O)OCH2Ph. Ph is a phenyl ring, and CO2Me is a methyl ester.
- **Stereochemistry:** Only the CO2Me bond is a wedge, so the centre is defined and the implicit hydrogen points away from the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2Ph > H. On the page, N (lower left) → CO2Me (right) → CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise, which gives S. This is the L-phenylalanine configuration.
- **SMILES:** Writing the centre as C(=O)[C@H](CH2Ph)N reproduces the S configuration, which I checked against the known L-Phe SMILES N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)O by swapping the N and carbonyl neighbours.
