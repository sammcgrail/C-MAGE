I read the molecule as a pyridazine-fused bicyclic piperazine core (carrying a 2-hydroxyphenyl group) linked through two piperidines to a thalidomide-type unit.

**Left-hand core**
- The pyridazine ring carries a 2-hydroxyphenyl at the carbon next to one ring N.
- The pyridazine is fused to a piperazine, which is fused to a second piperazine.
- The first piperazine has an NH and a CH2, which leads to the stereocentre.
- The stereocentre is the ring-fusion CH. Its wedge points to the CH2 that bears the NH.
- The other ring-fusion atom is a tertiary N bonded to the pyridazine carbon.
- The second piperazine's remaining N is substituted with a CH2 linker.

**Linker**
- That CH2 goes to the 4-position of a piperidine.
- The piperidine N is bonded to a CH2, which is bonded to the quaternary C4 of a second piperidine.
- This C4 also carries a methyl group.

**Right-hand end**
- The second piperidine N is attached to the 5-position of the isoindoline-1,3-dione.
- The isoindoline N carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

**Stereochemistry**
- With the H pointing away from the viewer, the wedge CH2 (the NH–CH2 side), the ring CH2 toward N7 and the ring-fusion N run clockwise in the drawing.
- The CIP priorities are ring-fusion N, then the CH2 toward N7, then the NH–CH2, which gives (S).
- In the SMILES, the neighbour order a (NH–CH2), H, ring-closure N, CH2 with `@` corresponds to this (S) arrangement.
