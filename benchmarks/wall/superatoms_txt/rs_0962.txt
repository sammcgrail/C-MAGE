The drawing shows a pyridine ring with N at the upper left. Reading around the ring:

- The carbon next to N at the top bears no substituent.
- The next carbon carries the OH.
- The next carbon carries the carboxamide, C(=O)N.
- The remaining two carbons are plain CH.

So the pyridine ring is N1=CC(O)=C(C(=O)N...)C=C1, with the OH at position 3 and the amide at position 4. This is a 3-hydroxyisonicotinamide.

The amide nitrogen is NH and is bonded to a CH(CH3) carbon, which is bonded to a para-substituted phenyl ring carrying CF3. The CH is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereochemistry.

SMILES: CC(NC(=O)c1ccncc1O)c1ccc(cc1)C(F)(F)F
