This molecule is a PROTAC-type compound: a VHL E3-ligase ligand joined through a spiro-azetidine/cyclohexyl/pyrimidine linker to a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane carrying an aminopyridazine-phenol. I read it in these pieces:

- **VHL ligand:** a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamide of a 4-hydroxyproline. The proline nitrogen is acylated by tert-leucine (tert-butyl on the alpha carbon). The alpha-carbon wedge and the hydroxyproline wedge/hash are written with the standard VHL configuration, (2S,4R) for the hydroxyproline.
- **Linker:** the tert-leucine NH is acylated by a 2-azaspiro[3.3]heptane-6-carboxylic acid. The azetidine nitrogen sits on a cyclohexane, and the cyclohexane's other carbon is attached to C5 of a pyrimidine. The cyclohexane is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.
- **Bicycle:** the pyrimidine C2 is bonded to N8, the one-atom bridge of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane. N3 of the bicycle carries the pyridazine.
- **Pyridazine end group:** the pyridazine has an NH2 on the carbon next to the ring nitrogens and a 2-hydroxyphenyl on the other carbon beside the ring nitrogens. The bicycle's N3 is on the ring carbon adjacent to the NH2 carbon.
