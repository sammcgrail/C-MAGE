I read this as a dimer of MMAF-type auristatin units joined through a branched PEG linker that ends in a propiolamide. I wrote the SMILES from the drawing region by region and did not run it through a validator.

**Linker core.** A central tertiary amine carries three arms:
- A GABA-type arm: C(=O)CH2CH2CH2NH–C(=O)C≡CH, ending in the terminal alkyne amide.
- A short arm: CH2CH2C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–C(=O)–, which is acylated onto the N-methyl-valine nitrogen of the lower auristatin unit.
- A long arm: CH2CH2C(=O)NH–(PEG2)–C(=O)NH–CH(CH3)–C(=O)NH–CH2CH2CH2–C(=O)–, which is acylated onto the N-methyl-valine nitrogen of the upper auristatin unit. The alanine carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo.

**Auristatin units.** Each unit runs N-Me-Val, then Val, then N-Me-dolaisoleuine (Dil), then dolaproine (Dap), then Phe.
- The upper unit ends as the Phe methyl ester (CO2Me).
- The lower unit ends as the free Phe acid (CO2H).

**Stereochemistry.** The wedges and hashes match the natural auristatin configuration, so I took the stereo descriptors from the standard MMAE/MMAF pattern. I kept the standard atom order for the upper unit. For the lower unit I wrote the chain in the opposite direction and re-derived each @/@@ by permutation parity.
