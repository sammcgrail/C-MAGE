This is delavirdine, a structure I know. I checked each region against the drawing.

- **Pyridine ring:** N at the bottom, with C2 bearing the piperazine and C3 bearing NH-iPr.
- **Piperazine:** one N is attached to the pyridine C2, the other N to the carbonyl.
- **Carbonyl:** C(=O) links the piperazine N to the indole C2.
- **Indole:** the NH sits at the bottom. The sulfonamide NHSO2Me is on the benzene ring at C5, which is the carbon para to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the indole N.

Position check on the indole: c1 (C2) is followed by cc2, so C3 is the CH and C3a is the ring-fusion carbon. After C3a comes c (C4, CH), then c(NHSO2Me) (C5), then cc (C6 and C7), then c2 (C7a) and [nH]. The substituent is therefore at C5, which matches the drawing, where the substituted carbon is attached to the CH next to C3a.
