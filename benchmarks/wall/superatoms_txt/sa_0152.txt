The drawing shows a sulfur atom with two double-bonded oxygens (S=O on the left and right). It also has an OTBS group on top and a CF3 group on the bottom.

TBS is tert-butyldimethylsilyl, which is [Si](C)(C)C(C)(C)C. The OTBS group is therefore O[Si](C)(C)C(C)(C)C.

The full structure is the TBS ester of triflic acid, with S bonded to O–TBS, CF3 and two =O.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)[Si](C)(C)OS(=O)(=O)C(F)(F)F
