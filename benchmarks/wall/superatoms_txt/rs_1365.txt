Reading the structure region by region:

**Left fragment.** This is an imidazo[4,5-b]pyridin-2-one. The C=O carbon is bonded to an NH and to an N that carries the piperidine. That N is attached to the pyridine ring carbon adjacent to the pyridine N. The NH is attached to the other fusion carbon.
- SMILES for this part: O=C1Nc2cccnc2N1C3CCN(CC3)...
- The pyridine N sits next to the fusion carbon that bears the substituted N (N3), so the fusion carbon on the NH side is C3a, and c2cccnc2 is the correct ring order.

**Linker.** The piperidine N connects through CH2–CH(OH)–CH2 to N1 of the pyrazole.

**Right fragment.** This is a 4,5,6,7-tetrahydropyrazolo[4,3-c]pyridine.
- N1 is bonded to N2, and N2 is double-bonded to C3.
- C3 carries the 4-CF3-phenyl group and is bonded to C3a.
- C3a is bonded to CH2 and then to N5, which carries the methanesulfonyl group (S(=O)(=O)C).
- N5 is also bonded to CH2, then CH2, then C7a, which is bonded to N1.
- C7a = C3a is the ring double bond.
