This reads the drawing as a resin-bound linear peptide, written from the N-terminus to the resin-linked C-terminus:

H-Thp-Gly – Ile – Cys(Trt) – Ser(tBu) – Arg(Pbf) – Ser(tBu) – Leu – Pro – Oic – Ile – Cys(Trt) – Ile – O-resin

- **Thp-Gly:** the N-terminal residue is a glycine bearing a tetrahydropyran-4-yl group, with a free NH2.
- **Ile and Cys(Trt):** the isoleucines carry an ethyl and a methyl branch. Each cysteine sulfur carries a trityl group (S–C(Ph)3).
- **Ser(tBu):** both serines are O-tert-butyl ethers.
- **Arg(Pbf):** the guanidine is sulfonylated by the 2,2,4,6,7-pentamethyl-2,3-dihydrobenzofuran-5-sulfonyl group.
- **Leu, Pro, Oic:** leucine and proline are standard; the residue after proline is octahydroindole-2-carboxylic acid (Oic), a fused six/five-membered ring with two ring-fusion H atoms.
- **C-terminus:** the last isoleucine is attached through its carboxyl oxygen to the resin bead, written here as O[*].

I assumed natural L-configuration for the amino acids, and I did not check every wedge and hash against that. The Oic ring-fusion stereochemistry is a best guess and may not match the drawing.
