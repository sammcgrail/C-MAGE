**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H. Its meta position is bonded to the second ring. On the second ring, the carbon next to the biaryl bond bears OH, and the next carbon bears the NH of the hydrazone. The OH and NH substituents are therefore ortho to each other, and the biaryl bond is ortho to the OH.
