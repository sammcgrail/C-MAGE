I read the molecule as a DOTA-type macrocycle linked through a 4-aminopiperidine to a peptide chain that ends in prolinamide. I checked each stereocentre against the wedges and hashes.

**Fragments, left to right**
- **Macrocycle:** a 12-membered ring with four nitrogens (cyclen). Three nitrogens carry CH2COOH arms, and the fourth carries CH2C(=O)NH– to the next fragment.
- **Linker:** a piperidine joined through its C4 amine to that acetamide, with its ring nitrogen bearing CH2C(=O)–.
- **Peptide chain:** D-Phe, then L-Gln, then α-methyl-L-Trp (a quaternary carbon bearing a methyl, drawn with a wedge to the CH2). Next come L-Ala, L-tert-leucine (a tert-butyl side chain), Gly and 5-fluoro-L-Trp.
- **C-terminus:** a reduced leucine, –NH–CH(CH2CH(CH3)2)–CH2–, in which the carbonyl is replaced by CH2 and the carbon is R as drawn. This CH2 is bonded to the nitrogen of an L-prolinamide, N1CCC[C@H]1C(=O)N.

**Stereochemistry as read from the drawing**
- D-Phe is R, and the L-amino acids (Gln, α-Me-Trp, Ala, tert-leucine, 5F-Trp, Pro) are all S.
- The reduced-leucine carbon comes out R from the wedge on its isobutyl bond.
