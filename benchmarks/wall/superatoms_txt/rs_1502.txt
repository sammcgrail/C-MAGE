The drawing shows a 4-cyanobenzenesulfonamide whose nitrogen is attached to a substituted phenyl ring.

**Sulfonyl side:** a benzene ring carries a nitrile (NC, drawn at the upper left) at the para position and an SO2 group at the other end. The sulfur bears two oxygens (drawn as =O) and is bonded to the NH.

**Aniline ring:** numbering from the carbon bonded to NH as C1:

- Going clockwise, C2 (top) bears a methyl.
- C3 (upper right) bears the pyrrolidin-1-yl group.
- C4 (lower right) bears a methyl.
- C5 (bottom) is CH.
- C6 (lower left) bears a methyl.

**Pyrrolidine:** a five-membered ring (N plus four CH2) attached through its nitrogen to C3.
