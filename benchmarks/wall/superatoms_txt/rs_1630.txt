The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- C2 carries the carboxamide, C(=O)NH-aryl.
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- N1 is the ring nitrogen, bonded to C2.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a benzene ring. At the ortho position of that ring there is an oxygen, which links to a second benzene ring. That second ring carries a CN group ortho to the oxygen.

Writing the SMILES from the pyridine: COc1ccnc(C(=O)Nc2ccccc2Oc2ccccc2C#N)c1O

Checking the pyridine ring atom by atom: c1 (C4, bearing OMe), c (C5), c (C6), n (N1), c (C2, bearing the amide), then c1O (C3, bearing OH), which closes the ring back to C4. This matches the drawing.
