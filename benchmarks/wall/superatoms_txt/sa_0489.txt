I read the structure region by region and expanded the SEM abbreviation.

**Thiophene.** The ring is S–C2(CO2H)=C3(F)–C4(imidazolyl)=C5H, with C5 bonded back to S. The carboxylic acid sits on C2, fluorine on C3, and the imidazole on C4.

**Imidazole.** N1 carries the SEM group. C2 bonds to the cyclopenta ring carbon. N3 is the double-bonded ring nitrogen, C4 is a CH, and C5 attaches to the thiophene.

**SEM.** This is 2-(trimethylsilyl)ethoxymethyl, written as CH2OCH2CH2Si(CH3)3. It is attached to N1 through the CH2 of the OCH2 end: N–CH2–O–CH2CH2–Si(CH3)3.

**Cyclopenta[b]pyridine.** The ring CH (C7) is bonded to the imidazole C2, to a CH2, and to the pyridine carbon C7a. The CH2–CH2 pair leads to C4a. In the pyridine, C7a is bonded to N, N is bonded to CH (C2), C2 to C3 (which bears the aryl group), C3 to C4 (CH), and C4 to C4a.

**Aryl group.** The phenyl ring is attached to pyridine C3 at its C1. Its ortho carbon carries the tetrazol-1-yl group, and Cl sits para to that tetrazole position. Numbering the phenyl from the pyridine-bearing carbon, c1 is attached to the pyridine, c2 carries the tetrazole, and the ring runs c3, c4, c5(Cl), c6. In the image the Cl carbon is bonded to the CH that is adjacent to the pyridine-bearing carbon, so Cl is meta to the pyridine attachment and para to the tetrazole attachment.

**Tetrazole.** It is attached through N1 to the phenyl. The ring is n1cnnn1, with N1 bonded to CH, which is bonded to N, then N, then N, closing back to N1.
