This SMILES is my best reading of the drawing. The stereochemistry is the least certain part: I worked out the sugar centres from the wedges and hashes, but for most residues I assumed the natural L configuration rather than confirming it from the drawing.

**Sugar end.** A 1,6-anhydro bicyclic acetal carries NHAc at C2 and OH groups at C3 and C4. The quaternary C5 carries a CH2–O linker, which gives CH2–(OCH2CH2)₄ before the triazole. Stereo tags for C1–C5 were derived from the wedges and hashes shown.

**Linker.** The chain runs as follows:
- A 1,4-disubstituted triazole.
- CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)CH2CH2.
- (OCH2CH2)₄.
- C(=O)–NH.

**Lysine.** The linker amide is followed by four CH2 groups to Lys Cα, which is C-terminal CONH2. Its Nα is acylated by Gly.

**Macrocycle.** Gly leads to Cys, whose sulfur forms a thioether with CH2C(=O). Reading from that thioacetyl N-terminus, the ring is Phe–Val–Pro–Thr–Thr–Bip–N-MeAla–Bip–N-MeAla–Glu–Ala–Pro, closing back onto the Cys nitrogen. Bip is 4-biphenylalanine and Glu has a free CO2H.
