I read the drawing as a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core carrying three substituents.

- **N1 of the pyrazinone:** bears a CH(CH2CH3)(CH2OCH3) group. The stereocentre has a hashed H, so the H points away from the viewer.
- **C5 of the pyrazinone:** carries Br.
- **C3 of the pyrazinone:** carries an indolin-1-yl group. The indoline has OMe at C5 and a methyl at C7, which sit on the benzene ring.

For the stereocentre, the CIP priorities are N > CH2OMe > CH2CH3 > H. With H pointing away, the path from N (down) to CH2O (upper right) to ethyl (upper left) runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. In the SMILES written as CC[C@@H](COC)N…, the neighbour order is ethyl, H, CH2O, N, and `@@` encodes this S configuration.
