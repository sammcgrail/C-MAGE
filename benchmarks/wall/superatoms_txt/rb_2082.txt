I read the molecule as a bifunctional degrader. A Trk-inhibitor-like fragment is joined through a piperazine–piperidine linker to a thalidomide-type group.

**Fragments, left to right:**
- **Aryl group:** a 3-fluorophenyl ring.
- **Pyrrolidine:** attached to the aryl group at C2 by a hashed bond, and through its nitrogen to C6 of an imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine.
- **Pyridine:** the imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine C3 carries a pyridin-2-yl group, and the pyridine's 6-position carries a piperazine.
- **Linker:** the far piperazine nitrogen is bonded to a CH2 group, which is bonded to C4 of a piperidine.
- **Isoindoline-1,3-dione:** the piperidine nitrogen is bonded to C5 of this ring.
- **Glutarimide:** the imide nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. This carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.

**Stereocentre:** only the pyrrolidine C2 has a wedge or hash. The aryl bond is hashed (pointing back), so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > aryl C > ring CH2 > H. In the drawing, N is to the east, aryl to the north and CH2 to the southwest, which runs counterclockwise. With H toward the viewer, the true configuration is the reverse, so it is R. In the SMILES, `[C@H]` with the neighbour order aryl, H, N, CH2 gives R.
