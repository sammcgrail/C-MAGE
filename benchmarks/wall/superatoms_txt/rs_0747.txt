The drawing shows a 2-methoxy-5-methylbenzenesulfonamide whose nitrogen is attached to a substituted phenyl ring.

**Left ring (sulfonyl side):** The carbon bearing S is C1. The carbon next to it bears OMe (C2). The methyl sits on the lower-left carbon of the ring. Going C1 (S), C6 (lower left, CH), C5 (methyl), C4, C3, C2 (OMe), the methyl is at position 5, which is para to the OMe. This gives 2-methoxy-5-methylbenzenesulfonyl.

**Right ring (N-aryl side):** The carbon bearing N is C1'. Going around the ring:
- The upper carbon, C2', carries a methyl.
- The next carbon, C3', carries the pyrrolidine nitrogen.
- C4' (lower right) carries a methyl.
- C5' is CH.
- C6' (lower left) carries a methyl.

**SMILES:** Writing the sulfonamide with the left ring first and the N-aryl ring after it gives COc1ccc(C)cc1S(=O)(=O)Nc1c(C)c(N2CCCC2)c(C)cc1C.
