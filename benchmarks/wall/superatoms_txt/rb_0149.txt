I read this as a rifamycin-type ansa macrolide fused to a phenoxazine ring (a rifamycin S–aminophenol condensation product).

**Core.** The aromatic part is a naphthoquinone-like ring system. A furanone ring carries a quaternary carbon with a methyl and the ether oxygen (wedge bond). A methyl and a hydroxyl sit on the benzene ring. A phenoxazine unit carries a hydroxyl on its outer benzene ring and is fused to the quinone ring. The quinone ring bears the amide NH.

**Ansa chain.** Starting from the amide nitrogen, the chain runs:
- C(=O)–C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH
- then CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OAc), CH(CH3), CH(OMe)
- then CH=CH–O, which returns to the quaternary ketal carbon.

**Double bonds.** The CH=CH next to the methyl-bearing alkene is trans. The methyl-bearing alkene is drawn cis between the carbonyl carbon and the following chain carbon, so I wrote it as Z.

**Stereocentres.** I assigned the `@`/`@@` tags by analogy with natural rifamycin rather than by tracing each wedge in the drawing, so those tags are less certain than the connectivity.
