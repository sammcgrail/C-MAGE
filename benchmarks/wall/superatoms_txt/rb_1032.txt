I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule: a SMARCA2-binding aminopyridazine-phenol head group joined through a linker to a VHL ligand. I assembled the SMILES in these pieces, left to right:

- **Head group:** a 3-amino-6-(2-hydroxyphenyl)pyridazine, attached at C4 to N3 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane.
- **Bicycle to pyrimidine:** the bridging N8 of the bicycle is bonded to C2 of a pyrimidine.
- **Linker:** the pyrimidine C5 carries a piperidin-4-yl group. The piperidine N is joined by a propyl chain to an O on C3 of an isoxazole.
- **VHL ligand:** the isoxazole C5 bears the carbon that carries the isopropyl group and the C=O. That C=O is bonded to the N of a 4-hydroxyproline, whose amide N is attached to a benzyl group carrying a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl substituent.

For stereochemistry, I took the hydroxyproline as the standard VHL (2S,4R) configuration.

For the side-chain carbon, the hashed bond to the isopropyl group makes it S by CIP priorities (C=O, then isoxazole C5, then isopropyl, then H). That corresponds to `[C@H]` in the SMILES order used.
