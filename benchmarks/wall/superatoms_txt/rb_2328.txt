I read the image as the peptide named under it, H-Cpa-cyclo[D-Cys-Tyr-D-Aph(Cit)-Lys-Thr-Cys]-D-Tyr-NH2, and wrote it residue by residue from the N-terminus.

- **N-terminus:** 4-chlorophenylalanine (Cpa) with a free amine, attached to the first Cys.
- **Ring:** D-Cys, Tyr, D-Aph(Cit), Lys, Thr and Cys are linked in that order. The two Cys side chains are joined by a disulfide bond (ring closure label 2 in the SMILES).
- **Aph(Cit):** a 4-aminophenylalanine whose ring nitrogen is acylated by citrulline. The citrulline keeps a free α-amine and a terminal ureido group, NHC(=O)NH2.
- **C-terminus:** D-Tyr as a primary amide, attached to the last Cys.

I did not read the wedges and hashes atom by atom. I took the stereochemistry from the residue names: L for Cpa, Tyr, Lys, Thr, Cys and Cit, and D for the first Cys, Aph and the C-terminal Tyr. The Cit configuration in particular is an assumption.
