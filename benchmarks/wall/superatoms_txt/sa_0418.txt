This drawing is Fmoc-protected tryptophan, with the Fmoc group on the α-amine, the free carboxylic acid, and the indole side chain.

**Stereochemistry:** The only stereo marker is the hashed bond from the α-carbon to the CO2H group. I assigned priorities N > CO2H > CH2(indolyl) > H. The hashed CO2H points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Viewed from the front, N → CO2H → CH2 runs clockwise. Because the H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, which gives counterclockwise, so the centre is S. This matches the natural L-tryptophan configuration.

**SMILES:** I wrote the chirality tag by converting from the known L-Trp form N[C@@H](Cc1c[nH]c2ccccc12)C(=O)O. Starting with the carboxylic acid instead swaps the N and CO2H neighbours in the neighbour order, which flips @@ to @.
