I read this as a cyclosporin A–type cyclic undecapeptide whose MeBmt side chain has been modified and linked through an amide to a lapatinib-like quinazoline.

**Macrocycle.** Going around the ring from the modified amino acid, the residues are:
- the modified MeBmt, which is N‑methylated and carries a CH(OH) group
- Abu (ethyl side chain)
- Sar
- N‑Me‑Leu
- Val
- N‑Me‑Leu
- Ala
- D‑Ala
- N‑Me‑Leu
- N‑Me‑Leu
- N‑Me‑Val, which closes back onto the MeBmt nitrogen

**Side chain on the modified residue.**
- The CH(OH) carbon bears a CH(CH3) carbon, then three CH2 groups, then C(=O)NH.
- The amide nitrogen continues as CH2CH2–NH–CH2, which is attached to a 2,5‑disubstituted furan.
- The furan is bonded to C6 of a quinazoline.
- Quinazoline C4 carries an NH–aryl group, where the aryl is 3‑chloro‑4‑[(3‑fluorobenzyl)oxy]phenyl.

**Stereochemistry.** I took the L/D configurations of the ring residues from the cyclosporin pattern rather than reading each wedge. I assigned the MeBmt C2 as S, the CHOH carbon as R and the CH(CH3) carbon as R, which I did not re-check against the individual wedges in the drawing. The Ala next to MeLeu6 is L and the following Ala is D.
