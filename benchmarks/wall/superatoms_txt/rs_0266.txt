The drawing shows a para-disubstituted benzene ring.

- One substituent is N(Boc)(SO2CH3) attached to the ring through nitrogen. Boc is C(=O)OC(C)(C)C, and the methylsulfonyl group is S(=O)(=O)C.
- The other substituent is a three-carbon chain, CH2CH2CH2, ending in O–S(=O)(=O)CH3, a mesylate.

Counting the chain from the ring: the bonds go ring to the first CH2, then the second, then the third, then O. This gives three carbons.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N(S(C)(=O)=O)c1ccc(CCCOS(C)(=O)=O)cc1
