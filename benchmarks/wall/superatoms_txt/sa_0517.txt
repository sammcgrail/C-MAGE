I read the drawing as a cytosine-type base joined to a tetrahydrofuran ring. I derived the three stereocentres from the wedge and hash bonds on the ring.

- **Base:** a 5-fluoro-cytosine ring. The ring nitrogen attached to the sugar is N1, with C2=O and N3. C4 carries an NH–Alloc group and C5 carries F.
- **Alloc:** expanded to allyloxycarbonyl, C(=O)OCC=C, so the substituent is NC(=O)OCC=C.
- **Sugar ring:** a five-membered ring containing O. The anomeric carbon (C1') bonds to N1 and is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label. C2' and C3' each carry an OH on a hashed bond. C4' carries a CO2H group on a wedge bond.

For each stereocentre I took the implicit H as the opposite face to the wedge or hash bond and read the other three neighbours in the order they appear in the SMILES. This gives @ for C4' and @@ for both C3' and C2'.

SMILES: OC(=O)[C@H]1O[CH](n2cc(F)c(NC(=O)OCC=C)nc2=O)[C@@H](O)[C@@H]1O
