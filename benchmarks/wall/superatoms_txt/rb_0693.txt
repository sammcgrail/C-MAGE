I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule: a VHL-ligand half joined by a ten-carbon chain to a KRAS G12C-type quinazoline half.

**VHL-ligand half (left)**
- A 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group is attached to a para-substituted benzene.
- The benzene carries a CH2–NH–C(=O) amide linking to a hydroxyproline ring. The ring has an OH on a wedged bond.
- The proline nitrogen is acylated by a tert-leucine unit (a C(CH3)3 group). That unit's NH is acylated by the linker.
- I used the standard VHL-ligand stereochemistry, (2S,4R)-hydroxyproline and (S)-tert-leucine, which matches the wedges and hashes as drawn.

**Linker**
- From the amide C=O, I counted ten CH2 groups along the zigzag, ending at an ether oxygen.

**Right-hand half**
- The ether oxygen sits on a benzene ring that has F ortho to the biaryl bond.
- That ring is bonded to C7 of a quinazoline. C8 carries F and C6 carries Cl.
- C4 of the quinazoline carries a piperazine, whose far nitrogen is acylated as an acrylamide (C(=O)C=C).
