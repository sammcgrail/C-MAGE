The drawing shows nilutamide: a hydantoin ring (5,5-dimethylimidazolidine-2,4-dione) whose N3 is bonded to a phenyl ring.

**Hydantoin ring:** HN is bonded to a C=O carbon (top) and to a quaternary carbon carrying two methyl groups. The other N is bonded to both C=O carbons (top and bottom) and to the aryl ring. The bottom C=O is bonded to the quaternary carbon.

**Aryl ring:** Number the carbon attached to N as C1. Going around the ring from C1, the vertices are:
- C2, the top-left vertex (CH)
- C3, the top-right vertex (CH)
- C4, the right vertex, bearing NO2
- C5, the bottom-right vertex, bearing CF3
- C6, the bottom-left vertex (CH)

The NO2 group is therefore para to N, and the CF3 group is meta to N and ortho to NO2.
