**Nine-membered ring**, in order from the amide-bearing carbon:
- CH(NH–C(=O)–pyridine)
- CH2
- ring O
- C(=O)
- CH(CH2Ph)
- CH(O–C(=O)–cyclohexyl)
- CH(CH3)
- ring O
- C(=O), which closes back to the CH(N) carbon

**Stereochemistry**, read from the wedges and hashes and assigned by CIP priorities:
- The CH(N) carbon has the hashed NH bond and comes out S.
- The CH(CH2Ph) carbon has the wedged benzyl and comes out R.
- The CH(O-cyclohexanecarbonyl) carbon has the hashed ester oxygen and comes out R.
- The CH(CH3) carbon has the wedged methyl and comes out S.
