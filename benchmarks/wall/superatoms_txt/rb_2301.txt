I read the structure as a head-to-tail macrocyclic peptide, traced from the C-terminal glycinamide to the N-terminal chloroacetyl-Tyr.

**Sequence, N- to C-terminus:** ClAc-Tyr – N-Me-Ala – Asn – Pro – Dap(squarate) – Leu – Hyp – Trp – Dab – Trp(N-CH2COOH) – N-Me-Nle – N-Me-Nle – Leu – Cys – Gly-NH2.

- **Macrocycle closure:** the Tyr nitrogen is acylated by a chloroacetyl-derived CH2C(=O). Its CH2 is bonded to the Cys sulfur, giving a thioether.
- **Dap side chain:** the beta-amino group is attached to a squarate ring (cyclobutene-dione) bearing an OMe group.
- **Hyp:** 4-hydroxyproline.
- **Dab:** the long line to the H2N label passes through two CH2 vertices, so I read it as 2,4-diaminobutyric acid (Dab).
- **N-methylation:** N-Me groups are on Ala and on both norleucines. Each Nle carries an n-butyl side chain.
- **First Trp:** the indole nitrogen carries CH2COOH.
- **Stereochemistry:** from the wedges and hashes, every stereocentre works out to the natural L-configuration. The Hyp 4-OH is 4R, giving (2S,4R). The Cys carbon comes out R only because of the CIP priority of sulfur.
