I read the molecule as a 4-substituted benzoic acid joined through an ethylene linker to the nitrogen of a pyrrolidin-2-one.

- **Left ring:** the γ-lactam (C=O at the top) has a side chain on the carbon next to N.
- **Linker:** N–CH2–CH2–aryl, with the aryl being a para-substituted benzoic acid (CO2H).
- **Side chain:** from the lactam carbon, CH=CH (drawn trans, E), then CH2, then CH(OH).
- **Quaternary carbon:** the carbinol carbon is attached to a cyclobutane carbon that also bears a CH2–cyclopropyl group.
- **Stereocentre:** the OH is on a hashed bond. With priorities OH > quaternary C > CH2 > H, the hashed OH places H toward the viewer. OH → quaternary C → CH2 appears clockwise, which reverses to counterclockwise for the true view, giving S. In the SMILES written below, `[C@H]` corresponds to S.
- **Unmarked centre:** the lactam ring carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

SMILES: OC(=O)c1ccc(CCN2C(=O)CCC2/C=C/C[C@H](O)C2(CC3CC3)CCC2)cc1
