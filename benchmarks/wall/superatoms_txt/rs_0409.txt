I read the molecule as a benzoxazol-2-one core carrying an iodine at C4 and, at C6, a CH2–CH(NH2)–CO2CH3 side chain.

**Ring system:** The five-membered ring has O1, C2(=O) and N3(H), fused to the benzene ring at C3a and C7a. Going round the benzene ring from C3a: C4 bears the iodine, C5 is CH, C6 bears the side chain, C7 is CH, and C7a is bonded to the ring oxygen.

**Side chain:** C6 is bonded to a CH2, then to a CH carrying NH2 (drawn with a hashed bond), then to a CO2CH3 group.

**Stereochemistry:** The hashed bond puts NH2 behind the plane, so the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- The CIP priorities are NH2 > CO2CH3 > CH2Ar > H.
- Viewed from the front, NH2 (down) → CO2CH3 (upper right) → CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise.
- Because H faces the viewer, the sense reverses, so the centre is R (a D-amino acid).
- In SMILES this is written N[C@H](CAr)C(=O)OC.
