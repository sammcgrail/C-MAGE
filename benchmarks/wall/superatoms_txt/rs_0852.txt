The drawing shows a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core joined to an indoline.

**Pyrazinone ring.** N1 carries a sec-butyl group, CH(CH3)CH2CH3. C2 is the carbonyl carbon (C=O). C3 is bonded to the indoline nitrogen and double-bonded to N4. C5 carries Cl and is double-bonded to C6, which is the CH next to N1.

**Indoline.** The nitrogen is bonded to C3 of the pyrazinone, to CH2 (C2) and to the aromatic C7a. C2 is bonded to CH2 (C3), which is bonded to the aromatic C3a. Going around the benzene ring from C7a: C7 carries Br, C6 is CH, C5 carries OMe, C4 is CH, and C3a closes the ring.
