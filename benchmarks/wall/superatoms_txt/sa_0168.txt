The image shows several separate components: two water molecules, a Ca2+ ion, and two identical anions.

Each anion is drawn as ⁻OOC–CH2–CH2–Ac. The label "Ac" would normally be read as acetyl, but here it is drawn in a way that could also mean the element actinium. The task says to expand abbreviations such as Ac, so I read it as acetyl, C(=O)C.

Each anion therefore has the chain ⁻OOC–C–C–C(=O)CH3, which is the levulinate anion (4-oxopentanoate). The SMILES for one anion is [O-]C(=O)CCC(C)=O.

This gives calcium levulinate dihydrate, and the full SMILES is:

SMILES: O.O.[Ca+2].[O-]C(=O)CCC(C)=O.[O-]C(=O)CCC(C)=O
