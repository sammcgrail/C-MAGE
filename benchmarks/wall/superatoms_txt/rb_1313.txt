Reading the structure region by region:

**Top bicyclic system.** An N-methyl cyclic urea (C=O) is fused to a piperidine.
- The ring atoms are N(Me), C(=O), N (the ring-fusion nitrogen), then the CH at the fusion, then C(Ar)=CH–N(Me).
- The second ring is N–CH2–CH2–CH2–CH2–CH (the fusion carbon).
- The fusion carbon is drawn without stereo.
- SMILES for this part: CN1C=C(Ar)C2CCCCN2C1=O

**Central aryl ring.**
- Position 1 carries the bicyclic system.
- Positions 3 and 5 carry OMe.
- Position 4 carries the CH2–N linker.

**Spiro amine.** The piperidine N is attached to the benzylic CH2. The piperidine's 4-position is a spiro carbon shared with a cyclobutane. This is 7-azaspiro[3.5]nonane, and the cyclobutane carbon opposite the spiro atom bears the isoindoline aryl group.

**Isoindoline-dione.**
- A phthalimide with the cyclobutyl group at position 5.
- The imide N is attached to the 3-position of the 2,6-dioxopiperidine.
- The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn plain, so no stereo is given.

**Check of the ring closures.**
- The ring-1 closure is the urea ring: N1, C=, C(, C2 (the fusion CH), then N2 and C1(=O). Writing C(C2CCCCN2C1=O) places the aryl on the vinyl carbon and puts the fusion CH inside the parentheses. That is a problem, because the aryl group would then be written after the parentheses and attached to the vinyl carbon, which is the intended connection.
- The fusion CH (C2) is bonded to the vinyl carbon, and the chain CCCC runs to N2, which closes the ring to C2. N2 is the urea N and is followed by C1(=O), closing the urea ring to the N-methyl nitrogen. This is correct.
- The aryl group follows after the parentheses, attached to the vinyl C, which is also correct.
