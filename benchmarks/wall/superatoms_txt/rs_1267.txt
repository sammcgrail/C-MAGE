I read the molecule as a 9-membered dilactone (antimycin-type core) with a substituted pyridine amide, assembled in four parts.

**Ring.** Going around the 9-membered ring: lactone C(=O)–O–CH(CH3)–CH(O-isobutyrate)–CH(CH2Ph)–C(=O)–O–CH2–CH(NH–), closing back to the first carbonyl.

**Pyridine.** The ring N is position 1. Position 2 carries the C(=O)NH amide that links to the ring. Position 3 carries the O-C(=O)-thiophen-2-yl ester, and position 4 carries OMe.

**Stereocentres.** I assigned each from the wedges and hashes, treating the implicit H as pointing opposite the wedged or hashed substituent:
- The NH-bearing carbon (hashed N) is S.
- The CH(CH3) carbon (bold methyl) is S.
- The CH–O-isobutyrate carbon (hashed O) is R.
- The CH–CH2Ph carbon (bold CH2) is R.
