The drawing shows a benzene ring carrying three substituents.

- One ring carbon bears an O–P(=S)(OMe)2 group, a dimethyl thiophosphate ester.
- The ring carbon at the lower left bears a methyl group.
- The ring carbon next to that one bears an SMe group.

Reading the ring positions: the vertices are C1 (top right, attached to O), C2 (top), C3 (upper left), C4 (left, attached to SMe), C5 (lower left, attached to CH3) and C6 (bottom right). The ring carbon bearing O is bonded to C2 and C6. Going around from C1, C2 and C3 are plain CH, then C4 carries SMe, C5 carries CH3, and C6 is CH. So the SMe group is para to the O and the methyl is meta to the O, which matches fenthion.
