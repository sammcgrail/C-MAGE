I read the drawing as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide whose amide nitrogen is bonded to the CH of a nine-membered dilactone ring fused to a cyclohexane.

**Pyridine part**
- The ring nitrogen sits at the bottom left.
- The carbon next to it bears the C(=O)NH group.
- The next carbon bears OH, and the one after that bears OMe.

**Macrocycle**
- Starting at the NH-bearing CH, the ring runs CH2, O, C(=O), ring-fusion CH, ring-fusion CH, C(=O), O, CH2, and back to the starting CH. That is nine ring atoms.
- The two ring-fusion CH carbons are shared with a cyclohexane ring.
- The NH-bearing CH has plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo label.

**Stereochemistry**
- The two ring-fusion hydrogens are drawn with a hashed bond on the upper carbon and a wedged bond on the lower carbon, so the fusion is trans.
- Working through the neighbour order in the SMILES string, the upper carbon comes out as `@` and the lower carbon as `@@`.

SMILES: COc1ccnc(C(=O)NC2COC(=O)[C@H]3CCCC[C@@H]3C(=O)OC2)c1O
