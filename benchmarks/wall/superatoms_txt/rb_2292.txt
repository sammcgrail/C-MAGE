I read the drawing as a resin-bound protected peptide, written N- to C-terminus:

Fmoc-Ile-Cys(Trt)-Ser(tBu)-Arg(Pbf)-Ser(tBu)-Leu-Pro-Oic-Ile-Cys(Trt)-Ile-O-resin

- **N-terminus:** Fmoc is the fluorenylmethyl carbamate on the first Ile.
- **Cys side chains:** each is protected as the S-trityl (CPh3) thioether.
- **Ser side chains:** each is protected as the tert-butyl ether.
- **Arg side chain:** the guanidine carries the Pbf group, a 2,2,4,6,7-pentamethyl-2,3-dihydrobenzofuran-5-sulfonyl.
- **Pro-Oic unit:** Oic is octahydroindole-2-carboxylic acid, a cis-fused bicycle (both ring-fusion H atoms hashed).
- **C-terminus:** the last Ile is esterified to the resin bead, which I wrote as `O*`.
