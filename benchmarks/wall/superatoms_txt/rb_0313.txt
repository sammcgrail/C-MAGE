I read the molecule as a maleimidocaproyl linker, a peptide spacer, a methylene-oxy-acetyl unit, and a camptothecin-type payload. The SMILES below is built from that reading.

**Linker and spacer (left to right)**
- The maleimide ring is attached through its nitrogen to a chain of five CH2 groups, then a C(=O)NH. I counted five CH2 vertices between the maleimide N and the first carbonyl.
- The peptide sequence is Gly-Gly-Phe-Gly.
- After the last Gly comes NH–CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–N(CH3)–CH2, which attaches to the aromatic ring of the payload.

**Phenylalanine stereocentre**
- The benzyl group is on a wedge, with N at lower left and C(=O) at lower right.
- With the implicit H pointing away, N → C(=O) → CH2 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S (L-Phe).
- That gives `N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)` in the written order.

**Payload**
- It is a camptothecin-type pentacycle (quinoline, pyrroline, pyridone, lactone) with the CH2N(Me) side chain at the quinoline 7-position.
- On the benzo ring, the methyl sits upper left and the fluorine lower left. Counting from the quinoline N, the ring reads: junction carbon, CH, C–F, C–CH3, CH, junction carbon.
- The quaternary carbon carries a hashed ethyl and a wedged OH.
- The lowest-priority substituent (ethyl) points away, and OH → lactone C(=O) → ring carbon runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S, as in natural camptothecin.
- Written in the order C(=O), ethyl, OH, ring carbon, this is `[C@@]`.
