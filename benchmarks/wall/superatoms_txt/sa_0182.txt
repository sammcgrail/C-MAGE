This is bisacodyl. A central CH carbon is bonded to a pyridin-2-yl group and to two para-substituted phenyl rings. Each phenyl ring carries an OAc group, where Ac is acetyl, so each is an acetate ester O–C(=O)CH3.

The pyridine ring is drawn with N bonded to the ring carbon that bears the CH, so the attachment is at C2.

The central carbon has two identical aryl groups, so it is not a stereocentre.

SMILES: CC(=O)Oc1ccc(cc1)C(c1ccccn1)c1ccc(OC(C)=O)cc1
