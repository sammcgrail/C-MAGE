I read the structure in four parts and then fixed the stereochemistry from the single wedge.

1. **Pyrimidine ring:** NH2 sits at C4, NO2 at C5 and Me at C6. C2 carries the NH that links to the stereocentre.
2. **Central butanediamide chain:** the stereocentre carries the NH, one C(=O) and one CH2. The C(=O) leads to a 4-(piperidin-1-yl)piperidine amide. The CH2 leads onward to a second C(=O).
3. **Right-hand amide:** that second C(=O) is attached to a piperidine nitrogen. The piperidine's 4-position bears N3 of a 3,4-dihydroquinazolin-2(1H)-one, which is a benzo-fused ring containing NH–C(=O)–N–CH2.
4. **Stereochemistry:** the NH is on a wedge, with the H implicit and pointing away from the viewer. The priorities are N > C(=O)N > CH2 > H. Tracing N → C(=O) → CH2 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. In the SMILES below, written with the neighbours in the order N, H, CH2, C(=O), this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.
