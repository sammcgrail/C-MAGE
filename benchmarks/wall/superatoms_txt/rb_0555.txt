I read this as a cyclic hexapeptide: hexanoyl‑Asp‑His‑D‑1Nal‑Arg‑Trp, with the Asp side‑chain carboxyl closing the ring onto the Trp C‑terminus through a pentane‑1,5‑diamine linker.

**Constituents, traced around the ring**
- **N‑terminus:** an n‑hexanoyl group (CH3(CH2)4C=O) on the Asp α‑nitrogen.
- **Asp:** its side‑chain C=O forms an amide with one NH of the –NH(CH2)5NH– linker. I counted five CH2 groups in the linker from the drawing; this count is the least certain part of the reading.
- **His:** an imidazole side chain.
- **Nal:** a 1‑naphthylmethyl side chain.
- **Arg:** a –(CH2)3–NH–C(=NH)NH2 side chain.
- **Trp:** an indol‑3‑ylmethyl side chain. Its C=O forms the amide with the other NH of the linker, which closes the macrocycle.

**Stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes**
- **Asp:** the side‑chain CH2 is on a wedge, which gives the L configuration.
- **His:** the side‑chain CH2 is hashed, which I read as L.
- **Nal:** the CH2 is on a wedge, which gives D.
- **Arg:** the CH2 is hashed, which gives L.
- **Trp:** the CH2 is hashed, which gives L.

I derived each centre by working out the clockwise or counterclockwise order of N, side chain and C=O with the hydrogen pointing away from the viewer.
