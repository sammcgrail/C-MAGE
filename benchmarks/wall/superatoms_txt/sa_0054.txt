The drawing shows MeO2C–CH2–NH–Boc, which is Boc-glycine methyl ester.

- MeO2C is a methyl ester, written COC(=O).
- The CH2 links the ester carbon to the nitrogen.
- NH carries the Boc group, which is C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.

Assembled, this gives COC(=O)CNC(=O)OC(C)(C)C. The molecule has no stereocentres, so no stereo marks are needed.

SMILES: COC(=O)CNC(=O)OC(C)(C)C
