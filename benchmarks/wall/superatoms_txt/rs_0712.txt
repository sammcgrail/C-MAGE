I read the structure as an ethyl 2-methoxypropanoate with a 4-aminophenyl group on C3, where the aniline nitrogen carries a 3-(4-mesyloxyphenyl)propyl chain.

**Fragments, left to right:**
- A methanesulfonate ester: CH3–S(=O)(=O)–O attached to a para-substituted benzene ring.
- A three-carbon chain (CH2CH2CH2) from that ring to an N–H.
- The N–H is bonded to a second para-substituted benzene ring.
- That ring carries a CH2 attached to the stereocentre, which bears OMe, H and CO2Et.

**Stereocentre:** the CO2Et and CH2 bonds are plain, the OMe bond is a bold wedge and the H bond is hashed. The CIP priorities are OMe > CO2Et > CH2Ar > H. With H pointing away, OMe, CO2Et and CH2Ar run counterclockwise as drawn, so the centre is S.

**SMILES check:** in `CCOC(=O)[C@@H](OC)Cc1...`, the neighbours of the stereocentre are listed as CO2Et, H, OMe, CH2. Moving H to the front takes one swap, which gives `@`. Viewed from H, CO2Et → OMe → CH2 is then counterclockwise. Viewed from the side opposite H, as in the CIP assignment, it is clockwise. That makes OMe → CO2Et → CH2 counterclockwise, which is S, matching the drawing.
