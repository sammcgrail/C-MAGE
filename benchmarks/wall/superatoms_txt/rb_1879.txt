I read the drawing as a cyclosporin-like cyclic peptide with an exocyclic tail. The tail is an N-methyl-Phe, N-methyl-Phe, Ala unit that ends in a piperidine amide. I traced the ring residue by residue, and the stereo tags are partly assumed, as explained below.

**Macrocycle.** The ring runs through these residues in order:
1. An Asp-type residue whose side-chain carbonyl is part of the ring. Its α-carbonyl carries the exocyclic tail.
2. N-Me-Ala.
3. Sarcosine (N-methylglycine).
4. Threonine, with a free OH.
5. N-Me-Ala.
6. Phe.
7. N-Me-Leu.
8. N-Me-Ala.
9. N-Me-Val.
10. Ala, which closes back onto the Asp NH.

**Tail.** From the Asp α-carbonyl: N-Me-Phe, then N-Me-Phe, then Ala, then the piperidine amide.

**Stereochemistry.** I could not reliably read every wedge and hash in the drawing. I assigned L-configuration to all residues by default, except one Ala. That Ala, the one next to the Asp NH, I made D, by analogy with cyclosporin. Several of these tags are therefore guesses rather than readings of the drawing.
