The drawing shows a cyclobutane ring with four vertices. One of its carbons is quaternary and is bonded to two groups: a 4-chlorophenyl ring and a CH carbon. That CH carbon carries an isobutyl group (iBu) and an NMe2 group.

Isobutyl expands to CC(C)C, attached through its CH2. The CH carbon is therefore C(CC(C)C)N(C)C.

Assembling the pieces gives CC(C)CC(N(C)C)C1(CCC1)c1ccc(Cl)cc1.

The CH carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but the drawing uses only plain bonds, so I assign no stereochemistry.

SMILES: CC(C)CC(N(C)C)C1(CCC1)c1ccc(Cl)cc1
