The drawing is a pyridine ring with a CO2Me group on each carbon next to the nitrogen (positions 2 and 6). That makes it dimethyl pyridine-2,6-dicarboxylate.

Reading the ring: the nitrogen is at the bottom, and the two carbons beside it each carry a CO2Me substituent. The ring has three double bonds, and the remaining three ring carbons are CH.

Each substituent is a methyl ester, written MeO2C on the left and CO2Me on the right, so each is C(=O)OC.

SMILES: COC(=O)c1cccc(n1)C(=O)OC
