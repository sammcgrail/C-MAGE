I read the molecule as a para-substituted benzoic acid joined through an N-CH2CH2 linker to a pyrrolidin-2-one, with a hydroxyheptenyl side chain on the ring carbon next to nitrogen.

**Connectivity**
- The aromatic ring is para-disubstituted: a CO2H group on one side and a CH2CH2 chain on the other.
- The CH2CH2 chain is attached to the lactam nitrogen.
- The lactam is a five-membered ring: N, C(=O), CH2, CH2, and the CH next to N.
- That ring CH carries a CH=CH-CH(OH)-CH2CH2CH3 side chain. The double bond is drawn trans.

**Stereochemistry**
- **Ring carbon:** The wedge bond goes to the vinyl carbon, so H points away. The priorities are N > vinyl > ring CH2, and they run clockwise as drawn. This gives R, which corresponds to `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order I wrote.
- **Carbinol carbon:** The OH is on a hashed bond, so H points toward the viewer. The priorities are OH > vinyl > CH2. They run clockwise as drawn, and reversing for the H toward the viewer gives S. This corresponds to `[C@@H]` in my SMILES order.
- **Double bond:** It is trans (E), written as `/C=C/`.

SMILES: OC(=O)c1ccc(CCN2C(=O)CC[C@@H]2/C=C/[C@@H](O)CCC)cc1
