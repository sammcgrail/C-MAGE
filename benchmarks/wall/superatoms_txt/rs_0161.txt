Following the drawing atom for atom, the chain is ring–CH3 (as a linker)–CH(N(CH3)2)–CH3. A linker carbon with three H cannot also be bonded to the ring and to the CH, so the intended structure is ring–CH2–CH(CH3)N(CH3)2.
