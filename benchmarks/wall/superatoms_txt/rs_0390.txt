I read the structure in four parts.

- **Left end:** a 4-methoxybenzoyl group joined through an amide NH to the stereocentre.
- **Stereocentre:** it carries NH on a wedge, a C(=O) that leads to a 4-(piperidin-1-yl)piperidine amide, and a CH2.
- **CH2 side:** the CH2 leads to a second C(=O), which forms an amide with a piperidine bearing a 2-oxo-1,4-dihydroquinazolin-3(2H)-yl group at the 4-position.
- **Stereochemistry:** with H pointing away, the priorities N > C(=O)N > CH2 run counterclockwise in the drawing, so the centre is S. In the SMILES written as N[C@@H](CC...)C(=O)..., `@@` corresponds to S.
