The drawing shows a phenoxy-substituted benzamide whose amide nitrogen carries a four-carbon chain ending in a Boc-protected amine.

- **Left ring:** a phenyl ring joined through an ether oxygen to a para-substituted benzene ring (4-phenoxyphenyl).
- **Amide:** the para-substituted ring bears a C(=O)NH group.
- **Chain:** from the amide NH, the zigzag runs through four carbons (the vertices between NH and the second N). Reading the bends left to right, the vertices go from the NH to a vertex, then up, down, up, and finally to the NH(Boc). That gives four CH2 groups, i.e. a butyl linker.
- **Terminus:** the chain ends in NH–Boc, where Boc is C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.

SMILES: O=C(NCCCCNC(=O)OC(C)(C)C)c1ccc(Oc2ccccc2)cc1
