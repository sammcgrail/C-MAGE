I assigned stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes. Each α-carbon has a hashed H, and I worked out the SMILES chirality tag for each from the drawn geometry. Every α-carbon of residues 3–7 and 9–12 comes out as the L configuration, which is `@@` in the N–Cα–C(=O) writing order. Residue 1 is also `@@` and residue 2 is `@`, as worked out from their wedges.
