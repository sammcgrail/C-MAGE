I read the structure in five regions and wrote each into the SMILES, then assigned stereo only at the carbons drawn with wedges or hashes.

**Bicyclic core.** An eight-membered ring is fused to a pyrrolidine. The eight ring atoms are N(Me), CH2, CH(NH–C=O), C(=O), the shared N, the ring-fusion CH, CH2 and CH2. The pyrrolidine carbon next to the shared N carries the C(=O)NH group.

**Stereocentres in the core.** Three wedged centres were read from the drawing:
- the ring-fusion CH, wedged to the CH2 above it
- the CH bearing the NH, wedged
- the pyrrolidine CH bearing the carbonyl, wedged

**Glutamine unit.** The alpha carbon is wedged and corresponds to the L configuration. Its side chain is CH2CH2C(=O)NH2.

**Phenylglycinol-type unit.** The NH–CH(Ph)–CH2–O carbon has a hashed bond to the CH2.

- Linker: nine CH2 groups run from the ether O to the alkyne, then C≡C.
- Aryl group: the alkyne attaches at C4 of a lenalidomide-type isoindolinone.
- Imide ring: the isoindolinone N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring, drawn without stereo.
