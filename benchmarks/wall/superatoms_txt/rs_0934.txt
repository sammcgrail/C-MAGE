Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left:** two benzyl groups (each a CH2 on a phenyl ring) are attached to one nitrogen, which is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring.
- **Middle:** the ring carbon opposite the nitrogen carries a CH2 that leads to the stereocentre.
- **Stereocentre:** this carbon bears an OC2H5 group on a bold wedge, a CO2Et group, the CH2 and an implicit H.
- **Right:** the ester is C(=O)OEt.

The molecule is therefore ethyl 3-[4-(dibenzylamino)phenyl]-2-ethoxypropanoate.

**Stereochemistry.** The CIP priorities at the stereocentre are OEt > CO2Et > CH2Ar > H.

- The OEt group points down on the wedge, so it is toward the viewer, and the H is at the back.
- Viewed from the front, OEt (down), then CO2Et (upper right), then CH2Ar (upper left) runs counterclockwise.
- With H pointing away, counterclockwise means S.

In the SMILES `CCO[C@@H](Cc1...)C(=O)OCC`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is O, H, CH2, C(=O). Working through the permutation, `@@` corresponds to S for this order.
