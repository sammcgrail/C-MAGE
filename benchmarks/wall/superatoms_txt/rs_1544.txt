The drawing shows a phenyl ring joined through an oxygen to a para-substituted benzene ring. The second ring carries an O–CH2CH2CH2–NH–Boc chain on the opposite side.

Reading the chain: the oxygen is followed by three CH2 groups, since the line goes down, up, then down to the N. Tracing the vertices, O connects to a vertex at the upper right, then a lower vertex, then an upper vertex, then N. That gives three carbons in the chain.

Boc is expanded as C(=O)OC(C)(C)C.

The full structure is therefore PhO–C6H4–O–CH2CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)OC(CH3)3.
