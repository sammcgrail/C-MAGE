I traced the macrocycle as a cyclic peptoid-like ring and wrote the SMILES by ring closure. The ring runs in this order:

1. A 4-substituted benzamide, Ar–C(=O)NH–, on the left. Its ring is linked through CH2–S–CH2CH2–C(=O) to the tertiary amide nitrogen at the bottom right.
2. A phenylalanine-derived unit, NH–CH(CH2Ph)–CH2–N, on the left. Its nitrogen carries a cyclopropylacetyl group and continues to –CH2–C(=O)–NH–.
3. A tryptophan-derived unit, NH–CH(CH2-indol-3-yl)–CH2–N. Its nitrogen again carries a cyclopropylacetyl group and continues to –CH2–C(=O)–NH–.
4. A lysine-derived unit, NH–CH((CH2)4NH2)–CH2–N. Its nitrogen carries a 4-methylpentanoyl group (isocaproyl) and continues to –CH2–C(=O)–NH–.
5. A phenylalanine-derived unit, NH–CH(CH2Ph)–CH2–N, on the right. Its nitrogen carries the CH2C(=O)NH2 arm and the C(=O)CH2CH2–S acyl group that closes the ring.

Each of the four stereocentres is drawn with a wedge to its side chain, with H pointing away. In each one the sequence NH → CH2N → side chain runs counterclockwise. In the SMILES order used below (CH2N, H, side chain, NH), that gives [C@H] at all four.
