I read the molecule as an MDM2-type spiro-oxindole pyrrolidine linked through a picolinamide and a pentynyl chain to a thalidomide-like isoindolinone–glutarimide.

**Fragments, from left to right**
- **Spiro-oxindole:** a 6-chloro-2-oxindole, spiro-fused at C3 to the pyrrolidine.
- **Pyrrolidine:** its C2 is also spiro to a cyclohexane, and its NH sits between C2 and the carboxamide-bearing carbon. C4 carries a 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl group.
- **Amide link:** the carboxamide on the pyrrolidine goes through NH to C5 of a pyridine. C2 of that pyridine carries a second amide, C(=O)NH–CH2CH2CH2–C≡C–.
- **Isoindolinone:** the alkyne attaches to the aromatic ring of a 1-oxo-isoindoline. Its N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.

**Stereochemistry**
- The wedges and hashes at three centres were converted to @/@@ by working out the handedness from the drawn coordinates. These are the carboxamide-bearing carbon (hashed bond), the aryl-bearing carbon (wedge), and the oxindole spiro carbon (hashed to the aromatic ring, wedge to the C=O).
- The cyclohexane spiro carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it unspecified.
- The glutarimide CH is also plain, so it is unspecified too.
