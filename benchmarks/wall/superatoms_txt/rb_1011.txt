Reading the drawing region by region gave this structure:

- **Pyrazine core:** a carboxamide (C(N)=O) sits on one ring carbon, and the next carbon carries the NH–aryl group. The ring carbon opposite the carboxamide carries the piperidine nitrogen. The remaining ring carbon is a plain CH, and the ring has two nitrogens.
- **Aryl–piperidine:** the NH is attached to a para-substituted benzene. The benzene carries a 4-methylpiperidin-4-yl group, whose nitrogen is acylated.
- **Linker:** from the acyl carbon the chain is C(=O)–CH2–CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH. This is a PEG3 chain ending in an NH on the aromatic ring.
- **Thalidomide-type end:** the NH is on position 4 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring, drawn without stereo.
- **Left-hand piperidine:** the nitrogen is attached to the pyrazine, and C3 carries a 3-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl group on a wedge bond. Taking the priorities as N(imidazolidinone) > C2 > C4 > H, with H pointing back, the wedge gives an R centre. In the SMILES this corresponds to `[C@H]` when written as `N1CCC[C@H](C1)N...`.
