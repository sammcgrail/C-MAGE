I read the molecule as a VHL-type hydroxyproline ligand joined through an isoxazole–bipiperidine–pyrimidine linker to a diazabicyclooctane carrying an aminopyridazine phenol. This is my reading of the drawing, written out fragment by fragment.

1. **Hydroxyproline end:** The 4-hydroxypyrrolidine has a wedged OH at C4 and a hashed bond to the C2 amide carbonyl. I took this as the (2S,4R) VHL-ligand configuration and wrote the ring stereo to match it, checking the chirality order against the known VH032 SMILES.
2. **Amide side:** The C2 carbonyl is an amide, NH–CH2–C6H4 (para), bearing a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.
3. **Acyl side:** The ring nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)–CH(isopropyl)–isoxazol-5-yl. The isoxazole is attached at its 3-position to a piperidine nitrogen. The CH carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
4. **Linker:** That piperidine is joined at its 4-position to the nitrogen of a second piperidine. The second piperidine's 4-position carries pyrimidin-5-yl.
5. **Bicycle:** Pyrimidine C2 is bonded to N8 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane. N3 of that bicycle is bonded to the pyridazine.
6. **Pyridazine end:** The pyridazine has NH2 on the carbon next to ring N, the bicycle N on the adjacent carbon, and a 2-hydroxyphenyl at the other carbon adjacent to ring N.
