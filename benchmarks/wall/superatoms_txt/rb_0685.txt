I read the structure in five parts, left to right:

- **Glutarimide:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring, `O=C1CCC(C(=O)N1)`.
- **Isoindoline-1,3-dione:** the imide nitrogen is attached to the glutarimide C3. The benzo ring carries a piperazine at position 5.
- **Linker:** the piperazine N-CH2 is bonded to azetidine C3. The azetidine nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)-CH2CH2CH2-O-CH2CH2.
- **Stereocentre:** the CH at the end of that chain carries a hashed NH, a CH2 on one side and a C(=O) on the other. Working through the hashed bond (N behind, H toward the viewer) gives `[C@H]` when written CH2-first.
- **Right-hand end:** that NH is acylated by a 1-(methylsulfonyl)pyrrole-3-carbonyl group. The C(=O) of the stereocentre forms an amide with the 2-amino group of a 4-phenylthiazole.

The only stereo shown in the drawing is the hashed bond; the glutarimide C3 is drawn with plain bonds and gets no stereo label.
