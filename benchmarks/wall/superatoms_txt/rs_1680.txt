The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-benzyl-5-methoxyisatin core.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H. The second ring is attached to it at the position meta to the CO2H. On the second ring, the carbon next to the biaryl bond bears OH, and the carbon after that bears the HN–N= group. So the substituents sit at C1 (aryl), C2 (OH) and C3 (NH).

**Hydrazone and oxindole.** The NH connects through N= to C3 of the oxindole. C2 of the oxindole is C=O, and N1 carries a benzyl group. The benzo ring of the oxindole has OCH3 at C5. In the drawing, C3a is the ring carbon attached to C3, and the methoxy-bearing carbon is the next one up and to the right of C3a, so it sits at C4 or C5. The ring runs C3a, then C4 (top, attached to C3a), then C5 (bearing OCH3), then C6, C7, C7a. This matches the name 5-methoxy.
