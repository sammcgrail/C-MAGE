I read the structure as a head-to-tail-free macrocyclic peptide. A thioether (S–CH2–C(=O)–) links the Cys side chain to the N-terminus of the Tyr residue.

The sequence, from Tyr and following the peptide bonds, is:

1. Tyr
2. N-Me-Ala
3. Asn
4. Pro
5. Dap, with its side-chain NH on a dimethylamino-squaramide
6. Leu
7. trans-4-hydroxy-Pro
8. Trp
9. Dab, with its side-chain NH on a dimethylamino-squaramide
10. Trp, with N1 carrying CH2COOH
11. N-Me-norleucine
12. N-Me-norleucine
13. Leu
14. Cys
15. Gly-NH2

The ornithine-like side chain whose long bond crosses the Tyr region has only two CH2 groups before the NH, so it is Dab, not Orn.

I checked each α-carbon against its wedge or hash bonds. All residues come out L (S for the amino acids with the usual priorities, R for Cys), and the hydroxyproline C4 comes out R.
