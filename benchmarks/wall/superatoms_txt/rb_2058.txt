I read this as a thalidomide-type (4-substituted phthalimide–glutarimide) group joined through an alkyne and a short PEG chain to a carbamate on a statin core.

- **Left end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the nitrogen of a phthalimide. The phthalimide carries a C≡C substituent at the 4-position of the benzene ring.
- **Linker:** the alkyne continues as –C≡C–CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–C(=O)–O–, ending in a carbamate.
- **Right end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on C1 of the hexahydronaphthalene ring of a lovastatin/monacolin J-type core. The ring has methyls at C3 and C7 and a C=C–C=C diene pattern. It carries the CH2CH2 chain to a 4-hydroxy-6-oxotetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone, which is drawn with wedges and hashes.

For the stereochemistry I used the natural lovastatin configuration. I took the lovastatin SMILES from memory and replaced its 2-methylbutanoyl ester with this carbamate linker, checking that the neighbour order at the C1 stereocentre stays the same. I did not independently re-derive each stereocentre from the wedges in the drawing. The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
