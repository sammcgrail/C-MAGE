This SMILES is my reading of the drawing: a daptomycin core whose ornithine side-chain amine carries a trans-cyclooctene (TCO) carbamate, with the TCO ring linked through an amide to aspartic acid. I took most amino-acid stereocentres from natural daptomycin and did not check them against every wedge in the image. Only the TCO centres and the trans double bond were derived directly from the drawn wedges.

**Reading the structure**
- **N-terminus:** a decanoyl group (H19C9–C(=O)–) acylates the Trp nitrogen.
- **Peptide chain, in drawn order:** Trp (indole side chain), D-Asn, Asp, Thr, Gly, Orn, Asp, D-Ala, Asp, Gly, D-Ser, 3-methyl-Glu, Kyn.
- **Ring closure:** the Thr side-chain oxygen forms an ester with the C-terminal carbonyl of kynurenine. Kynurenine carries the o-aminophenyl ketone.
- **Orn side chain:** the terminal NH forms a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O–, to the TCO ring.
- **TCO ring (8-membered):** the carbamate O sits on one ring carbon. A trans C=C lies next to it. A quaternary carbon carries a methyl group and a C(=O)NH link to Asp (CH(CH2COOH)COOH, drawn without stereo).

**Stereochemistry**
- **TCO quaternary carbon and the CH–O carbon:** I derived these from the wedge and hash bonds in the drawing.
- **Amino-acid centres:** these follow natural daptomycin. The L/D assignments are Trp L, Asn D, Asp L, Thr (2S,3R), Orn L, Asp D, Ala D, Asp L, Ser D, 3-methyl-Glu (2S,3R), Kyn L.
