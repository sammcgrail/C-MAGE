- **Macrocycle:** Counting from the N attached to the benzo ring, the ring runs N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–N(aryl)–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2 and back to the first N. That gives 2 N, 3 O and 10 C, so 15 ring atoms: a diaza-15-crown-5.
- **N-aryl group:** The second N carries a 2-methoxyphenyl group.
- **Lactone benzo ring:** It has an OMe, then the crown-ether N on the adjacent carbon, then a CH, then the carbon bearing the lactone C=O. The remaining ring carbon (the other CH, next to the OMe carbon) is attached to the spiro carbon.
- **Spiro core:** The spiro carbon is bonded to the lactone O and to two aryl rings. These rings are bridged by Si(CH3)2, and each carries an OAc group para to the spiro-bearing carbon. No stereochemistry is drawn, so none is included.
