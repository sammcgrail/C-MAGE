The molecule is methyl 2-(benzyloxycarbonylamino)-3-(6-benzyloxypyridin-3-yl)acrylate, which I read as follows:

- **Carbamate:** a Cbz group (benzyl–O–C(=O)–NH–) sits on the alpha carbon of the acrylate.
- **Ester:** the alpha carbon also carries CO2Me.
- **Pyridine:** the beta carbon (=CH–) is attached to C3 of a pyridine ring. The ring nitrogen is N1, and C6 carries an O–CH2–phenyl (benzyloxy) group.
- **Double bond:** the NH and the pyridyl group are drawn on the same (left) side of the C=C. NH outranks the ester carbon on the alpha carbon, and pyridyl outranks H on the beta carbon, so the two higher-priority groups are cis, giving the Z isomer.

In the SMILES, `N/C(...)=C\c` places N and the pyridyl carbon cis to each other.

SMILES: O=C(OCc1ccccc1)N/C(C(=O)OC)=C\c1ccc(OCc2ccccc2)nc1
