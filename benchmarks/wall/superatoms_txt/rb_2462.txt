I read the structure as a macrocyclic ester built from these parts:

- **Piperazic acid:** a hexahydropyridazine ring whose C3 carries the ester carbonyl and whose ring N1 is acylated.
- **Macrocycle linker:** the ester O–CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2 chain runs to C3 of the indole.
- **Indole:** N-ethyl, with a 2-(1-methoxyethyl)pyridin-3-yl group at C2 and a thiazole attached at C5.
- **Thiazole to piperazic N1:** the thiazole C2 bears CH2–CH(NH–acyl)–C(=O), which closes onto piperazic N1.
- **Side chain:** the NH is acylated by a 1-substituted cyclopropane carboxamide, whose CH2–O links to an azetidin-3-yl. That azetidine N carries C(=O)–C≡C–C(CH3)2–(azetidin-1-yl).
