I read the structure in three parts.

**Piperidinone core.** The ring is N1–C2(=O)–C3–C4H2–C5–C6.
- C3 is quaternary and carries a methyl (wedge) and a CH2C(=O)NH– arm.
- C5 carries a 3-chlorophenyl (wedge).
- C6 carries a 4-chlorophenyl (hash).
- N1 carries CH(iPr)CH2SO2iPr, with the CH–CH2 bond drawn as a wedge.

**Linker.** The amide NH is followed by six CH2 groups and then a C≡C. I counted these zigzag vertices by eye, so the chain length is the least certain part of the reading.

**Right-hand end.** The alkyne is attached to the isoindolinone at the aromatic carbon next to the CH2-bearing carbon. The isoindolinone nitrogen is bonded to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

I assigned each stereocentre from the wedge or hash and the atom order in the SMILES. The four labelled centres are C3, C5, C6 and the N-substituent CH.
