The structure is a carbazole whose ring nitrogen is bonded to the 4-position of a pyridine. The pyridine carries a 2,4-difluorophenyl group at its 2-position, and the carbazole has a methoxy group on each benzo ring.

**Carbazole.** The upper ring has OMe at its top right, and the lower ring has OMe at its bottom left. Numbering the carbazole with N as 9, the carbon next to N is C8a and the next carbon around the ring is C8. The upper ring's OMe carbon sits two bonds from the carbon attached to N, so it is a meta-type position relative to that carbon, which makes it C2 (or C7). The lower ring's OMe is likewise at C7. This gives a 2,7-dimethoxycarbazole.

**Pyridine.** The ring nitrogen is at the right. The carbon attached to the carbazole N is para to the pyridine N, which is position 4. The difluorophenyl group is on the carbon adjacent to the pyridine N, which is position 2.

**Difluorophenyl.** The phenyl carbon attached to the pyridine is C1. One F is on the adjacent carbon (C2, ortho). The other F is at the bottom, para to the attachment point (C4).
