I read the molecule as six connected pieces, left to right:

1. A benzimidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine core carrying two adjacent fluorines on the benzene ring. The imidazole bridgehead nitrogen sits in the pyrimidine ring, and the pyrimidine C2 carries the pyridine.
2. A pyridine attached through its 3-position to the pyrimidine, with its 6-position bonded to the piperidine nitrogen.
3. A piperidine substituted at C4 by a two-carbon (ethylene) chain.
4. A piperazine, with one nitrogen on the ethylene chain and the other on the aryl ring of the next piece.
5. A phthalimide (isoindoline-1,3-dione) attached to the piperazine at its 5-position.
6. A 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the imide nitrogen. Its stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo marker.
