I read this as Boc-His(Trt)-OH, an N-Boc-protected histidine with a trityl group on the imidazole nitrogen.

- **Backbone:** the stereocentre carries an NH-Boc group (drawn upward), a CO2H group on a wedge bond (lower left), and a CH2 group (lower right). The implicit hydrogen points away from the viewer.
- **Imidazole ring:** the CH2 is attached to ring carbon C4. C4 is bonded to a ring CH and to an N= atom. The other ring nitrogen carries the Trt group, and a CH sits between the two nitrogens.
- **Stereochemistry:** with the hydrogen at the back, the priorities are N > CO2H > CH2. Going N (up) to CO2H (lower left) to CH2 (lower right) is counterclockwise, so the centre is S, as in L-histidine. The SMILES `N[C@@H](R)C(=O)O` matches this configuration.
- **Expanded groups:** Boc is `CC(C)(C)OC(=O)` on the nitrogen, and Trt is `C(c2ccccc2)(c2ccccc2)c2ccccc2` on the ring nitrogen.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N[C@@H](Cc1cn(C(c2ccccc2)(c2ccccc2)c2ccccc2)cn1)C(=O)O
