I read the molecule as a maleamic acid methyl ester (cis alkene) on a six-carbon aminocaproyl linker. That linker is followed by Val–Cit, a PAB carbamate and the MMAE payload, as a drug-linker conjugate. I wrote the payload stereocentres by comparing them with the known MMAE SMILES. I did not independently check every wedge in the image against it.

**Fragments, left to right**
- **Head group:** methyl ester, then a cis (Z) CH=CH, then an amide C(=O)NH. The two carbonyl substituents are cis in the drawing.
- **Spacer:** five CH2 groups then C(=O), i.e. an aminocaproyl unit, bound to the Val N.
- **Dipeptide:** Val (isopropyl side chain) then citrulline, whose side chain is (CH2)3–NH–C(=O)NH2. Both are drawn as L-amino acids.
- **PAB carbamate:** the citrulline C(=O) is bonded to NH on a para-substituted benzene, which carries CH2–O–C(=O)–N(CH3).
- **MMAE payload:**
  - N-methyl-Val, then Val, then N-methyl-dolaisoleuine (sec-butyl, methoxy, CH2C(=O)).
  - That unit is acylated onto a pyrrolidine (dolaproine) bearing a methoxy and a methyl-branched C(=O).
  - The chain ends in an NH–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–phenyl group (the norephedrine unit).

**Stereochemistry:** I set the carbon tags in my fragment order by permutation-checking against the known MMAE SMILES. That check flipped the methoxy-bearing carbon of the dolaisoleuine unit and the N-methyl-Val carbon from my first draft.
