The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-benzyl oxindole.

**Left ring (benzoic acid):** CO2H sits on one carbon, and the ring is attached to the second aryl ring at the meta position relative to it.

**Middle ring:** The carbon bonded to the left ring is adjacent to the carbon bearing OH. The next carbon over carries the NH–N= group. So the substituents are biaryl, OH and NH on three consecutive carbons, with OH in the middle.

**Hydrazone linkage:** The NH is bonded to N, which is double-bonded to C3 of the oxindole.

**Oxindole:**
- C3 is the hydrazone carbon.
- C2 is the C=O carbon.
- N1 carries the benzyl group.
- The methyl is on the benzo ring at C5, which is para to N1 and meta to C3a.
