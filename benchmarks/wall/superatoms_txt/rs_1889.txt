The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- N1 is the ring nitrogen.
- C2 carries the carboxamide, C(=O)NH-aryl.
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are plain CH.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring. The carbon opposite the NH bears an ether oxygen, which links to a 2-methoxyphenyl ring.

SMILES: COc1ccnc(C(=O)Nc2ccc(Oc3ccccc3OC)cc2)c1O

Check of the pyridine ring: c1(OMe) is C4, followed by c (C5), c (C6), n (N1), c(C(=O)N...) (C2), and c1O (C3). Closing the ring gives C3 bonded to C4, which is correct.
