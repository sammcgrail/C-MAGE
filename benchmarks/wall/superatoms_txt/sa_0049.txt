The drawing shows an N-Cbz-protected valine.

- **Cbz:** the benzyloxycarbonyl group on nitrogen is PhCH2–O–C(=O)–, so the nitrogen carries NH–C(=O)OCH2Ph.
- **Core:** the stereocentre carries NH(Cbz), CO2H, iPr and an implicit H.
- **Stereochemistry:** the iPr bond is hashed, so iPr points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > iPr > H. On the page, N (lower left) → CO2H (top) → iPr (lower right) runs clockwise. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, which gives S, the L-valine configuration.

I wrote the SMILES in the same pattern as L-valine, `CC(C)[C@H](N)C(=O)O`, with the Cbz group added on nitrogen.
