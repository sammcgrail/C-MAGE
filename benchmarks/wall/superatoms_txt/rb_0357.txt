I read the molecule as a macrocyclic peptide with an N-terminal hexanoyl cap. The ring runs through five residues and a diamine linker.

**Reading the structure**
- **Cap:** a hexanoyl chain on the Glu α-nitrogen. I counted six carbons, but the zigzag was hard to count, so heptanoyl is possible.
- **Ring order:** Glu → His → 4-fluoro-Phe → Arg → Trp → 1,4-diaminobutane. The diamine closes back onto the Glu side-chain carboxyl, so the ring is formed by a side-chain amide.
- **Side chains:**
  - His is an imidazol-4-ylmethyl group.
  - 4-F-Phe is a para-fluorobenzyl group.
  - Arg is a propyl chain ending in a guanidine.
  - Trp is an indol-3-ylmethyl group.

**Stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes**
- Glu, His, Arg and Trp work out as L (S).
- The 4-F-Phe centre has a bold wedge to the benzyl CH2, with N up and C=O to the lower right. That gives R, so it is D-4-fluorophenylalanine.
- The Arg bond is hard to read and I assigned it as L.
