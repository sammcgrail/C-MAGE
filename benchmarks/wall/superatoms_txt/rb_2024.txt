I read this as a rapamycin derivative joined through its cyclohexyl 40-O to a DBCO-capped Trp-Trp-Lys-Trp-Trp-NH2 peptide. I built the SMILES from three parts and did not run a validity check on the assembled string.

1. **DBCO cap:** the dibenzo-azacyclooctyne nitrogen carries a succinyl group, which is amide-linked to 6-aminohexanoic acid (five CH2 plus the C=O). That acid is acylated onto the N-terminus of the peptide.
2. **Peptide:** the sequence is Trp-Trp-Lys-Trp-Trp-NH2. All residues are drawn as L (S) with wedged bonds. The lysine ε-nitrogen is acylated by the glycolic-type linker NC(=O)CO–.
3. **Rapamycin core:** I took the stereochemistry from the standard rapamycin SMILES. The only edits are that the 40-O carries the –CH2C(=O)NH– linker to the lysine, and the ketone next to the pipecolate amide is drawn as C(=P), so I wrote it as drawn rather than as C=O. The phosphorus takes one implicit hydrogen.
