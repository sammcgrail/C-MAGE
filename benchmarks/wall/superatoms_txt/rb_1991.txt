I read the molecule as a 3-chloro-4-cyanophenoxy group on a trans-1,4-disubstituted cyclohexane, whose other end carries an amide NH to a pyridazine-3-carboxamide. The pyridazine's 6-position bears a piperidin-1-yl ring, and the piperidine's 4-position carries a CH2 link to a piperazine. That piperazine is N-aryl on a 5-fluoro isoindoline-1,3-dione, whose ring nitrogen is attached to the 3-position of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine (glutarimide).

**Stereochemistry:**
- **Cyclohexane:** the O is on a hashed bond and the N on a wedged bond at the 1,4-positions. They sit on opposite faces, so the ring is trans. Working through the neighbour order at each carbon gives `[C@@H]` at the O-bearing carbon and `[C@H]` at the N-bearing carbon.
- **Glutarimide C3:** the bond to the isoindoline nitrogen is hashed, with the implicit H toward the viewer. This gives `[C@H]` in the SMILES order used below.
