This is a maleimidocaproyl–Gly‑Gly‑L‑Phe‑Gly linker joined through a p‑aminobenzyl carbamate to exatecan. I read it in sections and took the stereochemistry from the wedges plus the known configurations.

- **Maleimide end:** a maleimide N‑linked to a pentamethylene chain ending in an amide carbonyl (6‑maleimidocaproyl).
- **Peptide:** NH‑CH2‑CO (Gly), NH‑CH2‑CO (Gly), NH‑CH(CH2Ph)‑CO (Phe, drawn with a bold wedge, L/S), NH‑CH2‑CO (Gly).
- **Spacer:** the last Gly NH is attached to a CH2 on a para‑substituted benzene. The other ring CH2 carries O–C(=O)–NH, a carbamate onto the drug.
- **Drug:** exatecan, a camptothecin analogue with a fluoro, methyl‑substituted aromatic ring and an extra six‑membered ring bearing the carbamate NH at a hashed stereocentre. The lactone has a tertiary C(OH)(Et) with a wedged OH.
- **Stereochemistry:** I did not derive the two drug stereocentres from the wedges alone. I took the exatecan SMILES from memory (PubChem) and substituted the free NH2 with the carbamate NH, which leaves the chirality order unchanged. Phe is written as the L form.
