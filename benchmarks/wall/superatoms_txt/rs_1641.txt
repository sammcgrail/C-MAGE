I read the molecule as a benzoxazol-2-one bearing a chlorine and an alanine methyl ester side chain.

- **Ring system:** a benzene ring is fused to a five-membered oxazolone ring. The ring contains O, a C=O, and an N–H, so it is a 2-oxo-2,3-dihydrobenzoxazole.
- **Ring substituents:** the carbon next to the ring CH that is adjacent to the C–O carbon carries the CH2 side chain. The neighbouring ring carbon carries Cl, which sits next to the CH adjacent to the C–N carbon.
- **Side chain:** CH2 leads to an alpha carbon bearing NH2 and a CO2CH3 group, so the unit is an amino acid methyl ester.
- **Stereochemistry:** the NH2 bond is hashed, which places NH2 behind the plane and the implicit H toward the viewer.
  - The CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H.
  - Viewed from the front, N → CO2Me → CH2 runs counterclockwise. Because H points toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving clockwise, which is R.
  - In the SMILES below, `[C@H]` with neighbour order CO2Me, H, N, CH2 corresponds to R.
