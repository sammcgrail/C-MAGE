The drawing shows a diaza-18-crown-6 macrocycle (1,10-diaza-18-crown-6) carrying a substituted picolinate arm on each nitrogen.

**Macrocycle.** Going around the ring from the left nitrogen: N, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, N, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, back to the first N. That gives 2 N and 4 O, with ethylene links between each heteroatom.

**Left arm.** The left nitrogen bears a CH2 attached to a pyridine ring. Numbering the ring from the nitrogen, C2 carries the CH2 (to the macrocycle), C4 carries the O–CH2CH2–C6H4–NH2 group (para-aminophenethyl ether), and C6 carries the CO2Me ester.

**Right arm.** The right nitrogen bears a CH2 attached to a pyridine C2, with a CO2Me group on C6 and no other substituents.
