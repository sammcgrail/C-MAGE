I read the molecule as a symmetric dimer, two identical SMAC-mimetic-type halves joined by a hexane-1,6-diyl diether linker. I derived the stereodescriptors from the wedges and hashes on the left half and carried them over to the right half, which is the same unit rotated 180°.

**One half:**
- An N-methyl-L-alanine amide, drawn with the methyl on a hash, which gives S.
- A fused bicyclic core: a seven-membered ring containing S and N, fused to a pyrrolidine that carries a gem-dimethyl group. The ring carbons are the amide-bearing C–H (S), the S–CH–N carbon (S, H on a hash) and the carbon bearing the carboxamide (S, on a wedge). The core also has a lactam C=O.
- A carboxamide whose N is attached to C1 of a 2-oxy-indane. The N and O are both on hashes, which gives S at C1 and R at C2.

**Linker:** the two indane C2 oxygens are joined by a chain of six CH2 groups.

I wrote the right half in reverse order, so its chirality tags were recomputed from the neighbour ordering rather than copied. The ring-closure digits were checked for reuse and for closing the correct atoms.
