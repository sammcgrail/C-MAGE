**Left half, from the drawing:**

- **Bicyclic core:** a seven‑membered ring (S, two CH2, the C–NH carbon, C=O, N, and the S–CH bridgehead) fused to a five‑membered ring (N, bridgehead CH, CH2, CMe2, and the CH bearing the carboxamide). By my CIP reading the three ring stereocentres are S (the C–NH carbon), S (the S–CH bridgehead, H hashed) and S (the carboxamide‑bearing CH).

**Right half:** it is the left half rotated 180°. I checked the C–NH carbon and the alanine centre directly (both S), and took the remaining centres to match the left half by that symmetry.
