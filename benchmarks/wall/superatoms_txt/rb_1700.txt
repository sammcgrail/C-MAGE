This is a PROTAC-type molecule built from three parts. I read the stereochemistry as matching the lovastatin core rather than deriving each centre independently from the wedges.

- **Left:** a thalidomide-type unit, with a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the phthalimide nitrogen. The ring carries an NH at the 4-position of the isoindoline-1,3-dione.
- **Linker:** the 4-NH is acylated as NH–C(=O)–CH2–NH–C(=O)–CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, which ends in a carbamate NH–C(=O)–O.
- **Right:** a lovastatin-like hexahydronaphthalene core carrying the carbamate oxygen at C1, with the two ring methyls and the ring diene. A CH2CH2 chain links it to the 4-hydroxy-6-oxotetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone.

The thalidomide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label in the SMILES.
