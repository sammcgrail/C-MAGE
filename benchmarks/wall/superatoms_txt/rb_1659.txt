I read the molecule as three connected parts.

1. **Thalidomide-type end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the nitrogen of a phthalimide. The amino substituent sits at the 4-position of the phthalimide ring.
2. **Linker:** NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, which ends in a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O.
3. **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen is on C1 of a hexahydronaphthalene.
   - The ring has methyls on C3 and C7, with double bonds at C4=C4a and C5=C6.
   - C8 carries an ethylene chain to a six-membered lactone.
   - The lactone has a triisopropylsilyl ether (O–Si(iPr)3) on the carbon next to the CH2–C(=O).

For the stereochemistry I did not trace each wedge and hash individually. The ring skeleton matches simvastatin, so I used simvastatin's configuration at the ring and lactone stereocentres, and I did not check the drawn wedges against it. The carbon bearing the piperidinedione is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
