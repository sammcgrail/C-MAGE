The drawing shows a six-membered 1,3-oxazinane ring (tetrahydro-1,3-oxazine). Reading around the ring:

- O1 is bonded to a CH2 that leads to the quaternary carbon C5, and to a second CH2 (C2) that leads to N3.
- N3 carries an isopropyl group.
- C4 is a CH2 between N3 and C5.
- C5 is quaternary and bears both a nitro group (O2N) and a CH2–NH–isopropyl side chain.

SMILES: CC(C)NCC1(CN(C(C)C)CO C1)[N+](=O)[O-], written without the stray space as CC(C)NCC1(COCN(C1)C(C)C)[N+](=O)[O-]

Formula check: the carbons are 3 (isopropyl) + 1 (CH2) + 1 (C5) + 2 (ring CH2 next to C5) + 1 (C2) + 3 (isopropyl) = 11. That gives C11H23N3O3, and the mass is 11(12.011) + 23(1.008) + 3(14.007) + 3(15.999) = 245.32, which matches the F.W. in the image.
