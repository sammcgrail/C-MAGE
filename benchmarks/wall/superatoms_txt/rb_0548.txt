I read this as an N-acetylated cyclic peptide: Ac-Nle-cyclo[Glu-His-D-Phe(2-Me)-Arg-Trp] closed through a 1,4-diaminobutane linker. The Glu side-chain amide connects through NH-(CH2)4-NH to the Trp C-terminal carbonyl.

Reading by region:
- **N-terminus:** acetyl on norleucine (n-butyl side chain), with the butyl bond hashed.
- **Glu:** the γ-carboxyl is the macrocycle amide, with the side chain on a wedge.
- **His:** imidazole attached at its 4-position through CH2, hashed.
- **Phe:** 2-methylbenzyl side chain on a wedge.
- **Arg:** a three-carbon chain ending in a guanidine, hashed.
- **Trp:** indol-3-ylmethyl side chain, hashed.

Stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes: Nle, Glu, His, Arg and Trp come out L. The 2-methyl-Phe comes out D. In the SMILES, residues written from the carbonyl side use `[C@H]` for L and `[C@@H]` for D.
