The drawing shows a sulfonamide with two aromatic rings.

**Left ring (sulfonyl side):** a benzene ring with OMe at the top, Cl at the left, and the S(=O)(=O) attached at the bottom right. The sulfonyl-bearing carbon is C1, the OMe carbon is C2, and the Cl carbon is C5. That makes it 5-chloro-2-methoxybenzenesulfonyl.

**Linker:** the sulfur carries two =O and is bonded to NH.

**Right ring (amine side):** the NH-bearing carbon is C1. Going around the ring:
- C1: attached to N.
- C2 (top): methyl.
- C3 (upper right): pyrrolidin-1-yl.
- C4 (lower right): methyl.
- C5 (bottom): H.
- C6 (lower left): methyl.

So the ring is 2,4,6-trimethyl-3-(pyrrolidin-1-yl)phenyl.

**SMILES:** the left ring, written from the sulfonyl carbon, is c1(S...)cc(Cl)ccc1OC. The right ring is c1(C)c(N2CCCC2)c(C)cc(C)c1N.

SMILES: COc1ccc(Cl)cc1S(=O)(=O)Nc1c(C)c(N2CCCC2)c(C)cc1C
