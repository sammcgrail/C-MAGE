This is nimesulide: a benzene ring carrying an NH–SO2–Me group, an O–Ph group and a nitro group.

Reading the labels, "MeO2S" is a methylsulfonyl group, CH3S(=O)(=O)–, attached to NH. "Ph" is attached to O, giving a phenoxy group. "NO2" is a nitro group.

Ring positions: the carbon bearing NH is C1. The adjacent carbon bearing OPh is C2. The next carbon, C3, is CH, and C4 carries NO2. The remaining ring carbons are CH.

The nitro-bearing carbon is the vertex at upper right (1097, 777). From the OPh carbon (722, 737), the path goes to the CH at (921, 649), then to the nitro carbon. The nitro group is therefore meta to OPh and para to NH, since NH sits at (700, 953) and the nitro carbon is opposite it. This matches nimesulide.
