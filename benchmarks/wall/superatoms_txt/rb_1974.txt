The molecule is a PROTAC-type degrader. It joins an entrectinib-like indazole benzamide to a VHL ligand (hydroxyproline / tert-leucine / 4-methylthiazolylbenzylamine) through a piperazine and a decanedioyl (sebacoyl) linker.

**Left-hand ligand**
- The 3,5-difluorobenzyl group sits on C5 of a 1H-indazole.
- The indazole C3 carries an NH–C(=O) amide to a benzene ring.
- On that benzene ring, ortho to the amide, is an NH-(tetrahydropyran-4-yl) group.
- Para to the amide is a piperazine nitrogen.

**Linker**
- The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated by a decanedioyl chain: C(=O), eight CH2, C(=O).
- The chain atoms from carbonyl to carbonyl total ten.

**VHL ligand**
- The far carbonyl forms an amide with the NH of tert-leucine, which is drawn with a hashed bond.
- The tert-leucine carbonyl is attached to the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine-2-carboxamide.
- The pyrrolidine C2 carries a hashed bond and C4 the wedged OH. I assigned the stereodescriptors by comparison with the standard VHL ligand (2S,4R, tert-leucine S), checking the permutation parity of each centre.
- The carboxamide nitrogen bears a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzyl group.
