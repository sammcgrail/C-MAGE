The drawing shows a pyridine ring joined through an amide to a spiro-fused cyclohexane.

**Pyridine part:** The ring nitrogen is at the bottom left. Going around the ring, C2 carries the C(=O)N amide, C3 carries OH, C4 carries OMe, and C5 and C6 are CH. The pyridine fragment is therefore c1(C(=O)N...)nccc(OC)c1O.

**Spiro ring:** The cyclohexane has the following atoms:
- C1 bears the NH.
- C2 is a CH2 leading to the spiro carbon.
- The spiro carbon C3 is also part of a cyclopropane that carries CF2.
- C4 is a CH2.
- C5 is the gem-dimethyl carbon.
- C6 is a CH2 that returns to C1.

The cyclopropane consists of the spiro carbon, a CH2, and a CF2.
