The drawing shows a fluorene skeleton: two benzene rings joined by a five-membered ring whose CH carbon (C9) carries the wavy line. That wavy line is the attachment point, so it becomes one * in the SMILES.

There is also a small dot at the right edge, near the lower ring. It looks like a stray mark, so I ignored it.

The SMILES is therefore a 9-fluorenyl group with the attachment point on C9:

*C1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12

Checking atom by atom: C1 is the CH carbon. It bonds to an aromatic ring (c2ccccc2) whose last atom is joined by a single bond to a second aromatic ring (c2ccccc12), and that ring closes back to C1. This gives the five-membered ring C9, two carbons of the first ring, and two carbons of the second ring.

SMILES: *C1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12
