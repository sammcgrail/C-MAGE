The drawing is a PROTAC-type molecule. I read it as three parts joined by a linker.

**Left fragment.** A pyrimidine carries a 2-fluoro-6-methoxyphenyl group at C2 and a carboxamide at C4.
- The phenyl ring is c1(F)cccc(OC)c1 attached to the pyrimidine C2.
- Pyrimidine SMILES: c1(-c2c(F)cccc2OC)nccc(n1)C(=O)N...

**Central aryl ring.** The amide NH is attached to a benzene ring.
- Ortho to the NH is a piperazine N.
- Para to the NH (at the ring carbon below the piperazine-bearing carbon) is a 4-cyanopyridin-3-yl group.
- The ring is c1(NC(=O)...)c(N2CCN(CC2)...)cc(-c3cnccc3C#N)cc1.
- Checking the positions: C1 carries the NH, C2 carries the piperazine, C3 is CH, C4 carries the pyridyl, then C5 and C6 are CH. The pyridyl sits para to the NH, which matches the drawing.

**Pyridine.** It is attached at C3, with the CN on the adjacent C4. The ring is c3cnccc3C#N: c3 (attachment), c (C2), n (N1), c (C6), c (C5), c3 (C4) bearing C#N.

**Linker.** The piperazine N is acylated: C(=O)CCOCCOCCN-, giving C(=O), two CH2, O, two CH2, O, two CH2, then NH.

**Right fragment.** The NH is attached to the 4-position of a thalidomide-type isoindoline-1,3-dione, whose N bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
- Isoindoline part: c1cccc2c1C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C2=O.
