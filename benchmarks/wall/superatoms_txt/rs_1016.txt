The drawing shows a pyrrolidine ring, a five-membered ring with one nitrogen and four carbons. That nitrogen is bonded to a central carbon, C.

The central carbon carries three substituents:

- a single bond to the ring nitrogen,
- a single bond to NH–NH2 (a hydrazine group).

The C=N double bond is drawn without a defined cis/trans geometry, so I give no stereo marks.
