The drawing shows a central carbon bonded to three groups:
- a tBu label, which expands to C(C)(C)C
- a double-bonded O (C=O)
- a PivO label, which is a pivaloyloxy group, OC(=O)C(C)(C)C

So the central carbon is a carbonyl carbon carrying tBu and a PivO group. That makes the molecule pivalic anhydride: tBu-C(=O)-O-C(=O)-tBu.

Atom check: the central carbon has the tBu, the =O and the PivO oxygen, so it has four bonds. The PivO group contributes O–C(=O)–C(CH3)3.
