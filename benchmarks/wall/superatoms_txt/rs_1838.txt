**Fragments**
- **Morpholine amide:** the morpholine nitrogen is acylated by a C=O, which is followed by a CH2.
- **Stereocentre 1:** the CH2 leads to a CH carrying a hashed bond down to a CH2–CH2–C(CH3)3 chain (the quaternary carbon bears three methyls). Its other substituent is a C(=O)NH amide.
- **Stereocentre 2:** the amide N is bonded to a CH carrying a wedged methyl and a CH2–NH–aryl arm.
- **Aryl group:** the aniline NH is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring bearing OCF3.

**Stereochemistry**
- **Stereocentre 1 (hashed chain):** the hashed chain points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Working through the neighbour order in the SMILES gives `[C@@H]`.
- **Stereocentre 2 (wedged methyl):** the wedged methyl points toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away. Working through the neighbour order in the SMILES gives `[C@@H]`.
