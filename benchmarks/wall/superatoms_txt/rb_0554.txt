This is a PROTAC-type molecule. I read it in sections from left to right.

**Left aryl group.** The benzene ring carries a CH2N(CH3)2 group at the position between two OMe groups. The ring attaches to the thienopyridinone at the position para to the CH2NMe2 carbon. SMILES for this fragment: c1(OC)c(CN(C)C)c(OC)cc(c1)-

**Thieno[3,2-c]pyridin-4-one core.**
- The thiophene S is bonded to C2, which carries the carboxamide, and to C7a.
- C7a is bonded to C7, which carries the aryl group.
- C7 is bonded to C6H, which is bonded to N5(CH3).
- N5 is bonded to C4(=O), which is bonded to C3a, and C3a to C3H.
- C3H is double-bonded to C2.

SMILES for the core: c1(-Ar)c2sc(C(=O)N...)cc2c(=O)n(C)c1. Written with explicit ring closures: O=C1N(C)C=C(Ar)C2=C1C=C(C(=O)N...)S2.

**Amide and spiro linker.** The amide N is bonded to a cyclobutane CH. The cyclobutane has a spiro carbon that is also part of a piperidine ring, so this is a 7-azaspiro[3.5]nonane. The cyclobutane CH is bonded to two CH2 groups, and both CH2 groups are bonded to the spiro carbon.
- Fragment: NC1CC2(C1)CCN(CC2)C(=O)...

**Chain to the piperazine.** The piperidine N is acylated: C(=O)CCN3CCN(CC3)-Ar.

**Isoindoline-dione.** The piperazine N is bonded to the aryl ring of the isoindoline-1,3-dione, at the 5-position. The isoindoline N is bonded to the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
- Fragment: c1ccc2c(c1)C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C2=O

**Full SMILES:**
CN(C)Cc1c(OC)cc(cc1OC)C1=CN(C)C(=O)C2=C1SC(=C2)C(=O)NC1CC2(C1)CCN(CC2)C(=O)CCN1CCN(CC1)c1ccc2C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C(=O)c2c1

The cyclobutane uses ring label 1, and that label is reused later for the piperazine. By that point ring 1 has already closed (C1CC2(C1)), so the reuse is valid. Inside the spiro fragment, ring 2 is opened at the spiro carbon and closed at the end of the piperidine (CC2), so it is also balanced. The thiophene closure is C2=C1SC(=C2): the label 2 opens at "C2=C1" and closes at "=C2", and ring 1 closes at the S-bearing carbon, which is consistent with the core above.
