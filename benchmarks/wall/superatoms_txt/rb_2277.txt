I read this as a cyclic peptide closed by a thioether bridge between two homocysteine-type residues. One part of the drawing is ambiguous: the N-terminal Asp nitrogen is attached to a wavy-bonded carbon that carries a methyl. I wrote that group as an isopropyl on the nitrogen.

Reading the ring from the N-terminal Asp, each residue's carbonyl joins the NH of the next:

Asp – Hcy(1) – Ala – Trp – His – Leu – Gly – Glu – Leu – Val – Trp – Hcy(2) – Thr-NH2

- **Thioether bridge:** each Hcy side chain is CH2CH2, and the two chains are joined through one sulfur (Cα–CH2–CH2–S–CH2–CH2–Cα).
- **Hcy(1):** its hashed N-bond gives S configuration, so it is L.
- **Hcy(2):** its hashed H gives R configuration, so it is D.
- **Other residues:** I took all other stereocentres as L, based on the wedges shown. For the C-terminal Thr (primary amide) I wrote the L-Thr configuration from memory of the known SMILES, rather than re-deriving it from the drawn wedge and hash.
- **Ring closure:** I used ring-closure label 1 for the sulfur bridge. Label 2 is reused for the Trp and His side-chain rings.
