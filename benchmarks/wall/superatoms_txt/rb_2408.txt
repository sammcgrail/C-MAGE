**Central linker.** The chain runs CH(COO)–CH2CH2–C(=O)–CH2–CH, as drawn, including the ketone. The CH carries a C(=O)NH arm and a CH2CH2C(=O)NH arm. The wedge on this centre fixes it as [C@@H] in the SMILES order used.

I assigned L-Arg, L-Asp, L-Lys and D-Tyr from the "RGDyK" name and the drawn wedges, then converted these to @/@@ by neighbour-order permutation. The left and right peptides are written identically.
