This is an MMAE-type payload attached through a Val-Cit-PAB linker to a cysteic acid unit, which is in turn linked to a bis-amide scaffold carrying two bromomethylpyridine groups.

**Left-hand fragment.** The central carbon bears CH2NH on each side and a C(=O)NH group that leads to cysteic acid. Each CH2NH is acylated by a 2,6-bis(bromomethyl)pyridine-4-carbonyl group.
- Pyridine unit: C(=O)c1cc(CBr)nc(CBr)c1
- Core: C(CNC(=O)Ar)(CNC(=O)Ar)C(=O)N...

**Peptide chain.**
- Cysteic acid: NC(CS(=O)(=O)O)C(=O)
- Valine: NC(C(C)C)C(=O)
- Citrulline: NC(CCCNC(N)=O)C(=O)
- PAB: Nc1ccc(COC(=O)...)cc1
- Carbamate: OC(=O)N(C)C(C(C)C)C(=O)

**MMAE portion.**
- N-Me-Val is linked to Val: NC(C(C)C)C(=O)
- Then N(C)C(C(C)CC)C(OC)CC(=O)N1CCCC1
- Dolaproine: C(OC)C(C)C(=O)
- Norephedrine: NC(C)C(O)c1ccccc1

**Assembly.** The SMILES is written without stereochemistry, since the drawing shows no wedges or hashes.
