I read this as a thalidomide-based PROTAC: a thalidomide aryl ether linked through an amide and a diethylene-glycol diamine chain to a carbamate on a statin core.

- **Thalidomide end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the isoindoline-1,3-dione nitrogen. The aromatic ring carries an O–CH2–C(=O)–NH substituent at the 4-position.
- **Linker:** NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, ending in a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O.
- **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on the ring carbon that would carry the 2-methylbutanoate ester in lovastatin. The core is therefore the lovastatin framework with the ester side chain replaced, which matches monacolin J. It has the hexahydronaphthalene with two methyls and a ring diene. A CH2CH2 chain leads to the 4-hydroxy-6-oxotetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone.
- **Stereochemistry:** I did not assign each wedge and hash independently. I assumed the wedge/hash pattern matches lovastatin and took the stereo descriptors from the known lovastatin SMILES, replacing the ester oxygen's acyl group with the carbamate. The thalidomide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
