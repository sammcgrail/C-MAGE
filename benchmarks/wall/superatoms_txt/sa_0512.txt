The drawing is a flavone (apigenin skeleton) with a 5-OH, a 7-OMOM and a 4'-OMOM group.

MOM is methoxymethyl, –CH2OCH3, so OMOM is –O–CH2–O–CH3. The label "MOMO" on the left is the same group written in reverse.

Core atoms: the pyranone ring is O1, C2 (bearing the aryl group), C3, C4 (=O), C4a and C8a. The A ring carries OH at C5 and OMOM at C7.

SMILES: O=C1C=C(c2ccc(OCOC)cc2)Oc2cc(OCOC)cc(O)c12

Check: C1(=O) is C4, C=C is C3=C2, and C2 carries the aryl group. Oc2 is O1 attached to C8a. Then cc(OCOC) gives C8 and C7 with its OMOM. cc(O) gives C6 and C5 with OH, and c12 is C4a, which closes both rings. This matches the drawing.
