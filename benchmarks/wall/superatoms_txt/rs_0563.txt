I read the drawing as a pyrido-fused benzoxazepine tricycle joined through its ring nitrogen to a pyrrolidine, with the substituents below.

**Tricycle**
- The benzene ring carries a Cl. It sits on the carbon meta to the ring carbon bonded to the N, and para to the ring carbon bonded to the CH2.
- A CH2 and an O link the benzene ring to the pyridine ring, forming the seven-membered oxazepine ring.
- The oxazepine ring nitrogen is bonded to the pyridine carbon next to the pyridine N. The O is bonded to the neighbouring pyridine carbon.

**Side chain**
- The oxazepine nitrogen carries a CH2 that is attached to C2 of a pyrrolidine.
- The pyrrolidine nitrogen carries a CH2CH2 chain ending in a 4-methoxyphenyl group.

**Stereochemistry**
- Only the pyrrolidine C2 is a drawn stereocentre. The bond to the exocyclic CH2 is hashed, so that group points away from the viewer and the H points toward the viewer.
- CIP priorities are ring N, then the exocyclic CH2 (N,H,H), then ring C3 (C,H,H), then H.
- In the drawing, N to exocyclic CH2 to ring C3 runs counterclockwise. With H toward the viewer this reverses, so the centre is R.
- In the SMILES below, the neighbour order at that carbon is C3, H, N, exocyclic CH2. This order is S with `@`, so R needs `@@`.

SMILES: COc1ccc(CCN2CCC[C@@H]2CN2c3cc(Cl)ccc3COc3cccnc32)cc1
