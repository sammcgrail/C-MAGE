I read the molecule as 4-[2-[(2R)-2-[(E)-3-[1-(4-chlorophenyl)cyclopentyl]-3-hydroxyprop-1-enyl]-5-oxopyrrolidin-1-yl]ethyl]benzoic acid.

- **Left ring:** a pyrrolidin-2-one, with the C=O at the top and N at the upper right.
- **N-substituent:** a CH2CH2 chain leading to a benzene ring that carries CO2H para to the chain.
- **C5 of the lactam (ring carbon next to N):** it bears a vinyl group drawn on a wedge.
- **Side chain from that carbon:** CH=CH, drawn trans, then CH(OH), which is a plain stereocentre with no stereo shown. This carbon is attached to a quaternary cyclopentane carbon, and that carbon also bears a 4-chlorophenyl group.

For the stereocentre at the lactam carbon, the substituents are N, the wedged vinyl, the ring CH2 and an implied H pointing away. Priorities are N > vinyl > ring CH2. Going N (up) → vinyl (down-right) → CH2 (left) is clockwise with H at the back, so the centre is R. In the SMILES below this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.

The CH(OH) carbon is left without stereo marks because it is drawn with plain bonds.
