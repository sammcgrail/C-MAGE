The drawing shows an imidazo[4,5-b]pyridine: a pyridine ring fused to an imidazole ring.

**Pyridine ring.** Going around the ring:
- C5 bears NH2.
- N4 is the ring nitrogen, drawn with a double bond to C5.
- C3a is the fusion carbon next to that nitrogen, attached to the NH of the imidazole.
- C7a is the other fusion carbon, attached to the imidazole N.
- C7 bears Cl.
- C6 is the CH between C7 and C5.

The double bonds in the pyridine ring are C5=N4, C6=C7, and the fusion bond C3a=C7a.

**Imidazole ring.**
- The N on the left is drawn as =N, with a double bond to C2.
- C2 bears OEt.
- The NH on the right is attached to C3a.
