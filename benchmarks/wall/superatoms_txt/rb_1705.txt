I read the molecule as an EZH2-inhibitor-type benzamide joined through a piperazine-acetamide and ether linker to a VHL-type ligand.

- **Left end:** a 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl methyl group, attached through NH to a benzamide C=O.
- **Central benzene ring:**
  - It carries a methyl group ortho to the amide.
  - It carries an N-ethyl-N-(tetrahydropyran-4-yl)amino group.
  - It carries a para-substituted phenyl ring (drawn with unusual double bonds, read here as an ordinary aromatic phenyl). That phenyl bears a CH2 attached to a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Linker:** the second piperazine nitrogen is attached to CH2–C(=O)–NH–(CH2)3–O–(CH2)2–NH.
- **VHL ligand end:** that NH is bonded to the tert-leucine carbon, which has a tert-butyl group and a C=O. The C=O acylates a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine, whose 2-carboxamide is attached to a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamine.
- **Stereochemistry:** the hashed and wedged bonds are at the tert-leucine carbon and at C2 and C4 of the hydroxyproline. I assigned them to match the standard VH032 amine SMILES, which I recalled rather than derived from the drawing.
