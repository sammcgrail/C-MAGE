The drawing shows a benzamide ring with these substituents:

- **Amide carbon:** attached at C1 of the ring, with C=O and an NH leading to CH2CH2NEt2.
- **OMe:** on the ring carbon adjacent to C1 (the lower-left position).
- **NH2:** on the ring carbon at the far right.
- **Cl:** on the ring carbon between the NH2 carbon and the CH that sits next to C1.

Going around the ring from the amide-bearing carbon (C1): C1 (amide), C2 (OMe), C3 (H), C4 (NH2), C5 (Cl), C6 (H). This gives a 4-amino-5-chloro-2-methoxybenzamide, which is the metoclopramide skeleton.
