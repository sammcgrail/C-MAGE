The drawing shows benzoin: a phenyl group on a CH that carries H and OH, bonded to a C=O that carries the second phenyl group.

The bonds to the stereocentre are all plain lines, with no wedges or hashes, so I leave out stereochemistry.

Ph–CH(OH)–C(=O)–Ph gives the SMILES O=C(c1ccccc1)C(O)c1ccccc1.
