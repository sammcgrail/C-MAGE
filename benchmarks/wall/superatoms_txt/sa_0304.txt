I read this as repaglinide, the (S)-enantiomer.

- **Left ring:** a benzene ring carrying a carboxylic acid (HO2C), an ethoxy group (Et–O) on the adjacent carbon, and a CH2 on the carbon meta to the ethoxy group.
- **Linker:** the CH2 is bonded to a C(=O)–NH amide.
- **Stereocentre:** the amide nitrogen is bonded to a carbon that carries an isobutyl group (iBu, drawn on a wedge) and a second benzene ring.
- **Right ring:** this benzene has a piperidine nitrogen on the carbon ortho to the stereocentre.

For the stereocentre, the priorities are N, then the aryl carbon, then the isobutyl CH2, then H. The wedged isobutyl group points toward the viewer, so H points away. Going N (upper left) to aryl (bottom) to isobutyl (upper right) is counterclockwise, which gives S. This matches the SMILES written with `N[C@@H](CC(C)C)c...`.

SMILES: CCOc1cc(CC(=O)N[C@@H](CC(C)C)c2ccccc2N2CCCCC2)ccc1C(=O)O
