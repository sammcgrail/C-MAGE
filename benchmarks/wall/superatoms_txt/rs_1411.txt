The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl oxindole.

**Left part.** The top-left ring carries CO2H. A second ring is attached to it at the meta position. On that second ring, the carbon next to the biaryl bond bears OH, and the carbon next to that bears NH–N=.

**Hydrazone.** The NH–N= unit links to C3 of the oxindole, so this is a 3-hydrazono-oxindole.

**Oxindole.** C2 is C=O, and the ring nitrogen carries a 4-isopropylphenyl group. The benzo ring of the oxindole has two fluorines. Numbering from C3a: C4 is the carbon next to C3a and bears F, and C5 bears the second F. In the drawing, the upper-right carbon of the benzo ring carries F and the next one to the right also carries F, so the fluorines are at C4 and C5.
