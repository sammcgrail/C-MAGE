I read the molecule in four parts and joined them.

4. **Right end:** the chain oxygen is on C5 of an isoindolin-1-one. The lactam nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a piperidine-2,6-dione (glutarimide).

The glutarimide C3 is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave no stereo marks.
