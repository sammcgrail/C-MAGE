I read the molecule as a symmetric, carbamate-linked bis-amine, with undecyl chains counted from the zigzag spacing of about 58 px per carbon.

**Left half**
- Two undecyl amide arms, each CH3(CH2)10–NH–C(=O)–CH2–, are attached to one tertiary nitrogen.
- That nitrogen connects through a three-carbon chain (CH2CH2CH2) to a second tertiary nitrogen.
- The second nitrogen carries a 3-hydroxybutyl group (CH2CH2CH(OH)CH3) and a CH2CH2–O chain leading to the carbamate.

**Carbamate link**
- The unit is O–C(=O)–NH, followed by CH2CH2 to the right-hand nitrogen.

**Right half**
- This is the mirror of the left half. The nitrogen after the carbamate's CH2CH2 carries a 3-hydroxybutyl group, then a CH2CH2CH2 chain, then a nitrogen bearing two CH2–C(=O)–NH–undecyl arms.

The chain-carbon count is the least certain part of the reading. The hydroxyl-bearing carbons are stereocentres by connectivity, but they are drawn with plain bonds, so I gave them no stereo marks.
