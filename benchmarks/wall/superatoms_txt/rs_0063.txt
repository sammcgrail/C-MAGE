The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- N1 is the ring nitrogen.
- C2 carries the carboxamide, C(=O)NH-aryl.
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are unsubstituted CH.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a benzene ring:
- C1 bears the NH.
- C2 bears CH2OCH3.
- C4, para to the NH, bears an O-aryl group, and that aryl is a 4-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl.
