This SMILES is my reading of the drawing, with all residues assumed L-configured. The stereo was checked against the drawing only for the Glu in the left lipid chain and for the Pen2 alpha carbon. The other centres are inferred, not verified.

**Peptide backbone.** I read it as Ac-Pen-Asn-Thr-Trp(7-Me)-Lys-Pen-Tyr(OCH2CH2NH2)-2Nal-Thp-Lys-Asn-3Pal-Sar-NH2:
- The two Pen (penicillamine) residues are joined through an S–S bond, which closes the macrocycle. Pen1 carries the N-acetyl group.
- The Trp is 7-methylindol-3-yl.
- The Tyr has a 2-aminoethyl ether on its phenol oxygen.
- 2Nal is 2-naphthylalanine.
- Thp is 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid.
- 3Pal is 3-pyridylalanine.
- The C-terminus is N-methylglycinamide (sarcosine amide).

**Lipid side chains.** Both lysine side chains (the macrocycle Lys and the later Lys) carry the same substituent: two 8-amino-3,6-dioxaoctanoyl (AEEA) linkers, then γ-Glu, then an N-acyl chain. I counted 14 carbons in the acyl chain on the left, giving tetradecanoyl. The right chain I assumed identical by symmetry, since I could not count its vertices reliably.
