I read the molecule as a DBCO–PEG4–Val–Cit–PAB linker carrying a tubulysin-type payload, and wrote the SMILES fragment by fragment.

**Linker end**
- DBCO: the strained dibenzo-azacyclooctyne, with N attached to a CH2 and to an aryl ring, and the alkyne bridging the two benzo rings.
- The DBCO nitrogen carries a succinamide (C(=O)CCC(=O)N) leading into the PEG4 chain.
- The PEG4 chain is NH–CH2CH2–(OCH2CH2)₄–C(=O), with four ether oxygens counted in the drawing.
- Next come L-Val and L-citrulline (ureido side chain), then a p-aminobenzyl carbamate (PAB) attached through O–C(=O)–NH to the aryl ring of the payload.

**Payload**
- A tubuphenylalanine-type unit carries a gem-dimethyl carboxylic acid; its amine-bearing carbon is the stereocentre.
- It is amide-linked to a thiazole-4-carboxamide. The thiazole bears a CH(OAc) centre, then CH2, then a CH(iPr) centre.
- That CH(iPr) centre carries an N-(pent-4-ynyloxy) tertiary amide nitrogen, written N(OCCCC#C).
- The chain ends in isoleucine and N-methylpipecolic acid (Mep).

**Stereochemistry**
- Val and Cit are L.
- The OAc carbon and the CH(iPr)N carbon are both R, which matches the wedges as I read them.
- The tubuphenylalanine carbon was set from the wedged N bond.
- Ile is taken as the natural L-Ile (2S,3S), and Mep as the R (D) configuration. I assumed these two from the natural tubulysin series rather than reading every wedge and hash in the drawing.
