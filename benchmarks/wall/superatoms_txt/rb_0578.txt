**Thieno[3,2-c]pyridin-4-one core.**
- The thiophene S is bonded to C2, which carries the carboxamide, and to C7a.
- C3 is the CH next to C2, and C3a bonds to C4 (C=O).
- N5 carries the methyl group, C6 is CH, and C7 carries the aryl group and bonds to C7a.
- SMILES for the core: c1(C(=O)N...)cc2c(s1)c(Ar)cn(C)c2=O.

- SMILES: C1(CNC(=O)...)OCC2(C1)CCN(CC2)...
