I read the molecule as a rifamycin-type ansa macrocycle fused to a phenoxazine, with a dimethylaminoethoxy-ethoxy side chain on the phenoxazine ring. I did not check the SMILES with any software.

**Core and side chain**
- The naphthoquinone-furanone core has a 1-oxo furanone and a ketal carbon carrying a methyl group. It also has an aromatic methyl, an aromatic OH and a quinone C=O.
- The amide NH sits on the quinone ring.
- A phenoxazine is fused onto this core through the ring N and O, and its benzene ring carries an OH.
- The other benzene carbon carries the –O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–N(CH3)2 chain.

**Ansa chain**
- From the NH, the chain runs: C(=O)–C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–CH(OAc)–CH(CH3)–CH(OMe)–CH=CH–O–, ending at the ketal carbon.
- I took the stereocentres and the double-bond geometry from the standard rifamycin pattern, which the drawn wedges and hashes agree with. I kept the chain in the same atom order as the rifampicin SMILES I recalled, so I could reuse its descriptors.
- For the ketal carbon I re-derived the chirality tag for the new neighbour order and it stays @@.
