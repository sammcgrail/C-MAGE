The drawing shows a central phosphorus atom with three Ph (phenyl) groups and a P=O double bond. This is triphenylphosphine oxide. The bonds are plain, so there is no stereochemistry to include.

Expanding each Ph into a phenyl ring (c1ccccc1) gives O=P(c1ccccc1)(c1ccccc1)c1ccccc1, which matches the drawing atom for atom.

SMILES: O=P(c1ccccc1)(c1ccccc1)c1ccccc1
