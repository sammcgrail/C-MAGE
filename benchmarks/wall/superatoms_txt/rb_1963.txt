I read the structure as a PROTAC: a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand, a PEG linker ending in a sulfonamide, and a pyrimidine-based kinase-inhibitor warhead.

**Left end (cereblon ligand).** A glutarimide ring (NH between two C=O groups, with the CH at position 3) is attached through its ring N to a phthalimide. The phthalimide carries an NH at position 4, which leads into the linker.

**Linker.** From the aryl NH the chain is NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–SO2. Reading the drawing, the oxygens are at four positions (the labels at x≈530, 705, 880 and 1055). The chain runs from the NH: CH2, CH2, O, CH2, CH2, O, CH2, CH2, O, CH2, CH2, O, CH2, CH2, then the NH on the sulfonamide. That gives four oxygens and five ethylene units: NCCOCCOCCOCCOCCN.

**Right end (warhead).**
- The sulfonamide S is attached to a benzene ring.
- On that ring, the position para to S carries the NH that links to the pyrimidine.
- The methyl sits ortho to that NH, which makes it meta to S.
- The pyrimidine is attached through its C2 to this NH. Its C5 carries Br, and its C4 carries an NH that leads to the fluorobenzamide ring.
- On the benzamide ring, the carbon bearing the NH is adjacent to the carbon bearing C(=O)NH2, and F is on the other side of that carboxamide carbon.

**SMILES assembly.**
- Glutarimide and phthalimide: O=C1CCC(N2C(=O)c3c(NCCOCCOCCOCCOCCNS(=O)(=O)...)cccc3C2=O)C(=O)N1
- Sulfonamide aryl ring: c1ccc(Nc2ncc(Br)c(Nc3cccc(F)c3C(N)=O)n2)c(C)c1, with S at the first atom of the ring.
