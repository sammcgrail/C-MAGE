I read the molecule as a macrocycle built from these pieces:

- **Urea cap:** a 2-ethynylazetidine joined through a urea to the NH of the amino acid. The azetidine carbon is (S), with the ethynyl group drawn hashed.
- **Amino acid unit:** an (S) α-carbon bearing the NH, a CH₂ leading to the thiazole, and a C(=O) bonded to a piperazic acid nitrogen.
- **Piperazic acid:** a six-membered ring (Cα, NH, N, and three CH₂). Its (S) α-carbon carries the ester carbonyl, drawn hashed.
- **Ester linker:** C(=O)–O–CH₂–C(CH₃)₂–CH₂, ending on indole C3.
- **Indole:** N-ethyl, with the pyridyl group at C2, the linker at C3, and the thiazole at C5.
- **Thiazole:** its C4 is bonded to the indole, and its C2 carries the CH₂ of the amino acid unit.
- **Pyridine:** the ring carbon next to the nitrogen bears an (S) CH(OMe)CH₃ group (methyl drawn wedged), and the adjacent carbon is bonded to the indole C2. I ignored the bold wedge on that bond, since it only indicates atropisomer geometry.

I assigned CIP priorities at each of the four stereocentres from the drawn wedges and hashes. I then chose the @/@@ symbols so that each centre comes out (S), as labelled.
