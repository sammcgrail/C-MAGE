I read the structure as a morpholine amide, a carbamate and a 4-(trifluoromethoxy)aniline joined by short chains.

**Fragments**
- **Morpholine amide:** the morpholine nitrogen is acylated by a C=O group.
- **First stereocentre:** the carbon next to that C=O carries the carbamate oxygen and a CH2–cyclohexyl group. The wedge on the CH2 bond marks this centre.
- **Carbamate:** the oxygen is joined to C(=O)NH.
- **Second stereocentre:** the carbamate NH is attached to a CH bearing a CH3 on a hashed bond and a CH2 that continues to the aryl NH.
- **Aryl group:** the aryl NH is bonded to a benzene ring carrying OCF3 at the para position.

**Stereochemistry**
- The first stereocentre (wedge on the CH2) works out as S by CIP priorities (O > C(=O)N > CH2 > H).
- The second stereocentre (hashed CH3) also works out as S (N > CH2N > CH3 > H).
- Written in the SMILES atom order below, these are `[C@H]` for the first centre and `[C@@H]` for the second.

SMILES: O=C(N1CCOCC1)[C@H](CC2CCCCC2)OC(=O)N[C@@H](C)CNc1ccc(OC(F)(F)F)cc1
