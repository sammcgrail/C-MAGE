I read the structure as a rifamycin-type ansa macrocycle fused to a phenoxazine, with a side chain on the phenoxazine oxygen. The SMILES below is written from the drawing, but the stereodescriptors come from my own wedge/hash analysis and are not independently checked.

**Core**
- The naphthalene-furanone core carries a methyl group, a phenol OH and a quinone C=O.
- The ring nitrogen N= and the ring oxygen close a phenoxazine, fused to a benzene ring that bears the substituent.
- The amide NH sits on the carbon next to the quinone C=O and joins the macrocycle.

**Side chain on the phenoxazine benzene ring**
- The chain is O–CH2CH2–NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–N(aziridine).
- The aziridine is a three-membered ring containing the terminal N.

**Macrocycle, read from the amide to the ketal carbon**
- The amide C(=O) is attached to C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH. I read the first double bond as cis between the C=O and the chain, and the second as trans.
- Next come CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OAc), CH(CH3) and CH(OMe).
- Then the trans vinyl ether CH=CH–O, which closes onto the ketal carbon.
- The ketal carbon also carries a methyl group and the furanone ring oxygen.

**Stereochemistry**
- I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash bonds: eight centres on the chain, plus the ketal carbon.
