I read this as an antimycin-type nine-membered dilactone carrying an amide-linked pyridine.

**Pyridine side chain.** The pyridine has a 4-OMe group and a 3-O-C(=O)-O-CH2CH(CH3)2 (isobutyl carbonate) group. Its C2 carries the C(=O)NH amide.

**Nine-membered ring.** Going around the ring from the NH-bearing carbon:
- CH(NH) is attached to a ring C(=O), then ring O, then CH(CH3).
- Next is CH bearing an isobutyrate ester, OC(=O)CH(CH3)2, then CH bearing a benzyl group.
- The ring closes through a C(=O), a ring O and a CH2 back to the CH(NH).

**Stereochemistry.** I assigned each centre from the wedges and hashes, treating the implicit H as the opposite face to the drawn wedge or hash:
- The CH(NH) carbon (hashed N) is S.
- The CH(CH3) carbon (wedged methyl) is S.
- The CH(O-isobutyrate) carbon (hashed O) is R.
- The CH(benzyl) carbon (wedged CH2Ph) is R.

I then chose the @/@@ marks in the SMILES so that each centre comes out with these configurations.
