I read the molecule as three parts joined by a nine-carbon amide chain.

**Left part (apremilast-like unit):**
- A 3-ethoxy-4-methoxyphenyl ring is attached to a stereocentre, CH(CH2SO2CH3), which is bonded to the phthalimide nitrogen.
- The phthalimide (1,3-dioxoisoindoline) carries an NH–C(=O) amide at its 4-position.
- The stereocentre has the CH2SO2Me bond drawn hashed. The priorities are N > CH2S > aryl, with H toward the viewer. The sequence N→CH2S→aryl runs clockwise, so reversing for the H gives S, which matches apremilast.

**Linker:**
- Counting vertices from the amide carbonyl to the piperidine nitrogen gives C(=O) followed by 8 CH2 groups, so it is a nonanamide.

**Right part:**
- The chain ends on the nitrogen of a piperidine, which is substituted at C4 by an isoindolinone.
- On the isoindolinone benzene ring, the piperidine-bearing carbon is adjacent to the carbon bearing the CH2, and F sits meta to the piperidine carbon.
- The isoindolinone nitrogen carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl (glutarimide) group, and its lactam carbonyl is C=O.

For the stereocentre I used the apremilast pattern, aryl–[C@@H](CH2S)–N, which is the S configuration.
