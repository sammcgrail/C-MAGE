I read the structure in five parts and joined them into one SMILES.

1. **Pyrazine head group:** a pyrazine carries a carboxamide (C(N)=O) on one carbon. The adjacent carbon bears the NH link to the phenyl ring. The carbon on the other side of the ring nitrogen bears a pyrazole joined through its N1.
2. **Pyrazole:** the CF3 sits on C3, the carbon adjacent to the second pyrazole nitrogen.
3. **Linker:** a para-substituted phenyl ring is attached to C4 of a piperidine. The piperidine nitrogen carries a CH2 that leads to C3 of a pyrrolidine.
4. **Pyrrolidine stereocentre:** this is the only stereocentre drawn with a wedge or hash. The CH2 bond is hashed, so the hydrogen points toward the viewer. Working through the neighbour order in the SMILES (ring C2, H, CH2, ring C4) gives `@@`.
5. **Right-hand end:** the pyrrolidine nitrogen is bonded to C5 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. The imide nitrogen of that group is attached to C3 of a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidine). That glutarimide carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
