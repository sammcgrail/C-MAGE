The drawing shows a central nitrogen bonded to three groups: one ethyl (Et) and two isopropyl (iPr) groups. This is N,N-diisopropylethylamine (Hünig's base).

Expanding the abbreviations gives ethyl as CC and isopropyl as C(C)C, so the SMILES is CCN(C(C)C)C(C)C. There are no wedges, hashes or charges, so no stereochemistry is needed.
