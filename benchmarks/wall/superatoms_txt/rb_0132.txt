I read this as a rifamycin-type phenoxazine: a naphthoquinone core fused to a benzophenoxazine, carrying a 2-(diethylamino)ethoxy group on the benzo ring and the usual ansa macrocycle. The macrocycle runs from the amide NH through the diene, the methyl, hydroxyl, acetoxy and methoxy stereocentres, and the enol ether, back to the ketal carbon.

Stereochemistry:
- Each stereocentre was assigned from the drawn wedges and hashes.
- The trisubstituted C=C next to the amide is cis between the carbonyl carbon and the chain.
- The other two double bonds (the diene CH=CH and the enol ether CH=CH) are drawn trans.
- I judged the two OH bonds on the ansa chain to be hashed, since the image is hard to read at that point. That is an assumption.
