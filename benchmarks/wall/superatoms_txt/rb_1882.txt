I read the drawing as a cyclic undecapeptide-like macrocycle carrying a linear side chain. The stereo-assignments for the top side chain and several ring residues came from the wedges and hashes; for the rest I assumed natural (L) configuration, so those are less certain.

**Side chain (top, from the piperidine end):**
- Piperidine amide, then L-Ala (H wedged, assigned S), then N–H.
- Next come N-Me-Phe and a second N-Me-Phe, each with a CH2Ph group.
- The second N-Me-Phe is acylated onto the ring α-carbon.

**Ring α-carbon:**
- This is an Asp-type residue whose β-carbonyl is part of the ring.
- Its α-carbon carries the exocyclic C(=O)N(Me) group, the ring NH and the ring CH2.

**Ring sequence (from the Asp CH2, going around):**
- CH2–C(=O)–N(Me)Ala
- N(Me)Phe
- N(Me)Leu
- Thr (NH, with CH(OH)CH3)
- N(Me)Ala
- Val (NH, with isopropyl)
- N(Me)Ala
- N(Me)Ala
- back to the NH of the Asp-type α-carbon

**Stereochemistry:**
- The wedges and hashes at Ala, Phe, Asp, Leu and Thr (Cα and the hydroxyl-bearing Cβ) give L-amino-acid configurations, with Thr as (2S,3R).
- The remaining ring Ala and Val centres are too ambiguous to read, so I assigned them L.
