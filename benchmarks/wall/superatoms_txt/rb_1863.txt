I read the structure as a linear peptide with one disulfide-bridged macrocycle (two penicillamine residues) and a lipidated Lys side chain. I assigned stereochemistry by assuming L-configuration at every α-carbon. I did not read wedges or hashes at each centre, so some centres may be wrong, particularly the Lys that carries the PEG–diacid chain and the trimethylammonium-bearing carbon.

**Sequence, from the C-terminal Gly-NH2 end:**
1. The C-terminus is a primary amide, H2N–C(=O)–CH2–N(CH3), i.e. N-methylglycinamide.
2. Next come 3-pyridylalanine, Asn, Glu (free side-chain acid) and 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid.
3. Then 2-naphthylalanine and Tyr, with its phenol O-alkylated as OCH2CH2NH2.
4. Pen1 begins the ring, followed by Lys(NHAc), 7-methyl-Trp, Thr and Asn.
5. Pen2 closes the ring. The two penicillamine S atoms are joined by a disulfide (ring label %10).

**Lys side chain on Pen2's N-terminal neighbour:**
- The α-amine carries two OCH2CH2OCH2CH2NH–C(=O)CH2 (PEG-type) units in series. These lead to a γ-Glu whose α-NH is acylated by an 18-carbon diacid (C(=O) + 16 CH2 + COOH). I counted the diacid chain length from the zigzag spacing in the drawing.
- The ε-amine carries a succinyl group. This links to an Asp-like unit whose side chain is CH2COOH and whose other arm is CH2–N⁺(CH3)3.
