This is terfenadine, drawn with plain bonds, so no stereochemistry is included.

Reading the structure region by region:
- Left end: a tBu group on a para-substituted benzene ring.
- The ring carbon opposite the tBu carries CH(OH).
- Chain: CH(OH), then CH2, CH2, CH2, then the piperidine N. That is 3 CH2 groups after the carbinol carbon, so 4 carbons in total counting the carbinol.
- Piperidine: the N sits in the ring, and the carbon opposite it bears C(OH)(Ph)2.

The ring's tBu and chain substituents are para to each other, as drawn.
