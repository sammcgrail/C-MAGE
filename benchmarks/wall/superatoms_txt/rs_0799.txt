Reading the drawing from left to right:

1. A benzene ring is attached to a carbonyl carbon, C(=O).
2. That carbonyl carbon connects to an oxygen, then a second oxygen (a peroxide, O–O), then a second carbonyl carbon, C(=O).
3. The second carbonyl carbon connects to an oxygen (O) and then to a chain ending in COOH.

To count the chain carbons, I followed the zigzag after the O: the O joins the first CH2 (vertex 1) at about x=450, then the next vertex is at about x=510 (2), then about x=565 (3), and finally the COOH carbon at about x=610. That gives three CH2 groups followed by COOH.

This gives O–CH2–CH2–CH2–COOH, which is a 4-carbon acid chain (butanoic acid with the oxygen on C4).

Full SMILES: O=C(OOC(=O)c1ccccc1)OCCCC(=O)O
