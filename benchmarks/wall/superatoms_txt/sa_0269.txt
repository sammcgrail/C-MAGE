The drawing shows a benzene ring with an HO group on the left-hand vertex. I name the ring positions from that carbon.

- C1 (left, at about 463,748) carries the OH.
- C2 (upper left, at about 600,510) carries a tBu group.
- C3 (upper right, at about 875,510) has no substituent, so it is CH.
- C4 (right, at about 1012,748) carries a tBu group.
- C5 (lower right, at about 875,987) has no substituent, so it is CH.
- C6 (lower left, at about 600,987) carries a tBu group.

So the tBu groups sit at C2, C4 and C6, which makes this 2,4,6-tri-tert-butylphenol.

The double bonds are C2=C3, C4=C5 and C6=C1, which is a valid Kekulé structure.
