This SMILES was assembled from the drawing for connectivity, but I did not check the stereocentres against it. I copied them from my recollection of the rifampicin and rifamycin SV stereochemistry, and I am only moderately sure of them. The cis/trans geometry of the diene was checked against the drawing.

**Aromatic core.** A fluoro-hydroxy phenoxazine is fused to the rifamycin naphtho-furanone. The phenoxazine ring carries F next to the ring N, with the OH meta to F. The central ring has a quinone C=O and an NH-bearing carbon. The other ring has a phenolic OH and an aryl methyl.

**Furanone.** The ring is closed through a quaternary carbon that bears a methyl and a ketone C=O. This carbon is also the attachment point of the enol-ether O, which starts the ansa chain.

**Ansa chain.** Reading from the NH, the chain is:
- an amide C(=O), then C(CH3)=CH, which is cis (Z) between the amide carbon and the vinyl carbon;
- CH=CH, which is trans (E);
- CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OAc), CH(CH3), CH(OMe);
- CH=CH (E), then the enol-ether O back to the quaternary carbon.
