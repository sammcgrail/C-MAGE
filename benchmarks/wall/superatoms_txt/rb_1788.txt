I read the structure as a PROTAC-type molecule: a pyrazolo[4,3-d]pyrimidine core linked through a phenylpiperazine and a glycyl amide to a pomalidomide unit.

**Left aryl group:** a benzene ring bearing F and OMe on the two positions flanking the attachment point (2-fluoro-6-methoxyphenyl). SMILES: c1(F)cccc(OC)c1-.

**Core (1H-pyrazolo[4,3-d]pyrimidine):**
- The pyrimidine ring runs C5 (bearing the aryl), N4, C3a, C7a, C7 (CH), N6.
- C5 is bonded to N4 and to N6. N6 is bonded to C7(H), which is bonded to C7a. C7a carries the NH of the pyrazole, and C3a is bonded to N4.
- The pyrazole ring is C3a, C3, N2, N1H, C7a. C3 bears the phenyl group.
- SMILES for the core with aryl: c1(-Ar)nc2c(cn1)[nH]nc2-Ar'.

**Linker:**
- The phenyl is para-substituted, with its other end on a piperazine nitrogen.
- The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated: C(=O)CNH-Ar.
- The NH is attached to the pomalidomide benzene ring at position 4.

**Pomalidomide unit:**
- Isoindoline-1,3-dione with N-linked 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl.
- The stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
