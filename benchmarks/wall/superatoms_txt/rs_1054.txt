I read the drawing as a nine-membered dilactone ring carrying all the substituents.

- **Pyridine ring:** the carboxamide sits at C2, the 4-methoxybenzoyloxy group (OC(=O)C6H4-OMe) at C3, and OMe at C4.
- **Amide link:** the pyridine C(=O)NH group is attached to the ring carbon C9, the ring CH next to the ring ester C=O.
- **Nine-membered ring**, in order: C(=O), O, CH(CH3), CH(O-isobutyryl), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), O, CH2, CH(NH). That is nine ring atoms.
- **Substituents on the ring:** methyl on the CH next to the ring oxygen, an isobutyrate ester (OC(=O)CH(CH3)2) on the next CH, and benzyl on the CH next to the second ring C=O.

I assigned the four stereocentres from the wedges and hashes: the NH bond is hashed, the methyl and benzyl bonds are wedged, and the isobutyrate O bond is hashed. Working through each centre's neighbour order gave the @/@@ marks in the SMILES below.
