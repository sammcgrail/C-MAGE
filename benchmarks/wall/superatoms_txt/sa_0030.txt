This is the enol form of teriflunomide (the active metabolite of leflunomide).

Reading the structure region by region:
- A methyl group is attached to a carbon that bears an OH and is double-bonded to the next carbon.
- That next carbon carries a nitrile (C≡N) and is bonded to a C(=O)NH amide carbon.
- The amide nitrogen is bonded to a benzene ring that has a CF3 group para to the nitrogen.

The drawing shows no explicit cis/trans geometry about the C=C, so I have not included double-bond stereochemistry.
