Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Aromatic ring:** the vinyl substituent sits at C1, the OH at C3 (meta to the vinyl) and the NO2 at C4 (para to the vinyl, adjacent to the OH).
- **Side chain:** Ar–CH=C(NHCbz)(CO2Me). The Cbz group expands to the benzyl carbamate NH–C(=O)OCH2Ph, and CO2Me is the methyl ester.
- **Double bond geometry:** the aryl group on the CH and the NHCbz on the other alkene carbon appear on the same side of the double bond, so the alkene is Z (N outranks the ester carbon, and aryl outranks H). The ester is therefore trans to the aryl, which is what `/C(...)=C/` encodes in the SMILES below.

SMILES: COC(=O)/C(NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1)=C/c1ccc([N+](=O)[O-])c(O)c1
