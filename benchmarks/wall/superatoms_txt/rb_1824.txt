I read the molecule as seven connected parts, working left to right.

1. **Left end:** a 2-hydroxyphenyl group attached to a pyridazine ring.
2. **Tricyclic core:** the pyridazine is fused to a six-membered ring containing an NH and a CH2. That ring is fused in turn to a piperazine.
3. **Stereocentre:** the ring-fusion CH carries a hashed H. Working through the neighbour order, this gives `[C@H]` in the SMILES as written.
4. **Linker, first piperidine:** the piperazine nitrogen bears a CH2 that leads to the 4-position of a piperidine.
5. **Linker, second piperidine:** the first piperidine's nitrogen carries another CH2 that leads to the 4-position of a second piperidine.
6. **Isoindoline-1,3-dione:** the second piperidine's nitrogen is attached to the 5-position of this ring system. Its imide nitrogen is bonded to C3 of a glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidine). That glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
7. **Right end:** the glutarimide nitrogen carries a CH2–O–C(=O) group joined to a 1-aminocyclopropane.
