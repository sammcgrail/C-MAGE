I did not derive the stereochemistry independently from the wedges and hashes. The drawing matches the connectivity of bromocriptine, and I used its known SMILES for the stereocentres.

- **Ergoline part:** a 2-bromo indole fused to a tetrahydropyridine ring, with an N-methyl group and a ring C=C. The ring carbon next to that C=C carries the amide carbonyl.
- **Linker:** the carbonyl is joined through NH to a quaternary carbon bearing an isopropyl group.
- **Peptide part:** that carbon is also bonded to a ring oxygen and to a ring C=O. The oxygen leads to a carbon bearing OH. This carbon is bonded to the ring N, to the proline-derived CH, and to the oxygen.
- **Side groups:** the ring nitrogen is attached to a CH bearing an isobutyl group, then a C=O, then the proline nitrogen, which closes the pyrrolidine ring.
