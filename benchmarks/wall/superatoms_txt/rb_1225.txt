I read the structure in five parts, left to right:

1. **Glutarimide:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring (HN flanked by two C=O groups), attached through its C3 to the phthalimide nitrogen.
2. **Phthalimide:** a 1,3-dioxoisoindoline with an NH substituent at the 5-position of the benzo ring.
3. **Linker:** the NH leads into a saturated chain ending in an amide C=O. I counted this as a nonanamide, i.e. eight CH2 groups plus the carbonyl carbon. This is the least certain part. Chain length is judged from bond spacing in the drawing, and the spacing fits 9, 10 or 11 bonds depending on the bond length I assume. I chose 9.
4. **Bicyclo[1.1.1]pentane:** the amide N–H is bonded to one bridgehead. The other bridgehead carries a CH2CH2–O chain.
5. **Pyridazine and phenol:** the O links to C4 of a pyridazine, which has NH2 at C3 and a 2-hydroxyphenyl group at C6.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I included no stereochemistry. The glutarimide C3 is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds.
