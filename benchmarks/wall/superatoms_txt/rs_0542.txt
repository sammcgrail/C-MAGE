I read the molecule as a 2-methoxy-tetrahydropyran carrying a benzoate, a pent-4-enyl chain, and an N-PMB thiazolidin-2-one. The pent-4-enyl chain length is the least certain part of the reading.

**Ring and substituents**
- The six-membered ring is O1, C2 (anomeric), C3 (CH2), C4 (CH–OBz), C5 (CH2), C6 (CH–chain).
- C2 carries OMe on a bold wedge and is bonded to the thiazolidinone C4'.
- C4 carries the benzoate, OC(=O)Ph, on a bold wedge.
- C6 carries the pent-4-enyl chain, CH2CH2CH2CH=CH2, on a hashed bond. I counted the chain vertices as five carbons, ending in the terminal alkene.
- The thiazolidin-2-one ring is C4'(H)–CH2–S–C(=O)–N. The nitrogen bears a PMB group, which is CH2–C6H4–OMe (para).
- C4' has its H on a bold wedge.
