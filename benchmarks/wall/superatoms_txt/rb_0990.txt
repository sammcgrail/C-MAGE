I read the structure as a VHL-type PROTAC: a VHL ligand joined through a spiro linker to a bicyclic-amine/aminopyridazine-phenol warhead. This is the SMILES I derived from the drawing.

**Regions, left to right**
- **Top:** a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl phenyl ring attached to a CH(CH3)–NH group, which is an (S)-1-arylethylamide.
- **Hydroxyproline:** a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine-2-carboxamide with a hashed OH at C4.
- **Tert-leucine:** the proline nitrogen is acylated by a tert-leucine (C(CH3)3 on the alpha carbon) whose NH is acylated in turn.
- **Spiro linker:** that acyl group is a carboxamide on a spiro[3.5]nonane. The cyclohexane carbon carries the carboxamide on a wedge, and the cyclobutane carbon carries the piperidine nitrogen on a hash.
- **Piperidine–pyrimidine:** the piperidine's 4-position is bonded to the 5-position of a pyrimidine.
- **Bicyclic amine:** the pyrimidine's 2-position is bonded to the bridging nitrogen (N8) of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane. Its other nitrogen (N3) is bonded to the pyridazine.
- **Pyridazine:** a 3-amino-pyridazine bearing a 2-hydroxyphenyl group.

**Stereochemistry**
- I assigned the spiro-ring centres from the wedge/hash geometry in the drawing.
- The (S)-1-arylethylamine, the hydroxyproline and the tert-leucine centres are written as the standard VHL ligand configuration. I matched them against that known ligand rather than re-deriving each from the drawing.
- The bicyclic amine is drawn without stereo, so it has none in the SMILES.
