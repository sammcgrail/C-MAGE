The drawing shows a central nitrogen with three labelled substituents: iPr, Bn and Piv.

- iPr (isopropyl) expands to CH(CH3)2.
- Bn (benzyl) expands to CH2Ph.
- Piv (pivaloyl) expands to C(=O)C(CH3)3.

The nitrogen is therefore a tertiary amide: N-benzyl-N-isopropylpivalamide.

SMILES: CC(C)N(Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)C(C)(C)C
