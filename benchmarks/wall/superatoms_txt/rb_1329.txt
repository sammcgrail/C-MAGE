I read this as a KRAS G12C-type PROTAC: a quinazoline warhead joined through a PEG-like linker to a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

- **Warhead:** a quinazoline with an N-acryloylpiperazine at C4, Cl at C6, F at C8, and an NH at C2.
- **C7 aryl group:** the ring is drawn oddly, with hashed bonds and a clipped outline. I read it as a 2-fluoro-6-hydroxyphenyl group. I gave it no stereochemistry, since a real aromatic ring has no stereocentre there.
- **Linker:** the C2 NH is followed by CH2CH2C(=O)N(CH3)CH2CH2OCH2CH2O–, which ends at the phthalimide ring.
- **Cereblon ligand:** the 5-oxy isoindoline-1,3-dione carries an N-linked 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
