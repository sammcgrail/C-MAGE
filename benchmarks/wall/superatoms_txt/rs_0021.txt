The drawing shows a cyclohexene ring with a CF3 group on one carbon and a triflate (OSO2CF3) on a vinyl carbon.

Ring: the carbon bearing the CF3 is at the top left. Going around, the ring has a CH2 at the top right, then the C(OTf) carbon at the right. A C=C double bond runs from that carbon to the bottom CH, followed by a CH2 at the lower left, which closes back to the CF3 carbon.

So the ring is C1(OTf)=CCC(CF3)CC1. The C=C sits between the C–O carbon and the CH at the bottom. The CF3 carbon is separated from the C–O carbon by CH2 on the top path (two bonds) and by CH2–CH= on the bottom path (three bonds), which makes it the 4-position of a cyclohex-1-enyl triflate.

The CF3-bearing carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is assigned.

The triflate is O–S(=O)(=O)–CF3.
