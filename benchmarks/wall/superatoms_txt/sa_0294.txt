This is verapamil hydrochloride.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- The left ring is a benzene carrying two OMe groups (3,4-dimethoxyphenyl).
- It is attached to a quaternary carbon that bears iPr, CN and a propyl chain.
- The chain runs from the quaternary carbon through three CH2 groups to N(CH3).
- From N, a CH2CH2 link leads to the right ring, a 3,4-dimethoxyphenyl.
- HCl is written as a separate component.

All bonds at the quaternary carbon are plain, so no stereochemistry is included.

SMILES: COc1ccc(CCN(C)CCCC(C#N)(C(C)C)c2ccc(OC)c(OC)c2)cc1OC.Cl
