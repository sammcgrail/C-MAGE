I read the molecule as a pyrrolidin-2-one ring with three attachments:

- **Ring nitrogen:** an N-CH2CH2 chain leading to a benzene ring that carries CO2H para to the chain.
- **C5 of the ring:** a trans CH=CH linker, drawn with a bold wedge on the bond from the ring carbon, which ends in a CH(OH) carbon.
- **The CH(OH) carbon:** it carries a hashed OH and is bonded to a CH2 that leads to a benzene ring with Cl meta to that CH2.

**Stereochemistry**
- Ring C5 has priorities N > vinyl carbon > ring CH2 > H. The wedge puts H at the back, and N → vinyl → CH2 runs clockwise, so this centre is R.
- The carbinol carbon has priorities OH > vinyl carbon > CH2 > H. The hashed OH puts H toward the viewer, and OH → vinyl → CH2 appears clockwise, which reverses to S.
- The double bond is drawn trans (E).

Writing the SMILES with the ring closure on the N side, C5 needs `@@` for R and the carbinol carbon needs `@@` for S.

SMILES: OC(=O)c1ccc(CCN2C(=O)CC[C@@H]2/C=C/[C@@H](O)Cc2cccc(Cl)c2)cc1
