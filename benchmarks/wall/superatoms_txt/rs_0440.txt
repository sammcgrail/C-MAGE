The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-cyclopentyl oxindole.

**Biphenyl part.** The top-left ring carries CO2H. A second ring is attached to it at the meta position. On that second ring, the carbon bonded to the first ring is adjacent to the carbon bearing OH. The OH carbon is in turn adjacent to the carbon bearing the NH–N= group. This gives 2-hydroxy-3-hydrazinyl substitution relative to the biaryl bond.

**Hydrazone and oxindole part.** NH is bonded to N, which is double-bonded to C3 of the oxindole. C3 is bonded to C2 (C=O), which is bonded to N1. N1 carries the cyclopentyl group and is fused to the benzo ring.
