**Structure.** The molecule is a rifamycin-type ansa macrolide in which the naphthoquinone core is fused to a phenoxazine.

- **Core.** The NH amide carbon sits next to the quinone C=O and the oxazine C–O. The other ring has an OH, a methyl and the furanone, which holds a spiro quaternary carbon bearing a methyl.

**Stereochemistry.** I took the macrocycle stereocentres and the diene geometry from the rifampicin pattern, since the drawing's wedges and hashes match it. I reordered the spiro-carbon neighbours to fit my atom order and flipped its tag from @@ to @ to compensate.
