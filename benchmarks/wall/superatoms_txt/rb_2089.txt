I read the structure as a cyclic peptide and traced it clockwise from the isoleucine at the top left.

**Ring sequence (clockwise)**
1. Isoleucine, with its sec-butyl side chain and a free NH.
2. N-Me-Leu.
3. A β-amino-acid unit: C(=O)–CH(CH₂Ph)–CH₂–N(Me).
4. Valine, with its NH.
5. Alanine, with its NH.
6. N-Me-Leu.
7. A β-amino-acid unit: C(=O)–CH(CH₃)–CH₂–N(Me).
8. A β-amino-acid unit bearing a CH(OH)CH₃ group: C(=O)–CH₂–CH(CH(OH)CH₃)–NH.
9. N-Me-Phe.
10. N-Me-Ala, which closes back to the isoleucine carbonyl.

The β-amino-acid readings in units 3 and 7 depend on a CH₂ vertex in the drawing that I took to be a real atom. The bond lengths in the drawing are somewhat distorted, so these two assignments are the least certain part.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned each centre from its wedge or hash bond. The stereocentres are the isoleucine Cα and its side-chain CH, each Cα, the CH(NH) carbon of unit 8, and its carbinol carbon (hashed OH bond). Where a Cα carries an implicit H, I treated that H as pointing opposite the drawn wedge or hash.
