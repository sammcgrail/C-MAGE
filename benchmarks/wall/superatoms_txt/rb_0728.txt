I read the structure in five parts and joined them into one SMILES.

1. **Benzocycloheptene (left):** a phenol ring fused to a seven-membered ring that has three CH2 groups. The ring also contains a C=C whose carbons carry a phenyl group and a 4-substituted phenyl group.
2. **Aryl–piperidine:** the 4-substituted phenyl is attached to the nitrogen of a piperidine. The piperidine C4 carries a two-carbon (CH2CH2) chain.
3. **Piperazine:** that chain ends on one piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to the aromatic ring of the isoindolinone.
4. **Isoindolinone:** the piperazine-bearing carbon is next to the aromatic CH that neighbours the CH2-bearing ring-fusion carbon. The lactam nitrogen is attached to the glutarimide.
5. **Glutarimide stereocentre:** the hashed bond runs from the lactam N to the glutarimide C3, so N is behind the plane. With H toward the viewer, N → C2(=O) → C4 runs clockwise on the page, which corresponds to `[C@H]` when N is written first. This is my own chirality assignment from the drawing and was not cross-checked.

The C=C in the seven-membered ring is in a ring and has no cis/trans label, so I added no double-bond stereo.
