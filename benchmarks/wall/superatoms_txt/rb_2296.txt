I read the molecule as a maleimidocaproyl–Gly‑Gly‑Phe‑Gly linker joined through an NH–CH2–O–CH(cyclopropyl)–C(=O)– spacer to an exatecan core by an amide bond.

**Linker, left to right:**
- A maleimide (2,5‑dioxopyrrole) ring is attached through its nitrogen to a five‑carbon chain ending in C(=O).
- That carbonyl is followed by Gly, Gly, Phe and Gly, linked as amides.
- The Phe benzyl group is drawn on a wedge, which matches the L‑Phe pattern `N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)`.
- The last Gly nitrogen is followed by CH2–O.
- That O is attached to a CH carrying a cyclopropyl group on a hashed bond, then C(=O)–NH.
- Working through the neighbour order for the hashed cyclopropyl gives `O[C@H](C1CC1)C(=O)`.

**Exatecan core:**
- The NH‑bearing stereocentre is drawn on a wedge and sits in a ring with CH2CH2 and the fused aromatic system.
- The aromatic ring carries a methyl and a fluorine.
- It is fused to a quinoline‑type nitrogen ring, then a pyrrolidinone‑fused pyridone, and finally a lactone.
- The lactone carbon bears OH and ethyl, with the stereochemistry of the camptothecin‑type centre.
- I took the two core stereocentres from the known exatecan SMILES. I re‑derived the parity for my atom ordering (even permutation for the amine carbon, odd for the quaternary carbon, which gives `@` for each).

I checked that the ring‑closure digits are reused only after the earlier rings close, and that the SMILES accounts for every atom in the drawing.
