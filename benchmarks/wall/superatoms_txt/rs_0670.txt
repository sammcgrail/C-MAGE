I read the drawing in three parts and then fixed the stereochemistry at the one wedged carbon.

**Pyrazinone core.** The ring runs N1, C2(=O), C3, N4, C5(Br), C6(H). N1 carries the side chain, C3 carries the indoline nitrogen, and C5 carries the bromine.

**Side chain on N1.** This is a CH(CH2CH3)(CH2OCH3) group. Its carbon is the only stereocentre, and it is drawn with a wedged H.

**Indoline on C3.** The indoline nitrogen is bonded to C7a, and the five-membered ring is N–CH2–CH2–C3a. On the benzene ring, C7 carries Cl, C6 carries F, C5 carries OMe, and C4 is CH.

**Stereocentre.** The substituents on the stereocentre are N (down), CH2OMe (upper right), ethyl (upper left) and H (wedged toward the viewer). The CIP priorities are N > CH2O > ethyl > H. Going N → CH2O → ethyl appears counterclockwise on the page. Because H points toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is R. In the SMILES below, writing the centre as `[C@H]` with neighbours in the order ethyl, H, CH2O, N gives R.
