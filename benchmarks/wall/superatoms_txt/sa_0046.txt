I read the drawing as an isopropyl ester of a prostaglandin F-type structure, in four parts.

- **Ester end:** the isopropyl group (iPr) is attached through O to a C=O.
- **Upper chain:** three CH2 groups lead to a cis (Z) C=C, then one more CH2 reaches the ring. This chain is drawn with a wedge bond.
- **Ring:** a cyclopentane carries two OH groups, both on wedge bonds. Its second chain is on a hashed bond.
- **Lower chain:** from the ring, two CH2 groups lead to a ketone C=O, which carries an n-heptyl group (seven carbons).

For stereochemistry, the wedge chain and both wedge OH groups sit on one face of the ring, and the hashed chain sits on the other. I worked out the configuration of the ring carbon bearing the upper chain directly from the drawing, which gave `[C@H]`. I then set the other three ring centres from the relative wedge/hash pattern, which matches the standard prostaglandin F (latanoprost-type) arrangement. The double bond is written Z.
