This is a macrocyclic STING agonist-type dimer. I read it as two identical benzimidazole units joined by a butenyl linker on one side and an O-CH2-CH(piperidinyl)-CH2-O linker on the other.

**Each half of the molecule**
- Benzimidazole: N1 carries the butenyl chain, C2 carries the NH-C(=O)-pyrazole group, C5 carries the primary carboxamide, and C7 carries the ether oxygen.
- Pyrazole: 1-ethyl-3-methylpyrazole-5-carbonyl, i.e. C(=O)c1cc(C)nn1CC.

**Linkers**
- Butenyl: N-CH2-CH=CH-CH2-N, drawn as the E (trans) alkene. The drawing shows the chain zigzagging across the double bond, so I read it as trans.
- Ether: O-CH2-CH(C1CCN(C)CC1)-CH2-O, with N-methylpiperidin-4-yl on the central carbon.

**Top benzimidazole**
N1 is bonded to C2 (=N3), C7a, and the CH2. In SMILES: n1(CC=CC...)c(NC(=O)...)nc2cc(C(N)=O)cc(O...)c12.
