The drawing shows a C=C double bond. The left carbon carries two methyl groups. The right carbon carries OTMS (O–Si(CH3)3) and OMe (O–CH3).

This is the silyl ketene acetal of methyl isobutyrate: CC(C)=C(OC)O[Si](C)(C)C.

The double bond has two identical methyl groups on one end, so it has no E/Z stereochemistry, and no stereo marks are needed.

SMILES: COC(O[Si](C)(C)C)=C(C)C
