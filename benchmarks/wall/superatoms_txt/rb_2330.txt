This is my SMILES for the structure. It is a NODAGA-type chelator (a triazacyclononane with three arms) carrying ⁶⁷Cu, attached through a glutamic-acid arm to a disulfide-cyclised peptide. The stereochemistry follows my reading of the wedges and hashes, and a few of those are uncertain. I assigned each centre by working out its CIP label from the drawing.

**Chelator and linker**
- The 9-membered ring has three nitrogens, each separated by an ethylene bridge.
- Two nitrogens carry CH₂COO arms.
- The third carries the CH(COOH)CH₂CH₂C(=O)– arm that links to the peptide.
- The dashed bonds to ⁶⁷Cu are written as ordinary single bonds to the three nitrogens and the two carboxylate oxygens.
- I read the glutamate carbon as R from a hashed bond to its COOH. This is the least certain assignment.

**Peptide, N- to C-terminus**
1. D-4-chloro-Phe
2. Cys (R, i.e. L), forming the disulfide
3. Tyr (L)
4. 4-ureido-Phe (D)
5. Lys (L)
6. 2,3-diaminobutyric acid (2S,3R), which has a free β-amine and a methyl group
7. Cys (R, i.e. L), forming the disulfide
8. D-Tyr-NH₂
