The drawing shows Boc-protected tryptophan: a Boc-NH group on the α-carbon, a CO2H group, and a CH2 linking to the 3-position of an indole ring (with its N–H).

**Stereochemistry:** the α-carbon has a hashed bond to the CO2H carbon, so the implicit hydrogen points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H. On the page, N (below), CO2H (upper left) and CH2 (right) run clockwise. Because the hydrogen faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, which gives counterclockwise, i.e. (S). This is the L-tryptophan configuration.
