The drawing shows three parts joined by two amide links.

1. **Left ring:** a para-substituted benzene carrying an amidine, C(=NH)N(CH3)2, at one end and a C(=O)NH amide at the other.
2. **Central ring:** a benzene ring with the NH of that amide on one carbon and a C(=O)NH-pyridyl group on the adjacent carbon. The NO2 sits para to the carbonyl-bearing carbon and meta to the NH carbon.
   - In the drawing, C1 (upper left) carries the NH, C2 (lower left) carries the C(=O)NH, C3 is lower right, C4 is the lower-right CH, C5 is upper right bearing NO2, and C6 is the CH between the NH carbon and the NO2 carbon.
   - NO2 is attached to the upper-right carbon, which is adjacent to the CH next to C1. This puts NO2 meta to the NH carbon and para to the carbonyl-bearing carbon.
3. **Pyridine:** attached through its 2-position to the amide NH, with Cl at the 5-position (para to the amide-bearing carbon's ring nitrogen side, as drawn).
