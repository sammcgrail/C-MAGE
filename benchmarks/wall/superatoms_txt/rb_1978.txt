The drawing shows a PROTAC-type molecule, with pomalidomide joined through a short PEG linker to a lovastatin-like core. I read it in three parts.

1. **Pomalidomide end:** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group sits on the isoindoline-1,3-dione nitrogen. The aromatic ring carries an NH at position 4, which leads into the linker.
2. **Linker:** the chain runs NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH, and the last NH is the nitrogen of a carbamate, NH–C(=O)–O.
3. **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen is attached to the hexahydronaphthalene ring at the position where lovastatin carries its 2-methylbutanoate ester.
   - The ring has two C=C bonds and two methyl groups, one of them hashed.
   - A CH2CH2 chain leads to a six-membered lactone bearing a wedged OH.

For the stereochemistry I took the wedge and hash pattern to match lovastatin and reused its stereo assignments, swapping only the ester for the carbamate. I did not re-derive each centre independently from the wedges in the image.
