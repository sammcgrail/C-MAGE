I read this as pivampicillin: the ampicillin penicillin core with a pivaloyloxymethyl ester. The pieces are:

- **Core:** a β-lactam fused to a thiazolidine ring, with a gem-dimethyl carbon next to the sulfur.
- **Ester:** the carboxyl group on the thiazolidine ring is esterified with –CH2–O–Piv, where Piv is pivaloyl, C(=O)C(CH3)3.
- **Side chain:** the amide NH on the β-lactam carries –C(=O)–CH(NH2)–Ph, the phenylglycyl group.

For stereochemistry, I checked the phenylglycine carbon directly against the drawing. Its priorities are N > C(=O) > Ph > H, with Ph hashed and the implicit H toward the viewer. That gives R, matching the D-phenylglycyl group of ampicillin. For the three bicyclic stereocentres I did not rederive each one from the wedges and hashes. I used the natural penicillin configuration (2S,5R,6R), written in the same pattern as the standard ampicillin SMILES with the ester substituted in.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)C(=O)OCOC(=O)[C@@H]1N2C(=O)[C@@H](NC(=O)[C@H](N)c3ccccc3)[C@H]2SC1(C)C
