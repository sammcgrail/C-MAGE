This is a PROTAC-type molecule: an EGFR inhibitor (afatinib-like quinazoline) joined through a piperazine linker to a lenalidomide-type ligand.

**Quinazoline part:** the 4-anilinoquinazoline carries a 3-chloro-4-fluorophenylamino group at C4, a methoxy at C7, and an amide NH at C6.
- Aniline: Fc1ccc(Nc2ncnc3cc(OC)c(NC(=O)...)cc23)cc1Cl

**Linker:** the amide is C(=O)/C=C/CN1CCN(CC1)C(=O)CNc..., an E-alkene (drawn trans) leading to a piperazine, whose second nitrogen is acylated by a glycyl group, C(=O)CH2-NH-aryl.

**Lenalidomide part:** the aryl group is the isoindolinone, with the NH on the benzene ring at position 4. The isoindolinone nitrogen carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
- Isoindolinone: c1cccc2c1CN(C2=O)C1CCC(=O)NC1=O
- In the drawing, the NH-bearing carbon is adjacent to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the CH2 (which is attached to N), so the aryl is c1(N...)cccc2C(=O)N(...)Cc12.

**Assembled SMILES:**

The CH2 on the isoindolinone is attached to the ring carbon next to the NH-bearing carbon, which matches the drawing. The piperidinedione stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is assigned there.
