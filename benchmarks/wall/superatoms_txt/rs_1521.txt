The drawing shows a benzene ring with a methoxy group (MeO) at the top. At the lower right, a two-carbon chain leads to OMs (mesylate).

The ring carbon bearing OMe is at the upper left. The ring carbon bearing the chain is the one to its lower right, with one CH between them. That makes the substituents meta to each other, so this is a 3-methoxyphenethyl mesylate.

Expanding the abbreviations gives OMs = OS(=O)(=O)C and MeO = OC.

SMILES: COc1cccc(CCOS(C)(=O)=O)c1
