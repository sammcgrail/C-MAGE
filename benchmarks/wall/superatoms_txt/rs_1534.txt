The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- N at the bottom left of the ring.
- C2, next to N, carries the carboxamide C(=O)NH–R.
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are unsubstituted CH.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a cyclohexyl ring. Numbering from C1 (the carbon bonded to N):
- C1 is bonded to N.
- C2 is a CH2.
- C3 carries two methyl groups (gem-dimethyl, top right).
- C4 is a CH2.
- C5 carries two methyl groups (gem-dimethyl, bottom).
- C6 is a CH2.

So the substituent is 3,3,5,5-tetramethylcyclohexyl. C1 is not a stereocentre because the ring is symmetric, and no stereochemistry is drawn.
