I read the molecule as a 2-oxo-benzoxazole core (O1, C2 carbonyl, N3–H) fused to a benzene ring. On the benzene ring, one carbon bears a bromine. The adjacent carbon bears a CH2–CH(NHCbz)–CO2CH3 side chain.

- **Ring connectivity:** The ring CH next to the O-bearing carbon is bonded to the side-chain carbon. Br sits on the next carbon around the ring, with one aromatic CH between it and the N-bearing carbon.
- **Side chain:** CH2 leads to the alpha carbon. The alpha carbon carries CO2CH3 and NH–Cbz, where Cbz is C(=O)OCH2Ph.
- **Stereochemistry:** The bond from the alpha carbon to NHCbz is hashed, so N points away from the viewer and H points toward the viewer.
  - Priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2 > H.
  - Seen from the front, N (down) → CO2Me (upper right) → CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise.
  - H faces the viewer, so the sense reverses to clockwise, which is R.
  - In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]` with neighbour order CO2Me, H, CH2, N corresponds to R.

SMILES: COC(=O)[C@@H](Cc1cc2oc(=O)[nH]c2cc1Br)NC(=O)OCc1ccccc1
