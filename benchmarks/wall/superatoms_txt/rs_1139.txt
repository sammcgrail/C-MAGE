I read the molecule as an oxindole (2-oxindoline) ring carrying a CH2–CH(NHCbz)–CO2Me side chain at the 5-position.

- **Oxindole:** the left ring is a benzene fused to a five-membered lactam. It has a C=O at C2, an NH at position 1 and a CH2 at C3. The side chain is attached to the benzene carbon para to the NH-bearing carbon, which is position 5.
- **Side chain:** an aryl-CH2 group leads to the α-carbon, which carries CO2Me, NHCbz and H. NHCbz expands to NH–C(=O)–O–CH2–Ph.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the α-carbon, and its H is drawn on a bold wedge, pointing toward the viewer.
  - CIP priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2Ar > H.
  - Seen from the front, N (down) → CO2Me (right) → CH2 (left) runs counterclockwise.
  - With H toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is R.
  - In the SMILES below, writing the carbon as `[C@@H]` with neighbour order (CO2Me, H, CH2, N) gives R.
