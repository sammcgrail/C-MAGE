The drawing is a glycerol triester. Each of the three oxygens on the glycerol backbone carries an acyl group, C(=O)-nHex.

nHex is n-hexyl, so each acyl group is heptanoyl: C(=O)CCCCCC. The molecule is therefore glycerol triheptanoate (triheptanoin).

The drawing shows no wedges or hashes, so the central carbon gets no stereo marking.

SMILES: CCCCCCC(=O)OCC(OC(=O)CCCCCC)COC(=O)CCCCCC
