This molecule is a PROTAC-type conjugate. It joins a sunitinib-like kinase-inhibitor head to a VHL-ligand (VH032-type) tail through a piperazine and PEG-like linker. I read it in four parts and checked stereochemistry against known reference structures rather than deriving it from the drawing alone.

**Left side (kinase-binding head):**
- A 5-fluoro-2-oxindole carries a 3-ylidene methylene linking to a pyrrole. I assigned the Z geometry, as in sunitinib.
- The pyrrole is 2,4-dimethyl with a carboxamide at C3, and the amide nitrogen leads into the linker.

**Linker:**
- A propyl chain (NCCC) connects the amide to a piperazine nitrogen.
- The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated: C(=O)CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH.

**VHL ligand:**
- The NH is attached to an L-tert-leucine unit, whose carbonyl acylates the proline nitrogen.
- The proline is a 4-hydroxyproline (2S,4R) whose carboxyl forms an amide with a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamine.
- I set the tert-leucine and hydroxyproline stereocentres to match the standard VH032 configuration, and checked the SMILES chirality ordering against it.

**Assembled SMILES check:** every ring is closed and the heteroatom labels match the drawing.
