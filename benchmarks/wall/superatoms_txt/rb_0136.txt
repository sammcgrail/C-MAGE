I read the molecule as a linear peptide with several modified ends. From the drawing, region by region:

- **Peptide backbone:** the sequence runs from the left as Asp, a modified Lys, Asp, Lys, Asp, Cys. Re-deriving the CIP label at each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes gave all six residues as L (S for the Asp and Lys centres, R for Cys).
- **Left Asp:** its β-carboxyl forms the amide to the modified Lys α-amine, and its α-COOH is free. Its α-NH is acylated by a succinyl linker to the 8-amino group of a quinoline.
- **Modified Lys:** the ε-nitrogen is the ring N of a pyrrolidine carrying a 3-CH2NH-succinyl link, which leads to a second 8-aminoquinoline.
- **Quinoline caps:** each quinoline is 4-carboxamide-linked to Gly, which is acylated onto the N of a (2S)-2-cyano-4,4-difluoropyrrolidine. Both cyano stereocentres work out to S.
- **Cys end:** the Cys sulfur is attached to a succinimide ring. The succinimide N is bonded to a 5-substituted fluorescein, a spiro lactone with a 3,6-dihydroxyxanthene.
- **Free side chains:** the Lys side chain ends in NH2, and both Asp side chains end in COOH.
