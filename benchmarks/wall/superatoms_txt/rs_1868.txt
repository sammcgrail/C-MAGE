I read the structure as two ring systems joined by a single bond: a chromone on the left and a xanthone on the right.

**Chromone (left):**
- The pyranone ring contains O1, C2 (attached to the xanthone), C3 (=CH), and C4 (=O).
- C4a and C8a are the ring-fusion carbons.
- In the benzo ring, C5 carries COOH, C6 carries OH, C7 carries OH, and C8 is CH.

- Full unit: c1(-X)cc(=O)c2c(C(=O)O)c(O)c(O)cc2o1, where X is the xanthone.
