Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left fragment:** a benzothiophene ring attached through its 3-position (the carbon next to the CH= that sits beside S) to a chain.
- **Chain:** two CH2 groups, then a C=O that is bonded to the ring nitrogen, so the group is an N-acyl.
- **Right fragment:** a 2-oxazolidinone ring (N–C(=O)–O–CH2–CH). The CH carbon next to N (C4) carries a benzyl (Bn) group on a bold wedge.

Stereochemistry at C4:
- The CIP priorities are N > ring CH2–O > CH2Ph > H, and the wedged benzyl puts H at the back.
- In the drawing, N is at the upper right, the ring CH2 at the bottom and Bn at the upper left. Going N → CH2 → Bn is clockwise, so the centre is R.
- In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]` is written with neighbour order ring CH2, H, Bn, N, and that order gives R.
