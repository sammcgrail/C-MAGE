I read the molecule as a PROTAC: a spiro-oxindole MDM2-binding unit joined through a benzamide–propyl–imidazole linker to a lenalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**Cereblon end (top right).** An isoindolin-1-one carries the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on its nitrogen. At C4 of the isoindolinone there is a CH2CH2 chain that runs to C5 of an imidazole.

**Linker.** The imidazole N1 carries a propyl chain, N-CH2CH2CH2-NH-C(=O), which ends in a para-disubstituted benzene. The other para position of that ring holds an NH that acylates the pyrrolidine carboxamide.

**MDM2 end (bottom).** A pyrrolidine ring has these substituents:
- C2 carries the carboxamide.
- C3 carries a 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl group.
- C4 is the spiro carbon to a 6-chlorooxindole.
- C5 is the spiro carbon to a cyclohexane.
- The ring nitrogen is NH.

**Stereochemistry.** I assigned the three drawn stereocentres from the wedge and hash bonds and their 2D positions.
- C2 has the amide on a hash bond.
- C3 has the aryl group on a wedge bond.
- The oxindole spiro carbon has the aromatic carbon on a hash and the C=O on a wedge.

The cyclohexane spiro carbon and the piperidinedione C3 have plain bonds, so I gave them no stereo.

I did not run the SMILES through a parser. I checked it by hand against the drawing: ring closures, the ortho/meta placement of F and Cl, and the attachment points on the isoindolinone and oxindole.
