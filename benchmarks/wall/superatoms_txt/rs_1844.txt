The drawing shows a pyrazin-2(1H)-one ring joined to an indoline.

**Pyrazinone ring.** N1 carries a sec-butyl group, CH(CH3)CH2CH3. C2 is the carbonyl carbon (C=O). C3 is bonded to the indoline nitrogen and double-bonded to N4. C5 carries Cl and is double-bonded to C6, the CH next to N1.

**Indoline.** The indoline nitrogen is bonded to the pyrazinone C3. From the nitrogen, the five-membered ring runs through CH2 and CH2 to C3a, then to C7a, which closes back to the nitrogen. On the benzene ring, C7 (adjacent to C7a) carries Cl, C6 is CH, C5 carries OMe, and C4 is CH.
