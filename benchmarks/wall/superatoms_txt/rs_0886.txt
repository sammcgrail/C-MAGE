I read the molecule as a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core with three substituents, and worked out the stereocentre from the wedge.

- **Pyrazinone ring:** N1 carries the side chain and is bonded to the C2 carbonyl (C=O). C3 bears the indoline nitrogen, and C5 bears Cl.
- **N1 side chain:** this is a CH carrying ethyl, cyclopropyl and a wedged H. It is the only stereocentre in the drawing.
- **Indoline:** its nitrogen is attached to pyrazinone C3. On the benzene ring, Cl sits at C7 (next to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to N) and OMe at C5.

For the stereocentre, the CIP priorities are N > cyclopropyl > ethyl > H. In the drawing N is at the bottom, cyclopropyl at the upper right and ethyl at the upper left. Going N → cyclopropyl → ethyl is counterclockwise. The H is on a wedge, pointing toward the viewer, so the sense reverses and the centre is R.

Written as `CC[C@H](C1CC1)n...`, the neighbour order is ethyl, H, cyclopropyl, N. That order with `@` corresponds to R, so `@` is the correct tag.
