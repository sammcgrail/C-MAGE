This SMILES was read from the drawing by tracing the peptide backbone residue by residue. Stereochemistry is only partly checked: I assumed L-configuration for most residues, and the stereocentre on the trimethylammonium β-amino acid is a guess.

**Backbone, N-terminus to C-terminus:**
- Ureido-Arg: an H₂N–C(=O)–NH cap on arginine.
- Pen: a penicillamine, one half of the disulfide.
- Asn.
- Thr.
- Trp with a 7-methylindol-2-yl side chain.
- Lys(Ac).
- Pen: the other half of the disulfide. The macrocycle runs from the first Pen through Asn, Thr, Trp and Lys(Ac) to this second Pen, closed by the S–S bond.
- Tyr, with its phenol O-alkylated by CH₂CH₂NH₂.
- 2-Nal: 2-naphthylalanine.
- A 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid residue.
- Lys, whose side-chain amine is acylated as described below.
- Asn.
- 3-Pyridylalanine.
- N-methylglycine (sarcosine).
- Orn, as the C-terminal primary amide.

**Substituents:**
- The Lys side chain carries a succinyl group, which is amide-linked to a β-amino acid bearing CH₂COOH and CH₂N⁺(CH₃)₃.
- The Orn side-chain amine carries a linker of two –NHC(=O)CH₂O(CH₂)₂O(CH₂)₂– units, then γ-Glu.
- The γ-Glu nitrogen is acylated by a C20 diacid.

The C20 chain length is an estimate. I got it by measuring the length of the zigzag in the drawing, so it could be off by a carbon or two.
