I read the molecule as a 2-pyrrolidinone (γ-lactam) with two substituents.

- **Lactam ring:** a five-membered ring with the C=O next to N. N carries a CH2CH2 chain ending at a para-substituted benzene ring bearing CO2H.
- **Ring carbon next to N (C5):** it carries a trans CH=CH–CH(OH)–CH2–CH(CH3)2 side chain. The bond to the vinyl carbon is a bold wedge.
- **Stereochemistry at C5:** the priorities are N > vinyl C > ring CH2 > H. With H pointing away, N → vinyl → ring CH2 runs clockwise, so this centre is R.
- **Stereochemistry at the carbinol carbon:** the OH is on a hashed bond, so H points toward the viewer. The priorities are OH > vinyl C > CH2 > H. OH → vinyl → CH2 appears clockwise from the front, which reverses to counterclockwise, so this centre is S.
- **Alkene:** drawn trans (E).

Translating these into SMILES chirality marks gives `[C@H]` at C5 (R) and `[C@@H]` at the carbinol carbon (S).

SMILES: O=C1CC[C@H](/C=C/[C@@H](O)CC(C)C)N1CCc1ccc(C(=O)O)cc1
