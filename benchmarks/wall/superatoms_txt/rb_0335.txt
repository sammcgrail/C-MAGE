I read the molecule as a rifamycin-type ansa macrocycle fused to a phenoxazine chromophore carrying a 2-(dimethylamino)ethoxy group. I did not check the SMILES with software.

**Chromophore**
- The phenoxazine's benzo ring carries the OCH2CH2N(CH3)2 substituent.
- The adjacent quinone ring has the amide NH, a C=O and an aromatic OH.
- The aromatic ring carries a methyl and the furanone oxygen, and it is fused to the furanone ring, which bears a C=O.

**Ansa chain**, running from the amide round to the ketal carbon:
- The chain starts with an amide C(=O) attached to C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH. The first double bond is Z and the second is E.
- Next come CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OAc), CH(CH3) and CH(OMe).
- It ends with CH=CH–O (E) into the ketal carbon, which also carries a methyl and the furanone oxygen.

**Stereochemistry**
- I took each centre from the wedges and hashes in the drawing.
- For centres with an implicit H, I took H to point opposite the drawn wedge or hash.
- For the ketal carbon I used a signed-volume check on the wedge and hash geometry.
