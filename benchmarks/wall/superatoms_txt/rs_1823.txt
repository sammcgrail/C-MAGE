The drawing shows a benzyl ether on a para-substituted benzene ring. That ring carries a two-carbon chain (CH2CH2) ending in a nitrogen. The nitrogen bears a benzyl group and a Boc group.

Reading it region by region:
- Left: a phenyl ring attached through CH2 to an oxygen (OCH2Ph).
- Middle: the oxygen is bonded to a para-disubstituted benzene ring, which carries the CH2CH2 chain.
- Right: the chain ends at N, which has Boc (C(=O)OC(C)(C)C) and a CH2Ph group.

The SMILES is therefore: CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N(Cc1ccccc1)CCc1ccc(OCc2ccccc2)cc1
