I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type conjugate with three parts:

- **Cereblon-binding end:** a 4-amino thalidomide (pomalidomide-type) unit. It has a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the phthalimide nitrogen, and the 4-position NH is attached to the 4-position of a piperidine.
- **Linker:** the piperidine nitrogen carries a straight chain of five CH2 groups, which ends in an NH that forms a carbamate (NH–C(=O)–O).
- **Statin end:** the carbamate oxygen sits on the C1 position of a lovastatin-type hexahydronaphthalene, in place of the usual 2-methylbutanoate ester. The ring carries the two methyl groups and the two ring C=C bonds. The ethylene chain leads to a 4-hydroxy-6-oxo-tetrahydropyran-2-yl lactone.

The stereocentres in the drawing match lovastatin's configuration, so I took the stereo descriptors for the statin core from the lovastatin SMILES. The glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.

The chain length of five CH2 groups is the least certain count, because I estimated it from the zigzag vertices between the piperidine nitrogen and the carbamate NH.
