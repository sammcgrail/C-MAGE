I read the structure as a cyclic peptide amide and wrote it out residue by residue, starting from the C-terminal glycinamide.

**Residue sequence (C-terminus to ring closure):**
1. Glycinamide, C(=O)NH2 at the top.
2. Cysteine, whose sulfur carries CH2C(=O)– (a thioether) that acylates the Tyr nitrogen.
3. Tyr, then N-methyl-Ala, then Asn, then Pro.
4. 2,3-Diaminopropionic acid (Dap), whose side-chain NH carries a squarate: C1=C(OC)C(=O)C1=O, i.e. a methoxy-cyclobutenedione.
5. Leu, then 4-hydroxyproline, then Trp.
6. Ornithine, whose side-chain NH carries the second methoxy-squarate.
7. N-carboxymethyl Trp: the indole nitrogen bears CH2COOH, and the alpha nitrogen is acylated by the Orn carbonyl.
8. Two N-methyl norleucines, each with an n-butyl side chain.
9. Leu, which closes the macrocycle back onto the Cys alpha-nitrogen.

**Stereochemistry:** The @/@@ tags are my best assignment from the wedges and hashes. I did not verify each centre one by one, so some may be wrong.
