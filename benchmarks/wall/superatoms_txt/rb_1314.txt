Reading the structure region by region:

**Left end (EGFR warhead):** a quinazoline carrying a 3-chloro-4-fluoroanilino group at C4, an acrylamide (NHC(=O)C=C) at C6, and an O-propyl chain at C7.

**Linker:**
- The propyl chain ends on a piperidine nitrogen.
- Piperidine C4 is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen.
- The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated: C(=O)CH2–O.
- Next come four ethylene-oxy units, then CH2CH2–S. Counting the oxygens in the drawing (O, O, O, O, O) gives five in total. The first is attached to the CH2 of the acyl group, and the chain is C(=O)CH2 O CH2CH2 O CH2CH2 O CH2CH2 O CH2CH2 O CH2CH2 S.

**Right end (thalidomide-type group):** an isoindoline-1,3-dione with the sulfur at position 4 of the benzene ring. The imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, with the stereocentre drawn plain, so no stereo is assigned.

**Quinazoline SMILES:** the C4 carbon bears NH-aryl and is bonded to N3 and to C4a. C4a continues to C5(H), then C6 (with NHC(=O)C=C), then C7 (with O), then C8(H), then C8a, which is bonded to N1.

Ring-closure labels need care here. Ring 3 is used for the piperidine and closed before it is reused. In "N3CCC(CC3)N3CCN(CC3)", the piperidine closes at CC3, and the piperazine then opens a new 3 and closes it at CC3. Later "Sc3cccc4c3C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C4=O" uses 3 again after it has been closed, which is valid. The aromatic ring opens at c3, runs through cccc4, and closes at c3; ring 4 closes at C4=O.

The aromatic ring is c3(S) c c c c4 c3: the S-bearing carbon, three CH, a fused carbon (c4), and another fused carbon (c3) that closes the ring. That fused carbon is adjacent to the S-bearing carbon, so the sulfur sits at position 4. The c4 carbon then bonds to C4=O, and the c3 carbon bonds to C(=O)N, which is consistent with the isoindoline-dione.
