I read this as an echinomycin/triostin-type cyclic depsipeptide with two quinoxaline-2-carboxamide chromophores and a thioacetal cross-bridge. No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I have given no stereochemistry.

**Macrocycle (traced from the upper N-methyl residue clockwise):**
1. The upper α-carbon (A) carries an N-methyl amide, a C(=O)–N(Me)–Val link, and a CH(SMe) group.
2. From A, the C(=O) goes to N(Me)–CH(iPr), which is a valine residue. Its C(=O) forms an ester with O–CH2 of a serine.
3. That serine bears an NH–C(=O)–quinoxaline group on its α-carbon. Its C(=O) links to NH–CH(CH3)–C(=O), an alanine, then to N(Me) and the lower α-carbon (B).
4. B carries a C(=O) that leads to N(Me)–CH(iPr)–C(=O)–O–CH2–CH(NHC(=O)quinoxaline)–C(=O)–NH–CH(CH3)–C(=O)–N(Me), which returns to A.
5. Across the ring, A is bonded to CH(SMe), and that carbon is bonded through S directly to B. B has no β-carbon in the drawing, so S is attached to it directly.

**Substituents:** each quinoxaline is attached through its C2 carbonyl. Each valine carries an isopropyl group.
