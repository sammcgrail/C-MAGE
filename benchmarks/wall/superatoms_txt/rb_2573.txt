I read the structure as a macrocycle built from an N-ethyl indole core, a thiazole, a piperazic acid and a neopentyl ester linker. The SMILES below was written by hand from the drawing and not checked by software.

**Fragments**
- **Indole:** N1 carries an ethyl group. C2 carries a pyridine ring. C3 carries a CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2–O chain. C5 is bonded to the thiazole.
- **Pyridine:** it has a 1-methoxyethyl group at the carbon next to the ring N, and a 4-methylpiperazin-1-yl group at the meta position. The methyl on the methoxyethyl carbon is a wedge.
- **Thiazole:** C4 is bonded to the indole, and C2 carries a CH2 that leads to the amino-acid α-carbon.
- **Amino-acid α-carbon:** it bears an NH acylated by a (fluoromethyl)cyclopropane carbonyl and a C(=O) that links to the piperazic acid N.
- **Piperazic acid (hexahydropyridazine):** the ring N–N has one N acylated by the α-carbon C=O and the other N–H next to the ring carbon. That ring carbon bears the hashed ester C(=O)–O–CH2 that closes the macrocycle.
- **Cyclopropane:** the carbonyl is on a wedge and the CH2F is on a hash.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned each centre from the wedge and hash bonds and the 2D positions of its neighbours:
- the methoxyethyl carbon,
- the α-carbon,
- the piperazic carbon,
- the two cyclopropane carbons.
