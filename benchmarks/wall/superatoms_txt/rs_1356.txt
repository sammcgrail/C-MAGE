I read the molecule as an EDTA-type tri-acid amide joined to a para-substituted aniline that carries a heavily methylated alkyl group. The alkyl group is the least certain part, because the drawn branch lines are ambiguous.

**Left part (aminopolycarboxylic acid).**
- A CH2CH2 bridge links two nitrogens.
- The left nitrogen carries two CH2CO2H arms.
- The right nitrogen carries one CH2CO2H arm and a CH2C(=O)NH– arm.
- The NH attaches to the benzene ring.

**Ring.** The ring is para-disubstituted. One substituent is the NH, and the other is a quaternary carbon.

**Alkyl group on the ring.** The quaternary carbon carries one methyl and two identical chains.
- Each chain is four consecutive CH(CH3) units followed by a CH2CH3 end.
- The left chain reads from the ring as CH(CH3)–CH(CH3)–CH(CH3)–CH(CH3)–CH2–CH3.
- The right chain reads the same way.

No wedges or hashes are drawn as stereo bonds, so I added no stereochemistry.
