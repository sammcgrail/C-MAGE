Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left end:** a para-disubstituted benzene carrying a mesylate (OS(=O)(=O)CH3).
- **Linker:** a trans CH=CH–CH2 chain runs from that ring to a nitrogen.
- **Nitrogen:** it carries a benzoyl group (C(=O)Ph) and a para-disubstituted phenyl ring.
- **Right end:** that phenyl ring bears CH2–CH(OMe)–COOMe. The OMe on the stereocentre is drawn with a bold wedge.

**Stereocentre:**
- The CIP priorities are OMe > COOMe > CH2Ar > H.
- With the OMe wedge pointing toward the viewer, H is at the back.
- Going OMe (down) → COOMe (upper right) → CH2 (upper left) is counterclockwise on the page, so the centre is S.
- Written as `COC(=O)[C@@H](OC)Cc1...`, the `@@` tag gives S.

**Alkene:** the chain is drawn as trans, so I used `/C=C/`.
