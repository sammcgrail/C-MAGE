The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-pyridyl isatin.

**Biphenyl part.** The top ring carries CO2H. The second ring is attached to it at the position meta to the CO2H. On the second ring, the carbon bearing the first ring is next to the carbon bearing OH, and the carbon after that bears the HN–N= group. So the second ring is 1-aryl, 2-OH, 3-NH.

**Hydrazone and isatin part.** The NH–N= unit connects to C3 of an indolin-2-one. C2 is the C=O, and N1 carries a pyridin-3-yl group. The ring fused to the five-membered ring is an ordinary benzene ring.
