I read the structure as a bicyclic-looking but single-ring peptide: a disulfide-cyclised core carrying an N-terminal betaine-type cap and a long lipidated C-terminal tail. This is my reading of the drawing, and I could not count the long fatty-acid chain exactly.

**Cyclic core (clockwise from the top right):**
- An N-terminal Lys(ε-succinyl) residue, acetylated on its α-nitrogen, sits on the right-hand penicillamine (Pen, an S–S bridged CMe2 residue).
- The ring then runs Pen → Asn → Thr → 7-methyl-Trp → Lys(ε-NHAc) → Pen, closed by the S–S bond between the two CMe2 carbons.

**N-terminal cap:** the succinyl group is amidated by a trimethylammonio-β-amino acid, Me3N⁺–CH2–CH(NH–)–CH2–COOH.

**C-terminal chain from the left Pen:**
- The left Pen's carbonyl is bonded to the NH of Tyr(O–CH2CH2NH2).
- This is followed by 2-Nal, then 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid, Glu, Asn and 3-pyridylalanine.
- Next come N-methyl-Gly and Lys-NH2.
- The Lys ε-nitrogen carries two –C(=O)CH2OCH2CH2OCH2CH2NH– units, then γ-Glu, then a C18 diacid.

I took the diacid as C(=O)(CH2)16COOH, the semaglutide-type chain. The drawn zigzag is long and I assumed this length rather than counting every vertex.

Stereocentres use the usual L-amino-acid configuration, and the stereo at the two Pen carbons and the trimethylammonium-bearing carbon is a best guess from the wedges.
