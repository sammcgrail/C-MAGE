The drawing shows Fmoc-Lys(Boc)-OH, a lysine with both amino groups protected.

- **Backbone:** the central carbon carries the carboxylic acid (HO₂C), the α-NH and a four-carbon chain. The chain ends in an NH, which gives the lysine side chain.
- **Protecting groups:** the α-NH carries Fmoc (fluorenylmethoxycarbonyl) and the terminal NH carries Boc (tert-butoxycarbonyl).
- **Stereochemistry:** the only wedge is on the bond to the CO₂H group, so the central carbon is the only stereocentre. With the implicit H pointing away from the viewer, the priorities N > CO₂H > CH₂ run counterclockwise on the page. That makes the centre S, which is L-lysine.
- **SMILES chirality:** written starting from the carboxyl carbon, S corresponds to `[C@H]`. I checked this by swapping the N and C(=O) neighbours relative to the standard L-lysine SMILES `N[C@@H](CCCCN)C(=O)O`.
