Left ring, going around from the top: N, then C bearing CF3, then CH, then C bearing Cl, then the lower fusion carbon, then the upper fusion carbon (bonded to both N atoms).

Right ring: the upper fusion carbon, then N, then C bearing CH3, then CH, then CH, then the lower fusion carbon.
