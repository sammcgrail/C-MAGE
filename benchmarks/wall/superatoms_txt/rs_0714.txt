I read the drawing in three parts.

- **Left ring:** a pyrrolidin-2-one (5-membered lactam). The carbonyl carbon is at the top, and the ring nitrogen carries an N–CH2–CH2– chain. The ring carbon next to nitrogen is plain, so it gets no stereo label.
- **Right ring:** a furan with CO2H on one α-carbon and the CH2CH2 chain from the lactam nitrogen on the other α-carbon (a 2,5-disubstituted furan).
- **Lower side chain:** from the lactam ring carbon comes a trans (E) CH=CH, then a CH(OH) carbon drawn with a hashed OH bond, then an n-pentyl chain (five carbons ending in CH3).

For the hashed carbon, the OH points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The substituent priorities are OH > vinyl > pentyl > H. Working through the geometry gives S, which corresponds to `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order O, H, pentyl, vinyl.

The double bond is written as `/C=C/` to give the E geometry shown.

SMILES: O[C@@H](CCCCC)/C=C/C1CCC(=O)N1CCc1ccc(C(=O)O)o1
