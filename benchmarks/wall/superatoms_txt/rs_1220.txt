I read the drawing as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide attached through its NH to a nine-membered ring.

**Pyridine part:** the ring N is at the bottom left, C2 carries the C(=O)NH group, C3 carries OH and C4 carries OMe.

**Nine-membered ring:** going around from the stereocentre, the atoms are:
- C-alpha, which bears the amide NH
- a C(=O)
- O
- CH2
- CH, which bears NHBoc
- CH2
- O
- a C(=O)
- CH2, which closes back to C-alpha

**Boc group:** NH–C(=O)–O–C(CH3)3 on the ring CH.

**Stereochemistry:** only C-alpha has a hashed bond, so only it gets a descriptor. The NHBoc-bearing CH is drawn with plain bonds and gets none.

With the hashed N pointing away, the implicit H faces the viewer. From the H side, N → C(=O) → CH2 runs clockwise, which corresponds to `@` in the SMILES order N, H, ring-closure C(=O), CH2.

SMILES: COc1ccnc(C(=O)N[C@H]2CC(=O)OCC(NC(=O)OC(C)(C)C)COC2=O)c1O
