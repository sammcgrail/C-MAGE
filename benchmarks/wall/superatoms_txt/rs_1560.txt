I read the molecule as a pyrazin-2(1H)-one core with three substituents, and worked out the stereocentre from the wedge.

- **Pyrazinone ring:** N1 carries the side chain, C2 is the C=O, C3 bears the indoline nitrogen, and C5 bears Cl.
- **Side chain on N1:** a CH bearing ethyl, cyclopropyl and an H drawn on a wedge.
- **Indoline on C3:** attached through its nitrogen. Going round the benzo ring from the ring-fusion carbon next to N, the substituents are 7-Cl, 6-F and 5-OMe.

**Stereocentre:** The priorities are N, then cyclopropyl (C,C,H), then ethyl (C,H,H), then H. The H is on the wedge, toward the viewer. Read on the page, N → cyclopropyl → ethyl runs counterclockwise. Reversing for the wedged H gives R.

In the SMILES `CC[C@H](C1CC1)n1...`, the neighbour order is ethyl, H, cyclopropyl, N. I checked that `@` encodes R for this order.
