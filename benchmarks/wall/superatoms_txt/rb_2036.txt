This is a cyclosporin A analogue: the 11-residue cyclic peptide is kept, and the MeBmt side chain is modified. I wrote the SMILES by adapting the known cyclosporin A SMILES rather than deriving every stereocentre independently, so the stereochemistry assumes the natural cyclosporin configuration. I did not check each wedge and hash against it.

**Ring sequence (read from the drawing):** MeBmt – Abu – Sar – MeLeu – Val – MeLeu – Ala – D-Ala – MeLeu – MeLeu – MeVal, closed back to MeBmt. The drawn N-methyl groups, isobutyl and isopropyl side chains, and the ethyl group on the Abu residue all match this sequence.

**Modified MeBmt side chain:** in cyclosporin A, the CH(CH3) carbon of MeBmt carries CH2–CH=CH–CH3. Here it carries three CH2 groups and then a C=O. That carbonyl acylates one nitrogen of a piperazine.

**Substituent on the piperazine:**
- The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)CH2–O.
- That fragment is attached to a para-substituted phenyl ring.
- The phenyl ring is bonded to N1 of a pyrazole that has CF3 at C5 and a carboxamide at C4.
- The carboxamide nitrogen is attached to a 3-chloro-5-acrylamidophenyl ring.
