The drawing shows a benzene ring with three substituents.

- The top-left carbon of the ring carries an O–CH2–O–CH3 group (a methoxymethyl ether).
- The adjacent carbon, on the left, carries OH.
- The bottom carbon carries CF3.

Numbering the ring: C1 (top) bears OMOM and C2 (upper left) bears OH. C3 (lower left) is CH and C4 (bottom) bears CF3. C5 (lower right) and C6 (upper right) are CH.

The OH carbon and the CF3 carbon are separated by one CH, so they are meta to each other. The OMOM carbon and the CF3 carbon are para to each other, since C1 and C4 are opposite across the ring.
