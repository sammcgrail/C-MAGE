I read the structure in three parts and wrote the SMILES from them.

**Left tricyclic core (pyridazine fused to a bis-piperazine system):**
- The pyridazine ring carries a 2-hydroxyphenyl group on the carbon next to the ring N=N.
- The carbon on the other side of the N=N bears the NH.
- That NH leads to a CH2 and then to the stereocentre. The stereocentre is bonded to the NH-side CH2 (wedge), a CH2 leading to the piperidine-bearing N, a ring N, and an implicit H.
- The ring N on the stereocentre is bonded to the pyridazine carbon. The second ring closes through CH2–N(piperidin-4-yl)–CH2–CH2–N.

**Linker:** the piperidine N is joined by a three-carbon chain (CCC) to an NH on the aromatic ring of the next fragment.

**Right-hand fragment:** an isoindoline-1,3-dione whose N carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. This is the pomalidomide-type unit, attached through the 4-amino group.

**Stereochemistry:** the wedge points to the NH-side CH2, so the implicit H is behind the plane. Working through the neighbour order in the SMILES (CH2 of the NH side, H, ring-closure N, CH2) gives `@`, written as `[C@H]`. The piperidinyl and glutarimide carbons are drawn with plain bonds, so they get no stereo.
