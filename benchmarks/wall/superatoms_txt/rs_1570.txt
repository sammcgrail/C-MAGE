I read the molecule as a nine-membered dilactone ring carrying a mesitoate ester on a pyridine amide.

**Fragments**
- **Mesitoate ester:** a 2,4,6-trimethylbenzoate whose ester oxygen is attached to C3 of the pyridine.
- **Pyridine:** it has a 4-OMe group and a C2 carboxamide, and the amide nitrogen is attached to the ring.
- **Nine-membered ring**, going round from the NH-bearing carbon:
  - CH(NH)
  - C(=O)
  - ring O
  - CH(CH3), with the methyl on a wedge
  - CH(O-isobutyrate), with the O on a hash
  - CH(CH2Ph), with the benzyl on a wedge
  - C(=O)
  - ring O
  - CH2, which closes back to the CH(NH)
- **Isobutyrate:** the ester on the ring is O–C(=O)–CH(CH3)2.

**Stereochemistry**
There are four stereocentres, and I assigned each from its wedge or hash:
- The NH-bearing carbon has the N on a hash.
- The methyl-bearing carbon has its methyl on a wedge.
- The isobutyrate-bearing carbon has its O on a hash.
- The benzyl-bearing carbon has its CH2Ph on a wedge.

I worked out @/@@ for each by hand from the drawn geometry rather than with software, so the stereo tags are the least certain part of the answer.
