I read this as a silicon-rhodamine (Si-rhodamine) dye carrying a diaza-crown ether (a 1,4-dioxa-7,10-diazacyclododecane ring, a calcium/metal-sensing motif) on its pendant benzoate ring.

**Xanthene core.** The top ring carries NMe2 and is bonded to Si(Me)2. The bottom-right ring carries the =N+(Me)2 iminium. The central carbon (C9) joins the two rings and bears the pendant aryl group.

**Pendant benzoate ring.** Going around the ring from the carbon attached to C9:
- C1 is attached to the xanthene C9.
- C2 bears the carboxylate (C(=O)[O-]).
- C3 is an aromatic CH.
- C4 bears OMe.
- C5 bears the crown-ether nitrogen.
- C6 is an aromatic CH, which closes back to C1.

**Crown ring.** The ring is N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2 back to the first N, which is 12 atoms in total. One N is attached to the benzoate ring. The other N is attached to a 2-methoxyphenyl group.

**SMILES construction.**
- Xanthene part: CN(C)c1ccc2c(c1)[Si](C)(C)c1cc(=[N+](C)C)ccc1C2=... Here the central carbon is double-bonded to the ring so that the iminium ring is quinoid. Written out, the iminium ring is C2=C1C=CC(=[N+](C)C)C=C1[Si].
- Full SMILES: CN(C)c1ccc2c(c1)[Si](C)(C)C1=CC(=[N+](C)C)C=CC1=C2c1cc(N2CCOCCN(CCOCC2)c2ccccc2OC)c(OC)cc1C(=O)[O-]

**Check on the iminium ring.** C1(=CC(=[N+](C)C)C=CC1=C2…) has Si–C1, then C1=C, then C(=N+), then C=C, then C1(=C2). The ring atoms are C1, CH, C(=N), CH, CH, C(=C9), which is 6 atoms. C1 has a double bond to CH and a single bond to Si. C(=N) is attached to CH, which is double-bonded to CH in "C=C", and that CH is attached to the final ring carbon, which carries the exocyclic double bond to C9. The ring is therefore conjugated correctly.

**Check on the crown ring.** N2CCOCCN(...)CCOCC2 gives N, C, C, O, C, C, N, C, C, O, C, C and back to the first N, which is 12 atoms. The ring-closure digit 2 is reused after the xanthene ring 2 has already closed, which is valid.
