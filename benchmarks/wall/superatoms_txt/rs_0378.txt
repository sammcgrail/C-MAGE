I read the drawing as an ezetimibe-type azetidin-2-one carrying a substituted biphenyl in place of the 4-hydroxyphenyl group.

**Structure, region by region**
- **Core:** a four-membered β-lactam ring with N, C=O, and two CH carbons.
- **Nitrogen:** it carries a 4-fluorophenyl group.
- **C4 (next to N):** it carries a 4-substituted phenyl ring, drawn with a wedge. That phenyl is joined to a second ring bearing a methyl, a hydroxyl and a methoxy. The methyl and methoxy sit meta to the biaryl bond, and the OH sits para to it, between them.
- **C3 (next to C=O):** it carries an H drawn with a wedge, and a chain CH2–CH2–CH(OH)–(4-fluorophenyl). The carbinol OH is also on a wedge.

**Stereochemistry from the wedges**
- **C4:** the CIP priority order is N > aryl > C3, and the lowest-priority H points away. N, aryl, C3 run counterclockwise on the page, so C4 is S.
- **C3:** the priority order is C=O > C4 > CH2. These run counterclockwise on the page, but H points toward the viewer, so the true sense is clockwise and C3 is R.
- **Carbinol carbon:** the priority order is OH > aryl > CH2. These run counterclockwise on the page, and H points away, so it is S.

This gives (3R,4S) in the ring and S at the carbinol, the same configuration as ezetimibe. I therefore started from the ezetimibe SMILES and replaced the 4-hydroxyphenyl group with the substituted biphenyl.
