The drawing shows a quaternary carbon bearing a phenyl group (Ph), two ethyl groups (Et), and a carbonyl carbon. That carbonyl carbon is an ester: C(=O)–O–CH2–CH2–O–CH2–CH2–N(Et)2.

Reading the chain from the left, the central carbon has Ph, Et, Et and the C(=O) group. The ester oxygen then connects through two CH2 groups to an ether oxygen. Two more CH2 groups follow, ending in NEt2 (a nitrogen with two ethyl groups).

Written as SMILES, this is CCC(CC)(c1ccccc1)C(=O)OCCOCCN(CC)CC.

The molecule has no stereocentres, so no stereochemistry is needed.
