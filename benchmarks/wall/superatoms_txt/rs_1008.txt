I read the molecule as an L-tryptophanamide whose α-amino group is alkylated with a CH2 link to the 5-position of a 2-substituted indole. That indole carries a 5-amino-2-methyl-3H-imidazo[4,5-b]pyridin-7-yl group at its 2-position.

- **Tryptophan part:** the indol-3-yl-CH2 sits on the α-carbon, which also carries CONH2 and the secondary NH. The α-carbon is a stereocentre. The bold wedge points to the CH2 group, so the hydrogen is at the back. With priorities N > CONH2 > CH2 > H, the sequence N → CONH2 → CH2 runs counterclockwise, giving S (the L-tryptophan configuration). In the SMILES below, `[C@H]` written after the carbonyl carbon encodes this S centre.
- **Linker and middle indole:** the NH is joined through a CH2 to C5 of an indole, and C2 of that indole bears the heteroaryl group.
- **Heteroaryl part:** the pyridine ring has an NH2 next to the ring nitrogen. The C7 attachment point is adjacent to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the imidazole N (the non-NH nitrogen). The imidazole NH is next to the carbon bonded to the pyridine nitrogen, and the 2-position carries a methyl group.

SMILES: NC(=O)[C@H](Cc1c[nH]c2ccccc12)NCc1ccc2[nH]c(cc2c1)-c1cc(N)nc2[nH]c(C)nc12
