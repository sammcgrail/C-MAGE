I read the drawing as a trans-1,4-disubstituted cyclohexane linking a benzothiazole core to a spiro-azetidine chain that ends in a thalidomide-type aminoisoindoline-dione.

**Left to right:**
- **Pyridine amide:** a 6-(1,1-difluoroethyl)pyridine-2-carboxamide, whose NH is attached to the benzothiazole at C6.
- **Benzothiazole:** it carries a C(CH3)2OH group at C5, and its C2 is attached to the cyclohexane.
- **Cyclohexane:** the benzothiazole is on a wedge bond at one carbon and the CH2 is on a hashed bond at the para carbon, so the ring is trans-1,4. Both ring carbons are pseudo-symmetric, so the absolute configuration does not need to be assigned.
- **Spiro linker:** the CH2 leads to the nitrogen of a 2-azaspiro[3.3]heptane. The other ring of the spiro system bears a CH2CH2NH chain at its far carbon.
- **Right end:** the NH connects to C4 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its ring nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, drawn without stereochemistry.
