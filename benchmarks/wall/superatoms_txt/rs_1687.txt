The drawing shows a naphthalene (two fused benzene rings) with a CHO group on the right-hand ring.

The right ring's left vertical edge is the fusion bond. The CHO-bearing carbon is the upper-right vertex. It is separated from the fusion carbon by one CH (the top vertex), so it sits at the β position (C-2), not the α position next to the ring fusion.

This is 2-naphthaldehyde. The SMILES `O=Cc1ccc2ccccc2c1` has the aldehyde carbon on an aromatic carbon that is one carbon removed from the ring-fusion atom, and it has 10 aromatic ring carbons plus the CHO carbon, matching the drawing.
