The drawing shows a piperazine ring with an N-formyl group (OHC–N) on one nitrogen. The other ring nitrogen is bonded to a carbon that carries a double bond to N–CH3 and a single bond to NH–NH2. This carbon is therefore an amidrazone-type carbon.

Reading the pieces:
- Formyl group on the ring nitrogen: O=CN
- Piperazine ring: N1CCN(CC1)
- Central carbon: C(=NC)NN

The C=N double bond is drawn without clear cis/trans geometry, so I give no stereo label.

SMILES: O=CN1CCN(CC1)C(=NC)NN
