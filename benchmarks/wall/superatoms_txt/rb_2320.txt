I read the drawing as a disulfide-cyclised peptide with a lipidated Lys side chain, and wrote the SMILES in the order below.

1. **N-terminus and ring:** the chain starts as Ac-Pen, then Asn, Thr and 7-methyl-Trp, then Lys, then a second Pen. The two Pen sulfurs are joined by the S–S bond that closes the ring.
2. **Lys side chain:** the Lys ε-amine carries two repeats of the –CO–CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH– spacer. These are followed by γ-Glu, whose α-amine is acylated by the fatty acid.
3. **Fatty chain length:** the chain runs off the bottom edge of the image, so its length is uncertain. From the bond spacing I counted about 13 bonds beyond the carbonyl, which gives a myristoyl (C14) group. A palmitoyl (C16) chain would also be plausible, but I went with the count.
4. **C-terminal tail:** after the second Pen come Tyr(O-CH2CH2NH2), 2-naphthylalanine, the 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid residue, Lys(Ac), Asn, 3-pyridylalanine and N-methylglycinamide.
5. **Stereochemistry:** I assigned L-configuration to the α-carbons by default rather than reading each wedge individually. These assignments are therefore not carefully checked against the drawing.
