Reading the structure region by region:

- **Left end:** an N-formyl hydroxylamine, O=CH–N(OH)–CH2–.
- **First stereocentre:** it carries an n-butyl group on a wedge, the CH2N group and the amide carbonyl. The CIP priorities are C(=O)N > CH2N > butyl > H. With H pointing away, the order C(=O)N → CH2N → butyl runs clockwise, so this centre is R.
- **Amide linkage:** –C(=O)–NH–.
- **Second stereocentre:** it carries a tert-butyl group on a hashed bond, the NH and the C(=O). The priorities are N > C(=O) > tBu > H. The tBu is hashed, so H points toward the viewer. N → C(=O) → tBu looks clockwise from the front, so the true order is counterclockwise and this centre is S, as in L-tert-leucine.
- **Right end:** the carbonyl is bonded to a piperidine nitrogen. The piperidine ring has a methyl ester (CO2Me) at C4.
