I read the molecule as a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand joined through a linker to a pyrimidine kinase-binding group. I worked through it region by region.

**Left end (glutarimide and isoindoline-dione).**
- The 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring is attached through its C3 to the isoindoline-1,3-dione nitrogen.
- The benzo ring of the isoindoline carries an O–CH2–C(=O)–NH group at the 4-position.
- The glutarimide C3 is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.

**Linker.** I counted the zigzag between the amide NH and the piperidine nitrogen as 7 CH2 groups.

**Piperidine.** It is N-substituted by the heptyl chain. Its C3 carries an NH drawn on a wedge bond.
- At C3, the neighbours are C4 (drawn up), C2 (the CH2 next to the ring N, lower left) and the wedged NH (lower right).
- The priorities are N > C2 > C4 > H, and the H points away from the viewer.
- Going N → C2 → C4 runs clockwise on the page, so the centre is R.
- In the SMILES I write this centre as `[C@H]`, which I checked by permutation to correspond to R.

**Right end.** The NH on piperidine C3 attaches to C2 of a pyrimidine. That pyrimidine has Cl at C5 and an NH-aryl group at C4. The aryl group is a phenyl bearing an ortho isopropylsulfonyl group, S(=O)(=O)CH(CH3)2.
