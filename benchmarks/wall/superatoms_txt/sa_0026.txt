The drawing is Fmoc-protected aspartic acid with a tert-butyl ester on the side chain (Fmoc-Asp(OtBu)-OH).

- **Backbone:** the stereocentre carries an NH, a CH2 and a CO2H. The NH is capped with Fmoc and the CH2 leads to CO2tBu.
- **Fmoc group:** I expanded it to the fluorenylmethyl carbamate, `NC(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12`.
- **Side-chain ester:** CO2tBu expands to `CC(=O)OC(C)(C)C`.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only wedge is the bold bond to HO2C, so the implicit H points away from the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CO2H > CH2 > H. Going N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise as seen from the front, which gives S, the L-aspartic acid configuration.
- **SMILES chirality tag:** written starting from the CO2H carbon, the neighbour order is CO2H, H, CH2, N. That order needs `@` for S.

SMILES: OC(=O)[C@H](CC(=O)OC(C)(C)C)NC(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc12
