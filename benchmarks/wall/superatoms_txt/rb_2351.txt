I read the drawing as a macrocycle assembled from five parts, and wrote the SMILES in that order.

- **Piperazic ester:** a hexahydropyridazine ring whose C3 carbon carries the ester C=O. Its ring N1 is acylated.
- **Linker:** the ester oxygen is joined through CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2 to C3 of the indole.
- **Indole:** N-ethyl, with a 2-(1-methoxyethyl)pyridin-3-yl group on C2 and the thiazole on C5.
- **Thiazole and side chain:** thiazole C4 bonds to indole C5. Thiazole C2 carries CH2–CH(NHC(=O)-cyclopropyl)–C(=O), and that carbonyl closes the ring onto the piperazic N1.
- **Cyclopropane:** a 2-methylcyclopropane-1-carboxamide.

All five stereocentres are labelled S in the drawing. I assigned each @/@@ by working out the CIP priorities. The piperazic C3 needed @@, and the other four came out as @, @, @@ and @ in the order they appear in the string.
