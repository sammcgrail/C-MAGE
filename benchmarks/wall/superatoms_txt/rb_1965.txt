This molecule is a PROTAC-type conjugate. I read it in four parts and joined them:

- **Target-binding end:** a 5-(3,5-difluorobenzyl)-1H-indazol-3-yl amide of a benzamide. The benzamide ring carries a tetrahydropyran-4-ylamino group ortho to the amide and a piperazin-1-yl group para to it.
- **Linker:** the piperazine nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)CH2-O-CH2-C(=O), and the second carbonyl is joined to the amine of the next unit.
- **E3-ligase end:** an L-tert-leucine unit linked to (2S,4R)-4-hydroxyproline. The proline carboxamide is attached to a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzyl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** the tert-leucine carbon and the two hydroxyproline carbons are the stereocentres. I assigned them by matching the standard VHL-ligand configuration (S, 2S, 4R) rather than reading each wedge individually.
