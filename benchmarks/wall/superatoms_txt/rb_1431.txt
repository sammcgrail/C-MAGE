I read the structure in four pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

2. **Pyridine and linker:** the pyridine has an NHMe group and, at the adjacent carbon, a 1,3,4-thiadiazole. The thiadiazole's other carbon is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to C4 of a piperidine.

4. **Right end:** the isoindolinone nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The CH2 substituent sits at position 5 of the isoindolinone, meta to the carbonyl-bearing ring carbon.
