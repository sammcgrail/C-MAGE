I read the structure in four parts and wrote the SMILES from them.

- **Quinoline:** a 7-methoxy-2-phenylquinolin-4-yl group, attached through an ether oxygen to C4 of the pyrrolidine.
- **Pyrrolidine nitrogen:** acylated with a 2-hydroxy-2-phenylacetyl group. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.
- **Pyrrolidine C2:** carries the carboxamide, which is bonded to the cyclopropyl nitrogen.
- **Cyclopropane:** a 1-amino-2-vinylcyclopropane-1-carbonyl unit, with the carbonyl linked to NH–SO2–cyclopropyl (an acylsulfonamide).

For stereochemistry, the drawing puts the carboxamide wedge at C2 and the hashed O at C4 on opposite faces of the pyrrolidine. That is the trans arrangement, which I wrote as (2S,4R). I derived the C2 and C4 chirality tags from the known L-trans-4-hydroxyproline SMILES and checked them against the order I wrote the atoms in.

I did not derive the cyclopropane tags from the wedges. I took them from the standard (1R,2S)-vinyl-cyclopropane pattern (vinyl-ACCA, 1-amino-2-vinylcyclopropanecarboxylic acid) used for this class of compounds, which I recalled from memory, so that part is the least certain.
