This is a maleimide-free fumarate-capped linker (methyl fumarate amide, 6-aminohexanoyl, Val-Ala, PAB carbamate) attached to monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE). I read it region by region and took the MMAE stereochemistry from the known structure rather than from the wedges.

1. **Left end:** a methyl ester on a trans C=C (`COC(=O)/C=C/`) leads into an amide, `C(=O)NCCCCCC(=O)`. This is a 6-aminohexanoyl spacer with five CH2 groups between the NH and the C=O.
2. **Dipeptide:** L-Val (isopropyl side chain, wedged) then L-Ala. The Ala methyl is hashed, and rotating the drawing shows this is also the L configuration. The Ala C=O is attached to an aryl NH (`Nc1ccc(...)cc1`).
3. **Linker:** the para-substituted benzene carries a CH2–O–C(=O)–N(CH3) carbamate (PAB).
4. **MMAE core:**
   - N-methyl-valine
   - valine
   - an N-methyl dolaisoleuine unit (sec-butyl and OMe)
   - a dolaproine unit (pyrrolidine with OMe and CH3)
   - a norephedrine-type end, `N[C@@H](C)[C@@H](O)c2ccccc2`
5. **Stereochemistry:** I wrote the L-Val and L-Ala centres from the drawing and the auristatin centres from known MMAE, which I assumed matches the wedges.
