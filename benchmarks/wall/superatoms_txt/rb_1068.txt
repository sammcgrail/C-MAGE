I read the structure as a PROTAC-type molecule: a VHL ligand joined through an ether–acetamide linker to an aminopyridazine–phenol ligand. I wrote it out region by region.

- **Left end:** a 2-hydroxyphenyl group sits on a pyridazine. The pyridazine carries an NH2 and is attached through its 4-position to a bicyclic diamine.
- **Bicycle:** this is 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane. N3 binds the pyridazine and the bridging N8 binds the pyrimidine.
- **Linker:** the pyrimidine is 2-substituted by N8 and carries a 5-propyl chain. The chain continues as –CH2CH2CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH–.
- **VHL ligand:** the NH is attached to tert-leucine, then to 4-hydroxyproline, then to a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzylamide.
- **Stereochemistry:** only the tert-leucine α-carbon and the two proline carbons (C2 and C4) are drawn with wedges or hashes. I assigned these as the usual VHL-ligand configuration: S at tert-leucine, 2S and 4R at hydroxyproline. I took this from the standard form of the VHL ligand rather than deriving each centre from the wedges, so the chirality tags are the least certain part. The bicycle bridgeheads are drawn with plain bonds and get no stereo.
