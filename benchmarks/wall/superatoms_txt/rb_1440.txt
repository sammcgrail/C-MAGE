Reading the drawing region by region, left to right:

**Left end.** A glutarimide (2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl) is attached through its C3 to the N of an isoindolinone. The isoindolinone has a C=O at C1, and a CH2 on the other side of the N. The benzene ring carries a CH2NH substituent at position 5.

**Urea and aryl ring.** The CH2 is bonded to NH, then C(=O), then NH, then a benzene ring. On that ring, the NH sits at position 1, Cl at position 3, and the chain at position 5.

**Chain.** The ring is attached to (CH2)13, which ends in O. This is followed by repeating CH2CH2O units. The ether oxygens are labelled in the image as O (after the 13 CH2), O, O, O, O, O, which makes six oxygens in total.

The first O is directly after the (CH2)13. Then come CH2CH2–O units, with each O separated by two carbons, and the last one leads to CH2CH2–N. So the chain is (CH2)13–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–N. That gives six O atoms and six CH2CH2 units, the last of which connects to N.

**Amine and right end.** N carries an ethyl group and a CH2CH2–O–aryl arm. The aryl is a para-substituted phenyl bearing a C(=O) attached to benzothiophene C3. The benzothiophene has 6-OH and a 2-(4-hydroxyphenyl) group.

**Benzothiophene SMILES.** c1(C(=O)Ar)c(–c2ccc(O)cc2)sc2cc(O)ccc12. The carbon at C3 is bonded to C3a, which is part of the benzo ring. The OH is at C6, which is meta to C3a and adjacent to C7, which is next to C7a bonded to S. In the SMILES, sc2cc(O)ccc12 starts at C7a, then C7, then C6 (OH), C5, C4, and C3a.
