I read the molecule as a sulfamide, N(CH3)(pyrrolidin-3-yl)–SO2–NH–CH2CH2–imidazole.

- **Pyrrolidine side:** the pyrrolidine nitrogen carries a benzyl group. Its C3 carries the N-methyl sulfamide nitrogen on a wedge bond.
- **Sulfamide:** S(=O)(=O) joins the N-methyl nitrogen to an NH, which leads into a CH2CH2 chain.
- **Imidazole:** the chain is attached at C4. Going round the ring from there: C4 (chain), C5 (4-trifluoromethylphenyl), NH, C2 (phenyl), and N= back to C4.

**Stereocentre at C3 of the pyrrolidine:**
- The CIP priorities are the exocyclic N first, then the ring CH2 bonded to the ring N, then the ring CH2CH2 carbon, then H.
- The wedge puts the exocyclic N toward the viewer, so H points away.
- Going from the exocyclic N (upper left) to the CH2–N carbon (upper right) to the CH2CH2 carbon (bottom) is clockwise, so the centre is R.
- In the SMILES below, writing the centre as `[C@@H]` with the neighbour order N, H, ring-closure CH2, then CH2CH2 gives R.

SMILES: CN([C@@H]1CCN(Cc2ccccc2)C1)S(=O)(=O)NCCc1nc(-c2ccccc2)[nH]c1-c1ccc(C(F)(F)F)cc1
