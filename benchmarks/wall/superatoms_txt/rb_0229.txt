I read the structure as a macrocycle built from a 2-thio-pyridine and a 2-thio-aryl ring joined through sulfur, with three amino-acid residues closing the ring. The SMILES below was assembled by hand from the drawing and not run through a parser.

**Aryl and pyridine rings (bottom)**
- The pyridine is attached at C3 to a CH2–NH group and at C2 to the sulfur.
- The benzene ring carries the sulfur on one carbon.
- Its neighbouring carbon carries a CH2–NH(C=O) group.
- The next carbon carries Br.
- Two CH positions follow, and the last carbon, which is also adjacent to the sulfur-bearing carbon, carries Cl.

**Residues around the ring**
- The Dab residue has its α-carbon bonded to the NH of the pyridine CH2–NH, to a C(=O)NH amide, and to a wedged CH2CH2NHC(=O)C(CH3)3 (pivalamide) side chain.
- The Lys residue is attached through that amide NH. Its wedged side chain is (CH2)4NH–Boc, and its C=O links to N(CH3).
- The N-methyl Trp residue has a wedged CH2 to an indol-3-yl group whose N1 carries Boc. Its C=O links to the NH–CH2 of the benzene ring, closing the macrocycle.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned CIP priorities from the wedge positions at each of the three α-carbons. All three work out as S, which matches L-Dab, L-Lys and L-Trp. In the SMILES this corresponds to `[C@@H]` for the Dab carbon and `[C@H]` for the Trp and Lys carbons.
