I read this as Fmoc-L-histidine with a trityl group on one imidazole nitrogen (Fmoc-His(Trt)-OH).

- **Imidazole ring:** The ring is a five-membered imidazole. The bottom-left nitrogen carries the Trt group, which I expanded to C(Ph)3. The other ring nitrogen is a plain =N–. The ring carbon at the lower right carries the CH2 side chain.
- **Side chain:** The CH2 leads to the α-carbon, which bears the CO2H and the NH–Fmoc group.
- **Fmoc:** I expanded this to the carbamate O=C(OCH2–fluorenyl), where fluorenyl is the 9H-fluoren-9-yl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** The CO2H is on a wedge, so the implicit H points away from the viewer. Viewed from the front, the priority order N → CO2H → CH2 runs counterclockwise, which gives S. This matches the L-amino acid, written as N[C@@H](CH2R)C(=O)O.
