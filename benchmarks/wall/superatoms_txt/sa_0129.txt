The drawing shows a piperidine ring (six-membered, one nitrogen) with an N-methyl group. The carbon opposite the nitrogen (C4) carries two substituents: a CO2Et group (ethyl ester) and a phenyl group. This is pethidine (meperidine).

Writing the SMILES: the N-methyl and ring are CN1CCC(CC1)(...), with the ester written as C(=O)OCC and the phenyl as c1ccccc1. All bonds are plain, so there is no stereochemistry.

SMILES: CCOC(=O)C1(c2ccccc2)CCN(C)CC1
