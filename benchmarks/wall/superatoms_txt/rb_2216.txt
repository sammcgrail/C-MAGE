I read this as a cyclosporin A–type cyclic undecapeptide in which the MeBmt side chain has been modified and linked to an aryl pyrazole carrying an acrylamide.

**Macrocycle.** I traced the ring from the MeBmt α-carbon: MeBmt, Abu (ethyl side chain), Sar, MeLeu, Val, MeLeu, Ala, D-Ala, MeLeu, MeLeu, MeVal, and back to MeBmt. The N-methyl groups sit on MeBmt, Sar, MeLeu (×4) and MeVal, as drawn.

**Modified MeBmt side chain.** The usual CH(CH₃)–CH₂–CH=CH–CH₃ chain is replaced by CH(CH₃)–CH₂CH₂CH₂–C(=O)NH–. The OH-bearing carbon is unchanged.

**Linker and tail.**
- The chain continues –CH₂CH₂–NH–C(=O)–CH₂–O– to a para-substituted phenyl ring.
- The phenyl is bonded through N1 of a pyrazole. The pyrazole has CF₃ on C5 and a C(=O)NH-aryl group on C4.
- That aryl is a benzene bearing Cl and NH–C(=O)–CH=CH₂ in a 1,3,5 pattern.

**Stereochemistry.** The drawing shows wedges and hashes on the peptide α-carbons, the MeBmt hydroxyl carbon and the methyl-bearing carbon of the side chain. I did not re-derive each one independently.
