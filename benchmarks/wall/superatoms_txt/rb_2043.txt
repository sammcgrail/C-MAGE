This is a cyclic undecapeptide in which a urea unit, –N(Me)–C(=O)–N(Me)–, replaces one amide link. I traced the ring clockwise in the drawing (N→C direction) and read each residue in turn:

1. Ala, NH–CH(CH3)–C(=O)
2. N‑Me‑Leu
3. N‑Me‑Ser (CH2OH side chain)
4. Val
5. Urea linker: Val C(=O)–N(Me)–C(=O)–N(Me), with no stereocentre
6. Leu (its N carries the second urea N‑methyl)
7. Val
8. N‑Me‑Leu
9. Leu
10. Ala, drawn with a bold wedge on H
11. N‑Me‑Leu, which closes the ring back onto the first Ala NH

For stereochemistry I assigned each α‑carbon from its hashed or wedged H and the arrangement of its N, C(=O) and side chain. Every residue comes out L except residue 10, whose bold wedge gives D‑Ala. The methyl and N bonds on that carbon are drawn close together, so this assignment is the least certain.
