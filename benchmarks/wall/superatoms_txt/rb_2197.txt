I read the drawing as a linear peptide on a resin bead, with the resin attached through an ester O at the C-terminus. The sequence, N-terminus to C-terminus, is:

Ile – Cys(Trt) – Ser(tBu) – Arg(Pbf) – Ser(tBu) – Leu – Pro – Oic – Ile – Cys(Trt) – Ile–O–resin

Here Oic is octahydroindole-2-carboxylic acid, Trt is trityl on the cysteine sulfur, tBu is tert-butyl on the serine oxygen, and Pbf is the sulfonyl protecting group on the arginine guanidine.

I checked the connectivity amide by amide. The Ser carbonyl acylates the Leu N, the Leu carbonyl acylates the Pro N, and the Pro carbonyl acylates the Oic N. The Oic 2-carboxyl then acylates the next Ile.

Stereochemistry: I assumed L-configuration at all the amino acid α-carbons and the Ile β-carbons. I did not verify each wedge against this. For Oic I used the (2S,3aS,7aS) form, which is consistent with the two ring-fusion hydrogens drawn on the same side.

The resin bead is written as [*].
