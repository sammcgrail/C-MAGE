The structure breaks down into these parts:

- **Thiomorpholine amidine:** a thiomorpholine ring (N, two CH2, S, two CH2) whose nitrogen is bonded to a C(=NH) carbon, which is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring.
- **First amide:** the benzene ring carries a C(=O)NH group on the opposite (para) position.
- **Central ring:** that NH is attached to a second benzene ring. On this ring, the carbon ortho to the NH bears a C(=O)NH-pyridyl group. The OMe sits para to the NH-bearing carbon.
- **Pyridine:** the amide NH is bonded to the 2-position of a 5-chloropyridine.

Checking the positions on the central ring: the NH-bearing carbon is at the bottom left, the carboxamide-bearing carbon is at the bottom right, and the OMe carbon is at the top. The ring runs C1 (NH), C2 (C(=O)N), C3 (CH), C4 (OMe), C5 (CH), C6 (CH). So the OMe is para to the NH carbon and meta to the carboxamide carbon.
