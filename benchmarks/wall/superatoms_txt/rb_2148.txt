I read the molecule as a cyclic peptide (an ε-acetyl-lysine-containing disulfide macrocycle) with two lipidated side-chain linkers. The stereodescriptors in the SMILES below are my best guess rather than a derivation from the wedges, so treat them as uncertain.

**Reading of the drawing, from the N-terminus:**
- **N-terminal cap:** Ac-Lys, whose ε-NH carries a linker.
- **Ring (disulfide-bridged):** Pen, Lys (carrying a second linker), Thr, 7-methyl-Trp, Lys(Ac), Pen. The two penicillamine (Pen) residues are joined through their sulfurs by an S–S bond.
- **Tail after the ring:** Tyr(O-CH2CH2-NH2), 2-naphthylalanine, 4-aminotetrahydropyran-4-carboxylic acid, Lys(Ac), Asn, 3-pyridylalanine, then Sar-NH2 (N-methylglycine amide).
- **Each linker:** two consecutive –C(=O)CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH– units, then a γ-Glu, then an α,ω-diacid acyl group.

**Uncertainty in the diacid length:** I estimated the diacid chain from drawing proportions as 8 carbons in total (HOOC–(CH2)6–CO–). I could not count the vertices with certainty, so this length may be off by one or two CH2 groups.
