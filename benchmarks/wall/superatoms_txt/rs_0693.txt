The drawing shows a 2-(trifluoromethyl)benzamide whose amide nitrogen is attached to a substituted phenyl ring.

**Benzoyl part:** a benzene ring carries CF3 on the carbon next to the carbonyl-bearing carbon (ortho). The carbonyl carbon has a double-bonded O and is bonded to the NH.

**Anilide ring:** the carbon attached to NH (C1) has a methyl on each flanking carbon.
- C2 (upper right) carries a methyl.
- C3 carries the pyrrolidin-1-yl group, attached through N.
- C4 carries a methyl (lower right).
- C5 is an unsubstituted CH.
- C6 (lower left) carries a methyl.

So the ring is 2,4,6-trimethyl-3-(pyrrolidin-1-yl)phenyl.

**SMILES:** starting from the CF3 ring, then the amide, then the substituted ring:
O=C(Nc1c(C)c(N2CCCC2)c(C)cc1C)c1ccccc1C(F)(F)F

Atom check on the anilide ring: c1 (N-bearing), c(C) at C2, c(N2CCCC2) at C3, c(C) at C4, cH at C5, c1C at C6 with its methyl. This matches the drawing.
