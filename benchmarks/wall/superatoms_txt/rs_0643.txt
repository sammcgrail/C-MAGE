I read this as a cholesterol 3-O-carbamate linked through a 6-aminohexanoyl chain to a pyrrolidine nitrogen. The pyrrolidine carries a succinate half-ester at C3 and a DMT-protected hydroxymethyl group at C4.

**Pieces, region by region**
- **Succinate:** HOOC–CH2CH2–C(=O)–O– is attached to the pyrrolidine C3.
- **Pyrrolidine:** a five-membered ring containing N, with CH2–O–DMT on the adjacent ring carbon (C4).
- **DMT group:** a central carbon bearing one phenyl and two 4-methoxyphenyl rings.
- **Linker:** N–C(=O)–(CH2)5–NH–C(=O)–O–. I counted five CH2 vertices between the amide carbonyl and the HN, so this is a 6-aminohexanoyl chain.
- **Cholesterol:** the 5-ene steroid core (rings A–D) has the carbamate O at C3. The side chain is CH(CH3)–CH2CH2CH2–CH(CH3)2.

**Stereochemistry**
- Wedges are drawn only at C10 (methyl), C13 (methyl) and C17 (side chain). I assigned these as in natural cholesterol.
- C3, C8, C9, C14, C20 and the two pyrrolidine stereocentres are drawn with plain bonds, so they carry no stereo marks.
