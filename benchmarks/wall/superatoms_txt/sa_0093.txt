Reading the drawing from left to right:

- The left end is a methanesulfonate ester, MsO–CH2, with Ms = CH3SO2.
- The first stereocentre has an OH on a wedge bond pointing down, so its implicit H points away from the viewer.
- The second stereocentre has an OH on a wedge bond pointing up, so its implicit H also points away from the viewer.
- The right end is CH2–OMs, another methanesulfonate ester.

This gives a butane-2,3-diol with a mesylate on each terminal carbon, 1,4-bis(methylsulfonyloxy)butane-2,3-diol.

I worked out the @/@@ labels from the wedge directions by looking at each centre with its H pointing away, then converting to SMILES neighbour order. Both centres come out as S, which matches treosulfan, (2S,3S)-configured.

SMILES: CS(=O)(=O)OC[C@H](O)[C@@H](O)COS(C)(=O)=O
