This is metamizole (the free acid form of dipyrone), a pyrazolone ring carrying a sulfonic acid side chain.

**Ring.** Five-membered pyrazolone:
- N1 bears the phenyl group (drawn as "Ph").
- N2 bears a methyl group.
- C3 carries a methyl group and is double-bonded to C4.
- C4 carries the N(CH3)CH2SO3H substituent.
- C5 is the carbonyl carbon (C=O) and closes the ring back to N1.

**Side chain.** An N-methyl nitrogen bonded to CH2–SO3H. The label reads "SO3H", so I wrote it as S(=O)(=O)O.

**SMILES.** Writing the ring from the carbonyl carbon: O=C1C(N(C)CS(=O)(=O)O)=C(C)N(C)N1c1ccccc1

Atom check:
- Ring atoms: C(=O), C4, C3, N2, N1, which is five.
- Methyl groups: one on C3, one on N2, and one on the side-chain nitrogen.
- The phenyl is on N1, which is the nitrogen attached to the carbonyl carbon, as drawn.
