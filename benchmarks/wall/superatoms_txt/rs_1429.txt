The drawing shows a pyridine ring with these substituents:
- The ring nitrogen is at the lower left.
- The carbon next to the nitrogen (C2) carries the carboxamide, C(=O)NH.
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are CH.

The amide nitrogen is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring. That ring is linked through an oxygen to a second benzene ring, which carries a CN group meta to the oxygen.

SMILES for the pyridine part: COc1ccnc(C(=O)N...)c1O

Full SMILES: COc1ccnc(C(=O)Nc2ccc(Oc3cccc(C#N)c3)cc2)c1O

Checking the cyanophenyl ring: the carbon bearing O is at the bottom left of that ring and the CN carbon is at the bottom right. These are separated by one carbon, so the substitution is meta, which matches the SMILES.
