**Nine-membered dilactone ring.** Going around the ring from the NH-bearing carbon:

- C1(=O)
- O
- CH(CH3)
- CH(O-isobutyryl)
- CH(CH2Ph)
- C(=O)
- O
- CH2, which closes back to the CH(NH-acyl)

The ester on the ring is isobutyrate, OC(=O)CH(CH3)2. The substituent on the ring carbon next to the lactone carbonyl is a benzyl group.

**Stereochemistry.** I assigned each centre from the wedges and hashes, ranking substituents by CIP priority and taking the implicit H as opposite the wedge or hash:
- The NH-bearing carbon has a hashed N and is S.
- The CH(CH2Ph) carbon has a wedged benzyl and is R.
- The CH(O-isobutyryl) carbon has a hashed O and is R.
- The CH(CH3) carbon has a wedged methyl and is S.

I then converted these to @/@@ according to the neighbour order in the SMILES string.
