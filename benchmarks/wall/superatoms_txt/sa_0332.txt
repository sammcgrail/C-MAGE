The drawing is Fmoc-Cys(Trt)-OH, an N-Fmoc, S-trityl protected cysteine.

- **Backbone:** the stereocentre carries CO2H, NH–Fmoc and CH2–S–Trt, plus an implicit H.
- **Protecting groups:** Fmoc is the fluorenylmethoxycarbonyl group on nitrogen. Trt is the trityl group, C(Ph)3, on sulfur.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CO2H bond is hashed, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
  - CIP priorities are N > CH2S > CO2H > H. CH2S outranks CO2H because S outranks O at the first point of difference.
  - On the page, N (lower left) → CH2S (right) → CO2H (upper left) runs counterclockwise. With H toward the viewer this reverses to clockwise, so the centre is R. That is the L-cysteine configuration.
- **SMILES chirality tag:** I wrote the atoms in the order CO2H, H, CH2, N. Relative to the known L-Cys SMILES N[C@@H](CS)C(=O)O, this order differs by one swap, so the tag becomes @.
