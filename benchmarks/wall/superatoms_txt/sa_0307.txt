The drawing shows Fmoc-NH on a central carbon that also carries a CO2H group and an isopropyl group (iPr). The iPr group is on a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer.

**Connectivity:** The Fmoc group is the fluorenylmethoxycarbonyl carbamate, written Fmoc-N as `NC(=O)OCC1c2ccccc2-c2ccccc21`. The central carbon is bonded to N, CO2H, iPr and one implicit H. This is the skeleton of valine, so the molecule is Fmoc-valine.

**Stereochemistry:** The CIP priorities at the stereocentre are N > CO2H > iPr > H.
- The implicit H points toward the viewer, because the iPr group is hashed (behind the plane).
- Viewed from the front, N (lower left) → CO2H (up) → iPr (lower right) runs clockwise.
- Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S.

This matches Fmoc-L-valine. The SMILES `CC(C)[C@H](N…)C(=O)O` encodes the S configuration, as in L-valine `CC(C)[C@H](N)C(=O)O`.
