I read the molecule as a nine-membered dilactone ring carrying four stereocentres, joined through an amide to a 4-methoxypyridine whose 3-oxygen is esterified by a 3-(2,6-dichlorophenyl)-5-methylisoxazole-4-carbonyl group.

**Ring and substituents**
- The ring runs: C(=O), O, CH(CH3), CH(O-isobutyryl), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), O, CH2, CH(NH-). The CH(NH-) carbon closes back to the first C(=O).
- The amide nitrogen on the CH(NH-) carbon is acylated by the pyridine-2-carbonyl group.
- The pyridine has OMe at C4 and the ester oxygen at C3.
- The isoxazole is O1, N2, C3 (2,6-dichlorophenyl), C4 (carbonyl), C5 (methyl).

**Stereochemistry** (assigned from the drawn wedges and hashes)
- The CH bearing N has a hashed N bond, giving `[C@H]`.
- The CH bearing CH3 has a wedged methyl, giving `[C@H]`.
- The CH bearing the isobutyrate oxygen has a hashed O bond, giving `[C@@H]`.
- The CH bearing the benzyl group has a wedged benzyl, giving `[C@H]`.
