I read the drawing as follows, going from left to right.

- **Cyclohexane:** a 1,4-disubstituted ring with CF3 on a bold wedge at one end and the vinyl group on a hashed bond at the other. One wedge and one hash means a trans arrangement.
- **Vinyl linker:** a trans (E) C=C joins the cyclohexane to the pyridine.
- **Pyridine:** reading from the ring nitrogen, the carbon next to N carries the carboxamide. The next ring carbon is a plain CH. The next carries the vinyl group, and the one after that carries OMe. The last carbon is a CH, adjacent to N.
- **Benzylamide:** the pyridine carboxamide NH is bonded to a CH2, which is attached to a meta-disubstituted benzene. The other benzene substituent is CH2–C(=O)–N(CH3).
- **Linker chain:** counting bonds from the amide N to the ether O gives five CH2 groups, so it is a pentyl chain ending in –O–.
- **Phthalimide end:** the O-bearing aromatic carbon is position 5 of the isoindoline-1,3-dione. The imide N carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.

For the stereo SMILES, I worked out that `[C@H]…[C@H]` on the 1,4-cyclohexane gives the trans isomer. This matches the wedge/hash pair in the drawing.
