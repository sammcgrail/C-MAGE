The structure is a coumarin (2H-chromen-2-one) core with the following substituents:

- **C7:** Cl
- **C6:** methyl
- **C4:** a phenyl ring that carries, at its meta position, a 1,2,4-oxadiazol-5(4H)-one
- **C3:** CH2C(=O)NH-aryl, where the aryl is 4-fluoro-2-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl

**Coumarin core.** Starting from the lactone oxygen, O1 is bonded to C2(=O), then C3, C4, C4a, C5, C6, C7, C8 and C8a. C8a closes back to O1. The ring-fusion carbons are C4a and C8a. In the drawing, C6 bears Me and C7 bears Cl. C5 is the CH next to C4a, and C8 is the CH next to C8a.

SMILES for the coumarin portion: O=C1Oc2cc(Cl)c(C)cc2C(c3...)=C1CC(=O)N...

**Oxadiazolone.** The ring is C3(aryl)=N–O–C(=O)–NH, closing back to C3. As a SMILES fragment: c1cccc(c1)C1=NOC(=O)N1.

**Amide aryl group.** The nitrogen is attached to ring carbon C1. C2 carries CF3, and C4 carries F. As a fragment: Nc1ccc(F)cc1C(F)(F)F.
