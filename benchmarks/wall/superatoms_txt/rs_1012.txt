The drawing shows N-(4-methoxybenzyl)cysteine.

- **Aromatic ring:** a para-substituted benzene with an OMe group at one end and a CH2 at the other.
- **Side chain:** the benzylic CH2 is bonded to NH, which is bonded to the α-carbon. The α-carbon carries CO2H on a hashed bond and CH2–SH.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the α-carbon. The CIP priorities are N > CH2SH > CO2H > H. The hashed CO2H puts the implicit H toward the viewer. Going N → CH2SH → CO2H appears counterclockwise from the front. With H pointing toward the viewer, that reverses to clockwise, so the centre is R, the same as L-cysteine.
- **SMILES check:** the L-cysteine pattern `N[C@@H](CS)C(=O)O` is R. Writing the substituted amine in the same neighbour order gives `COc1ccc(CN[C@@H](CS)C(=O)O)cc1`.
