I read the molecule as a PROTAC-type degrader made of three parts joined by a linker. The SMILES below was checked by hand for validity and atom count, but I did not run it through a parser.

**Fragments**
- **Spiro-oxindole end:** a 6-chlorooxindole whose C3 is spiro-fused to a pyrrolidine. The pyrrolidine carbon next to the ring NH is itself spiro to a cyclohexane. The pyrrolidine also carries a 3-chloro-2-fluorophenyl group and a carboxamide.
- **Linker:** the carboxamide nitrogen is bonded to a para-substituted benzene, which carries a second amide. That amide continues through NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH.
- **Degrader end:** the linker ends on the 4-position of an isoindolin-1-one. Its ring nitrogen is bonded to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, the lenalidomide-type unit. The stereocentre on this piperidine is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo label.

**Stereochemistry**

I assigned the three pyrrolidine and spiro stereocentres from the wedges and hashes:
- The carboxamide-bearing carbon has a hashed bond to the amide carbon.
- The aryl-bearing carbon has a wedge to the aryl ring.
- The oxindole spiro carbon has a wedge to the lactam C=O and a hash to the aromatic ring carbon.

I worked each centre out from the drawn geometry. The resulting labels are `[C@@H]`, `[C@@]` and `[C@H]` in the SMILES below.
