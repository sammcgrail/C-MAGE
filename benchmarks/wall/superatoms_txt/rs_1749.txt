I read the molecule as a morpholine amide linked to a cyclohexylethyl-substituted succinamide, with an N-aryl 1,2-diaminopropane on the other end.

**Connectivity**
- The morpholine nitrogen is acylated by a C=O.
- That C=O leads to a CH2 and then to a stereocentre carrying a CH2CH2-cyclohexyl chain.
- The stereocentre is also bonded to a second C(=O), an amide to NH.
- That NH is bonded to a CH(CH3) stereocentre, then a CH2, then an NH.
- The last NH is bonded to a para-substituted benzene ring bearing OCF3.

**Stereochemistry**
- First stereocentre: the cyclohexylethyl chain is on a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer. The implicit H therefore points toward the viewer. Reading CH2 → C(=O) → chain as clockwise on screen gives `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order I wrote.
- Second stereocentre: the methyl is on a wedge, so the implicit H points away. Reading N → CH3 → CH2 as clockwise on screen gives `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order I wrote.

SMILES: O=C(N1CCOCC1)C[C@@H](CCC1CCCCC1)C(=O)N[C@@H](C)CNc1ccc(OC(F)(F)F)cc1
