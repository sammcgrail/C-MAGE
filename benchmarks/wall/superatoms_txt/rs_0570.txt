I read this as an antimycin-type nine-membered dilactone carrying an acylated 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide.

**Substituents**
- **Pyridine:** the ring N is adjacent to C2, which carries the C(=O)NH amide. C3 bears O–C(=O)–S–propyl (a thiocarbonate), and C4 bears OMe.
- **Dilactone ring (9 atoms):** C(=O), O, CH(CH3), CH(O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), O, CH2, CH(NH). The isobutyrate ester sits on the carbon next to the CH(CH3).

**Stereocentres**

I assigned these from the wedges and hashes using CIP priorities.
- The NH-bearing carbon (hashed N) is S.
- The CH(CH2Ph) carbon (wedged benzyl) is R.
- The CH(O-isobutyryl) carbon (hashed O) is R.
- The CH(CH3) carbon (wedged methyl) is S.
