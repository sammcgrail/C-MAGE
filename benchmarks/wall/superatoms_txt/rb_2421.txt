I read the structure as a macrocycle and wrote it out by following the ring from the leucine side chain around.

**Ring path**
1. Start at the isobutyl group on N-methyl-leucine, whose carbonyl carries an N-methyl amide nitrogen.
2. That nitrogen is attached to CH2–CH=CH–C(=O)–O–CH2–C(CH3)2–C(=O)–C(=O)– (an α,β-unsaturated ester followed by a dimethyl α-diketone).
3. The diketone ends on the nitrogen of a piperidine-2-carboxylate (pipecolate). Its carboxyl forms an ester with a benzylic CH(O).
4. That benzylic carbon carries a CH2CH2-(3,4-dimethoxyphenyl) arm and a meta-substituted phenylene.
5. The phenylene bears NH–C(=O)CH2CH2C(=O)–NH (a succinyl linker) that connects to a phenylglycine.
6. The phenylglycine carbonyl joins the NH of a phenylalanine whose para position carries CH2NHC(=O)CH2Ph.
7. The phenylalanine carbonyl joins the nitrogen of a proline. The proline carbonyl joins the N-methyl nitrogen of the leucine, closing the ring.

**Stereochemistry, read from the wedges and hashes**
- Leucine α-carbon: S.
- Pipecolate α-carbon: S.
- Benzylic CH–O: R (hashed bond to the CH2).
- Phenylglycine α-carbon: S.
- Phenylalanine α-carbon: S.
- Proline α-carbon: S.

The CH=CH bond is drawn as a wavy line, so I gave it no cis/trans geometry.
