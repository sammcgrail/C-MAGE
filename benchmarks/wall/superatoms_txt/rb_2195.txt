**Stereochemistry:** Every α-carbon has a wedge on its side-chain bond, drawn with the NH on the left and the CH2 on the right. That geometry matches the L-amino-acid pattern N[C@@H](R)C…, so I used @@ at all ten α-carbons. The Thr and Ile side-chain stereocentres carry no wedges in the drawing, so I left them unspecified.
