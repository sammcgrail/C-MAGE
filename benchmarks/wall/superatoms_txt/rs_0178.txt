The drawing shows a central CH carbon with three substituents:

- a CH2 bonded to OMe (upper left)
- a second CH2 bonded to OMe (lower left)
- a CH2 bonded to NH, which carries a methyl group (right)

The molecule is therefore CH3NH-CH2-CH(CH2OCH3)2. It has no stereocentre, because the central carbon carries two identical CH2OMe arms.

SMILES: CNCC(COC)COC
