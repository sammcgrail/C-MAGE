I read the molecule as three parts joined by a chain.

- **Benzoxaborole:** a B(OH)–O–CH2 ring fused to a benzene ring. The benzylic CH2 sits on the ring carbon para to boron.
- **Central benzene:** it carries three groups. The benzylic CH2 is at one position, the nitrile is meta to it, and the polyether chain is ortho to it and para to the nitrile.
- **Linker:** ring–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2CH2–NH, counted from the drawing.
- **Isoindolinone end:** the NH is on the aromatic carbon next to the one bearing the ring CH2, i.e. position 4. The ring nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, and the other ring carbon is C=O.

The 2,6-dioxopiperidine carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but the drawing uses plain bonds there, so I added no stereo marks.
