I read the structure in pieces and then closed the macrocycle with ring-closure digits.

- **Left side:** HOOC–C(CH3)2–CH2– is attached to a benzene ring at a meta position.
- **Stereocentre:** the other meta carbon of that benzene carries a quaternary carbon with a hashed methyl. This carbon is also bonded to the triazole C3 and to the macrocycle chain. Working from the hashed methyl (pointing away from the viewer) gives the `[C@@]` tag in the SMILES below.
- **Chain:** from that carbon the macrocycle runs CH2–CH2–CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2–SO2–CH2–CH2. I counted three CH2 groups before the gem-dimethyl carbon, but the drawing is ambiguous there, so that count is my best reading.
- **Indole:** the chain ends on indole C4. C5 carries the CH(CH3) group, which is drawn with a wavy bond and so has no assigned stereo. C6 and C7 each carry F, and N1 is NH.
- **Pyridine and triazole:** the CH(CH3) carbon is bonded to pyridine C4, and pyridine C2 is bonded to the triazole. The triazole is a 1-methyl-1,2,4-triazole: N1 carries the methyl, C5 is bonded to the pyridine, and C3 is bonded to the quaternary stereocentre.
