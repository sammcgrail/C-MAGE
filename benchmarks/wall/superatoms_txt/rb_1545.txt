I read the structure in four pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

1. **Left end:** a 6-methylpyridine-2-carboxamide. Its amide NH is attached to the 6-position of a benzothiazole that carries a 5-methoxy group.
2. **Cyclohexane:** the benzothiazole C2 carries a 1,4-disubstituted cyclohexane. The bond to the benzothiazole is a bold wedge and the CH2 on the far carbon is a hashed bond, so the two substituents are trans. I wrote this as `[C@H]3CC[C@@H](...)CC3`. That `@`/`@@` pattern for trans is from memory of the trans-1,4-dimethylcyclohexane SMILES (`C[C@H]1CC[C@@H](C)CC1`), not something I derived.
3. **Spirocycle:** the CH2 leads to the nitrogen of a 7-azaspiro[3.5]nonane (a piperidine spiro-fused to a cyclobutane). The cyclobutane carbon opposite the spiro carbon bears a CH2CH2NH chain. That cyclobutane carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
4. **Right end:** the NH is attached to the 4-position of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. Its ring nitrogen bears a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, a thalidomide-type unit. That carbon is also drawn with plain bonds and gets no stereo label.
