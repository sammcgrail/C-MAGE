I read this as the phosphatidylcholine 1,2-dipalmitoyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine (DPPC).

- **Head group:** a trimethylammonium (N⁺(CH₃)₃) joined through a two-carbon chain to a phosphate carrying one O⁻ and one P=O.
- **Glycerol backbone:** the phosphate is bonded to a CH₂ group, which leads to a central CH carrying an acyl-O and then a second CH₂–O-acyl.
- **Acyl chains:** each chain is a carbonyl carbon, then five drawn CH₂ carbons, then the "nDec" label, which I expanded to an n-decyl group (10 carbons). That gives 1 + 5 + 10 = 16 carbons per chain, so both are palmitoyl.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the central glycerol CH, with a hashed bond to the CH₂–O-acyl group.
  - Its priorities are O-acyl > CH₂–O–P > CH₂–O-acyl > H.
  - With the hashed CH₂ pointing away and H toward the viewer, the sequence O → CH₂OP → CH₂O-acyl runs counterclockwise on the page. Reversing for the H, this is the R configuration, the natural form.
