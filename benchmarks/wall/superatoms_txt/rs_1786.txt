The drawing shows a biphenyl carboxylic acid joined through a hydrazone to an N-aryl isatin.

**Biphenyl part:** The top ring carries CO2H. The ring below it is attached at the position meta to the acid. On that second ring, the carbon next to the biaryl bond bears OH, and the next carbon bears the NH–N= group.

**Hydrazone and isatin:** The NH–N= unit links to C3 of an oxindole (2-oxo-indolin-3-ylidene). The oxindole nitrogen carries a para-substituted phenyl ring.

**Substituent on the N-aryl ring:** The para position carries CH(OMe)CF3.
