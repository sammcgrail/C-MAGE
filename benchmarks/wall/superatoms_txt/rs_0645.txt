The left ring is a benzene bearing the sulfonyl group at C1, OCF3 at C2 and Br at C4, which are the substituents at the top-left, top-right and left of the ring in the drawing.

The sulfonamide is S(=O)(=O) with an NH linking it to the right ring.

The right ring has the following substituents:
- C1 carries the N (attached to NH).
- C2 carries a methyl (top).
- C3 carries the piperidin-1-yl group.
- C4 carries a methyl (bottom right).
- C5 is an H-bearing carbon.
- C6 carries a methyl (bottom left).

So the aryl group is 2,4,6-trimethyl-3-piperidinyl phenyl.
