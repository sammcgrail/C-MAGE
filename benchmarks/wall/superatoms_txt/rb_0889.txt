Reading the structure region by region:

**Left fragment (ribociclib-like core).** The pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidine carries a cyclopentyl group on N7 and a C(=O)N(C)C amide at C6. C2 bears an NH linker.

**Pyridine.** The NH attaches at C2 of a pyridine ring. The ring nitrogen is next to this carbon, and a carboxamide sits at C5.

**Linker.** The amide NH leads into a chain of carbons. Counting the vertices from the NH: the bond from N goes to the first CH2 at about 790, then 845, 900, 960, 1020, and 1080, which is where the O attaches. That is 6 CH2 groups, so the chain is NCCCCCCO with six carbons.

**Right fragment (thalidomide-type).** The oxygen is attached to the benzene ring at position 4 of the isoindoline-1,3-dione. The imide nitrogen is attached to a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group. The stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
