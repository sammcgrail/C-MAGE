The drawing shows a central tertiary amine nitrogen with three substituents: an anthracen-9-yl group and two phenyl rings.

Each phenyl ring is attached to the central N at one carbon and carries an N(CH2CH3)2 group at the meta position. In the left ring, the attachment carbon is at the lower right and the diethylamino carbon is at the left. These are separated by one carbon, so the substitution is 1,3.

The anthracene is attached to the central N through its 9-position, the middle ring carbon at the top. The bottom carbon (C10) is unsubstituted.
