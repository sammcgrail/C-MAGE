This SMILES is my best reading of the drawing, with some uncertain points that I list below. The structure is a bicyclic peptide made of these parts:

- **N-terminal cap:** a hexanoyl group on the N-terminal Cys. I counted six carbons including the C=O, but the end of the chain at the top right is hard to read.
- **Peptide sequence:** hexanoyl-Cys-Pro-Pro-Thr-Gln-Phe-Cys-Asp-NH2, with the C-terminal Asp as a primary amide.
- **Core:** the two Cys thiols are each linked through S-CH2 to a 1,3,5-trisubstituted benzene. This closes the macrocycle.
- **Third benzene arm:** it carries a CH2 on one nitrogen of a piperazine. The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)CH2 to a DOTA-type tetraaza macrocycle.
- **Lutetium:** Lu is bonded to all four ring nitrogens and to the three remaining acetate oxygens. The fourth arm is the amide.

Points I could not settle:

- **Stereochemistry:** I wrote every residue as L, which matches the wedge on the first Cys. The Thr alpha centre and some other residues are drawn with hashes that I could not read reliably.
- **Lu–N bonds:** I wrote them as plain single bonds, which gives the ring nitrogens four bonds on neutral atoms. I chose this over dropping the bonds so the Lu coordination stays in the string. A strict valence checker may flag it.
