I read the molecule as a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxypicolinamide on a norbornane (bicyclo[2.2.1]heptane) core that carries two ester groups.

**Pyridine part:** The ring has N at the bottom left. The carbon next to N carries the C(=O)NH amide, the next carbon carries OH, and the next carries OMe. This gives `COc1ccnc(C(=O)N...)c1O`.

**Norbornane core:** The top CH2 is the one-carbon bridge. It joins the two bridgehead CH carbons, and the two-carbon bridges run between them.
- One two-carbon bridge is the NH-bearing CH followed by a CH2.
- The other is the pair of ester-bearing CH carbons.

**Substituents:**
- The upper ester is C(=O)OCH2CH2Ph (phenethyl).
- The lower ester is C(=O)OCH2CH(CH3)2 (isobutyl).

**Stereochemistry:** Only three centres have wedges: the C–N carbon and the two ester-bearing carbons. All three wedges point toward the viewer. I worked out @/@@ for each from the 2D coordinates, treating the implicit H as pointing back. The bridgeheads have no wedges, so I left them unspecified.
