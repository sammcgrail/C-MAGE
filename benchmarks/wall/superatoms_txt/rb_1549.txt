I read the structure in four pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

**Left end:** a benzothiophene-2-carboxamide with a CF2–PO3H2 group at C5. The carboxamide N is attached to the bicyclic core.

**Bicyclic core:** an eight-membered ring fused to a pyrrolidine. Going round the eight-membered ring: N(CH2CHF2), CH2, CH(NH-amide), C(=O), the pyrrolidine N, the fusion CH, CH2, CH2, back to the first N. The pyrrolidine carbon next to its N carries the C(=O)NH link to the next residue.

**Peptide chain:** the arginine residue has its guanidine side chain, CCCNC(N)=N. It is followed by a 4-tert-butylphenylalanine residue whose carbonyl forms an amide with a piperazine nitrogen.

**Right end:** the other piperazine N carries a three-carbon chain (CCC) ending in an NH on C4 of a 3-(2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl)isoindolin-1-one (the lenalidomide-type ligand). The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned @/@@ at the five wedged centres from the drawing, treating the implicit H as pointing away from the viewer in each case. These are the amide-bearing carbon of the eight-membered ring, the ring-fusion CH, the pyrrolidine CH, the arginine α-carbon and the phenylalanine α-carbon.
