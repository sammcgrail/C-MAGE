The drawing shows a central benzene ring substituted at the para positions. Each substituent is a CH2CH2 bridge leading to an outer benzene ring.

Each outer ring carries an N(C2H5)2 group on the carbon adjacent (ortho) to the carbon bonded to the CH2CH2 bridge. The left ring has the bridge at its upper right and the N at its lower right, which are adjacent positions. The right ring is the same.

The molecule is therefore 1,4-bis[2-(2-diethylaminophenyl)ethyl]benzene.
