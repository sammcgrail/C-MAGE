I read the molecule as a PROTAC-style conjugate with a VHL-type ligand at one end and an aminopyridazine-phenol group at the other. I worked through it region by region.

**VHL end**
- The left-hand ring is a 4-hydroxypyrrolidine-2-carboxamide. I took the ring stereochemistry as the usual VHL (2S,4R) arrangement, `N1C[C@H](O)C[C@H]1C(=O)...`, rather than re-deriving it from the wedge and hash bonds.
- The amide NH is linked through a CH2 to a phenyl ring that carries a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.
- The ring nitrogen is acylated by a CH(isopropyl) carbon.
- That carbon carries an isoxazol-5-yl group and has a hashed bond to the isopropyl. I worked out its chirality from the drawing (H toward the viewer, then C=O → iPr → isoxazole clockwise), which gives `[C@H]` when written after the carbonyl carbon.

**Linker**
- The isoxazole is attached at its 3-position to the nitrogen of a piperidine.
- That piperidine's 4-carbon is bonded to the nitrogen of a second piperidine.
- The second piperidine's 4-carbon is bonded to C5 of a pyrimidine.

**Target-binding end**
- The pyrimidine C2 is bonded to N8 of a 3,8-diazabicyclo[3.2.1]octane.
- N3 of the bicycle carries a pyridazine. This pyridazine has an NH2 next to the bicycle attachment and a 2-hydroxyphenyl group on the opposite side.
- The bicycle has no stereo marks in the drawing, so I left it unspecified.
