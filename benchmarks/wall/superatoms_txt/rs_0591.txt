I read the molecule as a 4-methoxy-3-acetoxypyridine-2-carboxamide whose amide nitrogen is attached to C1 of a substituted tetrahydropyran ring.

**Pyridine part:** Ring N, C2 carries the C(=O)NH group, C3 carries OC(=O)CH3, C4 carries OMe, and C5 and C6 are plain CH.

**Pyran ring (six-membered, one O):**
- C1 carries the NH (bold wedge).
- C2 carries CH2C(=O)CH3 (hashed bond).
- C3 carries OAc (bold wedge).
- C4 carries OAc (bold wedge).
- C5 carries CH2OAc (bold wedge).
- The ring oxygen sits between C5 and C1.

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned @/@@ for each of the five ring stereocentres from the wedge/hash geometry and the position of the implicit hydrogen. I did this by hand and did not check it with software. The pyridine ring and the acetyl groups have no stereo marks.
