I read the structure as a cyclic peptide and traced it from the C-terminal glycinamide around the macrocycle.

**Sequence, in the order I traced it:**
1. Glycinamide, then Cys. The Cys sulfur carries a CH2C(=O) group (thioether), which acylates the Tyr nitrogen.
2. Tyr, then N-Me-Ala, then Asn, then Pro.
3. Dap (its side-chain NH is one end of the cyclic bridge), then Val, then 4-hydroxy-Pro (trans, 2S,4R), then Trp.
4. A diamino acid with a two-carbon side chain (Dab). Its side-chain NH is the other end of the bridge.
5. N1-carboxymethyl-Trp, then N-Me-Nle, then N-Me-Nle, then Leu. The Leu carbonyl closes the ring onto the Cys NH.

**Bridge between the Dab and Dap side chains:** the drawing shows Dab–NH–CH2–CH=CH–CH2–NH–Dap. The double bond is drawn cis, so I wrote it as Z.

**Ambiguities in the drawing:**
- The long bond from the top NH down to the Dab chain could hide an extra carbon; I read it as a single bond, giving a two-carbon Dab side chain.
- The wedge/hash on the Val α-carbon is hard to read. I took it as hashed, which gives L-Val; a wedge would make it D.

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned each centre from the drawn wedges and hashes. All residues come out L (S, except Cys, which is R as in L-Cys), and the hydroxyproline comes out 2S,4R.
