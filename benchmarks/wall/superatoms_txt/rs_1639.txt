I read the central ring as a 9-membered dilactone. Going around it, the atoms are: C(=O), ring O, CH(CH3), CH(O-isobutyrate), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), ring O, CH2, and CH(NH). The NH carries a 3-(3,4-difluorobenzoyloxy)-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide group.

Each stereocentre is set from its wedge or hash:
- The CH(CH3) carbon has the methyl on a wedge, giving `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order below.
- The CH(O-isobutyrate) carbon has the ester O on a hash, giving `[C@H]`.
- The CH(CH2Ph) carbon has the benzyl CH2 on a wedge, giving `[C@@H]`.
- The CH(NH) carbon has the N on a hash, giving `[C@@H]`.

I wrote each centre's SMILES chirality tag by working out the viewing direction for the drawn neighbours, but I did not independently re-verify the tags with a toolkit.
