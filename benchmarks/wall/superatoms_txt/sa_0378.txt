The drawing shows an NH group flanked by two groups labelled "TMS", which is the abbreviation for trimethylsilyl, Si(CH3)3. This is hexamethyldisilazane (HMDS).

Expanding each TMS gives a silicon bearing three methyl groups, attached to the central nitrogen, which also carries one hydrogen.

SMILES: C[Si](C)(C)N[Si](C)(C)C
