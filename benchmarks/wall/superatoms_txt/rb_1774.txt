I read the structure in three parts and wrote the SMILES by joining them.

- **Left end:** a 2-fluoro-6-methoxyphenyl group sits on C5 of a 1H-pyrazolo[4,3-d]pyrimidine. C3 of that bicycle carries a para-phenylene, which is bonded to a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Linker:** the other piperazine nitrogen is acylated by an adipoyl chain, C(=O)–(CH2)4–C(=O). That chain ends in an amide to the nitrogen of tert-leucine.
- **Right end:** the tert-leucine carbonyl is bonded to the nitrogen of a 4-hydroxyproline. Its carboxamide nitrogen carries a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzyl group.

For stereochemistry, I took the three wedge/hash centres (tert-leucine α-carbon, proline α-carbon, carbinol carbon) to be the usual VH032 (VHL ligand) configuration. I set the @/@@ labels by checking neighbour order against the known VH032 SMILES, which gives the tert-leucine α-carbon as L (`@@`) and both proline centres as `@` in the order I wrote them.
