This structure is drawn as a fragment of a 2D periodic network, so I have written it as one 12-ring macrocycle with the outward bonds left open as wildcard atoms.

**Reading the drawing**
- The central macrocycle has 12 six-membered rings that alternate around the ring: 6 benzene rings and 6 para-benzoquinone rings.
- Each quinone ring carries two C=O groups on opposite carbons. Its other four carbons each carry an S.
- Each benzene ring has six S substituents. Four of them bridge to the two neighbouring quinone rings, giving fused 1,4-dithiine rings (thianthrene-like links) on each side.
- The remaining two S atoms on each benzene point outward and end in dashed bonds to the neighbouring cells of the network. I wrote each of these as `S*`.

**Building the SMILES**
- The string walks around the macrocycle as B1, Q1, B2, Q2, … B6, Q6, where B is a benzene ring and Q is a quinone ring.
- Ring-closure digits provide the second S bridge between each pair of neighbouring rings. The last quinone closes back onto the first benzene.
- Quinone ring: C(=O)–C(S)=C(S)–C(=O)–C(S)=C(S).
- Benzene ring: fully aromatic, six S substituents.

The string was assembled by hand and not parsed by a toolkit, so I checked it only by tracing atom valences and closure pairs.
