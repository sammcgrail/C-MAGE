I read the drawing as a 9-membered dilactone core carrying a serine-derived amide, a benzyl group, a methyl group and a 2-methylbutanoate ester.

**Pyridine part (left):**
- The ring is N, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6.
- C2 carries the C(=O)NH amide.
- C3 carries OAc (acetoxy).
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are unsubstituted CH.

**Nine-membered dilactone ring**, going around: CH(NH–) – C(=O) – O – CH(CH3) – CH(O-acyl) – CH(CH2Ph) – C(=O) – O – CH2, then back to the first CH.

**Ester side chain:** the OC(=O)CH(CH3)CH2CH3 group is 2-methylbutanoate. Its CH is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

**Stereocentres on the ring:** I assigned each from the wedges and hashes, then matched it to the SMILES neighbour order.
- CH(N): the N is on a hash, giving S.
- CH(CH3): the methyl is on a wedge, giving S.
- CH(O-acyl): the O is on a hash, giving R.
- CH(CH2Ph): the benzyl is on a wedge, giving R.
