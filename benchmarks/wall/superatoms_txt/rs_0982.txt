The drawing shows methyl 4-nitrocinnamate.

- **Ring:** a benzene ring with a nitro group (O2N) at the lower left and a vinyl substituent at the upper right, which places them para to each other.
- **Chain:** the ring carbon is attached to CH=CH, which leads to COOMe, a methyl ester.
- **Double bond geometry:** the ring attaches at the lower left of the double bond and the ester at the upper right, so the two substituents are on opposite sides. That is the trans (E) arrangement.

Writing the E geometry in SMILES: `COC(=O)/C=C/c1ccc(cc1)[N+](=O)[O-]`. The `/C=C/` pattern places the ester and the aryl group on opposite sides, which is E.
