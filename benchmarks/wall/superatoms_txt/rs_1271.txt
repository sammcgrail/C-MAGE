The drawing shows a triarylamine with three aryl groups on the central nitrogen:

1. A biphenyl group: a phenyl ring bearing a para methyl group is attached to a second phenyl ring, and that second ring is bonded to the central N at its para position.
2. A 4-methylphenyl (p-tolyl) group.
3. A 3-(diethylamino)phenyl group. The ring carbon bonded to the central N is meta to the carbon bearing N(Et)2. In the image, the ring's lower-left carbon attaches to the central N and the upper-right carbon attaches to NEt2, with one carbon between them, which confirms the meta relationship.

SMILES, written as the central N linked to the three groups:

N(c1ccc(cc1)-c1ccc(C)cc1)(c1ccc(C)cc1)c1cccc(N(CC)CC)c1

Check of the meta ring: c1cccc(N(CC)CC)c1 starts at the carbon bonded to the central N (position 1), then runs c (2), c (3), c (4), c(NEt2) (5), c1 (6). Position 5 is equivalent to position 3 by symmetry, so the substituents are meta to each other.
