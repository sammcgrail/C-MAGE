Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Pyridine ring:** the ring nitrogen sits at the bottom. The carbon next to it bears the amide carbonyl (C2). C3 carries OH and C4 carries OMe.
- **Amide:** the C2 carbonyl (C=O) is bonded to an NH.
- **Stereocentre:** the NH is attached by a hashed bond to the α-carbon. That carbon carries a COOCH2Ph group (up) and a CH2 (down) that leads to a para-benzyloxyphenyl ring (OCH2Ph).
- **Configuration:** the priorities are N > COOBn > CH2Ar > H. The implicit H points toward the viewer, so the N → COO → CH2 sequence, which looks clockwise in the drawing, is reversed. This gives S, the L-tyrosine configuration, which is written `N[C@@H](Cc1ccc(O)cc1)C(=O)O`.

The SMILES below follows that pattern, with the benzyl groups on the tyrosine oxygen and the ester.
