- **Linker and aryl groups:** the CH2–O leads to a para-disubstituted phenyl ring, whose other position bears the nitrogen of a piperidine.
- **Piperidine:** the 4-position carries an O-aryl group, a para-(trifluoromethoxy)phenyl ring.

**Stereochemistry:** the methyl on the quaternary carbon is drawn with a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer. The other three substituents are the ring oxygen (left), the CH2O (right) and the ring CH2N (down). In the front view they run clockwise (left → right → down), so viewed from the methyl side they run counterclockwise. In the SMILES neighbour order (methyl, ring-closure O, CH2O, CH2N), that corresponds to `@`.
