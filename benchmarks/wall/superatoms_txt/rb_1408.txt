I read this as a PROTAC-type molecule with three parts joined in sequence: an aminopyridazine–phenol ligand, a PEG linker, and a VHL-type hydroxyproline ligand.

**Left end.** A pyridazine carries an NH2 and, on the carbon next to a ring nitrogen, a 2-hydroxyphenyl group. Its remaining ring carbon is bonded to the C4 of a pyrazole.

**Linker.** The pyrazole N1 is bonded to a chain with five ether oxygens: N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O. The last oxygen is bonded to C3 of an isoxazole.

**Isoxazole to VHL ligand.** The isoxazole C5 is bonded to a CH that carries an isopropyl group and a C(=O) attached to the pyrrolidine nitrogen. The pyrrolidine is a 4-hydroxyproline whose C2 carries a C(=O)NH–CH2–aryl amide. The aryl is a para-substituted phenyl bearing a 4-methylthiazol-5-yl group.

**Stereochemistry.**
- The isopropyl-bearing CH has the isopropyl on a hashed bond. Its CIP priorities are C(=O) > isoxazole C5 > isopropyl > H, which gives S.
- Hydroxyproline: I took the hashed OH and wedged carboxamide as the standard VHL (2S,4R) configuration rather than re-deriving each centre from the drawing.
