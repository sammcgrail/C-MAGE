I read the molecule as three parts, going left to right.

- **Cyclooctyne end:** an 8-membered ring with one C≡C and a ring CH bearing an ether O, written `OC4CCCCCC#C4`. This is followed by OCH2C(=O)NH.
- **Linker:** four glycine units, each NH–CH2–C(=O), then NH–CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)–NH. Counting the carbonyls along the chain gives four glycines.
- **Drug end:** an exatecan-type camptothecin core. It has an aromatic ring carrying F and CH3, a saturated six-membered ring bearing the NH-amide carbon, a pyridone-lactone, and a tertiary carbinol with an ethyl group.

For stereochemistry I used the two drawn centres, the amine-bearing CH (hashed bond) and the quaternary C(OH)(Et) (wedge/hash). I took the configurations from the known exatecan SMILES rather than deriving them independently from the wedges. The cyclooctyne CH is drawn with plain bonds, so it has no stereo label.
