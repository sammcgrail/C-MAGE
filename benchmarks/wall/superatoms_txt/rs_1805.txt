The drawing, read from left to right:

- **Left ring:** a para-substituted benzene carrying a methanesulfonate group, O–S(=O)(=O)–CH3. The S has two =O and a methyl drawn as a line going down.
- **Linker:** the ring is joined to a CH=CH double bond, which is drawn trans (E). This leads to a C(=O)–NH amide.
- **Right ring:** the NH is attached to a para-substituted benzene. Its other substituent is CH2–CH(OEt)–COOEt.

The double bond is drawn with the aryl group going up-left to the alkene and the carbonyl carbon going up-right. The pattern is aryl at the lower left, then a vertex up, then a vertex down, then C(=O). That is a trans arrangement.

The carbon bearing OEt is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
