I read the structure in four pieces and joined them into one SMILES.

1. **Pyrazine core:** The ring carries a carboxamide (C(N)=O) on one carbon. The adjacent carbon bears an NH link to the phenyl ring, and the carbon across the ring from the carboxamide bears the piperidine nitrogen. One ring CH sits between that carbon and the next ring nitrogen.
2. **Piperidine on the pyrazine:** Its 3-position carries a 3-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl group. The hashed bond puts the imidazolidinone nitrogen behind the plane, so the hydrogen faces the viewer. With priorities N > ring CH2 next to N > ring CH2 > H, I get S. In the SMILES this is written as `[C@@H]`.
3. **Phenyl and spiro unit:** A para-substituted benzene links the NH to the nitrogen of a 2,6-diazaspiro[3.5]nonane. Its azetidine nitrogen carries a CH2 group.
4. **Isoindoline-1,3-dione:** The CH2 attaches to the benzo ring of a phthalimide. The phthalimide nitrogen is bonded to the 3-position of a 2,6-dioxopiperidine. That carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.

I checked the ring closures and ring sizes (the six-membered piperidine and four-membered azetidine at the spiro carbon) against the drawing.
