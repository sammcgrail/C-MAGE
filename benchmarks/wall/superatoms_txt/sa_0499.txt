Reading the drawing:

- The central carbon has three drawn bonds and an implicit hydrogen. It bears CO2H on a wedge pointing up, an OTBDPS group at the lower left, and a methyl group at the lower right.
- TBDPS is the tert-butyldiphenylsilyl group, Si(Ph)2C(CH3)3, so the oxygen is O–Si(C6H5)2C(CH3)3.
- This is therefore lactic acid with its hydroxyl protected as the TBDPS ether.

For the stereochemistry, the CO2H is on a wedge toward the viewer, so the implicit hydrogen points away. The CIP priorities are O > CO2H > CH3 > H. Going from O (lower left) to CO2H (up) to CH3 (lower right) is clockwise with H at the back, so the centre is R.

In SMILES, writing the neighbours in the order CH3, H, O, CO2H, `@@` corresponds to R.

SMILES: C[C@@H](O[Si](c1ccccc1)(c1ccccc1)C(C)(C)C)C(=O)O
