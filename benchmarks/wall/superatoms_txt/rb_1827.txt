This is an EZH2-degrader PROTAC. It has an EZH2 ligand (a benzamide with a pyridone), a PEG linker, and a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand.

**Left fragment (EZH2 ligand)**
- The central benzene ring carries three substituents: a 4-(morpholinomethyl)phenyl group, a C(=O)NHCH2-pyridone group, and an N(ethyl)(piperidin-4-yl) group.
- A methyl group sits between the amide and the amino group.
- The pyridone is 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl, attached through a CH2 to the amide NH.
- Ring order, starting at the carbon bearing the aryl group: C(aryl), CH, C(N), C(CH3), C(C=O), CH, back to C(aryl).
- SMILES for this ring: c1(-c2ccc(CN3CCOCC3)cc2)cc(N(CC)C2CCN(...)CC2)c(C)c(C(=O)NCc2c(C)cc(C)[nH]c2=O)c1

**Linker**
- The piperidine N is acylated: C(=O)CCOCCOCCOCCOCCC(=O)NCCO-Ar.
- Counting oxygens in the drawing from the carbonyl: CH2CH2 then O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, then CH2CH2C(=O). That gives four ether oxygens.
- The chain is therefore C(=O)CCOCCOCCOCCOCCC(=O)N.

**Right fragment (cereblon ligand)**
- The amide N is attached to CH2CH2-O-aryl.
- The aryl is the isoindoline-1,3-dione bearing the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on its N.
- The O-aryl sits at the 4-position of the isoindoline.
- SMILES for this fragment: NCCOc1cccc2c1C(=O)N(C1CCC(=O)NC1=O)C2=O
