The drawing shows a thiazole ring with two wavy-line attachment points.

Reading the ring: S is at the bottom left, C2 at the left, N at the top, C4 at the right, and C5 at the bottom right, which is bonded to S. The double bonds are C2=N and C4=C5. The left wavy line attaches at C2 and the right wavy line attaches at C4. C5 carries only a hydrogen.

Writing the SMILES from C2: *c1nc(*)cs1. Here c1 is C2 (bonded to the left attachment), n is N3, c(*) is C4 (bonded to the right attachment), c is C5, and s1 is S1 closing the ring back to C2.
