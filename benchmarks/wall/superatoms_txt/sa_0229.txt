I read this as the pentapeptide Tyr‑Gly‑Gly‑Phe‑Leu (leu‑enkephalin), with the Ph and iBu abbreviations expanded.

- **Tyrosine end:** a free NH2 on a CH bearing a CH2 and a 4‑hydroxyphenyl ring (para OH). The wedged NH2 gives the S (L) configuration.
- **Linker:** two glycines, drawn as NH–CH2–C(=O) units joined by amide bonds.
- **Phenylalanine:** the CH2–Ph side chain is on a hashed bond, and the CIP analysis gives S (L).
- **Leucine end:** the iBu (CH2CH(CH3)2) side chain is on a hashed bond, the carbon carries a free CO2H, and the CIP analysis gives S (L).
