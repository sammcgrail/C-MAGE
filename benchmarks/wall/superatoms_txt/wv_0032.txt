The drawing is a fragment with a quaternary carbon on the left. This carbon has two wavy bonds, which are attachment points, plus a plain line going to the lower left, which I read as a methyl group. It also bonds to the CH2CH2 chain that leads to the piperazine.

Going through the pieces:

- The quaternary carbon carries two wavy bonds (two * atoms), one methyl, and the chain.
- The chain runs from the quaternary carbon to a CH2, then another CH2, then the piperazine N.
- The piperazine has a second N bearing a methyl, which is the line extending to the right at the top.

The central carbon is therefore C(*)(*)(C)CC–N. The line at the lower left ends at a free vertex, so it is a methyl.

SMILES: *C(*)(C)CCN1CCN(C)CC1
