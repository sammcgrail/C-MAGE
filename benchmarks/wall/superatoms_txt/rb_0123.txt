The drawing shows a diaza-18-crown-6 macrocycle (a 1,10-diaza ring with four ring oxygens) with a picolinic acid arm on each nitrogen.

**Left arm.** A pyridine ring carries a COOH at C2 and a CH2 linked to the left nitrogen at C6. At C4 there is an O–CH2CH2–C6H4–NCS group, with the NCS para to the ethyl chain.

**Right arm.** A pyridine ring carries a COOH at C2 and a CH2 linked to the right nitrogen at C6.

**Macrocycle.** Starting at the left N, the path is: N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–N (top half), then N–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–N (bottom half). Each half has two oxygens separated by an ethylene, and each oxygen is separated from a nitrogen by an ethylene. This gives a 1,10-diaza-18-crown-6 ring.
