The drawing shows bis(4-nitrophenyl) carbonate. A central carbonyl carbon (C=O) is bonded to two ester-type oxygens. Each of those oxygens is attached to a benzene ring that carries a nitro group para to the oxygen.

Checking the para relationship on the left ring: the O-bearing carbon is at (595, 717) and the nitro-bearing carbon is at (307, 649). The ring runs (407, 540), (553, 575), (595, 717), (492, 825), (347, 791), (307, 649). The O-bearing carbon is therefore the third vertex and the nitro carbon is the sixth, so they are para. The right ring has the same arrangement.
