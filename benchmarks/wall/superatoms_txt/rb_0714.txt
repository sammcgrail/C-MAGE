I read the molecule in four pieces and joined them in SMILES.

- **Left end:** a 4-phenylthiazol-2-yl group on an amide NH. That amide carbonyl is attached to the stereocentre.
- **Stereocentre:** it carries the amide C(=O), a CH2–O group, and an NH drawn with a hashed bond. The NH acylates a 1-(methylsulfonyl)pyrrole-3-carboxylic acid.
- **Linker:** CH2–O–CH2 leads to a para-disubstituted benzene. Its second CH2 is on an NH that forms an amide with a CH2CH2 chain, which ends on a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Right end:** the other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to position 5 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. The isoindoline nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, which has no stereo drawn.

For the stereocentre, the priorities are N > C(=O)N > CH2O > H. The hashed NH points away from the viewer, so H points toward the viewer. Reading N → C(=O) → CH2 on the page gives clockwise, and reversing for the H position gives S. In the SMILES order I wrote (C(=O), H, N, CH2), S corresponds to `@@`.
