I read the drawing as an eight-carbon-plus-oxygen dilactone ring (a nine-membered ring, antimycin-like) carrying three substituents, with a 3-acyloxy-4-methoxypyridine-2-carboxamide on the ring nitrogen.

**Substituents**
- The pyridine has OMe at C4 and an ester oxygen at C3. That ester is O–C(=O)–CH(Ph)–O–C(=O)CH3, an O-acetyl mandelate.
- The carboxamide at C2 is C(=O)–NH, attached to the ring.

**Ring, going around from the NH carbon**
- CH(NH) → CH2 → O → C(=O) → CH(CH2Ph) → CH(O–C(=O)CH(CH3)2) → CH(CH3) → O → C(=O) → back to CH(NH).

**Stereochemistry from the wedges and hashes**
- CH(NH), hashed N: S.
- CH(CH3), bold methyl: S.
- CH(O-isobutyrate), hashed O: R.
- CH(CH2Ph), bold benzyl: R.
