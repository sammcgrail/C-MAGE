I read the molecule as a stapled peptide: Tyr–Lys–(α-methyl alkenyl residue)–Thr–Gln–Ser–(α-methyl alkenyl residue), closed into a macrocycle through the hydrocarbon staple, with a C-terminal amide.

**Linear part**
- Tyr: a 4-hydroxyphenyl-CH2 side chain with a free NH2. The wedged N gives S (L).
- Lys: a four-CH2 side chain ending in NH2. The wedged side chain gives S (L).

**Staple**
- The first quaternary carbon carries a methyl on a hashed bond, then three CH2 groups, then the alkene.
- The second quaternary carbon carries a methyl on a hashed bond, then three CH2 groups back to the alkene.
- Comparing the two alkene substituents against the double bond, they lie on opposite sides, so the alkene is E (trans).
- Both quaternary centres work out as S from the drawn hashes.

**Ring residues**

- Gln: a CH2CH2C(=O)NH2 side chain. The hashed bond gives S (L).
