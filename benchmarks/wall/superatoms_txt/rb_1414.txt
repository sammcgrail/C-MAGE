Reading the structure region by region:

**Left fragment:** a pyridine ring with N, with Cl at both positions adjacent to the C4 carbon. C4 carries the NH, which is bonded to a C(=O) attached to a benzene ring. This gives the 3,5-dichloropyridin-4-yl amide: c1(Cl)cncc(Cl)c1NC(=O)...

**Benzene ring:** C1 bears the C=O. C3 bears the O-pentyl chain and C4 bears OCHF2, as in roflumilast-type compounds.

**Chain:** from the oxygen, the vertices run O–CH2–CH2–CH2–CH2–CH2–N. In the drawing the chain goes from O to a vertex (~530,340), then 590,315, 650,340, 710,315, 765,340, and then N at 815,318. That is 5 carbons: 530, 590, 650, 710, 765. So the chain is OCCCCCN, a pentyl linker.

**Piperidine:** N-linked, with C4 attached to the isoindolinone.

**Isoindolinone:** the aromatic ring carries the piperidinyl at C4 and F at C6. The C=O is at C1 and CH2 at C3. The ring N bears the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.

The aromatic ring atoms are: C3a (attached to CH2), C4 (piperidinyl), C5 (H), C6 (F), C7 (H), and C7a (attached to C=O).
