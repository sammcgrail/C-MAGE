I read the drawing as a 4-chloro-2-oxo-benzoxazole (benzoxazolone) ring carrying a CH2–CH(NH2)–CO2CH3 side chain.

- **Ring:** The five-membered ring has O at the top, a C=O carbon, and an NH at the bottom. The fused benzene ring carries Cl on the carbon next to the NH-bearing ring carbon, which makes it position 4. The side chain sits on position 6, meta to the Cl.
- **Side chain:** A CH2 links the ring to the stereocentre. That carbon bears CO2CH3 and NH2, and the NH2 is drawn with a hashed bond.
- **Stereochemistry:** The priorities are NH2 > CO2Me > CH2 > H. With NH2 pointing back, the implicit H points toward the viewer. Going NH2 → CO2Me → CH2 appears counterclockwise from the front. Because H faces the viewer, this reverses to clockwise, so the centre is R. I checked that `COC(=O)[C@H](N)Cc1...` encodes R with this neighbour order.
