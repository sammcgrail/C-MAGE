I read the structure as a thalidomide-type (pomalidomide) ligand joined by a PEG linker to a sulfamide on a piperidine, which carries a bromopyrimidine kinase-inhibitor fragment.

**Left side (glutarimide and isoindoline-1,3-dione).** The glutarimide is C1(=O)NC(=O)C(CC1)N…, with its CH attached to the isoindolinone nitrogen. The isoindoline-1,3-dione carries an NH substituent at the 4-position of the benzene ring. This CH is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so I give it no stereo.

**Linker.** From the aryl NH the chain runs NH–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–O–CH2CH2–NH–S(=O)(=O)–N(piperidine). In the image, the aryl NH is followed by CH2CH2, then O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, CH2CH2, O, and finally CH2CH2 to the NH. That is 4 oxygens, with 5 ethylene units counting both ends.

**Right side.** The piperidine nitrogen bears the sulfonyl group, and the 4-position of the piperidine carries NH attached to C2 of the pyrimidine. The pyrimidine has Br at C5 and an NH-aryl at C4. The aryl ring is a benzamide with C(=O)NH2 and F ortho to the amide, and the NH sits on the other ortho position.
