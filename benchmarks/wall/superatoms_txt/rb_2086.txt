I read this as a cyclosporin A-type cyclic undecapeptide whose MeBmt side chain has been replaced by a CH(CH3)CH2CH2NH–C(=O)CH2CH2NH– linker. The linker ends at C2 of a quinazoline bearing a KRAS G12C-type warhead. The stereocentres are assigned from the standard cyclosporin pattern (L residues, D-Ala8), and I checked that pattern against the wedges drawn for Ala7 and Ala8 and the MeBmt hydroxyl and methyl. I did not verify every other wedge individually.

**Ring, traced from the Abu carbonyl:**
1. Abu (ethyl side chain, NH).
2. Sar: N(CH3)CH2C(=O).
3. N-Me-Leu4.
4. Val5 (NH).
5. N-Me-Leu6.
6. L-Ala7 (NH).
7. D-Ala8 (NH).
8. N-Me-Leu9.
9. N-Me-Leu10.
10. N-Me-Val11.
11. MeBmt-derived residue: N-methyl alpha carbon, CH(OH), then CH(CH3)CH2CH2NH, closing back to the Abu nitrogen.

**Warhead and linker:**
- The linker NH–C(=O)–CH2CH2–NH attaches to C2 of the quinazoline.
- The quinazoline carries F at C8, a 2-fluoro-6-hydroxyphenyl at C7 and Cl at C6.
- C4 carries a piperazine whose far nitrogen is acylated with acryloyl (C(=O)C=C).

**SMILES notation:** I wrote the side chain before the carbonyl in each residue, so L residues are `[C@@H]` and the D-Ala is `[C@H]`.
