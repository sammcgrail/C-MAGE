I read the structure as a 2-benzoxazolone core (a fused benzene and oxazolone ring) with three substituents:

- **Ring system:** the oxazolone ring has O at the top, a C=O carbon, and an NH at the bottom. It is fused to the benzene ring.
- **Bromine:** Br sits on the benzene carbon adjacent to the ring-fusion carbon bearing the NH (position 4).
- **Side chain:** at position 6, para to the fusion carbon bearing N, a CH2 leads to a CH that carries NH2 and CO2Me (a methyl ester).
- **Stereochemistry:** the NH2 is on a hashed bond, so the H is toward the viewer. The priorities are N > CO2Me > CH2. Seen from the front, N → CO2Me → CH2 runs counterclockwise. Because H points toward the viewer, the true sense is clockwise, which gives the R configuration.

For the SMILES `COC(=O)[C@H](N)Cc1cc(Br)c2[nH]c(=O)oc2c1`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is CO2Me, H, N, CH2. The `@` label for this order corresponds to R, which matches the drawing.
