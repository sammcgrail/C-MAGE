The drawing shows an N-(benzothiazol-2-yl)acetamide whose benzothiazole carries a pyridyloxy group at position 4. The pyridine carries a cyclohexenyl ring bearing CF3.

**Acetamide and benzothiazole.** The acetyl group is CC(=O)NH–, attached to C2 of the benzothiazole. C2 is double-bonded to N3 and single-bonded to S1. N3 connects to C3a, which is fused to the benzene ring. C4, next to C3a, bears the O. The ring then runs C5, C6, C7, and C7a, which bonds to S.

**Pyridine.** The O attaches at pyridine C2, which is adjacent to the ring N. Moving around the ring from C2: C3 (CH), C4 (bearing the cyclohexenyl), C5, C6, then N.

**Cyclohexene.** The ring carbon attached to the pyridine (C1) is double-bonded to a CH at the lower left. From there the ring continues through CH2, then the CH(CF3), then CH2, and back to C1. So the CF3 sits on C4 of the cyclohex-1-enyl ring, and this carbon has no stereo marking.
