I read the molecule as three parts joined by amide linkers.

1. **Isoquinoline head.** The ring carries 8-NH2 and 7-F. At C6 there is a 5-amino-4-methylpyridin-3-yl group. C3 bears an amide NH.
2. **Linker.** The C3 NH is acylated by a β-alanine unit, NC(=O)CCN. That nitrogen is acylated in turn by a 7-aminoheptanoyl chain, C(=O)CCCCCCN, which I counted as six CH2 groups. The chain length is the least certain part of the reading, because the zigzag spacing in the drawing is slightly ambiguous.
3. **Pomalidomide-type tail.** The chain ends in an NH attached to position 4 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. The imide nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl ring.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so the piperidine stereocentre is left unspecified.
