I read the structure as a cyclic peptide and wrote it out residue by residue.

**Backbone, starting at the C-terminal glycinamide:**
- Gly-NH2, then Cys, whose side-chain sulfur is alkylated by an acetyl group (the thioether) that acylates the Tyr nitrogen and closes the macrocycle.
- Tyr, then N-Me-Ala, Asn, Pro, and a diaminopropionic acid (Dap) residue whose side-chain NH carries the ethoxy-squaramide group (3-ethoxy-cyclobutenedione).
- Leu, trans-4-hydroxy-Pro, and Trp with a free indole NH.
- A diaminobutyric acid (Dab) residue whose side-chain NH also carries an ethoxy-squaramide. This is the long line crossing the Tyr region in the drawing: Cα, two CH2 groups, then NH.
- Trp with the indole nitrogen alkylated as N–CH2–CO2Et, and N-Me-norleucine twice.
- Leu, whose carbonyl bonds back to the Cys nitrogen to close the ring.

**Ring closure:** I used `%10` on the Cys nitrogen and on the final Leu carbonyl carbon.

**Stereochemistry:** I did not read each wedge and hash individually. I assigned L-configuration to every stereocentre and trans-4-hydroxy to the Hyp, so some centres may not match the drawing.
