This is a benzoxazino-rifamycin: a rifamycin ansa macrocycle fused to a phenoxazine, carrying a 2-morpholinoethoxy group on the benzo ring.

I wrote the SMILES in these steps:

1. **Chromophore:** The naphthoquinone core carries a fused furanone (quaternary carbon with a methyl, C=O and ring O). On the other side, the amide N is attached to the quinone carbon next to the C=O. That quinone carbon is fused to a phenoxazine, with N= and O bridging to the benzo ring. The benzo ring has an OH next to the ring N and an OCH2CH2-morpholine on the other side.
2. **Ansa chain:** Starting from the furanone quaternary carbon, I traced the chain as O–CH=CH–CH(OMe)–CH(CH3)–CH(OAc)–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–CH=CH–CH=C(CH3)–C(=O)–NH.
3. **Stereochemistry:** I did not derive each centre from the wedges and hashes. I took the stereodescriptors from the natural rifamycin pattern (as I remember it from the rifampicin SMILES), which I assumed this compound shares. The ring-closure ordering at the quaternary carbon follows that same template. The chain double bonds use the E,E and Z geometry of rifamycin.
4. **Aromaticity:** I wrote the benzene rings in lowercase and the quinone C=O as a non-aromatic carbon, to keep the SMILES valid.
