- **Quinoline:** it carries a 4-isopropylthiazol-2-yl group at C2, a methoxy at C7 and a methyl at C8. The C4 oxygen links to the cyclopentane.
- **Cyclopentane:** one carbon bears the quinolinyl ether oxygen. Two adjacent carbons carry the two amide carbonyls. One of these is an N-methyl amide, the other an NH amide.

- **Cyclopropane:** the quaternary carbon bears the NH amide to the cyclopentane and a C(=O)NH–SO2–cyclopropyl group.
