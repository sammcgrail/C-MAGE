Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Top left:** a benzene ring bearing SO2NH2 at the ortho position. It is joined to a second para-substituted benzene ring, forming a biphenyl.
- **Amide:** the second ring carries a C(=O)NH group, and the NH attaches to a third benzene ring.
- **Ether:** that third ring carries an ortho oxygen (O–Ar), so the NH and the O are on adjacent carbons.
- **Naphthalene:** the oxygen is attached to C3 of a naphthalene ring, and the adjacent carbon (C2) carries CO2Me. The ester and the ether are therefore on neighbouring carbons of the naphthalene.
