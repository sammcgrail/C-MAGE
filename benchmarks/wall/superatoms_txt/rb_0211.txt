The structure is a rifamycin-type ansa macrocycle fused to a phenoxazine, with a 2-(piperidin-1-yl)ethoxy group on the benzo ring.

**Core and substituents**
- The naphthoquinone-type core carries a C=O, an aromatic OH, an aromatic methyl and the furanone ring.
- The furanone ring contains the quaternary carbon, which bears a methyl, a ketone and an enol-ether oxygen.
- The phenoxazine is closed through a C=N and a ring oxygen.
- The benzo ring bears an OCH2CH2–piperidine group para to the carbon attached to the ring nitrogen.

**Ansa chain**
- Starting from the NH–C(=O): C(CH3)=CH (Z), CH=CH (E), then the stereocentres.
- The stereocentres are CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OH), CH(CH3), CH(OAc), CH(CH3) and CH(OMe).
- The chain ends with CH=CH (E) and O attached to the quaternary carbon.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned each centre from the wedges and hashes in the drawing. I checked the CIP labels against the known rifampicin configuration (7S, 11S, 12R, 13S, 14R, 15R, 16R, 17S, 18S) and they agree. At the OH carbon whose bond is hard to read, I took the hashed bond that gives the 15R configuration.
