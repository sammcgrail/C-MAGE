I read the structure in four parts and wrote it as one SMILES.

- **Pyrazine core:** the ring carries a primary carboxamide (C(N)=O) on one carbon and an anilino NH on the adjacent carbon. A piperidin-1-yl group sits on the carbon across the ring, and the remaining ring carbon is CH.
- **Piperidine on the pyrazine:** its 3-position carries a 3-methyl-2-oxoimidazolidin-1-yl group on a wedge bond. Ranking the substituents (imidazolidinone N > ring CH2 next to N > the other ring CH2 > H) and viewing with H pointing away gives a clockwise sequence, so the centre is R. In the SMILES this is `[C@H]` for the neighbour order written.
- **Linker:** the anilino NH is attached to a para-phenylene that carries a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is linked through CH2 to the 4-position of a piperidine.
- **Right-hand end:** that piperidine's nitrogen is bonded to C5 of an isoindoline-1,3-dione. The isoindoline nitrogen carries a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group, drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo.
