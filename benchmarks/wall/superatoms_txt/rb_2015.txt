I read the molecule as follows, region by region.

- **Quinazoline core:** the benzo ring carries two methoxy groups (C6 and C7) and the chain at C8. The pyrimidine ring has a methyl at C2 and the NH substituent at C4.
- **Chain:** I counted seven CH2 groups between C8 of the quinazoline and the piperidine nitrogen. The zigzag was hard to count exactly, so this is my best reading.
- **Piperidine:** it is N-alkylated by the chain and attached through its C4 to the isoindolinone.
- **Isoindolinone:** the piperidinyl group sits at C5 and fluorine at the adjacent C6. The lactam nitrogen carries the glutarimide-3-yl group (piperidine-2,6-dione). The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
- **Amine side:** C4 of the quinazoline bears NH–CH(CH3)–aryl. The methyl is on a wedge. The aryl ring is a benzene with a CF2–CH2OH group at the meta position.
- **Stereo assignment:** with the methyl wedged toward the viewer and H pointing away, the methyl → N → aryl sequence runs clockwise. Written as N[C@H](C)Ar, this corresponds to that arrangement.
