I read this as ulipristal acetate: a 19-norpregna-4,9-diene-3,20-dione steroid. The stereochemistry below is the one I recall for ulipristal acetate (the PubChem form), which I did not re-derive centre by centre. I only checked that it agrees with the drawn wedges and hashes.

- **Ring A/B:** the enone has C=O at C3 with the C4=C5 double bond, plus a second C=C between C9 and C10, which is the 4,9-diene.
- **Ring C:** C11 carries a 4-(dimethylamino)phenyl group on a bold wedge (11β).
- **C13:** a bold-wedge methyl, the C18 methyl.
- **Ring junction hydrogens:** the H at C14 is hashed and the H at C8 is bold.
- **Ring D, C17:** an acetyl group (C(=O)CH3) on a bold wedge, and an OAc on a plain bond.
