The drawing shows a para-disubstituted benzene ring.

- One substituent is a C(=O)NH-iPr group, an amide with an isopropyl group on nitrogen.
- The other substituent is CH2-NH-NH-Me, a methylhydrazine attached through a benzylic CH2.

Expanding the abbreviations (iPr to isopropyl, Me to methyl) gives CC(C)NC(=O)c1ccc(CNNC)cc1. This is the structure of procarbazine.
