This is a rifamycin-type ansa macrolactam whose naphthoquinone core is fused to a phenoxazine. The benzo ring of the phenoxazine carries a 2-(azepan-1-yl)ethoxy group.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- **Ansa chain:** the amide NH–C(=O)–C(CH3)=CH–CH=CH diene is followed by the methyl-, hydroxyl-, OAc- and OMe-bearing stereocentres, and then the enol ether O–CH=CH. The chain closes onto the quaternary ketal carbon.
- **Furanone ring:** the quaternary carbon carries a methyl, the enol-ether oxygen, the ring oxygen and the lactone C=O.
- **Aromatic core:** a methyl-substituted, hydroxylated benzene ring is fused to the quinone-type ring. That ring carries the NH, the C=O and the phenoxazine C–O and C=N carbons.
- **Phenoxazine benzo ring:** it bears the OCH2CH2–azepane substituent.

I did not derive the stereochemistry of the chain independently from the wedges. I copied it from the known rifamycin/rifampicin stereo-SMILES, which I recalled from memory, and I re-derived the quaternary-carbon chirality symbol for my atom ordering. The drawing matches that rifamycin series.
