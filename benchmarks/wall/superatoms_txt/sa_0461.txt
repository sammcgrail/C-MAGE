I read the drawing as a 4-amino-imidazo[4,5-c]quinoline core with three substituents:

- **Amine:** the NH2 sits on the pyridine-like ring carbon next to the ring nitrogen.
- **C2 substituent:** an n-butyl group on the imidazole carbon (CCCC).
- **N1 substituent:** an N–O bond to a chain of four CH2 groups ending in NH–C(=O)–.

The acyl chain, counted vertex by vertex, is the carbonyl carbon, then 7 chain carbons, then the nDec label (10 carbons). That makes an 18-carbon acyl group (octadecanamide). No wedges, hashes or double-bond geometry are drawn, so there is no stereochemistry.
