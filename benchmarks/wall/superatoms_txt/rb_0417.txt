I read the structure as a macrocyclic bis-benzimidazole carrying a phosphorylated pyrrolidine.

- **Pyrazole amides:** each is a 1-ethyl-3-methylpyrazole-5-carboxamide, attached through its amide NH to C2 of a benzimidazole.
- **Benzimidazoles:** each has a primary carboxamide (CONH2) at C5 and an ether oxygen at C7.
- **Ring bridge:** the two benzimidazole N1 atoms are joined by an N–CH2–CH=CH–CH2–N linker. The two CH2 groups sit on opposite sides of the double bond in the drawing, so I took it as trans (E).
- **Central linker:** each C7 oxygen is attached to a CH2, and the two CH2 groups meet at one CH. That CH carries the pyrrolidine nitrogen and is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo label.
- **Pyrrolidine:** the C3 carbon bears an O–P(=O)(OH)2 group on a wedge. Using CIP priorities O > C2 > C4 > H, with H pointing away, the drawing gives S, which matches the (S) label. The `[C@H]` in the SMILES encodes that same S configuration.
