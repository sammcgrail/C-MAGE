This is a symmetric dimer: two identical bicyclic pyrrolidine-thiazepinone cores, each carrying an N-methyl-L-alanyl amide, joined through two aminoindanol units by a but-2-yne-1,4-diyl diether linker.

**Left half, fragment by fragment**
- N-Methylalanine: the α-carbon has N to the left, C=O to the right and a hashed methyl. This works out as S (L-Ala), written `CN[C@@H](C)C(=O)N…`.
- Thiazepinone ring: S, CH2, CH2, CH(NH-acyl), C(=O), N and the ring-fusion CH (hashed H) form the seven-membered ring. The CH(NH) carbon is S and the ring-fusion CH next to S is S.
- Pyrrolidine ring: the N, the CH bearing the amide (wedged), a CMe2, a CH2 and the ring-fusion CH. The amide-bearing carbon is S.
- Indane: C1 bears the NH (hashed) and C2 bears the O (hashed). This works out as C1 S and C2 R.
- Linker: the indane O–CH2–C≡C–CH2–O connects to the second indane.

**Right half**
The right half is the same unit rotated by 180°, so it has the same absolute configuration. I rewrote its chirality tags for the reversed atom order by counting permutations of the neighbour lists.
