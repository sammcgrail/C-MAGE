I read the molecule as a 4-substituted benzoic acid joined through an N-ethyl linker to a γ-lactam (pyrrolidin-2-one), which carries an (E)-alkenyl side chain.

**Structure read from the drawing**
- The aryl ring is a para-disubstituted benzene with CO2H at one end.
- At the other end of the ring, a CH2CH2 chain leads to the lactam nitrogen.
- The lactam is a five-membered ring made of N, C=O, two CH2 groups and one CH. This CH sits next to N and is the stereocentre drawn with a wedge bond.
- From that CH, the side chain is CH=CH (drawn trans), then CH(OH) with a hashed OH bond, then CH2–O–CH2–CH=CH2 (an allyl ether).

**Stereochemistry**
- Lactam C5 (wedged vinyl, H pointing back): the priorities are N > vinyl > ring CH2 > H. Going N → vinyl → CH2 runs clockwise in the drawing, so the centre is R. In the SMILES this is `[C@@H]`.
- Carbinol carbon (hashed OH, H pointing toward the viewer): the priorities are OH > CH2O > vinyl > H. In the drawing OH → CH2O → vinyl runs counterclockwise, and reversing for the H facing the viewer gives R. In the SMILES this is `[C@@H]`.
- The double bond is trans, written `/C=C/`.
