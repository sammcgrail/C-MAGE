This is an EZH2-degrader PROTAC. It combines an EZH2 inhibitor (a biphenyl benzamide with a dimethylpyridone) and a thalidomide-type cereblon ligand, joined through a piperazine linker.

**Pyridone fragment:** The 4,6-dimethyl-2-oxo-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl methyl group is written Cc1cc(C)c(CNC(=O)...)c(=O)[nH]1.

**Central benzamide ring:**
- The amide carbon is attached at C1.
- C2 carries a methyl.
- C3 carries N(ethyl)(tetrahydropyran-4-yl).
- C5 carries the aryl group.
- C4 and C6 are plain CH.
- SMILES: C(=O)c1c(C)c(N(CC)C2CCOCC2)cc(c1)-c1ccc(CN2CCN(CC(=O)NCCOc3cccc4c3C(=O)N(C3CCC(=O)NC3=O)C4=O)CC2)cc1

**Right-hand side:**
- The benzyl CH2 is attached to a piperazine nitrogen.
- The other piperazine nitrogen carries CH2C(=O)NH–CH2CH2–O–.
- The O is attached to the aromatic ring of the phthalimide at position 4.
- The phthalimide nitrogen bears the 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group.
- The glutarimide stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
