- **Thalidomide end (left):** a 2,6-dioxopiperidin-3-yl group on the nitrogen of a phthalimide. The 4-position of the isoindoline-1,3-dione carries an NH. The glutarimide carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo.
