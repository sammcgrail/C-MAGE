- **Amino acid part:** The alpha carbon bears COOH (up), the amide NH on a hashed bond (left), and CH2 leading to a para-substituted benzene ring (lower right). That ring carries OCH2Ph, which is a benzyl ether.
- **Stereochemistry:** The NH is on a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > COOH > CH2 > H. Going N (left), COOH (up), CH2 (lower right) is clockwise as drawn. Reversing for the H toward the viewer gives counterclockwise, which is S. This matches the L-tyrosine configuration, written as N[C@@H](CH2Ar)C(=O)O.
