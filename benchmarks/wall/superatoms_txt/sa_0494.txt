I read the drawing as 6-(tert-butyldimethylsilyloxy)hexanal.

- The left end is labelled OHC, which is an aldehyde carbon.
- Between it and the OTBS label there are five zigzag vertices, each a CH2 carbon. That gives six chain carbons in total, counting the CHO carbon.
- The right end is O–TBS, which expands to O–Si(CH3)2–C(CH3)3.
- All bonds are plain lines, so no stereochemistry is included.

SMILES: O=CCCCCCO[Si](C)(C)C(C)(C)C
