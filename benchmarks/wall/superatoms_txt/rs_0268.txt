The structure is a quinazoline core with three substituents:

- **Position 2:** an indol-1-yl group. The indole carries a bromine at C5 and a 4-oxobutanoic acid chain, C(=O)CH2CH2CO2H, at C3.
- **Position 4:** an NH group attached to the alpha carbon of phenylalanine, with CO2H and CH2Ph on that carbon.
- **Position 6:** a furan-2-yl group.

For the stereocentre, the hashed bond puts the N behind the plane. The CO2H is up and to the right, the CH2Ph is down, and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > CO2H > CH2Ph > H. N to CO2H to CH2Ph runs clockwise as drawn, and reversing for the H at the front gives S. This matches the L-phenylalanine pattern `N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)O`, which I reused in the SMILES.
