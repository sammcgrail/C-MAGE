The drawing is a cis-stilbene, which matches combretastatin A-4.

- **Left ring:** The ring carbon bonded to the alkene is position 1. The OH sits two ring bonds away (position 3, meta), and a MeO sits on the next carbon (position 4). This gives a 3-hydroxy-4-methoxyphenyl group.
- **Right ring:** Three OMe groups sit on adjacent carbons, at positions 3, 4 and 5 relative to the alkene attachment. This gives a 3,4,5-trimethoxyphenyl group.
- **Alkene:** Both aryl groups hang down from the double bond, on the same side. That makes it the cis (Z) isomer.
