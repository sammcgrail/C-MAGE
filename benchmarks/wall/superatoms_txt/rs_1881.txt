The drawing shows a pyridine ring joined through an amide to a diaryl ether.

**Pyridine ring.** The ring nitrogen is at the bottom left. Going around the ring from the nitrogen:
- C2 (next to N) carries the C(=O)NH amide.
- C3 carries OH.
- C4 carries OMe.
- C5 and C6 are plain CH carbons.

**Amide.** The carbonyl carbon is bonded to C2 of the pyridine and to the NH.

**Aryl side.** The NH is attached to a benzene ring. The ortho position of that ring carries an O link to a second benzene ring, which has Cl at the meta position relative to the oxygen.

**Checking the chlorophenyl position.** In the drawing, the ring carbon attached to O is at the lower left. Going around from there, the next carbon (upper left) is a plain CH. The carbon after that, at the top, is also a plain CH. The Cl sits on the upper right carbon. That carbon is the third atom counting the O-bearing carbon as the first, so it is meta, giving 3-chlorophenoxy.
