The drawing shows a naphthalene core. A nitrogen bearing two ethyl groups (H3C-CH2 on the left, CH2-CH3 on the right) is attached at position 1, and a methyl group is at position 2, on the adjacent carbon.

Reading the ring: the carbon bearing N is the upper-right carbon of the left ring's fusion, so it is C1, next to the ring-fusion carbon. The methyl-bearing carbon is C2. The remaining ring carbons, C3 and C4, run down to the lower fusion carbon.

This gives N,N-diethyl-2-methylnaphthalen-1-amine.

SMILES: CCN(CC)c1c(C)ccc2ccccc12
