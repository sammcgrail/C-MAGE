The left ring is a pyridine. Its N sits at the bottom left, C2 carries the carboxamide, C3 carries OH, and C4 carries OMe.

The linker is C(=O)NH followed by a chain of CH2 groups, then an ester. Counting from the NH: the zigzag runs N, then CH2 (at about x=290), CH2 (345), CH2 (405), CH2 (460), and then C(=O) at about x=520. That gives four CH2 groups and then the carbonyl, so the acid part is a pentanoate (5-aminovalerate).

The ester oxygen connects to the 2-bromopyridin-3-yl ring: the carbon bearing O is C3, the adjacent carbon bearing Br is C2, and that carbon is next to N.
