The drawing shows EtO2C joined to a zigzag chain with two vertices, then CO2Et.

Reading the chain from the left, the EtO2C carbon bonds to a vertex at the top, which bonds to a vertex at the bottom, which bonds to the CO2Et carbon. That gives two CH2 groups between the two ester carbons.

The molecule is therefore diethyl succinate: EtO2C–CH2–CH2–CO2Et.

There are no wedges, hashes or double-bond geometry, so no stereochemistry is included.

SMILES: CCOC(=O)CCC(=O)OCC
