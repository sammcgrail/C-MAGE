I read the structure as a triterpenoid-type core carrying a 4-pyridyl-triazole and an aminoalkoxy ether. The stereo marks below are my own reading of the wedges and hashes, and a few of them are uncertain.

**Substituents**
- Triazole: a 1,2,4-triazol-1-yl ring with a 4-pyridyl group on C5.
- Ether chain: –O–CH2–C(NH2)(CH3)(tBu), with a wedged tBu.
- Acid and side chain: CO2H on the A ring, and a CH(CH3)(iPr) group on the quaternary carbon next to it, with a hashed bond to the iPr.

**Core, as I read it**
- Rings A, B and C are fused six-membered rings. Ring A carries two wedged methyls and the CO2H. Ring B has a C=C at the B/C junction, and the junction hydrogens are marked by wedge and hash.
- A bridged lower region contains a CH2–O–CH2 pyran bridge. It has a wedged methyl on one bridgehead and a hashed CH2 on the other.
- I took the triazole N and the ether O to sit on adjacent carbons of this bridged ring.
- The triazole wedge is drawn very long and overlaps other bonds. I assigned it to the lower carbon, the one with the H label.

**Uncertain points**
- Both the attachment of the long triazole wedge and the stereo at the bridgehead carbon next to the methyl are inferred.
- The core carbon count I got is one short of what I would expect for an ibrexafungerp-type triterpenoid, so I may have missed a methyl. I followed the drawing as I read it.
