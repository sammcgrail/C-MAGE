The drawing shows a quinazoline core: a benzene ring fused to a pyrimidine ring.

- The pyrimidine ring runs C8a, N1, C2, N3, C4, C4a. N1 is at the top right of the ring, C2 carries a methyl group, N3 sits below it, and C4 at the bottom carries OH.
- The benzene ring carries OMe at the position para to C4a... more precisely, it sits on the carbon one position away from C4a, which is position 6.
- Checking the position: C4a is the lower-left fusion carbon, and the carbon adjacent to it on the benzene ring is C5, at the bottom. Next is C6, at the lower left, which bears OMe. So the compound is 6-methoxy-2-methylquinazolin-4-ol.
