**Upper unit.** The benzene ring carries a methyl at the top (C8), a methyl at the upper left (C7), and OH at the lower left (C6). The bottom carbon (C5) is attached to the CH2 bridge. The ring oxygen is attached to C8a, the carbon next to C8. The pyran ring runs O, then C2, which carries both ethyl and methyl groups, then C3 (CH2), C4 (CH2), and back to C4a. C4a is the ring-fusion carbon adjacent to C5.

**Lower unit.** This is the mirror image of the upper one, with the same substituents: C5 bears the CH2 bridge, C6 bears OH, C7 and C8 bear methyls, and C2 bears ethyl and methyl.
