I read the molecule as a 2-pyrrolidinone (γ-lactam) carrying a benzoic acid side chain on nitrogen and an unsaturated hydroxy-octenyl chain on C5.

- **Ring:** a five-membered lactam with a C=O at C2 and N1 at the lower right. The ring carbons are C2 (C=O), C3 and C4 (CH2), and C5 (the stereocentre).
- **N-substituent:** N–CH2–CH2–C6H4–CO2H, with the carboxylic acid para to the ethylene linker.
- **C5 substituent:** a hashed bond leads to a trans CH=CH, then CH(OH), then an unbranched n-pentyl chain. I counted the chain in the drawing as five carbons after the carbinol carbon.
- **Stereochemistry at C5:** the priorities are N > vinyl > ring CH2 > H. The vinyl group is hashed, so H points toward the viewer. N, vinyl and CH2 run clockwise as drawn, so the true sense is reversed, giving S. In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]` encodes S for the neighbour order written.
- **Alkene:** drawn trans, written as `/C=C/`.
- **Carbinol carbon:** drawn with a plain bond, so it carries no stereo label.
