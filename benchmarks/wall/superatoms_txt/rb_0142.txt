I read the molecule as a VHL-type ligand joined through a long amide–alkyl–ether linker to a quinazoline bearing a substituted benzoic acid.

- **VHL end:** a 4-(4-methylthiazol-5-yl)benzyl amide of a 4-hydroxyproline. The proline is drawn as the (2S,4R) isomer, with the OH on a wedge. It is acylated by a tert-leucine residue, which is drawn as the S isomer.
- **Linker:** the tert-leucine nitrogen carries an amide C(=O) followed by 11 CH2 groups, counted vertex by vertex, and then an ether oxygen.
- **Quinazoline:** the ether oxygen sits on C7 of the quinazoline.
- **Aryl group:** C4 of the quinazoline carries a benzene ring with a cyclopentyl group and a carboxylic acid on adjacent carbons. The acid is para to the quinazoline attachment.

For stereochemistry I wrote the proline and tert-leucine centres in the usual VH032-style form and adjusted the @/@@ marks to match the neighbour order in my SMILES. I did this from the standard VH032 SMILES as I remember it, not by an independent CIP check.
