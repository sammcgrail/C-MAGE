I read this as a nine-membered dilactone ring (antimycin-type) carrying a substituted picolinamide side chain.

**Substituents**
- **Pyridine:** it carries OMe at C4 and a methoxyacetate ester (OC(=O)CH2OMe) at C3. The C2 carbon is the amide carbonyl attached to NH.
- **Ring atoms, in order:** C(=O), O, CH(CH3), CH(OC(=O)iPr), CH(CH2Ph), C(=O), O, CH2, CH(NH).

**Stereocentres**

I assigned each centre from the wedges and hashes, then wrote the matching @/@@ in the SMILES.
- CH–NH (hashed N): S
- CH(CH3) (wedged methyl): S
- CH(OCOiPr) (hashed O): R
- CH(CH2Ph) (wedged benzyl): R
