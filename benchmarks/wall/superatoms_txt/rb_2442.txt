**Ring and chain assembly**
- **Macrocycle ester:** a piperazic acid ring whose carboxyl is esterified to an O–CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2 linker. That linker attaches to C3 of the indole.
- **Indole:** N-ethyl, with a 2-(1-methoxyethyl)pyridin-3-yl group at C2. C5 is bonded to a 3-hydroxyphenyl ring.
- **Phenyl side chain:** the phenyl ring carries CH2–CH(NH–)–C(=O) at the 5-position, and that carbonyl acylates the piperazic ring nitrogen.
- **Amide chain on the NH:** the NH is acylated by an N-methyl valine unit. Its N-methyl nitrogen carries a pyrrolidine-3-carbonyl group.
