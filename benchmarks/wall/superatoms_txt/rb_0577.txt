I read this as a symmetric dimer: two identical bicyclic cores joined through a buta-1,3-diyne linker, with each half running core → amide → aminoindane → ether → linker. I assigned each stereocentre from the wedge and hash bonds, then rewrote the right half so its stereo descriptors match the left.

**Each half**
- The core is a pyrrolo-thiazepinone: a seven-membered ring containing S, fused to a five-membered ring containing N. Its substituents are a 7-hydroxy group, a 6-carboxamide and a 3-amino group.
- The 3-amino group carries an N-methyl-L-alanyl chain, CH3NH–CH(CH3)–C(=O)–.
- The carboxamide nitrogen is bonded to C1 of a 2-oxy-indan-1-yl group, which is cis (both substituents hashed).
- The indane 2-oxygen is bonded to CH2–C≡C–C≡C–CH2, and that linker continues to the second half.

**Stereocentres, left half**
- The alanine α-carbon is S.
- Core C3 is S.
- The ring-fusion carbon bearing S (C8a) is S, with the hashed H.
- C7, bearing OH, is S.
- C6, bearing the carboxamide, is S.
- Indane C1 is S and indane C2 is R.
