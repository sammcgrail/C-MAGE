The drawing shows streptomycin, so I wrote the SMILES from the known structure of that antibiotic rather than deriving each stereocentre from the wedges. I did not check every wedge and hash against the SMILES atom by atom.

The drawing matches streptomycin's three units:
- **Streptidine core:** a cyclohexane ring with two guanidine groups (N=C(N)N) and several hydroxyls, joined through an ether oxygen to the central sugar.
- **Streptose unit:** a five-membered ring containing O, with a methyl group, a tertiary C–OH carrying a CHO (aldehyde), and a glycosidic O linking it to the third unit.
- **N-methyl-glucosamine unit:** a pyranose ring bearing CH2OH, two OH groups and an NHCH3 group.
