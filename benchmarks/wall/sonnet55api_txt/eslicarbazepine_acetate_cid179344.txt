I read the drawing as eslicarbazepine acetate: a dibenzo[b,f]azepine core with a carboxamide on the ring nitrogen and an acetate ester on the saturated ring carbon next to one benzo ring.

- **Core:** two benzene rings fused to a seven-membered ring. The seven-membered ring contains the ring nitrogen, one CH2, and the CH bearing the acetate.
- **Nitrogen substituent:** the ring N carries a C(=O)NH2 group (urea-type carboxamide).
- **Ester:** the CH carbon bears O–C(=O)–CH3, an acetate, drawn with a hashed bond to O.

**Stereochemistry:** the hashed bond puts the O behind the plane, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The three other neighbours are O at the left, the aromatic carbon at the upper right, and the CH2 at the bottom. Seen from the H side, O → aromatic C → CH2 runs clockwise.

In the SMILES below the neighbour order at the stereocentre is O, H, ring-closure aromatic C, CH2. Putting H first gives @@, and moving H back to second place is one swap, so the tag becomes @. The centre is therefore written [C@H].
