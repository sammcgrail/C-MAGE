This is thiamine diphosphate (thiamine pyrophosphate), drawn with one phosphate oxygen as O⁻.

**Pyrimidine ring.** Going around: C(CH3)=N–C(NH2)=C(CH2–)–CH=N. The ring atoms are N (top), C bearing the methyl, N (right), C bearing NH2, C bearing the CH2 linker, and CH.

**Thiazolium ring.** N⁺ is bonded to the CH2 linker and to a C that carries a methyl. That carbon is double-bonded to the C bearing the side chain, which connects to S. S connects to CH, and CH=N⁺ closes the ring.

**Side chain.** The ring carbon bears CH2CH2–O–P(=O)(O)–O–P(=O)([O-])O.

SMILES, written in order:
- Pyrimidine: Cc1ncc(c(N)n1)C[n+]2...
- Thiazolium: c[s]c(CCO...)c2C

Full SMILES: Cc1ncc(C[n+]2csc(CCOP(=O)(O)OP(=O)([O-])O)c2C)c(N)n1

Check of the pyrimidine ring: c1(C) n c c(CH2...) c(N) n1 gives six atoms: C, N, CH, C, C(NH2), N. This matches the drawing.
