I read the molecule as amorolfine, which has a cis-2,6-dimethylmorpholine ring.

- **Left end:** a tert-amyl group (CCC(C)(C)–) sits on a para-substituted benzene ring.
- **Linker:** from the ring, a CH2 leads to a CH(CH3), then a CH2, then the morpholine nitrogen.
- **Morpholine ring:** N, CH2, C(CH3), O, C(CH3), CH2, closing back to N. Both ring methyls are drawn with hashed bonds, so each carbon bearing a methyl is a stereocentre.
- **Stereo assignment:** I worked out each hashed centre from the 2D coordinates, with the methyl pointing away from the viewer and the hydrogen towards it. Written as N–CH2–C–O–C–CH2, both centres come out as `@`. Both methyls point the same way, so the ring is cis.
- **Unmarked centre:** the CH(CH3) in the linker is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
