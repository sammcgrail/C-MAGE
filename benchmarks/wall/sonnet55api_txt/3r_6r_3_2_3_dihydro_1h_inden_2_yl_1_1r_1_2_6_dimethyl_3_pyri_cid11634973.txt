I read the molecule as a diketopiperazine (piperazine-2,5-dione) core with four substituents.

**Core ring.** Going round the six-membered ring: C3 bears a sec-butyl group, then a C=O, then NH, then a CH bearing an indan-2-yl group, then a second C=O, then a tertiary N.

**Substituents.**
- The sec-butyl group is CH(CH3)CH2CH3, with a wedged methyl.
- The indan-2-yl group is a benzene ring fused to a cyclopentane, attached at the cyclopentane carbon opposite the ring fusion. That carbon is not a stereocentre.
- The ring N carries a CH that is bonded to a 2,6-dimethylpyridin-3-yl group and to a C(=O) joined to a morpholine nitrogen.

**Stereochemistry.** I assigned CIP descriptors from the wedges and hashes, then matched each to @/@@ in the SMILES neighbour order:
- The sec-butyl CH, with its wedged methyl, is S.
- The ring carbon with the hashed bond to N, next to the sec-butyl group, is R.
- The ring carbon bearing the indanyl group, with a hashed bond to the indane, is R.
- The exocyclic CH, with a hashed bond to the ring N, is R.
