I read this as an ertugliflozin-type bicyclic ketal C-aryl glycoside carrying a fluorine on the ethoxyphenyl ring. Ertugliflozin itself has no fluorine there.

**Aromatic part**
- The left ring carries OEt on one carbon, with F on the adjacent carbon. The CH2 bridge sits para to the OEt, so F is meta to the CH2.
- The right ring is attached to the CH2 bridge, with Cl on the neighbouring carbon. The sugar-bearing carbon is meta to the CH2, and Cl is para to it.

**Bicyclic core**
- The anomeric carbon bears the aryl group, a ring oxygen leading to OCH2, and a bridging oxygen.
- The bridging oxygen links to the quaternary carbon that carries CH2OH.
- That quaternary carbon is also bonded to the OCH2 carbon.
- Three CH(OH) carbons run from the quaternary carbon round to the anomeric carbon.
