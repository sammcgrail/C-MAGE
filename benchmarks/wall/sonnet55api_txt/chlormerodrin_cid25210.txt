The drawing is a mercury-containing urea, the structure of chlormerodrin.

Reading the atoms in order:
- A methyl group is attached to O (methoxy).
- That O is bonded to a CH carbon.
- The CH carbon carries two substituents: a CH2 bonded to NH, and a CH2 bonded to Hg.
- The NH is bonded to a C(=O)NH2 group (urea).
- Hg is also bonded to Cl.

The SMILES is COC(CNC(N)=O)C[Hg]Cl.

The CH carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I added no stereo marks.
