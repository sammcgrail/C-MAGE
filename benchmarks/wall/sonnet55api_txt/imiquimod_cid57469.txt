This is imiquimod, an imidazo[4,5-c]quinoline carrying an isobutyl group on N1 and an amino group at C4.

Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Isobutyl group:** two terminal methyls on a CH, then a CH2 attached to the imidazole nitrogen (N1).
- **Imidazole ring:** N1 is bonded to CH=N, and that N is bonded to the fused carbon C3a. C3a is double-bonded to the other fused carbon, which is bonded back to N1.
- **Pyridine ring:** C3a is bonded to C(NH2), which is double-bonded to the ring N. That N is bonded to an aromatic carbon of the benzene ring. The other fused carbon, bonded to N1, attaches to the second benzene fusion carbon.
- **Benzene ring:** four CH carbons plus the two fusion carbons.

Writing this as SMILES: CC(C)Cn1cnc2c(N)nc3ccccc3c12
