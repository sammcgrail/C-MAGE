- **Left acyl chain:** the carbonyl carbon plus seven CH2 groups lead to the nitrogen, which makes an octanoate whose C8 is bonded to N.
- **Nitrogen substituents:** the nitrogen is tertiary, carrying a 2-hydroxyethyl group (CH2CH2OH), the octanoate chain, and a hexyl chain.
- **Right ester:** the hexyl chain is five CH2 groups followed by a C(=O), a 6-oxohexyl unit. It is esterified to an undecyl (11-carbon) chain through oxygen.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CH of the heptadecan-9-yl group is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I assigned no stereo.
