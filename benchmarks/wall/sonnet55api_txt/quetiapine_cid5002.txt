This is quetiapine, a dibenzo[b,f][1,4]thiazepine bearing a piperazine that carries a 2-(2-hydroxyethoxy)ethyl chain.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- The left chain is HO–CH2–CH2–O–CH2–CH2–N, ending at a piperazine nitrogen.
- The piperazine ring has two nitrogens. One carries the chain and the other is bonded to the amidine carbon.
- The amidine carbon (C=N) is attached to the upper benzene ring.
- That upper benzene ring is bonded to S.
- S is bonded to the lower benzene ring.
- The lower benzene ring carries the N of the C=N.
