The drawing matches nicomorphine, which is morphine esterified with nicotinic acid at both the 3-phenol and the 6-alcohol. Each ester carbonyl carries a pyridin-3-yl ring. I did not derive the stereocentres from the wedges and hashes myself. I took them from the heroin SMILES I remember, swapping acetyl for nicotinoyl, and assumed the drawing shows natural morphine stereochemistry.

I checked the SMILES for validity: the ring-closure labels are reused only after they are closed. The first stereocentre has the same neighbour order as in the heroin SMILES, so its chirality tag carries over unchanged.
