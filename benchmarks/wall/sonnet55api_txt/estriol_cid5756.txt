The drawing shows the estra-1,3,5(10)-triene steroid skeleton (aromatic A ring, three further fused rings) with three features:

- a phenolic OH on the aromatic ring (C3);
- two OH groups on the five-membered D ring, one hashed and one wedged (C16 and C17);
- a wedged methyl at the ring-fusion carbon (C13), and three ring-fusion hydrogens drawn with wedges or hashes (C8, C9, C14).

The 16-OH is hashed (α) and the 17-OH is wedged (β), and the ring-fusion stereo matches the natural steroid pattern. The molecule is therefore estriol. The drawing is the standard steroid layout rotated 180° in the plane, which does not change the chirality.
