The structure is a chromone (4H-chromen-4-one) core with three substituents.

- At C3 (the carbon next to the C=O carbon) there is an NHCHO (formamide) group.
- At C6 there is a phenoxy group (O–Ph), on the benzene carbon adjacent to the ring fusion carbon C4a.
- At C7 there is a methanesulfonamide group (NHSO2CH3), on the carbon adjacent to C6.

Reading the pyranone ring: the ring-fusion carbon C4a (at about 743,803) bonds to the carbonyl carbon C4 (868,710). C4 bonds to C3 (1015,770), which bears the NH. C3 is double-bonded to C2 (1032,928), and C2 bonds to O1 (907,1022). O1 bonds to C8a (762,960). The benzene ring runs C4a–C5 (598,742)–C6 (472,836, bearing OPh)–C7 (490,994, bearing NHSO2Me)–C8 (636,1054)–C8a.
