This structure is tizanidine: a 2,1,3-benzothiadiazole core carrying a chlorine and an NH-linked 4,5-dihydroimidazol-2-yl group.

**Benzothiadiazole ring.** The five-membered ring contains S, N, C, C, N. S is bonded to both ring nitrogens. The upper N is double-bonded to the fused carbon at the top (the one bonded to the carbon bearing NH). The lower N is double-bonded to the other fused carbon.

**Benzene ring.** The fused carbon at the top (C3a) is bonded to the carbon bearing the NH substituent. That carbon is double-bonded to the carbon bearing Cl. Next come two CH carbons, one double-bonded to the other, and the second CH is bonded to the lower fused carbon.

**Side chain.** The NH is attached to C(=N–CH2–CH2–NH), which is the imidazoline ring.
