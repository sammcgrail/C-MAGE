I read this as imipenem, the carbapenem antibiotic. The structure has a bicyclic β-lactam fused to a five-membered ring containing a C=C bond. The ring carries a carboxylic acid on the carbon next to N, and a thioether chain –S–CH2CH2–N=CH–NH2 on the other alkene carbon. The β-lactam carbon next to the lactam C=O bears a 1-hydroxyethyl group.

I took the three stereocentres (the two ring-fusion carbons and the CH(OH) carbon) from the known configuration of imipenem rather than deriving each one from the hashed bonds. The N=C double bond has no cis/trans geometry drawn, so I left it unspecified.
