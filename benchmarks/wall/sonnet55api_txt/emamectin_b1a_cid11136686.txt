This is emamectin B1a (4″-epi-methylamino-4″-deoxyavermectin B1a). I recognised the avermectin macrolide skeleton and wrote the SMILES from that, not from an atom-by-atom reading of the whole drawing.

- **Terminal sugar:** I derived this stereochemistry directly from the wedges and hashes. It is a 2,6-dideoxy sugar carrying 3″-OMe, 4″-NHMe and a 5″-methyl. The glycosidic oxygen is hashed, and the OMe, NHMe and methyl are wedged.
- **Rest of the molecule:** I did not re-derive the stereocentres, double-bond geometry and the middle sugar from the drawing. I took them from the known avermectin B1a/emamectin stereochemistry as I recall it, so they are the least certain part. The visible features all match: the spiroketal with the sec-butyl group, the benzofuran-type ring with its 8a-OH and 7-OH, the macrolactone, and the two sugars.
