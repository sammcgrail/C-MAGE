I read the structure as a 3,5-dichloro-4-fluorophenyl and CF3-bearing dihydroisoxazole linked to a spiro isobenzofuran–azetidine, which carries an N-acyl methylsulfonylacetyl group.

- **Left end:** a benzene ring with Cl at both meta positions and F at the para position. It is attached to C5 of a 4,5-dihydroisoxazole (O1, N2=C3, CH2 at C4). C5 also carries CF3 on a wedge bond.
- **Centre:** C3 of the isoxazoline bonds to a benzene ring. This ring is fused to a five-membered ring containing a CH2 and an O, and the ring carbon attached to O is a spiro carbon.
- **Spiro junction:** that spiro carbon is also part of an azetidine ring (spiro[isobenzofuran-1,3'-azetidine]). The isoxazoline-bearing aryl carbon is para to the aryl carbon attached to the spiro carbon.
- **Right end:** the azetidine nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)CH2S(=O)(=O)CH3.
- **Stereochemistry:** only the isoxazoline C5 is drawn with a wedge. The CIP priorities are O > CF3 > aryl > CH2. Taking the neighbours in SMILES order (O, CH2, aryl, CF3) with CF3 toward the viewer, the geometry works out to `@@`.
