This is the gabapentin prodrug with an isobutyryloxyethyl carbamate on the nitrogen.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- The left end is an isopropyl group, CH(CH3)2, attached to a C(=O) that is bonded to an O.
- That O is bonded to a CH carrying a methyl group, and the CH is bonded to a second O.
- The second O is bonded to a C(=O), which is bonded to NH.
- The NH is bonded to a CH2 on the quaternary cyclohexane carbon.
- The cyclohexane ring has six vertices, and its quaternary carbon also bears CH2–C(=O)OH.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so there is no stereochemistry.

SMILES: CC(C)C(=O)OC(C)OC(=O)NCC1(CC(=O)O)CCCCC1
