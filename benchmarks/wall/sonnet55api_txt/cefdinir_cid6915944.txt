This is cefdinir, a cephalosporin antibiotic. I read it region by region.

- **Bicyclic core:** a β-lactam (the ring with the C=O) is fused to a six-membered dihydrothiazine ring containing S. The dihydrothiazine ring carries a vinyl group and a carboxylic acid on its C=C.
- **Side chain:** an amide NH on the β-lactam carbon leads to C(=N–OH), which is attached to a 2-aminothiazol-4-yl ring.
- **Ring-fusion carbon (bonded to S):** the hashed H gives the R configuration.
- **Carbon bearing the NH:** the wedged N gives the R configuration.
- **Oxime:** in the drawing the OH and the amide carbon lie on the same side of the C=N bond. That makes it the Z oxime, written `/C(=N\O)/`.

I checked both stereocentres against the CIP priorities. The SMILES encodes the same (6R,7R) configuration as the drawing.
