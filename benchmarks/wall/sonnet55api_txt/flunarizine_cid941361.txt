This is flunarizine: a piperazine with a bis(4-fluorophenyl)methyl group on one nitrogen and a cinnamyl group on the other.

The double bond is drawn with the CH2 and the phenyl on opposite sides, so it is trans (E). The chain runs N–CH2 (917,497) – CH= (1010,575) = CH (1123,533) – phenyl (1217,611).

For the geometry, the CH2 at (917,497) is above-left of the first alkene carbon at (1010,575). The phenyl at (1217,611) is below-right of the second alkene carbon at (1123,533). The substituents are therefore on opposite sides of the double bond, which gives E.
