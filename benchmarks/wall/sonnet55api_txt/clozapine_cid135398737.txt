This structure is a dibenzodiazepine: a seven-membered ring fused to two benzene rings. It matches the clozapine skeleton.

**Left ring.** The chlorinated benzene carries Cl at the top left. Its fused carbons connect to the ring N (the imine N= side) and to the NH.

**Seven-membered ring.** The atoms run: aromatic C (upper left ring) – N= – C(piperazinyl) – aromatic C (right ring) – aromatic C (right ring) – NH – aromatic C (left ring).

**Right ring.** An unsubstituted benzene fused to the seven-membered ring.

**Substituent.** The amidine carbon bears an N-methylpiperazine.

**Chlorine position.** The left ring has Cl at the carbon meta to the imine-N-bearing carbon, which is para to the NH-bearing carbon. In the drawing, the ring carbon bonded to N= (at 481,606) is adjacent to the CH at 388,458, which is adjacent to the C–Cl at 214,465. So Cl is meta to the N= carbon and para to the NH carbon (399,760). This matches clozapine, where Cl is para to the NH.
