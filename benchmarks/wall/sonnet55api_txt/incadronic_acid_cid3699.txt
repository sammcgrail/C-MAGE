The drawing shows a central carbon bonded to two phosphonic acid groups, an NH, and an implicit hydrogen. The NH connects to a cycloheptyl ring.

Each phosphorus carries one P=O and two OH groups, so each group is P(=O)(O)O.

Counting the ring vertices one at a time: the attachment carbon at (918,903), then (1078,1063), (1303,1040), (1424,849), (1351,634), (1137,558) and (945,678). That is 7 carbons, so the ring is cycloheptane.

The central carbon has no stereo marks, so no stereochemistry is included.
