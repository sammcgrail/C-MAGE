I read this as lonafarnib, a tricyclic benzo-cycloheptapyridine core joined to two piperidines.

- **Core:** a pyridine ring carrying Br fused through a CH2–CH2 bridge to a benzene ring carrying Cl and Br. The benzene Br sits next to the stereocentre carbon.
- **Stereocentre:** the CH linking the core to the first piperidine. Its bond to the piperidine carbon is hashed (pointing away), so the implicit H faces the viewer. Taking the piperidine, pyridine-side and benzene-side neighbours in turn gives a counterclockwise sense on the page, which works out to `[C@H]` in the SMILES below.
- **Side chain:** the first piperidine nitrogen is acylated by C(=O)CH2, which attaches to the 4-position of a second piperidine. That piperidine's nitrogen carries a primary urea, N–C(=O)NH2.
