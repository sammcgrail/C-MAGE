I read this as ergocalciferol (vitamin D2), and I wrote the SMILES from that identification and from my recall of the standard D2 SMILES. I did not derive each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes myself.

- **Side chain:** a branched chain with a trans (E) C=C, ending in an isopropyl group and carrying a methyl on the carbon next to it.
- **Five-membered ring:** it carries the side chain and is fused to the six-membered ring. The ring-fusion carbon bears a wedged methyl, and the other fusion carbon bears a hashed H.
- **Triene unit:** an exocyclic C=C from the six-membered ring of the bicycle, then a CH=CH linking to the cyclohexane ring.
- **Cyclohexane ring:** it has an exocyclic =CH2 and a hashed OH, which gives the (3S) alcohol.
- **Overall configuration:** this matches the (3S,5Z,7E,22E) form of D2.
