This structure is quinupristin, so I wrote the SMILES from the structure as I recall it rather than deriving every stereocentre from the wedges and hashes. The quinuclidine drawing is distorted, so I did not trace its stereocentre bond by bond. Its stereo comes from my recall of the quinupristin SMILES.

Fragments read from the drawing:
- **Quinuclidine:** a bicyclic amine bonded through a thioether (S–CH2) to the keto-piperidine ring.
- **Keto-piperidine ring:** N–CH2–CH(CH2S)–C(=O)–CH2–CH(C=O)–N, closed back onto the ring nitrogen.
- **Phenylglycine unit:** a phenyl-bearing carbon linking an amide NH to an ester C(=O)–O.
- **Threonine-type unit:** the ester oxygen sits on a CH(CH3), next to an NH that carries the 3-hydroxypicolinoyl amide (a pyridine with OH ortho to the C(=O)NH).
- **Aminobutyric acid unit:** an ethyl-bearing carbon with NH and C(=O).
- **Proline:** a five-membered ring.
- **N-methyl-dimethylaminophenylalanine:** N-methylated, with a 4-(dimethylamino)benzyl side chain.

All of these join in one macrocycle through amide and ester links.
