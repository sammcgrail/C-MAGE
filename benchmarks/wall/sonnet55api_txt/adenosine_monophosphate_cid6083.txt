The drawing shows adenosine 5'-monophosphate (AMP). It has three parts:

- **Base:** an adenine unit with an NH2 on the six-membered ring, joined to the sugar through N9.
- **Sugar:** a five-membered ribose ring (four carbons and one oxygen). The base and the CH2 group are both on wedge bonds, and the two OH groups are both on hashed bonds. This is the β-D-ribose arrangement.
- **Phosphate:** the 5'-CH2 is bonded to O–P(=O)(OH)2.

I did not derive the stereo descriptors atom by atom. The wedge/hash pattern matches natural β-D-ribose, so I used the recalled PubChem SMILES for natural AMP and checked its connectivity against the drawing.
