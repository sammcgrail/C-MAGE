I read this as decitabine (5-aza-2'-deoxycytidine), a 1,3,5-triazin-2-one base attached to a 2-deoxyribose.

**Base ring.** The six-membered ring contains three nitrogens. Going around it: N1 (attached to the sugar), C2(=O), N3, C4 bearing NH2, N5, and C6 as a CH. The double bonds are C4=N3 and C6=N5, plus the C2=O.

**Sugar.** The five-membered ring is O, C1', C2' (a CH2), C3', C4'. C3' carries an OH and C4' carries CH2OH.

**Stereochemistry.** I assigned each centre from the wedges and hashes, with the implicit hydrogen placed opposite the drawn wedge or hash:
- C1' has a hashed bond to N, so the base is behind the plane.
- C3' has a wedged OH, so the OH is in front.
- C4' has a hashed bond to CH2OH, so the CH2OH is behind.

The base and CH2OH are on the same side and the 3'-OH is on the opposite side, which is the β-D-2'-deoxynucleoside arrangement. I worked out the @/@@ labels by hand from the drawing and did not check them against a reference SMILES.

SMILES: Nc1nc(=O)n([C@H]2C[C@H](O)[C@@H](CO)O2)cn1
