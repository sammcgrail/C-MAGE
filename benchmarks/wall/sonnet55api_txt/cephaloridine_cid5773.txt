I read this as cephaloridine, a cephalosporin. The fused ring system is a β-lactam joined to a six-membered ring containing S, and the groups on it are:

- A carboxylate (C(=O)[O-]) on the ring carbon next to the lactam N.
- A CH2–pyridinium group on the neighbouring alkene carbon.
- At the β-lactam carbon bearing NH (drawn with a wedge), an NH–C(=O)–CH2–(2-thienyl) amide.

Stereochemistry comes from the two drawn stereocentres:

- The ring-fusion carbon bonded to S has a hashed H. Its neighbour order in the SMILES is S, H, N, C7, which gives @@.
- The carbon bearing NH has a wedged NH bond. Its neighbour order is C6, H, N, C8, which gives @.

SMILES: [O-]C(=O)C1=C(C[n+]2ccccc2)CS[C@@H]3[C@H](NC(=O)Cc4cccs4)C(=O)N13
