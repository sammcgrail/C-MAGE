This structure is bromadiolone-like but without the hydroxy group: it is the coumarin anticoagulant difenacoum, specifically its brominated analog, which is brodifacoum.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- **Coumarin:** a 4-hydroxycoumarin with the benzo ring at the top right, the ring oxygen, a C=O, and an OH on C4. C3 carries the tetralin substituent.
- **Tetralin:** C1 (attached to coumarin C3) is bonded to CH2, which is bonded to CH (C3 of the tetralin) bearing the biphenyl. That CH is bonded to another CH2, which joins the aromatic ring.
- **Biphenyl:** the tetralin is attached to a phenyl ring that is para-linked to a 4-bromophenyl ring.
- **Stereochemistry:** no wedges or hashes are drawn, so none is included.
