The drawing shows two carbons joined by a single bond.

- The left carbon carries three Cl atoms (one up, one left, one down), so it is a CCl3 group.
- The right carbon carries an OH on the right and two plain line ends, one up and one down. Each of those line ends is a methyl group.

That makes the right carbon C(CH3)2(OH), bonded to CCl3. The molecule is 1,1,1-trichloro-2-methylpropan-2-ol, the chlorobutanol structure.

SMILES: CC(C)(O)C(Cl)(Cl)Cl
