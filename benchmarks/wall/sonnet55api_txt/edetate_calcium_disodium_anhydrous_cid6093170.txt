The drawing shows an EDTA tetra-anion with three counter-ions: Ca²⁺, Na⁺ and Na⁺. This is calcium disodium EDTA.

The EDTA backbone has two nitrogens joined by a CH2CH2 bridge. Each nitrogen carries two CH2COO⁻ arms, giving four carboxylates in total.

The charges balance: the four carboxylate charges (−4) are offset by Ca²⁺ (+2) and two Na⁺ (+2).

The ions are written as separate components in the SMILES, since the drawing shows them unbonded.

SMILES: [Ca+2].[Na+].[Na+].[O-]C(=O)CN(CC([O-])=O)CCN(CC([O-])=O)CC([O-])=O
