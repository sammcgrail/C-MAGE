**Ethanolamine:** HO-CH2-CH2-NH2, written as NCCO.

**Pyridone ring:** N-hydroxy-2-pyridone.
- The ring nitrogen carries an OH.
- C2 is the carbonyl carbon (C=O).
- C3 is CH, C4 bears a methyl, C5 is CH, and C6 bears the side chain.
- The ring bonds are C3=C4 and C5=C6.

**Side chain on C6:** CH2-CH(CH3)-CH2-C(CH3)3, which is the 2,4,4-trimethylpentyl group.
