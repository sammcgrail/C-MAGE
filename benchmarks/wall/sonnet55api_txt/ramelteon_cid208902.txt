Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left side:** an ethyl group (CH3–CH2) attached to a C=O, which is bonded to an NH. This is a propanamide.
- **Linker:** from the NH, two CH2 groups lead to the ring stereocentre. The bond from the second CH2 to the stereocentre is drawn as a hashed wedge.
- **Five-membered carbocycle:** the stereocentre (CH), then two CH2 groups, then two aromatic carbons that close the ring.
- **Benzene ring:** it carries two CH atoms and is fused to both five-membered rings.
- **Dihydrofuran ring:** it contains the O, two aromatic carbons and a CH2–CH2 unit.

**Stereochemistry:** the hashed bond puts the chain carbon behind the plane, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are aromatic C > chain CH2 > ring CH2 > H. On the page the sequence aromatic C (lower right), chain CH2 (lower left), ring CH2 (up) runs clockwise. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, which gives S. This matches (S)-ramelteon.

For the SMILES, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is chain, H, ring-closure aromatic C, ring CH2. For that order `@@` corresponds to S.

SMILES: CCC(=O)NCC[C@@H]1CCc2ccc3OCCc3c21
