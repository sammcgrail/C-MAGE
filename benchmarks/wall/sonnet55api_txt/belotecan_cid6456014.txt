I read this as a camptothecin-type scaffold with a 2-(isopropylamino)ethyl group on the quinoline ring.

- **Left ring system:** a lactone ring (O–C(=O) with a CH2 next to the ring oxygen) is fused to a pyridone ring that carries the C=O and the ring nitrogen.
- **Quaternary stereocentre:** it carries an ethyl group, an OH on a wedge, the lactone C=O and the pyridone ring carbon. Taking the OH as pointing towards the viewer and the other three substituents in the plane, I worked out the chirality as `@@` for the neighbour order ethyl, ring carbon (via the ring-closure digit), OH, lactone C=O.
- **Middle and right rings:** the pyridone nitrogen is joined through a CH2 to the ring that links the pyridone to the quinoline. The quinoline contains the aromatic N and a benzo ring, with the CH2CH2NH–CH(CH3)2 chain on the carbon next to the benzo ring.

This matches belotecan.

SMILES: CC[C@@]1(O)C(=O)OCc2c1cc1-c3nc4ccccc4c(CCNC(C)C)c3Cn1c2=O
