- The carbonyl carbon is bonded to an alpha carbon at (750, 798).
- The alpha carbon carries an ethyl group: (425, 1017) then (75, 841).
- The alpha carbon is also bonded to a carbon at (1100, 973). That carbon bears a methyl at (1125, 1360) and an ethyl: (1425, 757) then (1400, 368).

So the alpha carbon is CH(Et)(C(=O)NH2) attached to CH(CH3)CH2CH3. This is 2-ethyl-3-methylpentanamide.

Both the alpha carbon and the CH(CH3) carbon are stereocentres by connectivity, but all bonds are drawn plain, so no stereochemistry is given.
