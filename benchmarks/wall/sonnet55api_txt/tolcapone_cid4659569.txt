The drawing shows a benzophenone core. A p-tolyl ring (methyl at the para position) is joined through a C=O group to a second ring.

On the second ring, going around from the carbonyl-bearing carbon:
- C1 carries the carbonyl.
- C2 is CH.
- C3 carries OH (the upper right OH).
- C4 carries OH (the lower right OH).
- C5 carries the nitro group (N+ with =O and O−).
- C6 is CH.

This ring is therefore 3,4-dihydroxy-5-nitrophenyl, which is the same substitution pattern as tolcapone.

SMILES: Cc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)c1cc(O)c(O)c([N+](=O)[O-])c1
