I read the structure as lasofoxifene, a 5,6,7,8-tetrahydronaphthalen-2-ol core carrying a phenyl and a 4-(2-pyrrolidin-1-ylethoxy)phenyl group on adjacent ring carbons.

**Connectivity**
- The left end is a pyrrolidine ring whose nitrogen is joined to a two-carbon chain (N–CH2–CH2–O).
- The oxygen of that chain is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring.
- That benzene ring is bonded to C5 of the tetrahydronaphthalene, which is the stereocentre at the lower wedge.
- The adjacent stereocentre, C6, carries an unsubstituted phenyl ring (the upper wedge) and the CH2–CH2 portion of the saturated ring.
- The fused aromatic ring has a phenolic OH at the far right.

**Stereochemistry**
Both wedges point toward the viewer, so each implicit hydrogen points away. Tracing the three drawn neighbours of each stereocentre from the front gives a clockwise sequence at C5 (aryl → C6 → C4a) and at C6 (phenyl → CH2 → C5).
