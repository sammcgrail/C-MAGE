The drawing shows ethyl pyruvate.

Reading the structure from left to right:
- A methyl carbon at the far left is bonded to a carbon with a double-bonded O (the ketone).
- That ketone carbon is bonded to a second carbon, which carries a double-bonded O (C=O) and a single-bonded O (the ester oxygen).
- The ester oxygen is bonded to a CH2, which is bonded to a CH3 (the ethyl group).

This gives CC(=O)C(=O)OCC. All bonds are plain, so there is no stereochemistry.

SMILES: CCOC(=O)C(C)=O
