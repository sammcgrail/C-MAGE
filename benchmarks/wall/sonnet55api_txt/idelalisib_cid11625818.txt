I read this as an idelalisib-type molecule: a 5-fluoro-3-phenylquinazolin-4-one with an (S)-1-(purin-6-ylamino)propyl group at C2.

- **Quinazolinone core:** C2 is attached to the side chain. N3 carries the phenyl ring. C4 is the C=O. The benzo ring has fluorine on the carbon next to C4a.
- **Side chain:** a stereocentre bears an ethyl group (wedge bond), an NH linking to the purine, and the quinazolinone C2.
- **Purine:** the NH-bearing carbon is C6. The imidazole N–H sits on the nitrogen next to C5, the carbon bonded to C6, so it is the N7-H tautomer. This differs from the usual idelalisib drawing, so I wrote it as drawn.
- **Stereochemistry:** the ethyl group is on a wedge, so H points away from the viewer. Viewed from the front, ethyl → quinazolinone carbon → NH runs clockwise. That corresponds to `CC[C@H](N…)c1…` in SMILES order.

SMILES: CC[C@H](Nc1ncnc2nc[nH]c12)c1nc2cccc(F)c2c(=O)n1-c1ccccc1
