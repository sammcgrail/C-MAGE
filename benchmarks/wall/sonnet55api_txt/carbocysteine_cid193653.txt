Reading the drawing gives S-carboxymethyl-cysteine (carbocisteine) with one defined stereocentre.

- **Left end:** a carboxylic acid, C(=O)OH.
- **Stereocentre:** the alpha carbon carries an NH2 on a hashed bond, so the NH2 points away from the viewer. Its implicit H points toward the viewer.
- **Chain:** the alpha carbon connects to a CH2, then to a thioether sulfur, then to another CH2.
- **Right end:** the second CH2 is bonded to a second carboxylic acid, C(=O)OH.

To assign the stereo tag, I viewed the centre from the H side (toward the viewer). From there the carboxyl, CH2 and N substituents run clockwise on the page. Written in the order carboxyl, H, N, CH2, that arrangement corresponds to @@. This matches the L-cysteine configuration.

SMILES: OC(=O)[C@@H](N)CSCC(=O)O
