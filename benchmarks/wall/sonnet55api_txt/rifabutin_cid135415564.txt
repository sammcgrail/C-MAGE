I read this as rifabutin, a rifamycin-type macrocycle. The drawing shows the following parts:

- **Aromatic core:** a naphthalene-derived core carrying two OH groups and one methyl group.
- **Furanone:** a ring fused to the core, containing the quaternary C–CH3 carbon, an O atom and a C=O.
- **Spiro-imidazole:** an imidazole ring fused to the core and joined by a spiro carbon to an N-isobutyl piperidine.
- **Ansa chain:** an amide NH–C(=O) linked through a diene, a run of CH(CH3) and CH(OH) centres, an OAc group, an OMe group and an enol ether to the furanone.
