The structure is zearalenone, a 14-membered macrocyclic lactone fused to a resorcinol ring. I read it as follows:

- **Lactone:** the ring oxygen links the aromatic ketone-side carbonyl (C=O) to a CH carrying the methyl.
- **Chain from the CH:** three CH2 groups, then a ketone C=O, then three more CH2 groups.
- **Alkene:** a CH=CH drawn with trans geometry, joined to the aromatic ring.
- **Aromatic ring:** a benzene ring with two OH groups meta to each other. The lactone carbonyl sits on the ring carbon between the ring-fusion carbon and one of the OH-bearing carbons.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the CH–CH3 carbon, drawn with a wedged methyl. The priorities are O > CH2 > CH3, with H pointing away. O sits on the right, the CH2 on the left and the methyl at the bottom, which runs counterclockwise and gives S.
