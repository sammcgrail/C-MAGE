The drawing shows thalidomide: a phthalimide group (a benzene ring fused to an imide ring) attached through its nitrogen to the 3-position of a glutarimide (piperidine-2,6-dione) ring.

Glutarimide ring: the NH sits between two carbonyl carbons. One carbonyl carbon is bonded to the CH that carries the phthalimide nitrogen. The other carbonyl carbon is bonded to a CH2, which connects through a second CH2 to that same CH.

The CH is drawn with plain bonds, so I assign no stereochemistry.

SMILES: O=C1CCC(N2C(=O)c3ccccc3C2=O)C(=O)N1
