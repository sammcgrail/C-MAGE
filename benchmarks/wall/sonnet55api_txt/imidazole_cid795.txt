Reading the ring atoms:
- The top-left N has a double bond to the lower-left carbon, so it is the pyridine-type nitrogen.
- The top-left N also has a single bond to the top carbon.
- The top carbon and the right carbon are joined by a double bond.
- The right carbon is single-bonded to the bottom NH.
- The bottom NH is single-bonded to the lower-left carbon, which closes the ring.
