I read this as the dimethylarsino–glutathione conjugate: an (CH3)2As group bonded through sulfur to the cysteine side chain of γ‑Glu‑Cys‑Gly.

- **Arsenic end:** an As atom carries two methyl groups and is bonded to S, which connects to a CH2.
- **Cysteine unit:** the CH2 is attached to the cysteine α‑carbon. Its NH is acylated by the glutamate side‑chain carbonyl, and its C(=O) forms an amide with the glycine NH.
- **Glycine unit:** NH–CH2–C(=O)OH.
- **Glutamate unit:** the side chain is C(=O)–CH2–CH2, ending at the α‑carbon, which bears NH2 (drawn hashed) and COOH.

For stereochemistry, I assigned each centre from the drawing:

- **Glutamate α‑carbon:** the hashed NH2 puts H toward the viewer. With priorities N > COOH > CH2, the sequence reads clockwise from the front, so the true configuration is reversed to counterclockwise. This is S, the L‑glutamate configuration.
- **Cysteine α‑carbon:** the hashed CH2 puts H toward the viewer. With priorities N > CH2S > C(=O) > H, the sequence reads counterclockwise from the front, so the true configuration is reversed to clockwise. This is R, matching natural glutathione.
