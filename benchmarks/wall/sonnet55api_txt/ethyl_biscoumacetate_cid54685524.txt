**Left coumarin:** the ring oxygen O1 connects to the benzo ring carbon and to C2, which is a C=O. C3 is double-bonded to C4, which carries the OH. C4 attaches to the benzo carbon C4a. C3 is bonded to the central CH.

**Right coumarin:** the connectivity is the same as the left unit.

**Central CH:** it bears both coumarin C3 atoms and the ester group C(=O)OCC.
