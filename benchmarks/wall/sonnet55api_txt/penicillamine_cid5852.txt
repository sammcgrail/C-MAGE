The image shows penicillamine (2-amino-3-mercapto-3-methylbutanoic acid).

- **Connectivity:** a carboxylic acid (HO–C=O) is bonded to the α-carbon. The α-carbon carries an NH2 group and a quaternary carbon bearing two methyl groups and an SH group.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereo marker is the hashed bond from the α-carbon to NH2, so that is the only stereocentre assigned.
- **CIP priorities at the α-carbon:** N > C(CH3)2SH > COOH > H. The C(CH3)2SH carbon outranks COOH because S beats O at the first point of difference.
- **Assigning the descriptor:** NH2 points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Going N (up) → C(CH3)2SH (lower right) → COOH (lower left) looks clockwise. Reversing for the H toward the viewer gives counterclockwise, which is (S), the D-penicillamine configuration.
