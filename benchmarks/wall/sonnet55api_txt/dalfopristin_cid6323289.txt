I read the structure as the dalfopristin-type macrocycle and assigned the stereocentres from the wedges and hashes as drawn.

**Side chain:** a diethylaminoethyl group (Et2N–CH2CH2–) is attached to a sulfone (S(=O)(=O)), which sits on the pyrrolidine ring.

**Pyrrolidine ring:** the ring N is acylated by the oxazole C=O. The sulfonyl-bearing carbon is a hashed stereocentre. The adjacent α-carbon carries a hashed H and is bonded to the ester C(=O)–O.

**Macrocycle, from the ester O onward:**
1. The O-bearing CH carries an isopropyl group on a wedge.
2. Next is a CH(CH3) with the methyl on a wedge.
3. A trans CH=CH follows, then C(=O)–NH.
4. The NH is followed by CH2, then a trans CH=CH, then C(CH3)=CH. At this trisubstituted alkene the chain and the CH(OH) carbon are trans to each other.
5. Next is a CH(OH) with a hashed OH, then CH2, then a ketone C(=O), then CH2.
6. The CH2 leads to the oxazole C2. The ring runs C2=N–C4(=C5H–O), and C4 bears the C(=O) that closes onto the pyrrolidine N.
