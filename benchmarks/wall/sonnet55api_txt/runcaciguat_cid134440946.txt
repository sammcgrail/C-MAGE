I read the molecule as a 3-cyclopropyl-propanoic acid whose C3 carries a 4-chloro-3-amidophenyl ring. The amide nitrogen is acylated by a 2-(4-chlorophenyl)-4,4,4-trifluoro-3-methylbutanoyl group.

**Skeleton**
- The carboxylic acid is HOOC–CH2–CH, and that CH carries a cyclopropyl ring and the aryl ring.
- The aryl ring has the CH attached at one carbon. Going round the ring from there, the next two carbons are CH, then C–Cl, then C–NH, then a CH that closes the ring.
- The amide is NH–C(=O)–CH(4-chlorophenyl)–CH(CH3)–CF3.
