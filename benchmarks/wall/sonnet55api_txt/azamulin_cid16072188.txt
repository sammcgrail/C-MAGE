I read this as a dihydropleuromutilin-type skeleton: the usual tricyclic diterpene core, but with an ethyl group where pleuromutilin has a vinyl group. The C14 ester is a (5-amino-1H-1,2,4-triazol-3-yl)thioacetate.

I did not derive the stereo tags atom by atom from the wedges and hashes. I took the known pleuromutilin stereochemistry, which the drawing appears to match, and changed only the vinyl to ethyl and the glycolate OH to the triazolylthio group.

- **Core:** the quaternary carbon carries the ethyl and a wedged methyl. The adjacent hashed CH–OH connects to the CH bearing a wedged methyl, and the five-membered ring ends in the ketone.
- **Ester:** the wedged O–C(=O)–CH2–S chain sits on the ring carbon next to the hashed methyl-bearing carbon.
- **Triazole:** the sulfur-bearing carbon is flanked by N= and N–N(H). The other ring carbon carries NH2 and sits between NH and N=, so the ring is written Sc1n[nH]c(N)n1.
