This is etravirine, a diarylpyrimidine.

**Pyrimidine ring.** C5 carries Br. C4 carries NH2 and sits between C5 and N3. C2 carries the NH-aryl group and sits between N3 and N1. C6 carries the O-aryl group and sits between N1 and C5.

**Left aryl ring (via O).** The carbon attached to O has a methyl on each neighbouring carbon. The carbon para to O carries the nitrile (C#N). The remaining two ring positions are CH.

**Right aryl ring (via NH).** This is a para-cyanophenyl group.

**SMILES.** Writing the pyrimidine as Nc1nc(Nc2ccc(C#N)cc2)nc(Oc2c(C)cc(C#N)cc2C)c1Br gives the connectivity above.
