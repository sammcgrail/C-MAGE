I read this as a pyrrolobenzodiazepine (PBD) dimer joined by a propane-1,3-diyl dioxy linker. One half carries a 4-methoxyphenyl group. The other half carries a phenyl ring that leads into an Ala–Val dipeptide and a maleimidocaproyl group.

- That amide NH is on the valine α-carbon, which carries an isopropyl group.
- The valine C=O is bonded to the alanine NH, and the alanine C=O is bonded to an anilide NH on a para-substituted phenyl ring.

**Stereocentres in the peptide.** Both α-carbons are drawn with wedges. From the wedge directions I assigned them as L-valine and L-alanine.
