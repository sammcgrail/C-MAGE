The drawing shows diclazuril.

- **Nitrile and central carbon:** a nitrile (N≡C) is attached to a CH carbon. That CH carries a 4-chlorophenyl ring and a 2,6-dichlorophenyl ring.
- **Dichlorophenyl ring:** the carbon bonded to the CH sits between two chlorine-bearing carbons. The carbon para to it is bonded to the triazine nitrogen.
- **Triazine ring:** the ring runs N1 (attached to the aryl group), then N=CH, then C(=O), then NH, then C(=O), closing back to N1. This is a 1,2,4-triazine-3,5-dione.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CH carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
