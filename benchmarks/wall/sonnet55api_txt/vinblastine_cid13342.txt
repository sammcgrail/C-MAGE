I adapted this from the vinblastine skeleton, which this drawing closely resembles, and changed it at the two places where the drawing differs. The stereo descriptors are copied from vinblastine's known SMILES rather than re-derived from each wedge and hash, so they are the least certain part of the answer.

Differences from vinblastine as drawn:
- The lower ester is a free carboxylic acid (C(=O)OH) rather than a methyl ester.
- The aromatic carbon next to the one bearing the lower quaternary carbon carries a bare O⁻ with no methyl, so it is [O-] rather than OMe.

The rest matches the drawing: the vindoline half has an ethyl group, a ring C=C, an N-methyl, an acetoxy, a hydroxyl and a methyl ester. The catharanthine half has an indole with a CH2CH2N bridge and a tertiary alcohol bearing an ethyl group.
