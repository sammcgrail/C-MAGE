The drawing is naloxone hydrochloride. It shows the morphinan skeleton with these features:

- an N-allyl group on the nitrogen
- a phenol OH on the aromatic ring
- the furan-type ether oxygen bridge
- a ketone in the cyclohexanone ring
- a tertiary OH at the ring-fusion carbon, drawn with a hashed bond
- HCl as a separate component

There are four stereocentres, each drawn with a hashed bond. I took the stereo descriptors from the known (–)-naloxone structure rather than deriving them from the wedge geometry in the image.
