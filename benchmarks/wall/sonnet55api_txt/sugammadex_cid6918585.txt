The drawing is a cyclodextrin with eight glucose units in the ring. Each unit carries a –CH2–S–CH2CH2–COOH group at C6, and I counted eight such sulfur chains. This matches the γ-cyclodextrin derivative sugammadex, a per-6-deoxy-6-(2-carboxyethylthio) γ-cyclodextrin.

I wrote the SMILES largely from recall of the sugammadex structure rather than deriving each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes. I checked the connectivity (eight units, eight thioether acid chains) against the image. I did not verify the stereo descriptors centre by centre.
