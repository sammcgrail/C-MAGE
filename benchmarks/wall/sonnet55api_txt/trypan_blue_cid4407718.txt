I read this as the trypan blue dianion skeleton, here drawn as the tetrasulfonate. It has a 3,3'-dimethylbiphenyl-4,4'-diyl core with an azo group (N=N) on each para position and a methyl ortho to each azo group.

Each azo group links to a naphthalene unit. On each naphthalene, working outward from the azo-bearing carbon:
- The carbon next to the azo-bearing carbon carries a sulfonate (SO3⁻).
- The other neighbour carries OH. Beyond it, across the ring fusion, the peri carbon carries NH2.
- A second sulfonate sits on the other ring, meta to the NH2 carbon.

The SMILES below was written from this reading and checked ring by ring against the drawing. The methyl on each biphenyl ring is ortho to the azo group.
