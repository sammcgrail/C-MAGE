This is stanozolol, a 17α-methyl-17β-hydroxy androstane steroid with a pyrazole fused to the A ring.

- **Pyrazole ring:** it has an N= at the top, bonded to a CH=, and an NH at the bottom. The NH sits on the carbon next to the CH2 that links to C5, and the CH= sits on the carbon next to the CH2 that links to C10.
- **D ring:** it carries a tertiary carbon with OH and a hashed methyl, which gives 17α-methyl and 17β-OH.
- **Methyl groups:** there is a wedged methyl at C13 and a wedged methyl at C10.
- **Ring-fusion hydrogens:** the H atoms at the ring fusions are drawn with wedges or hashes in the standard steroid arrangement.

I wrote the stereocentres from my recollection of the known stanozolol SMILES and only partly checked them against the wedges and hashes in the drawing.
