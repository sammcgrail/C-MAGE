The drawing shows hydroxyurea. Reading it from left to right:

- An HO group is attached to an NH.
- That NH is bonded to a central carbon, which carries a double-bonded O below it.
- The same carbon is also bonded to an NH2 on the right.

Written as SMILES, this is a hydroxylamine nitrogen joined to a urea carbonyl: ON–C(=O)–N, giving ONC(N)=O. The structure has no stereochemistry.
