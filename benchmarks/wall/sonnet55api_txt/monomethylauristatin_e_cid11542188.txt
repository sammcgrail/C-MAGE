The drawing matches monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE). I read it as five connected units, left to right:

- **Norephedrine unit:** a phenyl ring on a carbon bearing OH (hashed bond), then a carbon with a methyl (wedge) and an amide NH.
- **Dolaproine unit:** a methyl-bearing carbon (hashed methyl) next to the amide C=O, then a methoxy-bearing carbon (hashed O) attached to the 2-position of a pyrrolidine ring.
- **Dolaisoleuine unit:** the pyrrolidine nitrogen acylated by a C(=O)CH2 group, then a methoxy-bearing carbon, then a carbon carrying an N-methyl amide nitrogen (wedge) and a sec-butyl group (ethyl plus wedged methyl).
- **Valine unit:** an isopropyl-bearing carbon (hashed NH) with a C=O linked to the N-methyl nitrogen.
- **N-methylvaline unit:** a terminal isopropyl-bearing carbon (hashed NHMe) linked to the valine NH through its C=O.

I wrote the stereodescriptors from the known MMAE structure rather than deriving each one from the wedges and hashes, so they are not independently checked against the drawing.
