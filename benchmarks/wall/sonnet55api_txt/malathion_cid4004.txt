The drawing shows malathion: a diethyl succinate backbone with a dimethyl dithiophosphate group attached through sulfur.

Reading the structure region by region:
- Left side: an ethyl group (CH3-CH2) bonded to O, which is bonded to a C=O carbon. This carbon connects to a CH2, which connects to a CH.
- The CH carries the S-P(=S)(OCH3)2 group.
- The same CH also bonds to a second C(=O)O-ethyl group, on the lower right.
- The phosphorus has a double bond to S, two single-bonded OCH3 groups, and a single bond to the bridging S.

The drawing has plain bonds only, so the CH stereocentre gets no stereo marking.
