The drawing shows magnesium bis(esomeprazole), i.e. two identical 5-methoxybenzimidazolide–sulfinyl–pyridine units with one Mg²⁺.

**Each ligand:**
- **Pyridine ring:** the CH2 on the carbon next to N, then methyl, methoxy and methyl on the next three ring carbons, and a CH next to the methyl at the far end of the ring.
- **Linker:** the CH2 connects to a sulfoxide S(=O), which is bonded to C2 of the benzimidazole.
- **Benzimidazole:** one ring nitrogen carries the negative charge ([n-]). The benzo ring has an OCH3 group.

**Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the sulfur. The wedge to the CH2 puts that group toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are O > C2 of the benzimidazole > CH2 > lone pair. Viewed from the front, O → C2 → CH2 runs counterclockwise, so the sulfur is S, as in esomeprazole. In SMILES, written as `c(...)[S@@](=O)Cc`, this corresponds to `@@`.
