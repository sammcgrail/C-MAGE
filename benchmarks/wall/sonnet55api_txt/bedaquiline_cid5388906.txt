This molecule is bedaquiline, read from the drawing as follows.

**Ring systems**
- A quinoline carries Br on the benzo ring. Its C2 bears OMe and its C3 is the point of attachment.
- A phenyl ring and a 1-naphthyl ring are the other two aromatic groups.

**Chain**
- The quinoline C3 is bonded to a CH carbon, which also carries the phenyl ring (hashed bond).
- That CH is bonded to a quaternary carbinol carbon. This carbon carries OH (wedge), the 1-naphthyl ring, and a CH2CH2N(CH3)2 chain.

**Stereochemistry**
- I worked out the two stereocentres from the wedge and hash bonds. The CH centre, with the phenyl hashed, is written `[C@@H]` in the SMILES below.
- The carbinol carbon, with OH wedged, is written `[C@@]`.
