This is my best reading of the drawing. I matched the connectivity to the picture, but I recalled the stereodescriptors from the known compound rather than deriving each one from the wedges and hashes, so they are unverified.

The molecule is a 2-deoxystreptamine core (a cyclohexane carrying two NH2 groups and one OH) with two sugar rings attached through ether oxygens:

- **Left ring:** a pyranose with a ring oxygen, two OH groups, one NH2 and a CH2OH. This is a 3-amino-3-deoxyglucose unit.
- **Right ring:** a tetrahydropyran with a ring oxygen, an NH2 on the carbon next to the anomeric carbon, two CH2 groups, and a CH2NH2 on the carbon next to the ring oxygen. This is a 2,6-diamino-2,3,4,6-tetradeoxyglucose unit.
- **Core attachment:** the right-ring oxygen sits on a core carbon between a C–NH2 and a C–OH. The left-ring oxygen sits on a core carbon between the other C–NH2 and the same C–OH.

This connectivity matches dibekacin (3',4'-dideoxykanamycin B).
