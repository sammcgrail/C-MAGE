The drawing shows leucine: an isobutyl group (CH3)2CH–CH2– attached to the α-carbon, which carries an NH2 on a wedge bond and a carboxylic acid, C(=O)OH.

For the stereocentre, the CIP priorities are NH2 > COOH > CH2 > H. The wedge puts NH2 toward the viewer, so the implied H points away. Going NH2 (bottom), then COOH (upper right), then CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. This is L-leucine.

The SMILES CC(C)C[C@H](N)C(=O)O encodes the S configuration, matching the standard L-leucine SMILES.
