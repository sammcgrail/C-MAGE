The drawing shows an aza-epothilone: a 16-membered macrolactam ring with these features:

- a gem-dimethyl carbon next to a ketone
- a CH(CH3) bearing the ketone neighbour, then a CH(OH), then another CH(CH3)
- a three-carbon CH2 chain leading to a methyl-substituted epoxide
- a CH2 and an N-bound CH carrying the 2-methylthiazol-4-yl vinyl side chain
- a secondary alcohol next to the lactam C(=O)–NH
