I read this as indacaterol, with the (R) configuration at the single stereocentre.

- **Left fragment:** a benzene ring carrying two ethyl groups on adjacent carbons, fused to a five-membered ring (an indane). The indane's C2 carbon bears the secondary amine (NH).
- **Linker:** NH–CH2–CH(OH)–, where the CH(OH) carbon is drawn with a wedged OH.
- **Right fragment:** a quinolin-2(1H)-one. Its ring nitrogen is NH, and C2 is a C=O. C8 carries an OH, and C5 is attached to the CH(OH) carbon.

For the stereocentre, the OH is wedged toward the viewer and the implicit H points away. Taking the CIP priorities as OH > CH2N > aryl, the sequence OH → CH2 → aryl runs clockwise, which gives R. In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]` written with the CH2 as the preceding atom and then aryl and OH encodes that same R arrangement.
