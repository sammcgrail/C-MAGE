The structure is fulvestrant, an estradiol core with a 7α side chain. I read it region by region from the drawing.

- **Steroid core:** The A ring is aromatic and carries a phenolic OH on the carbon next to C4. Rings B, C and D follow, with a 17-OH on the five-membered D ring. A methyl at C13 is drawn as a wedge, and the ring-fusion hydrogens at C8, C9 and C14 are drawn as wedges or hashes.
- **Side chain:** C7 carries a hashed bond to a chain of nine CH2 groups. The chain ends in a sulfoxide S(=O), then three more CH2 groups, then CF2 and CF3.
- **Stereochemistry:** The core and 17-OH wedges and hashes match the natural estradiol configuration, so I used the estradiol SMILES pattern for those centres. For C7 I worked out the chirality directly from the hashed chain bond, with the implicit H pointing toward the viewer. That gives `@` in the order written below.

The sulfur is also a stereocentre, but it is drawn without a wedge, so I left it unspecified.
