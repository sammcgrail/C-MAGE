I read the drawing as cisapride, with a 4-amino-5-chloro-2-methoxybenzamide joined through its amide NH to a substituted piperidine.

- **Aromatic acid part:** The benzene ring carries the amide C(=O) at one carbon. Its neighbours are OMe at the ortho position, then NH2, then Cl on the far side of the ring.
- **Piperidine:** The ring has an N-substituent and two substituted carbons. C4 bears the amide NH on a wedge. C3 bears OMe on a wedge, so the two substituents are cis.
- **N-substituent:** This is a three-carbon chain (CH2CH2CH2) leading to an ether O and a 4-fluorophenyl ring.
