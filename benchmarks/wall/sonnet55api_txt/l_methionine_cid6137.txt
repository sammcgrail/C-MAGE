The structure is methionine: CH3–S–CH2–CH2–CH(NH2)–COOH, with a thioether sulfur and a carboxylic acid (C=O and OH).

The only stereocentre is the α-carbon, and the NH2 is on a wedge, so stereochemistry is included. The CIP priorities there are N > COOH > CH2 > H. With the wedged N toward the viewer, H points away. Going N (bottom) → COOH (upper right) → CH2 (upper left) is counterclockwise, which gives S. This matches L-methionine, which is written `CSCC[C@H](N)C(=O)O`.
