The drawing is ropivacaine, (S)-N-(2,6-dimethylphenyl)-1-propylpiperidine-2-carboxamide.

- **Left ring:** a piperidine (one N and five carbons) with an N-propyl chain running down from the nitrogen.
- **Stereocentre:** the ring carbon next to N carries the amide carbon C(=O) on a wedge bond.
- **Right side:** the C(=O) is bonded to an NH, which is attached to a benzene ring. That ring has methyl groups on both carbons adjacent to the N-bearing carbon (2,6-dimethylphenyl).

For the stereocentre, the priorities are N > C(=O) > ring CH2 > H. The wedge puts the amide carbon toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away. On the page, N (south) → C(=O) (east) → CH2 (northwest) runs counterclockwise, which gives S. Writing the SMILES as `CCCN1CCCC[C@H]1C(=O)N...` and working through the neighbour order gives S for `@`, so `@` is correct here.
