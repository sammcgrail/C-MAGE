This structure is a phenothiazine, which is the drug thioproperazine-like scaffold carrying a piperidine side chain (it resembles pipotiazine).

Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Sulfonamide:** the dimethylamino group N(C)C is bonded to S(=O)(=O), and that sulfur is attached to the aromatic carbon at the upper left of the left ring.
- **Phenothiazine core:** the left benzene ring has a carbon bearing the sulfonamide, a CH next to it, and then the carbon attached to the ring nitrogen N10. The other fusion carbon is attached to S. The right benzene ring is unsubstituted.
- **Linker:** the ring nitrogen carries a three-carbon chain (CCC) that ends at the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Piperidine:** the ring has a 2-hydroxyethyl substituent (CCO) at the 4-position.
