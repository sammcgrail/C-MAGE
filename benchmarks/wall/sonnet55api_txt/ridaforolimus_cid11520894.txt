I identified it from the overall skeleton rather than tracing every stereocentre from the wedges, so the stereodescriptors come from my recollection of the rapamycin/ridaforolimus SMILES and are not checked against the drawing atom by atom.

**What the drawing shows**
- A 31-membered macrolactone ring with a trans-fused tetrahydropyran (bearing a methyl) carrying a hemiketal OH.
- A vicinal tricarbonyl region (the amide C=O and the adjacent ketone) linking the pipecolate nitrogen to the hemiketal carbon.
- A piperidine (pipecolic acid) ring closed through the ester O–C(=O) and the ring N.
- A conjugated triene, several ring methyl and methoxy groups, two ring ketones, and one secondary OH.
- A side chain running from the ester oxygen through CH(CH3) and CH2 to a cyclohexane ring. That ring carries an OMe and an O–P(=O)(CH3)2 group.
