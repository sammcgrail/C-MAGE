This structure is ibogaine, the 10-methoxy ibogamine skeleton, and I read the stereochemistry from the four wedge/hash bonds in the drawing.

**Skeleton**
- A 5-methoxyindole unit sits on the right: a benzene ring carrying OMe, fused to a pyrrole ring with an NH.
- The indole C3 carries a CH2CH2 chain that leads to the tertiary amine N.
- The indole C2 is bonded to a CH, which I call C_d. This carbon carries a hashed H.
- C_d also bonds to a CH2 and to the CH next to N, which I call C_b. C_b has a hashed H.
- The CH2 leads to C_c, a bridgehead CH with a wedged H. C_c is bonded to a CH2 that runs to C_a and to another CH2 that goes to N, giving the isoquinuclidine cage.
- C_a is a CH with a hashed H and an ethyl group. It is bonded to C_b.
