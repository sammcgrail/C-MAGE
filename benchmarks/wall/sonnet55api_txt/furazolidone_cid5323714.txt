**Nitrofuran side.** The furan ring has O at the bottom of the upper left, a nitro group (N+ with =O and O−) on the carbon next to the ring O, and the CH=N chain on the other carbon next to the ring O. The ring double bonds are C(NO2)=CH and CH=C(CH=N).

**Linker and oxazolidinone.** The chain runs ring C – CH=N – N(ring). The oxazolidinone ring is N–C(=O)–O–CH2–CH2, closing back to N.

The N at (945, 779) carries the other N at (1100, 789) to the right. The C=N axis runs from lower left to upper right. The furan carbon sits on the left side of the axis, and the N substituent on the right. Checking which side of the axis each lies on:

- Axis direction: from C (845, 922) to N (945, 779), so d = (100, −143).
