Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left end:** a para-substituted benzene ring carrying a nitro group, drawn as [N+](=O)[O-].
- **Linker to the central nitrogen:** from the ring, a chain of three CH2 groups (propyl) leads to a tertiary nitrogen.
- **Central nitrogen substituents:** besides the propyl chain, it carries a CH2CH2OH group and a CH2CH2NH group.
- **Right end:** the NH is attached to C6 of a uracil ring.
  - The ring runs N1(Me)–C2(=O)–N3(Me)–C4(=O)–C5H=C6.
  - The N bearing the lower methyl is bonded to C6 and to the lower C=O.
  - The N bearing the upper methyl is bonded to both carbonyl carbons.
  - So this is a 6-amino-1,3-dimethyluracil.
