I read the drawing as a fused isoxazolo–benzodiazepine core with these parts:

- A 3-methylisoxazole ring (C–CH3, then N, O) is fused to a seven-membered ring containing one imine N.
- A benzene ring is fused to the seven-membered ring.
- The imine carbon carries a 4-chlorophenyl group.
- The sp3 CH next to the imine N carries a CH2C(=O)NH2 side chain.
- A separate H2O is drawn as a hydrate.

The only stereocentre is the CH bearing the side chain. The CH2 of the side chain is on a hashed bond, so it points behind the plane and the implicit H points toward the viewer. Viewed from the H, the order CH2 → N → isoxazole C runs clockwise. In the SMILES neighbour order (CH2, H, isoxazole C, N) this corresponds to `@@`.

SMILES: NC(=O)C[C@@H]1N=C(c2ccc(Cl)cc2)c2ccccc2c2c(C)noc12.O
