I read the molecule as a macrocyclic bisindolylmaleimide, close to the ruboxistaurin type.

- **Core:** a maleimide ring (C(=O)NC(=O) with a C=C between the two carbons) carries an indol-3-yl group on each alkene carbon.
- **Left indole:** its nitrogen carries the chain N–CH2–CH2–O.
- **Right indole:** its nitrogen carries the chain N–CH2–CH2–CH(…)–.
- **Macrocycle closure:** the CH bearing the oxygen of the first chain is the same CH that ends the second chain, and it also carries a CH2–N(CH3)2 side group.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CH2NMe2 bond is hashed. The substituents rank O > CH2N > CH2C > H. With the hashed CH2N pointing away, H points toward the viewer. On the page, O, CH2N and CH2C run clockwise, so the real configuration is reversed, giving S. In the SMILES, with neighbours written in the order CH2N, H, ring-closure to O, CH2, this corresponds to `@@`.

SMILES: CN(C)C[C@@H]1CCn2cc(c3ccccc32)C2=C(C(=O)NC2=O)c2cn(CCO1)c1ccccc21
