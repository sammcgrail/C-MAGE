The drawing shows testosterone undecanoate: the testosterone steroid core esterified at the 17-oxygen with a straight-chain undecanoyl group.

- **Ester chain:** the carbonyl carbon plus ten more carbons to the left makes an 11-carbon (undecanoyl) acyl chain, attached through the ester O to C17.
- **Steroid core:** the four fused rings carry an enone in ring A (C=O at C3 with the C4=C5 double bond). There are two methyl groups, one at C10 and one at C13.
- **Stereochemistry:** the wedges and hashes on C13 methyl, C10 methyl, C17–O and the ring-fusion hydrogens are all drawn. In this image the hash/wedge pattern looks flipped relative to the usual steroid drawing, which is consistent with the same molecule drawn in a different orientation. I therefore wrote the natural testosterone configuration (17β-O, 13β-methyl, 10β-methyl). I took this from the known PubChem SMILES for testosterone undecanoate rather than deriving each centre from the wedges.
