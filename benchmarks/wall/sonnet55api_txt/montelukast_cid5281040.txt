This structure is montelukast, and I read it region by region.

- **Left side:** a benzene ring is ortho-substituted with a C(CH3)2OH group (the HO-bearing carbon with two methyls) and a CH2CH2 chain.
- **Stereocentre:** the chain leads to a carbon bearing S, a 1,3-disubstituted benzene ring and an implicit H. The bond to the CH2 is hashed.
- **Sulfur side chain:** S–CH2–(cyclopropane-1,1-diyl)–CH2–COOH.
- **Right side:** the 1,3-disubstituted benzene carries a trans CH=CH linked to C2 of a 7-chloroquinoline.

For the stereochemistry, the priorities at the stereocentre are S, then aryl, then CH2, then H. The CH2 is hashed (pointing away), so H points toward the viewer. S (lower left), aryl (lower right) and CH2 (top) run counterclockwise from the front. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving R.

In the SMILES I write the neighbours of the stereocentre in the order CH2, H, S, aryl. For R in that order, the tag is @@.

SMILES: CC(C)(O)c1ccccc1CC[C@@H](SCC2(CC2)CC(=O)O)c1cccc(c1)/C=C/c1ccc2ccc(Cl)cc2n1
