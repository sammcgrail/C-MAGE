The drawing shows a phosphatidylcholine with two saturated acyl chains, which I read as distearoyl phosphatidylcholine (DSPC).

- **Head group:** a trimethylammonium, N⁺(CH₃)₃, is attached through a CH₂CH₂ link to an oxygen on the phosphorus. The phosphorus carries one P=O, one O⁻ and two ester oxygens.
- **Glycerol backbone:** the phosphate is bonded to a CH₂ of a three-carbon glycerol unit. The central CH and the other CH₂ each carry an acyloxy group (–O–C(=O)–chain).
- **Left chain:** counting vertices from the carbonyl carbon to the terminal methyl gives 18 carbons, so it is a stearoyl group.
- **Right chain:** the same count gives 18 carbons, another stearoyl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** the central glycerol carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I give no stereo marks.
