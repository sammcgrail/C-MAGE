The drawing is a steroid skeleton with a 3-keto-4,6-diene in the A/B rings (the C=O on the right, two ring C=C bonds along the bottom). Two methyl groups sit at the ring junctions (C10 and C13), and a spiro γ-lactone (O–C(=O)–CH2–CH2) is attached at C17. This is canrenone.

The structure is drawn flipped vertically relative to the usual steroid orientation. Inverting the wedges accordingly (10-methyl and 13-methyl β, 9-H α, 8-H β, 14-H α) gives the natural steroid configuration. I took the stereo descriptors from the known canrenone SMILES rather than deriving each centre independently.
