This is mebeverine hydrochloride.

Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Amine end:** an ethyl group and a CH(CH3) group are both attached to the nitrogen, which also carries the butyl chain.
- **CH(CH3) branch:** this carbon continues through a CH2 to a 4-methoxyphenyl ring.
- **Butyl chain:** N–CH2–CH2–CH2–CH2–O. Counting the vertices from N gives four carbons before the ester oxygen (N, then 477/520, 541/610, 656/600, 722/692, then O).
- **Ester:** the oxygen links to a C(=O) attached to a benzene ring carrying two methoxy groups (3,4-dimethoxybenzoate).
- **Salt:** HCl is drawn separately.

The stereocentre at the CH(CH3) carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
