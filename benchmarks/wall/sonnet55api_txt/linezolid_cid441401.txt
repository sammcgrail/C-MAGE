This structure is linezolid, the (S)-oxazolidinone antibiotic.

- **Left side:** an acetamide (CH3–C(=O)–NH–) is attached through a CH2 to the oxazolidinone ring.
- **Oxazolidinone ring:** a five-membered ring containing O, C(=O) and N. The ring carbon bearing the CH2NHAc group is the only stereocentre. Its bond to the CH2NHAc carbon is drawn as a hash.
- **Aryl group:** the ring nitrogen is bonded to a benzene ring. That ring has a fluorine on one carbon and, on the neighbouring carbon, a morpholine nitrogen. The morpholine is a six-membered ring with N and O opposite each other.

For the stereocentre, the CIP priorities are ring O > ring CH2 (leading to N) > CH2NHAc > H. With the hashed CH2NHAc pointing away, the implicit H points toward the viewer. The sequence O → ring CH2 → CH2NHAc runs clockwise on the page. Reversing that for the H pointing toward the viewer gives S.
