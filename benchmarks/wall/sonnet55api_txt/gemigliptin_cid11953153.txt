I read the molecule as a sitagliptin-like amide, built from three parts:

1. **Left ring:** a 5,5-difluoropiperidin-2-one. The ring runs N–C(=O)–CH2–CH2–CF2–CH2 back to N.
2. **Linker:** the lactam nitrogen carries CH2–CH(NH2)–CH2–C(=O), and the carbonyl is bonded to a ring nitrogen on the right.
3. **Right ring system:** a 5,6,7,8-tetrahydropyrido[4,3-d]pyrimidine. Its amide nitrogen is joined to two CH2 groups, one leading to the aromatic carbon next to the CF3-bearing carbon and one leading to the carbon next to a ring nitrogen. The pyrimidine ring has CF3 groups on both carbons that lie between its two nitrogens and the fused carbons.

The only stereocentre is the CH bearing NH2, drawn with a hashed bond to the NH2 (pointing away from the viewer). Written in the order preceding CH2, H, N, following CH2, this gives [C@@H].
