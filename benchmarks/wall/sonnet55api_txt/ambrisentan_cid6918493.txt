This molecule is ambrisentan, (S)-2-(4,6-dimethylpyrimidin-2-yloxy)-3-methoxy-3,3-diphenylpropanoic acid.

**Reading the structure**
- The left ring is a pyrimidine with methyl groups at positions 4 and 6, joined through its C2 to an ether oxygen.
- That oxygen is attached by a wedge bond to the central CH carbon.
- This CH carbon also carries a carboxylic acid (C(=O)OH) and a quaternary carbon.
- The quaternary carbon bears two phenyl rings and an OCH3 group.

**Stereochemistry**
- The wedge oxygen points toward the viewer, so the implicit hydrogen points away.
- The CIP priorities at the stereocentre are O > COOH (O,O,O) > C(Ph)2OMe (O,C,C) > H.
- With H at the back, O (upper left) → COOH (down) → quaternary C (upper right) runs counterclockwise on the page, so the centre is S.
