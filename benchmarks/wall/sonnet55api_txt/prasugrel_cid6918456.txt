The drawing is prasugrel, which I read region by region as follows.

- **Acetate:** a methyl group on a C(=O) that is bonded to an O, giving CC(=O)O–.
- **Thiophene ring:** the acetate O is on a thiophene carbon. That carbon is bonded to S and, through a double bond, to a CH. The CH connects to a ring-fusion carbon, which is double-bonded to the other fusion carbon, and that carbon is bonded to S.
- **Saturated ring:** one fusion carbon connects through CH2 to N. The other fusion carbon connects through CH2–CH2 to N.
- **N substituent:** N is bonded to a CH that carries the 2-fluorophenyl group and a C(=O)-cyclopropyl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** all bonds are plain, so no stereo is assigned.
