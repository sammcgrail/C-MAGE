The drawing shows three components: frovatriptan, succinic acid and one water molecule.

- **Frovatriptan:** a tetrahydrocarbazole (an indole fused to a cyclohexane ring) with a carboxamide on the benzene ring and an NHMe group on the saturated ring.
- **Stereocentre:** the carbon bearing NHMe has a hashed bond to N, so N points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. Seen from the H side, the ring CH2 on the right, the CH2 on the upper left and N run counterclockwise. That corresponds to `CN[C@@H]1...C1` in the SMILES atom order. I assigned this by hand and did not run it through a toolkit.
- **Succinic acid:** HOOC-CH2-CH2-COOH.
- **Water:** H2O.
