I read this as a doxorubicin-type anthracycline whose sugar nitrogen carries a fused methoxy-oxazolidine/morpholine ring system.

**Aglycone**
- The tetracyclic core has a 4-methoxy group on the terminal benzene ring.
- Both quinone C=O groups are present, and the two phenol OH groups sit on the middle ring.
- C9 carries a wedged OH and a C(=O)CH2OH side chain.
- C7 carries the wedged glycosidic O.

**Sugar and fused rings**
- The sugar is a pyranose with the anomeric carbon (C1′) bonded to the glycosidic O, plus a hashed C5′ methyl.
- C3′ is bonded to N, and C4′ is bonded to an oxazolidine O.
- That oxazolidine O leads to a carbon bonded to N and to a CH(OMe), which closes through O–CH2–CH2 back to N, forming the morpholine ring.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned each centre from the drawn wedges and hashes by working out the geometry of its four neighbours.
