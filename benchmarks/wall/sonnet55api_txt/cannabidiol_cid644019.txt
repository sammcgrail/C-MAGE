This drawing is cannabidiol (CBD): a 5-pentylresorcinol ring joined to a 4-isopropenyl-1-methylcyclohexene ring.

**Regions read from the image**
- **Chain:** a plain pentyl chain (five carbons) is attached to the aromatic ring.
- **Aromatic ring:** six carbons, with an OH on each side of the carbon that carries the cyclohexene ring (a resorcinol pattern).
- **Cyclohexene ring:** one ring C=C bearing a methyl group, and two adjacent stereocentres.
- **Isopropenyl group:** a C(CH3)=CH2 group attached to the second stereocentre.

**Stereochemistry**
- The hashed bond from the aryl carbon to the first ring stereocentre puts the aryl group behind the plane. I assumed the narrow end of the hash sits at the stereocentre, as is usual. This gives `[C@@H]` for that centre.
- The wedge from the second ring stereocentre to the isopropenyl carbon puts that group toward the viewer. This gives `[C@H]` for that centre.
