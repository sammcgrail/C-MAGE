I read this as leucine with every carbon-bound hydrogen replaced by deuterium (leucine-d10). The nitrogen and the acid OH are drawn as NH2 and OH, so they stay protium.

- **Backbone:** the alpha carbon carries NH2, COOH, a hashed ²H, and a CD2 group.
- **Side chain:** the CD2 is bonded to a CD carbon, which bears two CD3 groups. That gives an isobutyl side chain, so the amino acid is leucine.
- **Deuterium count:** 1 on the alpha carbon, 2 on CD2, 1 on CD and 6 on the two CD3 groups, which totals 10.
- **Stereochemistry:** the ²H on the alpha carbon is on a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer. With the CIP priorities N > COOH > CH2 > ²H, the sequence N → COOH → CH2 runs counterclockwise. That makes the centre S, the same as L-leucine.
- **SMILES:** in L-leucine written as `CC(C)C[C@H](N)C(=O)O`, replacing the implicit H with `[2H]` keeps the neighbour order, so `[C@]([2H])(N)` keeps the S configuration.

SMILES: [2H]C([2H])([2H])C([2H])(C([2H])([2H])[2H])C([2H])([2H])[C@]([2H])(N)C(=O)O
