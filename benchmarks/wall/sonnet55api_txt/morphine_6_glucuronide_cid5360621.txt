This is morphine-6-glucuronide: a morphine core carrying a glucuronic acid unit on the C6 oxygen. I assigned the stereochemistry by matching the drawing to the natural morphine skeleton and a β-D-glucuronide. I did not derive each wedge and hash independently, and the SMILES below is from memory of the PubChem entry.

- **Aromatic ring:** a phenol OH sits on the aromatic ring next to the furan-type ether oxygen.
- **Morphinan core:** the ether oxygen bridges to the carbon that carries the hashed H. A CH=CH double bond runs through the cyclohexene ring. The N-methyl nitrogen is bridged to the carbon bearing the hashed H at the bottom, and the atom labelled "C" is the CH2 linking that carbon to the aromatic ring.
- **Glucuronide:** the C6 oxygen is bonded to the anomeric carbon of a pyranose ring. That ring carries three OH groups and a terminal COOH, which makes it glucuronic acid.
