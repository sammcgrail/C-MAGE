I read the molecule as a cyclohexylmethyl group on a stereocentre that also carries a 2-pyridyl group and an NH. That NH links to C2 of a 5-methylbenzoxazole.

- **Left side:** a cyclohexane ring is attached to a CH2, and a wedge bond runs from the stereocentre to this CH2.
- **Stereocentre:** it bears the CH2 (wedge), a pyridin-2-yl group (N in the ring next to the attachment carbon), an NH and an implicit H.
- **Right side:** the NH is bonded to C2 of the benzoxazole. The ring O is attached to the fused carbon at the top and the ring N to the fused carbon at the bottom. Counting from the fused carbon next to N, the methyl sits on the third ring carbon, which is position 5.

For the stereo, I put the implicit H at the back, since the CH2 is wedged toward the viewer. Viewed from the front, the order CH2 → pyridyl → N runs clockwise. Written as `C[C@@H](pyridyl)N` with the CH2 preceding the centre, this corresponds to `@@`.
