The drawing shows a steroid skeleton of three six-membered rings and one five-membered ring, with a pentanoic acid side chain carrying a methyl group. It has hydroxyls at C3, C7 and C12 and two angular methyl groups. This is cholic acid (3α,7α,12α-trihydroxy-5β-cholan-24-oic acid).

I did not derive the @/@@ marks atom by atom from the wedges. I took them from the standard cholic acid SMILES, after checking that the drawing agrees with its stereochemistry:
- The three hydroxyls are all hashed (α).
- The H at C5 is wedged (β).
- The H at C8 is wedged (β).
- The H at C9 is hashed (α).
- The H at C14 is hashed (α).
- The two angular methyls and the side-chain methyl are wedged.

I could not fully make sense of the wedge at the C16–C17 position in the drawing, so that one point rests on the match to cholic acid rather than on a direct reading.
