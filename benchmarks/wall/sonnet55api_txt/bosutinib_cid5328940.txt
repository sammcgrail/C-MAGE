This is bosutinib.

Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Quinoline core:** the ring nitrogen sits at the top and is bonded to a CH. That CH connects to the carbon bearing the nitrile (C#N), which connects to the carbon bearing NH (C4).
- **Benzo ring substituents:** the carbon at position 7 carries the OCH2CH2CH2-N-methylpiperazine chain. The adjacent carbon at position 6 carries the OMe.
- **Aniline ring:** the NH-bearing carbon is flanked by a Cl-bearing carbon on one side and a CH on the other. Going around from the NH carbon: C1 (NH), C2 (Cl), C3 (H), C4 (Cl), C5 (OMe), C6 (H). This gives the 2,4-dichloro-5-methoxyphenyl group.
- **Side chain:** propoxy linker to the piperazine N, with the other piperazine N carrying a methyl.
