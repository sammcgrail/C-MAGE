The drawing shows dexrazoxane: two piperazine-2,6-dione rings, each joined through its ring nitrogen to a CH(CH3)–CH2 linker.

- **Rings:** each ring has a ring N, two C=O groups flanking an NH, and two CH2 groups. This gives N1CC(=O)NC(=O)C1 for each ring.
- **Linker:** one ring N is bonded to the stereocentre CH(CH3), and the other ring N is bonded to the adjacent CH2.
- **Stereocentre:** the methyl group is drawn with a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > CH2N > CH3 > H.
  - On the page, N (upper left) → CH2 (right) → CH3 (lower left) runs clockwise.
  - Because H faces the viewer, the sense reverses, so the centre is S.
- **SMILES chirality:** for the written order C[C?H](CN…)N…, the symbol @@ corresponds to S.
