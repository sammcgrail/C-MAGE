I read this as zimelidine, (Z)-3-(4-bromophenyl)-N,N-dimethyl-3-(pyridin-3-yl)prop-2-en-1-amine.

- **Amine end:** an N,N-dimethylamino group is attached to a CH2, which is bonded to the alkene CH.
- **Alkene:** the CH= is double-bonded to a carbon that carries two aryl groups.
- **Aryl groups:** one is a 4-bromophenyl ring, with Br para to the attachment point. The other is a pyridine ring attached at its 3-position, so the ring nitrogen is separated from the attachment carbon by one CH.
- **Geometry:** from the drawn coordinates, the CH2N group lies on the same side of the double bond as the pyridyl ring and opposite the bromophenyl ring. The pyridyl carbon next to the ring nitrogen outranks the bromophenyl carbons in CIP priority, so CH2N and the higher-priority pyridyl are cis, which is the Z isomer.

In the SMILES, `C/C=C(\c1cccnc1)` puts the pyridyl cis to the CH2.

SMILES: CN(C)C/C=C(\c1cccnc1)c1ccc(Br)cc1
