I read the molecule as a symmetric bis-oxime: two HO–N=C(CH3)–CH(CH3)–NH– units joined through a CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2 linker.

**Connectivity**
- Left end: HO–N=C(CH3)–CH(CH3)–NH–
- Centre: –CH2–C(CH3)2–CH2–
- Right end: –NH–CH(CH3)–C(CH3)=N–OH

**Stereochemistry**
- The two oxime C=N bonds are drawn without cis/trans geometry, so I left them unspecified.
- Each CH(CH3) carbon carries a hashed methyl, so both are stereocentres.
- At each one the priorities are N > C(=N) > CH3 > H, and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- Reading the drawing from the front, the sequence N → C(=N) → CH3 runs counterclockwise at both centres. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is clockwise, so both centres are R.
- In the SMILES below, the left centre needs `@@` and the right centre needs `@` to give R at each.

SMILES: ON=C(C)[C@@H](C)NCC(C)(C)CN[C@H](C)C(C)=NO
