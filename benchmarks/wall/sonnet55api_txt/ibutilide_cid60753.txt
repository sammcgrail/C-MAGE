I read the structure in three parts:

- **Amine end:** the nitrogen carries an ethyl group and an unbranched chain of 7 carbons (heptyl), counted vertex by vertex from the nitrogen to the chain end.
- **Linker:** from the nitrogen, 4 more carbons run toward the ring. The fourth carbon bears the OH and is bonded directly to the benzene ring. It is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo marker.
- **Ring end:** the benzene ring is para-substituted. The other substituent is NH–S(=O)(=O)–CH3, a methanesulfonamide.
