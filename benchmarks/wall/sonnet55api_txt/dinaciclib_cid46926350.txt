I read the molecule as a pyrazolo[1,5-a]pyrimidine core with three substituents:

- **C3:** an ethyl group.
- **C5:** a piperidine nitrogen. The piperidine carbon next to that nitrogen carries a 2-hydroxyethyl chain, drawn on a hashed bond.
- **C7:** an NH–CH2 linker to the 3-position of a pyridine N-oxide, drawn as N⁺–O⁻.

For the one stereocentre (the piperidine C2), the hashed bond points the hydroxyethyl chain away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Viewed from the H side, the ring CH2, ring N and the chain run clockwise. In the SMILES neighbour order (preceding ring CH2, H, N, chain) this corresponds to `@`, giving `[C@H]`.

SMILES: CCc1cnn2c(NCc3ccc[n+]([O-])c3)cc(N3CCCC[C@H]3CCO)nc12
