I read the molecule as a tetrahydrofuran core carrying four substituents, plus the two aromatic fragments:

- **Ring:** O1, C2, C3, C4 and C5.
- **C2:** a wedged amide, C(=O)NH, attached to the 4-position of a pyridine. The pyridine carries a primary carboxamide, C(N)=O, at its 2-position.
- **C3:** a hashed bond to a benzene ring. Going around that ring from the attachment carbon, the substituents are OMe, F, F, then H, H. So it is a 2-methoxy-3,4-difluorophenyl group.
- **C4:** a hashed methyl.
- **C5:** a wedged methyl and a CF3 group.
