The ring is five-membered: an O at the upper left and four carbons.

Going around the ring from O:
- O to the top carbon C1 (single bond).
- C1 to the right carbon C2, drawn with a double bond (the inner line runs parallel to this bond).
- C2 to the bottom carbon C3 (single bond).
- C3 to the bottom-left carbon C4, drawn with a double bond (the inner parallel line).
- C4 back to O (single bond).

This gives two C=C double bonds, C1=C2 and C3=C4, with O bonded to C1 and C4. That is a furan ring.
