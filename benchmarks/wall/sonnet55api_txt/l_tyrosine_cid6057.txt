The drawing shows tyrosine: a para-hydroxyphenyl ring joined through a CH2 to an alpha carbon that carries NH2 and a carboxylic acid (C(=O)OH).

The alpha carbon has a bold wedge to NH2, so the amine points toward the viewer and the implicit hydrogen points away. The CIP priorities are N > COOH > CH2. Viewed with H at the back, N (down) → COOH (right) → CH2 (upper left) runs counterclockwise, which is S. That matches L-tyrosine.

SMILES: N[C@@H](Cc1ccc(O)cc1)C(=O)O
