The image shows flupirtine, which I read as follows.

- **Left end:** an ethyl group (CH3–CH2) bonded to an oxygen, then a carbonyl carbon (C=O), then an NH. Together these form an ethyl carbamate, CCOC(=O)N.
- **Pyridine ring:** the carbamate NH is attached to a ring carbon. The adjacent ring carbon bears NH2 and sits next to the ring nitrogen. The carbon on the other side of the ring nitrogen bears the NH–CH2 substituent. The remaining two ring carbons are CH.
- **Right end:** the NH is bonded to a CH2, which is bonded to a benzene ring carrying fluorine at the para position.

Writing the pyridine ring from the carbamate-bearing carbon: c1(NC(=O)OCC)c(N)nc(NCc2ccc(F)cc2)cc1.

SMILES: CCOC(=O)Nc1ccc(NCc2ccc(F)cc2)nc1N
