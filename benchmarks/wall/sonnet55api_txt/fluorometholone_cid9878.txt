The drawing is a steroid skeleton (four fused rings, A–D) with these features:

- Ring A is a cross-conjugated 1,4-dien-3-one.
- C9 carries F and C11 carries OH.
- C6 carries a methyl, and both ring-junction carbons C10 and C13 carry methyls.
- C17 carries both an OH and an acetyl group (C(=O)CH3).

Taken together, this is fluorometholone (9-fluoro-11,17-dihydroxy-6-methylpregna-1,4-diene-3,20-dione).

The methyls on C10 and C13 are hashed, which is the reverse of the usual steroid drawing, so the molecule is shown from the opposite face. Reading every wedge and hash with that in mind gives the natural configuration:

- 9α-F
- 11β-OH
- 6α-methyl
- 17α-OH
- 14α-H
- 8β-H

I did not derive the @/@@ labels independently from the drawing. I wrote them from my memory of the fluorometholone SMILES and checked only that the drawn wedges and hashes correspond to those configurations.
