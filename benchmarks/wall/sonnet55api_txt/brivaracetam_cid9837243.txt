I read this as brivaracetam, (2S)-2-[(4R)-2-oxo-4-propylpyrrolidin-1-yl]butanamide.

**Structure read from the drawing**
- On the left is a primary amide, H2N–C(=O)–.
- Its carbonyl carbon connects to a stereocentre. That carbon carries an ethyl group drawn on a wedge and is bonded to the ring nitrogen.
- The ring is a five-membered lactam: N, C(=O), CH2, CH and CH2. The ring CH carries a propyl chain drawn on a wedge.

**Stereochemistry**
- **Side-chain carbon:** the priorities are N > C(=O)NH2 > ethyl, with H pointing back. Tracing N (upper right) to C(=O)N (upper left) to ethyl (down) runs counterclockwise, so this centre is S.
- **Ring carbon:** the priorities are CH2–N > CH2–C(=O) > propyl, with H pointing back. Tracing down-left to up-left to right runs clockwise, so this centre is R.
- I checked that `[C@H]` for the ring carbon and `[C@@H]` for the side-chain carbon in the SMILES below give R and S respectively.
