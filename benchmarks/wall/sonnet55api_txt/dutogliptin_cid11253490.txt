I read the drawing as a pyrrolidine-2-boronic acid whose ring nitrogen is acylated by a glycyl-type unit, C(=O)CH2–NH–(pyrrolidin-3-yl).

**Fragments**
- **Right ring:** a five-membered pyrrolidine with N at the lower left. The carbon next to N carries B(OH)2 on a bold wedge.
- **Linker:** the ring N is bonded to a C=O, then a CH2, then an NH.
- **Left ring:** the NH is attached by a hashed bond to C3 of a second pyrrolidine, whose ring NH is drawn as "HN" at the upper left.

**Stereochemistry**
- **Right stereocentre (carbon bearing B):** the wedge puts B toward the viewer, so H points away. The priorities are N > C > B. N, then ring CH2, then B run clockwise in the drawing, which gives R. In SMILES, `[C@@H]1` written as `OB(O)[C@@H]1CCCN1…` corresponds to R.
- **Left stereocentre (carbon bearing the NH linker):** the hash puts the NH linker away from the viewer, so H points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > CH2 next to the ring NH > CH2 next to CH2. They run counterclockwise in the drawing; because H faces the viewer, the true sense is clockwise, which gives R. In SMILES, `CN[C@@H]1CCNC1` corresponds to R.
