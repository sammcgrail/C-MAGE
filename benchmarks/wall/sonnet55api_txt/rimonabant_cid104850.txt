**Pyrazole core:** N1 carries the 2,4-dichlorophenyl group. N2 is double-bonded to C3. C3 carries the carboxamide. C4 carries the methyl group. C5 carries the 4-chlorophenyl group.

**Dichlorophenyl ring:** The ring carbon attached to N1 has a Cl on the adjacent (ortho) carbon. Going around the ring, the next carbon is CH, then the carbon bearing the second Cl. This puts the second Cl para to the attachment point, giving a 2,4-dichlorophenyl group.

**Amide side chain:** C3 is bonded to C(=O)NH–N, and that N is part of a piperidine ring (N1CCCCC1).
