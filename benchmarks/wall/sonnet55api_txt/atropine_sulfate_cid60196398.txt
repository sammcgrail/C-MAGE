This is atropine sulfate: two identical atropine units plus one sulfuric acid, written as three dot-separated fragments.

**Each atropine unit**
- A tropane core: a bicyclic system with an N-methyl bridge, with the 3-position carbon bearing the ester oxygen.
- The ester is O–C(=O)–CH(CH2OH)–phenyl, a tropic acid ester. Its CH is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
- The two bridgehead carbons carry explicit H wedges: the top bridgehead has its H wedged up, the bottom one has its H wedged down. I assigned @/@@ from the 2D geometry of those wedges (viewed from the H, the top bridgehead's neighbours run left, right, N clockwise; for the bottom one, N, left, right run counter-clockwise). This gives `[C@@H]2` for the top bridgehead and `[C@H]1` for the bottom one in the SMILES order used below.
- The two units are drawn identically, so they get the same stereo labels.

**Counter-acid:** S(=O)(=O)(OH)2 is written as `OS(=O)(=O)O`.

SMILES: CN1[C@@H]2CC[C@H]1CC(OC(=O)C(CO)c1ccccc1)C2.CN1[C@@H]2CC[C@H]1CC(OC(=O)C(CO)c1ccccc1)C2.OS(=O)(=O)O
