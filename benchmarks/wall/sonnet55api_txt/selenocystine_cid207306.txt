This is selenocystine, two selenocysteine units joined through a Se–Se bond.

- **Chain:** each half is HOOC–CH(NH2)–CH2–Se, and the two CH2–Se units are linked Se–Se. That gives two carboxylic acids, two primary amines, two CH2 groups and two selenium atoms.
- **Stereochemistry:** both α-carbons carry a hashed NH2, so both are stereocentres. For the left one, the priorities are N > CH2Se > COOH > H. Viewed with H toward the viewer, N (down), then CH2 (upper right), then COOH (upper left), runs counterclockwise on screen, so the true sense is clockwise. That makes the centre R, the L-configuration for selenocysteine. The right-hand centre is the same by symmetry.
- **SMILES:** the first centre is written as N[C@@H](C[Se]...)C(=O)O. In the second centre the neighbour order is CH2, H, N, COOH, which swaps N and CH2. The same configuration is therefore written [C@H].

SMILES: N[C@@H](C[Se][Se]C[C@H](N)C(=O)O)C(=O)O
