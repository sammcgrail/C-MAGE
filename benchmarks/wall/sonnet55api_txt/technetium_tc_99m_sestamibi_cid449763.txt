The image shows six identical isocyanide ligands (the sestamibi ligand) plus one 99Tc atom drawn as a radical, [99Tc]. The drawing gives no bond between Tc and the ligands, so I wrote them as separate components.

Each ligand is [C-]#[N+]CC(C)(C)OC. Reading the drawing: the isocyanide carbon C⁻ is triple-bonded to N⁺, N⁺ is bonded to a CH2, the CH2 to a quaternary carbon carrying two methyls, and that carbon to an O–CH3 group.

The SMILES is the Tc atom followed by six copies of this ligand, separated by dots.
