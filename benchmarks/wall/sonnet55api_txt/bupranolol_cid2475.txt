Reading the structure region by region:

- **Left end:** a tert-butyl group (a carbon bearing three methyls) is attached to an NH.
- **Chain:** NH–CH2–CH(OH)–CH2–O, with the OH drawn with a plain bond.
- **Ring:** an aromatic ring joined to the chain through the ether O. The ring carbon bearing O has an adjacent carbon bearing Cl (ortho). The ring continues CH, CH, then a carbon bearing a methyl, then CH, and back to the carbon bearing O.

Numbering the ring from the O-bearing carbon as C1:
- C1 has the O.
- C2 has the Cl, at the upper left of the ring.
- C3 is CH (top).
- C4 is CH (upper right).
- C5 has the methyl (right).
- C6 is CH (bottom).

The methyl carbon sits on the ring carbon at the right, which is para to the Cl carbon. The ring is therefore 2-chloro-5-methylphenoxy.

The CH(OH) carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
