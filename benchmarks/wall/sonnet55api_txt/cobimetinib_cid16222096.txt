This structure is cobimetinib. I read it region by region:

- **Left ring:** a benzene ring carrying iodine at the para position and fluorine at the ortho position relative to the NH link.
- **Central ring:** a benzene ring with the carbonyl carbon on one carbon, the anilino NH on the adjacent carbon, and two adjacent fluorines on the next two ring carbons.
- **Amide and azetidine:** the carbonyl is bonded to the nitrogen of a four-membered azetidine ring. The ring carbon opposite that nitrogen carries both an OH and the piperidine substituent.
- **Piperidine:** a six-membered ring attached through its C2, which is next to the ring NH.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only wedge is on the bond between the azetidine carbon and the piperidine C2. The azetidine carbon is not a stereocentre, because its two ring CH2 groups are equivalent. The wedge therefore defines the piperidine C2. With priorities N > azetidine C > ring CH2 > H, and the azetidine carbon toward the viewer, the sequence runs counterclockwise, giving S. In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]1CCCCN1` with the azetidine carbon as the preceding atom encodes that S centre.
