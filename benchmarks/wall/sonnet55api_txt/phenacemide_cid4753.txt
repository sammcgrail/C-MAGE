The drawing shows a benzene ring with a CH2 attached to it. The CH2 is bonded to a C(=O), which is bonded to an NH, which is bonded to a second C(=O), which ends in NH2. This is phenylacetylurea.

The ring has six vertices: the bonds at the top left, left and bottom each appear as double bonds, so it is a standard Kekulé benzene. I wrote it in aromatic form.
