The drawing shows a central Ga atom bonded to three O atoms. Each of those O atoms is bonded to an N⁺, and each N⁺ carries one =O and one O⁻, so each is a nitrate group bound through O. Separately there is a water molecule, H2O. This is gallium nitrate hydrate.

Writing each nitrate branch as O[N+](=O)[O-] on the Ga, with water as a separate component:
