- **Thiophenes:** two 3-methylthiophen-2-yl rings, both attached to the same alkene carbon.
- **Chain:** that alkene carbon is double-bonded to a CH, which continues through two CH2 groups to the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Piperidine:** a six-membered ring with the nitrogen at position 1 and a carboxylic acid on C3.
- **Stereochemistry:** the C3 stereocentre has a hashed bond to the COOH group, so that group points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.

  - Seen from the front, COOH (up) → CH2N (left) → ring CH2 (lower right) runs counterclockwise.
  - With H toward the viewer this reverses, so the centre is R.
  - Writing the stereocentre as `CCC[C@H](C2)C(=O)O` gives R in SMILES order.
