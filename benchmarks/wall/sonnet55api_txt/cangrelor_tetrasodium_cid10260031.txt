This structure is cangrelor as its tetrasodium salt. I read it region by region.

- **Purine base:** an adenine-type core carries a 2-(methylthio)ethylamino group (CH3-S-CH2CH2-NH) at C6 and a 3,3,3-trifluoropropylthio group (S-CH2CH2CF3) at C2.
- **Sugar:** a ribofuranose is attached through N9. The drawing shows a wedge at the glycosidic bond, a wedge at the CH2 carbon, and hashes on both OH groups. This matches the D-ribose pattern of adenosine, so I used the adenosine stereo pattern.
- **Phosphate chain:** the 5'-O is linked to P(=O)(O⁻), then to a bridging O, then to P(=O)(O⁻), then to a CCl2 carbon, then to a terminal P(=O)(O⁻)O⁻. This gives a dichloromethylene bisphosphonate group.
- **Counterions:** four Na⁺ balance the four negative charges on the phosphate groups.
