The drawing shows acetic acid in which every hydrogen is replaced by deuterium (²H).

- A carboxylic acid carbon is double-bonded to one O and single-bonded to another O.
- The single-bonded O carries a ²H, so the acid hydrogen is deuterium.
- The carboxyl carbon is bonded to a methyl carbon, which carries three ²H atoms.
