This drawing matches carfilzomib, a tetrapeptide epoxyketone, and I wrote the SMILES from that recognition and from my memory of the published SMILES. I did not independently re-derive each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes, so the @/@@ marks rest on that recalled structure.

Reading the drawing from left to right:
- A 2-methyl epoxide (three-membered ring with O) carries a methyl on its quaternary carbon and is bonded to a ketone C=O.
- The ketone is attached to a leucine-type residue (isobutyl side chain) through its NH.
- Next comes a phenylalanine-type residue (benzyl side chain), then a second leucine-type residue (isobutyl).
- The next residue is homophenylalanine, with a CH2CH2-phenyl side chain.
- The chain ends in an acetamide, C(=O)CH2, attached to the nitrogen of a morpholine ring (N and O across a six-membered ring).
