This is a digoxigenin-type cardenolide with a four-sugar chain on the 3-OH. I read the stereochemistry from the drawing and then cross-checked it against the known digoxin SMILES, so it is partly from memory rather than fully derived from the wedges and hashes.

- **Aglycone:** a steroid core with a butenolide (the unsaturated five-membered lactone) at C17, 12-OH, 14-OH, and methyls at C10 and C13. This is digoxigenin.
- **Sugar chain at C3:** three 2,6-dideoxy-hexose (digitoxose) units joined in a row through their 4-O positions, each with a free 3-OH.
- **Terminal sugar:** a glucose attached through O to C4 of the outermost digitoxose.

Together these match deslanoside, which is digoxin with a terminal glucose on the sugar chain. I took the digoxin stereodescriptors for the aglycone and the three digitoxoses. For the glucose I rewrote the standard β-D-glucopyranoside stereo so that it starts from the anomeric carbon.
