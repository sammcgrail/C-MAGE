This is L-carnosine (β-alanyl-L-histidine).

- **Imidazole ring:** The ring carbon bearing the CH2 is bonded to the ring NH and to a CH. That CH leads to the ring N, then to another CH, and back to the NH. This reads as `c1cnc[nH]1`.
- **Side chain and acid:** The CH2 links the ring to the α-carbon, which carries the COOH group.
- **Amide chain:** The α-carbon's NH is acylated by C(=O)CH2CH2NH2, the β-alanyl unit.
- **Stereochemistry:** The α-carbon has a wedge bond to the CH2. The priorities are N > COOH > CH2 > H. With H pointing away, the sequence N → COOH → CH2 runs counterclockwise on the page, so the centre is S. This matches the L-histidine configuration, written as `N[C@@H](Cc1cnc[nH]1)C(=O)O`.
