I read the drawing as the 17-propionate ester of 11-deoxycortisol (cortexolone), a pregn-4-ene-3,20-dione steroid. The stereochemistry is the natural steroid configuration, assigned by comparison with the known cortexolone stereo-SMILES rather than derived atom by atom.

- **A ring:** an enone with the C3 ketone conjugated to the C4=C5 double bond.
- **Ring junction:** the C10 methyl is drawn hashed.
- **B and C rings:** the C9 hydrogen is a wedge and the C8 hydrogen is hashed.
- **D ring:** the C13 methyl is hashed and the C14 hydrogen is a wedge.
- **C17 side chain:** C17 carries a wedged propionate ester (–O–C(=O)–CH2CH3) and a C(=O)CH2OH group.

The drawing has the D ring on the left, so it is the natural steroid flipped horizontally. That flip explains the hashed methyls and the wedged 17-oxygen, which match the usual β-methyls and 17α-oxygen.
