I read this as safinamide, the (S)-enantiomer.

- **Left end:** a primary amide, C(N)=O, attached to a stereocentre. The stereocentre carries a methyl group (hashed bond) and an NH.
- **Middle:** the NH links through a CH2 to a para-substituted benzene ring. That ring carries an O–CH2 group at the position opposite the NH–CH2.
- **Right end:** the O–CH2 leads to a second benzene ring with fluorine in the meta position relative to the CH2.
- **Stereochemistry:** the methyl is hashed, so it points away and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > C(=O)N > CH3 > H. Reading N, then C(=O), then CH3 on the page runs clockwise. With H toward the viewer, the true configuration is the reverse, giving S.
- **SMILES check:** in `C[C@H](N...)C(N)=O` the neighbour order is CH3, H, N, C. This `@` notation works out to S, matching the drawing.

SMILES: C[C@H](NCc1ccc(OCc2cccc(F)c2)cc1)C(N)=O
