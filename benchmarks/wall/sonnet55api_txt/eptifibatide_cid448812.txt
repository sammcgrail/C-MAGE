I read the structure as a cyclic heptapeptide, with a 3-mercaptopropionyl (Mpa) cap joined by a disulfide to a C-terminal cysteine amide. Reading around the ring, the sequence is Mpa–Arg–Gly–Asp–Trp–Pro–Cys-NH2.

- **Disulfide ring:** the Arg carbonyl attaches to CH2–CH2–S–S–CH2, which leads to the Cys α-carbon. That makes the N-terminal residue 3-mercaptopropionic acid rather than a second cysteine.
- **Cys-NH2:** the wedged bond on the Cys α-carbon points to a C(=O)NH2 group. Working through the CIP priorities with the implicit H pointing away, this centre is R, which is L-cysteine.
- **Other stereocentres:** Arg, Asp, Trp and Pro carry hashed or wedged bonds. I took these as the natural L-configurations and did not check each one against the drawing. The SMILES encodes them as L.
- **Side chains:** Arg has the guanidine on a three-carbon chain. Asp has CH2COOH. Trp has an indole attached through CH2. Pro is the five-membered ring.
