The drawing shows an isoxazole ring joined through an amide to a para-substituted benzene ring.

**Isoxazole ring.** The atoms run N, O, C(CH3), C, CH, and back to N. N is double-bonded to the CH carbon, and the C(CH3) carbon is double-bonded to the ring carbon that carries the carboxamide. The O sits between the N and the C(CH3) carbon, so the ring is a 5-methylisoxazole with the amide at position 4.

**Amide and aryl group.** The amide carbon has a C=O and an NH, and the NH is bonded to a benzene ring. A CF3 group sits para to the NH on that ring.

This matches leflunomide.
