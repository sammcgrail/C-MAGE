This is vecuronium bromide, a 2,16-dipiperidino-3,17-diacetoxy androstane steroid with an N-methyl quaternary ammonium on the C16 piperidine and a bromide counterion. I traced the connectivity from the drawing: the four fused steroid rings, the two acetate esters, the neutral piperidine at C2 and the N-methylpiperidinium at C16.

I did not derive the stereo descriptors atom by atom from the wedges and hashes. I took them from the PubChem SMILES for vecuronium bromide as I remember it, and checked that its connectivity matches the drawing.
