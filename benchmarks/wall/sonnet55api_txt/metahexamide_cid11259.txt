The drawing shows a cyclohexyl group bonded to an NH, which is bonded to a C=O, then another NH, then a sulfonyl group (S with two =O) attached to a benzene ring.

On the ring, the sulfonyl carbon is at position 1. Going around from there, the next ring carbon is CH (position 6, upper right), then CH (position 5), then the carbon bearing the methyl (position 4), then the carbon bearing NH2 (position 3), then CH (position 2), which closes back to position 1.

So the ring is 3-amino-4-methylbenzenesulfonyl, and the whole molecule is a sulfonylurea.

SMILES: Cc1ccc(cc1N)S(=O)(=O)NC(=O)NC1CCCCC1
