I read this as naloxone: a morphinan core with an N-allyl group, a phenol OH, a furan-type ether bridge, a ketone, and a tertiary OH. The odd "C" label in the drawing is the bridging CH2 between the aromatic ring and the C–N carbon.

The hashed bonds mark four stereocentres: the quaternary carbon bearing the CH2CH2N bridge, the H on the carbon next to the ether O, the tertiary C–OH, and the H on the carbon attached to N. I did not derive the @/@@ labels from the drawing. I wrote them from my recollection of the standard naloxone SMILES, and the hashed positions are consistent with that natural configuration.
