The drawing shows these parts:

- A 4-chlorophenyl ring on the left, bonded to a CH(OH) carbon.
- A CH2 linking that carbon to the nitrogen of a piperidine ring.
- At the piperidine 4-position, a CH2 leading to a 4-fluorophenyl ring.

The CH(OH) carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity but is drawn with plain bonds, so I include no stereochemistry.

SMILES: OC(c1ccc(Cl)cc1)CN1CCC(Cc2ccc(F)cc2)CC1
