I read the molecule as an 8-hydroxy-quinolin-2(1H)-one core carrying a hydroxy-aminoethyl side chain at C5. The amine nitrogen is connected through a six-carbon chain to an ether oxygen, then CH2–CF2–phenyl.

- **Core:** the lactam carbonyl (C=O) is next to the ring NH, with a C=C between C3 and C4. The OH sits on C8, next to the carbon bearing the NH.
- **Side chain at C5:** a carbon bearing OH on a wedge bond, then CH2, then NH. The wedge is the only stereo information in the drawing.
- **Linker:** counting the chain vertices from N to O gives six CH2 groups. Then comes O, CH2, CF2 and the phenyl ring.
- **Stereochemistry:** the priorities at the stereocentre are OH > CH2N > aryl C > H. The OH is on a wedge, so H points away from the viewer. Going OH (down), CH2N (upper left), aryl (upper right) is clockwise, so the centre is R. In the SMILES, written with the aryl carbon first, this is `[C@@H]`.

SMILES: O=C1C=Cc2c(ccc(O)c2N1)[C@@H](O)CNCCCCCCOCC(F)(F)c1ccccc1
