The drawing is a tetracycline-type skeleton, and I identified it as doxycycline. I read it region by region and then used the known doxycycline SMILES, whose stereodescriptors I did not re-derive from the individual wedges, so the stereochemistry is the least certain part.

- **Left ring (ring A):** it carries a primary amide (C(=O)NH2), an enol OH, a ketone, and a dimethylamino group on a hashed carbon.
- **Ring B (bottom centre):** the ring-fusion carbon bearing an OH is hashed, and the neighbouring ring-fusion carbon has a hashed H.
- **Ring C (middle right):** it has a ketone and an enol OH. The ring-fusion carbon bearing a hashed H is next to a hashed methyl carbon, which has no OH, so C6 is a CH–CH3.
- **Ring D (right):** an aromatic ring carrying one phenolic OH.
