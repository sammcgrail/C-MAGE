The drawing is fluvoxamine. Reading it region by region:

- **Methoxy chain:** a methyl is bonded to an oxygen, which continues into a four-carbon chain (O–CH2–CH2–CH2–CH2) ending at the oxime carbon.
- **Oxime carbon:** this carbon is double-bonded to nitrogen and also carries the aryl ring.
- **Oxime ether:** the nitrogen is bonded to an oxygen, which leads to a CH2–CH2–NH2 group.
- **Aryl ring:** a para-substituted benzene whose far substituent is CF3.

For the C=N geometry I used the drawn coordinates. The oxime O lies on the same side of the C=N bond as the alkyl chain, so it is opposite the aryl ring. Aryl is the higher-priority group on carbon and O is the higher-priority group on nitrogen, so these are on opposite sides, which is the E configuration. In SMILES this is chain/C(=N\O).

SMILES: COCCCC/C(=N\OCCN)c1ccc(C(F)(F)F)cc1
