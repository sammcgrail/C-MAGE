I read this as the 3-oxime of levonorgestrel. This is a norgestrel-type 19-nor steroid skeleton with these features:

- a 13-ethyl group
- a 17-hydroxy-17-ethynyl group
- a 4-ene
- a 3-position C=N–OH oxime in place of the ketone

I took the ring-fusion stereocentres, the hashed ethyl and the hashed OH to match the natural levonorgestrel configuration, as in its known SMILES, rather than deriving each one from the wedges. In the drawing the oxime OH points down, on the same side as the C4 carbon, so I set the C=N geometry accordingly.
