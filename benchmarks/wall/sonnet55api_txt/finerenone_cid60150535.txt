- **Dihydropyridine ring (left):** it has an NH, a methyl-bearing C=C carbon, and a carbon carrying a primary amide, C(=O)NH2. It also has a stereogenic sp3 carbon (C4).
- **Pyridine ring (lower left):** it is fused to the first ring. It carries a methyl, a ring nitrogen, and an ethoxy group (OCC) on the carbon next to the fusion carbon.
- **Aryl group on C4:** the ring is attached through a wedge bond. Going round from the attachment carbon, it has an OMe-bearing carbon, a CH, a carbon bearing C#N, and two more CH carbons.
- **Stereochemistry:** the aryl bond is a wedge (toward the viewer), so the implicit H points away. Viewed from the front, the three carbon neighbours run clockwise from the fusion carbon (C4a), through C3, to the aryl carbon.
