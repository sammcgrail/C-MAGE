The drawing shows a pyrrolidin-2-one (lactam) ring, which I read as follows.

- The lactam nitrogen carries an ethyl group.
- The carbonyl carbon is bonded to N, to =O, and to the quaternary carbon.
- The quaternary carbon carries two phenyl rings and is bonded to the ring CH.
- That ring CH bears a CH2CH2 chain ending at the nitrogen of a morpholine ring.
- The ring CH is also bonded to a CH2 that closes the ring back to the lactam N.

The five-membered ring is therefore N, C(=O), C(Ph)2, CH, CH2.

The morpholine is N, CH2, CH2, O, CH2, CH2.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I assign no stereochemistry.

SMILES: CCN1CC(CCN2CCOCC2)C(c2ccccc2)(c2ccccc2)C1=O
