The drawing shows an imidazole ring with a 2-aminoethyl chain, which is histamine.

**Ring.** It is a five-membered ring with these atoms in order:
- NH at the top.
- A substituted carbon at the right, bonded to the chain.
- A CH carbon at the bottom right.
- N at the bottom left.
- A CH carbon at the left, between the two nitrogens.

**Bonds.** There are two double bonds in the ring:
- C=N, drawn between the left CH carbon and the bottom-left N.
- C=C, drawn between the substituted carbon on the right and the bottom-right CH carbon.

**Chain.** The substituted ring carbon carries CH2–CH2–NH2.

**SMILES.** Writing the ring from the substituted carbon: C1(=CN=CN1)CCN. Here the substituted carbon is double-bonded to the bottom CH carbon, which is bonded to N. That N is double-bonded to the left CH carbon, which is bonded to NH, which closes the ring back to the substituted carbon.
