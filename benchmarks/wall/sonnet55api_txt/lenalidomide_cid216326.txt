The drawing is lenalidomide: a piperidine-2,6-dione ring joined through its C3 carbon to the nitrogen of an isoindolinone, with an amino group on the benzene ring.

**Glutarimide ring (left):** the ring atoms are C(=O), NH, C(=O), CH2, CH2 and the CH that carries the isoindolinone nitrogen. The CH has plain bonds, so it gets no stereo.

**Isoindolinone (right):**

- The CH2 is attached to an aromatic carbon (C3a), which is adjacent to the aromatic carbon bearing NH2.
- The C=O is attached to the other fused aromatic carbon (C7a).
- Going around the benzene ring from C3a: the C–NH2 carbon, then CH, CH, CH, then C7a.

**Check:**
- Ring closure 1 runs from the first C(=O) through CH2, CH2 and the CH to C(=O) and NH, then back to the start, giving a six-membered ring.
- The isoindolinone ring N2, CH2, c3, c (the aromatic carbon bearing NH2), then c, c, c, c3 and C2=O closes with the aromatic ring. The aromatic atoms are c3, c(N), c, c, c, c3, which makes six, so the benzene ring is complete.
