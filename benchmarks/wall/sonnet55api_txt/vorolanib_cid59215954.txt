I read the drawing as a sunitinib-type oxindole–pyrrole amide carrying a dimethylcarbamoyl-substituted pyrrolidine.

- **Left end:** a dimethylurea, Me2N–C(=O)–, attached to the pyrrolidine nitrogen.
- **Pyrrolidine:** a five-membered ring with one stereocentre. A wedge bond runs from that carbon to the amide NH.
- **Stereochemistry:** with the hydrogen pointing away, the wedged N, then the ring CH2 bonded to N, then the ring CH2 bonded to carbon run counterclockwise on the page. That is the S configuration, which is written as `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order below.
- **Middle:** the NH–C(=O) amide joins the pyrrolidine to the pyrrole. The pyrrole has an NH and two methyl groups, one on each side of the carboxamide-bearing carbon.
- **Right end:** the pyrrole is linked through a CH= bridge to C3 of a 5-fluoro-2-oxindole, which has a lactam NH and C=O. The fluorine sits on the benzene ring. The bridge double bond is drawn with the pyrrole and the oxindole carbonyl on the same side, but I did not encode this geometry because the drawing does not clearly mark it with E/Z labels.
