This is abacavir sulfate: two identical abacavir molecules drawn with one sulfuric acid.

**Abacavir unit**
- The core is a purine bearing a 2-amino group and a 6-(cyclopropylamino) group.
- N9 of the purine is attached to a cyclopent-2-ene ring.
- That ring carries a CH2OH group on the carbon across from the N-bearing carbon, with the C=C between the two remaining ring carbons.

**Stereochemistry**
- Two centres have hashed bonds: the ring carbon bonded to N9, and the ring carbon bonded to CH2OH. I took each stereocentre to be at the narrow end of its hashed bond.
- Reading the drawn geometry gives `[C@@H]` for the N-bearing carbon and `[C@H]` for the CH2OH-bearing carbon, in the fragment `n([C@@H]3C[C@H](CO)C=C3)`.

**Sulfate**
- It is drawn as neutral H2SO4: S with two =O and two OH.
