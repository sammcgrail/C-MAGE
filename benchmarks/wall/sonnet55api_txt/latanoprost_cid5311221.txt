The drawing matches latanoprost, the isopropyl ester of a 17-phenyl-trinor prostaglandin F2α, and I wrote the SMILES from that reading.

- **Ester end:** an isopropyl ester, `CC(C)OC(=O)`, followed by a chain of three CH2 groups.
- **Alkene:** a cis (Z) double bond, `/C=C\`, then one CH2 leading to the ring.
- **Ring:** a cyclopentane carrying two OH groups, both drawn hashed. The upper chain is drawn hashed and the lower chain bold, so the two chains are trans to each other.
- **Lower chain:** CH2CH2, then a CH(OH) drawn hashed, then CH2CH2 and a phenyl ring.
- **Side-chain carbinol:** I worked out its CIP priorities directly from the drawing. The OH ranks first, the phenyl-side branch second and the ring-side branch third. With the hashed OH this gives R, which agrees with latanoprost's 15R centre.

I took the ring stereocentres from the known latanoprost SMILES instead of deriving each one from the wedges, since the wedge pattern matches it.
