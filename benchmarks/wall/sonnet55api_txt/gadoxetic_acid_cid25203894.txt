I read this as the gadolinium complex gadoxetate (Gd-EOB-DTPA), written as the Gd³⁺ ion, two H⁺ and the ligand anion.

- **Aryl part:** a para-disubstituted benzene ring carries an ethoxy group (CH3CH2–O–) on one side and a CH2 on the other.
- **Stereocentre:** that CH2 is attached by a wedge bond to a stereocentre. The stereocentre also bears a CH2–N group and an N, so there is a three-carbon chain from the ring to the nitrogens.
- **Nitrogens:** three nitrogens are present. The N on the stereocentre carries two CH2COO⁻ arms. The CH2 on the stereocentre leads to a second N that carries one CH2COO⁻ arm and a –CH2CH2– link. That link leads to a third N carrying two CH2COO⁻ arms. That makes five carboxylates in total.
- **Stereochemistry:** the wedged benzyl group points toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away. Viewed with H at the back, benzyl → CH2N → N runs clockwise. Written as `C[C@@H](CN…)N…`, with neighbours in the order benzyl, H, CH2N, N, this gives `@@`.
- **Counter-species:** the charges are Gd³⁺ plus two H⁺, drawn as separate components.
