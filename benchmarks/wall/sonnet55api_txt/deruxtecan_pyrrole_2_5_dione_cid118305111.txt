I read this as a maleimidocaproyl–Gly-Gly-Phe-Gly linker joined through an NH–CH2–O–CH2–C(=O) spacer to an exatecan-type camptothecin core. This is the same layout as deruxtecan.

**Reading by region:**
- **Maleimide end:** a 2,5-dioxopyrrole ring whose nitrogen carries a five-carbon chain, then C(=O)NH (the caproyl amide).
- **Peptide chain:** the caproyl amide continues through Gly, Gly, Phe (benzyl side chain, hashed bond, which I read as L-Phe) and Gly, each joined by amide bonds.
- **Spacer:** the last Gly amide nitrogen is bonded to CH2–O–CH2–C(=O)NH.
- **Core:** the spacer amide nitrogen attaches, through a hashed bond, to the saturated ring carbon of the hexacyclic camptothecin-type system. The core also carries F and CH3 on the aromatic ring, the lactone with an ethyl group and a wedged OH at the quaternary carbon, and the pyridone C=O.

I did not derive the two core stereocentres independently from the wedge geometry. I took their SMILES labels from the known deruxtecan structure. I did check the Phe centre against L-Phe by permuting the neighbour order.
