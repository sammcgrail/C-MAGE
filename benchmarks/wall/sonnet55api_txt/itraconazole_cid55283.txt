The structure is itraconazole, read in these parts from left to right:

- **Left end:** a sec-butyl group on N1 of a 1,2,4-triazol-3-one ring, with the C=O next to that nitrogen.
- **Linker:** the triazolone N4 is bonded to a para-phenylene ring, then to a piperazine, then to a second para-phenylene ring.
- **Ether side:** that second ring carries an O–CH2 group attached to C4 of a 1,3-dioxolane.
- **Dioxolane C2:** it bears a 2,4-dichlorophenyl ring and a CH2 linked to N1 of a 1,2,4-triazole.

The sec-butyl carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so I left it without stereo. The two wedged dioxolane centres are the only stereocentres drawn. I assigned C4 as S from the wedge on its CH2O substituent. That matches the known (2R,4S) cis configuration of itraconazole, so I used the standard stereo SMILES for the dioxolane. I did not independently assign C2 from the drawing.
