The OH carbon (B) is adjacent to the isopropyl carbon (A). The methyl carbon (D) is para to B, since B→C→D is only two steps but D lies opposite A... checking the positions: A–B–C–D gives D three bonds from A, so D is para to A, not B. Relative to B, D is meta (B→C→D).

So the substituents are: OH at B, isopropyl at A (ortho to OH), and methyl at D (meta to OH, para to the isopropyl). This is thymol, 2-isopropyl-5-methylphenol.
