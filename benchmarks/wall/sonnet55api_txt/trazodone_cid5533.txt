This is trazodone: a 3-chlorophenyl group on a piperazine, a three-carbon chain, and a triazolopyridinone.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- **Chlorophenyl ring:** Cl sits on a carbon, and the piperazine nitrogen is attached to the ring carbon meta to it, giving Clc1cccc(c1)N.
- **Piperazine:** a six-membered ring with two N atoms. The aryl-bearing N is bonded to two CH2 groups, and the other N carries the chain.
- **Chain:** three CH2 groups run from the second piperazine N to the triazole N.
- **Triazolopyridinone:** the chain N is bonded to C(=O), which is bonded to the ring-fusion N. That fusion N is bonded to the pyridine CH and to the fusion carbon. The fusion carbon is double-bonded to the other triazole N (=N), and that N is bonded back to the chain N.

The SMILES below writes the ring-fusion carbon as C(=N2) and closes the triazole ring through the chain N.

SMILES: O=C1N(CCCN2CCN(CC2)c2cccc(Cl)c2)N=C2C=CC=CN12
