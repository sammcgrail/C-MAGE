I read the image as a linear pentasaccharide. It has a methyl glycoside at the top ring and four glycosidic links running down to the bottom ring. The rings, from top to bottom, are:

1. A glucosamine ring (methyl glycoside) carrying an N-sulfate (NHSO3H), a 3-OH and a 6-O-sulfate. Its 4-O is the glycosidic link to ring 2.
2. A uronic acid ring with a COOH, a 3-OH and an O-sulfate on the carbon next to the anomeric carbon.
3. A glucosamine ring with an N-sulfate, an O-sulfate on the ring carbon next to the N-bearing carbon, and a 6-O-sulfate.
4. A uronic acid ring with a COOH and two OH groups, and no sulfate.
5. A terminal glucosamine ring with an N-sulfate, two OH groups and a 6-O-sulfate.

I worked out each stereocentre from the wedge and hash bonds in the drawing. Ring 4's COOH bond is drawn as a hash that is partly hidden by the N-hash from ring 5, so that centre is the least certain.
