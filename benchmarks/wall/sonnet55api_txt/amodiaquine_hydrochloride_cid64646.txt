The drawing is amodiaquine dihydrochloride dihydrate, so I wrote it as one SMILES with each component separated by dots.

**Main molecule**
- The left part is a diethylaminomethyl group, CH2N(Et)2, attached to a benzene ring.
- On that ring, the CH2 group sits ortho to the OH, and the NH sits para to the OH.
- The NH links to C4 of a 7-chloroquinoline. In the drawing, the ring nitrogen is at the top, the carbon bearing the NH is adjacent to the ring-fusion carbon, and Cl is on the benzo ring.

**Quinoline numbering check**
- N1 is the ring nitrogen, C2 and C3 follow, and C4 carries the NH.
- C4a is the fusion carbon next to C4. From C4a the benzo ring runs C5, C6, C7 (bearing Cl), C8, then C8a, which bonds to N1.
- In the drawing, C8a connects to C8 at the upper right, and C8 connects to C7, which carries Cl. This matches 7-chloro.

**SMILES for the amodiaquine core**
CCN(CC)Cc1cc(Nc2ccnc3cc(Cl)ccc23)ccc1O

The quinoline part reads c2ccnc3cc(Cl)ccc23: c2 is C4, then c (C3), c (C2), n (N1), c3 (C8a), c (C8), c(Cl) (C7), c (C6), c (C5), c23 (C4a). This is correct.
