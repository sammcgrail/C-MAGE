This structure is glipizide, built from these fragments:

- A cyclohexyl group is attached to an NH, which is bonded to a C=O. That carbonyl carbon is bonded to a second NH, which is bonded to S(=O)(=O).
- The sulfonyl sulfur is attached to a para-substituted benzene ring.
- The other para position of the benzene carries a CH2CH2 chain leading to NH–C(=O).
- That carbonyl is attached to a pyrazine ring at C2, with a methyl group at C5.

Checking the pyrazine ring in the drawing:

- The carbon bearing the carbonyl (C2) is bonded to N1 at the bottom and to CH (C3) at the top.
- C3 is bonded to N4, the upper-right nitrogen.
- N4 is bonded to the carbon bearing the methyl (C5).
- C5 is bonded to CH (C6), which is bonded back to N1.
- The ring is therefore c1(C(=O)N...)cnc(C)cn1, which matches glipizide.
