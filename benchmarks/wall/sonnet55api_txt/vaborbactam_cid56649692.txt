Reading the drawing from left to right:

- **Thiophene:** a 2-substituted thiophene, with S and four ring carbons, joined through its C2 to a CH2.
- **Amide:** that CH2 is attached to a C(=O), which is attached to NH.
- **Six-membered ring:** it contains, in order, a carbon bearing the NH, a boron bearing OH, a ring oxygen, a carbon bearing the CH2COOH group, and two CH2 groups that close the ring back to the carbon bearing the NH.
- **Side chain:** the ring carbon next to the ring oxygen carries CH2–C(=O)OH.

Both stereocentres are drawn with wedge bonds (to N and to the CH2COOH group). I assigned the chirality tags from the 2D geometry, with the implicit H pointing away from the viewer in each case.

- The carbon bearing N comes out as `[C@H]`, with neighbour order N, H, B, CH2.
- The carbon bearing the side chain comes out as `[C@@H]`, with neighbour order ring CH2, H, side-chain CH2, O.
