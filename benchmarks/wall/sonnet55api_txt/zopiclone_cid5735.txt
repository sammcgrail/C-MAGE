This is zopiclone, drawn with plain bonds, so no stereochemistry is included.

Reading the structure region by region:

- **Pyrazine ring (top):** it has two nitrogens. Going around the ring: C=C at the top, then N, then a fused carbon, a second fused carbon, and another N. The fused carbons are drawn as C(=N)–C(=N).
- **Five-membered lactam ring:** the ring runs from the fused carbon to C(=O), then N, then CH(O–), then the other fused carbon.
- **Chloropyridine substituent:** the lactam N is attached to a pyridine carbon. That ring runs C2 (attached to the lactam N), C3, C4, C5 (bearing Cl), C6, and the ring N. The N is adjacent to C2, and Cl sits on C5, which is para to C2.
- **Carbamate side chain:** the CH carbon carries an O that is bonded to a C(=O) group, which in turn is attached to a piperazine N. The other piperazine N carries a methyl group.

Written as a SMILES string:

O=C1c2nccnc2C(OC(=O)N2CCN(C)CC2)N1c1ccc(Cl)cn1

Checking the pyrazine ring: c2nccnc2 contains c2, n, c, c, n, c2, which is six atoms. The aromatic ring closure is consistent with the drawing.
