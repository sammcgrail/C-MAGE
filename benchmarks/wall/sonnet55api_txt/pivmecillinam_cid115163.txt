I read this as pivmecillinam (the pivaloyloxymethyl ester of mecillinam), a penicillin-type β-lactam.

- **Left:** a saturated seven-membered ring (azepane) with one nitrogen. That nitrogen is bonded to a CH= carbon, which is double-bonded to an exocyclic N (an amidine, N=CH–N).
- **Centre:** a fused bicyclic β-lactam–thiazolidine core. The four-membered ring holds the lactam C=O and N, and the five-membered ring holds S, a gem-dimethyl carbon and the carboxylate-bearing carbon.
- **Right:** the carboxylate is esterified as C(=O)–O–CH2–O–C(=O)–C(CH3)3, a pivaloyloxymethyl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** the three stereocentres are the ring-fusion CH (H wedge), the amidine-bearing CH (hashed bond to N) and the carboxylate-bearing CH (wedge to C=O).
