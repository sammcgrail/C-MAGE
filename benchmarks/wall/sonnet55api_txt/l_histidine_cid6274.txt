The drawing shows histidine: an imidazole ring joined through a CH2 to an alpha carbon that carries NH2 and COOH.

**Ring:** The ring carbon bearing the CH2 is bonded to the N–H. The other ring atoms run from that N–H to a CH, then to a ring N, then to a second CH that closes back onto the substituted carbon. This gives `Cc1[nH]cnc1`, with the CH2 on the carbon next to the N–H.

**Stereocentre:** The NH2 is on a bold wedge, so it points toward the viewer and the implicit H points away. The CIP priorities are N > COOH > CH2 > H. In the drawing, N (down), COOH (upper right) and CH2 (upper left) run counterclockwise, so the centre is S. This is the L-histidine configuration.

**SMILES:** Writing the stereocentre as `N[C@@H](...)C(=O)O` with the CH2 branch inside the parentheses gives the S configuration.

SMILES: N[C@@H](Cc1[nH]cnc1)C(=O)O
