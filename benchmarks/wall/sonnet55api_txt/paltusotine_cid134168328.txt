The drawing shows a quinoline core with three substituents.

- **Quinoline ring:** N1 is at the top. C2 is the CH next to it, C3 carries the aryl group, and C4 carries the piperidine. C4a and C8a are the fusion carbons, and C5 is the CH adjacent to C4a. C6 carries the second aryl group, followed by C7 (CH) and C8 (CH) back to C8a.
- **Position 3:** a 3,5-difluorophenyl group.
- **Position 4:** a 4-aminopiperidin-1-yl group, attached through its ring nitrogen.
- **Position 6:** a phenyl ring bearing OH at the carbon next to the attachment point and CN on the carbon next to that. The ring is therefore 2-hydroxy-3-cyanophenyl.
