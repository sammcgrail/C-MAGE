**Substituents on the sulfonamide.** The left side is an N,N-diethylsulfamide. A diethylamino group is attached to S, and S carries two =O and an NH.

**Bicyclic core.** This is a hexahydrobenzo[g]quinoline-type system.
- The NH is bonded to the saturated-ring carbon C3 by a hashed bond.
- C3 is bonded to a CH2 that leads to the ring nitrogen, which carries an N-propyl group.
- C3 is also bonded to a CH2 that leads to the ring-fusion carbon C4a.
- The other ring-fusion carbon, C8a, is bonded to the ring nitrogen, to a CH2 that leads to the aromatic ring, and to C4a. It has a wedged H.
- C4a has a hashed H. It is bonded to C8a, to the CH2 in the C3 ring, and to a CH2 that leads to the aromatic ring.
- The fused benzene ring carries an OH on the carbon next to the carbon bonded to the C4a-side CH2.
