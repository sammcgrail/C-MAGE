Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left:** a tert-butyl group (a carbon bearing three methyls) is attached to an NH.
- **Chain:** NH–CH2–CH(OH)–CH2–O. The OH on the middle carbon is drawn with a solid wedge, so that is the only stereocentre.
- **Right:** the O is bonded to a benzene ring, and the ring carbon next to it carries a cyclopentyl group (a 5-membered saturated ring).

This is penbutolol.

**Stereochemistry:** the CIP priorities at the stereocentre are OH > CH2O > CH2N > H. The OH is on a wedge, so H points away from the viewer. Going from OH (down) to CH2O (upper right) to CH2N (upper left) is counterclockwise, which gives S. Written with the neighbours in the order CH2N, H, OH, CH2O, that is `[C@H]`.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)NC[C@H](O)COc1ccccc1C1CCCC1
