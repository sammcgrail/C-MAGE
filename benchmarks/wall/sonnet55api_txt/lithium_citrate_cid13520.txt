The image shows lithium citrate: three Li⁺ cations and a citrate trianion.

The citrate part is a central carbon bearing an OH group and three carboxylate arms. Two arms are CH2–C(=O)[O⁻] and one is a carboxylate attached directly to the central carbon. The central carbon is not a stereocentre, since two of its substituents are identical.

The three lithium ions are written as separate fragments, each [Li+].
