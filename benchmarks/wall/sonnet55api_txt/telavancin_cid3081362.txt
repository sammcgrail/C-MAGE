This is telavancin: a vancomycin-type glycopeptide core, with the decylaminoethyl group on the vancosamine nitrogen and a phosphonomethylaminomethyl group on the resorcinol ring. I did not build the stereo-SMILES from scratch. I took the vancomycin isomeric SMILES from memory and edited it to match the drawing, so the stereocentres rest on that recalled string rather than on a fresh reading of each wedge.

1. **Decyl chain:** I counted the zigzag vertices from the secondary amine at the lower right to the free end at the lower left. There are 10 carbons, so the chain is NCCNCCCCCCCCCC (ethylenediamine linker plus n-decyl).
2. **Vancosamine nitrogen:** In the vancomycin string the sugar carbon ends in `(C)N`. I replaced that terminal N with `NCCNCCCCCCCCCC`.
3. **Resorcinol ring:** The ring carbon between the two hydroxyl-bearing carbons carries CH2–NH–CH2–P(=O)(OH)2. In the vancomycin string this is the ring CH written as `C=C9O`. I replaced it with `C(CNCP(=O)(O)O)=C9O`.
4. **Everything else:** The N-methylleucine, asparagine side chain, two aryl chlorides, glucose with its hydroxymethyl, and the carboxylic acid are unchanged from vancomycin.
