I read this as digoxin with the terminal sugar's 4-OH replaced by a methoxy group. I started from the known digoxin stereo-SMILES and adjusted it to match the drawing.

- **Aglycone:** a steroid core with a butenolide (C8=CC(=O)OC8) at C17, hydroxyls at C12 and C14, and methyl groups at C10 and C13.
- **Orientation check:** the C17 substituent and both ring-junction methyls are drawn hashed, so the page shows the molecule from the opposite face. The C8-H, C9-H and C5-H wedges are consistent with natural 5β-digoxin stereochemistry on that basis.
- **Sugar chain:** three 2,6-dideoxy sugars (digitoxose type) are linked through glycosidic oxygens to C3 of the steroid.
- **Terminal sugar:** the top-left ring carries a free OH at C3 and an OMe at C4, which is the change from digoxin.

I took the sugar stereocentres from the digoxin SMILES rather than re-deriving each one from the wedges.
