The drawing shows the linear pentapeptide Tyr-Gly-Gly-Phe-Leu (Leu-enkephalin), read from the N-terminal tyrosine to the C-terminal leucine.

- **Tyrosine:** a para-hydroxyphenyl ring on a CH2, with a free NH2 on the hashed α-carbon. Working out the CIP priorities from the hashed bond gives S, which is L-Tyr.
- **Glycine ×2:** two CH2 units, each flanked by amide NH and C=O groups, with no stereocentre.
- **Phenylalanine:** an unsubstituted phenyl ring on a CH2. The CH2 sits on a wedged bond at the α-carbon, which works out to S (L-Phe).
- **Leucine:** an isobutyl side chain on a hashed bond at the α-carbon, ending in a free COOH. This also works out to S (L-Leu).
