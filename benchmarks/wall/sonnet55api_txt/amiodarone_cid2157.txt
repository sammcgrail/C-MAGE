- **Benzofuran core:** the furan O is bonded to C2, which carries an n-butyl chain (4 carbons). C3 is bonded to the carbonyl carbon, and the fused benzene ring completes the bicycle.
- **Ketone:** a C=O links benzofuran C3 to the substituted phenyl ring.
- **Phenyl ring:** it carries two iodines at the positions flanking the carbon that bears the O–CH2CH2–N(Et)2 chain.
- **Side chain:** the oxygen is followed by two CH2 groups and a diethylamino group.
