I read the structure as a sulfonamide-linked aryl system with a diol side chain.

- **Sulfonamide end:** a cyclopropane carbon carries the S(=O)(=O) group and a CH2–CH(OH)–CH2OH chain.
- **Central ring:** the sulfonamide NH is attached to a benzene carbon. One neighbour of that carbon bears OMe, and the other bears an NH–aryl group. The remaining ring positions, going from the NH-aryl carbon, are two adjacent C–F carbons and then a CH next to the OMe carbon.
- **Second aryl ring:** the NH is attached to a benzene carbon that is ortho to F and para to I.
- **Stereocentre:** the CH(OH) carbon is the only stereocentre drawn with a hash (the OH is hashed). With priorities OH > CH2OH > CH2(cyclopropyl) > H, the hashed OH puts H toward the viewer. The OH → CH2OH → CH2 sequence appears clockwise, so the true configuration is reversed, giving S. In the SMILES order I wrote, S corresponds to `[C@H]`.
