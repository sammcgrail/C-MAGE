This is flavoxate: a 3-methylflavone-8-carboxylic acid ester of 2-(piperidin-1-yl)ethanol.

Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Phenyl ring:** a benzene ring at the upper left, attached to C2 of the chromone.
- **Pyranone ring:** C2 is bonded to the phenyl, to the ring oxygen, and by a double bond to C3. C3 carries a methyl group. C4 is a C=O, and C4a and C8a are the ring-fusion carbons.
- **Fused benzene ring:** C8a is bonded to the ring oxygen, and C8 carries the ester group.
- **Ester side chain:** C8 bears C(=O)O–CH2CH2–N, and the nitrogen is part of a piperidine ring.
