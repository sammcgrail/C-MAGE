The drawing shows aspartic acid: a central CH carrying NH2, one COOH group directly attached, and a CH2 leading to a second COOH group.

For stereochemistry, the only stereo bond is the hashed bond to NH2, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > COOH > CH2 > H. Going N (down) → COOH (upper left) → CH2 (upper right) looks clockwise on the page. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is counterclockwise, which is S (L-aspartic acid).
