Reading the drawing, I get the following.

- **Left end:** a quaternary carbon carries two methyl groups and a CH2OH group.
- **Stereocentre:** the quaternary carbon is bonded to a CH carbon with a wedged OH. This is the only stereocentre drawn with a wedge.
- **Amide:** the CH carbon is bonded to a C(=O) that is joined to an NH.
- **Right end:** the NH is bonded to a three-carbon chain (CH2CH2CH2) ending in OH.

Together these describe a panthenol-type structure.

For the stereochemistry, I viewed the wedged centre with the implicit H pointing away from the viewer. The neighbours then run quaternary C, then OH, then C=O in a clockwise direction. Writing the SMILES as `CC(C)(CO)[C@@H](O)C(=O)NCCCO`, the neighbour order is quaternary C, H, O, C=O, and `@@` matches that clockwise arrangement.
