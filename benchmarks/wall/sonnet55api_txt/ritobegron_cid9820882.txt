I read the molecule as a 4-hydroxyphenyl ring joined to a CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–NH–CH2CH2 chain. That chain leads to a 2,5-dimethylphenyl ring carrying an OCH2COOH group para to the ethyl chain.

**Skeleton**
- Left ring: a para-substituted phenol, with an OH at one end and the CH(OH) carbon at the other.
- Chain: aryl–CH(OH)–CH(CH3)–NH–CH2–CH2–aryl.
- Right ring: six aromatic carbons.
  - The ethyl chain is on one carbon.
  - A methyl group is on each of the two ring carbons adjacent to the O-bearing carbon.
  - The ring carbon opposite the ethyl chain bears O–CH2–C(=O)OH.

**Stereochemistry**
- The CH(CH3)N carbon has a hashed methyl. The implicit H therefore points toward the viewer.
  - The priority order is N > CH(OH) > CH3 > H.
  - As drawn this runs clockwise, and reversing for the H gives S.
- The CH(OH) carbon has a hashed OH. Its implicit H also points toward the viewer.
  - The priority order is OH > CH(N) > aryl > H.
  - As drawn this runs counterclockwise, and reversing for the H gives R.
- Writing the SMILES in the order methyl, N-bearing carbon, carbinol carbon, the tag `[C@H]` gives S at the first centre and `[C@H]` gives R at the second.

SMILES: C[C@H](NCCc1cc(C)c(OCC(=O)O)cc1C)[C@H](O)c1ccc(O)cc1
