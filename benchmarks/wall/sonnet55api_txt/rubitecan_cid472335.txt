This is 9-nitrocamptothecin (rubitecan): the camptothecin pentacyclic core with a nitro group on the benzo ring.

- **Core:** the E-ring lactone (O–C(=O)–C(OH)(Et)) is fused to the pyridone, which is fused through the N–CH2 to the pyrroloquinoline portion.
- **Nitro group:** it sits on the benzo-ring carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon that is bonded to the CH. That puts it peri to the C7–H, which is position 9.
