The drawing shows a central phosphorus atom with a double bond to oxygen (P=O) and single bonds to three nitrogen atoms. Each nitrogen carries two methyl groups, so each is an N(CH3)2 group. This is hexamethylphosphoramide (HMPA). There are no wedges, hashes or charges, so no stereochemistry is needed.
