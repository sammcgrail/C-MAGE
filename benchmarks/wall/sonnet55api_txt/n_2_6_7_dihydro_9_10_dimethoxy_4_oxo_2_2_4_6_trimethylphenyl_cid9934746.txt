The drawing shows a pyrimido[6,1-a]isoquinolin-4-one core with these features:

- The dimethoxybenzene ring is fused to a CH2CH2 bridge that leads to the ring nitrogen N.
- That N is bonded to the C=C carbon, which sits on the aromatic ring, and to the C=O carbon.
- The C=O carbon is bonded to the second ring nitrogen, which carries the CH2CH2NHC(=O)NH2 chain.
- The second ring nitrogen is also bonded to the carbon bearing the exocyclic imine, C(=N-mesityl).
- That carbon is single-bonded to the =CH carbon, and the =CH carbon is double-bonded to the aromatic-attached carbon.

Reading the atoms in order:

- Ring N (bottom): N1, bonded to CH2, to the C=O carbon, and to the C= carbon.
- The C= carbon (C11b) is bonded to the aromatic ring carbon, with a double bond to the CH.
- The CH is bonded to C(=N-Ar), which is bonded to N(CCNC(N)=O), which is bonded to C(=O), which closes back to N1.

After that comes c(OC), then cc3 for the CH and the fusion carbon bearing the CH2CH2. This matches the drawing.
