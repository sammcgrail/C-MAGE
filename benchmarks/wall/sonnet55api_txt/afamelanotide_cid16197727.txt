The stereochemistry is partly an assumption. I assigned all residues as L except Phe as D. I did not verify each wedge or hash against the drawing, since hash direction depends on how the chain is oriented.

Region by region, from the N-terminus:
- **Ac-Ser-Tyr-Ser:** an N-acetyl cap, then serine, tyrosine (para-hydroxyphenyl) and serine.
- **Nle:** a norleucine with a plain n-butyl side chain.
- **Glu-His:** glutamic acid with a free carboxylic acid, then histidine with an imidazole.
- **D-Phe:** phenylalanine with a hashed bond to the benzyl group, drawn as the D form.
- **Arg-Trp-Gly:** arginine with a guanidine, tryptophan with an indole, then glycine.
- **Lys-Pro-Val-NH2:** lysine with a free amine, proline in a five-membered ring, and valine ending in a primary amide.

For the proline centre, I checked the written ring-closure order against L-proline and used `[C@H]` for it.
