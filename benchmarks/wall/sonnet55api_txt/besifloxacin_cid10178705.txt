I read the structure as a fluoroquinolone: a 4-oxo-quinoline-3-carboxylic acid core with an N-cyclopropyl group, 8-Cl, 6-F, and a 3-aminoazepane attached through its ring nitrogen at C7.

**Core and substituents**
- The carboxylic acid is on C3 of the pyridone ring, with the C4 ketone next to it.
- The ring nitrogen carries the cyclopropyl group.
- On the benzo ring, Cl is on the carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to N. The azepane nitrogen is on the next carbon, and F is on the one after that.

**Azepane ring**
- Counting around the ring gives 7 atoms: N, a CH2, the CH bearing NH2, then four more CH2 groups back to N.
- The NH2 is drawn on a bold wedge, so this carbon is a defined stereocentre.

**Stereochemistry**
- The CIP priorities at the stereocentre are NH2, then the CH2 leading to the ring nitrogen, then the other ring CH2, then H.
- The wedge puts the NH2 toward the viewer and the H away. In the drawing, going from NH2 (up) to the CH2–N (lower right) to the CH2–C (lower left) is clockwise, so the centre is R.
- In the SMILES below, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is the preceding CH2, then H, then N, then the CH2 attached to ring N. For that order, `@@` corresponds to R.

SMILES: OC(=O)C1=CN(C2CC2)c2c(Cl)c(N3CCCC[C@@H](N)C3)c(F)cc2C1=O
