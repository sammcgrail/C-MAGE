The drawing shows tenofovir disoproxil (the bis-isopropyl carbonate prodrug of tenofovir).

- **Base:** an adenine (6-aminopurine) attached through N9 to a CH2.
- **Stereocentre:** the CH2 leads to a CH that carries a hashed methyl, an ether O and an implicit H. Ranking O > CH2N > CH3 > H, the O → CH2 → CH3 sequence runs counterclockwise with the hashed methyl pointing away, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Reversing for the H gives R, which I wrote as `C[C@H](CN…)O…`.
- **Phosphonate:** the ether O connects through OCH2 to a P(=O) centre.
- **Side chains:** P carries two identical –O–CH2–O–C(=O)–O–CH(CH3)2 groups (isopropyl carbonates).

SMILES: C[C@H](Cn1cnc2c(N)ncnc12)OCP(=O)(OCOC(=O)OC(C)C)OCOC(=O)OC(C)C
