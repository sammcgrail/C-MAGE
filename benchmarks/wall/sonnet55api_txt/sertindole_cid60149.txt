This structure is sertindole, which I identified from the drawing and then checked region by region.

- **Imidazolidin-2-one:** a five-membered ring made of C(=O), NH, CH2, CH2 and N. The ring nitrogen N is attached to a two-carbon chain.
- **Linker and piperidine:** the chain is N–CH2–CH2–N(piperidine). The piperidine carbon opposite its nitrogen (C4) bonds to the indole C3.
- **Indole:** the indole nitrogen N1 carries the 4-fluorophenyl group. Chlorine sits on the benzo ring at C5. This position is meta to the C3a ring-fusion carbon, on the carbon bonded to the CH between C3a and C5, which is C4.
