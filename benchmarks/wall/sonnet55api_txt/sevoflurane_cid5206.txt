The drawing shows a fluoromethyl ether: F–CH2–O–CH(CF3)2.

- On the left, F is bonded to a CH2 carbon.
- That CH2 is bonded to O.
- O is bonded to a central CH carbon.
- The central CH carbon carries two CF3 groups, one at the upper right and one below.

This is sevoflurane. All bonds are plain, so there is no stereochemistry. The central carbon is not a stereocentre because its two CF3 groups are identical.

SMILES: FCOC(C(F)(F)F)C(F)(F)F
