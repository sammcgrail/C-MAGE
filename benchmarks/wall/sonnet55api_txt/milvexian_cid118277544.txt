I read the structure as a macrocyclic lactam fused around a pyridine and a pyrazole, with a pyrimidinone carrying a chloro-aryl triazole substituent.

**Macrocycle.** The ring runs: a CH(CH3) carbon, then C(=O), then NH. The NH attaches to the pyrazole, which connects to the pyridine. The pyridine carbon next to its ring nitrogen bonds to a CH that sits at the end of a three-carbon CH2 chain leading back to the CH(CH3).

**Pyrazole.** It has an N-CHF2 group. Its carbon bearing the NH is adjacent to a CH, and the ring is C(NH)–CH=N–N(CHF2)–C(pyridyl).

**Pyrimidinone.** The ring nitrogen N3 bonds to the stereocentre CH. The ring continues N3–CH=N–C(aryl)=CH–C(=O).

**Aryl and triazole.** The aryl ring carries a Cl para to the triazole nitrogen attachment. The triazole is a 1,2,3-triazole bonded through N1 and has a Cl on C4.

**Stereochemistry.** Two wedges are drawn.
- The methyl-bearing carbon has a wedged methyl, which gives `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order below.
- The CH attached to the pyrimidinone nitrogen has a wedged N, which gives `[C@H]`.
