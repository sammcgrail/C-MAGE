This is baricitinib. I read it in four parts.

- **Ethylsulfonyl group:** CH3-CH2-S(=O)(=O)- is attached to the azetidine nitrogen.
- **Azetidine ring:** a four-membered ring made of N, CH2, a quaternary C and CH2. The quaternary carbon carries the CH2C#N group and the pyrazole nitrogen.
- **Pyrazole:** N1 is bonded to the azetidine carbon, N2 is the double-bonded nitrogen, then C3H, then C4, which bears the bicyclic substituent, then C5H.
- **Pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidine:** the pyrimidine carbon attached to the pyrazole is bonded to the ring N and to the fused carbon. The fused pyrrole ring has NH, CH=CH, and the two shared carbons.

- Ethyl: CC

- Pyrazole: n1cc(cn1)
