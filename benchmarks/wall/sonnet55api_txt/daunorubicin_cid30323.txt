The drawing shows an anthracycline of the daunorubicin type: a tetracyclic aglycone carrying a daunosamine-type sugar. I read it region by region and then matched the stereochemistry to daunorubicin from memory, so I did not derive every centre from the wedges independently.

- **Aromatic ring:** one methoxy group sits next to the ring-fusion carbon that connects to the lower quinone C=O.
- **Quinone and hydroquinone rings:** two C=O groups and two phenolic OH groups, with the lower OH adjacent to the carbon attached to the C–O–sugar carbon.
- **Saturated ring:**
  - one quaternary carbon bearing OH (wedge) and an acetyl group;
  - one CH2;
  - one CH bearing the glycosidic oxygen (wedge).
- **Sugar ring:** a tetrahydropyran with:
  - an anomeric carbon with a hashed bond to the glycosidic oxygen;
  - a CH2;
  - a CH–NH2 (wedge);
  - a CH–OH (wedge);
  - a CH–CH3 (wedge) next to the ring oxygen.
- **Sugar stereochemistry:** the NH2 and OH wedges are cis, which fits daunorubicin rather than the 4'-epimer, epirubicin.
