This drawing matches 25-hydroxyvitamin D3 (calcifediol). I identified it as this known molecule and wrote the SMILES largely from memory of its standard form. I did not independently derive the stereocentres or the double-bond geometry from the wedges, hashes and bond angles in the image.

- **Side chain:** a C20 methyl on a wedge, then a three-carbon chain ending in a tertiary carbon bearing two methyls and an OH (the 25-hydroxy group).
- **Hydrindane core:** a cyclopentane fused to a cyclohexane, with a wedged methyl at the ring-fusion carbon and a hashed H at the other ring-fusion carbon.
- **Triene link:** an exocyclic C=C from the six-membered ring, then CH=CH, then C=C into the A-ring.
- **A-ring:** a cyclohexane with an exocyclic =CH2 and a hashed OH on the carbon at the far right of the drawing.
