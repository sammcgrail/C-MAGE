This structure is iopamidol, a tri-iodinated isophthalamide contrast agent.

- **Core:** A benzene ring carries three iodines. Two adjacent ring positions carry C(=O)NH–CH(CH2OH)2 amide arms, and the third carries the lactamide NH.
- **Side chains:** Each of the two amide arms ends in a serinol-type group, N–CH(CH2OH)CH2OH. The third substituent is NH–C(=O)–CH(OH)CH3.
- **Stereochemistry:** The only drawn stereo-bond is the wedge on the lactamide carbon, so only that centre gets a stereo mark. The wedged methyl points toward the viewer and the implicit H points away. Going OH → C(=O) → CH3 then runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S. The SMILES `C[C@H](O)C(=O)N...` encodes this S configuration.
- **Serinol carbons:** The two serinol CH carbons are drawn with plain bonds, so they get no stereo marks.

SMILES: C[C@H](O)C(=O)Nc1c(I)c(C(=O)NC(CO)CO)c(I)c(C(=O)NC(CO)CO)c1I
