I read this as irinotecan, a camptothecin-type pentacyclic core with a 4-piperidinopiperidine carbamate.

- **Core:** a lactone ring (C(=O)–O–CH2) carries a quaternary carbon bearing an ethyl group and an OH. That carbon is fused to a pyridone ring, which is fused through a CH2–N five-membered ring to a quinoline unit.
- **Quinoline substituents:** one ethyl sits on the carbon next to the benzo ring fusion. An O–C(=O)–N carbamate is on the benzo ring, two carbons away from that fusion carbon.
- **Carbamate:** the carbamate nitrogen is part of a piperidine ring. That ring's 4-position carries the nitrogen of a second, plain piperidine ring.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the quaternary carbon with the wedged OH. The CIP priorities there are OH > lactone C(=O)O > aromatic C > ethyl. With OH on the wedge, I get the S configuration. The SMILES below was checked to give S as well.
