This is my best reading of the drawing as hydroxocobalamin, with the Co³⁺ and hydroxide drawn as separate ions. The connectivity was traced atom by atom. The stereodescriptors are partly assigned from the known cobalamin pattern rather than read cleanly from every wedge and hash, so some may be wrong. The corrin carbons carrying only plain bonds have no stereo marks.

- **Base:** a 5,6-dimethylbenzimidazole attached through N to the ribose C1.
- **Sugar and linker:** the ribose carries a CH₂OH and a 3-phosphate (O⁻). The phosphate is esterified to a 1-aminopropan-2-yl group, which is acylated through an amide to the propionamide side chain on ring D.
- **Corrin ring:**
  - One anionic N⁻ in ring D.
  - Imine-type N atoms in the other rings.
  - Direct ring A–D bond.
  - Gem-dimethyl on ring C.
  - Three meso-methyl groups.
  - Seven amide side chains (CH₂CONH₂ and CH₂CH₂CONH₂).
- **Counter-ions:** a separate Co³⁺ and a separate hydroxide.
