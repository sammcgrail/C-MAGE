I read this as ipratropium bromide: an N-isopropyl, N-methyl nortropane quaternary ammonium with a tropic acid ester, and a bromide counterion.

**Structure read from the drawing**
- **Bicyclic core:** The N⁺ bridges two bridgehead carbons, each carrying a wedged H. One bridge is a two-carbon CH₂–CH₂ unit on the left. The other is a three-carbon CH₂–CH(O)–CH₂ unit on the right, and the middle CH bears the ester oxygen.
- **N substituents:** The N⁺ also carries a methyl and an isopropyl group.
- **Ester side chain:** The oxygen leads to C(=O)–CH(CH₂OH)–phenyl.
- **Counterion:** Br⁻.

**Stereochemistry**
- Only the two bridgehead carbons have wedges, so only they get stereo marks. I assigned their @/@@ from the drawn geometry. The ester-bearing CH carbon and the tropic acid CH are drawn with plain bonds, so they get none.
