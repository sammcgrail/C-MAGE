This is rolapitant hydrochloride monohydrate. The skeleton is a 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)phenyl group on a CH(CH3)–O–CH2 chain. The CH2 is attached to a quaternary carbon that carries a phenyl ring and sits in a piperidine. That piperidine is spiro-fused to a γ-lactam (a pyrrolidin-2-one). HCl and H2O are drawn as separate components.

Stereochemistry was read from the three drawn wedge or hash bonds:
- **CH(CH3)–O carbon:** the methyl is on a wedge, which gives `[C@@H]`.
- **Quaternary carbon bearing phenyl:** the CH2O bond is hashed, which gives `[C@@]`.
- **Spiro carbon:** the ring CH2 bond is wedged, which gives `[C@]`.
