Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Amine:** the nitrogen carries two methyl groups (the two lines to the upper left and lower left) and is bonded to a CH carbon.
- **Central CH:** this carbon connects to the nitrogen, to a quaternary carbon, and to a CH2.
- **Isobutyl chain:** the CH2 leads to a CH that carries two methyl groups, so the chain is CH2–CH(CH3)2.
- **Cyclobutane:** the quaternary carbon is a vertex of a four-membered ring. The ring atoms are the quaternary carbon, the carbon at (318, 418), the carbon at (404, 204), and the carbon at (618, 291).
- **Aryl group:** the quaternary carbon is also bonded to a benzene ring. The ring has a chlorine on the carbon para to this attachment point.

Putting these together gives CN(C)C(CC(C)C)C1(CCC1)c1ccc(Cl)cc1. There are no wedges or hashes in the drawing, so no stereochemistry is included.
