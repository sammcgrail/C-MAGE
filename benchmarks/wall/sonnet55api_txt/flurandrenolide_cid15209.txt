I read this as the 1,2-dihydro analogue of flunisolide: a 6α-fluoro, 11β,21-dihydroxy, 16α,17-isopropylidenedioxy pregn-4-ene-3,20-dione. Ring A shows only the C4=C5 double bond, with the C3 ketone beside it.

I did not derive the stereo marks atom by atom from the wedges. I took them from the flunisolide SMILES I remember and changed ring A from C=C to CC. That makes the stereochemistry the least certain part of this answer.

The other features match the drawing:
- The fluorine sits on C6, next to the C4=C5 double bond.
- The hydroxyl is on C11 and the angular methyls are on C10 and C13.
- The acetonide is a five-membered dioxolane bridging C16 and C17, with two methyls on the ketal carbon.
- The side chain on C17 is C(=O)CH2OH.
