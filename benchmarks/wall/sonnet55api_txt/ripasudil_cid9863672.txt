I read the molecule as 4-fluoro-5-[(2S)-2-methyl-1,4-diazepan-1-yl]sulfonylisoquinoline, which matches ripasudil.

- **Isoquinoline core:** The bicyclic aromatic system has one ring nitrogen. Fluorine sits on the carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon. The sulfonyl group sits on the benzo-ring carbon adjacent to that same fusion carbon.
- **Sulfonamide:** The sulfur carries two double-bonded oxygens and links the aromatic carbon to a ring nitrogen.
- **Diazepane ring:** This is a seven-membered ring containing N(SO2), a CH(CH3) carbon, a CH2, an NH, and then three CH2 groups closing back to the sulfonamide nitrogen.
- **Stereochemistry:** The methyl on the CH carbon is drawn with a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer. With the implicit hydrogen toward the viewer, the sequence methyl → CH2 → N runs counterclockwise on the page. For the SMILES atom order Me, H, N (ring closure), CH2, this corresponds to `@`, giving `C[C@H]1CNCCCN1...`.
