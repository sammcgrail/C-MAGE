This is binimetinib, a benzimidazole carboxamide with a hydroxyethoxy amide, a 4-bromo-2-fluoroanilino group, a fluorine on the core, and an N-methyl.

**Aniline ring.** Br sits on the top carbon. Going around the ring, the carbon bearing F is meta to Br, and the carbon bearing NH lies between that F carbon and the next ring carbon. So the ring is 4-bromo-2-fluorophenyl attached to NH.

**Benzimidazole core.**
- The carbon bearing F is bonded to C3a, which is bonded to N3 (the "N=" nitrogen).
- N3 connects to CH=, then to N1(CH3), then to C7a, which is bonded to the aromatic CH.
- That CH is bonded to the carbon bearing C(=O)NH, which is bonded to the carbon bearing the NHAr group, which is bonded back to the carbon bearing F.

**Side chain.** The amide is C(=O)NHOCH2CH2OH.

**SMILES.**
Cn1cnc2c(F)c(Nc3ccc(Br)cc3F)c(C(=O)NOCCO)cc21

I checked the ring closures. Here n1 is N1 with the methyl, c is C2, n is N3, c2 is C3a, c(F) is C4, c(N...) is C5, c(C(=O)...) is C6, c is C7, and c21 is C7a, which closes both rings. This matches the drawing.
