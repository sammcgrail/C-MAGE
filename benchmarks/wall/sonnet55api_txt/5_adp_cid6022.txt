The drawing shows adenosine 5'-diphosphate (ADP):

- **Base:** an adenine unit, a purine with an NH2 group on the six-membered ring, attached through its N9 nitrogen to the sugar.
- **Sugar:** a five-membered ring made of four carbons and one oxygen. The base (at C1') and the CH2 group (at C4') are both on wedged bonds, and the two ring OH groups (at C2' and C3') are on hashed bonds. This matches the natural β-D-ribose configuration.
- **Phosphates:** the CH2 carries an O–P(=O)(OH)–O–P(=O)(OH)2 chain, which is a diphosphate.
