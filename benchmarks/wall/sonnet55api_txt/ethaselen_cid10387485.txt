The drawing shows two benzisoselenazolone units joined through their nitrogen atoms by an ethylene bridge (–CH2–CH2–).

Each unit is a benzene ring fused to a five-membered ring. That five-membered ring contains Se, N, a C=O carbon, and the two fused aromatic carbons, with an Se–N bond closing the ring. This is the ebselen-type scaffold. Each N carries the CH2 of the linker.

One unit written as SMILES: O=C1c2ccccc2[Se]N1R, where R is the linker CH2.

Joining the two units through the two-carbon linker gives:

O=C1c2ccccc2[Se]N1CCN1[Se]c2ccccc2C1=O

Ring-closure check for the second unit: N1 opens ring 1, then [Se], then c2ccccc2 (the benzene ring), then C1=O closes ring 1 back to N. The ring is N, Se, c, c, C, which is five atoms. This matches the drawing.
