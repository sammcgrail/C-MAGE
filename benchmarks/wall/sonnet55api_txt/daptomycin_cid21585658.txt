I read the drawing as a decanoyl-capped peptide: a cyclic depsipeptide ring with a three-residue tail. It matches the daptomycin type of structure, but I assigned each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes in the image rather than from memory.

**Tail, from the decanoyl end:**
- A decanoyl chain, counted as 10 carbons including the C=O.
- Trp, with the L configuration taken from its wedged CH2.
- Asn, which comes out as the D configuration.
- Asp, with the L configuration.

**Ring:**
- The ring closes through an ester between the Thr side-chain oxygen and the C-terminal carbonyl of the Kyn residue, written as ring closure 1 in the SMILES.
- Going around from Thr: Gly, Orn (free NH2 side chain), Asp, Ala, Asp, Gly, Ser, 3-methyl-Glu, then Kyn.
- Kyn carries a 2-aminophenyl ketone side chain.
- Ala and Ser come out D, and Orn and both ring Asp residues come out L.
- The 3-methyl-Glu carries two stereocentres, both assigned from its wedge and hash.
