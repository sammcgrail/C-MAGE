The drawing shows a 1,3-indandione core: a benzene ring fused to a five-membered ring that carries two C=O groups. The carbon between the two carbonyls (C2) bears a 4-chlorophenyl group. This is chlorindione.

The ring-fusion carbons and the remaining benzo ring carbons are aromatic, and the para-substituted chlorophenyl ring is attached at C2. C2 is drawn with plain bonds and has an H, so it gets no stereo marking.
