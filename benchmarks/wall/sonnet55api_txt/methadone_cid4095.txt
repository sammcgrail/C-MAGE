The drawing shows methadone: an ethyl ketone (CH3CH2–C(=O)) attached to a quaternary carbon that carries two phenyl rings. That carbon is also bonded to a CH2, which leads to a CH bearing a methyl and an N(CH3)2 group.

Reading the structure atom by atom:
- Top chain: CH3–CH2–C(=O)–.
- The carbonyl carbon is bonded to the quaternary carbon, which carries two phenyl rings.
- The quaternary carbon is also bonded to CH2–CH(CH3)–N(CH3)2.

The CH(CH3) carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I give no stereo.

SMILES: CCC(=O)C(CC(C)N(C)C)(c1ccccc1)c1ccccc1
