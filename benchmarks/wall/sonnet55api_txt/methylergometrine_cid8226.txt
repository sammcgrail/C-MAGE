I read this as methylergometrine (methylergonovine), the lysergic acid amide of (S)-2-aminobutan-1-ol. I assigned the side-chain stereocentre from the drawing and took the two ring stereocentres from the known compound rather than working them out separately from the wedges.

- **Core:** an ergoline ring system with an indole NH, an N-methyl piperidine ring, and a C=C in the D ring conjugated to the aromatic ring.
- **Amide:** a carboxamide on the D ring, with its carbon on a hashed bond.
- **Side chain:** the amide N bears CH(CH2OH)CH2CH3, with the ethyl group on a hashed bond. Reading the drawing, this carbon is S, and the SMILES `CC[C@@H](CO)N` also works out to S.
- **Ring stereocentres:** the hashed H at the ring-fusion carbon and the hashed amide bond at C8 follow the known configuration of methylergometrine.
