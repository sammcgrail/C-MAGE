The drawing shows a 1,3,5-triazinane-2,4,6-trione ring (a triazinetrione) attached to a substituted diaryl ether.

**Triazinetrione ring.** The ring atoms, going around, are:
- N(CH3)
- C(=O), reached from the N-methyl nitrogen and bonded to the aryl-bearing nitrogen
- N(aryl)
- C(=O), bonded to N(aryl) and NH
- NH
- C(=O), bonded to NH and closing back to N(CH3)

**Aryl group.** The ring nitrogen is bonded to a benzene ring. Going around that ring from the nitrogen attachment:
- C1, attached to the triazine N
- C2, an aromatic CH
- C3, bearing the methyl group
- C4, bearing the oxygen of the ether
- C5, an aromatic CH
- C6, an aromatic CH

The methyl and the ether oxygen are on adjacent carbons (C3 and C4). The ether oxygen is therefore para to the triazine nitrogen and ortho to the methyl.

**Second aryl ring.** The ether oxygen links to a para-substituted benzene ring that carries S-CF3 at the position opposite the oxygen.
