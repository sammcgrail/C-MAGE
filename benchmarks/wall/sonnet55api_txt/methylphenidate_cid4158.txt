The drawing shows a methyl ester (CH3–O–C(=O)–) attached to a central CH. That CH also carries a phenyl ring and a piperidin-2-yl ring (a six-membered ring containing NH). This is the structure of methylphenidate.

All bonds are plain lines with no wedges or hashes, so I have included no stereochemistry.

SMILES: COC(=O)C(c1ccccc1)C1CCCCN1
