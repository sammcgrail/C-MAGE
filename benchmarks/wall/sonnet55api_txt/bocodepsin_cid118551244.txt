**Macrocycle** (going around the ring):

- That leads to the ring CH–O, which closes through the ester O–C(=O) to the ring valine α-carbon.
- The valine α-carbon carries an isopropyl group and an NH.
- The NH is bonded to C(=O)–C(CH3)2–NH–C(=O), which returns to the thiazole C4.

**Side chain:** the ring CH–O carries a CH=CH–CH2–CH2–S–C(=O)–CH(NH2)–CH(CH3)2 chain. The double bond is drawn in the trans zigzag, so I wrote it as E.

**Stereochemistry:** I assigned the three stereocentres from the wedges and hashes using CIP priorities.
- The side-chain valine α-carbon (hashed NH2) is S.
- The ring valine α-carbon (hashed isopropyl) is S.
- The ring CH–O carbon (wedged vinyl) is S.
