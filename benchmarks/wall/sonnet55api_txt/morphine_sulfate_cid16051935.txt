This drawing is morphine sulfate: two identical morphine molecules plus one sulfuric acid, written as three dot-separated components.

- **Morphine core:** a phenanthrene-derived pentacyclic skeleton with a phenol OH on the aromatic ring and an ether oxygen bridge. It has a cyclohexene ring carrying a secondary alcohol (the wedged OH) and an N-methylpiperidine ring.
- **Sulfuric acid:** S with two =O and two OH groups.
- **Stereochemistry:** the five stereocentres are marked by wedges and hashes. I did not derive the @/@@ labels from the drawing. I wrote them from my recall of the standard morphine SMILES, so they are not independently confirmed against the wedges.
