**Left part.** A pyridine ring carries an N-methyl carboxamide (C(=O)NHCH3) at C2. The ring nitrogen is adjacent to C2, and the ether oxygen sits at C4.

**Middle part.** The oxygen links the pyridine to a central benzene ring. On that ring, the urea NH is para to the oxygen, and a fluorine is ortho to the urea NH, which makes it meta to the oxygen.

**Right part.** The urea carbonyl connects through a second NH to a phenyl ring. That ring has Cl at the para position and CF3 at the meta position, adjacent to the Cl.
