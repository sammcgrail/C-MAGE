Reading the structure region by region:

- **Benzo ring:** the sulfur sits on the ring carbon on the right, at the position para to the fused carbon bonded to the N= atom, as drawn.
- **Right:** the sulfur carries an =O and is bonded to a phenyl group, so it is a sulfoxide (S(=O)c1ccccc1).
