I read this as valrubicin (N‑trifluoroacetyladriamycin‑14‑valerate), an anthracycline, and wrote the SMILES from that identification rather than from independently assigning each wedge and hash.

- **Ester side chain:** a pentanoate ester (CCCCC(=O)O–) is attached to a CH2 that sits on the ketone, giving C(=O)COC(=O)CCCC on the tertiary carbinol carbon.
- **Aglycone:** a tetracyclic anthraquinone core with two phenolic OH groups, two ring C=O groups and one OMe, plus the tertiary alcohol.
- **Sugar:** a daunosamine‑type pyranose with a ring O, a methyl, an OH and an NHC(=O)CF3 group, linked through the anomeric carbon to the aglycone via O.

For stereochemistry I took the doxorubicin SMILES (same aglycone and sugar configuration), kept its stereocentre order, and replaced the free NH2 with the trifluoroacetamide and the terminal CH2OH with the valerate ester. These substitutions leave the stereocentres unchanged.
