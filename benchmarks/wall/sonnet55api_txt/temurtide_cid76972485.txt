I read this as an open-chain N-acetylmuramic acid (an aldehyde) whose lactyl ether is joined by an amide to a threonine. That threonine is in turn amide-linked to an isoglutamine, which has a free γ-carboxylic acid and a primary amide.

Stereochemistry was assigned only at the wedge or hash centres. I worked each centre out from the 2D drawing, with the implicit H taken as the opposite face to the wedge or hash.

- The sugar chain runs from the acetamido carbon, bearing the CHO and hashed NH, through three further carbons. The ether O (wedge) is on the second, a hashed OH on the third, and a wedged OH on the fourth, followed by CH2OH.
- The lactyl carbon has a wedged methyl.
- The threonine α carbon has a wedged NH, and its β carbon has a wedged methyl.
- The isoglutamine α carbon has a wedged CH2.
