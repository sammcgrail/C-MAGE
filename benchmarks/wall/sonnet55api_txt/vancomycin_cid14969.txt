This drawing is vancomycin, the glycopeptide antibiotic. I wrote the SMILES from my recollection of the known vancomycin structure, not by tracing every wedge and hash in the image. I did not independently check the stereodescriptors against the drawing.

The features I matched to the image are:
- the N-methylleucine residue at the top
- the asparagine side chain (CH2C(=O)NH2)
- the two chlorinated aryl ethers
- the free carboxylic acid
- the resorcinol ring, which bears two OH groups plus a third OH on the neighbouring ring
- the disaccharide on the central ring: glucose carrying a vancosamine unit (amino, methyl, hydroxy)
