- **Tetrazole:** a 1-methyltetrazol-5-yl ring. Its carbon is bonded to S, which links through a CH2 to C3 of the dihydrothiazine ring.
- **Dihydrothiazine ring:** S–CH2–C(CH2S-tetrazole)=C(COOH)–N, fused to the β-lactam at the CH carbon (C6, carrying a wedged H) and at N.
- **β-Lactam:** the ring has C6, C7 and the C=O carbon, plus N. C7 carries OMe and an NH-acyl group.
- **Side chain:** the amide is NH–C(=O)–CH2–S–CH2–C≡N.

**Stereochemistry:** C6 has a wedged H, and C7 has a hashed bond to NH.

- At C7, the SMILES order is O, C8 (carbonyl), N, C6. With N pointing away, C8→O→C6 runs counterclockwise on the page, which gives `@`.
- At C6, the order is C7, H, N, S. With H toward the viewer, C7→N→S runs clockwise, which gives `@` for this neighbour order.
