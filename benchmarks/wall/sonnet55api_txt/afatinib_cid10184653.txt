The drawing shows afatinib. Reading it region by region:

- **Side chain:** a dimethylamino group (NMe2) is attached to a CH2, then a trans CH=CH, then a C(=O)NH amide.
- **Core:** the amide NH sits on a quinazoline, at the benzo-ring carbon next to the carbon bearing the ether oxygen.
- **Position 4 of the quinazoline:** an NH links to a 3-chloro-4-fluorophenyl ring.
- **Ether substituent:** the benzo-ring oxygen is attached to the 3-position of a tetrahydrofuran ring, with a wedge bond on the C–O bond.

For the tetrahydrofuran stereocentre, the wedged O points toward the viewer, so the implicit H is at the back. The priorities are O > CH2–O > CH2–C > H. Going from O (down) to CH2O (upper right) to CH2C (left) is counterclockwise, which gives S. The trans double bond is written with `/C=C/`.
