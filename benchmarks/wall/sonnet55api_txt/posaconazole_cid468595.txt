I read this as posaconazole. I worked out the two stereocentres in the left-hand side chain from the drawing and took the tetrahydrofuran stereocentres from the known posaconazole structure rather than deriving them from the wedges.

**Regions read from the drawing**
- **Left side chain:** an ethyl group on a CH that carries the triazolone N, next to a CH(OH)CH3.
- **Triazolone:** a 1,2,4-triazol-3-one ring with a C=O. Its other N is bonded to a para-phenylene.
- **Linker:** the phenylene connects through a piperazine to a second para-phenylene, which carries an O–CH2 group.
- **Tetrahydrofuran:** the O–CH2 attaches to a tetrahydrofuran ring. The ring carbon next to the ring oxygen bears a 2,4-difluorophenyl group and a CH2 linked to N1 of a 1,2,4-triazole.

**Stereochemistry**
- The ethyl-bearing CH has a hashed bond to the ethyl group, which puts the implicit H toward the viewer. With priorities N > CH(OH) > ethyl > H, this gives S.
- The CH(OH) carbon has a wedged methyl, which puts H at the back. With priorities OH > C(N) > CH3 > H, this also gives S.
- Both match the (2S,3S)-2-hydroxypentan-3-yl group of posaconazole, and I checked that the SMILES chirality tags reproduce these assignments.
