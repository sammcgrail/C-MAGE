This is eletriptan hydrobromide. I read it region by region:

- **Pyrrolidine:** an N-methylpyrrolidine ring (N with a methyl, then three ring CH2 groups and the stereocentre carbon).
- **Link to indole:** a CH2 joins the stereocentre to C3 of a 1H-indole (the drawn NH is part of the five-membered ring).
- **Indole substituent:** a CH2CH2 chain on C5 of the indole ends in a sulfonyl group (S with two =O), which carries a phenyl ring.
- **Salt:** HBr is drawn as a separate component.

**Stereochemistry:** The only wedge is on the bond from the pyrrolidine C2 to the exocyclic CH2. The CIP priorities at that carbon are N, then the exocyclic CH2 (its next atom is indole C3, with substituents C,C,C), then the ring CH2, then H. The H sits behind the plane. Going N → exocyclic CH2 → ring CH2 runs clockwise on the page, so the centre is R. In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]` written with the ring CH2 first encodes that R configuration.
