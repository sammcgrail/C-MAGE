I read this as flavopiridol: a chromone core with two ring hydroxyls, a 2-chlorophenyl group, and an N-methyl-3-hydroxypiperidin-4-yl substituent.

**Chromone core**
- The benzene ring carries two OH groups. One sits peri to the C=O carbon and the other is meta to it.
- The carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon bearing the ring oxygen carries the piperidine substituent.
- The pyranone ring contains a C=O, a CH=, and a C(Ar)–O unit, where Ar is 2-chlorophenyl.

**Piperidine ring**
- It is N-methylated.
- C4 is attached to the aromatic carbon by a hashed bond.
- C3 carries an OH, also drawn with a hashed bond.

**Stereochemistry**
- For both hashed carbons I took the implicit H to point toward the viewer. I then worked out the chirality from the 2D positions of the neighbours.
- C4 comes out as `[C@H]` with the neighbour order CH2, H, aryl, C3.
- C3 comes out as `[C@H]` with the neighbour order C4, H, O, CH2.
