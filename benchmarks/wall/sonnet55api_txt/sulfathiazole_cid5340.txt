This is sulfathiazole: a 4-aminobenzenesulfonamide whose sulfonamide nitrogen is attached to the 2-position of a thiazole ring.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- **Benzene ring:** a six-membered ring with an NH2 group on one carbon and the sulfonyl group on the carbon opposite it (para).
- **Sulfonyl group:** a sulfur with two double-bonded oxygens, joined to the benzene ring and to an NH.
- **Thiazole ring:** the NH connects to the ring carbon that sits between S and N. That carbon is double-bonded to the ring N (C=N). The ring then runs N–CH=CH–S back to the carbon, giving a five-membered ring of S, C2, N, C, C.

SMILES: Nc1ccc(cc1)S(=O)(=O)NC1=NC=CS1
