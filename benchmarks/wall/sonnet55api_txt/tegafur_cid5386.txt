The drawing shows tegafur: a 5-fluorouracil ring whose N1 carries a tetrahydrofuran-2-yl group.

**Pyrimidine ring.** The atoms run as follows:
- N1 is bonded to the THF carbon.
- C2 is a carbonyl (C=O).
- N3 is an NH.
- C4 is a carbonyl (C=O).
- C5 carries F and is double-bonded to C6.
- C6 is a CH bonded back to N1.

**Tetrahydrofuran ring.** The five-membered ring is O–CH(N)–CH2–CH2–CH2, with the CH attached to N1. The drawing has no wedges or hashes, so I added no stereochemistry.

**SMILES.** Writing the ring from the THF carbon: C1(CCCO1) attached to N1, then C(=O)NC(=O)C(F)=C1 closes the pyrimidine ring.

SMILES: O=C1NC(=O)C(F)=CN1C1CCCO1
