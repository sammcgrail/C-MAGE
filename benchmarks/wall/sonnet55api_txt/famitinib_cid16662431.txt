I read the molecule as a diethylaminoethyl lactam fused to a methylpyrrole, joined through a methylidene to a 5-fluoro-oxindole.

- **Left side:** an N,N-diethylaminoethyl chain (CCN(CC)CC) is attached to the lactam nitrogen of a 6,7-dihydro-pyrrolo[3,2-c]pyridin-4-one. This bicyclic core has a C=O at position 4, and the pyrrole NH is drawn.
- **Pyrrole:** the carbon next to the pyrrole NH carries the methylidene linker. The adjacent pyrrole carbon carries a methyl group.
- **Right side:** the linker CH is double-bonded to C3 of an oxindole (indolin-2-one) with a lactam NH. The fluorine sits on the benzo ring at position 5, para to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to the NH.
- **Double-bond geometry:** from the drawn coordinates, the pyrrole substituent and the oxindole C=O lie on the same side of the C=C, so the bond is Z. I encoded this with `/` and `\` marks.

The core is the sunitinib scaffold, but with the lactam-fused ring in place of sunitinib's carboxamide.
