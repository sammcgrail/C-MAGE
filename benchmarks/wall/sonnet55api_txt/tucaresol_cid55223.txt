The drawing shows two benzene rings joined by an –O–CH2– linker.

**Left ring.** It carries three substituents on adjacent carbons:
- a CHO group on the top carbon,
- an OH on the carbon to its left,
- the ether oxygen on the carbon to its lower right.

The CHO carbon sits between the OH-bearing and O-bearing carbons, so it is a 2-hydroxy-6-(benzyloxy)benzaldehyde pattern.

**Linker.** The ether oxygen connects to a CH2, which attaches to the right ring.

**Right ring.** The CH2 is on one carbon and the COOH is on the carbon para to it, giving a 4-substituted benzoic acid.
