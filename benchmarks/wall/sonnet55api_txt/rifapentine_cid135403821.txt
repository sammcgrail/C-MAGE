I read this as rifapentine, a rifamycin-type ansa macrolide. I wrote the SMILES from a recalled rifampicin SMILES rather than reading each stereocentre off the drawing, so the stereo descriptors are inferred from the natural rifamycin series and not independently checked against the wedges.

- **Naphthalene core:** the ring system carries the hydroxyls, a methyl, and the fused furanone with its ketone and quaternary methyl carbon.
- **Ansa chain:** it runs from the amide NH through the diene, the hydroxyl- and methyl-bearing carbons, the acetate ester, the methoxy carbon and the enol ether back to the furanone oxygen.
- **Side chain:** the aromatic ring bears a CH=N–N link to a piperazine, whose far nitrogen carries a cyclopentyl group. In rifampicin this nitrogen carries a methyl instead, so I swapped the methyl for cyclopentyl.
