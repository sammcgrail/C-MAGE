The image shows the tricyclic antidepressant skeleton of amitriptyline:

- A dimethylamino group (N with two methyls) is attached to a propylidene chain: N–CH2–CH2–CH=C.
- The double-bond carbon is the ring carbon of a central seven-membered ring. That ring is fused to two benzene rings and contains an –CH2–CH2– bridge.
- The two aryl branches on the exocyclic alkene carbon are constitutionally equivalent, because each leads through the ethylene bridge to the other ring. The double bond therefore has no E/Z designation, and I added no stereo marks.
