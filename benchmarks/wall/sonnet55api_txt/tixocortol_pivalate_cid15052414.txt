I read this as a cortisol-type steroid whose C21 hydroxyl is replaced by a pivaloyl thioester. I took the stereochemistry from the drawn wedges and hashes and matched it to the natural cortisol configuration rather than assigning each centre independently.

- **Side chain:** the left end is a tert-butyl group on a C(=O) that is bonded to S. The S connects through a CH2 to a ketone carbon, which is attached to C17. This gives the pivaloyl thioester of a 21-thiol.
- **D ring (five-membered):** C17 carries a hashed OH and the C20 ketone side chain. C13 carries a wedged methyl (C18).
- **C ring:** C11 carries a wedged OH (11β).
- **A ring:** there is a C4=C5 double bond and a C3 ketone, with a wedged methyl (C19) on C10.
- **Ring-fusion hydrogens:** H-8 is wedged, H-9 is hashed and H-14 is hashed, as in natural cortisol.
