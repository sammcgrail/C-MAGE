I read this as azithromycin, a 15-membered azalide macrolide. I did not check each stereocentre against the wedges and hashes in the drawing. The stereochemistry comes from my recollection of the published azithromycin SMILES, so it is not independently verified.

- **Macrocycle:** a 15-membered lactone ring containing one ring nitrogen, which carries an N-methyl group.
- **Ring substituents:** an ethyl group on the carbon next to the lactone oxygen, several ring methyl groups, and three hydroxyl groups.
- **Sugars:** cladinose (with its methoxy group) on the left and desosamine (with its dimethylamino group) on the upper right, each attached through a glycosidic oxygen.
