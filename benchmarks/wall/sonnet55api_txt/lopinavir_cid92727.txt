This structure is lopinavir, which I identified from the drawing. I worked out the stereochemistry at the carbon bearing the NH–C(=O)CH2OAr group and the hashed benzyl group directly from the wedges. I took the other three stereocentres from my recollection of the known lopinavir SMILES rather than deriving them from the wedges.

- **Left ring:** a 2,6-dimethylphenyl ring carries an aryl ether O–CH2–C(=O)–NH.
- **Core chain:** that NH is on a stereocentre bearing a hashed benzyl group. Next is a CH(OH) with a wedged OH, then a CH2, then a stereocentre bearing a benzyl group and an NH.
- **Lower unit:** that NH is acylated by a valine-derived carbon with an isopropyl group (wedged) and a ring nitrogen. The ring is a six-membered cyclic urea, N–CH2CH2CH2–NH–C(=O).
