I read this as ethambutol, with both stereocentres S.

**Connectivity.** The skeleton is HOCH2–CH(Et)–NH–CH2CH2–NH–CH(Et)–CH2OH. Each end has an ethyl group and a CH2OH on the same carbon as the NH. The two ends are linked through an N–CH2–CH2–N bridge.

**Stereochemistry.** Each stereocentre has a wedge bond to its ethyl carbon, so the implicit H points away from the viewer.
- Priorities at each centre are N > CH2OH > CH2CH3 > H.
- On the left centre, going N (right) → CH2OH (up) → ethyl (lower left) runs counterclockwise on the page. With H at the back, that is S.
- The right centre has the same arrangement (N left, CH2OH down, ethyl upper right), so it is also S.

**SMILES check.** In `CC[C@@H](CO)N…N[C@@H](CC)CO`, the first centre has neighbour order ethyl, H, CH2OH, N. With `@@` this works out to S. The second centre has neighbour order N, H, ethyl, CH2OH. With `@@` this also works out to S. Both match the drawing.
