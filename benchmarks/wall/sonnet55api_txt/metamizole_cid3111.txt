This is metamizole (dipyrone) in its sulfonic acid form.

**Pyrazolone ring.** The ring atoms are:
- N1, which carries a methyl group.
- N2, which carries the phenyl group.
- C3, which is the C=O carbon.
- C4, which bears the N(CH3)CH2SO3H substituent.
- C5, which bears a methyl group.

The ring has a C4=C5 double bond.

**Side chain.** C4 is bonded to N(CH3)–CH2–S(=O)(=O)OH.

**SMILES.** Writing the ring as CN1C(C)=C(N(C)CS(=O)(=O)O)C(=O)N1c1ccccc1 gives:
- N1 (methyl), then C5 (methyl) double-bonded to C4 (side chain), then C3 (=O), then N2 (phenyl), which closes back to N1.

This matches the drawing atom for atom.
