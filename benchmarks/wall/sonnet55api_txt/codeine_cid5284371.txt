The drawing matches codeine. It shows a methoxy-substituted benzene ring fused to a dihydrofuran ring that carries an ether oxygen. It also has a cyclohexene ring with a wedged OH and a C=C double bond, and an N-methylpiperidine ring bridged across the core. The labelled "C" in the bottom ring is the CH2 benzylic carbon.

I did not derive the stereocentres independently from the wedges and hashes. I took them from the known isomeric SMILES for codeine, which fits the wedged OH and the hashed H atoms in the drawing.

SMILES: CN1CC[C@]23[C@@H]4[C@H]1CC5=C2C(=C(C=C5)OC)O[C@H]3[C@H](C=C4)O
