I read the structure as a lanthionine-bridged peptide with a C-terminal macrocycle. Starting from the N-terminus, the residues are:

1. Ile
2. Ala
3. Ala, the first half of a lanthionine thioether bridge
4. Lys
5. Phe
6. Leu
7. Ala, the second half of that lanthionine bridge
8. β-methyl-Ala, bridged by sulfur to the next Ala-like residue
9. Pro
10. Gly
11. Ala-like residue closing the methyllanthionine bridge
12. Ala
13. Lys
14. Dehydrobutyrine (drawn as the E isomer)
15. Gly
16. Ala, the start of a lanthionine bridge
17. Phe
18. Asn
19. Ala, which is S-linked to an aminovinyl unit (–S–CH=CH–NH–)
20. Tyr
21. Ala, the other end of the lanthionine bridge from residue 16

The aminovinyl link is the S–CH=CH–NH– unit that closes the C-terminal ring.

I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash. The Ile, Ala, Lys, Phe, Leu and Pro centres come out as the natural L configuration. Several of the lanthionine and Cys-derived centres, and the Asn, come out as D by the geometry as drawn, and I kept them that way. The Tyr aryl group is attached through a CH2, though the bond lengths in that corner of the drawing are slightly uneven.
