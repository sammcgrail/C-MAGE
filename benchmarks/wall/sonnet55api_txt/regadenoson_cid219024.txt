I read this as regadenoson, an N-methyl pyrazole-4-carboxamide attached at C2 of adenosine.

- **Left side:** a methylamide (CH3–NH–C(=O)–) on C4 of a pyrazole ring. The pyrazole N1 is bonded to the purine.
- **Centre:** a purine bearing NH2 at C6, with the pyrazole at C2. N9 carries the sugar.
- **Right side:** a ribofuranose with a bold wedge from C1′ to N9 and a bold wedge to the CH2OH at C4′. Both ring OH groups (C2′ and C3′) are hashed. This matches the natural β-D-ribo configuration, so I used the standard adenosine stereo pattern for the sugar.

I checked that the neighbour order around C1′ matches the usual adenosine SMILES, so the stereodescriptors carry over unchanged.
