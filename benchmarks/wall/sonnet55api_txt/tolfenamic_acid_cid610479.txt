The drawing shows an anthranilic acid core, with a benzoic acid ring whose carboxylic acid is ortho to an NH linker.

The NH connects to a second aromatic ring. Starting from the ring carbon bonded to N, one neighbour carries a methyl group. The next carbon after that carries Cl. This gives a 3-chloro-2-methylphenyl group, which is the structure of tolfenamic acid.

Reading the SMILES from the acid end: OC(=O)c1ccccc1Nc1cccc(Cl)c1C

Atom check:
- Left ring: c1 (bearing COOH), then c, c, c, c, then c1 (bearing N). That is 6 carbons, which is correct.
- Right ring: c1 (bearing N), c, c, c, c(Cl), c1(C). That is 6 carbons, with the methyl on the carbon adjacent to the N-bearing carbon and Cl on the next carbon.
