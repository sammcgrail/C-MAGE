I read this as an α-galactosyl ceramide of the KRN7000 type. The stereochemistry is taken from the known compound, not derived from the wedges, so it is the least certain part.

- **Sugar:** a pyranose ring with a CH2OH group, three ring OH groups and an O-glycosidic link at the anomeric carbon. The ring OH groups match galactose and the linkage is α.
- **Sphingoid base:** the glycosidic oxygen sits on a CH2 (C1). C2 carries the amide nitrogen. C3 and C4 each carry an OH, which gives a phytosphingosine core. From the picture I counted a tail of 14 further CH2/CH3 carbons after C4, which makes the base C18.
- **Acyl group:** the amide carbonyl is followed by a long straight chain. Counting zigzag vertices gave 25 carbons beyond the carbonyl carbon, so the acyl group is C26 (cerotoyl).

I counted the chain lengths from the zigzag spacing and bond length in the image, which is approximate.
