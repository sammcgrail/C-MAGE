I read the drawing as a vancomycin/teicoplanin-like glycopeptide: a macrocyclic peptide core with a central ring, a 6-aryl-indole and a diaryl ether, plus a 3,5-dichloro-4-hydroxyphenylglyoxylamide cap and a 4-hydroxyphenylglycine tail. The SMILES below follows that reading, with two caveats.

**Caveats**
- Near the left-hand stereocentre, the drawing shows an N–O line that would give the oxygen three bonds. I treated it as a drawing artefact and wrote an ordinary secondary amide NH there, so the N–O bond is not in the SMILES.
- I assigned the five stereocentres by hand from the wedge and hash bonds, so a chirality tag could be wrong.

**Fragments I read from the drawing**
- **Tail:** a 4-hydroxyphenylglycine (HOOC–CH(Ar)–NH–) acylated by the first peptide carbonyl.
- **Ether ring:** a para-substituted phenyl joined through CH₂ to the first stereocentre and through an ether oxygen to the central ring.
- **Central ring:** a benzene bearing the ether oxygen, an OH, the indole-linked aryl bond, and the CH(NH)–C(=O) side chain.
- **Indole:** attached through its 6-position to the central ring, with CH₂ at its 3-position.
- **Peptide chain:** two further amino-acid residues each carry a 3,5-dichloro-4-hydroxyphenyl group.
- **Cap:** the 3,5-dichloro-4-hydroxyphenylglyoxylamide on the indolylalanine nitrogen.
