This is troglitazone, with plain bonds throughout and no wedges or hashes, so I give no stereochemistry.

**Chroman part.** The aromatic ring carries three methyl groups and one OH. Its two fusion carbons connect to the ring oxygen (O1) and to the CH2CH2 side. The quaternary carbon C2 bears a methyl and a CH2–O–aryl group, and it is bonded to the ring oxygen and to the CH2CH2.

**Linker and aryl ring.** C2 continues through CH2–O to a para-substituted benzene. That benzene carries a CH2 attached to C5 of the thiazolidine-2,4-dione. The ring is S–C(=O)–NH–C(=O)–CH.

**Writing the SMILES.** I start at the chroman C2 and go around the ring: C2(C)(COc1ccc(CC2SC(=O)NC2=O)cc1) is the side chain, then CCc3 for the CH2CH2 and the first fusion carbon, then c(C)c(O)c(C)c(C)c3O1 for the aromatic ring and closure through the ring oxygen.

Aromatic ring order, going from the fusion carbon next to the CH2CH2 (C4a):
- C4a is bonded to C5, which carries a methyl (the bottom methyl in the drawing).
- C5 is bonded to C6, which carries the OH.
- C6 is bonded to C7, which carries a methyl.
- C7 is bonded to C8, which carries a methyl.
- C8 is bonded to C8a, which carries the ring oxygen.

Full SMILES: CC1(COc2ccc(CC3SC(=O)NC3=O)cc2)CCc2c(C)c(O)c(C)c(C)c2O1

Check on the aromatic ring: c2 (C4a), c(C) (C5), c(O) (C6), c(C) (C7), c(C) (C8), c2 (C8a) closing the ring, then O1. That gives six aromatic atoms.
