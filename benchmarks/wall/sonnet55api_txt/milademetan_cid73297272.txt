I read the structure as follows.

- **Oxindole:** a 6-chloro-oxindole (lactam NH–C=O), spiro-fused at C3.
- **Spiro carbon:** this carbon is also a ring carbon of the pyrrolidine and is bonded to a second spiro carbon.
- **Pyrrolidine:** a five-membered ring containing NH. One carbon carries a 2-chloro-3-fluoropyridin-4-yl group (bold wedge). The next carbon carries a carboxamide C(=O)NH (hashed bond).
- **Cyclohexane:** the second spiro carbon, next to the NH, belongs to a 4,4-dimethylcyclohexane ring.
- **Amide substituent:** the amide N bears a tetrahydropyran-3-yl group (ring O, wedge on the N-bearing carbon). The carbon next to the ring O carries a primary carboxamide, C(=O)NH2, on a hashed bond.

I assigned the @/@@ marks from the wedges and hashes. The wedge between the two spiro carbons I took as starting at the oxindole spiro carbon. The cyclohexane spiro carbon is not a stereocentre, because its two ring branches are identical.
