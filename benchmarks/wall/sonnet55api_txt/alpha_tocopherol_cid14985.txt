This is α-tocopherol (vitamin E). I read it as a 6-hydroxy-chromane whose aromatic ring carries three methyls, with a C2 quaternary stereocentre (methyl plus a saturated 16-carbon side chain). The side chain has two further methyl-bearing stereocentres and ends in an isopropyl group.

I worked out the CIP configuration only at C2. The methyl there is drawn on a wedge, and the priorities are O > ring CH2 > chain CH2 > CH3. This gives R. I did not derive the two side-chain centres (the hashed and wedged methyls) separately. I assumed they match natural RRR-α-tocopherol and wrote the SMILES from memory of the PubChem entry.
