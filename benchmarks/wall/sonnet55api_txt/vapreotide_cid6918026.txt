- **Cys (first):** its NH is hashed and its CH2 leads to the S–S bridge. The alpha carbon is R, which is L-Cys because sulfur raises the side-chain priority.
- **Tyr:** a wedged CH2 to a 4-hydroxyphenyl ring. The alpha carbon is S, so L-Tyr.
- **Trp (first):** a hashed CH2 to the indole. The alpha carbon is R, so D-Trp.
- **Lys:** a wedged four-carbon chain ending in NH2. The alpha carbon is S, so L-Lys.
- **Val:** a wedged isopropyl group. The alpha carbon is S, so L-Val.

- **Trp (C-terminus):** a hashed CH2 to the indole and a primary amide (C(N)=O). The alpha carbon is R, so D-Trp.
