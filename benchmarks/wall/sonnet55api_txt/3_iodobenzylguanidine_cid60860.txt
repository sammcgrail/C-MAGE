The drawing shows a guanidine group, with a carbon bearing two NH2 groups and a C=N double bond. That nitrogen is bonded to a CH2, which is attached to a benzene ring.

On the ring, the CH2 sits at one carbon. Going around the ring, the next carbon (top left) is CH, and the one after that (top right) carries iodine. So the iodine is meta to the CH2 attachment, giving a 3-iodobenzyl group.
