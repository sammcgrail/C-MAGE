The drawing shows a guanidine group, with two NH2 groups on one carbon and a C=N double bond to a third nitrogen. That nitrogen is bonded to a CH2, which is bonded to a benzene ring.

On the ring, the CH2 attachment is at one vertex (about 955,903). Going around, the next vertex is at 1048,686, then the iodine-bearing vertex at 1282,657. The iodine is therefore two ring bonds from the CH2 attachment, which makes it meta (3-iodo).
