This is nimesulide: a methanesulfonamide (CH3-SO2-NH-) attached to a benzene ring that carries a phenoxy group ortho to the NH and a nitro group.

Reading the central ring vertex by vertex:
- The NH-bearing carbon is at (662, 1054).
- The phenoxy-bearing carbon is at (614, 837), adjacent to it.
- The next carbon is at (778, 688), a CH.
- The nitro-bearing carbon is at (990, 755).
- Then come the CH at (1037, 972) and the CH at (873, 1122), which closes the ring back to the NH-bearing carbon.

The nitro group sits para to the NH-bearing carbon: the NH carbon is C1, the phenoxy carbon is C2, the CH is C3, the nitro carbon is C4, and the nitro is on C4. The ring is therefore 1-NHSO2Me, 2-OPh, 4-NO2.

The upper-left ring is an unsubstituted phenyl attached through the ether oxygen.
