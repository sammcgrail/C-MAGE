The ring has six vertices, going around: NH (top right), CH2 (right), CH2 (bottom right), NH (bottom left), CH2 (left), CH2 (top left), and back to the first NH. The order is N–C–C–N–C–C, with the two nitrogens opposite each other (1,4 positions). This is piperazine. All bonds are plain single bonds, so there is no stereochemistry.
