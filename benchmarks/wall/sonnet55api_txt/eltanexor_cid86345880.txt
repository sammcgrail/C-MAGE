I read the structure as follows:

- **Left ring:** a pyrimidin-5-yl group, with ring nitrogens at positions 1 and 3 and the attachment carbon at position 5.
- **Linker:** that attachment carbon bonds to an sp2 carbon bearing a primary amide, C(=O)NH2. This carbon is double-bonded to a =CH– that carries a 1,2,4-triazole nitrogen (N1).
- **Triazole:** its C3 is attached to a benzene ring.
- **Right ring:** a benzene with CF3 groups at both meta positions, so a 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)phenyl group.

**Double-bond geometry:** the pyrimidine and the triazole N sit on the same side of the C=C in the drawing, so the amide carbonyl and the triazole N are on opposite sides. By CIP priorities the amide carbon (O,O,N) outranks the pyrimidine carbon (C,C,C), and N outranks H, so this is the E isomer. In the SMILES, `C(=O)/C(...)=C/n` places the carbonyl and the N trans, which matches.

SMILES: NC(=O)/C(c1cncnc1)=C/n1cnc(n1)-c1cc(C(F)(F)F)cc(C(F)(F)F)c1
