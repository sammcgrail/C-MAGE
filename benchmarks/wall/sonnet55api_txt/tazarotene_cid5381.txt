This is tazarotene, the ethyl ester of a 6-substituted nicotinic acid.

**Ester end.** An ethyl group (CH3-CH2) is bonded to an O, which is bonded to a C(=O) attached to the pyridine ring.

**Pyridine ring.** The ring atoms are N, C2, C3, C4, C5 and C6. The ester carbon sits on one ring carbon. The alkyne sits on the carbon adjacent to N, which makes the ring a 2,5-disubstituted pyridine. Going around the ring from the ester-bearing carbon:
- C(ester) → CH (the carbon next to N) → N → C(alkyne) → CH → CH → back to C(ester).

**Linker.** A C≡C triple bond joins the pyridine carbon next to N to the benzene ring of the thiochroman.

**Thiochroman part.** The benzene ring carries the alkyne at one carbon. Two carbons further round the ring is the fused carbon bearing S, and the other fused carbon bears the CMe2 group. The saturated ring runs S–CH2–CH2–C(CH3)2, closing back onto the aromatic carbon.

Benzene atom order, starting from the alkyne-bearing carbon: C(alkyne) – CH – C(fused, attached to CMe2) – C(fused, attached to S) – CH – CH – back to C(alkyne).
