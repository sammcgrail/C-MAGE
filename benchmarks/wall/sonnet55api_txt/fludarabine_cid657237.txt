This structure is fludarabine (2-fluoro-ara-A), read as follows:

- **Base:** a purine ring with an NH2 group on the six-membered ring, a fluorine on the carbon between the two ring nitrogens, and N9 bonded to the sugar. This is 2-fluoroadenine.
- **Sugar:** a five-membered ring (tetrahydrofuran) with a CH2OH on C4′ and OH groups on C3′ and C2′.
- **Stereochemistry:** the N-glycosidic bond is a wedge, the CH2OH is a wedge, the 2′-OH is a wedge and the 3′-OH is hashed.
  - I worked out the anomeric carbon (C1′) from the drawing: viewed from the front with H at the back, N→C2′→O runs clockwise, which gives `[C@@H]`.
  - The remaining centres I took from the known arabinose (vidarabine-type) pattern, which agrees with the drawing: the 2′-OH is on the same face as the CH2OH, and the 3′-OH is trans to the 2′-OH. I did not derive each of these centres separately from the wedges.
