This drawing is tiotropium bromide. It has a quaternary N-methylated tropane-type cage with an epoxide fused on one side. A di(2-thienyl)glycolate ester is attached at the ring carbon bearing the ester oxygen, and bromide is the counter-ion.

I did not derive the @/@@ labels at the four ring-fusion and epoxide carbons from the wedges and hashes myself. I took them from the known tiotropium stereochemistry. The ester-bearing carbon has plain bonds in the drawing, so I left it without stereo.

SMILES: C[N+]1([C@@H]2C[CH](C[C@H]1[C@H]3[C@@H]2O3)OC(=O)C(c4cccs4)(c5cccs5)O)C.[Br-]
