The drawing is selenocysteine: a HOOC–CH(NH2)–CH2–SeH skeleton.

- **Carboxylic acid:** the carbon at the left has a double bond to O and a single bond to HO.
- **Stereocentre:** the alpha carbon carries NH2 on a hashed bond, so N points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- **CIP priorities:** N > CH2SeH > COOH > H. The CH2SeH carbon outranks the carboxyl carbon because Se outranks O.
- **Configuration:** viewed from the front, N (down) → CH2Se (upper right) → COOH (upper left) runs counterclockwise. The H points toward the viewer, so the true sense is reversed, giving clockwise, which is R. This is L-selenocysteine.
- **SMILES check:** this matches the L-cysteine pattern N[C@@H](CS)C(=O)O (also R), with S replaced by Se.
