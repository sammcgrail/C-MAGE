- **Stereocentre:** it carries the hashed CH2, the NH and the COOH. With H pointing toward the viewer, the priorities N > COOH > CH2 run clockwise on the page, so the true configuration is S. The SMILES `C[C@H](N…)C(=O)O` was checked to give S.
- **Centre ring:** the NH is acylated by an amide on a dichlorinated benzene ring, with Cl on both carbons flanking the amide carbon. This benzene is fused to a tetrahydroisoquinoline ring (CH2CH2–N–CH2).
