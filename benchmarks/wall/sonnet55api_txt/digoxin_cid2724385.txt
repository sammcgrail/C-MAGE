This is a digoxin-type cardiac glycoside. I assigned the constitution from the drawing and the stereochemistry by comparison with digoxin, so the stereo descriptors are inferred rather than read atom by atom from the wedges.

**Constitution**
- The aglycone is a steroid with the usual four fused rings, with a methyl on C10 and on C13.
- There are hydroxyls at C12 and C14.
- C17 carries a butenolide (2(5H)-furanone) ring.
- The C3 oxygen is linked to a chain of three 2,6-dideoxy sugars (digitoxose units). Each unit is a pyranose with a ring CH2 next to the anomeric carbon, a 3-OH and a 5-methyl.
- The two inner sugars are linked through their 4-oxygen. The terminal sugar has free 3-OH and 4-OH groups.

**Stereochemistry**
- Relative to digoxin's natural configuration, every drawn stereocentre is inverted. The C10 and C13 methyls, the C14-OH and the C17 butenolide are hashed, and the C9-H is wedged, which is the opposite of the natural configuration.
- I therefore took the digoxin SMILES and inverted every @/@@. I did not independently verify each sugar centre against the wedges, only the steroid ones.
