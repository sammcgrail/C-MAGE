The drawing shows testosterone enanthate (testosterone heptanoate), with the steroid core flipped left to right compared with the usual orientation.

- **Ester chain:** counting from the free methyl end to the carbonyl carbon gives 7 carbons, so the acyl group is heptanoyl, attached through the ester oxygen to C17.
- **Ring A:** an enone, with a C=O at C3 and a C4=C5 double bond.
- **Stereochemistry:** both methyl groups (C18 and C19) are hashed, the C9 and C14 hydrogens are wedged, and the C8 hydrogen and the C17 oxygen are hashed. In the usual orientation this pattern is the natural testosterone configuration (17β-ester, 8β-H, 9α-H, 14α-H, β-methyls). Flipping the drawing swaps wedges and hashes, so I used the natural configuration.
