The structure is a pyridine ring with four substituents, which matches pyridoxal.

Ring atoms, traced from the drawing:
- N at the top left.
- The carbon at the top (CH) is bonded to N and is double-bonded to the ring carbon on the right, which bears CH2OH.
- The next ring carbon, at the bottom right, carries the CHO group.
- The next ring carbon, at the bottom left, carries OH.
- The last ring carbon, on the left, carries the methyl group and is double-bonded to N.

The double bonds are N=C(CH3), C(OH)=C(CHO), and CH=C(CH2OH), which gives a valid aromatic Kekulé structure.
