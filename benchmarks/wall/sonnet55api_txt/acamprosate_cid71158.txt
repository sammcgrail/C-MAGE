The drawing shows an acetamide whose nitrogen is attached to a three-carbon chain ending in a sulfonic acid.

Reading from right to left:
- The methyl carbon connects to a carbonyl carbon, C(=O).
- That carbonyl carbon bonds to NH.
- The NH is followed by a chain of three CH2 groups. In the drawing, the first is at about (900, 780), the second at (710, 700) and the third at (543, 825).
- The third CH2 bonds to S, which carries two =O groups and one OH.

This gives CC(=O)NCCCS(=O)(=O)O. It has no stereocentres, so no stereo marks are needed.
