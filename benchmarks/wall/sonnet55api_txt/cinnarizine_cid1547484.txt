The structure is cinnarizine: a piperazine ring with a benzhydryl group (a CH bearing two phenyl rings) on one nitrogen and a cinnamyl group on the other.

- **Benzhydryl side:** the CH attached to the left nitrogen carries two phenyl rings. It is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label (and it is not a stereocentre anyway).
- **Piperazine:** a six-membered ring with two nitrogens in a 1,4 relationship and four CH2 groups.
- **Cinnamyl side:** the right nitrogen carries CH2–CH=CH–Ph. The CH2 lies up-left of the first alkene carbon and the phenyl lies down-right of the second, so they are on opposite sides of the double bond. That makes it trans (E).

SMILES: C(/C=C/c1ccccc1)N1CCN(CC1)C(c1ccccc1)c1ccccc1
