This structure is phentolamine.

- **Imidazoline ring:** a five-membered ring made of NH, CH2, CH2, N and a C that is double-bonded to N. That C also carries a CH2 substituent.
- **Linker:** the CH2 connects to a tertiary N.
- **Aryl groups on N:** a 4-methylphenyl (p-tolyl) group and a 3-hydroxyphenyl group.
- **Hydroxyphenyl substitution:** the ring carbon bonded to N is C1. Going around the ring, the next carbon (top-left) is C2, and the one after it carries OH, so OH is at C3 (meta). This matches the drawing.

SMILES: Cc1ccc(cc1)N(CC1=NCCN1)c1cccc(O)c1
