The drawing shows a pregn-4-ene-3,20-dione steroid skeleton (four fused rings, with the enone in ring A). It carries a 17-hydroxy group, a 17-acetyl group (the C(=O)CH3 on the left) and a 6-methyl group, plus the two angular methyls at C10 and C13. That is medroxyprogesterone (6α-methyl-17-hydroxyprogesterone).

The drawing is flipped vertically relative to the usual steroid orientation. The C13 and C10 methyls are hashed, the 6-methyl and 17-OH are wedged, and the ring-junction hydrogens are drawn the opposite way to the usual depiction. Every wedge and hash is inverted together, so this is the natural stereochemistry viewed from the other face.
