This drawing matches ursodeoxycholic acid (3α,7β-dihydroxy-5β-cholan-24-oic acid). The stereocentres below were read from the wedges and hashes, but I wrote the final SMILES from my recall of the known ursodeoxycholic acid string, not by deriving each @/@@ from the picture.

- **Side chain:** a carboxylic acid (C=O and OH at the lower left) on a three-carbon chain, ending at a methyl-bearing carbon drawn with a wedge. This attaches to the five-membered ring.
- **Ring system:** the standard four-ring steroid skeleton (three six-membered rings and one five-membered ring), with a wedged methyl at each of the two ring-fusion carbons that carry one.
- **7-OH:** the OH on the middle six-membered ring is a wedge (β).
- **3-OH:** the OH at the lower right is a hash (α).
- **Ring-fusion hydrogens:** the H at C5 is wedged (β), the H at C8 is wedged (β), and the H at C9 and the H at C14 are hashed (α). A 7β-OH with a 3α-OH distinguishes this from chenodeoxycholic acid, which has 7α.
