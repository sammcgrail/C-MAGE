The drawing shows an N,N-dimethylamino group (N with two methyls) attached to a two-carbon chain (CH2CH2). That chain leads to a CH carbon bearing a phenyl ring and a 2-pyridyl ring. The pyridine nitrogen is bonded directly to the carbon that carries the substituent, which is why it is a 2-pyridyl group. This is pheniramine.

The CH carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but all its bonds are plain, so I give no stereo.

SMILES: CN(C)CCC(c1ccccc1)c1ccccn1
