The drawing is niclosamide: a 5-chloro-2-hydroxybenzamide (salicylamide) core with an amide N attached to a 2-chloro-4-nitrophenyl ring.

Left ring: the carbon bearing OH sits at the top and is adjacent to the carbon carrying the C(=O) group. Going round the ring from there, the carbons are C(OH), CH, CH, C(Cl), CH, then C(C=O). The Cl carbon is para to the C(OH) carbon, so the ring is 5-chloro-2-hydroxybenzoyl.

Right ring: the carbon bonded to NH has an adjacent C(Cl), then a CH, then C(NO2), then two CH carbons back to the start. The NO2 is therefore para to the N-bearing carbon and the Cl is ortho to it.
