I read this as the sulfated CCK-8 octapeptide, H-Asp-Tyr(SO3H)-Met-Gly-Trp-Met-Asp-Phe-NH2. The chain runs from the free amine of the N-terminal Asp, through the sulfated Tyr, Met, Gly, Trp, Met and Asp, to a C-terminal Phe amide.

I checked the stereo at the N-terminal Asp alpha carbon, where the NH2 is hashed. Using CIP priorities (N > C(=O)N > CH2), that centre comes out as S, which is L-Asp. I took the other alpha carbons to be L as well, since they are drawn with the same wedge pattern, but I did not check each one individually. Gly has no stereocentre.
