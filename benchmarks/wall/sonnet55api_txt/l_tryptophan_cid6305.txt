The drawing shows tryptophan: an indole ring (a benzene ring fused to a five-membered ring containing NH) joined at C3 through a CH2 to an alpha carbon. That carbon carries an NH2 and a COOH.

The only stereo marker is the hashed bond from the alpha carbon to NH2, so the alpha carbon is the only stereocentre.

To assign it, the substituents rank N > COOH > CH2 > H. The hashed NH2 points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. On the page, N (up) → COOH (lower right) → CH2 (lower left) runs clockwise. Because the H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This matches L-tryptophan.
