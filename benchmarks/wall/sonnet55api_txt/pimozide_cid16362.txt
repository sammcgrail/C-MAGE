This structure is pimozide-like, but the chain has only three CH2 groups between the CH(Ar)2 carbon and the piperidine nitrogen, so I counted the chain atoms from the drawing.

Chain: the CH carbon at (480, 878) bonds to a carbon at (562, 943), then (660, 907), then (743, 973), then N at (843, 937). That gives CH–CH2–CH2–CH2–N, which is three CH2 groups, as in pimozide.

Fragments:
- Two 4-fluorophenyl groups on the CH carbon.
- Piperidine, with N bonded to the chain and C4 bonded to the benzimidazolone N.
- Benzimidazol-2-one: N1 attached to the piperidine C4, C2 as C=O, N3 as NH, fused to a benzene ring.
