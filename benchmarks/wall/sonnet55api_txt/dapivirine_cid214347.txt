This is the NNRTI dapivirine scaffold: a pyrimidine with a 2,4,6-trimethylanilino group at C4 and a 4-cyanoanilino group at C2.

**Pyrimidine ring.** Going round the ring: the top-left carbon (CH) is bonded to the C bearing the mesityl-NH, which is bonded to the lower N, then to the C bearing the NH-aryl, then to the right N, then to the top-right CH.

**Mesityl ring.** The ring carbon attached to NH has methyl groups on both neighbouring carbons (one upper-left, one right). The para carbon, at the bottom, also carries a methyl. This gives a 2,4,6-trimethylphenyl group.

**Cyanophenyl ring.** The NH is attached to a benzene ring that carries a C≡N at the para position.
