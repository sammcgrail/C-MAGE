I read the molecule as a cyclic lipopeptide with an acylated Asp tail. I worked out each stereocentre from the wedge and hash bonds, treating the H on each centre as implicit.

**Lipid tail.** An isopropyl terminus is followed by nine CH2 groups, then a trans CH=CH, then C(=O)–NH on the first Asp.

**First Asp.** Its side chain is CH2COOH, drawn with a wedge. I assigned it the L-like configuration.

**Ring, traced from the Asp acyl nitrogen.**
- The first Asp's C(=O) joins the exocyclic NH of a 2,3-diaminopropionic acid unit (Dap). Its alpha carbon carries NH (wedge), C(=O) and a CH2 that leads to the ring NH.
- Dap C(=O) goes to the nitrogen of a six-membered pipecolic acid (piperidine) ring, whose alpha H is drawn on a wedge.
- That alpha carbon's C(=O) leads to NH–CH2–C(=O) (a glycine-like unit), then to a second Asp. Its CH2COOH is on a hash.
- That Asp's C(=O) leads to a second NH–CH2–C(=O) unit, then to a third Asp, also hashed.
- That Asp's C(=O) leads to NH–C(=O)–CH2–C(=O) (a malonyl-type unit) and then to a Thr. The Thr NH is hashed, and its CH(OH)CH3 has a hashed methyl.
- The Thr C(=O) leads to an Ile. Its NH is hashed and its side-chain methyl is on a wedge.
- The Ile C(=O) goes to the nitrogen of a five-membered pyrrolidine (proline) ring, whose alpha H is on a wedge.
- The proline C(=O) leads to NH–CH2, which closes back onto the Dap alpha carbon.

**Stereochemistry.** I worked each centre out from the drawn wedges and hashes. The Ile side-chain centre comes out as the drawing shows it, which differs from natural L-isoleucine. I kept it as drawn.
