The image shows sodium thiocyanate, drawn as two separate ions.

- **Cation:** Na with a + charge, written [Na+].
- **Anion:** S with a − charge, joined by a single bond to a carbon, which is joined by a triple bond to N. This is [S-]C#N.

SMILES: [Na+].[S-]C#N
