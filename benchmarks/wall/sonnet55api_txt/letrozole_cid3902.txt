The drawing shows letrozole: a central CH bonded to two para-cyanophenyl rings and to N1 of a 1,2,4-triazole.

Reading the triazole: N1 (bonded to the CH) is connected to N2 on one side and to a CH carbon (C5) on the other. N2 is double-bonded to C3, and C3 is single-bonded to N4. N4 is double-bonded to C5, which closes back to N1. This is the 1,2,4-triazol-1-yl pattern.

Each benzene ring carries a nitrile (C≡N) at the para position relative to the CH attachment.

SMILES: N#Cc1ccc(cc1)C(c1ccc(C#N)cc1)n1cncn1
