I read the drawing as a four-carbon chain with an OH on every carbon: HO–CH2–CH(OH)–CH(OH)–CH2–OH, which is butane-1,2,3,4-tetraol.

Stereochemistry comes only from the two drawn centres:

- **C2** has a hashed OH (pointing away), so its implicit H points toward the viewer. Priorities are OH > C3 > C1 > H. OH, C3, C1 runs clockwise on the page, which becomes counterclockwise once the H-toward-viewer is accounted for. C2 is S.
- **C3** has a wedged OH (toward the viewer), so its H points away. Priorities are OH > C2 > C4 > H. OH, C2, C4 runs clockwise on the page. C3 is R.

This gives (2S,3R), the meso form, which is erythritol. Writing the SMILES from C1 to C4 and checking each centre's parity gives `[C@H]` for both.

SMILES: OC[C@H](O)[C@H](O)CO
