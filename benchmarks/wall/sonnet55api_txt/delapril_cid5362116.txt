I read this as delapril, with two stereocentres. The remaining groups are:

- Ethyl ester: CCOC(=O)–.
- Homophenylalanine part: the first stereocentre carries a CH2CH2–phenyl chain, drawn with a hashed bond.
- Alanine part: an NH links to the second stereocentre, which carries a methyl drawn with a hashed bond, then a C(=O) amide.
- Amide nitrogen: bears a CH2COOH group and a 2,3-dihydro-1H-inden-2-yl (indane) group.

For both stereocentres, I ranked the substituents by CIP priority and viewed the drawing with the implicit H toward the viewer. Reversing that sense gives S at both centres. The SMILES below encodes S at each, which I checked by working the chirality tags through by hand.
