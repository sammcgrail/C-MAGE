I read the structure left to right.

- The left end is a methyl group bonded to an oxygen, which is bonded to a carbonyl carbon (a methyl ester, COC(=O)).
- That carbonyl carbon is attached to a C=C double bond, which leads to a second carbonyl carbon bearing =O.
- The second carbonyl carbon is bonded to an ester oxygen, then a CH2CH2 chain, then the nitrogen of a succinimide ring.
- The ring is N, C(=O), CH2, CH2, C(=O), closing back to N.

Stereochemistry: the double bond is drawn as trans. The first carbonyl carbon sits at the upper left of the double bond and the second carbonyl carbon at the lower right, on opposite sides, so this is the E isomer.
