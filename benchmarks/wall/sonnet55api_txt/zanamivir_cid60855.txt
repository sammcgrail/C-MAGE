I read this as zanamivir (the 4-guanidino-5-acetamido dihydropyran carboxylic acid with a glycerol side chain) and derived each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes.

**Structure read from the drawing**
- The ring is a six-membered dihydropyran: ring O, then C(COOH)=CH, then the CH bearing the guanidine, then the CH bearing the acetamide, then the CH attached to the ring O.
- The carboxylic acid is on the ring carbon next to the ring O, and it is part of the C=C double bond.
- The guanidine is attached through its imine nitrogen, as N=C(N)N.
- The acetamide is NHC(=O)CH3.
- The side chain is CH(OH)–CH(OH)–CH2OH.

**Stereochemistry**

I assigned each centre by viewing from the implicit hydrogen and converting to SMILES neighbour order.

| Ring/side-chain centre | Drawn bond | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon bearing the acetamide | hashed NH | `[C@@H]` |
| Carbon bearing the guanidine | wedged N | `[C@@H]` |
| Ring carbon next to the ring O | wedged ring O | `[C@H]` |
| First side-chain carbon | wedged OH | `[C@H]` |
| Second side-chain carbon | hashed OH | `[C@H]` |

The acetamide and guanidine are trans, which fits zanamivir. I derived the guanidine-bearing centre as `@@`, though the version of the zanamivir SMILES I remember has `@` there. I kept the derived assignment.
