Reading the drawing, this is clavulanic acid, a bicyclic β-lactam fused to an oxazolidine ring.

- **Ring system:** the four-membered lactam ring has N, a C=O carbon, a CH2, and the bridgehead CH. The five-membered ring contains N, the C(COOH)H carbon, the exocyclic-alkene carbon, O, and the bridgehead CH.
- **Substituents:** the carboxylic acid is on the carbon next to N. The alkene carbon carries =CH–CH2OH.
- **Stereocentres:** the bridgehead CH has a hashed H, and the C–COOH carbon has a hashed bond to COOH. Working through the neighbour order gives `[C@@H]` for the bridgehead and `[C@H]` for the carbon bearing COOH.
- **Double bond:** the ring O and the CH2OH group both point downward in the drawing, so they are cis (Z). This is written `/C=C1\O`.
