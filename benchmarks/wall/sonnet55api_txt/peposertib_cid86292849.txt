I read the molecule as a 6-methoxypyridazin-3-yl carbinol attached to a 4-chloro-2-fluorophenyl-type ring, which in turn carries a 7-morpholinoquinazolin-4-yl group.

- **Pyridazine:** a six-membered ring with two adjacent N atoms, an OMe on the carbon next to one N, and the carbinol carbon on the carbon next to the other N.
- **Carbinol carbon:** CH(OH) joins the pyridazine to the central benzene ring. The OH is on a hashed bond.
- **Central benzene:** going round the ring from the carbinol carbon, the substituents are CH(OH), H, quinazolin-4-yl, F, H, Cl.
- **Quinazoline:** C4 is bonded to the central benzene ring, with N3, C2 and N1 following. The fused benzo ring carries the morpholine nitrogen at C7.
- **Morpholine:** N-linked, with an O opposite the N.

**Stereochemistry:** CIP priorities at the carbinol carbon are OH > pyridazinyl (N,N,C) > aryl (C,C,C) > H. The hashed OH puts the implicit H toward the viewer. On the page, O, then pyridazine, then aryl runs clockwise, which reverses to counterclockwise with H pointing at the viewer, so the centre is S. In SMILES, with neighbour order pyridazine, H, O, aryl, this corresponds to `[C@@H]`.

SMILES: COc1ccc(nn1)[C@@H](O)c1cc(c(F)cc1Cl)-c1ncnc2cc(ccc12)N1CCOCC1
