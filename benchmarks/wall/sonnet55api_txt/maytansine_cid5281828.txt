I read this as maytansine, a maytansinoid macrolide. The ring system and substituents are:

- **Aromatic ring:** a benzene ring carrying the OMe, the Cl, the ring N-methyl amide nitrogen and the CH2 link to the diene.
- **Macrocycle:** it contains the N-methyl lactam, the epoxide, the cyclic carbamate (NC(=O)O), a tertiary C–OH, a C–OMe next to the E,E-diene, and a methyl-bearing carbon.
- **Ester side chain:** an N-acetyl-N-methyl-alanine ester on the carbon next to the epoxide.

I did not derive every wedge and hash independently. The stereodescriptors and the diene geometry come from my recollection of the known maytansine isomeric SMILES, so the stereochemistry is the least certain part.
