I read this as a cyclic lipopeptide: a 3-hydroxyhexadecanoyl chain on a Glu–Orn tail, attached to a macrocycle closed by an ester to a Tyr phenol oxygen. I assigned each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes in the drawing. The 3-hydroxy carbon of the fatty chain has plain bonds, so it carries no stereo label.

**Lipid tail.** I counted the chain from the amide carbonyl: C1 is the C=O, C2 is CH2, C3 is CH(OH), and C4–C16 run up to the terminal CH3. That makes a 3-hydroxyhexadecanoyl group.

**Side chain.** The lipid is acylated onto a Glu (free γ-COOH), which is linked to an Orn (free δ-NH2). The Orn carbonyl then forms the amide to the Tyr that closes the ring.

**Macrocycle.** Going around the ring from that Tyr:
- Tyr: its benzylic CH2 is drawn as "C", and the para oxygen forms the ester ring closure.
- Thr: alpha carbon plus a CH(CH3)OH side chain.
- Glu: free γ-COOH.
- Ala.
- Pro.
- Gln: side-chain primary amide.
- Tyr: free para-OH.
- Ile: its carbonyl is esterified by the first Tyr's phenol oxygen.
