I read the structure as a microcystin-type cyclic peptide and wrote the SMILES by going around the macrocycle from the Leu residue.

**Residues, in ring order:**
1. Leu: isobutyl side chain, wedged CH2.
2. β-Methyl-Asp: free CO2H on the α-carbon, with a hashed methyl on the β-carbon.
3. Arg: the side chain ends in a guanidine, drawn as N=C(N)N.
4. Adda: the α-carbon carries a wedged vinyl and is followed by a CH(CH3) that is part of the ring. The side chain is a trans CH=CH, then C(CH3)=CH (E), then CH(CH3), then CH(OMe)CH2Ph.
5. Glu: free CO2H on the α-carbon, with the γ-carbon forming an amide to the N-methyl nitrogen of the next residue (the two atoms labelled "C" in the drawing are the CH2CH2).
6. N-Methyl-dehydroalanine: N(CH3)–C(=CH2)–C(=O).
7. Ala: hashed methyl, closing the ring back to Leu.

I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash by comparing the neighbour order in the SMILES with their arrangement on the page. The Leu, Arg and Glu centres come out as the usual L-configuration patterns, and both Adda double bonds are E.
