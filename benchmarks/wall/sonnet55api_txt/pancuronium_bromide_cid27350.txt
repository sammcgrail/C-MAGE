This is pancuronium bromide: a steroid core carrying two N-methylpiperidinium groups and two acetate esters, with two bromide counter-ions.

I identified the skeleton from the drawing. The structure is a four-ring steroid with a methyl at each of the two ring junctions (C10 and C13). An acetoxy group and an N-methylpiperidinium group sit on adjacent carbons of ring A (C3 and C2) and of ring D (C17 and C16). Two Br⁻ ions are shown separately.

The stereo descriptors in the SMILES are not derived atom by atom from the wedges and hashes. The drawing is rotated about 180° from the usual steroid orientation, so I could not check each centre reliably. I wrote the SMILES from the known configuration of pancuronium and took that to be what the drawing shows.
