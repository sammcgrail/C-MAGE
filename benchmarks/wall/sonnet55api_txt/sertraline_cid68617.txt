The structure is a tetralin (a benzene ring fused to a saturated six-membered ring) carrying three substituents:

- **Amine carbon:** a secondary N-methylamine (NHCH3) on one benzylic carbon, drawn with a hashed bond to N.
- **Aryl carbon:** a 3,4-dichlorophenyl group on the other benzylic carbon, also on a hashed bond. Counting around that ring from the attachment point, the two Cl atoms sit at the meta and para positions.
- **Linking carbons:** two CH2 groups join the two benzylic carbons.

This is the sertraline skeleton.

For stereochemistry, I worked out each centre from the hashed bond and the implicit H, which points toward the viewer.

- **Amine carbon:** N is hashed. Viewed from the H side, N, CH2 and the aromatic carbon run counterclockwise. Written in the SMILES neighbour order N, H, ring-closure aromatic C, CH2, this gives `[C@H]`.
- **Aryl-bearing carbon:** the aryl group is hashed. Viewed from the H side, CH2, aryl and the fused aromatic carbon run counterclockwise. Written in the SMILES neighbour order CH2, H, aryl, fused C, this gives `[C@@H]`.

SMILES: CN[C@H]1CC[C@@H](c2ccc(Cl)c(Cl)c2)c2ccccc21
