The molecule is a salicylic acid core: a benzene ring carrying COOH, an adjacent OH, and an NH substituent.

**Left ring.** Its vertices are C1 (bearing COOH), C2 (bearing OH), C3 (CH), C4 (CH), C5 (bearing NH) and C6 (CH). C5 is attached to C6, which is attached to C1. The NH is therefore para to the OH and meta to the COOH, giving 5-amino-salicylic acid.

**Linker.** The NH connects through a CH2 to the right ring.

**Right ring.** Going around from the CH2-bearing carbon:
- C1 carries the CH2.
- C2 carries F (upper left).
- C3 carries F (top).
- C4 carries CF3.
- C5 carries F (lower right).
- C6 carries F (bottom).

This gives 2,3,5,6-tetrafluoro-4-(trifluoromethyl)benzyl.
