The drawing shows a salicylic acid ring joined through an NH–CH2 linker to a fluorinated benzene ring.

**Left ring.** The ring carbon at the upper left carries the COOH group. The carbon next to it (lower left) carries OH. The carbon at the upper right of the ring carries the NH. The ring vertices run in this order:
- C1 at (317, 708) bears COOH.
- C2 at (447, 673) is CH.
- C3 at (541, 767) bears the NH.
- C4 at (508, 897) is CH.
- C5 at (378, 932) is CH.
- C6 at (283, 837) bears OH.

So COOH and OH are on adjacent carbons, and the NH is on C3, which is meta to the COOH carbon (C1) and para to the OH carbon (C6).
