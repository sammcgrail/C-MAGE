Reading the drawing region by region gives a tetracyclic aporphine skeleton, which matches apomorphine.

- **Catechol ring (left):** an aromatic ring carrying two adjacent OH groups.
- **Linking CH2:** this ring connects through a CH2 to the stereocentre, which is drawn with a hashed bond to H.
- **N-methyl piperidine ring (right):** the stereocentre bonds to N, which carries a methyl group. The N continues through two CH2 groups to an aromatic carbon of the lower benzene ring.
- **Lower benzene ring:** its other ring-fusion carbon is bonded directly to the stereocentre.
- **Biaryl bond:** the lower benzene ring is also bonded directly to the catechol ring.

For the stereocentre, the hashed bond puts H at the back. Viewed from the front, the sequence N → CH2 → aromatic C runs counterclockwise. In the SMILES below, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is aromatic C, H, N (ring closure 1), CH2. That order corresponds to `@`.

SMILES: CN1CCc2cccc3c2[C@H]1Cc1ccc(O)c(O)c1-3
