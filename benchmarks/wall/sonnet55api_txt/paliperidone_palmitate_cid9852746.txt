This is paliperidone palmitate. I read it as three parts:

- **Ester chain:** a straight chain of 16 carbons including the C=O, which is a palmitoyl group. I counted this from the zigzag spacing (about 15 bonds from the terminal methyl to the carbonyl), so the count is an estimate rather than an exact vertex-by-vertex tally.
- **Core:** a tetrahydropyrido[1,2-a]pyrimidin-4-one bearing the ester oxygen on the saturated ring carbon next to the ring fusion, a methyl group, and a two-carbon linker at the 3-position.
- **Side group:** the linker ends at the nitrogen of a piperidine whose 4-position carries a 6-fluoro-1,2-benzisoxazol-3-yl group.

The ester-bearing carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave no stereochemistry.
