I did not read every stereocentre off the image independently. For the vancomycin core I relied on my recollection of vancomycin's isomeric SMILES, so those centres are unchecked against the drawing.

The molecule is a vancomycin–cephalosporin conjugate with three parts:

- **Vancomycin aglycone and disaccharide.** This is the glycopeptide core with its vancosamine–glucose disaccharide, the N-methylleucine at the N-terminus, and the Asn side chain.
- **Linker.** The C-terminal carboxylic acid is converted to an amide, –C(=O)NH–CH2CH2CH2–O–N=, which carries the oxime.
- **Cephalosporin.** The oxime carbon bears a 2-amino-5-chlorothiazol-4-yl group and a C(=O)NH group on C7 of the cephem. The cephem has a pyridinium-methyl group at C3 and a carboxylate at C4.

Stereochemistry that I worked out from the drawing:

- **Oxime geometry.** From the bond directions, the O–N oxygen sits on the same side as the C(=O)N group and opposite the thiazole. I wrote this as `O/N=C(/thiazole)`, which places the oxygen and thiazole trans.
- **Cephem centres.** I mapped the C6 and C7 configurations from the cefotaxime SMILES onto my atom ordering.

I checked ring-closure labels and valences by hand, but I did not run the SMILES through a parser.
