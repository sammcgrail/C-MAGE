Reading the drawing, the molecule is a benzene ring attached through a CH2 to a stereocentre. That carbon carries an NH2 on a hashed bond and a CH2–O–C(=O)–NH2 carbamate chain. It is a phenylalaninol carbamate, with the benzene ring, CH2 and CH(NH2) as the first part and the CH2O–C(=O)NH2 group as the second.

For the stereocentre, I placed the hashed NH2 behind the plane and the implicit H in front. In the SMILES order (preceding CH2O, then H, N, CH2Ph) the other three neighbours then run counterclockwise when viewed from the CH2O carbon. That corresponds to `@`.

SMILES: NC(=O)OC[C@H](N)Cc1ccccc1
