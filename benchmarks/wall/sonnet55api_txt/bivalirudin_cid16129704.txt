I read the drawing as a linear 20-residue peptide, from the free-amine N-terminus to the free-acid C-terminus:

Phe-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Gly-Gly-Gly-Asn-Gly-Asp-Phe-Glu-Glu-Ile-Pro-Glu-Glu-Tyr-Leu

Each residue is as follows:
- The Arg side chain ends in a guanidine group, written `CCCNC(=N)N`.
- The Asn side chain is a primary amide, `CC(N)=O`.
- The Asp and the four Glu side chains are free carboxylic acids.
- The Tyr side chain carries a para-hydroxyl on the ring.
- The two Phe residues carry benzyl side chains.
- The Ile side chain is sec-butyl.
- The Leu side chain is isobutyl.

I did not trace each wedge or hash individually. I assigned the natural L configuration to every stereocentre, and for Ile also the natural (2S,3S) configuration, which I checked against the standard L-isoleucine SMILES.
