Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left end:** a tert-butyl group (a carbon with three methyls) is attached to an NH.
- **Chain:** NH connects to a CH2, then to a CH bearing an OH on a wedge bond, then to another CH2, which is bonded to an ether oxygen.
- **Ring system:** the ether oxygen is on an aromatic ring fused to a six-membered ring. That ring holds a ketone (C=O) next to the aromatic carbon and three CH2 groups, the last of which joins the aromatic carbon adjacent to the O-bearing carbon.
- **Stereochemistry:** only the carbinol carbon is a stereocentre. The wedged OH points toward the viewer, so the H is at the back. The substituent priorities are OH > CH2O > CH2N. Going OH → CH2O → CH2N is counterclockwise with H at the back, which gives the S configuration. In the SMILES order (CH2N, H, O, CH2O), this corresponds to `@`.
