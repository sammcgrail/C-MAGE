This is obeticholic acid, which is 6α‑ethyl‑chenodeoxycholic acid. The drawing shows a steroid core with a 3‑OH, a 7‑OH, a 6‑ethyl group, two angular methyls and a pentanoic acid side chain.

Reading the stereochemistry:
- The drawing is rotated and flipped relative to the usual steroid orientation, which swaps wedges and hashes.
- The two methyls are hashed in this view, which corresponds to β.
- The 3‑OH, 7‑OH and 6‑ethyl are wedged, which corresponds to α.
- The H at C5 is hashed, which corresponds to 5β, as in bile acids.
- The 8‑ and 9‑H and the other ring‑fusion H atoms are shown in the usual steroid configuration.

I did not derive every stereocentre independently from the picture. I built the SMILES from the chenodeoxycholic acid skeleton and added the 6α‑ethyl group. The configuration at C6 comes from my recollection of the obeticholic acid SMILES rather than from a separate check against the drawing.
