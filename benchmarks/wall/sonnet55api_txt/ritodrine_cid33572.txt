I read the drawing as ritodrine, with 1R,2S stereochemistry.

- **Left ring:** a para-substituted phenol, with an OH on one end and an ethylene chain (CH2CH2) on the other.
- **Linker:** that chain ends in an NH, which is bonded to a stereocentre carrying a hashed methyl.
- **Right side:** the next carbon carries a wedged OH and is bonded to a second para-substituted phenol ring.

Stereochemistry:

- **The N-bearing carbon:** the methyl is hashed, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The priorities are N > CH(OH) > CH3 > H. Read from the front, N → C1 → CH3 runs clockwise, and reversing for the H gives S.
- **The OH-bearing carbon:** the OH is wedged, so the implicit H points away. The priorities are OH > CH(N) > aryl > H. OH → CH(N) → aryl runs clockwise, which gives R.
