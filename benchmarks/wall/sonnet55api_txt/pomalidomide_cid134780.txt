The drawing shows pomalidomide: a glutarimide ring joined through its N-substituted carbon to a phthalimide that carries an NH2 on the benzene ring.

**Glutarimide ring:** NH is bonded to two C=O carbons. One C=O is bonded to CH2, which is bonded to CH2, which is bonded to the CH. That CH is bonded to the other C=O and to the phthalimide N. The ring has six members: N, C(=O), CH, CH2, CH2, C(=O).

**Phthalimide:** The N is bonded to two C=O carbons, each attached to the benzene ring at adjacent positions.

**Amino position:** The NH2 sits on the benzene carbon next to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the lower C=O (the one bonded to the bottom carbonyl carbon). This is the 4-amino position.

**Stereochemistry:** The stereocentre is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is specified.
