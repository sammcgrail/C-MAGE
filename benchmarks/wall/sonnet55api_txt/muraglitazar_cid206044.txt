This is a glycine N-substituted with a carbamate and a benzyl group (the muraglitazar structure).

Reading the drawing region by region:
- Top left: a methoxy group (CH3–O) on a para-substituted benzene ring. The ring's other para position carries O–C(=O), so this is a 4-methoxyphenyl ester of a carbamate.
- Carbamate: the C(=O) is bonded to N, and N carries both a CH2COOH group and a CH2 attached to a second para-substituted benzene ring.
- Second benzene ring: its para position carries an O–CH2CH2 chain leading to an oxazole.
- Oxazole: C4 bears the CH2CH2 chain and is bonded to N3. C5 bears the methyl and is bonded to O1. C2 bears the phenyl group and lies between N3 and O1.
- Far right: an unsubstituted phenyl ring on oxazole C2.

SMILES: COc1ccc(OC(=O)N(CC(=O)O)Cc2ccc(OCCc3nc(-c4ccccc4)oc3C)cc2)cc1
