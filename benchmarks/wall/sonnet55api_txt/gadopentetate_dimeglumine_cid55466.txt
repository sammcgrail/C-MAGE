The image shows three separate components: a gadolinium ion, a DTPA ligand and two meglumine molecules. This is gadopentetate dimeglumine.

**DTPA ligand (top).** Three nitrogens are linked by two –CH2CH2– bridges.
- The left nitrogen carries one CH2COOH and one CH2COO⁻.
- The central nitrogen carries one CH2COO⁻.
- The right nitrogen carries one CH2COOH and one CH2COO⁻.
- This gives three carboxylates and two free acids.

**Meglumine (two identical copies).** Each is CH3–NH–CH2 followed by four CH(OH) groups and a terminal CH2OH.
- Stereochemistry follows the hashes and wedges, taken from the NH end: hashed OH, hashed OH, hashed OH, then wedged OH.
- I assigned each centre from the drawn geometry, treating the implicit hydrogen as pointing opposite the drawn OH bond.
- This gives CNC[C@H](O)[C@@H](O)[C@H](O)[C@H](O)CO, which agrees with the standard N-methyl-D-glucamine SMILES as I remember it.
