The molecule has the connectivity of crizotinib, and the hashed methyl on the stereocentre gives the (R) configuration. I read it as follows:

- **Left:** a piperidine ring (NH at the bottom left) is attached through its 4-position to N1 of a pyrazole.
- **Middle:** the pyrazole C4 is bonded to C5 of a pyridine ring. That pyridine carries an NH2 on the carbon next to the ring nitrogen, and an ether oxygen on the neighbouring carbon.
- **Right:** the ether oxygen is bonded to a CH carbon bearing a hashed methyl. That carbon is also bonded to a benzene ring, which has Cl on both ortho positions and F on the ring carbon next to one of the Cl-bearing carbons.

For the stereocentre, the priorities are O > aryl carbon > CH3 > H. The methyl is hashed (pointing back), so H points toward the viewer. Going O → aryl → CH3 on the page appears counterclockwise, which reverses to clockwise once H is placed at the back, so the centre is R. Written as `C[C@@H](O...)c1...`, this gives R.
