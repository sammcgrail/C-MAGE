I read this as olodaterol. It has a 4-methoxyphenyl group on a CH2–C(CH3)2–NH–CH2 chain, then a CH(OH) stereocentre, then a 6-hydroxy-2H-1,4-benzoxazin-3(4H)-one ring system.

For the stereocentre, the OH is drawn on a hashed wedge, so it points away from the viewer, and the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are OH > CH2N > aryl carbon > H. Seen from the front, OH → CH2N → aryl runs counterclockwise. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is R.

Written as `NC[C@H](O)c2...`, the neighbour order is CH2, H, O, aryl, and `@` corresponds to R for that order.

SMILES: COc1ccc(CC(C)(C)NC[C@H](O)c2cc(O)cc3NC(=O)COc23)cc1
