This is oxprenolol. I read the drawing in three parts.

- **Amine end:** an isopropyl group (CH3, CH, CH3) is bonded to NH.
- **Linker:** the NH connects to CH2, then CH(OH), then CH2, then an ether O that attaches to the aromatic ring.
- **Ring:** a benzene ring carries two adjacent substituents. One is the ether O from the linker. The other is an O-allyl group, O–CH2–CH=CH2.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so the CH(OH) carbon gets no stereo.

SMILES: CC(C)NCC(O)COc1ccccc1OCC=C
