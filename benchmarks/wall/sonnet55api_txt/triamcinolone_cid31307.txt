The ring system is drawn flipped left to right compared with the usual steroid orientation (A ring on the right, D ring on the left). Flipping the molecule over inverts the wedge and hash marks. The drawn marks are then consistent with the natural configuration:

- The 11-OH, drawn hashed, is the usual β.
- The 9-F, drawn as a wedge, is the usual α.
- The 14-H, drawn as a wedge, is the usual α.
- The two methyls, drawn hashed, are the usual β.
