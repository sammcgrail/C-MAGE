This structure is elvitegravir, a 4-quinolone-3-carboxylic acid.

- **Quinolone core:** the ring nitrogen carries the side chain, C3 carries the COOH, and C4 is the C=O.
- **Benzene ring of the quinolone:** it bears the OMe and the benzyl CH2 substituent.
