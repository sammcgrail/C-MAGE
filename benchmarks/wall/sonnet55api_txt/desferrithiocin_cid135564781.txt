The drawing shows the sodium salt of desferrithiocin, a 4-methyl-4,5-dihydrothiazole-4-carboxylate carrying a 3-hydroxypyridin-2-yl group at C2.

- **Thiazoline ring:** the ring runs S–CH2–C(quaternary)–N=C. The C=N double bond is between N and the carbon attached to the pyridine.
- **Quaternary carbon:** it bears the carboxylate (C(=O)[O⁻]), a methyl on a wedge bond, the ring CH2 and the ring N.
- **Pyridine ring:** it is attached through C2, its ring N is adjacent to that carbon, and the OH sits on the next carbon (C3).
- **Counter-ion:** Na⁺.

**Stereochemistry:** the methyl is on a wedge, pointing toward the viewer. The other three substituents then run N (upper right), COO⁻ (upper left) and CH2 (down). Seen from the methyl side, N → COO⁻ → CH2 goes counterclockwise, which gives `@` for the neighbour order in the SMILES below.

As a check, the CIP priorities are N > CH2S > COO⁻ > CH3. With the methyl toward the viewer, N → CH2S → COO⁻ appears clockwise, so reversing it gives S, which matches the known (S)-desferrithiocin.
