I read this as the N-ethyl, N-methyl quaternary ammonium salt of scopolamine, with bromide as the counter-ion (the ethyl analogue of butylscopolamine bromide).

- **Core:** a tropane-type bicycle with an epoxide (the O bridging two adjacent carbons) and a positively charged nitrogen bearing one methyl and one ethyl group.
- **Ester:** the ring carbon bearing the ester oxygen is drawn with plain bonds, so I gave it no stereo label. The O–C(=O) carbon is attached to a CH carrying a CH2OH group and a phenyl ring; that carbon is drawn with a wedge, so it carries a stereo label.
- **Stereo marks:** the two bridgehead carbons and the two epoxide carbons carry the wedge/hash H atoms, so they carry stereo labels.
- **Source of the labels:** I did not derive the @/@@ assignments from the drawing. They are adapted from my recollection of the PubChem SMILES for butylscopolamine bromide, with butyl replaced by ethyl and the unmarked ring carbon left unspecified, so the stereo descriptors are the least certain part of the answer.
