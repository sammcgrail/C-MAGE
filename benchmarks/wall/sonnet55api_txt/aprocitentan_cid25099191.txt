This is the macitentan-like scaffold, so I read it region by region.

- **Central pyrimidine ring:** The ring atoms are C(NHSO2NH2), C(aryl), C(OCH2...), N, CH, N. The carbon bearing the sulfamide NH is bonded to ring N, and the carbon bearing the O-chain is bonded to the other ring N, with a CH between the two nitrogens.
- **Substituents on that ring:** The 4-bromophenyl group sits on the carbon between the NHSO2NH2-bearing carbon and the O-bearing carbon. The NH–S(=O)(=O)–NH2 group is on the left.
- **Linker:** The ring carbon on the right carries O–CH2–CH2–O, which connects to the second pyrimidine.
- **Second pyrimidine:** It is attached at C2 and carries Br at C5, giving 5-bromopyrimidin-2-yl.

SMILES: NS(=O)(=O)Nc1ncnc(OCCOc2ncc(Br)cn2)c1-c1ccc(Br)cc1
