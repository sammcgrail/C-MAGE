The structure is hexyl 5-amino-4-oxopentanoate (hexyl aminolevulinate).

Reading from the left of the drawing:
- H2N is attached to a CH2.
- That CH2 is attached to a C(=O) ketone.
- The ketone carbon is followed by two CH2 groups.
- The chain then reaches the ester carbonyl, C(=O)O.
- The ester oxygen carries a hexyl chain, which I counted as six carbons from the O.

To check the hexyl count, I followed the vertices to the right of the oxygen. They are about 890, 997, 1105, 1212, 1320 and 1425, which gives six carbons.

The SMILES is NCC(=O)CCC(=O)OCCCCCC.
