The drawing shows hexyl 5-amino-4-oxopentanoate (the hexyl ester of 5-aminolevulinic acid).

Reading the chain from the left:
- H2N is attached to a CH2.
- That CH2 is bonded to a C(=O), the ketone.
- Next come two CH2 groups.
- Then a C(=O) bonded to an O, the ester carbonyl.
- The ester O carries a chain of six carbons: the zigzag has O, then C at 890,770, 997,710, 1105,768, 1212,705, 1320,763, and 1423,700, which is six carbons.
