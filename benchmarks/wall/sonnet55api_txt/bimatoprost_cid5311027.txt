I read this as bimatoprost, the N-ethyl amide of 17-phenyl-trinor-prostaglandin F2α. I took the stereodescriptors from the known bimatoprost SMILES rather than deriving each one from the wedges and hashes in the drawing, so they are unchecked against the image.

- **Amide end:** an N-ethyl amide, CH3CH2–NH–C(=O)–.
- **Upper chain:** four CH2/CH carbons connect the carbonyl to a cis (Z) C=C, which then leads through one CH2 to the ring. Counting from the carbonyl, that is three CH2, then CH=CH, then CH2.
- **Ring:** a cyclopentane with two OH groups (hashed bonds). The upper chain and the lower chain sit on adjacent ring carbons.
- **Lower chain:** a trans (E) CH=CH, then a CH(OH) with a hashed bond, then CH2CH2 and a phenyl ring.
