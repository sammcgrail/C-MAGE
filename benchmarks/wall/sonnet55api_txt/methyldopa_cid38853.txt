I read this as methyldopa, (S)-2-amino-3-(3,4-dihydroxyphenyl)-2-methylpropanoic acid.

- **Ring:** a benzene ring with two adjacent OH groups (a catechol), at the 3 and 4 positions relative to the CH2 attachment.
- **Side chain:** the ring carries a CH2 that joins a quaternary carbon.
- **Quaternary carbon:** it bears NH2, a hashed methyl, the CH2 and a COOH group.

**Stereochemistry:** the methyl is hashed, so it points away from the viewer. The remaining substituents rank N > COOH > CH2Ar, and with the methyl pointing away they run counterclockwise in the drawing. That gives the S configuration.

In the SMILES, the neighbours of the stereocentre are written in the order CH3, N, CH2, COOH. Viewed from the methyl side, N → CH2 → COOH runs counterclockwise, which is `@`.

SMILES: C[C@](N)(Cc1ccc(O)c(O)c1)C(=O)O
