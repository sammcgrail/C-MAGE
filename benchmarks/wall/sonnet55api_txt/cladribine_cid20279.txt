The drawing is a 2-chloro-adenine base joined at N9 to a 2'-deoxyribose ring, which is cladribine (2-chloro-2'-deoxyadenosine).

- **Base:** the purine has Cl on C2 (between the two ring nitrogens) and NH2 on C6.
- **Sugar:** a five-membered ring made of O, C1', C2' (CH2), C3' (OH) and C4' (CH2OH).
- **Stereochemistry:** the N9 bond and the CH2OH bond are both drawn as hashes, and the C3' OH is a wedge. The base and CH2OH therefore sit on the same face with the OH on the opposite face, which matches the natural β-D-2'-deoxyribo configuration.

I wrote the stereocentres from the known cladribine SMILES, which I recalled rather than derived, and did not re-derive the @/@@ labels from the wedges.

SMILES: Nc1nc(Cl)nc2c1ncn2[C@H]1C[C@H](O)[C@@H](CO)O1
