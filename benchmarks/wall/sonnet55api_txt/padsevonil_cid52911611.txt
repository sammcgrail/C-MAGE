I read the molecule as an imidazo[2,1-b][1,3,4]thiadiazole core carrying three substituents.

- **Fused core:** the five-membered ring with S, the bridgehead N and the N=C is a 1,3,4-thiadiazole, fused to an imidazole. Starting from the CH2OMe carbon, the ring path is C(CH2OMe)=N–N(bridgehead)–C(=C–CF3)… with the second bridgehead carbon bonded to S.
- **Substituents on the core:**
  - A CH2OCH3 group on the thiadiazole carbon.
  - A CF3 group on the imidazole carbon.
  - A CH2 linking the neighbouring imidazole carbon to the lactam nitrogen.
- **Lactam:** a pyrrolidin-2-one ring (N, CH2, CH, CH2, C=O).
- **Stereocentre:** the ring CH at position 4 of the lactam carries a CH2CF2Cl group on a wedge bond.

For the stereocentre, I wrote the neighbours in the order: ring CH2 toward N, H, wedge CH2, ring CH2 toward C=O. The wedge puts H behind the page. Viewed from the front, the ring CH2 toward N, the wedge CH2 and the ring CH2 toward C=O run clockwise. That corresponds to @@ in this atom order.

SMILES: COCc1nn2c(CN3C[C@@H](CC(F)(F)Cl)CC3=O)c(C(F)(F)F)nc2s1
