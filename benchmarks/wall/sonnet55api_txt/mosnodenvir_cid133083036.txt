I read the structure as a 3-acylindole carrying a 5-OCF3 group, with the carbonyl bonded to a single stereocentre.

- **Indole:** The NH indole has the C(=O) group at C3 and OCF3 at C5.
- **Stereocentre:** The CH is bonded to the carbonyl carbon, an NH-aryl group, a chloro-methoxyphenyl ring, and an implicit H. The hashed bond points to the NH.
- **NH-aryl ring:** This benzene ring has the NH, an OMe and a methylsulfonyl group (SO2CH3) in a 1,3,5 pattern.
- **Chloro-methoxyphenyl ring:** The ring is attached to the stereocentre. It carries OMe ortho to that attachment and Cl para to it.

For the stereo, I took the hashed N as pointing away from the viewer, which puts the implicit H toward the viewer. Viewed from the front, N → C(=O) → aryl runs clockwise. In the SMILES neighbour order (C=O, H, N, aryl) this gives `@@`.
