This structure is carvedilol: a carbazole joined through an ether oxygen to a 2-hydroxypropyl chain, a secondary amine, an ethylene linker, and a 2-methoxyphenoxy group.

The drawing uses plain bonds only, so the CH(OH) carbon gets no stereo marker.

Reading the pieces:
- **Carbazole:** the NH sits in the five-membered ring between two benzo rings. The lower benzene ring carries the ether oxygen at the carbon adjacent to the ring-fusion carbon, which is carbazole position 4.
- **Chain:** the ether O connects to CH2, then CH(OH), then CH2, then NH, then CH2CH2, then O attached to the aryl ring.
- **Right ring:** the aryl ring has the O–CH2 substituent and, on the neighbouring carbon, an OCH3 (ortho).
