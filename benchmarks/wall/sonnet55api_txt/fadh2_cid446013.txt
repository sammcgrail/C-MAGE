This looks like reduced FAD (FADH2): a 1,5-dihydroisoalloxazine (flavin) ring joined to a ribityl chain, a pyrophosphate bridge, and adenosine.

- **Flavin ring:** the benzo ring carries two methyl groups. The central ring has NH at N5 and an N10 substituent, with both C=O groups on the pyrimidine ring (N1H, N3H), so the flavin is in its dihydro form.
- **Ribityl chain:** three stereocentres, each bearing OH. I worked out each centre from the wedges and hashes. The first and third OH groups are on wedges and the middle one is on a hash, which gives `[C@H]` at all three when written from N10 toward the phosphate.
- **Pyrophosphate:** two P(=O)(OH) groups linked by a bridging O, with one end on the ribityl C5 oxygen and the other on the adenosine 5′-oxygen.
- **Adenosine:** the ribose and adenine drawing matches natural D-ribose with an N9-linked adenine bearing NH2. For the sugar's stereo tags I relied on the standard natural-adenosine configuration rather than re-deriving them from the image.
