This structure is aprepitant. I read it region by region and worked out the stereochemistry from the three wedged bonds.

**Connectivity**
- The left ring is a 1,2,4-triazol-3-one: C(=O) between two NH groups, with C=N–NH closing the ring. Its ring carbon carries a CH2 that joins the morpholine nitrogen.
- The morpholine ring has N, O and two CH2 groups. The carbon next to the ring O bears an exocyclic O, and the carbon next to N bears a 4-fluorophenyl group.
- The exocyclic O is linked to a CH(CH3) carbon that carries a 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)phenyl ring.

**Stereocentres**
- The ethyl carbon has the methyl on a wedge, which gives `@@` in the SMILES order I used.
- The morpholine carbon bearing the exocyclic O has that O on a wedge, which gives `@`.
- The morpholine carbon bearing the fluorophenyl has that aryl group on a wedge, which gives `@`.
