I read the structure region by region, then worked out the stereocentre from the wedge.

- **Left ring:** an N-methyl pyridin-2-one. The ring nitrogen carries the methyl, the carbonyl carbon is next to it, and the nitrile sits on the ring carbon on the other side of that nitrogen. The ring carbon next to the carbonyl bears the NH linker.
- **Linker:** an NH joins the left ring to a stereogenic CH carbon, which also carries a methyl drawn as a wedge.
- **Right ring system:** a quinolin-2(1H)-one. The linker carbon attaches at C3, next to the C2 carbonyl, and there is a chlorine on the benzo ring.
- **Stereochemistry:** the stereocentre has N, the quinolinone C3, the wedged methyl and an implicit H. The CIP priorities are N > aryl carbon > CH3 > H. The methyl is wedged toward the viewer, so H points away. Viewed from the front, N (lower left) → aryl (right) → CH3 (top) runs counterclockwise, which makes the centre S. For the SMILES written as N[C@@H](C)c…, the neighbour order is N, H, CH3, aryl, and @@ corresponds to S.
