This is diethylstilbestrol, the E isomer of 4,4'-(hex-3-ene-3,4-diyl)diphenol.

- Two para-substituted benzene rings each carry an OH. The OH labels are at the far left and far right of the drawing.
- The central C=C has one ethyl group and one 4-hydroxyphenyl group on each alkene carbon.
- Geometry: the two aryl groups lie on opposite sides of the double bond, so the aryls are trans. Because each carbon carries one aryl and one ethyl, the ethyls are trans too. This is the E configuration.

The SMILES below writes the ethyls as trans, which matches the drawing.

SMILES: CC/C(c1ccc(O)cc1)=C(/CC)c1ccc(O)cc1
