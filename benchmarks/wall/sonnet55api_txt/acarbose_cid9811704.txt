This is acarbose with the reducing-end glucose drawn in its open-chain aldehyde form. I wrote the SMILES by adapting the known acarbose SMILES from memory, not by assigning each wedge in the drawing independently, so the stereocentres rest on that recalled structure.

The parts of the structure, left to right:
- **Valienamine unit:** a cyclohexene ring with a CH2OH on the alkene carbon, three OH groups, and an NH link on the ring carbon next to the double bond.
- **4-Amino-4,6-dideoxyglucose unit:** a pyranose ring carrying a methyl group, two OH groups and the ring nitrogen link. Its anomeric carbon is joined by an oxygen bridge to the next sugar.
- **Glucose unit:** a pyranose ring with a CH2OH and two OH groups, linked through its anomeric carbon by an oxygen bridge to C4 of the open-chain sugar.
- **Open-chain glucose:** CHO at C1, OH at C2, OH at C3, the glycosidic O at C4, OH at C5, and CH2OH at C6.

I checked the open-chain fragment by working out the CIP descriptors. They give C2 R, C3 S, C4 R and C5 R, which is D-glucose.
