The image shows four separate ansamitocin-type maytansinoid macrolactams. Each has the same core and differs only in the ester on the ring carbon next to the epoxide:

1. Top: isobutyrate, `OC(=O)C(C)C`.
2. Middle: acetate, `OC(C)=O`.
3. Bottom left: propionate, `OC(=O)CC`.
4. Bottom right: isovalerate (3-methylbutanoate), `OC(=O)CC(C)C`.

The shared core contains:
- a chloro-methoxy benzene ring bearing an N-methyl amide;
- a trisubstituted E-alkene conjugated with a second E-alkene;
- an OMe-bearing carbon next to a carbinolamide carbon (OH and carbamate NH);
- a methyl-bearing carbon next to the carbamate O–CH;
- a methyl epoxide.

I took the ring stereochemistry from the known configuration of ansamitocin P-3 (recalled, not re-derived from the wedges), and applied it to all four. The bottom-left molecule is drawn flipped, and I assumed it has the same configuration as the others.
