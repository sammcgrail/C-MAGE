The drawing shows a symmetric diester: propane-1,3-diyl bis[2-(4-chlorophenoxy)-2-methylpropanoate].

Each end of the molecule has a 4-chlorophenyl ring joined through an ether oxygen to a quaternary carbon carrying two methyl groups. That carbon is bonded to a C(=O) that forms an ester with O. The two ester oxygens are joined by a three-carbon chain (CH2–CH2–CH2). I counted three carbons in the chain: the two lower vertices and the apex between them.

The quaternary carbons carry two identical methyls, so there are no stereocentres and no stereo marks are needed.

SMILES: CC(C)(Oc1ccc(Cl)cc1)C(=O)OCCCOC(=O)C(C)(C)Oc1ccc(Cl)cc1
