The drawing shows deflazacort, the 21-acetate of an oxazoline-fused 11β-hydroxy-pregna-1,4-diene-3,20-dione steroid. I assigned the stereochemistry by matching it to the known natural configuration of deflazacort rather than deriving each centre from the wedges, so the @/@@ marks are the least certain part.

- **A ring:** a cross-conjugated dienone, with C=O at the right and a C=C–C=C pattern.
- **C10 and C13:** both carry methyl groups drawn with hashed bonds.
- **C11:** carries the OH.
- **D ring:** fused at C16/C17 to a 2-methyl-oxazoline, with N attached to C17 and O to C16.
- **C17 side chain:** C(=O)CH2–O–C(=O)CH3, i.e. the 21-acetate.
