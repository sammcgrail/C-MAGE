Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left ring:** a para-substituted benzene bearing OCF3 (trifluoromethoxy) at one end and a bond to the central benzene at the other.
- **Central benzene:** it carries the biaryl bond at one position, a methyl on the adjacent carbon, and the C(=O)NH amide on the carbon next to that methyl. So the substituents are 1-carboxamide, 2-methyl, 3-aryl.
- **Amide NH:** it is attached to a pyridine at the 3-position. The pyridine nitrogen sits next to the carbon (C6) that bears the morpholine.
- **Morpholine:** it is attached through its nitrogen. It carries two methyls on the carbons flanking the ring oxygen, both drawn with hashed bonds.

For the stereocentres, I assigned CIP priorities O > CH2(N) > CH3 > H at each carbon. With both methyls hashed, the top carbon works out as S and the bottom carbon as R. This is the cis-2,6-dimethylmorpholine, written in SMILES as `C[C@H]1CN(...)C[C@@H](C)O1`.
