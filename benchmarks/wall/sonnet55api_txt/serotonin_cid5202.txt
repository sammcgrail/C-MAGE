This is serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine): an indole ring with an aminoethyl chain at C3 and a hydroxyl on the benzene ring.

Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Pyrrole ring:** C3 is double-bonded to the carbon at (660, 963), which is C2. C2 bonds to NH at the bottom. NH bonds to C7a at (998, 908). C7a is also bonded to C3a at (902, 720), which closes the five-membered ring back to C3.
- **Benzene ring:** C3a is bonded to the carbon at (1016, 543), which is C4. C4 is double-bonded to C5 at (1227, 553), and C5 carries the OH. C5 bonds to C6 at (1324, 741), C6 is double-bonded to C7 at (1210, 919), and C7 bonds to C7a. The C3a=C7a bond is the remaining double bond in the aromatic ring.
