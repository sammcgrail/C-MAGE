The structure is doxepin: a dibenz[b,e]oxepine tricycle with an exocyclic double bond carrying a 3-(dimethylamino)propylidene chain.

**Connectivity**
- A dimethylamino group, N(CH3)2, is attached to a CH2–CH2–CH= chain.
- The =CH carbon is double-bonded to C11, the ring carbon of the seven-membered ring.
- C11 is bonded to two benzene rings. One ring carries the ring oxygen, and the other carries the ring CH2 that links to the oxygen.
- The oxygen and that CH2 close the seven-membered oxepine ring.

**Double-bond geometry**
- On C11, the aryl ring bearing the oxygen has higher CIP priority than the aryl ring bearing the CH2. At the first point of difference, the carbon bonded to oxygen has substituents (O,C,C), against (C,C,C) on the other ring.
- On the =CH carbon, the CH2 outranks H.
- In the drawing, the chain CH2 lies on the same side as the oxygen-bearing aryl ring, so the double bond is Z.

In the SMILES below, the `/` bonds place the chain CH2 trans to the CH2-bearing aryl ring, which puts it cis to the oxygen-bearing ring. That matches the Z geometry above.
