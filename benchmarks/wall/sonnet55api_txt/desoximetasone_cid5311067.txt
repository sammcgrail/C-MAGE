The drawing shows a pregna-1,4-diene-3,20-dione steroid with these features:

- a 9-fluoro group;
- an 11-hydroxyl;
- a 21-hydroxyl on the C(=O)CH2OH side chain;
- a 16-methyl;
- no hydroxyl at C17, only the side chain.

This is desoximetasone (17-deoxy dexamethasone).

The drawing is flipped relative to the usual steroid orientation, with ring A on the right and the wedges inverted. Reading the wedges and hashes with that in mind gives the usual configuration:

- 9α-fluoro;
- 11β-hydroxyl;
- 16α-methyl;
- β side chain at C17;
- β methyls at C10 and C13.

I derived the stereodescriptors by taking dexamethasone's SMILES and replacing its C17 hydroxyl with a hydrogen. I did not independently re-derive them from the drawing.
