The drawing shows a carboxylic acid (HO–C=O) attached to a straight chain of fully fluorinated carbons.

Counting along the chain, there is one carboxyl carbon, then CF2 groups at x = 360, 510, 660, 810, 960 and 1110, and finally a CF3 group at x = 1260.

- That makes 6 CF2 carbons plus 1 CF3 carbon, or 7 fluorinated carbons.
- Together with the carboxyl carbon, the chain has 8 carbons in total.
- The molecule is perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA).
