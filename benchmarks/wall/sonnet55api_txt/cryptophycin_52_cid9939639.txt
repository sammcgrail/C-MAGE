I read the structure as a 16-membered macrocyclic depsipeptide, a cryptophycin-type scaffold. This is my reading of the drawing, with the stereocentres assigned from the wedges and hashes.

**Macrocycle, traced clockwise from the isobutyl-bearing carbon**
1. The ring starts at a CH carrying an isobutyl group (drawn with a hashed bond) and bonded to a ring oxygen.
2. That oxygen links to an ester C(=O), then to a quaternary carbon bearing two methyls.
3. Next comes a CH2, then an NH, then an amide C(=O).
4. The following CH has a wedged CH2 leading to a 3-chloro-4-methoxyphenyl ring.
5. It is bonded to a second NH, which leads to a C(=O) and then a trans CH=CH, since the C=O and CH2 substituents lie on opposite sides of the double bond.
6. A CH2 follows, then a CH bearing a wedged bond and the second ring oxygen.
7. That oxygen connects to the C(=O) bonded back to the first CH, closing the ring.
