The drawing shows three separate components:

1. A cyclobutane ring with four carbons. One ring carbon carries two carboxylic acid groups, C(=O)O each, so this is cyclobutane-1,1-dicarboxylic acid.
2. An isolated NH2⁻ drawn at the top.
3. NH2⁻ attached to Pt²⁺, shown as "NH2⁻Pt2+".

The labels read as separate species, so I wrote them as separate components. The first NH2⁻ is a free ion, and the second is written next to Pt²⁺ with no drawn bond, so I treated it as an ion pair rather than a bonded unit.

Fragment SMILES:
- Acid: OC(=O)C1(CCC1)C(=O)O
- Amides: [NH2-] and [NH2-]
- Platinum: [Pt+2]

The charges balance: two NH2⁻ (−2) and Pt²⁺ (+2).
