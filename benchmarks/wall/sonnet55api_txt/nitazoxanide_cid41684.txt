This is nitazoxanide, a 2-acetoxybenzamide linked to a 5-nitrothiazole.

Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Acetyl group:** the methyl carbon is attached to a C(=O) that is bonded to an ester oxygen.
- **Benzene ring:** the ester oxygen sits on one ring carbon. The adjacent ring carbon carries the C(=O)NH amide group.
- **Amide:** the carbonyl carbon is bonded to NH, which is bonded to the thiazole C2.
- **Thiazole ring:** C2 is double-bonded to N3. N3 connects to C4 (CH), and C4 is double-bonded to C5. C5 bears the nitro group and is bonded to S1, which closes back to C2.
- **Nitro group:** drawn as the charge-separated form, [N+](=O)[O-].
