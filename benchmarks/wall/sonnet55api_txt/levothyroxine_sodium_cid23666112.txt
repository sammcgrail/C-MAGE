The structure is thyroxine (T4) as its sodium salt, which is levothyroxine sodium.

- **Amino acid end:** a carboxylate (O⁻ and C=O) is attached to a stereocentre bearing NH₂ on a wedge. A CH₂ then links this centre to the first aromatic ring.
- **Inner ring:** it carries two iodines, one at the top and one at the bottom right. A diaryl ether oxygen sits between the two iodines.
- **Outer ring:** the ether oxygen joins it to a ring bearing two iodines flanking an OH, which makes four iodines in total.
- **Counter-ion:** Na⁺ is drawn separately.
- **Stereochemistry:** with the wedged NH₂ pointing up, CH₂ at lower right, COO⁻ at lower left and H implied behind, N→CH₂→COO⁻ runs clockwise. That gives `N[C@@H](C...)C(=O)[O-]`, the L-amino acid configuration.
