This is apremilast, the (S)-enantiomer.

- **Left ring:** a benzene ring carries an ethoxy group (OCH2CH3) and a methoxy group (OCH3) on adjacent carbons.
- **Side chain:** the ring is attached to a stereocentre. That carbon bears a CH2–S(=O)(=O)–CH3 (methylsulfonylmethyl) group, a hydrogen, and the imide nitrogen.
- **Right fragment:** the imide nitrogen is part of an isoindoline-1,3-dione (phthalimide). The benzo ring of this unit carries an NH–C(=O)CH3 acetamide on the carbon next to one of the imide carbonyl carbons.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CH2 bond to the stereocentre is drawn as a hashed wedge, so the CH2 points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
  - The CIP priorities are N, then CH2S, then aryl, then H.
  - Seen from the front, N → CH2S → aryl runs clockwise.
  - Because H points toward the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving S.
  - In the SMILES below, the atom order around the stereocentre is aryl, H, CH2S, N, and `[C@@H]` in that order corresponds to S.
