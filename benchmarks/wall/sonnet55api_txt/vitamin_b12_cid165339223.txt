I wrote the SMILES from the drawing region by region, but I could not verify the stereodescriptors against the wedges and hashes. Treat the @/@@ marks as a best attempt and the connectivity as the reliable part.

- **Corrin macrocycle:** four reduced pyrrole-type rings. The ring nitrogen at the lower left carries the negative charge, and the other ring nitrogens are neutral imines. The ring on the upper right carries a gem-dimethyl carbon.
- **Side chains:** the macrocycle carries acetamide and propionamide arms (CH2C(=O)NH2 and CH2CH2C(=O)NH2) plus methyl groups.
- **Linker to the nucleotide:** a propionamide arm runs through NH–CH2–CH(CH3)–O–P(=O)(O⁻)–O.
- **Nucleotide:** the phosphate is attached to a ribofuranose bearing a CH2OH and a ring OH. The anomeric carbon is bonded to N1 of a 5,6-dimethylbenzimidazole.
- **Separate species:** Co³⁺ and C≡N⁻. The charges balance (+3 −1 −1 −1 = 0).
