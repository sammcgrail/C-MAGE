This is mifamurtide sodium hydrate (L-MTP-PE), a muramyl tripeptide phosphatidylethanolamine. I read it as a dipalmitoyl glycerophosphate linked through ethanolamine to the tripeptide. The count of 16 carbons per acyl chain is an estimate from the chain length in the drawing, matching palmitoyl.

The stereochemistry follows the known natural configurations, which I assumed to match the drawing rather than reading every wedge and hash:

- The glycerol centre is the natural R form, as in DPPC.
- The alanines are L, the isoglutamine is D, and the lactyl ether is R.
- The open-chain acetamido sugar is the D-gluco form (MurNAc).

The sodium counter-ion and the water of hydration are included as separate components.
