I read this as dolastatin 10, a linear pentapeptide-like chain. From right to left it runs: N,N-dimethylvaline, valine, an N-methyl dolaisoleuine unit carrying a methoxy group and an sec-butyl side chain, a pyrrolidine-containing dolaproine unit with a methoxy and a methyl group, and finally a phenethylamine unit bearing a thiazol-2-yl group.

I wrote the stereochemistry from my recollection of the known natural product rather than deriving each centre from the wedges and hashes. I did check the two valine-type centres: both come out L, which is consistent with the hashed bonds drawn to the nitrogens.
