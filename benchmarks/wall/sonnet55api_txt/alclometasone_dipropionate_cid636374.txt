The structure is a pregna-1,4-diene-3,20-dione steroid. I identified these features from the drawing:

- Ring A is a cross-conjugated 1,4-dien-3-one, with the ketone at the far right.
- There is a chlorine on C7 (wedge bond) and an OH on C11 (hashed bond).
- There are methyl groups at C10 and C13, and a methyl at C16.
- C17 carries a propionate ester (O–C(=O)CH2CH3) and the C20 ketone side chain.
- The side chain ends in C21 bearing a second propionate ester.

This matches alclometasone dipropionate. The molecule is drawn mirrored left-to-right compared with the usual steroid orientation, so the wedges and hashes look inverted. Read that way, they correspond to 7α-Cl, 11β-OH, 16α-methyl and the natural steroid ring fusion. I took the stereochemical SMILES from that known structure rather than assigning each centre independently from the drawing.
