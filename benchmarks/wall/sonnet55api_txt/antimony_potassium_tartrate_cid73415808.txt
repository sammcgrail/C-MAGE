The drawing shows these components, which I wrote as separate fragments joined with dots:

- Two Sb³⁺ ions and two K⁺ ions.
- Two identical tartrate tetraanions. Each is ⁻O₂C–CH(O⁻)–CH(O⁻)–CO₂⁻, with both alkoxide oxygens on wedge bonds.
- Three water molecules. Two are labelled H₂O. The third label is garbled, with H₂ above an O and a superscript, and I read it as a third water. This also balances the charge: 2 × (+3) + 2 × (+1) = +8 against 2 × (−4) = −8, so no extra O²⁻ is needed.

For the stereochemistry I assigned CIP priorities at each stereocentre as O > COO⁻ > C > H. With the hydrogen pointing away from the viewer, the order O → COO⁻ → C runs clockwise at both carbons, so both centres are R. This is the (R,R), or L-tartrate, form. The corresponding SMILES for each tartrate is `[O-]C(=O)[C@H]([O-])[C@@H]([O-])C([O-])=O`.
