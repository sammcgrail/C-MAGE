The drawing shows cytidine triphosphate (CTP):

- **Base:** a cytosine ring with the 4-NH2 and 2-C=O groups, attached through N1.
- **Sugar:** a ribofuranose ring with 2'-OH and 3'-OH, where the base sits on a wedge bond at C1' and the CH2 at C4' is also on a wedge.
- **Phosphates:** the 5'-CH2–O leads to a chain of three phosphorus atoms, each with a P=O and OH groups, linked through bridging oxygens.

I did not derive the @/@@ marks atom by atom from the wedges. I took the stereo SMILES from the standard form of CTP, which has the β-D-ribo configuration the drawing appears to show.
