The other two stereocentres, and the SMILES string itself, rely on my recall of amprenavir's published structure rather than being re-derived from the image.

**Stereochemistry:** At the hydroxyl carbon, the priorities are OH, then CH(N), then CH2N, then H. With OH hashed and H toward the viewer, I get R, which matches the published (2S,3R) assignment for the hydroxy-bearing carbon in amprenavir. The other two centres are written to match the known structure.
