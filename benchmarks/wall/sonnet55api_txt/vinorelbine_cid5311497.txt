This is anhydrovinblastine, a bisindole (vinca-type) alkaloid. I read it as an upper indole-fused azacycle joined to a lower vindoline-type unit.

**Upper half**
- The benzene ring is fused to an NH-indole, and C3 of the indole carries a CH2 that leads to a tertiary N.
- That nitrogen is part of a tetrahydropyridine ring bearing an ethyl group on a ring C=C.
- The ring CH next to the double bond has a wedged H.
- The quaternary carbon carries a hashed CO2Me, the indole C2, a CH2, and the aryl ring of the lower half.

**Lower half**
- The aryl ring carries an OMe and an N-methyl indoline nitrogen.
- Fused to it is a polycyclic core containing a tertiary N, a CH2CH2 bridge and a ring CH=CH.
- Around the cyclohexane ring there is a quaternary C bearing a wedged ethyl, a hashed OAc, a hashed OH together with CO2Me, and two ring-fusion H's.
