This is amphotericin B, a heptaene macrolide, and I wrote the SMILES by recognising that scaffold and checking it against the drawing rather than deriving every centre from the wedges. The checks I made:

- **Polyene:** I counted seven conjugated C=C bonds running along the bottom of the macrocycle, which matches a heptaene. I wrote all of them as E (trans); the drawing's zig-zag geometry is consistent with that.
- **Hydroxylated chain:** going from the ketal carbon toward the lactone C=O, the chain is CH2, CH(OH), CH2, CH(OH), CH(OH), CH2, CH2, CH(OH), CH2, CH(OH), CH2, C(=O)O. This matches the drawing.
- **Ketal ring:** it is a tetrahydropyran with a hemiketal OH, a ring OH and a COOH.
- **Sugar:** the mycosamine (3-amino-3,6-dideoxymannose) is attached through an O-glycosidic link to the CH just after the polyene.
- **Lactone end:** the ring closes through the ester O onto a CH(CH3), which is followed by CH(CH3), CH(OH) and CH(CH3), the last of which is attached to the diene.
