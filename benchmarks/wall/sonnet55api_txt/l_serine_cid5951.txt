The drawing is serine, HOOC–CH(NH2)–CH2–OH.

- The left end is a carboxylic acid: a carbon with a C=O (top O) and an HO group.
- That carbon is bonded to the alpha carbon, which carries NH2 on a hashed bond.
- The alpha carbon is also bonded to a CH2 that ends in OH.

For the stereocentre, the priorities are N > COOH > CH2OH > H. The hashed NH2 points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Going from N (down) to COOH (upper left) to CH2OH (upper right) reads clockwise on the page. Because the H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is S. That makes the molecule L-serine, written as N[C@@H](CO)C(=O)O.
