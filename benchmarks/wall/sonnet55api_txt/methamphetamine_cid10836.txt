Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left chain:** a methyl group is bonded to an NH, so the amine is a secondary N-methyl amine.
- **Stereocentre:** the NH is bonded to a carbon that carries a hashed methyl (pointing back) and a CH2 that leads to the ring.
- **Ring:** the CH2 is attached to a benzene ring drawn with three alternating double bonds, so it is a phenyl group.

Together this is N-methyl-1-phenylpropan-2-amine (methamphetamine).

**Stereochemistry:** the CIP priorities at the stereocentre are N > CH2Ph > CH3 > H.

1. The hashed methyl points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer.
2. In the drawing, N is up, CH2 is lower right and CH3 is lower left. Going N → CH2 → CH3 is clockwise as drawn.
3. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S.
