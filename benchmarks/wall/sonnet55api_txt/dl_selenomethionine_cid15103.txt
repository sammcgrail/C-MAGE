The drawing shows selenomethionine, with plain bonds throughout.

Reading the chain from left to right:
- A methyl carbon is bonded to Se.
- Se is bonded to a CH2, then a second CH2.
- That CH2 is bonded to the alpha carbon, which carries NH2.
- The alpha carbon is bonded to a carboxylic acid carbon, C(=O)OH.

The alpha carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I give no stereochemistry.

SMILES: C[Se]CCC(N)C(=O)O
