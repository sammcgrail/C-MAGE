This is isradipine, a dihydropyridine bearing a benzoxadiazole ring.

**Dihydropyridine ring.** The NH sits at the top. On either side of it is a carbon carrying a methyl group. Each of those carbons is double-bonded to a ring carbon that carries an ester. The sp3 CH at the bottom of the ring is attached to the aryl group.

**Esters.** The left ester is an isopropyl ester: C(=O)OC(C)C. The right ester is a methyl ester: C(=O)OC.

**Aryl group.** The benzoxadiazole is attached through the benzene carbon at position 4. That carbon is next to the fused carbon bonded to N, and that N is bonded to O, which is bonded to the second N. The benzene ring runs from the attachment carbon (c1) to CH, CH, CH, then the fused carbon bearing the second N.

In the drawing, the attachment carbon at (869, 875) is bonded to the fused carbon at (1036, 929). That fused carbon is bonded to the upper N at (1190, 840), which connects to the O and then to the lower N at (1245, 1115). The lower N is bonded to the other fused carbon at (1072, 1099).
