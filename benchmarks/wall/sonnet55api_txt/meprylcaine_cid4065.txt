Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Chain on the left:** a propyl group (CH3–CH2–CH2) is attached to an NH.
- **Central carbon:** the NH is bonded to a quaternary carbon that carries two methyl groups and a CH2.
- **Ester:** that CH2 is bonded to an O, which is bonded to a C(=O) that carries a phenyl ring (a benzoate ester).

This is 2-methyl-2-(propylamino)propyl benzoate (the compound known as meprylcaine). All bonds are plain, so there is no stereochemistry.
