The drawing is a linear chain of carbons with fluorine atoms on every carbon.

Counting the carbons along the chain, the x-positions are about 235, 382, 529, 676, 823, 970, 1117 and 1265. That gives 8 carbons.

Each carbon carries fluorines as follows:
- The two end carbons each have 3 fluorines: one terminal F plus one above and one below.
- Each of the 6 inner carbons has 2 fluorines, one above and one below.

This is perfluorooctane, C8F18: (2 × 3) + (6 × 2) = 18 fluorines.

SMILES: FC(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)C(F)(F)F
