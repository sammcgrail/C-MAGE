I read this as 5-formyltetrahydrofolic acid (leucovorin / folinic acid).

- **Glutamate end:** two carboxylic acids, with the alpha carbon bearing the amide NH. The hashed bond points to the CH2 of the side chain. That makes the alpha carbon S, the natural L-glutamate configuration.
- **Linker:** a para-substituted benzene ring, with a C(=O)NH amide on one side and an NH–CH2 on the other.
- **Pterin end:** a tetrahydropteridine ring. N5 carries a formyl group (N–CHO) and is bonded to the carbon next to the ring C=O. The ring also has the lactam NH–C=O, the 2-amino amidine carbon (C(N)=N) and a ring NH–CH2.
- **C6 stereocentre:** the hashed bond goes to the exocyclic CH2 (toward the NH–aryl). Working out the geometry with the implicit H toward the viewer gives `@` for the neighbour order used in the SMILES below.
