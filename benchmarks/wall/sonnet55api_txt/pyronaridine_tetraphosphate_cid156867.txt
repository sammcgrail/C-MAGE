This is pyronaridine tetraphosphate: one pyronaridine cation plus four phosphoric acid molecules.

**Core ring system.** The benzo[b][1,5]naphthyridine core is a tricyclic aromatic system.
- Ring A is a benzene ring carrying Cl.
- The middle ring contains an N at the top (N at 765,768) and a carbon at the bottom bearing the NH substituent.
- Ring C is a pyridine ring with an N and a methoxy group.
