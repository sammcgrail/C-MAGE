The drawing is a phthalazine ring with a hydrazine group attached, which is hydralazine.

**Ring system.** A benzene ring is fused to a six-membered ring containing two adjacent nitrogens. Going around the second ring:
- The top nitrogen (N) is double-bonded to the CH carbon, which is bonded to the ring-fusion carbon.
- The top nitrogen is single-bonded to the lower nitrogen (N).
- The lower nitrogen is double-bonded to the carbon that bears the NHNH2 group.
- That carbon is bonded to the other ring-fusion carbon.

**Substituent.** The carbon next to the lower ring nitrogen carries NH–NH2, which gives the hydrazine group.

**SMILES.** Writing the ring starting from the hydrazine-bearing carbon gives NNC1=NN=CC2=CC=CC=C12. The benzene ring is aromatic, and the Kekulé form shown above is valid.
