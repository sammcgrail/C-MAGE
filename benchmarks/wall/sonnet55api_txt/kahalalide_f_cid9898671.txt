I read the molecule as a lipopeptide: a 5-methylhexanoyl chain on a linear peptide that closes into a macrocyclic depsipeptide through a Thr ester bond.

**Linear part, from the acyl tail:**
- 5-Methylhexanoyl, CC(C)CCCC(=O)–
- Val, R at the α-carbon
- Thr, α S and β R, with a free OH
- Val, S
- Val, R
- Pro, drawn as R
- Orn, S, with a free 3-aminopropyl side chain
- Ile, R at the α-carbon and S at the β-carbon

**Macrocycle:**
- Thr, α R and β R. Its β-oxygen forms the ester that closes the ring.
- Ile, R at the α-carbon and S at the β-carbon
- Val, R
- Phe, S
- Dehydrobutyrine (Dhb), with the nitrogen and the methyl on the same side of the double bond, so Z
- Val, S, whose carbonyl is the ester carbonyl closing back onto the Thr β-oxygen

I assigned each stereocentre from the wedge or hash on its bond, then converted it to @/@@ by neighbour order. I did not run a SMILES validator on the result.
