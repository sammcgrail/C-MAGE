I read the structure as an N-substituted 3-aminocyclopent-2-enone attached to a fused bicyclic amine (an octahydroisoindole). The cyclohexane ring of that bicycle carries a 4-fluorophenyl group and an ether side chain.

**Regions of the drawing**
- **Cyclopentenone:** C(=O)–CH2–CH2–C(=CH), with the ring carbon at the C=C attached to the nitrogen.
- **Bicyclic core:** the nitrogen sits in a five-membered ring fused to a cyclohexane. The ring-fusion carbons are both drawn with H labels, one hashed and one wedged.
- **Aryl group:** a 4-fluorophenyl on the cyclohexane carbon next to the ring fusion, attached by a hashed bond.
- **Ether side chain:** the next cyclohexane carbon bears an O on a wedge. That O is bonded to a CH(CH3) with a hashed methyl, and the CH carries a 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)phenyl ring.

**Stereocentres:** I assigned the @/@@ marks by working out the geometry at each of the five centres from the wedge and hash bonds. These are the two ring-fusion carbons, the aryl-bearing carbon, the O-bearing carbon, and the side-chain CH(CH3). I did this by hand, so the stereo marks are the part most open to error.
