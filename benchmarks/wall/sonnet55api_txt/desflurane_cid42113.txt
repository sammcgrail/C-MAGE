The drawing shows a difluoromethyl group (CHF2) on the left, bonded through an oxygen to a CHF carbon. That carbon is bonded to a CF3 group. This is desflurane.

Reading the atoms in order:
- The left carbon carries two fluorines and connects to the oxygen: FC(F)O.
- The oxygen connects to the middle carbon, which carries one fluorine and is attached to CF3: C(F)C(F)(F)F.

All bonds are plain, with no wedges or hashes, so no stereochemistry is included. The middle carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn without stereo marks.
