I read the molecule as a 4-amino-pyrazolo[3,4-d]pyrimidine core:

- **Position 3** carries an alkyne linked to a 3,5-dimethoxyphenyl ring.
- **N1** is bonded to the 3-position of a pyrrolidine ring.
- **The pyrrolidine nitrogen** carries an acryloyl group, C(=O)CH=CH2.

The only stereocentre is pyrrolidine C3, drawn with a wedge to the pyrazole N. I took the wedge as having its narrow end at the carbon, so the N points toward the viewer and the implicit H points away.

CIP priorities at that carbon are N > CH2 bonded to the ring N > CH2 bonded to CH2 > H. Viewed with H at the back, N (left), then the CH2 bonded to the ring N (below), then the other CH2 (upper right) run counterclockwise, so the centre is S.

In the SMILES below, `[C@@H]` is the writing order that gives S for this neighbour sequence.

SMILES: C=CC(=O)N1CC[C@@H](C1)n1nc(C#Cc2cc(OC)cc(OC)c2)c2c(N)ncnc12
