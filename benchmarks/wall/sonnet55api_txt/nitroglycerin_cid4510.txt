The drawing shows a three-carbon chain: CH2, CH, CH2. Each carbon carries an O bonded to a nitro-type group, written O–[N+](=O)[O-]. This is nitroglycerin (glyceryl trinitrate).

The central CH is a stereocentre by connectivity only. Its two CH2 substituents are identical, so it is not a real stereocentre, and no stereo marks are drawn anyway.
