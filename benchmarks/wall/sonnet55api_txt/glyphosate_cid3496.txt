The drawing shows glyphosate, read left to right:

- A phosphorus atom (P) carries one double-bonded oxygen (=O) and two hydroxyl groups (OH).
- P is bonded to a CH2, which is bonded to an NH.
- The NH is bonded to a second CH2, which leads to a carboxylic acid group, C(=O)OH.

There are no wedges, hashes or charges, so no stereochemistry is needed.

SMILES: O=P(O)(O)CNCC(=O)O
