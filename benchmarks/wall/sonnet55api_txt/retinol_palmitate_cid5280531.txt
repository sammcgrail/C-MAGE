This is retinyl palmitate, the palmitic acid ester of all-trans retinol.

- **Acyl chain:** the left-hand zigzag has 16 carbons counting the carbonyl carbon (15 bonds), so it is a palmitoyl group, C(=O) with a 15-carbon saturated chain. It is joined through the ester oxygen to a CH2.
- **Polyene:** from that CH2 there are four C=C bonds in conjugation. Two of them carry methyl groups, at the 3- and 7-positions counted from the CH2. All four double bonds look trans in the drawing.
- **Ring:** the chain ends at a cyclohexene ring. The ring double bond carries a methyl on one carbon, and the carbon next to the chain attachment is a gem-dimethyl quaternary carbon.
