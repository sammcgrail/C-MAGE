This drawing is ethinylestradiol (17α-ethynylestradiol), and I wrote the SMILES from that identification. I did not re-derive each @/@@ symbol from the wedges, so the stereo string comes from my memory of the PubChem entry.

- **Skeleton:** the drawing is a steroid with a fused four-ring core. On the right is an aromatic A ring carrying a phenolic OH. Two six-membered rings follow, then a five-membered D ring on the left.
- **C17 (D ring):** this carbon bears both a tertiary OH, drawn with a bold wedge, and an ethynyl group (C≡CH).
- **C13:** carries a methyl drawn with a bold wedge.
- **Ring-fusion hydrogens:** H at C8 is drawn bold, and H at C9 and H at C14 are drawn hashed.

The wedge pattern is consistent with the natural estradiol configuration, so I used that stereochemistry.

SMILES: C#C[C@]1(CC[C@@H]2[C@@]1(CC[C@H]3[C@H]2CCC4=C3C=CC(=C4)O)C)O
