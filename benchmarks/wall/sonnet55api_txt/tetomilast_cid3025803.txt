The drawing shows three connected ring systems.

- **Pyridine ring:** the carboxylic acid (C(=O)O) sits on the carbon next to the ring nitrogen. The carbon on the other side of that nitrogen carries the thiazole.
- **Thiazole ring:** its C4 attaches to the pyridine. The ring runs C4, then N, then C2 (which bears the aryl group), then S, then C5 (CH), and back to C4.
- **Benzene ring:** it is attached to thiazole C2 and carries two ethoxy groups. Numbering the attachment carbon as 1, the ethoxy groups are at positions 3 and 4, so it is a 3,4-diethoxyphenyl.
