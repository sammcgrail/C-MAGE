I read this as diacetylmorphine (heroin). It is a morphinan skeleton with a 4,5-epoxy ether bridge, a 6-acetoxy group on the cyclohexene ring, a 3-acetoxy group on the aromatic ring and an N-methyl tertiary amine.

I did not derive the stereocentre symbols independently. The drawing shows hashed or wedged bonds at C5, C6, C9, C13 and C14, and I took the @/@@ pattern from the standard heroin SMILES, which I recalled from memory, rather than re-deriving it from the wedges. The overlapping O and H labels near the bottom of the drawing are the epoxy-bridge oxygen and the hydrogen on C5.

SMILES: CC(=O)O[C@H]1C=C[C@H]2[C@H]3CC4=C5[C@]2([C@H]1OC5=C(C=C4)OC(=O)C)CCN3C
