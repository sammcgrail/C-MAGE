I read the molecule as a boronic acid with an isobutyl group, two amide links and a 2,5-dichlorophenyl ring.

- **Left end:** an isopropyl group (CH(CH3)2) is attached to a CH2. That CH2 is joined to the stereocentre by a hashed bond. The stereocentre also carries B(OH)2 and an NH.
- **Middle:** the NH is acylated by a C(=O) that connects to a CH2, then to a second NH.
- **Right end:** the second NH is acylated by a C(=O) bonded to a benzene ring. Counting from the carbonyl-bearing ring carbon, one Cl is on the adjacent ring carbon (position 2) and the other is on the carbon para to that one (position 5).
- **Stereochemistry:** the only wedge or hash is the hashed bond from the stereocentre to the CH2, so the CH2 points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer. Viewed from the H side, the sequence CH2 → B → N runs counterclockwise. For the SMILES neighbour order (CH2, H, B, N), this corresponds to @@.

SMILES: CC(C)C[C@@H](B(O)O)NC(=O)CNC(=O)c1cc(Cl)ccc1Cl
