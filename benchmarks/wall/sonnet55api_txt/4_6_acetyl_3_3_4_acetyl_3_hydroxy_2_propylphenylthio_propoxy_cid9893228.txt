I read the structure as two substituted benzene rings joined by a sulfur–propyl–oxygen linker, with a butanoic acid chain on the right ring.

**Left ring** (going around from the carbon bearing the acetyl group):
- C1 carries the acetyl group, CC(=O).
- C2 carries OH.
- C3 carries the propyl group.
- C4 carries S.
- C5 and C6 are CH.

**Linker:** S–CH2CH2CH2–O, three carbons, with the O attached to the right ring.

**Right ring:**
- Ring carbon a carries the O of the linker.
- Ring carbon b carries the propyl group.
- Ring carbon c carries the O of the butanoic acid chain, O–CH2CH2CH2–COOH.
- Ring carbon d carries the acetyl group.
- The remaining two carbons are CH.

The ring order is: the carbon bearing the linker O (C-O), then the propyl carbon, then the carbon bearing O-butanoic acid, then the acetyl carbon, then two CH, and back to the start.
