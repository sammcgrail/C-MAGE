This drawing is suramin, a symmetric polysulfonated urea. I read it in three parts and joined them.

- **Terminal groups:** each end is a naphthalene bearing three sulfonic acids. The carbon carrying the amide NH is C8, and the SO3H groups sit at C1 (peri to the NH carbon), C3 and C5, giving an 8-aminonaphthalene-1,3,5-trisulfonic acid.
- **Middle units:** each naphthylamine is acylated by a 4-methylbenzoyl ring. The methyl carbon is adjacent to the ring carbon bearing the second NH, and the carbonyl-bearing carbon is meta to that NH carbon. That second NH is acylated by a meta-substituted benzoyl ring.
- **Centre:** the two meta-substituted benzoyl rings are joined through their ring carbons by a urea, NH–C(=O)–NH.
