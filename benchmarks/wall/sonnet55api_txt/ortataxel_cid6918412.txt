This is a taxane (a docetaxel/baccatin-type skeleton) with a cyclic carbonate bridging C1 and C14.

**Substituents read from the drawing:**
- C10 carries an acetate and C9 is a ketone.
- C7 carries an OH.
- C2 carries a benzoate.
- C4 carries an acetate and is part of the oxetane ring.
- C13 carries the side-chain ester.
- The side chain is –OC(=O)–CH(OH)–CH(NHBoc)–CH2CH(CH3)2, so the usual phenyl is replaced by isobutyl.

**Stereochemistry:** I based the ring-system stereocentres on the natural docetaxel configuration. I then checked the new or changed centres against the drawn wedges and hashes:
- C14 (H hashed, carbonate O on the same face as the C1 oxygen) gives `[C@@H]5`.
- C13 (O hashed) gives `[C@@H]1`.
- The side-chain C2′ (OH wedged) and C3′ (isobutyl hashed) are consistent with the docetaxel pattern.

I did not independently re-derive every ring-system centre from the image, so those rest on the natural-configuration assumption.
