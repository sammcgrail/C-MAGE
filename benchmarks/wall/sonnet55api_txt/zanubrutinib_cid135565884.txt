This structure is zanubrutinib, a tetrahydropyrazolo[1,5-a]pyrimidine BTK inhibitor.

- **Acryloyl piperidine:** an N-acryloyl group (CH2=CH–C(=O)–N) on a piperidine nitrogen. The piperidine C4 carries the bicyclic core.
- **Bicyclic core:** a pyrazole fused to a saturated six-membered ring that holds an NH.
  - The stereocentre is the CH next to the ring N–N bridgehead nitrogen. It is drawn with a wedge bond to the piperidine C4.
  - The pyrazole bears a primary carboxamide (C(=O)NH2) and, on the carbon adjacent to the pyrazole N, a 4-phenoxyphenyl group.
- **Stereochemistry:** viewed from the wedged piperidine carbon, the implicit H, the ring nitrogen and the ring CH2 run clockwise on screen. That corresponds to @@ in the SMILES neighbour order (piperidine C, H, N, CH2). This agrees with the known (S) configuration of zanubrutinib.
