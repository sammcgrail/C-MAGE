I read the structure region by region and wrote it as a maleimidocaproyl‑N‑methylvaline‑valine‑dolaisoleuine‑dolaproine‑phenylalanine chain, which is the mc‑MMAF framework.

- **Left end:** a maleimide ring, N‑linked to a five‑carbon chain (CCCCC) that ends in an amide carbonyl on an N‑methyl nitrogen.
- **First residue:** an N‑methylvaline. I checked its stereocentre against the wedge on the isopropyl group, and it comes out as `[C@H]` in the SMILES order used below.
- **Second residue:** a valine joined through an NH.
- **Next unit:** an N‑methylated carbon bearing a sec‑butyl group, then a CH(OMe) carbon and a CH2–C(=O) group. This is the dolaisoleuine unit.
- **Pyrrolidine unit:** the carbonyl is attached to the ring nitrogen. The ring carbon next to the nitrogen carries a CH(OMe)–CH(CH3)–C(=O) side chain. This is the dolaproine unit.
- **C‑terminus:** an NH‑linked phenylalanine with a free COOH and a phenyl ring.
