I read the drawing as a gold(I) cation paired with a thiolate on a pyranose ring. This is the aurothioglucose pattern, with S⁻ at the anomeric carbon.

**Connectivity**
- The ring has five carbons and one oxygen.
- The anomeric carbon (C1) carries S⁻, drawn as a wedge.
- C2, C3 and C4 each carry an OH.
- C5 carries a CH2OH group, drawn as a hash.
- Au⁺ is a separate ion.

**Stereochemistry**
I assigned each centre from its wedge or hash, with the implicit H taking the opposite side.

| Ring carbon | Substituent and bond | Tag in SMILES |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | S⁻ wedge | `[C@H]1` |
| C5 | CH2OH hash | `[C@H](CO)` |
| C4 | OH wedge | `[C@@H](O)` |
| C3 | OH hash | `[C@H](O)` |
| C2 | OH wedge | `[C@H]1O` |

I derived these by hand from the 2D geometry, so the @/@@ assignments carry some risk of a slip.
