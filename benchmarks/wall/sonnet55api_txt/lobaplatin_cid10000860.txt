Reading the drawing as three separate components:

1. **Lactate dianion.** A carboxylate (C(=O)O⁻) is attached to a stereocentre that carries a hashed methyl and an O⁻ (alkoxide). Taking the hashed methyl as pointing away and the implicit H toward the viewer, the stereocentre works out to `C[C@H]([O-])C(=O)[O-]`.

2. **Cyclobutane-1,2-diyl-bis(methylamine).** The four-membered ring has two adjacent substituted carbons, each bearing a CH2NH2 group.
   - The left ring carbon has a wedged CH2NH2, so its H points away.
   - The right ring carbon has a hashed CH2NH2, so its H points toward the viewer.
   - Working through the neighbour order for each centre gives `NC[C@@H]1CC[C@H]1CN`.

3. **Platinum.** It is drawn as a separate Pt²⁺ ion, written `[Pt+2]`.

The charges balance: the two O⁻ groups give −2 and Pt gives +2. I derived the stereo marks by hand from the drawing and did not run them through a toolkit.
