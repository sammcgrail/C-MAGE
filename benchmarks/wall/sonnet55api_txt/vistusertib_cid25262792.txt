I read the structure as a pyrido[2,3-d]pyrimidine core carrying three substituents:

- **C7 (next to N8):** a phenyl ring bearing an N-methylcarboxamide (C(=O)NHCH3) at the meta position.
- **C2 and C4:** each carries a morpholine attached through its nitrogen. Each morpholine has a methyl on the carbon next to that nitrogen.

Each methyl is drawn on a wedge bond, so both methyl-bearing carbons are stereocentres. Priorities at each are N > CH2–O > CH3 > H. In both morpholines the wedged methyl points toward the viewer, so H is at the back. Going N → CH2O → CH3 runs counterclockwise in each case, so both centres are S.

In the SMILES, writing `N1CCOC[C@@H]1C` gives S at that carbon, and I used the same fragment for both morpholines.

SMILES: CNC(=O)c1cccc(c1)-c1ccc2c(n1)nc(nc2N1CCOC[C@@H]1C)N1CCOC[C@@H]1C
