The image shows three separate nucleoside 5'-monophosphates, so the SMILES is three dot-separated fragments. Each has a β-D-ribose ring with 2',3'-diol, a 5'-O-phosphate and a base on C1'.

1. **Top: uridine 5'-monophosphate (UMP).** The base is uracil, attached through N1, with C2=O, N3–H and C4=O.
2. **Bottom left: cytidine 5'-monophosphate (CMP).** The base is cytosine, attached through N1, with C2=O and a C4 amino group.
3. **Bottom right: inosine 5'-monophosphate (IMP).** The base is hypoxanthine, a purine attached through N9, with a C6=O and an N1–H.

For stereochemistry, all three sugars are drawn with the same wedge/hash pattern. I used the stereo pattern of the standard D-ribonucleotides (as in AMP), `[C@@H]…[C@H](COP…)[C@@H](O)[C@H]…O`, for each fragment. I matched it to the drawing from memory of that pattern rather than by tracing each wedge individually.
