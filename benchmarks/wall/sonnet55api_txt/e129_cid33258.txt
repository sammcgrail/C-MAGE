This is Allura Red AC, the disodium salt of an azo dye.

**Left ring (benzene):** The ring carbons are:
- C1, bearing the azo N
- C2, bearing OMe (the O at the bottom)
- C3, an aromatic CH
- C4, bearing SO3⁻
- C5, bearing CH3
- C6, an aromatic CH, between C5 and C1

**Right ring system (naphthalene):**
- The azo N is attached to a carbon (C1) that is adjacent to the C-OH carbon (C2).
- C2 is followed by C3 (CH) and C4 (CH), then the fusion carbon C4a.
- From C4a the ring continues to C5 (CH), which is bonded to C6.
- C6 carries SO3⁻, then C7 (CH) and C8 (CH), and then C8a.
- C8a is bonded to C1.

Reading the coordinates, the fusion atom at (1079,750) connects to CH (1084,626) and to CH (1185,818). The CH at 1185,818 connects to C(SO3) at (1180,943). That carbon connects to CH (1070,1000), then CH (963,934), then the fusion carbon (968,808), which bonds to C1 (862,743).
