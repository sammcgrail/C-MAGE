The drawing shows methaqualone, a quinazolin-4-one core, built up as follows.

- **Quinazolinone core:** a benzene ring is fused to a six-membered ring. That ring contains the C=O carbon, the N bearing the aryl group, a C(CH3)= carbon, and a ring N double-bonded to that carbon.
- **C2 substituent:** the carbon between the two nitrogens carries a methyl group and is double-bonded to the lower N.
- **N3 substituent:** the upper N carries a 2-methylphenyl (o-tolyl) group. The methyl sits on the ring carbon adjacent to the one attached to N.
