The drawing shows a benzene ring with two substituents on opposite carbons (para). One substituent is a methoxy group (a methyl carbon bonded to O, which is bonded to the ring). The other is a hydroxyl group (OH). This is 4-methoxyphenol.

The ring has three double bonds, which I checked against the drawing:
- the upper-left edge (from the OMe carbon to the carbon above it),
- the upper-right edge (from the top vertex down to the OH carbon),
- the bottom edge.
