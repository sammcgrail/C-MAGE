The drawing shows salmeterol. I read it region by region:

- **Left end:** a monosubstituted benzene ring, joined to a chain of 4 CH2 groups that ends at an ether oxygen (O).
- **Middle:** after the ether oxygen, a chain of 6 CH2 groups leads to an NH. From the NH, a CH2 connects to a CH bearing an OH.
- **Right end:** a benzene ring attached at the CH(OH) carbon. It carries an OH on the carbon para to that attachment, and a CH2OH on the carbon next to the OH-bearing one.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CH(OH) carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.
