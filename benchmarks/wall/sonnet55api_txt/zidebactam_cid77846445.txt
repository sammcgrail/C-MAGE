I read this as an avibactam-type diazabicyclooctane bearing a piperidine-3-carbonyl hydrazide side chain. The stereochemistry on the bicycle was not derived from the drawing. I assumed it matches avibactam, (2S,5R).

**Piperidine ring (left).** A six-membered ring with one NH and five carbons. The carbon next to the CH2–NH carbon carries a wedged bond to a C(=O) group. This is the only stereocentre I assigned from the wedge: from the geometry, the carbonyl-first neighbour order gives `@@`.

**Linker.** The wedged carbonyl is joined to –NH–NH–, which is joined to a second C(=O). This makes a diacyl hydrazide.

**Bicyclic core (right).**
- The second carbonyl is attached to a ring carbon (C2) on a wedged bond.
- C2 bonds to the ring nitrogen N1, and also to CH2–CH2, which leads to the bridgehead C5.
- C5 carries a wedged H and bonds to N6, which carries an O–SO3H (sulfate) group.
- A CH2 bridges C5 to N1.
- A urea-type C(=O) bridges N1 and N6.

SMILES: O=C([C@@H]1CCCNC1)NNC(=O)[C@H]1CC[C@@H]2CN1C(=O)N2OS(=O)(=O)O
