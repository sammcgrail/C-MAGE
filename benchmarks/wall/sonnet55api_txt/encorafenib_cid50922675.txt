I read the drawing in four pieces and joined them.

- **Left end:** a methyl carbamate (CH3–O–C(=O)–NH–) attached to a stereocentre. That carbon carries a methyl group on a wedge bond and a CH2 that leads to an NH.
- **Pyrimidine:** the NH connects to C2 of a pyrimidine, and C4 of the pyrimidine links to the pyrazole.
- **Pyrazole:** N1 bears an isopropyl group. C4 is bonded to the pyrimidine and C3 is bonded to the aryl ring.
- **Aryl ring:** it carries Cl, F and an NHSO2CH3 group. The F sits between the pyrazole-bearing carbon and the sulfonamide-bearing carbon.

For the stereocentre, the methyl wedge points toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away. Seen from the front, N (down), CH3 (upper left) and CH2 (upper right) run clockwise. Written as N[C@@H](C)C…, with neighbour order N, H, CH3, CH2, this corresponds to @@.
