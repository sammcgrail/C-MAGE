The drawing shows dimethyl sulfoxide with every hydrogen labelled as the ²H isotope, so it is DMSO-d6.

- Sulfur sits in the centre and is double-bonded to an oxygen below it.
- Two carbons are attached to the sulfur, one on each side.
- Each carbon carries three ²H atoms, six in total.

Written as SMILES, each deuterated methyl group is `[2H]C([2H])([2H])`, joined through `S(=O)`.
