This is my reading of the drawing, built region by region. It looks like a calicheamicin-type enediyne glycoside carrying an iodinated aryl thioester, an N–O glycosidic link and a methyl trisulfide. I derived each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes in the image, and I did not run the SMILES through a parser.

**Fragments, from top left:**
- **Amino sugar:** a tetrahydropyran with NHEt (wedge) and OMe (hash), attached through an anomeric O (wedge) to the next sugar.
- **Central sugar:** carries a 3-OH (hash), a 4-NH (wedge) and a 5-methyl (hash). The 4-NH is bonded to O, which links to the third sugar. Its anomeric O (hash) is bonded to the aglycone.
- **Third sugar:** a 2-deoxy sugar linked through O to the NH. It has a 3-OH (wedge), a 4-S (wedge) and a 5-methyl (hash).
- **Aryl thioester:** S–C(=O) on a benzene ring bearing, in order around the ring, two OMe groups, an O-sugar, an iodine and a methyl.
- **Fourth sugar:** a 6-deoxy sugar with two OH groups and one OMe, attached to the aryl oxygen.
- **Aglycone:**
  - a cyclohexene ring with an NHCO2Me group, a ketone and a tertiary OH (wedge);
  - a 10-membered ring containing C≡C–C=C–C≡C;
  - a quaternary carbon (no stereo drawn) bearing the S–S–S–CH3 chain;
  - a CH2–CH= unit that closes the six-membered ring.
