The drawing shows alanine: a central carbon bonded to NH2, a carboxylic acid (C(=O)OH) and a methyl group, with an implicit hydrogen. The methyl is on a hashed wedge, so it points away from the viewer, and the hydrogen points toward the viewer.

To assign the stereocentre, the CIP priorities are N > COOH > CH3 > H. Viewed from the front, N (down-right), COOH (left) and CH3 (up-right) run clockwise. Because the hydrogen faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S. This is L-alanine.
