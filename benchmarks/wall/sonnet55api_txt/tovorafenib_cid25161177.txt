I read the structure as three parts joined by two amide links.

- **Left ring:** a pyrimidine carrying the amide carbonyl on one carbon, a Cl on the next, and an NH2 on the one after. As a fragment this is c1ncnc(N)c1Cl, with the carbonyl attached at the first atom.
- **Centre:** a stereocentre bearing a hashed methyl, the amide NH, a thiazole C2 and an implicit H. The thiazole has its second carbon attached to the stereocentre and its fifth carbon attached to a second carbonyl.
- **Right ring:** a pyridine joined through the amide NH at its C2. It has CF3 at C4 and Cl at C5.

For the stereocentre, the hashed methyl points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Looking from the H side, methyl, N and thiazole run counterclockwise. That corresponds to `C[C@@H](N…)c` when the SMILES is written starting from the methyl.

SMILES: C[C@@H](NC(=O)c1ncnc(N)c1Cl)c1ncc(s1)C(=O)Nc1cc(C(F)(F)F)c(Cl)cn1
