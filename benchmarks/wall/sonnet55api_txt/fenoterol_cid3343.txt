This is fenoterol, drawn without stereochemistry.

**Left ring:** a benzene ring carrying two OH groups at the 3 and 5 positions. The ring carbon attached to the side chain sits between them.

**Chain:** from the left ring, a CH(OH) carbon, then a CH2, then NH, then a CH(CH3) carbon, then a CH2 that connects to the right ring.

**Right ring:** a benzene ring with an OH para to the CH2 attachment.

All bonds in the drawing are plain, so I have added no stereochemistry.
