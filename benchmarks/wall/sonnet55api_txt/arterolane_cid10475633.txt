Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left chain:** an H2N group on a quaternary carbon carrying two methyls, then a CH2 and an NH. This is the 2-amino-2-methylpropyl group on nitrogen.
- **Amide:** the NH is bonded to a C=O, which is followed by a CH2 attached to a cyclohexane ring.
- **Cyclohexane:** the ring carbon opposite the CH2 substituent (the 4-position) is a spiro carbon bonded to an oxygen and to the O–O unit.
- **Trioxolane:** the five-membered ring is spiro-C, O, O, O and the adamantane C2 carbon, so it contains an O–O bond and a third ring oxygen.
- **Adamantane:** the spiro carbon is the C2 position, bonded to two bridgehead CH carbons of a standard adamantane cage.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I included no stereochemistry.
