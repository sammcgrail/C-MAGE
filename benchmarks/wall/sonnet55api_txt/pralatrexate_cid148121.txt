I read the molecule as an L-glutamic acid amide of a para-substituted benzoic acid, with a 2,4-diaminopteridine at the far end.

- **Glutamate end:** The α-carbon carries a free carboxylic acid, an NH amide, and a CH2CH2COOH side chain. The side-chain bond is a wedge. With H pointing away, N → COOH → CH2 runs counterclockwise, so the centre is S, as in L-glutamic acid.
- **Linker:** The amide nitrogen is acylated by a C=O attached to a para-disubstituted benzene ring.
- **Para substituent:** The other ring position carries CH(CH2C≡CH)CH2–, a propargyl branch on a carbon that is a stereocentre by connectivity. It is drawn with plain bonds, so it gets no stereo label.
- **Pteridine:** The CH2 is bonded to C6 of a pteridine. C7 is CH, and the amino groups are at C2 and C4, giving the 2,4-diaminopteridin-6-yl group.

To write the stereocentre, I started from L-glutamic acid, N[C@@H](CCC(=O)O)C(=O)O. Starting instead from the side-chain CH2 swaps the order of the first and third neighbours, so the tag becomes @.
