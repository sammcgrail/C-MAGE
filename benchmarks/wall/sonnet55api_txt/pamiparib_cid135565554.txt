Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Pyrrolidine ring:** an N and three CH2 groups close onto a quaternary carbon. That carbon carries a hashed methyl.
- **Seven-membered ring:** the pyrrolidine N is attached to a CH2. This CH2 leads to a C=N, then to an NH, then to a C=O, and the C=O is bonded to the benzene ring. The ring closes through the indole carbons.
- **Indole core:** a fluorinated benzene ring is fused to a pyrrole ring that contains an NH. The quaternary carbon is bonded to the indole C2 position.
- **Stereochemistry:** the methyl is hashed, so it points away from the viewer. The other three neighbours of the stereocentre, in the order N, indole carbon, CH2, run clockwise when seen from the front. Seen from the methyl side, as SMILES reads them, that order is counterclockwise, which gives `@`.

The SMILES, with the stereocentre neighbours ordered methyl, N (ring closure 1), indole carbon (ring closure 2), CH2, is:

SMILES: C[C@]12CCCN1CC1=NNC(=O)c3cc(F)cc4[nH]c2c1c34
