The drawing shows a benzene ring fused to a six-membered ring that contains two nitrogen atoms. This is quinoxaline.

- **Fused ring system:** The left ring is a benzene ring with six carbons. Two of them (at about x=750, y=555 and y=943) are shared with the right ring.
- **Right ring:** Going around it, the atoms are the shared carbon at the top, N (top), CH, CH, N (bottom), and the shared carbon at the bottom. That makes two nitrogens at positions 1 and 4, so the ring is a pyrazine.
- **Bond orders:** The top N=C bond is double, and the bottom N=C bond is double. The CH–CH bond between them is single. The benzene ring has its own alternating double bonds.
