The drawing shows a cyclopentene ring carrying two substituents on the ring double bond. One is a carboxylic acid (C(=O)O). The other is an amide carbonyl, C(=O)N, whose nitrogen is attached to an aromatic ring.

That aromatic ring has a fluorine ortho to the nitrogen. Para to the nitrogen, it is bonded to a second phenyl ring that carries a methoxy group at the meta position.

Ring positions on the fluoro ring, reading from the drawing:
- C1 is bonded to N (at about 848,787).
- C2 is bonded to F (at about 758,872).
- C3 is the CH at about 640,836.
- C4 is bonded to the aryl group (at about 612,714).
- C5 is the CH at about 700,631.
- C6 is the CH at about 820,666.

The aryl group is therefore para to N and meta to F.

The methoxyphenyl ring is attached at C(491,681). Going round the ring from that carbon, the next carbons are (402,765), then (283,731), which bears the O, and then (257,609). The O-bearing carbon is therefore meta to the attachment point.
