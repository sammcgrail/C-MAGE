I read the drawing as meropenem, the carbapenem antibiotic. Its structural parts are:

- a carbapenem core, a β-lactam fused to a five-membered ring containing a C=C
- a carboxylic acid on the alkene carbon next to the ring nitrogen
- a methyl group on the ring carbon next to the alkene
- a 1-hydroxyethyl side chain on the carbon next to the lactam carbonyl
- a thioether linking the alkene carbon to a pyrrolidine ring
- a dimethylamide (C(=O)N(CH3)2) on the pyrrolidine

I did not check every wedge and hash against the picture. Instead I assigned CIP descriptors to the stereocentres in the SMILES below and compared them with the known meropenem configuration, (4R,5S,6S) with 8R on the side chain and (3S,5S) on the pyrrolidine. They match.
