This is enzalutamide, which I identified from the drawing and checked region by region.

- **Left ring:** a benzene bearing a CF3 group and a nitrile on adjacent carbons, with its third substituent (the ring carbon bonded to N) para to the nitrile. Starting at the nitrile carbon (bottom left), the ring runs: the carbon bearing C#N, then the carbon bearing CF3, then a CH, then the carbon bearing N, then two more CH carbons. The N-bearing carbon is two atoms from the CF3 carbon (meta), and the nitrile carbon is para to the N-bearing carbon. This matches the 4-cyano-3-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl group.
- **Central ring:** a thiohydantoin. N1 is attached to the left aryl, then C(=S), then N3 attached to the right aryl, then C(CH3)2, then C(=O) back to N1.
- **Right ring:** a benzene with the N attached at one carbon. Going around from that carbon: a CH, a carbon bearing F, a carbon bearing C(=O)NHCH3, then two CH carbons. The F carbon is therefore ortho to the amide carbon and meta to the N-bearing carbon. This is the 2-fluoro-4-N-substituted benzamide.
