- a 4-en-3-one in ring A (C=C conjugated with a ketone)
- a methyl on the A/B ring fusion carbon (C10) and another on C13, both drawn as wedges
- a hydroxyl on C11, drawn as a wedge
- a –C(=O)CH2OH side chain on C17, drawn as a wedge
- ring-fusion hydrogens: C8 wedge (up), C9 hashed (down) and C14 hashed (down)
