The drawing shows a 2-amino-4,5-dihydrooxazole (2-oxazoline) ring. The ring atoms are O, a CH2, a second CH2, N, and the C2 carbon, which is double-bonded to N. C2 also carries an NH substituent.

The NH is attached to a CH that bears two cyclopropyl groups.

Writing this as SMILES gives C1COC(=N1)NC(C2CC2)C3CC3. The CH carries two identical cyclopropyl groups, so it is not a stereocentre.
