Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Alkyne end:** a terminal C≡C attached to a CH2, which is attached to the nitrogen (a propargyl group).
- **Nitrogen:** a tertiary amine carrying that propargyl group, a methyl group, and the stereocentre carbon.
- **Stereocentre:** it bears the N, a wedged methyl, a CH2 leading to the phenyl ring, and an implicit H.
- **Aromatic ring:** a benzene ring with three alternating double bonds, attached through the CH2.

This is selegiline, N-methyl-N-propargyl-1-phenylpropan-2-amine.

For the stereochemistry, the wedged methyl points toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away. The priorities are N > CH2Ph > CH3 > H. Going N (up), then CH2 (right), then CH3 (lower left) is clockwise with H at the back, so the centre is R.

In the SMILES `C#CCN(C)[C@H](C)Cc1ccccc1`, the neighbour order at the stereocentre is N, H, CH3, CH2. The `@` tag means that, viewed from N, the sequence H → CH3 → CH2 runs counterclockwise. Working this through gives R, which matches the drawing.
