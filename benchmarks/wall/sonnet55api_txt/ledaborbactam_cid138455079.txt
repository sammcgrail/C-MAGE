Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Left side chain:** a CH3–CH2–C(=O)–NH group, so a propanamide attached through N.
- **Stereocentre:** the N is bonded to a ring carbon by a wedge bond. That carbon also bears a CH2 and a boron, with an implicit H.
- **Ring containing boron:** the stereocentre carbon connects to CH2, which connects to an aromatic carbon. The neighbouring aromatic carbon bears an O, which links to B, and B closes back to the stereocentre. B also carries an OH. This gives a six-membered oxaborinine ring fused to the benzene.
- **Benzene ring:** it has six carbons. Two are fused to the oxaborinine ring, and the one next to the O-bearing carbon carries a COOH group.

**Stereo assignment:** the H on the stereocentre is implicit and points away from the viewer. Seen from the front, the neighbours N (wedge, lower left), CH2 (up) and B (lower right) run clockwise. In the SMILES `CCC(=O)N[C@H]1Cc2cccc(C(=O)O)c2OB1O`, the neighbour order at that carbon is N, H, ring-closure B, CH2. That order corresponds to `@`.
