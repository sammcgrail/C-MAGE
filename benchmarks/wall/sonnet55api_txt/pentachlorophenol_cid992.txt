The drawing shows a benzene ring with a hydroxyl group on one carbon and a chlorine on each of the other five carbons. That makes it pentachlorophenol.

Ring atoms, read from the drawing:
- The carbon at the right (about 1020, 748) carries OH.
- The upper right carbon (875, 498) carries Cl.
- The upper left carbon (587, 498) carries Cl.
- The left carbon (441, 748) carries Cl.
- The lower left carbon (587, 1000) carries Cl.
- The lower right carbon (875, 1000) carries Cl.

The ring has alternating double bonds (a Kekulé structure), so I wrote it that way.

SMILES: Oc1c(Cl)c(Cl)c(Cl)c(Cl)c1Cl
