I read the molecule as a 2-methylphenoxyacetic acid carrying a thioether at the 4-position of the ring.

- **Left end:** a carboxylic acid (HO–C=O) attached to a CH2, which is bonded to an aryl ether oxygen.
- **First ring:** a benzene ring with the ether oxygen at one position and a methyl group on the adjacent carbon. A sulfur is on the carbon para to the oxygen.
- **Linker:** S–CH2–CH(OEt)–CH2–O, where the ethoxy group (O–CH2–CH3) hangs off the middle carbon.
- **Right end:** the linker's terminal oxygen is bonded to a second benzene ring, which carries a CF3 group para to that oxygen.

**Stereochemistry:** the only wedge is on the CH(OEt) carbon, on its bond to the CH2–OAr group.
- CIP priorities at that carbon are OEt > CH2S > CH2OAr > H.
- The wedged bond points toward the viewer, so the implicit hydrogen points away.
- Viewed from the front, OEt (bottom) → CH2S (upper left) → CH2OAr (upper right) runs clockwise, so the centre is R.
- In the SMILES below, written with the neighbour order CH2S, H, OEt, CH2OAr, `[C@H]` encodes R.
