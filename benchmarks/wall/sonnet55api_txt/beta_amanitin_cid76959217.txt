I read this as a bicyclic amatoxin-type peptide, β-amanitin-like. The residues are Ile, Gly, Cys (as a sulfoxide), Asp, 4-hydroxy-Pro, a dihydroxy-Ile and a 6-hydroxy-Trp. The Trp indole C2 is bonded to the Cys sulfur, which forms the second ring.

I traced the connectivity around the macrocycle as follows:

1. Ile Cα carries an NH that leads to Gly, then Trp.
2. Ile C=O leads to Gly, then Cys NH.
3. Cys C=O leads to Asp NH.
4. Asp C=O is bonded to the Pro nitrogen.
5. Pro C=O leads to the dihydroxy-Ile NH.
6. Dihydroxy-Ile C=O leads to the Trp NH.

I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash. The Ile, Cys, Trp and Asp centres give the L-amino-acid configurations expected for the natural residues.

The sulfoxide sulfur is also a stereocentre, but I left it unspecified because its wedge is hard to interpret from the drawing alone.
