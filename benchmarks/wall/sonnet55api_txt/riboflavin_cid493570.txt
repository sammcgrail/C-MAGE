The drawing shows riboflavin (vitamin B2): a dimethyl-substituted isoalloxazine ring system with a ribityl side chain on the ring nitrogen.

**Ring system:**
- The benzene ring carries two methyl groups.
- It is fused to a central ring containing two nitrogens.
- That ring is fused to a pyrimidinedione ring with two C=O groups and an N–H between them.
- The central-ring nitrogen on the right carries the side chain.

**Side chain:** The chain is N–CH2–CH(OH)–CH(OH)–CH(OH)–CH2OH. The three stereocentres are drawn with a bold wedge, a hashed bond and a bold wedge, in that order from the ring. Working out each centre from the wedge or hash and the positions of the neighbouring atoms gives [C@H], [C@H], [C@H] when the chain is written starting from the CH2 on nitrogen. This matches natural D-ribityl riboflavin.
