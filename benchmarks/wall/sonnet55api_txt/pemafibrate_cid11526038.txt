I read the structure as a 1,3-benzoxazole whose C2 carries a tertiary amine nitrogen, an N-linked 3-(4-methoxyphenoxy)propyl chain and an N-benzyl group. The benzyl ring is meta-substituted with an ether oxygen. That oxygen is attached to the CH of a butanoic acid, and the ethyl group on that CH is drawn on a hashed bond.

Stereochemistry: the only drawn stereocentre is the CH bearing the ether O, the COOH and the hashed ethyl. The priorities are O > COOH > ethyl > H. The ethyl is behind the plane, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. In the drawing, O → COOH → ethyl runs counterclockwise. Because H faces the viewer, I reverse this to clockwise, which gives R. Written as `CC[C@@H](O...)C(=O)O`, the `@@` marks this centre as R.
