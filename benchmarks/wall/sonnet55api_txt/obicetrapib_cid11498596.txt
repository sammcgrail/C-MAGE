I read the molecule as a 1,2,3,4-tetrahydroquinoline core, with these substituents:

- **N1:** an ethyl carbamate, N–C(=O)OCH2CH3.
- **C2:** an ethyl group on a hashed bond.
- **C4:** a tertiary amine nitrogen on a hashed bond. That nitrogen carries a 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)benzyl group and a pyrimidin-2-yl group.
- **Benzo ring:** a CF3 group at C6.
- **Pyrimidine:** an O–CH2CH2CH2–COOH chain at C5.

For the two stereocentres, I treated the hashed substituent as pointing away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Reading the remaining three neighbours around each centre from the H side gave clockwise at both C2 and C4. Writing the neighbours in SMILES order gives [C@H] for both centres.
