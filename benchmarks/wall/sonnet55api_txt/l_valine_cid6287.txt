The drawing shows valine: an isopropyl group on the alpha carbon, which carries an NH2 and a carboxylic acid (C=O and OH).

Stereochemistry: the only stereo marking is the hashed bond from the alpha carbon to NH2, so the nitrogen points away from the viewer and the implicit hydrogen points toward the viewer.

- CIP priorities: N > COOH > CH(CH3)2 > H.
- In the drawing, N (up), then COOH (lower right), then isopropyl (lower left) runs clockwise.
- The hydrogen faces the viewer, so the true sense is reversed, giving counterclockwise, which is S.

This is L-valine, written as CC(C)[C@H](N)C(=O)O.
