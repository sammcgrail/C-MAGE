**Imidazo[2,1-b]benzothiazole.** The phenyl ring attaches at C2 of the imidazole ring. That imidazole has a CH, then an N that is part of the fused system, then a C bonded to S, and an N= closing back to C2. The benzo ring is fused through the N and the S. The aromatic carbon bonded to N is the ring-fusion carbon, and the carbon bonded to S is the other fusion carbon.
