The drawing is cimetidine, so I read it region by region and wrote the SMILES from that.

- **Imidazole ring:** N (top left) is bonded to CH, then to NH (bottom left), then to a carbon bearing the methyl group. That carbon is double-bonded to the carbon carrying the CH2 chain, which closes back to the first N. The ring is c1nc(CS...)c(C)[nH]1.
- **Chain:** the ring carbon bears CH2–S–CH2–CH2–NH.
- **Guanidine end:** that NH is attached to a central carbon, which is double-bonded to N–CH3 and single-bonded to NH–C≡N.

Reading the SMILES in order: CC1=C(CSCCNC(=NC)NC#N)N=CN1, which is the same as Cc1[nH]cnc1CSCCNC(=NC)NC#N.
