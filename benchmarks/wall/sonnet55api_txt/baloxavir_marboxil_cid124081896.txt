I read the structure as an isomer of baloxavir marboxil, with the sulfur and the CH2 swapped between the two benzo rings.

- **Side chain:** a methyl carbonate on an O–CH2–O link, COC(=O)OCO–, attached to a pyridone ring carbon.
- **Pyridone core:** a pyridone ring carrying a C=O, fused through N–N to a triazinone ring. That ring carries a second C=O and is fused to a morpholine ring through a stereocentre.
- **Morpholine stereocentre:** the H is drawn as a wedge. Working through the neighbour order gives `[C@H]` in my SMILES.
- **Tricyclic part:** the dibenzo-thiepine CH is attached to the ring N by a hashed bond, which gives `[C@@H]`. The CH sits between the two benzo rings.
- **Sulfur and CH2:** the unsubstituted benzo ring carries the S. The 3,4-difluorobenzo ring carries the CH2.

SMILES: COC(=O)OCOC1=C2C(=O)N3CCOC[C@H]3N(N2C=CC1=O)[C@@H]1c2ccccc2SCc2c(F)c(F)ccc12
