The drawing shows a five-membered succinimide ring with an N-methyl group, two carbonyl oxygens, and a phenyl group on the ring carbon next to one carbonyl. This is N-methyl-2-phenylsuccinimide (3-phenyl-1-methylpyrrolidine-2,5-dione).

Reading the ring atoms:
- The nitrogen carries a methyl group (the line going up-left).
- One carbonyl carbon (top) is double-bonded to O and bonded to N and to the CH that carries the phenyl group.
- The other carbonyl carbon (bottom left) is double-bonded to O and bonded to N and to the CH2.
- The CH2 connects to the CH bearing the phenyl group, closing the five-membered ring.
- The phenyl ring is drawn as a benzene with three double bonds.

All bonds at the CH carbon are plain lines, so no stereochemistry is given.
