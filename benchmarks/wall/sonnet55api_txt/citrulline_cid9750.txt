The drawing shows citrulline: a HOOC–CH(NH2)–CH2–CH2–CH2–NH–C(=O)–NH2 chain, with a ureido group at the end of the side chain.

For stereochemistry, the only stereocentre is the alpha carbon, which carries a wedged NH2. Viewed from the front, with the implicit H pointing back, the sequence NH2 (up), then COOH (lower left), then the CH2 chain (lower right) runs counterclockwise. That gives S, the L-configuration, which matches `N[C@@H](R)C(=O)O` as written for L-amino acids.
