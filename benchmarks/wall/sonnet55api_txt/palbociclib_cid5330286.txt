This structure is palbociclib.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- Acetyl group: CH3-C(=O)- attached to the ring carbon at the lower left.
- Pyridone ring: the carbon bearing the acetyl is double-bonded to the carbon bearing the methyl. The next carbon is C(=O), which connects to N, and that N carries the cyclopentyl group.
- Pyrimidine ring: it is fused to the pyridone. The shared carbons are C4a (attached to the methyl-bearing carbon) and C8a (attached to the ring N). The remaining ring atoms are N=C(NH-aryl)-N=C8a, plus CH=N back to C4a.
- Aryl substituent: the NH links to a pyridine whose carbons run C2 (NH), C3H, C4H and C5 (bearing the piperazine), then C6H, then N back to C2.
- Piperazine: attached through N1, with an NH at the far end.
