I read the molecule as a 7H-pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidine whose 4-position carries an NH link to C3 of a piperidine. The piperidine nitrogen is acylated with an acryloyl group (C=CC(=O)), and C2 of the piperidine bears a methyl group.

**Stereochemistry.** Two wedges are drawn.
- **C3 (carries the NH):** the wedge bond to NH puts the hydrogen at the back. The sequence NH → ring CH2 next to N → ring CH2 on the other side runs clockwise, so this centre is R.
- **C2 (carries the methyl):** the wedge bond to the methyl puts the hydrogen at the back. The sequence N → ring CH2 → CH3 runs counterclockwise, so this centre is S.

I then matched these to the SMILES chirality marks: `[C@H]` at C3 gives R and `[C@@H]` at C2 gives S.

SMILES: C=CC(=O)N1C[C@H](Nc2ncnc3[nH]ccc23)CC[C@@H]1C
