I read this as pentazocine, a benzomorphan with an N-prenyl group and a phenol.

**Skeleton**
- The chain on the nitrogen is a prenyl group: (CH3)2C=CH–CH2–N.
- The nitrogen sits in a bridged bicyclic core. It is bonded to the prenyl CH2, a CH2CH2 arm that leads to the quaternary carbon, and the stereocentre CH at the benzylic CH2.
- The quaternary carbon carries a methyl (wedge) and is bonded to the aromatic ring.
- The aromatic ring has a para-type OH, and its other ring carbon bears the benzylic CH2.
- The bridging CH next to the quaternary carbon carries a methyl and a hashed H.

**Stereochemistry**
Three centres carry wedges or hashes: the quaternary carbon (methyl wedge), the CH bonded to N (H wedge), and the CH bearing the methyl (H hash). I assigned each @/@@ by working out the neighbour order from the drawn coordinates, and got @@ for all three.
