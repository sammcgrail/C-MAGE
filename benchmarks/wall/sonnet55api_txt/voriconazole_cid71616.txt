This is voriconazole, which I read as follows.

- **Left ring:** a benzene ring with fluorines at the 2- and 4-positions relative to the carbon bearing the side chain.
- **Central carbon:** a quaternary carbon carrying an OH on a hashed bond, the difluorophenyl ring, and a CH2 linked to the N1 of a 1,2,4-triazole.
- **Neighbouring carbon:** a CH bearing a wedged methyl and a 5-fluoropyrimidin-4-yl ring.

Working out the stereocentres from the wedge and hash bonds:

- **CH carbon:** the CIP priorities are C(OH) > pyrimidinyl > CH3 > H. With H pointing away, the sequence runs counterclockwise, so this centre is S.
- **Carbinol carbon:** the priorities are OH > CH2N > aryl > CH. This gives R.

The result is the (2R,3S) configuration, which matches voriconazole.
