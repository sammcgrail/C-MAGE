Reading the drawing, this is valsartan.

- **Acyl chain:** a pentanoyl (valeryl) group, CCCCC(=O), is attached to the amide nitrogen.
- **Biphenyl:** the nitrogen carries a CH2 linked to a para-substituted benzene, which is joined to an ortho-substituted benzene.
- **Tetrazole:** the ortho carbon bears a tetrazole. The ring carbon is bonded to two ring nitrogens, and the NH sits between the other two nitrogens, so the ring is the 2H-tautomer, written c1nn[nH]n1.
- **Stereocentre:** the carbon bearing the nitrogen, the carboxylic acid and the isopropyl group has a hashed bond to the isopropyl carbon. With the isopropyl group pointing away and the hydrogen toward the viewer, the N → COOH → iPr sequence runs clockwise on the page, which reverses to counterclockwise for the true view. This gives the S configuration, written [C@@H] when the nitrogen is the preceding atom.
