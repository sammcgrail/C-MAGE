The drawing shows homocysteine: a carboxylic acid (C(=O)OH), a stereocentre bearing NH2 on a hashed bond, then two CH2 groups ending in an SH group.

**Stereochemistry:** The stereocentre has NH2 on a hashed bond (pointing away from the viewer), so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > COOH > CH2CH2SH > H. Reading N (bottom), then COOH (upper left), then CH2 (upper right) runs clockwise. Because H faces the viewer, the true sense is reversed, so the centre is S. This is L-homocysteine.

The SMILES `N[C@@H](CCS)C(=O)O` has the same chirality pattern as L-alanine, `N[C@@H](C)C(=O)O`, which is also S. It therefore matches the drawing.
