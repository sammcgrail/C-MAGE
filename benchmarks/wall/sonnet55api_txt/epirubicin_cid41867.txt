I read this as an anthracycline: a tetracyclic aglycone carrying a 2-hydroxyacetyl group, joined through an O-glycoside to an amino sugar. I assigned the stereocentres from the wedges and hashes at the sugar ring, the glycosidic C–O carbon and the tertiary carbinol.

**Aglycone**
- The anthraquinone core has two ring-fused carbonyls, two phenolic OH groups and one OMe group.
- The OMe sits on the benzo ring carbon next to the lower carbonyl.
- The saturated ring has a CH2–CH2 arrangement flanking the quaternary carbon C(OH)(C(=O)CH2OH). The OH on that carbon is drawn with a wedge.
- The adjacent ring carbon bears the glycosidic O, also drawn with a wedge.

**Sugar**
- It is a six-membered ring containing one ring O, with a CH3 on a wedge, a hashed OH, a wedged NH2, a CH2, and a hashed glycosidic O at the anomeric carbon.
