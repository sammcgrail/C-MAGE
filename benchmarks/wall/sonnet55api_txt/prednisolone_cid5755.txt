The drawing shows a pregna-1,4-diene-3,20-dione skeleton, which is prednisolone. It has these features:
- ring A: a cross-conjugated dienone (C=C–C(=O)–C=C),
- two angular methyl groups,
- an 11-hydroxyl,
- a 17-hydroxyl and a 17-side chain of C(=O)CH2OH.

The ring system is drawn mirrored left-to-right relative to the usual steroid orientation. The hashed methyls and the other wedge/hash marks are therefore consistent with the natural stereochemistry. I wrote the SMILES from the standard prednisolone stereochemistry rather than deriving each centre separately from the wedges.
