This is phosphocreatine. Reading the drawing region by region:

- A methyl group is attached to a nitrogen.
- That nitrogen carries a CH2 that leads to a carboxylic acid, drawn as C(=O)OH.
- The same nitrogen is bonded to a central carbon, which bears an NH2 group and a double bond to a second nitrogen (C=N).
- That second nitrogen is bonded to phosphorus, which carries one P=O and two OH groups.

SMILES: CN(CC(=O)O)C(N)=NP(=O)(O)O
