**Central carbon.** It is double-bonded to a ring carbon of the upper quinoid ring and single-bonded to two aryl groups: a para-phenylene bearing NH-C6H4-OEt, and a tolyl ring bearing the amine.

**Quinoid ring (top).** Starting from the carbon double-bonded to the central carbon, the ring runs C(=N+) … as follows:
- C1 is attached to the central carbon (C1=C central).
- C2 carries the methyl group (the "C(C)" at the left of the ring).
- C3 is CH, double-bonded to C2.
- C4 is C(=N+).
