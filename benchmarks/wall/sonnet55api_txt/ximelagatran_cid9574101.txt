- **Ethyl ester:** CH3CH2–O–C(=O)–CH2– on the left.
- **Secondary amine:** the –NH– attached to a stereocentre that carries a cyclohexyl ring and a C(=O).
- **Azetidine amide:** that C(=O) is bonded to the nitrogen of a four-membered azetidine ring. The ring carbon next to the nitrogen bears a C(=O)NH–CH2– group.

**Stereochemistry:**

- **Cyclohexyl-bearing carbon:** the N–C bond is hashed. The priorities are N > C(=O) > cyclohexyl > H. Tracing N → C=O → cyclohexyl on the page gives a counterclockwise path. The hydrogen points toward the viewer, so the true sense is reversed and the centre is R.
- **Azetidine C2:** the bond to the amide C(=O) is hashed. The priorities are N > C(=O) > ring CH2 > H. The path N → C=O → CH2 is clockwise on the page. The hydrogen again points toward the viewer, so the centre is S.
