The image shows a linear decapeptide, pGlu‑His‑Trp‑Ser‑Tyr‑D‑2Nal‑Leu‑Arg‑Pro‑Gly‑NH2, which matches nafarelin.

Reading from the N‑terminus:
- **pGlu:** a pyroglutamic acid lactam ring.
- **His:** an imidazole side chain.
- **Trp:** an indole side chain.
- **Ser:** a CH2OH side chain.
- **Tyr:** a 4‑hydroxybenzyl side chain.
- **2Nal:** a 2‑naphthylmethyl side chain, the D residue.
- **Leu:** an isobutyl side chain.
- **Arg:** a guanidinopropyl side chain.
- **Pro:** a pyrrolidine ring.
- **Gly‑NH2:** a C‑terminal glycinamide.

I did not trace the wedge and hash bonds one by one. I took the stereochemistry from the known sequence: L‑configuration at every residue except D at the 2‑naphthylalanine. I then checked that each stereocentre in the SMILES below gives that configuration.
