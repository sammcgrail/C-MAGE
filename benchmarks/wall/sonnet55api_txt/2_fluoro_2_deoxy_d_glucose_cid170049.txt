I read the molecule as a straight-chain aldehyde: O=C–CH(F)–CH(OH)–CH(OH)–CH(OH)–CH2OH. That is a 2-fluoro-2-deoxy pentose (five carbons plus the aldehyde carbon's oxygen counted in the chain). The aldehyde carbon is C1 and the CH2OH carbon is C6 in the drawing's numbering.

For stereochemistry I took each stereocentre's hashed or wedged substituent, placed the implicit H opposite it, and converted the clockwise or counterclockwise sense into @/@@ for the SMILES neighbour order.

- C2 has F on a hash, which gives `[C@H]`.
- C3 has OH on a hash, which gives `[C@@H]`.
- C4 has OH on a hash, which gives `[C@H]`.
- C5 has OH on a wedge, which gives `[C@H]`.

SMILES: O=C[C@H](F)[C@@H](O)[C@H](O)[C@H](O)CO
