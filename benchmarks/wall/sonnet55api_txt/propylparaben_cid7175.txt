The drawing shows propyl 4-hydroxybenzoate (propylparaben).

- **Propyl chain:** three carbons running from the left end to the ester oxygen.
- **Ester group:** the oxygen attached to the chain connects to a carbonyl carbon, which carries a C=O oxygen.
- **Ring:** a benzene ring is attached to the carbonyl carbon. An OH group sits on the carbon opposite that attachment point, so the ring is para-substituted.
