The bornane core is a bicyclo[2.2.1]heptane with a bridgehead methyl and a gem-dimethyl bridge carbon. It also carries a hydroxyl on one carbon and the urea nitrogen on the adjacent carbon.

Stereochemistry follows the drawn wedges and hashes:
- The bridgehead methyl is hashed (pointing away from the viewer).
- The hydroxyl is wedged (toward the viewer).
- The urea NH is wedged.
- The other bridgehead H is hashed.

I assigned each centre from the 2D coordinates, so the @/@@ marks are worked out by hand and not checked with software.
