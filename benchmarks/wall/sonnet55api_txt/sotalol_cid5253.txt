The drawing shows sotalol. Reading it region by region:

- **Left end:** an isopropyl group (two methyls on one CH) attached to an NH.
- **Linker:** the NH connects to a CH2, then to a CH bearing an OH, which attaches to the benzene ring.
- **Ring:** a para-substituted benzene.
- **Right end:** the carbon opposite the linker carries NH–S(=O)(=O)–CH3, a methanesulfonamide.

All bonds in the drawing are plain, with no wedges or hashes, so the CH(OH) carbon gets no stereo marking.

SMILES: CC(C)NCC(O)c1ccc(NS(C)(=O)=O)cc1
