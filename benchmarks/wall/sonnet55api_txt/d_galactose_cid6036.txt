I read the drawing as a pyranose (six-membered ring with one ring oxygen) carrying a CH2OH group and four OH groups.

**Ring and substituents**
- The ring atoms are C1, O, C5, C4, C3 and C2.
- C1 (top) carries an OH drawn with a plain bond, so it gets no stereo label.
- C5 carries the CH2OH on a hashed bond.
- C4 and C3 each carry an OH on a hashed bond.
- C2 carries an OH on a wedge bond.

**Stereo assignment**
