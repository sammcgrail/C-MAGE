The structure is sunitinib, which I read as follows:

- **Left side:** a diethylamino group (N with two ethyls) joined by a two-carbon chain to an amide NH, which is attached to a C=O.
- **Pyrrole ring:** the amide C=O sits on a 2,4-dimethylpyrrole (an NH ring with a methyl on each of two carbons).
- **Linker:** the pyrrole carbon next to the NH carries a CH= that forms an exocyclic double bond to C3 of the oxindole.
- **Oxindole:** a lactam (NH–C=O) fused to a benzene ring carrying one fluorine.
- **Double-bond geometry:** the pyrrole carbon on the CH and the lactam C=O carbon on the oxindole C3 lie on the same side of the C=C. That is the Z isomer, so I encoded it with `/` and `\` marks.
