This is granisetron hydrochloride: a 9-methyl-9-azabicyclo[3.3.1]nonane core carrying an amide NH at C3, linked to a 1-methylindazole-3-carbonyl group, with HCl as a separate component.

**Bicyclic amine.** The two bridgehead carbons are joined by the N-methyl nitrogen, a three-carbon bridge on the left, and a CH2–CH(NH)–CH2 bridge on the right. The upper bridgehead has a hashed H and the lower bridgehead has a wedged H, so only these two carbons carry stereo marks. The CH(NH) carbon is drawn with plain bonds and gets no stereo mark.

**Amide and indazole.** The NH is acylated by a C=O whose carbon sits on C3 of the indazole. The indazole N1 carries a methyl group and N2 is the other ring nitrogen, and the benzene ring is fused on the far side.

**Stereo assignment.** I wrote the SMILES with the N-bound bridgehead first and worked out each centre from the 2D geometry and the wedge or hash. Both centres come out as `@` in the string below.
