This is rufinamide: a 1,2,3-triazole carrying a carboxamide, with an N-benzyl group bearing two ortho fluorines.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- **Benzene ring:** it has fluorines on the two carbons flanking the carbon that bears the CH2 group, so the ring is 2,6-difluorophenyl.
- **Linker:** a single CH2 joins the ring to N1 of the triazole.
- **Triazole:** N1 (attached to the CH2) is bonded to a ring CH and to N2. N2 is double-bonded to N3. N3 is bonded to C4, which carries the amide, and C4 is double-bonded to the CH (C5), which closes back to N1.
- **Amide:** C4 bears C(=O)NH2.

Writing this as SMILES: NC(=O)c1cn(Cc2c(F)cccc2F)nn1

Atom check: the ring c1 (C4) – c (C5) – n (N1, with the benzyl) – n (N2) – n1 (N3) closes back to C4. This matches the drawing.
