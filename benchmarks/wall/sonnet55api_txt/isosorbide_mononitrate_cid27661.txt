I read this as isosorbide mononitrate: a fused bicyclic bis-tetrahydrofuran core carrying one nitrate ester and one hydroxyl.

**Connectivity**
- The nitrate group is O–N⁺(=O)O⁻, attached through its single-bonded oxygen to a ring carbon.
- That carbon (call it A) is bonded to a CH₂, which is bonded to a ring oxygen. The oxygen leads to a ring-fusion carbon (B).
- B is bonded to the second ring-fusion carbon (D) and to a carbon bearing OH (C).
- C is bonded to a CH₂, which is bonded to the second ring oxygen. That oxygen closes back onto D.
- D is also bonded to A, which closes the second five-membered ring.

**Stereochemistry**
- A has a hashed bond to the nitrate oxygen, so its implicit H points toward the viewer.
- B has a wedged H, C has a wedged OH, and D has a wedged H.
- I assigned the @/@@ symbols by working out, for each centre, the rotation of its neighbours as drawn. For the written neighbour order, A and B came out @@, C @@, and D @.

Ring-closure check: A opens ring 1 and D closes rings 1 and 2, with B opening ring 2, so the SMILES describes the drawing atom for atom.

SMILES: [O-][N+](=O)O[C@@H]1CO[C@@H]2[C@@H](O)CO[C@H]12
