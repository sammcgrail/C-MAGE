The drawing shows a dimethylamino group (N with two methyls) attached to a two-carbon chain (N–CH2–CH2), which connects to an ether oxygen. The oxygen is bonded to a CH carbon that carries two phenyl rings. This is diphenhydramine.

Atom by atom:
- N is bonded to two methyl carbons and one CH2.
- That CH2 is bonded to a second CH2, which is bonded to O.
- O is bonded to the CH, which bears the two phenyl rings.
- Each ring is a six-membered benzene ring with alternating double bonds drawn.
- The CH is not stereogenic, because its two substituents are identical phenyls, so no stereochemistry is needed.
