The drawing shows the penicillin-type bicyclic core of mecillinam (6-amidino-penicillanic acid type). I read it as follows:

- **Core:** a β-lactam fused to a thiazolidine ring. The β-lactam carbonyl is C=O, with N bonded to the ring-fusion carbon (drawn with a wedged H) and to the carbon bearing COOH. S is bonded to the ring-fusion carbon and to the gem-dimethyl carbon.
- **Substituents on the thiazolidine ring:** two methyls on the carbon next to S, and a wedged carboxylic acid (C(=O)OH) on the carbon next to N.
- **Side chain:** the hashed bond on the β-lactam carbon goes to N=CH–N, where that last N is part of a saturated seven-membered ring (azepane, six CH2 groups plus N).
- **Stereochemistry:** only the three stereocentres on the bicyclic core are marked (wedge, wedge-H, hash). I took their configuration from the natural penicillin core (2S,5R,6R), which I wrote from memory of the standard penicillin SMILES rather than deriving it from the wedges. The C=N double bond has no cis/trans geometry drawn, so I left it unspecified.
