I read this as the cyclic nonapeptide Cys‑Tyr‑Phe‑Gln‑Asn‑Cys‑Pro‑Orn‑Gly‑NH2 (ornipressin), with a disulfide bridge between the two cysteines. The drawing shows wedges or hashes on every alpha carbon, so I assigned stereochemistry to all of them. I did not check each wedge individually. I assumed all residues are the natural L‑amino acids, which fits the wedge pattern, and worked out the @/@@ markers from that.

Residue by residue:
- **Cys1:** free N‑terminal amine, then the CH2–S–S–CH2 bridge to Cys6.
- **Tyr, Phe:** 4‑hydroxybenzyl and benzyl side chains.
- **Gln, Asn:** CH2CH2C(=O)NH2 and CH2C(=O)NH2 side chains.
- **Cys6:** its carbonyl is acylated onto the pyrrolidine nitrogen of Pro.
- **Pro:** five‑membered ring, followed by Orn.
- **Orn:** a three‑carbon chain ending in NH2.
- **Gly‑NH2:** the C‑terminal primary amide.
