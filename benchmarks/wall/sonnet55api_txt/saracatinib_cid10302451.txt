This is saracatinib (AZD0530), and I read the structure region by region.

- **Quinazoline core:** N1 and N3 sit at the bottom, with C2 between them. C4 carries the NH. The benzo ring has the tetrahydropyranyloxy group at C5 and the OCH2CH2-(4-methylpiperazine) chain at C7.
- **Aniline part:** The NH links to a benzodioxole ring (a 1,3-benzodioxole). The carbon bearing NH is adjacent to the ring-fusion carbon that bears the O, and the chlorine is on the next carbon. This gives a 5-chloro-1,3-benzodioxol-4-yl group.
- **Tetrahydropyran:** It is a six-membered ring (O plus five carbons) attached through its C4 to the ether oxygen.
