This structure is fesoterodine fumarate: the fumaric acid (E-butenedioic acid) salt component plus the fesoterodine free base.

**Fumaric acid:** two carboxylic acids joined through a trans CH=CH, written `OC(=O)/C=C/C(=O)O`.

**Fesoterodine part:**
- A diisopropylamino group (N with two CH(CH3)2) is attached to a CH2CH2 chain.
- The chain ends at a stereocentre that also carries a phenyl ring and a substituted benzene ring.
- On the substituted ring, the carbon ortho to the stereocentre carries an isobutyrate ester, O–C(=O)–CH(CH3)2.
- The ring carbon para to that ester oxygen carries a CH2OH group.

**Stereochemistry:** the stereocentre has a wedge bond to the CH2 of the chain, the substituted aryl group at upper right, the phenyl group below, and the implicit H pointing away from the viewer. Viewed from the front, aryl → chain CH2 → phenyl runs counterclockwise. Writing the neighbours in the order aryl, H, CH2, phenyl, this gives `[C@H]`. This matches the (R) configuration of fesoterodine.
