**Dichlorophenyl ring:** The ring attaches to the oxazole C2 at its lower-right vertex. Going around from there, the carbons are the attachment carbon, a CH at the top, a C–Cl at upper left, a CH at left, a C–Cl at lower left, and a CH at the bottom. The two Cl atoms therefore sit meta to the attachment point, which makes it a 3,5-dichlorophenyl group.

**Benzo ring:** Its atoms are C7a (bonded to O), then C7 (top CH), then C6 (bearing COOH), then C5 (CH), C4 (CH), and C3a (bonded to N).
