This is an enzalutamide-like structure with an extra fluorine on the left aryl ring.

**Left aryl ring.** Reading from the ring carbon bonded to N: C1 is attached to N, C2 (above) carries F, C3 carries CF3, C4 carries C#N, and C5 and C6 are CH.

**Central ring.** This is a thiohydantoin. N(left) is bonded to C(=O) and to C(=S). The C(=O) is bonded to C(CH3)2, which is bonded to N(right). N(right) is bonded to C(=S) and to the right aryl ring.

**Right aryl ring.** Reading from the ring carbon attached to N: the next carbon is CH, then C–F, then C bearing C(=O)NHCH3, then CH, then CH, which closes the ring. In the drawing, the carbon bonded to N sits at (918, 723). Its neighbours are (993, 633) and (960, 834). The carbon at (960, 834) is CH and connects to (1077, 852), which carries F. That carbon connects to (1152, 762), which carries the amide, and the ring then continues through (1110, 652) and (993, 633).

**SMILES.** Left ring: c1(N2...)c(F)c(C(F)(F)F)c(C#N)cc1. Right ring: c1ccc(C(=O)NC)c(F)c1, starting at the ring carbon attached to N.
