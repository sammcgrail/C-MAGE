I read this structure as trelagliptin, which is the alogliptin skeleton with a fluorine on the benzonitrile ring.

- **Pyrimidinedione core:** a six-membered ring with two C=O groups. One ring N carries a methyl group. The other ring N carries the benzyl CH2. A C=C links the carbon bearing the piperidine to the CH between the carbonyls.
- **Piperidine:** attached through its ring N to the pyrimidinedione carbon. The carbon three atoms round the ring from that N carries NH2 on a hashed bond.
- **Aryl group:** a benzene ring bearing a C≡N and the CH2 on adjacent carbons. The fluorine sits para to the nitrile carbon.

**Stereochemistry:** The NH2 is on a hashed bond, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. Viewed from that H, the three other neighbours (the upper CH2, the NH2, the lower CH2) run counterclockwise. In the SMILES order below (upper CH2, H, N, lower CH2), this corresponds to `@@`.

SMILES: CN1C(=O)C=C(N2CCC[C@@H](N)C2)N(Cc2cc(F)ccc2C#N)C1=O
