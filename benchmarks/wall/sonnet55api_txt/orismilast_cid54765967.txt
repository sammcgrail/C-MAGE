Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Spiro ring (top left):** S(=O)(=O) is bonded to CH2, CH2, then the spiro carbon, then CH2, CH2, and back to S. This is a thiane 1,1-dioxide ring with the spiro carbon at position 4.
- **Dioxole:** the spiro carbon carries two oxygens, each bonded to an aromatic carbon, giving a benzodioxole.
- **Benzene ring:** it has six carbons. Going around the ring:
  - C3a bears the upper O (the one at about 510,630).
  - C7a bears the lower O (the one at about 313,748).
  - Next to C7a is a carbon bearing OCHF2.
  - Then two CH carbons.
  - Then a carbon bearing the C(=O).
  - That carbon is adjacent to C3a, the carbon bonded to the upper O.
- **Side chain:** C(=O)CH2 leads to the pyridine N-oxide ring. The CH2 is attached at the pyridine 4-position, with Cl at both the 3- and 5-positions.

SMILES: O=C(Cc1c(Cl)c[n+]([O-])cc1Cl)c1ccc(OC(F)F)c2OC3(CCS(=O)(=O)CC3)Oc12
