The drawing shows ethylenediamine tetra(methylenephosphonic acid) (EDTMP).

- Two nitrogens are joined by a two-carbon chain (N–CH2–CH2–N).
- Each nitrogen carries two CH2–P(=O)(OH)2 groups.
- That gives four phosphonic acid groups in total: two on the left nitrogen (top and left) and two on the right nitrogen (right and bottom).

SMILES: OP(O)(=O)CN(CP(O)(O)=O)CCN(CP(O)(O)=O)CP(O)(O)=O
