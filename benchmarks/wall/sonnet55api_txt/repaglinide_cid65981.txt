I read this structure as repaglinide, the (S)-enantiomer. The pieces are:

- **Left ring:** a benzoic acid bearing an ethoxy group ortho to the COOH. The CH2C(=O)NH side chain sits para to the COOH.
- **Amide:** the CH2 links to a C(=O)–NH amide.
- **Stereocentre:** the amide N sits on a carbon that carries an isobutyl group (CH2CH(CH3)2), drawn on a wedge bond, and an aryl ring.
- **Right ring:** a benzene ring with a piperidine N attached at the ortho position.

For the stereocentre, the wedged CH2 points toward the viewer, so the implicit H points away. The priorities are N > aryl carbon > CH2 > H. Going N → aryl → CH2 on the page runs counterclockwise, which gives S. In the SMILES below, `N[C@@H](CC(C)C)c...` encodes S.

SMILES: CCOc1cc(CC(=O)N[C@@H](CC(C)C)c2ccccc2N2CCCCC2)ccc1C(=O)O
