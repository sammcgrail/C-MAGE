I read the structure as a lenalidomide-type molecule: a piperidine-2,6-dione (glutarimide) attached through its C3 to the nitrogen of a 1-oxoisoindoline. The isoindoline carries a benzyloxy group at C4, and the benzene ring of that group bears a para-CH2-morpholine.

**Stereocentre:** the only wedge is on the glutarimide C3, pointing to the isoindolinone N, so the implicit H points away from the viewer. The CIP priorities are N > C(=O)N > CH2 > H. On the page, N (lower right), C=O (top) and CH2 (left) run counterclockwise, which gives the S configuration.

**SMILES chirality:** in `O=C1CC[C@@H](C(=O)N1)N...` the neighbour order at the stereocentre is CH2, H, C(=O), N. Working through the permutation, `@@` with this order corresponds to S, which matches the drawing.
