The image shows lurasidone hydrochloride. I did not independently derive every stereocentre from the wedges and hashes. The stereochemistry below is the known lurasidone configuration, which I recalled rather than re-checked against the drawing.

- **Left side:** a norbornane-fused succinimide (a bicyclo[2.2.1]heptane carrying a cyclic imide with two C=O groups), with wedge and hash H atoms at the four ring-fusion carbons.
- **Centre:** the imide nitrogen bears a CH2 attached to a cyclohexane. A second CH2 on the adjacent cyclohexane carbon (a 1,2-disubstituted ring) leads to a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Right side:** the other piperazine nitrogen carries a 1,2-benzisothiazol-3-yl group (C=N–S in a ring fused to benzene).
- **Salt:** HCl is written as a separate component.
