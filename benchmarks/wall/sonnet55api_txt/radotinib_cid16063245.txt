This structure is a nilotinib analogue in which the pyridin-3-yl group is replaced by pyrazin-2-yl.

**Left side:**
- The pyrazine ring has N at the top and N at the bottom left (a 1,4-arrangement). Its C2 is attached to the pyrimidine.
- The pyrimidine is attached through its C4 to the pyrazine. Its C2 carries the NH, and its N1 and N3 are both present.
- The NH links to the central benzene ring.

**Central benzene ring:**
- It carries the NH substituent, a methyl group ortho to it, and the C(=O)N amide group meta to the NH.
- The NH-bearing carbon is adjacent to the methyl-bearing carbon.
- The amide carbon is attached to the ring carbon next to the CH that sits beside the NH-bearing carbon, so the amide is meta to the NH and para to the methyl.

**Right side:**
- The amide N is bonded to an aryl ring that carries CF3 and a 4-methylimidazol-1-yl group, both meta to the amide N (a 3,5-disubstituted pattern).
- In the imidazole, N1 is attached to the aryl ring, C2 is the CH between the two nitrogens, N3 is the second nitrogen, C4 bears the methyl, and C5 is the CH.
