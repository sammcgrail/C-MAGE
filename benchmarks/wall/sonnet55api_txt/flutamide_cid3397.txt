The drawing is flutamide: an isobutyramide on an aromatic ring that also carries a nitro group and a CF3 group.

**Isobutyryl part:** An isopropyl group (two methyls on one CH) attaches to a carbonyl carbon with C=O, and that carbon bonds to NH. This gives CC(C)C(=O)N.

**Ring:** The NH-bearing carbon is the first ring carbon. Going around the ring from there:
- Next is a CH (upper left).
- Then comes the carbon bearing CF3 (top right).
- Then the carbon bearing the nitro group (right).
- Then two CH carbons at the bottom.

So the NH carbon is C1, the CF3 carbon is C3 and the nitro carbon is C4. That makes the ring 4-nitro-3-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl, as in flutamide.

**Nitro group:** Drawn in charge-separated form as [N+](=O)[O-].

SMILES: CC(C)C(=O)Nc1ccc([N+](=O)[O-])c(C(F)(F)F)c1
