The drawing shows phosphoenolpyruvic acid, read as follows:

- A terminal CH2 is double-bonded to a carbon.
- That carbon is bonded to an oxygen, which is bonded to a phosphorus carrying one P=O and two OH groups (a phosphate group).
- The same carbon is also bonded to a carboxylic acid carbon, C(=O)OH.

There are no wedges, hashes or charges, so no stereochemistry applies.

SMILES: C=C(OP(=O)(O)O)C(=O)O
