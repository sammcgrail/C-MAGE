The drawing shows a quinoline-8-sulfonamide whose nitrogen is attached to a para-substituted benzene. That benzene carries an amide to a piperazine, and the piperazine bears a cyclopropylmethyl group.

- **Quinoline:** the ring nitrogen sits next to the fused carbon, which is adjacent to the C8 carbon bearing the sulfonyl group. This gives the 8-sulfonyl quinoline.
- **Sulfonamide:** S(=O)(=O) links C8 to an NH.
- **Benzene:** the NH-bearing carbon is para to the carbonyl-bearing carbon.
- **Piperazine:** the amide nitrogen is joined by C(=O) to the benzene, and the opposite nitrogen carries CH2–cyclopropyl.
