The drawing shows two phosphorus atoms joined by a CH2CH2 bridge. Each phosphorus also carries two CH2CH2OCH2CH3 (2-ethoxyethyl) arms.

Reading the arms:
- Left phosphorus: one arm goes up to O and then an ethyl group (CH2CH2–O–CH2CH3). The other arm goes down to O and then an ethyl group.
- Right phosphorus: one arm goes up to O and then an ethyl group. The other arm goes right to O and then an ethyl group.
- The bridge between the two phosphorus atoms is P–CH2–CH2–P.

Each phosphorus therefore has three substituents, which makes the molecule a bis(diphosphine): (EtOCH2CH2)2P–CH2CH2–P(CH2CH2OEt)2.

SMILES: CCOCCP(CCOCC)CCP(CCOCC)CCOCC
