This structure is melphalan (L-phenylalanine mustard).

- **Ring:** a para-disubstituted benzene.
- **Ring substituents:** one is an N,N-bis(2-chloroethyl)amino group, N(CH2CH2Cl)2. The other is a CH2 leading to the amino-acid carbon.
- **Side chain:** CH2–CH(NH2)–COOH.
- **Stereochemistry:** the only stereocentre is the α-carbon, with a wedge to NH2, so the NH2 points toward the viewer and the implicit H points away. The CIP priorities are N > COOH > CH2 > H. Tracing N → COOH → CH2 in the drawing runs counterclockwise with H at the back, which gives S. That matches the L-amino-acid pattern N[C@@H](R)C(=O)O.
