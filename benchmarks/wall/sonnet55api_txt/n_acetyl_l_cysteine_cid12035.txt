The drawing shows N‑acetylcysteine: an acetyl group (CH3–C(=O)) bonded to the nitrogen of cysteine, which carries a CH2SH side chain and a COOH group on the α‑carbon.

The only stereocentre is the α‑carbon, and the wedge bond runs from it to the CH2SH group. The hydrogen is therefore pointing away from the viewer. The substituent priorities are N, then CH2SH (S outranks O), then COOH. In the drawing, N sits at the upper right, CH2SH at the bottom and COOH at the upper left, so tracing N → CH2SH → COOH goes clockwise. That gives the R configuration, which is the natural L‑cysteine form.
