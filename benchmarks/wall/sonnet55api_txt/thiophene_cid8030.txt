The drawing is a five-membered ring made of one sulfur and four carbons.

Going around the ring from S: S connects to the top carbon, and that bond is plain. The top carbon is double-bonded to the right carbon, as shown by the inner parallel line. The right carbon is single-bonded to the bottom carbon. The bottom carbon is double-bonded to the bottom-left carbon, again shown by an inner line. The bottom-left carbon is single-bonded back to S.

That gives S1C=CC=C1, which is thiophene.

SMILES: C1=CSC=C1
