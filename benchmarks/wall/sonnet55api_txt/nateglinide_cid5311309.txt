I read the structure as nateglinide, with these pieces:

- **Left side:** an isopropyl group on a cyclohexane ring, drawn with plain bonds, so I assigned no ring stereochemistry.
- **Middle:** the cyclohexane ring carries an amide C(=O)–NH, and the NH is bonded to the stereocentre.
- **Stereocentre:** it bears the N, a COOH group, and a CH2 linked to a phenyl ring, plus an implicit H. The CH2 bond is a wedge, so that CH2 points toward the viewer and the H points away.
- **Right side:** a benzene ring attached through the CH2.

For the stereocentre, the priorities are N > COOH > CH2Ph > H. With H pointing away, N (left), COOH (up) and CH2 (down-right) run clockwise, so the centre is R. This is the D-phenylalanine configuration. In the SMILES written with the amide N before the centre, `N[C@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)O` encodes R.

SMILES: CC(C)C1CCC(CC1)C(=O)N[C@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)O
