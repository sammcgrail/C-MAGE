I read this structure as naltrexone, a morphinan skeleton with these features:

- an N-cyclopropylmethyl group on the nitrogen,
- a ketone in the six-membered ring on the upper right,
- a tertiary hydroxyl drawn with a wedge,
- a dihydrofuran ether bridge (the ring oxygen on the right),
- a phenol OH on the aromatic ring.

The drawing has wedges on the two ring-fusion hydrogens, the hydroxyl-bearing carbon and the quaternary carbon bearing the CH2 bridge. I did not derive each @/@@ from the wedges myself. I used the stereochemistry of natural naltrexone, which I recalled from memory and took to match the drawing.
