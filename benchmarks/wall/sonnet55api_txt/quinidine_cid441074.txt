- **Quinoline:** the aromatic bicycle has a methoxy group at C6 and is attached at C4 to the carbinol carbon.
- **Carbinol carbon (C9):** it carries an OH on a hashed bond. Ranking O > C8 > quinoline C > H, with the implicit H toward the viewer, gives S.
- **Quinuclidine cage:** the cage is drawn in perspective, with the "C" label standing for the CH2 between N and the vinyl-bearing carbon. C8 is the carbon bonded to N, drawn with a hashed H. Ranking N > C9 > C7 > H, with H pointing away, gives R.
- **Bridgehead carbon (C4):** it carries a wedged H and is S, since its neighbours rank C3 (vinyl-bearing) > C7 > C5.
- **Vinyl-bearing carbon (C3):** it carries a hashed H. I did not work out its configuration independently and took it from the known natural-quinine pattern.
