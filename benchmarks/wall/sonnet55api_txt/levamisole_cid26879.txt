I read the drawing as a phenyl group on a fused bicyclic ring: a 2,3,5,6-tetrahydroimidazo[2,1-b]thiazole core, which is the tetramisole/levamisole skeleton.

**Ring system**
- The thiazoline ring is S–CH2–CH2–N–C(=N), closed through the amidine carbon.
- The imidazoline ring shares the N–C(=N) edge with the thiazoline ring. Its other atoms are the C=N nitrogen, the stereocentre bearing the phenyl group, and a CH2 attached to the bridgehead N.
- The benzene ring is drawn as a normal six-membered aromatic ring.

**Stereochemistry**
- The only wedge is a hashed bond from the stereocentre to the phenyl carbon, so the phenyl points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.
- Viewed from the H side, the remaining neighbours go N, phenyl, CH2 counterclockwise.
- In the SMILES order N, H, phenyl, CH2, that corresponds to `@@`.

The SMILES `C1CSC2=N[C@@H](c3ccccc3)CN12` is valid and matches the drawing atom for atom.
