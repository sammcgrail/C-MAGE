This is praziquantel, drawn without stereochemistry.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- The cyclohexane ring on the left is attached to a C(=O) group, which is bonded to the lower nitrogen (N).
- From the lower N, one CH2 leads up to the lactam carbonyl C(=O), and another CH2 leads to the ring-fusion CH.
- The lactam carbonyl is bonded to the upper N.
- The upper N is bonded to the ring-fusion CH and to a CH2CH2 chain that runs to the benzene ring.
- The ring-fusion CH is also bonded directly to the benzene ring.

The ring-fusion CH is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereo is given.

SMILES: O=C(C1CCCCC1)N1CC(=O)N2CCc3ccccc3C2C1
