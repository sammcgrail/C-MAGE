This drawing is mitomycin C. I wrote its SMILES from my recall of the standard PubChem SMILES for this compound, not by building it up from the picture. I then checked one stereocentre against the drawing and the other stereocentres were not checked.

The structure has these parts:
- a 2-amino-3-methyl-1,4-benzoquinone ring fused to a pyrrolidine (the quinone carbons carry the NH2, CH3 and two C=O groups)
- a fused aziridine ring (NH) with two hashed hydrogens
- a methoxy group on the carbon next to the ring nitrogen, drawn with a hashed bond
- a carbamate side chain, CH2–O–C(=O)–NH2, on a wedged bond

The check was at the methoxy-bearing carbon. With the OMe pointing away from the viewer, the other three neighbours (the aziridine-side carbon, the ring N and the CH(CH2O) carbon) run clockwise in that order. That matches the `@@` in the SMILES below.

SMILES: CO[C@@]12[C@H](COC(N)=O)C3=C(N1C[C@H]1N[C@@H]12)C(=O)C(C)=C(N)C3=O
