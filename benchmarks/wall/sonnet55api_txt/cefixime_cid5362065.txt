This is a cefixime-type cephalosporin. I read it region by region.

- **Bicyclic core:** a β-lactam (N, C=O, and two CH carbons) is fused to a six-membered dihydrothiazine ring containing S, a CH2, and a C=C. That C=C bears a vinyl group on one carbon and a COOH on the carbon attached to N.
- **Side chain:** an NH–C(=O) amide is attached to the β-lactam CH carbon through a wedge bond. The amide carbonyl is joined to a C=N–O–CH2–COOH oxime ether, and that same carbon also carries a 2-aminothiazol-4-yl ring.
- **Stereocentres:** the NH-bearing carbon has the wedge, and the S/N-bearing ring-fusion carbon has a hashed H. Working through the neighbour order in the SMILES gives `[C@@H]` for the NH carbon and `[C@H]` for the ring-fusion carbon.
- **Oxime geometry:** the oxime O sits on the same side as the amide carbonyl, which is the Z (syn) isomer. In the SMILES this is written as `C(=O)/C(=N\O...)`.

SMILES: C=CC1=C(C(=O)O)N2C(=O)[C@@H](NC(=O)/C(=N\OCC(=O)O)c3csc(N)n3)[C@H]2SC1
