I read this as the decapeptide goserelin: pGlu‑His‑Trp‑Ser‑Tyr‑D‑Ser(tBu)‑Leu‑Arg‑Pro‑NH‑NH‑C(=O)NH2.

- **Residue order:** pyroglutamic acid, histidine (imidazole), tryptophan (indole), serine (free CH2OH), tyrosine (4‑hydroxybenzyl), O‑tert‑butyl serine, leucine (isobutyl), arginine (guanidine side chain), then proline.
- **C‑terminus:** the proline carbonyl is joined to a semicarbazide group, C(=O)NHNHC(=O)NH2, in place of a free acid or simple amide.
- **Stereochemistry:** the Ser(OH) and Ser(tBu) residues are drawn with opposite wedge types, so Ser(tBu) is D and the free Ser is L. I took the remaining stereocentres from the known configuration of goserelin rather than assigning each wedge independently.
