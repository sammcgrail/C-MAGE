I read the molecule as a pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidine joined through its C4 position to the nitrogen of a pyrrolidine. That pyrrolidine is spiro-fused to an azetidine. The azetidine nitrogen carries a cyanoacetyl group (C(=O)CH2C≡N), and the azetidine carbon next to the spiro carbon bears a methyl group.

**Ring systems**
- **Azetidine:** the four ring atoms are the azetidine N, the spiro carbon, the CH(CH3) carbon and a CH2.
- **Pyrrolidine:** the five ring atoms are the spiro carbon, a CH2, the pyrrolidine N, another CH2 and the CH2 drawn with the hashed bond.

**Stereochemistry**
- The spiro carbon has a hashed bond to the lower CH2, which points away from the viewer. Working from the 2D coordinates, this gives `[C@]12` in the SMILES below.
- The CH(CH3) carbon has a wedged methyl group, which points toward the viewer. This gives `[C@H]` in the SMILES below.

I assigned both centres by a geometric (signed-volume) check on the drawn coordinates, not by CIP labelling.

SMILES: N#CCC(=O)N1C[C@H](C)[C@]12CCN(C2)c1ncnc2[nH]ccc12
