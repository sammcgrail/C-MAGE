The drawing shows metformin, drawn as a tautomer.

- On the left, a nitrogen carries two methyl groups (the two lines going up and down-left from it) and is bonded to a carbon.
- That carbon has a double bond to an NH (drawn at the bottom) and a single bond to the central N.
- The central N is double-bonded to the right-hand carbon.
- The right-hand carbon carries two NH2 groups.

Written out, the connectivity is CN(C)C(=N)N=C(N)N.
