I read the structure as follows:

- **Left ring system:** a 7H-pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidine, with the pyrimidine C4 bonded to the piperidine nitrogen.
- **Piperidine:** C4 carries both an NH2 and a C(=O)NH amide group.
- **Amide nitrogen:** attached to a stereocentre bearing a 4-chlorophenyl group and a CH2CH2OH chain.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CH2 bond at that stereocentre is hashed (pointing away from the viewer). The priorities are N > aryl > CH2CH2OH > H. Viewed from the front, N → aryl → CH2 runs clockwise, but the implicit H points toward the viewer, so the true sense is counterclockwise. That makes the centre S, which is written `[C@@H]` in the SMILES order below (N, H, CCO, aryl). This is consistent with the drug capivasertib.

The piperidine C4 has two identical ring branches, so it is not a stereocentre.

SMILES: NC1(CCN(CC1)c1ncnc2[nH]ccc12)C(=O)N[C@@H](CCO)c1ccc(Cl)cc1
