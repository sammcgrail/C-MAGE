The image shows an Fe atom and five separate carbon monoxide units. Each unit is drawn as a carbon with a lone pair, a double bond and an O label, and the carbon has no label of its own. Fe is not connected to any of them, so there are six disconnected fragments.

Each CO unit is a carbon with a lone pair (two dots) and a double bond to O. A carbon with a lone pair and a double bond to oxygen is a carbene-like drawing of carbon monoxide, written as [C]=O. That gives carbon 2 (double bond) + 2 (lone pair) = 4 electrons, which is a neutral carbene-type carbon. Written as [C]=O, the carbon would have two radical electrons, so the lone pair has to be implied separately. RDKit commonly writes this form as [C]=O for a dot-depicted lone pair.

Fe(CO)5 would be the intended compound. The drawing contains five CO units, so I write the answer as disconnected fragments.
