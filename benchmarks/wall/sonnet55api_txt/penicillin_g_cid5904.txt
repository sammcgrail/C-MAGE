This is penicillin G (benzylpenicillin), read as follows from the drawing:

- **Left side:** a phenyl ring joined through a CH2 to an amide C=O, which connects through NH to the β-lactam.
- **Core:** a four-membered β-lactam ring (C=O, N, and two CH carbons) fused to a five-membered thiazolidine ring containing S, a C(CH3)2 carbon, and a CH bearing the COOH.
- **Stereochemistry:** the NH bond is hashed, the H on the ring-fusion carbon is wedged, and the COOH is wedged. I checked the COOH-bearing carbon from the drawing, and it comes out as S, which matches natural penicillin G (2S,5R,6R). I took the other two centres from the known natural configuration rather than deriving them independently.
