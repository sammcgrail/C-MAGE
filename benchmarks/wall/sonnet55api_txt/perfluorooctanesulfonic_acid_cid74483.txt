The drawing shows a perfluorinated carbon chain ending in a sulfonic acid group (S with two =O and one OH).

Counting the carbons along the chain, left to right:
- C1 at x≈248 carries 2 F and the S.
- C2 at 395, C3 at 540, C4 at 685, C5 at 830, C6 at 975 and C7 at 1120 each carry 2 F.
- C8 at 1265 carries 3 F, including the one at 1410 on the right.

That makes 8 carbons, so the molecule is perfluorooctanesulfonic acid (PFOS).

Fluorine count: 2 on each of C1–C7 gives 14, plus 3 on C8 gives 17, which matches C8F17SO3H.
