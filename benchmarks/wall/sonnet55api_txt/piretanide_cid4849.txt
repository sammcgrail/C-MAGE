This is a bumetanide analogue in which the butylamino group is replaced by pyrrolidine.

The central benzene ring carries four substituents:
- A carboxylic acid, C(=O)O.
- A sulfonamide, S(=O)(=O)N, on the carbon two positions away from the acid-bearing carbon.
- A phenoxy group, O-Ph, on the carbon between the sulfonamide carbon and the pyrrolidine carbon.
- A pyrrolidine nitrogen on the next ring carbon.

Going around the ring from the acid: C1 (COOH), C2 (H, top right), C3 (pyrrolidinyl), C4 (OPh), C5 (SO2NH2), C6 (H, bottom). So the acid and the pyrrolidine are meta to each other, and the acid and the sulfonamide are also meta to each other.
