I read the structure as a cyclic pentapeptide core with a side chain, in the style of the tuberactinomycin/viomycin family. I assigned each stereocentre from its wedge or hash by hand and did not run the SMILES through a toolkit.

**Macrocycle.** Going round the ring from the α-carbon that carries the wedged N-acyl group:
- C(=O), then NH, then a serine α-carbon with a hashed CH2OH.
- C(=O), then NH, then a second serine α-carbon with a hashed CH2OH.
- C(=O), then NH, then a dehydro residue C(=CH–NH–C(=O)NH2). The ureido N and the ring NH are cis across this double bond.
- C(=O), then NH, then an α-carbon with a hashed N, bearing the cyclic guanidine substituent.
- C(=O), then NH, then CH2, then back to the first α-carbon.

**Cyclic guanidine substituent.** The α-carbon attaches to a six-membered ring: CH, N=C(NH2), NH, CH(OH) (wedged OH), CH2, back to the CH.

**Side chain.** The N-acyl group on the first α-carbon is C(=O)–CH2–CH(NH2)–CH2–CH2–CH2–NH2, with the NH2 on the CH drawn hashed.
