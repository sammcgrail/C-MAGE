The drawing shows a tert-butyl group (a carbon with three methyls) bonded to an NH. That NH connects to a CH2, which connects to a CH(OH) carbon, which is attached to the benzene ring.

On the ring, the carbon bearing the side chain sits between two CH positions. Going around from there, the ring carries Cl, then NH2, then Cl, so the substituents are 3,5-dichloro-4-amino. This is clenbuterol.

No wedges or hashes are drawn, so I give no stereochemistry.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)NCC(O)c1cc(Cl)c(N)c(Cl)c1
