The drawing shows a pyridine ring with a carbohydrazide group (C(=O)NN) attached at the 4-position. This is isoniazid.

Ring: the N sits at the lower left. Going around, N connects to a CH at the upper left, then a CH at the top, then the substituted C at the right, then a CH at the bottom right, then a CH at the bottom left, and back to N. The substituted carbon is therefore para to the nitrogen, which makes it the 4-position.

Substituent: the ring carbon bonds to a carbonyl carbon, which is double-bonded to O and single-bonded to NH, and that NH bonds to NH2.

SMILES: NNC(=O)c1ccncc1
