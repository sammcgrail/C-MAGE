I read the drawing as an ofloxacin-type tricyclic core with a 1-aminocyclopropyl group in place of the usual piperazine.

- **Core:** a benzene ring fused to a 4-pyridone-3-carboxylic acid (N–CH=C(COOH)–C(=O)), plus a six-membered oxazine ring made of the ring N, a CH(CH3), a CH2, an O and two aromatic carbons.
- **Benzene substituents:** fluorine on one carbon, and a 1-aminocyclopropyl group (cyclopropane carbon bearing NH2) on the adjacent carbon.
- **Stereocentre:** the CH(CH3) carbon next to the ring nitrogen is the only one with a wedge. The methyl is on a wedge, so it points towards the viewer and the hydrogen points away. Its neighbours N, CH2 and CH3 run counterclockwise when viewed from the front. In the SMILES `C[C@H]1COc...n1`, that corresponds to `@`.

SMILES: C[C@H]1COc2c(C3(N)CC3)c(F)cc3c(=O)c(C(=O)O)cn1c23
