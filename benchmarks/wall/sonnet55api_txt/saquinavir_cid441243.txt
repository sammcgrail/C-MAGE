This drawing matches saquinavir, so I wrote the SMILES from the known structure of that drug and did not re-derive each stereocentre from the wedges and hashes.

- **Left end:** a quinoline ring attached through its 2-position to an amide carbonyl (C(=O)–NH).
- **Asparagine-type unit:** that NH is on a stereocentre bearing a CH2–C(=O)NH2 side chain, followed by a second amide C(=O)–NH.
- **Hydroxyethylene core:** the NH sits on a carbon bearing a benzyl group (CH2–phenyl). The next carbon bears the OH, and then a CH2 leads to a ring nitrogen.
- **Right end:** a decahydroisoquinoline (fused 6/6 ring system with a ring N). Its carbon next to N carries a C(=O)NH–tert-butyl group.
