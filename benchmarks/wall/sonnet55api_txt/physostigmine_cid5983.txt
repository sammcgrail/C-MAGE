This structure is physostigmine (eserine), a tricyclic pyrroloindoline carrying an N-methylcarbamate on the benzene ring. I assigned the stereocentres from the two wedges in the drawing.

**Connectivity**
- The benzene ring has a carbamate oxygen at the right-hand carbon. The O–C(=O)–NH–CH3 group is on the right.
- The ring-fusion carbon attached to the benzene ring (C3a) bears a wedged methyl. Its other neighbours are a CH2 and the aminal carbon (C8a).
- The five-membered ring on the left runs C3a, CH2, CH2, N(CH3), C8a.
- C8a also bonds to the indoline N(CH3), which is attached to the benzene ring. C8a carries a wedged H.

**Stereochemistry**
- For the SMILES atom order aromatic C, ring closure to C8a, methyl, CH2, I found C3a to be `[C@]`. The wedged methyl points toward the viewer.
- For the atom order N(CH3) of the pyrrolidine, H, C3a, indoline N, I found C8a to be `[C@@H]`. The wedged H points toward the viewer.
