The drawing matches travoprost, the isopropyl ester of a prostaglandin F2α analogue. I wrote the SMILES from the structure, but I took the stereodescriptors from the known stereochemistry of travoprost rather than re-deriving each wedge and hash from the image.

- **Ester end:** an isopropyl group on the ester oxygen, then the carbonyl.
- **Upper chain:** three CH2 groups, then a cis (Z) C=C, then one more CH2 that joins the cyclopentane through a hashed bond. This gives the hept-5-enoate chain.
- **Cyclopentane:** two ring OH groups, drawn with hashed bonds, and a ring CH2 between them.
- **Lower chain:** a trans (E) CH=CH attached to the ring by a wedged bond. Next comes a CH(OH) with a hashed bond, then CH2–O–aryl.
- **Aryl group:** a benzene ring bearing CF3 meta to the ether oxygen.
