This structure is apalutamide.

**Pyridine ring.** The ring atoms are N, C(CN), C(CF3), CH, C(N-attached) and CH. The nitrile carbon sits on the carbon next to the ring N. The CF3 carbon is adjacent to that one. The thiohydantoin N attaches at the carbon between a CH and another CH, which is the 3-position relative to the ring N.

**Thiohydantoin core.** A five-membered ring: N1 (attached to the pyridine), C(=O), spiro C (part of a cyclobutane), N (attached to the aryl ring), and C(=S) bridging the two N atoms.

**Aryl ring.** The ring N attaches at one carbon. The ring then runs CH, CH, C(C(=O)NHMe), C(F), CH back to the start. The carbon bearing the amide is para to the ring N-attached carbon, and F is ortho to the amide carbon and meta to the N-attached carbon.
