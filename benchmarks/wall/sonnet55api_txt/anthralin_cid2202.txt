The drawing is an anthracenone skeleton: a central six-membered ring fused to two benzene rings, with a CH2 at the top and a C=O at the bottom. This is dithranol (anthralin).

Reading the central ring: the top vertex is CH2, bonded to two aromatic fusion carbons. The bottom vertex is C=O, bonded to the other two fusion carbons. Each outer ring carries an OH on the carbon adjacent to the fusion carbon next to the carbonyl, so both OH groups sit at the peri positions beside the C=O (positions 1 and 8).
