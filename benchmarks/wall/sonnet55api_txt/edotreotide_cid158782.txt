This is a DOTA-conjugated octreotide-type peptide (DOTA-TOC): DOTA–D-Phe–cyclo[Cys–Tyr–D-Trp–Lys–Thr–Cys] with a C-terminal threoninol, closed by a Cys–Cys disulfide bridge.

I read it region by region:
- **Left:** a 12-membered ring with four nitrogens, three N–CH2–COOH arms, and the fourth nitrogen acylated (N–CH2–C(=O)–NH) onto the Phe.
- **Phe:** D-configured, with a benzyl side chain.
- **Macrocycle:** Cys, Tyr (4-hydroxybenzyl), D-Trp (indol-3-ylmethyl), Lys (4-aminobutyl), Thr (CH(OH)CH3), then a second Cys. The two Cys are linked through CH2–S–S–CH2.
- **C-terminus:** the second Cys carbonyl is amidated onto threoninol, NH–CH(CH2OH)–CH(OH)CH3.

I assigned the stereocentres by working out the CIP descriptors for the threoninol and by following the standard L-amino-acid pattern for the rest, then converting each to SMILES chirality order. I did not re-check every wedge and hash against the drawing.
