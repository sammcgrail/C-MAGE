I read the structure as three parts joined together.

- **Imidazole:** a ring bearing a tert-butyl group on the carbon next to the NH. The ring carbon next to the ring N (the one without H) carries a =CH– link.
- **Central ring:** a piperazine-2,5-dione with two C=O groups and two NH groups. One ring carbon is double-bonded to the CH from the imidazole. The other ring carbon is double-bonded to a CH attached to a phenyl group.
- **Geometry of the two exocyclic C=C bonds:**
  - On the imidazolyl-methylidene bond, the imidazole and the adjacent ring NH lie on the same side, which gives Z.
  - On the benzylidene bond, the phenyl and the adjacent ring NH also lie on the same side, which gives Z.

SMILES: CC(C)(C)C1=C(/C=C2\NC(=O)C(\NC2=O)=C\c3ccccc3)N=CN1
