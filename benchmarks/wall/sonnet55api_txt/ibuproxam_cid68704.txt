The drawing shows a para-disubstituted benzene ring. One substituent is an isobutyl group, CH2-CH(CH3)2. The other is a CH(CH3)-C(=O)-NH-OH group, which makes the molecule ibuprofen hydroxamic acid.

The CH carbon is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I give no stereo.
