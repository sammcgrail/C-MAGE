Reading the structure region by region:

- **Left ring:** an isoxazole with O1 bonded to N2, then C3 (bearing the carbonyl), C4 (CH), and C5 (bearing the methyl). The ring is written Cc1cc(C(=O)...)no1.
- **Linker:** the C3 carbonyl is an amide, C(=O)NH, followed by CH2CH2 to the benzene ring.
- **Benzene:** para-disubstituted, with the CH2CH2 chain on one side and the sulfonyl group on the other.
- **Sulfonylurea:** the ring carbon carries S(=O)(=O), then NH, then C(=O), then NH, then N of the azepane.
- **Azepane:** the seven-membered ring has N plus 6 CH2 groups. Counting the drawn vertices gives N, then 6 carbons (two on the left, then the top, right, and bottom ones), which is consistent with seven atoms in total.

The N–NH linkage makes the end group an N-amino azepane (a hydrazine-type urea).

SMILES: Cc1cc(C(=O)NCCc2ccc(cc2)S(=O)(=O)NC(=O)NN2CCCCCC2)no1
