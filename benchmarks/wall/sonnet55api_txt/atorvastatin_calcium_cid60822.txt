This is atorvastatin calcium: two atorvastatin carboxylate anions and one Ca²⁺ ion.

Each anion has these parts:
- A central pyrrole ring whose nitrogen carries the side chain.
- On the ring carbon next to the nitrogen: a 4-fluorophenyl group.
- On the next ring carbon: a phenyl group.
- On the next ring carbon: a C(=O)NH-phenyl (anilide) group.
- On the last ring carbon, also next to the nitrogen: an isopropyl group.
- On the nitrogen: a –CH2CH2–CH(OH)–CH2–CH(OH)–CH2–COO⁻ chain.

The two hydroxyl-bearing carbons are drawn with hashed bonds. I took the (3R,5R) configuration from the known form of atorvastatin rather than deriving it from the drawing, and wrote the SMILES in the PubChem pattern for that configuration. I did not re-derive the CIP assignment from the hashes.
