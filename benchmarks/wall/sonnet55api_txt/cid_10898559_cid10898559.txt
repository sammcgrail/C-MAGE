This is methylcobalamin drawn in its disconnected, charged form. I read it region by region and wrote the SMILES by hand.

**What I read from the drawing**
- **Metal and ligand:** a separate Co³⁺ and a separate CH₃⁻.
- **Corrin ring:** the four ring nitrogens are one N⁻ (bottom-left ring) and three imine-type N (=N) atoms. There is a direct bond between the two ring carbons at the bottom, and a gem-dimethyl carbon at the top.
- **Side chains:** six primary amide side chains (CH₂C(=O)NH₂ or CH₂CH₂C(=O)NH₂) sit on the ring.
- **Linker:** one propionamide chain runs to NH–CH₂–CH(CH₃)–O–P.
- **Phosphate and sugar:** the phosphate carries one O⁻, and is bonded to the 3-position oxygen of a ribofuranose. The ribose has CH₂OH and OH groups and is attached through N to a 5,6-dimethylbenzimidazole.
- **Charge check:** Co³⁺ (+3), CH₃⁻ (−1), N⁻ (−1) and phosphate O⁻ (−1) sum to zero.

**Stereochemistry**
I could not reliably assign the corrin stereocentres, so I left them unspecified. I kept only the stereo I could place with confidence: the ribose ring and the propan-2-yl phosphate carbon.
