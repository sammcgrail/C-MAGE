This is bambuterol: a benzene ring carrying two O–C(=O)N(CH3)2 carbamate groups at the 3 and 5 positions and a CH(OH)CH2NH–C(CH3)3 side chain at position 1.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- Top right: the dimethylamino carbamate N(C)C–C(=O)–O is attached to the ring.
- Bottom right: a second, identical carbamate O–C(=O)N(C)C is attached to the ring.
- Left: the ring carbon bears CH(OH)–CH2–NH–C(CH3)3, a tert-butylamino side chain.
- The CH(OH) carbon is drawn with plain bonds, so no stereochemistry is given.

SMILES: CN(C)C(=O)Oc1cc(cc(c1)OC(=O)N(C)C)C(O)CNC(C)(C)C
