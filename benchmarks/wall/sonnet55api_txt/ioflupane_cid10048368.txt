**Skeleton and substituents**
- The bicyclic core has a nitrogen bridge between the two bridgehead carbons. A two-carbon bridge links the same bridgeheads, and a CH2 and two substituted carbons form the three-carbon bridge.
- The carbon next to the top bridgehead carries a methyl ester (C(=O)OCH3) on a wedge.
- The next carbon carries a 4-iodophenyl ring on a wedge.
- The nitrogen carries a 3-fluoropropyl chain (N–CH2CH2CH2–F).
