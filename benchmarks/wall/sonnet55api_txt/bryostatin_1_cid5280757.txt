This is my reading of the drawing: a bryostatin-type macrolide with three six-membered rings (two tetrahydropyrans and one more pyran ring). The stereo tags (`@`/`@@`) were assigned by eye from the wedges and hashes and may contain errors. I left the two exocyclic C=C bonds bearing the methyl ester groups without cis/trans markers.

**Macrolactone and substituents**
- The lactone C(=O) sits next to a CH2, then a CH(OH) (wedged OH), then another CH2.
- The 1-hydroxyethyl group, CH(OH)CH3, is on the carbon bearing the lactone oxygen.

**Ring A (six-membered, oxygen-containing)**
- It carries an acetate on one carbon, a gem-dimethyl carbon next to it, and a hemiketal carbon bearing OH.

**Ring B (tetrahydropyran)**
- It has an exocyclic =CH–CO2Me group.
- A trans CH=CH links it to a C(CH3)2 carbon.

**Ring C (six-membered, oxygen-containing)**
- It has an OH on the carbon next to the oxygen and a ring carbon bearing the exocyclic =CH–CO2Me.
- The adjacent ring carbon carries the (2E,4E)-octa-2,4-dienoate ester, O–C(=O)–CH=CH–CH=CH–C3H7.
