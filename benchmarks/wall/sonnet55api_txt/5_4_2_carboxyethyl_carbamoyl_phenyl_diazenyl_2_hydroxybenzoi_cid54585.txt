This structure is balsalazide. It has three parts:

- **Left ring:** a salicylic acid ring with COOH and OH on adjacent carbons. The azo group sits para to the OH.
- **Centre:** an N=N azo linkage joining the left ring to a para-substituted benzene ring.
- **Right side:** a C(=O)NH amide on the second ring, followed by CH2CH2COOH (a β-alanine unit).

Reading the left ring: the carbon bearing COOH is at about (257, 757), the carbon bearing OH is at (233, 855), and the carbon bearing the azo group is at (427, 795). Going around the ring, C(COOH) is next to CH at (350, 730), which is next to C(N=N). C(N=N) is then next to CH (402, 893), then CH (306, 922), then C(OH). So the azo carbon is para to the OH carbon, and the COOH carbon is adjacent to the OH carbon.
