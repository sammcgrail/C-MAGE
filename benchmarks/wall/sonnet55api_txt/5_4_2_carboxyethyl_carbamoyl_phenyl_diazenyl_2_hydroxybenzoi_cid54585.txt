This is balsalazide: a salicylic acid ring joined through an azo bond to a para-substituted benzamide, which carries a β-alanine chain.

Left ring: the carboxylic acid carbon sits on the ring carbon at about (258, 757). The adjacent ring carbon bears OH (at about 233, 855), and the azo group is on the ring carbon at about (427, 795). The ring runs C1 (COOH), C2 (OH), C3, C4, C5 (azo), C6. In the drawing, C1 (258, 757) connects to C6 (350, 730), which connects to C5 (427, 795, the azo carbon), then C4 (402, 893), C3 (307, 921), and C2 (233, 855, OH). The azo group is therefore para to the OH, and the COOH is ortho to the OH.

Chain: the azo N=N links to the para-substituted benzene, which carries C(=O)NH-CH2CH2-COOH.
