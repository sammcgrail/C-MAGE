The drawing shows glutamic acid: a five-carbon chain with a carboxylic acid at each end and an NH2 on the carbon next to the right-hand COOH.

**Stereochemistry:** The only wedge is the bold bond from that stereocentre to NH2, so the implicit H points away from the viewer. The CIP priorities are NH2 > COOH > CH2 > H. Going from NH2 (bottom) to COOH (upper right) to CH2 (upper left) turns counterclockwise, which gives the S configuration. This is L-glutamic acid.

The SMILES below is the standard one for L-glutamic acid, which I recalled rather than derived atom by atom.

SMILES: N[C@@H](CCC(=O)O)C(=O)O
