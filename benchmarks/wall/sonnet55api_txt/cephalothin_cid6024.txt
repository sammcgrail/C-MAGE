I read this as cephalothin: a cephalosporin core (a β-lactam fused to a dihydrothiazine) carrying an acetoxymethyl group, a carboxylic acid, and a thiophen-2-ylacetamido side chain.

- **Left side:** an acetyl group (CH3–C(=O)–O–) is attached through a CH2 to the ring C=C, and the other alkene carbon bears the COOH.
- **Core:** the ring nitrogen is shared by the four-membered β-lactam, which has a C=O, and the six-membered ring, which contains S and a CH2.
- **Side chain:** the amide NH–C(=O)–CH2 is attached to a thiophene ring through its 2-position.
- **Stereochemistry:** there are two drawn stereocentres.
  - C7, bearing the NH, has a wedge bond to N. With its implicit H pointing back, C(=O), N and C6 appear clockwise from the front, which is `@@` in the SMILES order below.
  - C6, the ring-fusion carbon, has a hashed H. With that H pointing back, N, C7 and S appear clockwise, which is also `@@` in the SMILES order below.

SMILES: CC(=O)OCC1=C(C(=O)O)N2C(=O)[C@@H](NC(=O)Cc3cccs3)[C@@H]2SC1
