The drawing shows sodium 4-hydroxybutanoate (sodium oxybate), made up of a sodium cation and a hydroxy-carboxylate anion.

Reading the structure region by region:
- **Sodium:** a separate Na⁺ label, not bonded to anything.
- **Carboxylate:** a carbon with a double bond to O and a single bond to O⁻.
- **Chain:** from that carboxylate carbon, three more carbons run in a line, ending in OH. That is four carbons in total.

The SMILES is therefore the sodium ion plus the chain written from the OH end to the carboxylate. It has no stereochemistry, since there are no wedges or hashes.

SMILES: [Na+].[O-]C(=O)CCCO
