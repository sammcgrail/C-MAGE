This looks like the risdiplam scaffold: a pyrido[1,2-a]pyrimidin-4-one core carrying a 2,8-dimethylimidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine and a 4,7-diazaspiro[2.5]octane.

**Left bicycle (imidazo[1,2-b]pyridazine)**
- Imidazole ring: N= C(ring fusion) – N(bridgehead) – CH= C(CH3) – back to N.
- Pyridazine ring: the bridgehead N is bonded to a ring N, which is bonded to a C that carries the pyrimidinone. That C connects to CH, then C(CH3), then the fusion carbon.
- SMILES for this part: Cc1cn2nc(cc(C)c2n1)-

**Central pyrido[1,2-a]pyrimidin-4-one**
- Substituent-bearing carbon C2, bonded to N1 (=N–), then the fusion carbon C9a, then N5 (bridgehead).
- C2=C3, then C4(=O) attached to the bridgehead N.
- Pyridine ring: bridgehead N–CH=C(N-substituent)–CH=CH–C9a.
- SMILES: c1(...)cc(=O)n2cc(N...)ccc2n1

**Right-hand amine**
- The N is attached to CH2, then the spiro carbon (part of a cyclopropane), then NH, then CH2CH2 back to N.
- SMILES: N1CC2(CC2)NCC1

**Assembled SMILES**
Cc1cn2nc(cc(C)c2n1)-c1cc(=O)n2cc(N3CCNC4(CC4)C3)ccc2n1
