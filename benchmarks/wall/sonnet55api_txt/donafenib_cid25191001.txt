This is sorafenib with a CD3 group on the amide nitrogen (d3-sorafenib).

Reading the structure region by region:
- **Pyridine ring:** the nitrogen sits at the lower left. The carbon next to it carries the C(=O)NH-CD3 amide. The ring carbon at the top carries the O-aryl ether.
- **Central ring:** a para-substituted benzene, linked through O to the pyridine and through NH to the urea.
- **Urea:** NH–C(=O)–NH.
- **Right ring:** the carbon bonded to the urea NH is attached to two ring carbons. One of these leads to the carbon bearing CF3, which sits next to the carbon bearing Cl. The ring is therefore 4-chloro-3-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl.

Writing the SMILES from the amide end: [2H]C([2H])([2H])NC(=O)c1cc(Oc2ccc(NC(=O)Nc3ccc(Cl)c(C(F)(F)F)c3)cc2)ccn1

Check of the pyridine ring: c1 (carbon bearing the amide), c (CH), c(O...) (carbon bearing the ether), c (CH), c (CH), n1 closes the ring. That is 6 atoms, and the amide carbon is adjacent to N, as drawn.
