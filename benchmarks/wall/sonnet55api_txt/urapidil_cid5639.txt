This is urapidil: a 1,3-dimethyluracil bearing a 6-amino substituent, linked through a propyl chain to a piperazine that carries a 2-methoxyphenyl group.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- **Methoxyphenyl:** a benzene ring with OCH3 on the carbon adjacent to the carbon bonded to the piperazine nitrogen (ortho).
- **Piperazine:** the ring has two nitrogens. One is bonded to the aryl ring and the other to the propyl chain.
- **Linker:** three CH2 groups run from the piperazine nitrogen to an NH.
- **Uracil ring:** the NH is attached to C6, which is double-bonded to C5 (CH). C5 connects to C4(=O), then N3 (methyl), then C2(=O), then N1 (methyl), and back to C6.
