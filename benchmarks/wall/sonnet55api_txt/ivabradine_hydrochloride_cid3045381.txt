This is ivabradine hydrochloride. I read it from the drawing as follows:

- **Left ring system:** a 3-benzazepin-2-one with two aromatic methoxy groups (OMe) and the lactam C=O next to the ring nitrogen.
- **Linker:** an N-propyl chain from the lactam nitrogen to a tertiary N-methylamine.
- **Right ring system:** a bicyclo[4.2.0]octatriene (benzocyclobutene) with two aromatic OMe groups. The ring carbon bearing the CH2N group is the only stereocentre, drawn with a hashed bond.
- **Salt:** HCl is written as a separate component.

For the stereocentre, the hashed CH2N points away from the viewer, so the implicit H points toward the viewer. The CIP priorities are CH2N, then the aromatic carbon, then the ring CH2. On the page these run clockwise, and because H faces the viewer the true sense is reversed, giving S. This matches the known (S)-configuration of ivabradine.
