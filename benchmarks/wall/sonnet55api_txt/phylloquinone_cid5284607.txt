This is phylloquinone (vitamin K1): a 2-methyl-1,4-naphthoquinone carrying a phytyl side chain at C3.

**Ring.** The quinone ring is fused to a benzene ring. Its two C=O carbons sit at the positions drawn with the O labels, and one ring carbon beside them carries a methyl group. The adjacent ring carbon carries the side chain.

**Side chain.** Reading from the ring outward, I counted:
- a CH2, then a CH= double-bonded to a carbon bearing a methyl;
- three CH2 groups, then a stereocentre with a hashed methyl;
- three CH2 groups, then a stereocentre with a wedged methyl;
- three CH2 groups, then a terminal isopropyl group.

**Double bond.** The methyl on the quaternary alkene carbon and the ring-side CH2 on the other alkene carbon both point up. So the two main-chain CH2 groups are on opposite sides, which makes the double bond E.
