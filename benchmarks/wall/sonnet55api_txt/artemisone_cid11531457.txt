This is an artemisinin-type structure: the dihydroartemisinin core with a thiomorpholine 1,1-dioxide ring on the acetal-adjacent carbon in place of the usual OH or OR group.

**Ring system**
- A cyclohexane ring carries a hashed methyl on one carbon and the ring-fusion CH, which has a hashed H.
- A tetrahydropyran ring contains the CH(CH3) carbon (wedged methyl), the CH bearing the nitrogen substituent (hashed bond), the ring oxygen, and the acetal carbon (wedged H).
- The 1,2,4-trioxane bridge runs from the quaternary carbon (hashed bond to the peroxide O) through the O–O bond to the carbon bearing a hashed methyl. That carbon is also bonded to the acetal carbon through the second bridging oxygen.
- A CH2CH2 bridge links the second ring-fusion CH (hashed H) to the methyl-bearing bridgehead carbon.

**Substituent**
- The nitrogen belongs to a six-membered ring N–CH2–CH2–S(=O)(=O)–CH2–CH2, which is thiomorpholine 1,1-dioxide.
