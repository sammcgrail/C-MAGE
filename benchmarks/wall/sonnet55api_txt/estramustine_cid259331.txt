This is estramustine, which is estradiol with its 3-hydroxyl converted to a bis(2-chloroethyl)carbamate. I read it region by region.

- **Aromatic A ring:** it sits on the right and carries an O–C(=O)–N(CH2CH2Cl)2 group, which is the carbamate ester.
- **Steroid core:** the B and C rings are six-membered and the D ring is a five-membered ring on the left, the usual estra-1,3,5(10)-triene skeleton.
- **D ring substituents:** the 17-OH and the C13 methyl are both drawn with wedges.
- **Ring-junction hydrogens:** H at C8 is a wedge, H at C9 is hashed, and H at C14 is hashed. This matches the natural estradiol configuration (8R, 9S, 13S, 14S, 17S).

I took the stereo SMILES from natural estradiol and replaced the phenol OH with the carbamate.
