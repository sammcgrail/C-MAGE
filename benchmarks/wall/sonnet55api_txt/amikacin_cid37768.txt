I read this as amikacin, a kanamycin A derivative. It has a central 2-deoxystreptamine ring with two sugar rings and an acylated amine:

- The left sugar is a 6-amino-6-deoxyglucose, with an aminomethyl group at its ring carbon next to O.
- The upper-right sugar is a 3-amino-3-deoxyglucose, with a CH2OH group.
- The central cyclohexane carries a free NH2, an OH, and two glycosidic O-links.
- The central ring's NH is acylated by a 4-amino-2-hydroxybutanoyl chain (the L-HABA side chain).
