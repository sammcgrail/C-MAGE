The drawing is a progesterone skeleton: a four-ring steroid with a 3-keto-4-ene in ring A, two angular methyl groups and an acetyl group on ring D.

- **Stereochemistry:** the drawing is flipped left to right compared with the usual steroid layout, so the wedges and hashes look reversed. Reading them in that flipped view, they match natural progesterone:
  - the C10 and C13 methyls are hashed (β in the standard view);
  - the C17 acetyl is hashed (β);
  - the C8 H is hashed (β);
  - the C9 H and C14 H are wedged (α).
- **Rest of the molecule:** a ring A enone (C=C conjugated to the C=O), a methyl ketone (acetyl) at C17, and no other heteroatoms.

I did not re-derive the @/@@ marks atom by atom. I took them from the standard natural-progesterone SMILES, which has the same stereochemical pattern.
