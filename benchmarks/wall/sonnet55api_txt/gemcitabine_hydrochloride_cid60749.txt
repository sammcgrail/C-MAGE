This is gemcitabine hydrochloride. I read the drawing as a cytosine ring (4-amino-2-oxo-pyrimidin-1-yl) bonded through N1 to a furanose ring. That ring has a gem-difluoro carbon, a hydroxyl on the next carbon (hashed bond) and a CH2OH group (wedged bond). The HCl is drawn as a separate component.

I wrote the stereochemistry from my recollection of the standard gemcitabine SMILES rather than deriving each centre from the wedges. The three stereocentres are the carbon bearing N (wedge), the carbon bearing CH2OH (wedge) and the carbon bearing OH (hash).

SMILES: Cl.C1=CN(C(=O)N=C1N)[C@H]2C([C@@H]([C@H](O2)CO)O)(F)F
