This structure is resmetirom, and I read it from the drawing as three parts.

**Left ring (1,2,4-triazine-3,5-dione):** N2 is attached to the aryl ring and sits next to C3 (=O). C3 connects to N4(H), which connects to C5 (=O). C5 connects to C6, which carries the C#N group. C6 is double-bonded to N1, and N1 is bonded back to N2.

**Central benzene ring:** it is attached to N2 of the triazine. Both ortho positions carry Cl, and the para position carries O.

**Right ring (pyridazinone):** the ether O connects to C6 of the ring. C6 is double-bonded to N1, and N1 is bonded to N2(H). N2 connects to C3 (=O), then C4 (carrying the isopropyl group), then C5(H), which is double-bonded to C4 and bonded to C6.
