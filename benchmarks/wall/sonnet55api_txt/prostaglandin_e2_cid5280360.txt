This is prostaglandin E2 (PGE2). I read the structure region by region:

- **Acid chain:** a carboxylic acid (C(=O)OH) followed by three CH2 groups, then a cis (Z) C=C, then one more CH2 that joins the ring.
- **Ring:** a cyclopentanone. The ring carbon next to the C=O carries the acid chain (wedge bond). The other ring carbon next to the C=O is a CH2. The ring carbon bearing a wedged OH sits between that CH2 and the carbon carrying the lower chain (hashed bond).
- **Lower chain:** a trans (E) C=C, then a carbon bearing a wedged OH, then a pentyl tail (five carbons ending in CH3).

I worked out the stereo at the lower-chain OH carbon directly from the wedge geometry, and it gives `CCCCC[C@H](O)/C=C/`. That is the same configuration as `CCCCC[C@@H](/C=C/...)O` with the last two substituents swapped. I did not derive the three ring stereocentres from the drawing. I took them from my recollection of the PGE2 SMILES, which fits the drawn skeleton.

SMILES: CCCCC[C@@H](/C=C/[C@H]1[C@@H](CC(=O)[C@@H]1C/C=C\CCCC(=O)O)O)O
