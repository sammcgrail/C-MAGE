**Pyridinium ring.** The ring is drawn in Kekulé form with N+ bonded to a methyl group. From N+ there is a double bond up to a CH, then a single bond to the next CH, which is double-bonded to the substituted carbon C4. The ring continues from C4 by a single bond to a CH, which is double-bonded to a CH, which closes back to N+ by a single bond. This gives C[N+]1=CC=C(C=C1)C(=O)NCc1ccccc1.
