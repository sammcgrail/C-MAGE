I read this as a cyclic hexadepsipeptide with a beauvericin-type skeleton: three N-methyl-phenylalanine units alternating with three 2-hydroxyisovaleric acid units. Amide and ester links join them in a 18-membered ring.

**Ring sequence.** Going around the ring: Hiv Cα, C(=O), N(CH3), Phe Cα, C(=O), O, and then the pattern repeats. Each Hiv Cα carries an isopropyl group, and each Phe Cα carries a benzyl group.

**Stereochemistry, worked out from the wedges:**
- Each Hiv Cα has its isopropyl on a hashed bond (pointing away). Its neighbours in SMILES order are the ester O, H, iPr and C(=O). This gives `O[C@@H](C(C)C)C(=O)`.
- Each Phe Cα has its benzyl CH2 on a wedge (pointing toward the viewer). Its neighbours in SMILES order are N, H, CH2 and C(=O). This gives `N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)`.

I worked these out explicitly for the top-left Hiv and the top Phe. The other four stereocentres are assumed to follow the same threefold pattern, since the wedges and hashes look identical in the drawing.
