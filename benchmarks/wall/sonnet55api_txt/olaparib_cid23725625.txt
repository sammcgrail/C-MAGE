This structure is olaparib.

Reading the drawing region by region:
- Left: a phthalazin-1(2H)-one. The C=O carbon is bonded to NH, which is bonded to N, which is double-bonded to the ring carbon C4. C4 carries the CH2 linker, and the fused benzo ring completes the bicycle.
- Middle: the CH2 links C4 to a fluorinated benzene ring. The ring carbon bearing the CH2 sits meta to the carbonyl-bearing carbon, and the carbon carrying F is ortho to that carbonyl-bearing carbon.
- Right: the aryl C(=O) is attached to a piperazine nitrogen. The other piperazine nitrogen is acylated with a cyclopropanecarbonyl group.
