I read the structure as a 4-acetamidophenoxy group joined through a CH2 to a quaternary carbon. That carbon carries a methyl group, an OH and a C(=O)NH link to a 4-nitro-3-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl ring.

- **Stereocentre:** The methyl is drawn with a hashed bond, so it points away from the viewer. Seen from the front, the other three substituents run OH (up), CH2 (lower right), C=O (lower left), which is clockwise. Seen from the methyl side (behind the plane), the same order is counterclockwise. With the neighbour order methyl, O, CH2, C(=O) in the SMILES, that is `@`.
- **Left ring:** The NH-bearing carbon, a CF3 carbon next to it, and a nitro carbon next to that give 4-nitro-3-(trifluoromethyl)aniline substitution.
- **Right ring:** The ring is para-substituted, with the ether O on one side and NHC(=O)CH3 on the other.

SMILES: C[C@](O)(COc1ccc(NC(C)=O)cc1)C(=O)Nc1ccc([N+](=O)[O-])c(C(F)(F)F)c1
