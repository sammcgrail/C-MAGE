I read this as clemastine, a 1-methylpyrrolidine carrying a 2-(2-alkoxyethyl) chain. The ether oxygen links to a quaternary carbon that bears a methyl, a phenyl and a 4-chlorophenyl group.

**Connectivity**
- The five-membered ring has one N, which carries a methyl, and four carbons.
- The ring carbon next to N bears a CH2CH2–O chain.
- The oxygen is bonded to a carbon with four different substituents: O, CH3, C6H5 and 4-ClC6H4.

**Stereochemistry**
- The pyrrolidine C2 has a wedged bond to the chain CH2, so H points away from the viewer. Reading the other three neighbours from the front gives N → chain → ring CH2 clockwise. For the SMILES neighbour order (ring CH2, H, N, chain), this corresponds to `@@`.
- The quaternary carbon has a wedged methyl pointing toward the viewer. Seen from the methyl side, O → phenyl → chlorophenyl runs clockwise. For the SMILES neighbour order (O, CH3, phenyl, chlorophenyl), this corresponds to `@`.

SMILES: CN1CCC[C@@H]1CCO[C@](C)(c1ccccc1)c1ccc(Cl)cc1
