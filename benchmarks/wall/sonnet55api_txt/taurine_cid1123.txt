The drawing shows a sulfonic acid group (S bearing two =O and one OH) attached to a two-carbon chain that ends in NH2. This is 2-aminoethanesulfonic acid, which is taurine.

Reading the atoms in order: NH2 is bonded to a CH2, which is bonded to a second CH2, which is bonded to S. The S carries two double-bonded O atoms and one OH. There are no wedges, hashes or charges, so no stereochemistry is needed.

SMILES: NCCS(=O)(=O)O
