The drawing shows anserine (β-alanyl-N(π)-methyl-histidine). I read it in four parts:

- **Left chain:** H2N–CH2–CH2–C(=O)–NH, a β-alanine unit joined through an amide.
- **Stereocentre:** it carries the amide NH, the carboxylic acid (HO–C=O), a CH2 drawn on a wedge bond, and an implicit H.
- **Ring:** the CH2 leads to an imidazole. The ring carbon bearing the CH2 sits next to the N–CH3 nitrogen, and the other ring nitrogen is the unsubstituted =N–.
- **Stereo assignment:** the wedged CH2 points toward the viewer, so H is at the back. Going from NH to CH2 to COOH is then clockwise with H pointing away. That corresponds to `N[C@@H](C…)C(=O)O` in SMILES, the L-histidine configuration.

SMILES: NCCC(=O)N[C@@H](Cc1cncn1C)C(=O)O
