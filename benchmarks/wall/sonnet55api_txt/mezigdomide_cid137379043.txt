Reading the drawing region by region:

- **Glutarimide:** a piperidine-2,6-dione ring (NH between two C=O groups). Its C3 carbon carries a wedge bond to the isoindolinone nitrogen.
- **Isoindolinone:** a lactam with one C=O and one CH2 on the nitrogen, fused to a benzene ring. The ring carbon next to the CH2-bearing ring fusion carries an O–CH2 ether.
- **Linker:** the ether CH2 connects to a para-disubstituted benzene, whose other substituent is a CH2 attached to a piperazine nitrogen.
- **Aryl end:** the other piperazine nitrogen is bonded to a benzene ring that has an ortho fluorine and a para nitrile (C≡N) relative to the piperazine.

**Stereochemistry:** the only drawn stereocentre is the glutarimide C3, with the wedge pointing to the isoindolinone N. Its neighbours are the ring CH2 (left), the C=O (up), the wedged N (lower right) and an implicit H pointing away. Seen from the front, CH2 → C=O → N runs clockwise. Writing the neighbours in SMILES order (CH2, H, N, C=O) then gives `[C@H]`.
