This structure is the methyl ester of DOPA (3,4-dihydroxyphenylalanine), with the stereochemistry read from the single wedge.

- **Ester end:** a methyl group sits on an oxygen, which is bonded to a carbonyl carbon (C=O), giving COC(=O).
- **Stereocentre:** the alpha carbon carries an NH2 on a bold wedge, a hydrogen that is implicit and therefore pointing away, and a CH2 chain.
- **Side chain:** the CH2 links to a benzene ring carrying two adjacent OH groups. One is on the carbon next to the ring-attachment carbon's neighbour, and the other is one carbon further round, so the ring is a 3,4-dihydroxyphenyl.
- **Configuration:** with the wedged NH2 toward the viewer and H behind, the sequence NH2 → CH2 → C(=O) runs clockwise. This corresponds to `[C@@H]` when written as COC(=O)[C@@H](N)C…, which is the L (S) configuration.
