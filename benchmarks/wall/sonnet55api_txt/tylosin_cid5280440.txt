This is tylosin (a 16-membered macrolide), and I wrote the SMILES from memory of its standard form rather than deriving each stereocentre from the wedges. I checked the connectivity against the drawing, but not each wedge and hash individually.

**Macrolactone ring**, in order around the ring:
- The ring oxygen is attached to the ethyl-bearing carbon, which is followed by the carbon carrying the CH2–O–sugar group.
- Next comes CH=C(CH3)–CH=CH–C(=O)–CH(CH3)–CH2, a diene-one with a methyl on the diene.
- The ring then reaches CH(CH2CHO), whose side chain is the aldehyde, then CH(O–sugar), then CH(CH3), then CH(OH), then CH2, and finally the lactone C(=O) closing back to the ring oxygen.

**Left sugar** (on the CH2–O side chain): a 6-deoxy sugar with two OMe groups and one OH, the 2,3-di-O-methyl-6-deoxyhexose mycinose.

**Right sugars**, on the ring CH–O:
- The first is a 6-deoxy sugar bearing NMe2 and OH, which is mycaminose.
- It carries a second sugar on its 4-O, a 2,6-dideoxy sugar with a C-methyl and OH groups, which is mycarose.
