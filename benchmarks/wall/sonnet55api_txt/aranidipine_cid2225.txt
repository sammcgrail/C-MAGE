- **Ring:** the NH is bonded to C2 (bearing a methyl), which is double-bonded to C3. C3 carries an ester and is bonded to C4, the sp3 carbon. C4 is bonded to C5, which is double-bonded to C6. C6 carries a methyl and is bonded back to the NH.

The sp3 C4 is a stereocentre by connectivity, but it is drawn with plain bonds, so I give no stereochemistry.
