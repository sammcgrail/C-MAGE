This structure is citalopram, and the single hashed bond sets it as the escitalopram enantiomer.

- **Amine chain:** an N,N-dimethylamino group, then three CH2 groups, ending at the quaternary stereocentre.
- **Stereocentre:** the quaternary carbon carries a 4-fluorophenyl group, the ring oxygen, the aromatic ring carbon, and the hashed CH2 of the chain.
- **Isobenzofuran ring:** the five-membered ring is C–O–CH2–aryl–aryl, fused to a benzene ring. That benzene ring carries the nitrile (C#N) at the position para to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to the stereocentre.

For the stereochemistry I wrote the SMILES with the neighbours of the stereocentre in the order chain CH2, ring-closure aromatic carbon, fluorophenyl, O. The chain CH2 is hashed, so it points away from the viewer. Seen from the front, the other three neighbours run aromatic carbon → fluorophenyl → O counterclockwise. Viewed from the chain side, which is behind the page, that order is clockwise, which corresponds to `@@`.
