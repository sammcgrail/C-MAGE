Reading the drawing from left to right, the molecule is HO‑CH2‑CH2‑CH2‑S‑CH2‑CH(NH2)‑COOH. This is a cysteine with a 3‑hydroxypropyl group on the sulfur.

Stereochemistry: only the α‑carbon is drawn with a stereo bond. The NH2 is on a hashed wedge, so it points away from the viewer and the implicit H points toward the viewer.

- The CIP priorities at this carbon are N > CH2S > COOH, because S outranks O at the first point of difference.
- Viewed from the front, N (up) → CH2S (lower left) → COOH (lower right) runs counterclockwise.
- The H is toward the viewer, so the sense is reversed and the centre is clockwise, giving R. This matches the L‑cysteine configuration.
