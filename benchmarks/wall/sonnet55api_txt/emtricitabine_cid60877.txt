I read the molecule as a 5-fluorocytosine ring (this matches emtricitabine) attached through N1 to an oxathiolane ring bearing a CH2OH group.

- **Pyrimidine ring:** N1 (attached to the sugar-like ring), C2 as a C=O, N3, C4 bearing NH2, C5 bearing F, and C6 as CH.
- **Oxathiolane ring:** five atoms, a carbon (C5) bonded to N1, then O, then a carbon (C2) bearing the CH2OH, then S, then a CH2 back to C5.
- **Stereochemistry:** the CH2OH bond and the N bond are both drawn as hashed wedges, so both ring carbons are stereocentres.
  - At the CH2OH carbon, the hashed CH2OH points away and the implicit H points toward the viewer. Seen from H, CH2OH → O → S runs clockwise, which gives `@@` in the order written.
  - At the N-bearing carbon, the hashed N points away and the implicit H points toward the viewer. Seen from H, O → CH2 → N runs counterclockwise, which gives `@@` in the order written.

SMILES: OC[C@@H]1O[C@@H](CS1)n1cc(F)c(N)nc1=O
