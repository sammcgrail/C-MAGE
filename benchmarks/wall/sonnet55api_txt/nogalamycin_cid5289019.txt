I read the structure as a nogalamycin-type anthracycline. It has a tetramethoxy deoxy-sugar (nogalose) on the left and a bridged aminosugar ring on the right.

**Left side**
- The sugar ring is a six-membered pyranose. Its anomeric carbon is hashed to the aglycone oxygen.
- Going round the ring: a methyl-bearing carbon (wedge), then OMe (hash), then a quaternary C(CH3)(OMe), then OMe (wedge).

**Aglycone**
- A cyclohexane ring carries, in order: C–O-sugar (hash), CH2, C(OH)(CH3) (methyl hashed), and CH–CO2Me (wedge).
- This ring is fused to the aromatic ring that bears a phenolic OH.
- A quinone-like ring with two C=O groups links that ring to a second aromatic ring. The second ring has a phenolic OH, an aryl–O bond to the acetal carbon, and a bond to the quaternary methyl carbon.

**Right side**
- A bicyclic ketal bridge links the quaternary C(CH3) and the acetal CH through an O bridge, with the aryl O on the same acetal CH.
- The ring carbons are C(OH), C(NMe2) (wedge) and C(OH).

I assigned each @/@@ from the wedge and hash geometry in the drawing. Plain-bond centres get no stereo.
