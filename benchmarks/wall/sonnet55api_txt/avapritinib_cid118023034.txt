I read the structure in four pieces and joined them.

- **Stereocentre:** a quaternary carbon bearing NH2, a hashed methyl, a 4-fluorophenyl and a pyrimidin-5-yl group.
- **Pyrimidine and piperazine:** the pyrimidine is attached at C2 to one piperazine nitrogen.
- **Core:** the other piperazine nitrogen is attached to C4 of a pyrrolo[2,1-f][1,2,4]triazine.
- **Pyrazole:** the triazine carries a 1-methylpyrazol-4-yl group on the pyrrole carbon.

For the chirality, the methyl is hashed, so it points away from the viewer. The other three substituents then run NH2 (left), aryl (up), pyrimidine (right), which is clockwise in the drawing. In the SMILES the methyl is written first, so I look from the methyl side. From there the order N → aryl → pyrimidine is counterclockwise, which gives `@`.
