The drawing shows a 2,3-dimethylphenyl ring and a 1H-imidazol-4-yl ring joined through a CH(CH3) carbon, which is the structure of medetomidine. The stereocentre carries a wedged methyl, so stereochemistry is included.

**Connectivity**
- The benzene ring has two adjacent methyl groups. One is at the carbon next to the ring carbon bearing the side chain, and the other is at the next carbon along.
- The imidazole ring has an NH and a ring N, and it is attached to the stereocentre through a ring carbon next to the NH.
- The stereocentre carries the wedged methyl, the aryl ring, the imidazole ring and an implicit H.

**Stereochemistry**
- CIP priorities: imidazolyl carbon (N,C,C) > aryl carbon (C,C,C) > methyl > H.
- The methyl is on a wedge, so the implicit H points away from the viewer.
- Viewed from the front, the sequence imidazole (lower left), then aryl (right), then methyl (up) runs counterclockwise. With H at the back, that is S.
- This matches dexmedetomidine, the (S)-enantiomer. I wrote the chirality tag from the known dexmedetomidine SMILES rather than deriving the @ symbol from the drawing.
