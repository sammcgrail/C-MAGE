This is a quinazoline bearing three substituents, plus an indole joined through an ether oxygen. I read it region by region.

**Indole part.** The 2-methylindole has an NH. C2 carries the methyl and C3 is CH. The fused benzene ring carries F at C4 and the ether O at C5.

**Linker.** The O at indole C5 joins quinazoline C4.

**Quinazoline part.** C4 is bonded to N3, then C2 (CH), then N1, then C8a. The benzo ring runs C8a, C8 (H), C7 (O-propyl chain), C6 (OMe), C5 (H), C4a.

**Side chain.** At C7 the chain is O–CH2CH2CH2–N(pyrrolidine).
