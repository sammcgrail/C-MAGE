This drawing is temozolomide, an imidazotetrazinone.

Atoms and bonds as drawn:
- The carboxamide is NC(=O)-, attached to ring carbon C1.
- C1 is double-bonded to C2 (the fusion carbon), and C1 is single-bonded to the imidazole N=, which is double-bonded to CH.
- That CH is bonded to the bridgehead N (N-fused).
- C2 is bonded to the bridgehead N and to the N of the N=N unit.
- N=N then connects to N(CH3), which connects to C(=O), which is bonded back to the bridgehead N.

SMILES: NC(=O)c1ncn2c1nnn(C)c2=O
