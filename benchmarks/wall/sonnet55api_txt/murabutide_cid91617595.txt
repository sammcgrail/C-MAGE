This is the open-chain form of muramyl dipeptide (MDP) as its butyl ester: an N-acetylmuramic acid aldehyde, linked through its lactyl ether to L-Ala, then D-isoglutamine with a butyl ester.

**Fragments read from the drawing**
- **Butyl ester:** the four-carbon chain at the top right is bonded to O, then to C(=O) (`CCCCOC(=O)`).
- **Isoglutamine unit:** the α-carbon carries a bold wedge to the CH2CH2C(=O)NH2 side chain and an NH to the alanine. Reading the wedge gives the D configuration.
- **Alanine unit:** the methyl is on a hashed bond, which gives L-Ala.
- **Lactyl ether:** the lactyl carbon has a wedged methyl and is bonded to the sugar O.
- **Sugar chain:** it runs CHO, then C(NHAc), then C(O-lactyl), then CH(OH), then CH(OH), then CH2OH. The NHAc and the lower OH are hashed, while the O-lactyl and the upper OH are wedged.
