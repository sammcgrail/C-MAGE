This is ambenonium chloride, drawn as a dication with two chloride counterions.

Reading the structure region by region:
- Each end has a 2-chlorobenzyl group (a benzene ring with Cl ortho to the CH2) attached to a quaternary N+.
- Each N+ also carries two ethyl groups and a CH2CH2 linker to an amide NH.
- The centre is an oxalamide, NH–C(=O)–C(=O)–NH.
- Two Cl⁻ ions are shown separately.
