Images are the fast path. A PDF goes through all three stages — figure extraction, structure segmentation, then reading each structure into SMILES — and takes minutes on this CPU server. An image skips stage 1 entirely, so a single cropped structure comes back in seconds and a whole figure with several structures in well under a minute. One job runs at a time; you get a queue position if the server is busy.

C-MAGE = VisualHeist (figures out of pages) → DECIMER segmentation (structures out of figures) → CXMolScribe (SMILES out of structures). The pipeline's own confidence threshold is 0.8431: above it a structure is filed as high confidence, below it as "review or discard". That number measures how sure the reader is of the drawing it was handed — not whether the drawing still held the whole molecule. The Benchmark tab measures the difference against known answers.
