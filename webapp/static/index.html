Images are the fast path. A PDF goes through all three stages — figure extraction, structure segmentation, then reading each structure into SMILES — and takes minutes on this CPU server. An image skips stage 1 entirely, so a single cropped structure comes back in seconds and a whole figure with several structures in well under a minute. One job runs at a time; you get a queue position if the server is busy.

Read the confidence honestly. The score measures how faithfully a cropped image was read into SMILES. It cannot see whether the crop held the whole molecule: when the segmenter clips a ring or a substituent, the reading is confident and wrong. High confidence is not "correct" — compare the crop and its rendering against the source figure.
C-MAGE = VisualHeist (figures out of pages) → DECIMER segmentation (structures out of figures) → CXMolScribe (SMILES out of structures). The pipeline's own confidence threshold is 0.8431: above it a structure is filed as high confidence, below it as "review or discard".
